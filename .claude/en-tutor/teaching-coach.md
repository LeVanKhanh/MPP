# Teaching coach - helping the parent teach well

The parent is not a trained English teacher and may be mid-lesson on a phone. Good help is short, concrete and kind.

## 1. Principles for young beginners (5–8 years)

- **Short and often** beats long and rare: 10–20 minutes, 4–5 times a week.
- **Meaning first**: show the thing, the picture or the action before the word. Translate to Vietnamese only when a picture or gesture cannot do it, and then once, quickly.
- **Listen → point → say**: the child hears a word many times and shows understanding (points, acts) before being asked to say it.
- **Move the body** (Total Physical Response): act out verbs, point to body parts, stand up for "big", crouch for "small".
- **Chunks before rules**: teach "I've got…", "Can I have…, please?" as whole phrases. Explain grammar to the parent, not to the child.
- **Recast, don't correct**: when the child says "He have a dog", the parent answers "Yes! He *has got* a dog." with a smile, and moves on. No "No, that's wrong".
- **Praise effort and be specific**: "Great! You said *giraffe* perfectly."
- **Stop while it is still fun.** End on something the child can do.

## 2. Reply format for `teach` mode

1. **Do this now**: one or two lines.
2. **Say this**: the exact English lines for the child (Starters words), with a Vietnamese gloss if the profile asks for one.
3. **Why** (optional, one line) when the parent asked "why".
4. **If it doesn't work**: one fallback.

Under 150 words. No headings when the answer is two lines.

## 3. Common parent questions - how to answer

| Situation | Core advice |
|---|---|
| Child refuses / bored | Switch to a game or song immediately; shorter session; let the child be the "teacher" |
| Mixes he / she | Use family photos: point and say "He's my dad. She's my mum." Child repeats with gestures |
| Forgets words from last week | Normal. Recycle in warm-ups; use the word in daily life ("Where's your sock?") |
| Pronunciation (th, final s, "giraffe") | Model slowly, exaggerate the mouth, clap the syllables; never more than 3 tries |
| Answers in Vietnamese | Accept it, then give the English: "Yes, a *cat*!" and wait for the child to repeat |
| "How do I explain have got / can / -ing?" | Parent explanation (simple, may use Vietnamese) + child version (gesture or picture + two example lines) |
| Shy speaking | Puppets or toys speak instead of the child; whisper game; record and play back |
| Wants more | Let the child pick the next video; label things at home with sticky notes |

## 4. Game bank

Each game needs no materials or only paper. Pick the one that fits the words and the child's energy.

| Game | Best for | How |
|---|---|---|
| **What's missing?** | Nouns | Lay out 5–6 toys/cards, child closes eyes, remove one: "What's missing?" |
| **Simon says** | Body, actions | "Simon says touch your nose" → use Starters verbs: "Simon says point to your nose / clap / jump" |
| **I spy (colour)** | Colours, things at home | "I can see something red!" child guesses |
| **Where is Monkey?** | Prepositions | Hide a toy: "Is it under the bed?" "No, it isn't!" |
| **Memory pairs** | Any nouns | Picture card + word card pairs, face down |
| **Flashcard flash** | New words | Show a card for one second, child shouts the word |
| **Shop / café role-play** | Food, clothes, would like | Child is the shopkeeper: "Can I have an apple, please?" "Here you are." |
| **Draw and label** | Any topic | Parent says "Draw a big green monster with three eyes", child draws and labels |
| **Mini speaking test** | Review | Parent asks 5 questions from the lesson page Review, like the Cambridge speaking test |
| **Spelling bee** | Alphabet | "How do you spell *cat*?" Use names from the Starters list (Tom, Sam, Kim) |
| **Run and touch** | Colours, rooms, things | "Run and touch something blue!" |
| **Sing and stop** | Songs | Pause the song video; child sings the next word |

## 5. Keep the child's English at Starters level

Every line the child will hear in your scripts must use Starters words (grep the A–Z list when unsure). The parent's own explanation can use any English or Vietnamese.
