# Capstone — AI-Led Digital Transformation Strategy

> Capstone Project · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

The closing project of the programme: combining technology strategy, portfolio, operating model, risk / governance and finance into one investment decision pack for the executive team.

- **In scope:** one enterprise scenario used throughout; current-state diagnosis, 3 strategic options, business case, transition architecture, operating model, AI governance, execution plan and value measurement.
- **Out of scope:** standalone exercises that do not connect to the same scenario.
- **Recommendation:** use a real initiative if you are allowed to, anonymising data where needed. Use a simulated scenario only when no real work is available.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Connect business goal → capability → initiative → investment → measurable result.
- [ ] Argue in financial terms: 3-year TCO, ROI, sensitivity, downside scenario.
- [ ] Present the trade-offs between options, not just defend the chosen one.
- [ ] Answer hard questions from the CEO ("Why now?"), the CFO ("Why this price?") and the CISO ("What is our downside?").

## Practical deliverables

**Minimum pack:**

- [ ] 10-slide board deck
- [ ] 2-page decision memo
- [ ] 3-year strategy roadmap
- [ ] Portfolio ranking
- [ ] 3-year TCO / ROI model
- [ ] Risk register
- [ ] KPI tree
- [ ] Execution milestones
- [ ] CTO first-100-days plan

**Done when:** you present for 15 minutes and take 15 minutes of challenge on value, assumptions, the downside scenario and resources. Not every assumption has to be right, but every assumption has to be checkable.

## Self-assessment rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Business strategy alignment, problem diagnosis and options | 15 |
| Financial logic (3-year TCO, assumptions, sensitivity, prioritisation) | 20 |
| Technology strategy and architecture trade-offs | 15 |
| Organisation, operating model, workforce and change management | 15 |
| Security, AI / data governance and risk | 10 |
| Execution plan and value measurement | 15 |
| Executive clarity, decision memo, Q&A defense | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Pass mark:** ≥ 80/100, with no criterion below 60% of its maximum. Ideally have one reviewer play the CFO and one play the business owner or CISO. Without reviewers, write 10 hard questions yourself and answer them with evidence.
