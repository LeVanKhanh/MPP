# Module 01 — The Evolving Role of the Strategic CTAIO

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

A CTAIO (Chief Technology & AI Officer) is ultimately accountable for technology and AI creating business value: choosing investments, keeping risk within acceptable limits, and building a team that can deliver.

- **In scope:** the boundaries between CTO / CIO / CAIO / VP Engineering; technology mandate; decision rights; working with the CEO, CFO, CPO and CISO; accountability for budget, risk and organisational capability.
- **Out of scope:** building a detailed strategy (Modules 03, 04) and day-to-day people leadership (Module 11).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell CTO, CIO, CAIO and VP Engineering apart by accountability and decision rights, not by title.
- [ ] Explain the CTO – CEO – CFO – CPO – CISO boundaries for investment, product and risk decisions.
- [ ] Understand what a technology mandate is and why strategy cannot be executed without one.
- [ ] Know which risks a CTO must escalate.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a 2–3 page `CTO Charter`: mission, outcomes, decision scope, stakeholders, decision principles, risks that must be reported.
- **Done when:** you can list 5 decisions the CTO owns and 5 that need consensus, with the reasoning for each group.
