# Module 02 — Emerging Technology & Business Innovation

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

How to discover, assess and filter new technologies by business value instead of chasing trends.

- **In scope:** technology scouting, horizon scanning, S-curves, disruptive vs sustaining innovation, Jobs-to-be-Done, value hypotheses.
- **Out of scope:** tracking macro and geopolitical trends (Module 18); specific AI strategy (Modules 03, 20).

## Free learning resources

- [Thoughtworks Technology Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar) — a real-world example of sorting technologies into adopt / trial / assess / hold.
- [Christensen Institute — Disruptive Innovation](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/disruptive-innovation/) — the original theory of disruptive vs sustaining innovation.
- [Christensen Institute — Jobs to Be Done](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/) — a lens on the customer need behind a technology.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Explain the S-curve and when to move to a new technology.
- [ ] Tell disruptive and sustaining innovation apart, with one example of each.
- [ ] Write a testable value hypothesis for a new technology.
- [ ] Have criteria for rejecting a technology on value / feasibility / risk.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a 4-ring Technology Radar (adopt / trial / assess / hold) for a sample company, each entry backed by evidence and a hypothesis.
- **Done when:** at least 3 technologies are placed in hold with clear reasons, rather than just listing appealing technologies.
