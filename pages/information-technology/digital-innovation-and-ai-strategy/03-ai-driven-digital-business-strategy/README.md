# Module 03 — AI-Driven Digital Business Strategy

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Using AI to change the business model, the value chain and competitive advantage, starting from the business problem rather than the technology.

- **In scope:** Business Model Canvas, value chain, sources of competitive advantage, AI use-case discovery, automation vs augmentation vs new AI products.
- **Out of scope:** technical ML frameworks (Module 20); GenAI system architecture (Module 22).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Analyse a business unit with the Business Model Canvas and the value chain.
- [ ] Tell automation, augmentation and new AI products apart, and how each affects revenue and cost differently.
- [ ] Find AI use cases from process bottlenecks.
- [ ] Write a business hypothesis with a baseline KPI.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** 3 AI strategies for one business unit, each stating how revenue, cost, quality or risk changes.
- **Done when:** each strategy has a business hypothesis, a baseline KPI, the expected impact, what needs verifying, and why it is prioritised.
