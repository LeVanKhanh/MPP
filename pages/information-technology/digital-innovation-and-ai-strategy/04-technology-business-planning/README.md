# Module 04 — Technology Business Planning

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Turning business goals into a technology plan with a clear budget, staffing, milestones and assumptions.

- **In scope:** strategic planning, technology capability maps, strategic bets, dependencies, budgeting, scenario planning, the annual plan and the 3-year roadmap.
- **Out of scope:** scoring and selecting the project portfolio (Module 05); target architecture (Module 13).

## Free learning resources

- [Learn Wardley Mapping](https://learnwardleymapping.com/) — mapping the value chain and the maturity of each component to decide where to invest.
- [Shell Scenarios](https://www.shell.com/news-and-insights/scenarios.html) — the classic example of scenario planning in a large company.
- [Strategyzer — The Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas) — a starting point for tying the technology plan to the business model.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Draw a capability map and show which capabilities need investment.
- [ ] Tell a strategic bet apart from business-as-usual work.
- [ ] Build a technology budget by CapEx / OpEx and by initiative.
- [ ] Build at least 2 scenarios and the signals that show which one is unfolding.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a 3-year `Technology Strategy & Business Plan`: strategic themes, initiatives, milestones, staffing, budget, assumptions.
- **Done when:** every initiative traces through business objective → capability → investment → expected result.
