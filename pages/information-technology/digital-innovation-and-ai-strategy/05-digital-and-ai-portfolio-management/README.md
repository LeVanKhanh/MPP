# Module 05 — Digital and AI Portfolio Management

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Allocating limited resources across a portfolio of digital and AI initiatives: what to do, what to defer, and what to stop.

- **In scope:** run / grow / transform, scoring, cost of delay, capacity allocation, stage-gates, kill criteria, portfolio risk.
- **Out of scope:** executing individual projects (Module 16); the overall 3-year plan (Module 04).

## Free learning resources

- [Black Swan Farming — Cost of Delay](https://blackswanfarming.com/cost-of-delay/) — how to quantify the cost of being late, the basis for prioritisation.
- [SAFe — Lean Portfolio Management](https://framework.scaledagile.com/lean-portfolio-management) — a portfolio model organised around value streams.
- [SAFe — WSJF](https://framework.scaledagile.com/wsjf) — Weighted Shortest Job First, a scoring method built on cost of delay.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Classify the portfolio as run / grow / transform, and know that the right mix changes with strategy.
- [ ] Calculate cost of delay and use it to prioritise.
- [ ] Design stage-gates and kill criteria before a project starts.
- [ ] Spot concentration risk in the portfolio (dependencies, people, vendors).

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a portfolio of 8–12 initiatives with ranking, spend, expected value, confidence and a dependency map.
- **Done when:** you can defend choosing 3 projects, deferring 3 and stopping 1 when the budget is cut by 20%.
