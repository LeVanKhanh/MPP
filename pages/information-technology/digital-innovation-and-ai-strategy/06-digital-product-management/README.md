# Module 06 — Digital Product Management

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Managing digital products by outcomes for customers and the business, not by the number of features shipped.

- **In scope:** product discovery vs delivery, customer outcomes, product economics, product operating model, retention, experimentation, product KPIs.
- **Out of scope:** taking the product to market (Module 07); marketing analytics (Module 19).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell output (features) apart from outcome (adoption, cycle time, revenue, quality).
- [ ] Understand how discovery and delivery run in parallel.
- [ ] Read basic product economics: retention, activation, unit economics.
- [ ] Design a product experiment with success criteria set before it runs.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a product strategy canvas and an outcome-based roadmap for an internal or customer-facing product.
- **Done when:** every roadmap item is tied to a measurable outcome; no item is just a feature name.
