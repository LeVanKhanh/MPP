# Module 07 — Go-to-Market (GTM)

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P2** (raise to P1 if you have to take a product or offering to market) · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

A plan for getting a product to the right buyers and users and measuring adoption, rather than stopping at the release.

- **In scope:** segmentation, ICP, positioning, pricing, channels, sales enablement, adoption funnel, feedback loop.
- **Out of scope:** product discovery and roadmap (Module 06); detailed marketing measurement (Module 19).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Identify the ICP, buyer and user for a B2B product.
- [ ] Write a positioning statement and choose a suitable pricing model.
- [ ] Draw an adoption funnel with a metric at each step.
- [ ] Set kill / scale rules before launch.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a GTM one-pager for an API, platform or AI product: buyer, user, channels, pricing, success metric.
- **Done when:** there is a plan to measure adoption in the first 90 days and a stop condition if adoption falls short.
