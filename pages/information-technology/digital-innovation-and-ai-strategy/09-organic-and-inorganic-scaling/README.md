# Module 09 — Organic and Inorganic Scaling

> Pillar 1: Digital Innovation and AI Strategy · Priority: **P2** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Growing teams and products through organic growth or through mergers and acquisitions (inorganic, M&A), and the role technology plays in both.

- **In scope:** scaling teams and products, technical due diligence for M&A, integration risk, platform standardisation, assessing synergy claims.
- **Out of scope:** designing team structures (Module 12); managing the integration programme (Module 16).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Recognise when a team needs to split, and how to split it without adding dependencies.
- [ ] Understand the M&A process and where technical due diligence fits in it.
- [ ] List the groups of post-merger technical risk: architecture, data, security, people, contracts.
- [ ] Be appropriately sceptical of synergy claims.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a due diligence checklist for merging two technical estates.
- **Done when:** it identifies 5 groups of post-M&A risk and includes a phased integration plan.
