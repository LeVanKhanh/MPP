# Module 10 — Digital Transformation

> Pillar 2: Enterprise Transformation & Tech-Driven Agility · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Changing how a business creates value with digital technology: its processes, operating model and decision-making, not just digitising paperwork.

- **In scope:** digital maturity assessment, transformation thesis, value streams, operating model redesign, change portfolio, adoption metrics.
- **Out of scope:** the people side of change (Module 26); managing multi-workstream programmes (Module 16).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Assess an organisation's digital maturity against a framework with clear criteria.
- [ ] Write a transformation thesis: why change, what changes, where the value is.
- [ ] Draw a value stream and point out waiting time, handoffs and waste.
- [ ] Measure adoption, not just completed deployment.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a current-state diagnosis of one end-to-end process, identifying 3 bottlenecks with significant cost.
- **Done when:** there is a 12–18 month roadmap with baseline, target, accountable owner and measurement plan.
