# Module 11 — Technology Leadership

> Pillar 2: Enterprise Transformation & Tech-Driven Agility · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Leading people and the management system of a technology organisation, so that decisions flow through clear roles instead of depending on one person.

- **In scope:** leadership vs management, delegation, coaching, succession planning, managing managers, a culture of accountability, conflict and trust.
- **Out of scope:** designing team structures (Module 12); executive-level persuasion and conflict resolution (Module 26).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell leadership and management apart, and know when each is needed.
- [ ] Delegate according to the maturity of the person taking the work.
- [ ] Understand how managing managers differs from managing individual contributors.
- [ ] Have a succession plan for key roles.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a leadership operating cadence: weekly review, monthly portfolio, quarterly strategy, talent review.
- **Done when:** every meeting in the cadence has a purpose, a decision owner and a clear output, and decisions no longer funnel to one person.
