# Module 12 — Organisational and Strategic Agility

> Pillar 2: Enterprise Transformation & Tech-Driven Agility · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Designing an organisation that can change direction quickly without breaking: team structure, decision rights and the flow of work.

- **In scope:** team topology, product / platform teams, dependencies, decision latency, organisational bottlenecks, adaptive planning, flow metrics.
- **Out of scope:** individual leadership skills (Module 11); programme management (Module 16).

## Free learning resources

- [Team Topologies — Key Concepts](https://teamtopologies.com/key-concepts) — 4 team types and 3 interaction modes.
- [DORA](https://dora.dev/) — research on delivery performance and the organisational factors behind it.
- [Flow Framework](https://flowframework.org/) — flow metrics for measuring the flow of value instead of busyness.
- [Atlassian Team Playbook — DACI](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci) — a framework for assigning decision rights.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell stream-aligned, platform, enabling and complicated-subsystem teams apart.
- [ ] Use RACI or DACI to make clear who decides what.
- [ ] Measure flow (lead time, flow efficiency) and tell it apart from utilisation.
- [ ] Explain the trade-off between autonomy and standardisation.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a team topology and RACI / decision rights for at least 3 teams, with a dependency risk assessment.
- **Done when:** it shows how to reduce dependencies and handoffs, optimising the whole system instead of maximising each team's utilisation.
