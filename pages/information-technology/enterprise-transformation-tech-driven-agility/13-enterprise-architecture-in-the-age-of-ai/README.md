# Module 13 — Enterprise Architecture in the Age of AI

> Pillar 2: Enterprise Transformation & Tech-Driven Agility · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Architecture at enterprise level: how business, data, application and technology fit together, and how to bring AI in without fragmenting the architecture.

- **In scope:** business / data / application / technology architecture, capabilities, integration patterns, modernisation, reference architectures, ADRs, governance.
- **Out of scope:** cloud strategy and cost (Module 14); detailed GenAI system architecture (Module 22).

## Free learning resources

- [The Open Group — TOGAF Standard](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf) — the enterprise architecture standard; a personal-use copy can be downloaded after registering.
- [Architecture Decision Records](https://adr.github.io/) — how to record architecture decisions and their reasoning.
- [Martin Fowler — Software Architecture Guide](https://martinfowler.com/architecture/) — collected writing on architecture and modernising legacy systems.
- [The C4 model](https://c4model.com/) — drawing architecture at several levels of detail for different audiences.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell the 4 architecture layers apart: business, data, application, technology.
- [ ] Know the modernisation patterns: strangler, replatform, refactor, replace.
- [ ] Write an ADR with context, options and consequences.
- [ ] Understand just-enough architecture governance that does not turn into an approval ritual.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a 3-year current-state and target-state architecture, the transition architectures, and a technical debt list ranked by business impact.
- **Done when:** you can defend the architecture against compliance, performance, migration-risk and cost constraints.
