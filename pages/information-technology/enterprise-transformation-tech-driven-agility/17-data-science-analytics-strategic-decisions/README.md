# Module 17 — Data Science and Analytics for Strategic Decisions

> Pillar 2: Enterprise Transformation & Tech-Driven Agility · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Using data to make sound strategic decisions: choosing metrics, reading results, and knowing when the data is not enough to draw a conclusion.

- **In scope:** KPI trees, leading vs lagging indicators, experimentation, causal inference basics, data quality, executive dashboards, decision analytics.
- **Out of scope:** building ML models (Module 20); marketing analytics (Module 19).

## Free learning resources

- [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) — short courses on data analysis, visualisation and SQL.
- [Causal Inference: The Mixtape — Scott Cunningham](https://mixtape.scunning.com/) — a free book on causal inference, to avoid confusing correlation with causation.
- [DORA](https://dora.dev/) — an example of delivery metrics backed by research, and how to use them within their scope.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Build a KPI tree from business goals down to operational metrics.
- [ ] Tell leading and lagging indicators apart.
- [ ] Understand A/B tests and their common traps (sample size, peeking, multiple metrics).
- [ ] Know why velocity or lines of code are not business outcomes.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** an executive scorecard covering value, delivery, reliability, security, workforce and financial metrics, with caveats noted.
- **Done when:** every metric has a definition, a data source and limits on interpretation, and none is misused as a target.
