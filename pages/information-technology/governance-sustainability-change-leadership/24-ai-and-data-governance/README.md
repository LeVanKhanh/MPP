# Module 24 — AI and Data Governance

> Pillar 4: Governance, Sustainability & Change Leadership · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

The rules, roles and oversight mechanisms that keep data and AI used for the right purpose, safely and lawfully, with someone accountable across the whole lifecycle.

- **In scope:** data ownership, classification, retention, privacy, model governance, responsible AI, data lineage, evaluation and monitoring.
- **Out of scope:** overall cybersecurity governance (Module 15); designing human–AI workflows (Module 23).

- [EU Artificial Intelligence Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/) — browse the EU AI Act by risk level.
- [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) — AI principles referenced by many countries.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Classify AI use cases by risk level and know which controls each level needs.
- [ ] Understand data ownership, classification, retention and data lineage.
- [ ] Know what goes into a model inventory and why you need one.
- [ ] Know how to monitor AI after deployment: drift, quality, incidents.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** AI policy v1, a data governance RACI, a use-case approval checklist, a model inventory.
- **Done when:** there is risk tiering and oversight across the whole AI lifecycle, with a clear approver for each tier.
