# Module 25 — Tech-Enabled ESG: Strategies for Sustainable Impact

> Pillar 4: Governance, Sustainability & Change Leadership · Priority: **P2** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Assessing the environmental and social impact of technology choices, and using technology to measure and improve ESG metrics.

- **In scope:** sustainability trade-offs, energy and compute footprint, responsible procurement, accessibility, ESG metrics.
- **Out of scope:** detailed ESG reporting under financial reporting standards.

## Free learning resources

- [Green Software Foundation — Green Software Practitioner](https://movement.greensoftware.foundation/collections/3086009) — a free course on green software principles.
- [GHG Protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/) — the standard for measuring greenhouse gas emissions (scope 1, 2, 3).
- [AWS Well-Architected — Sustainability Pillar](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/sustainability-pillar/sustainability-pillar.html) — designing cloud architecture to reduce environmental impact.
- [W3C — WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) — the accessibility standard, the social ("S") side of technology.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Understand scope 1, 2 and 3, and which part technology contributes to.
- [ ] Estimate the energy footprint of a cloud or AI workload.
- [ ] Know the principles of green software: energy efficiency, hardware efficiency, carbon awareness.
- [ ] Understand accessibility as an ESG metric.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a one-page impact assessment for a cloud or AI choice with a significant environmental cost.
- **Done when:** it does not equate "green" with "cheap", and it names the downsides of the chosen solution.
