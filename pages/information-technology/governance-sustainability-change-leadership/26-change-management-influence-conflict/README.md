# Module 26 — Change Management, Influence and Conflict

> Pillar 4: Governance, Sustainability & Change Leadership · Priority: **P0** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Bringing change into an organisation when people resist it: building coalitions, persuading, negotiating and resolving conflicts of interest.

- **In scope:** stakeholder mapping, coalition building, diagnosing resistance, negotiation, persuasion, conflict resolution, executive storytelling.
- **Out of scope:** designing the transformation programme (Module 10); day-to-day team leadership (Module 11).

## Free learning resources

- [Kotter — The 8-Step Process for Leading Change](https://www.kotterinc.com/methodology/8-steps/) — a model for leading change at organisation level.
- [Prosci — ADKAR Model](https://www.prosci.com/methodology/adkar) — a model for change at individual level.
- [Harvard Program on Negotiation](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/) — free articles and reports on negotiation and conflict resolution.
- [Successful Negotiation — University of Michigan (Coursera, free to audit)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/negotiation-skills) — negotiation skills with practice exercises.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Draw a stakeholder map by power and interest.
- [ ] Tell interests and positions apart in a negotiation.
- [ ] Diagnose the causes of resistance (understanding, ability, motivation, interests).
- [ ] Use Kotter at organisation level and ADKAR at individual level, and know the limits of both.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a change plan for a transformation with 3 opposing groups, with an executive narrative and a communication plan.
- **Done when:** it includes a behaviour-change plan, a way to resolve conflicts of interest, and adoption metrics.
