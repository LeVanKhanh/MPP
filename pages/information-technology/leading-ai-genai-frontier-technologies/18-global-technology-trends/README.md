# Module 18 — Global Technology Trends

> Pillar 3: Leading AI, GenAI & Frontier Technologies · Priority: **P2** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Tracking global technology trends methodically, to decide when to try, when to invest and when to wait.

- **In scope:** trend sensing, hype cycles, competitive signals, geopolitical and data-sovereignty risk, the option value of technology.
- **Out of scope:** assessing a specific technology for the business (Module 02).

## Free learning resources

- [Stanford HAI — AI Index Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index) — an annual report with data on AI capability, cost, investment and policy.
- [Thoughtworks Technology Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar) — updated twice a year on techniques, tools and platforms.
- [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index) — data on how AI is being used in real work.

## Theory to master

- [ ] Understand the hype cycle and why you should not invest at the peak of expectations.
- [ ] Tell strong signals (cost data, adoption) apart from noise (news, demos).
- [ ] Identify geopolitical and data-sovereignty risk in technology that depends on foreign vendors.
- [ ] Understand option value: small investments that keep the option open.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a quarterly technology outlook of 5 trends: opportunity, limits, trigger to revisit.
- **Done when:** each trend has a clear decision (try / invest / wait) and specific conditions for changing that decision.
