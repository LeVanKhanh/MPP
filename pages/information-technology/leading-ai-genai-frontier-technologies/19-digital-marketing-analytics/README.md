# Module 19 — Digital Marketing Analytics

> Pillar 3: Leading AI, GenAI & Frontier Technologies · Priority: **P2** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Reading and judging digital marketing numbers well enough to make investment decisions, telling attractive metrics apart from real unit economics.

- **In scope:** funnel metrics, the limits of attribution, CAC / LTV, cohort retention, experimentation, privacy.
- **Out of scope:** GTM strategy (Module 07); general analytics for strategic decisions (Module 17).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Calculate CAC, LTV and the LTV / CAC ratio.
- [ ] Read cohort retention.
- [ ] Understand the limits of attribution models.
- [ ] Tell vanity metrics apart from metrics tied to revenue.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** an analysis of a sample funnel that separates vanity metrics from unit economics.
- **Done when:** it states what data is needed to conclude that a campaign is genuinely effective for the business.
