# Module 23 — AI-Augmented Organisations: Designing the Hybrid Workforce & Autonomous Systems

> Pillar 3: Leading AI, GenAI & Frontier Technologies · Priority: **P1** · Status: Not started

## Definition and scope

Designing an organisation where people and AI agents work together: who decides what, how far AI may act on its own, and who is accountable when something goes wrong.

- **In scope:** agentic workflows, human-in-the-loop, approval boundaries, role redesign, escalation, exception handling, audit trail.
- **Out of scope:** the technical infrastructure of GenAI systems (Module 22); enterprise AI policy (Module 24).

## Theory to master

- [ ] Tell fixed workflows apart from autonomous agents, and the risks of each.
- [ ] Design approval boundaries: what AI does on its own and what needs human sign-off.
- [ ] Know how to redesign roles when part of the work is automated.
- [ ] Understand audit trails and incident response for agent systems.

## Practical deliverables

- **Deliverable:** a human–AI workflow design with an authority matrix, a rollback process and incident response.
- **Done when:** it sets clear limits on automation, names who is accountable, and defines real-world quality KPIs (not just speed).
