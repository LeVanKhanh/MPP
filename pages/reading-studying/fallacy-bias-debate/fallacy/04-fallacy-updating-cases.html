Fallacy Cases: An Updating Collection

A quick-reference list of fallacy examples, grouped by topic, without detailed analysis

Updated: July 27, 2026

About This Page

Unlike Article 3, where each case comes with full analysis and empirical evidence, this page only collects example sentences for quick reference, without naming the specific fallacy or explaining it further, leaving readers to identify it themselves. The main groups here are organized by topic, ordered from narrower to broader scope.

The page is updated over time as new cases come in.

Workplace / Organizational Life

  • "You've been at the company less than a year, what do you know about giving feedback on the workflow?"
  • "The director already said this way is best, so we should just follow it, no need to discuss or test anything further."
  • "Why does everyone keep picking on me instead of looking at how much the accounting department was out last week?"
  • "This needs improvement; to figure out whether it's actually useful, we just have to finish it, hand it to users, and collect their feedback."

Everyday Life

  • "Whatever you sell is what you hang up as your sign." (Vietnamese proverb: stick to your own trade/role)
  • "Everything I do, who is it all for?"
  • "Men are the beautiful sex, women are the ugly sex. Because women need makeup, men don't."
  • "I have freedom of speech, so I have the right to publicly comment on and judge whatever I see on social media."
  • "Don't do this if you don't know the following."
  • A: "Drinking coffee is bad for your health." B: "Drinking coffee is good for your health."
  • "Our ancestors have lived on rice since ancient times, nothing beats rice."
  • "I've noticed that eating too much of anything is bad. So eating a lot of eggs is bad for your health."

Political Outlook

  • "A segment, a small number of individuals, engaged in improper conduct that affected the organization's image."
  • A politician states: "Over time, people's quality of life has improved, therefore the agencies and departments have done their job well."
  • "An extreme heatwave/storm just happened, proving that climate change is accelerating."

Outlook on Life

  • "Morals today are far worse than in the past."
  • "Fish that won't take salt goes bad; a child who argues with their parents turns out bad in a hundred ways." (Vietnamese proverb)
  • "Ms. H has hundreds of billions and high social status, so what she says must be correct."
  • "Heaven that creates the elephant also creates the grass." (Vietnamese proverb: things will provide for themselves)
  • "He's a professor, so his analysis is always correct."
  • "Gen Z is lazy, unwilling to give their all the way the previous generation did."

Worldview

  • "AI will soon completely replace programmers, so learning to code now is pointless."
  • "Social media is the main cause of the mental health crisis among young people."
  • "AI got one answer wrong, so AI is completely untrustworthy."

Cosmological Outlook

  • "Technology is advancing so fast, we must be living in a simulation."
