Fun for Movers

Home plans that go with the 50 units of Cambridge Fun for Movers (third edition), the practice book for the A1 Movers exam. One unit a week, three short lessons each.

Course plan | Level: A1 Movers | Ages about 7–10

How These Plans Work

  • One unit, one week. Each unit becomes three 30-minute lessons at home: words, then sentences and reading, then a review with speaking.
  • Use the book in class or at home for its own pages. The home lessons add songs, videos, games and short texts of their own with the same words; they do not copy the book's exercises.
  • Every lesson links to a lesson page (like Actions or Animals) where you can tap a word to hear it and watch checked videos.
  • All the words are checked against the A1 Movers wordlist. Movers uses every Starters word too.
