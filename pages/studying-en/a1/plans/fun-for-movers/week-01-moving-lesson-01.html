Week 1 · Lesson 1: Moving Words

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Actions. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 1: Watch us! We're moving!

Fun for Movers · Week 1: Watch us! We're moving! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Moving words Lesson 2 · Who's good at it? Lesson 3 · Me and my friend

You Need

  • Ten word cards: walk, run, jump, hop, skip, climb, dance, skate, swim, kick. Draw a small picture on the back of each.
  • "Hop" is on one foot; "jump" is on two feet. "Skip" here means jumping over a rope (US: jump rope). "Exercise" is in the book but not on the Movers wordlist; say "move" with your child.
  • Short activities: stop early if your child is tired. Video today: about 10 minutes, songs included.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Action Verbs – Let's Move!. Stand up and do every action.
  3. Action cards (6 min): show a card; your child reads it and does the action: "Hop!" Then your child shows a card and you do it. Action words
  4. Simon says (5 min): "Simon says hop! … Simon says climb! … Dance!" Move only after "Simon says". Then your child is Simon.
  5. Missing letters (4 min): write the words with one letter missing: cl_mb, h_p, sk_p, d_nce, sk_te, w_lk. Your child writes the letter, reads the word and does the action.
  6. Watch (2 min): Walking Walking. Stop when the song stops!
  7. I can, I can't (3 min): your child puts the cards in two groups and says: "I can hop. I can't skate."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Hop! … Skip! … Climb!" … "Simon says dance!" … "I can hop. I can't skate."
