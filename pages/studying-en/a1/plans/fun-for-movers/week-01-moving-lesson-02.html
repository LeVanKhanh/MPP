Week 1 · Lesson 2: Who's Good at It?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Actions. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 1: Watch us! We're moving!

Fun for Movers · Week 1: Watch us! We're moving! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Moving words Lesson 2 · Who's good at it? Lesson 3 · Me and my friend

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a playground with six children. Don't write their names. A boy in a green T-shirt climbing a tree; a girl in a red skirt hopping; a boy in blue trousers dancing; a girl in a yellow jacket roller skating; a girl in a pink dress skipping; a boy in an orange hat running.
  • The playground poem below, written large.

Come to our playground, come and see!

Fred's good at climbing. He's up in the tree.

Daisy is hopping – one, two, three!

Paul's dancing round and round.

Lucy's roller skating. Kim's skipping.

And Nick's running. He's very good at running!

Come and play with us!

Answers: Fred – green T-shirt, tree · Daisy – red skirt, hopping · Paul – blue trousers, dancing · Lucy – yellow jacket, roller skating · Kim – pink dress, skipping · Nick – orange hat, running.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Action cards (3 min): quick review: hold up a card from Lesson 1; your child says the word and does the action.
  3. Watch (3 min): I Can Jump!. Move with the song.
  4. Read and write names (7 min): read the poem together. Your child finds each child in your picture and writes the name next to them.
  5. What's he doing? (5 min): point at the picture: "What's Paul doing?" "He's dancing." Then do an action: "What am I doing?" "You're hopping!" Sentences
  6. Good at (4 min): "What are you good at?" "I'm good at running." Then ask about the family: "Is Dad good at dancing?" "No, he isn't!"
  7. Watch (3 min): What are you doing? I'm jumping. Answer before the children in the video.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's Paul doing?" … "He's dancing." … "What are you good at?" … "I'm good at running."
