Week 1 · Lesson 3: Me and My Friend

Lesson 1 · Moving words Lesson 2 · Who's good at it? Lesson 3 · Me and my friend

You Need

  • The playground picture and the poem from Lesson 2.
  • Five questions on paper: "What's Fred climbing?" "What colour is Daisy's skirt?" "What's Lucy wearing?" "Who is skipping?" "What's Nick good at?"
  • Six jumbled words on cards: pho, kips, bimcl, nadce, ketas, nru.
  • In the Movers speaking test, the examiner asks your child about their own life. A short answer is fine; a full sentence is better. The writing about a friend has a real child's name, so keep it at home.

Answers: a tree · red · a yellow jacket · Kim · running. Jumbled words: hop, skip, climb, dance, skate, run.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): What Can You Do?. Answer "I can…" and do it.
  3. Read and answer (6 min): your child looks at the playground picture and writes one, two or three words for each question: "What's Fred climbing?" "a tree".
  4. Jumbled words (4 min): your child puts the letters in order, then does the action: "pho… hop!" Action words
  5. Follow the leader (4 min): your child leads: "Hop to the door! Skip to the window! Dance round and round!" You follow. Then swap.
  6. Me and my friend (6 min): ask: "Who do you play with?" "What are you good at?" Then: "Tell me about your friend. What's your friend's name? How old is your friend? What's your friend good at?" Your child answers, then writes three sentences: "My friend is Kim. She's seven. She's good at skipping." Family and friends words
  7. Watch (2 min): Walking Walking, the last time this week: walk, hop, run and stop!
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's Fred climbing?" … "Who do you play with?" … "Tell me about your friend." … "She's good at skipping."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say, read and spell ten moving words: walk, run, jump, hop, skip, climb, dance, skate, swim, kick.
  • Say what someone is doing and what they are good at: "Fred's climbing a tree." "She's good at skipping."
  • Answer questions about a picture in one to three words, and talk about a friend: "My friend is seven. He's good at running."
