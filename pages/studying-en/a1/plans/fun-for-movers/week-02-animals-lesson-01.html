Week 2 · Lesson 1: Fifteen Animals

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 2: Animals, animals…

Fun for Movers · Week 2: Animals, animals… | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Fifteen animals Lesson 2 · Crocodile facts Lesson 3 · What am I?

You Need

  • Pictures or toys of fifteen animals, with a word card for each: rabbit, kangaroo, dolphin, panda, parrot, penguin, whale, shark, bat, lion, snail, fly, and three your child knows: bear, crocodile, polar bear.
  • Four labels on the table: swim · fly · jump · walk.
  • "Bat" is a Starters word for a baseball bat; in Movers it is also the animal.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Wild animals for kids. Say each animal before the voice does.
  3. New animals (6 min): show each picture and say the word; your child says it back and finds its word card. Animal words
  4. Swim, fly, jump or walk? (5 min): your child puts each card under a label and says it: "A dolphin can swim. A bat can fly. A kangaroo can jump."
  5. Which animal isn't here? (4 min): put six cards face up. Your child closes their eyes and you take one away. "Which animal isn't here?" "The panda!" Then swap.
  6. Spelling race (5 min): say an animal; your child writes it, then checks it with the card. Start with short ones: bat, fly, lion, shark, panda, then kangaroo and penguin.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this?" "It's a kangaroo!" … "A dolphin can swim." … "Which animal isn't here?"
