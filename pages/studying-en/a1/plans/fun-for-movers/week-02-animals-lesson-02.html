Week 2 · Lesson 2: Crocodile Facts

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Animals and Movers Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 2: Animals, animals…

Fun for Movers · Week 2: Animals, animals… | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Fifteen animals Lesson 2 · Crocodile facts Lesson 3 · What am I?

You Need

  • A toy crocodile or a big crocodile drawing, and a sheet of paper with a small hole in it to look through.
  • The twelve quiz sentences below, on cards or read aloud.
  • "Its" (no apostrophe) means "of it": its tail, its teeth. "It's" means "it is". "Teeth" is a Movers word (tooth / teeth).

1 Dolphins live in the sea. · 2 Penguins can fly. · 3 A crocodile has got lots of teeth.

4 Baby crocodiles come out of eggs. · 5 Kangaroos jump on their back legs. · 6 A snail can run.

7 Bats sleep in the day. · 8 Parrots can talk. · 9 Pandas are black and white.

10 A whale is a small fish. · 11 Polar bears like hot places. · 12 Crocodiles have got short legs.

Answers: yes · no · yes · yes · yes · no · yes · yes · yes · no (it's very big, and it isn't a fish) · no (they like cold places) · yes.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Sea Animals. Swim with your arms for each sea animal.
  3. Through the hole (6 min): hold the paper in front of the crocodile and show one part through the hole: "What can you see?" "Its tail!" "Its eyes!" "Its teeth!" Then your child holds the paper.
  4. Draw and write (5 min): your child draws a crocodile and writes: its eyes, its mouth, its teeth, its feet, its tail. Then says: "Its tail is long. Its teeth are big." Movers animal words
  5. Animal quiz (6 min): read each sentence; your child says "Yes!" or "No!" and makes the "no" ones right: "No! Penguins can't fly. They can swim."
  6. Watch (3 min): Crocodile Song. Snap your arms like a crocodile's mouth.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What can you see?" … "Its tail!" … "Its teeth are big." … "Penguins can fly. Yes or no?"
