Week 2 · Lesson 3: What Am I?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 2: Animals, animals…

Fun for Movers · Week 2: Animals, animals… | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Fifteen animals Lesson 2 · Crocodile facts Lesson 3 · What am I?

You Need

  • The animal word cards from Lessons 1 and 2.
  • The eight riddles below. "It sounds like 'chair'" means the animal's name ends with the same sound: bear, chair.
  • Toy animals or the cards for a zoo shop.

1 It's big and brown. It sounds like 'chair'.

2 It's a bird and it swims on the lake. It sounds like 'truck'.

3 It lives on a farm and it can climb. It sounds like 'coat'.

4 It's white and it lives on a farm too. It sounds like 'sleep'.

5 It's red, green and blue and it can talk. It sounds like 'carrot'.

6 It lives with lots of friends and it likes flowers. It sounds like 'tree'.

7 It's very small and it can fly into the kitchen! It sounds like 'sky'.

8 It's long and green and it lives in the jungle. It sounds like 'lake'.

Answers: bear · duck · goat · sheep · parrot · bee · fly · snake.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): What Am I? Animal Guessing Game. Shout the animal before the answer comes.
  3. Riddles (6 min): read each riddle; your child guesses and writes the animal. Say the two words together: "bear, chair!"
  4. Make a riddle (5 min): your child picks a card and makes a riddle for you: "It's black and white. It can't fly, but it can swim. What am I?" You guess.
  5. Zoo shop (5 min): put the toy animals or cards on a table. You buy: "Can I buy a toy panda, please?" "Here you are." Then your child buys.
  6. My favourite animal (3 min): ask: "What's your favourite animal? Where does it live? What can it do?" Short answers are fine; a full sentence is better.
  7. Watch (2 min): Crocodile Song, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It's big and brown. It sounds like 'chair'. What is it?" … "It's a bear!" … "Can I buy a toy panda, please?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and write fifteen animals, with the new Movers ones: rabbit, kangaroo, dolphin, panda, parrot, penguin, whale, shark, bat.
  • Say what an animal can do and talk about its parts: "A kangaroo can jump." "Its tail is long."
  • Answer yes or no about animals, and guess and make animal riddles with "It sounds like…"
