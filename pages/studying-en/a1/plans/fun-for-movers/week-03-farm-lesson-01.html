Week 3 · Lesson 1: What's on the Farm?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 3: Fun at the farm

Fun for Movers · Week 3: Fun at the farm | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's on the farm? Lesson 2 · A day at the farm Lesson 3 · Where do animals live?

You Need

  • A toy farm, or a big farm drawing with a house with a red roof, a field, a tractor, a truck, a farmer, clouds, cows, sheep, ducks, chickens, a rabbit, a kitten and a puppy.
  • Sticky notes with the words: cloud, field, truck, roof, chicken, rabbit, duck, kitten, tractor, farmer, grass, puppy.
  • A kitten is a baby cat; a puppy is a baby dog (both Movers words). British "lorry" and American "truck" are both fine.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Walk Around the Farm. Walk on the spot and say each animal.
  3. Label the farm (7 min): your child reads each sticky note and puts it in the right place: "A tractor… here!" Farm words
  4. What can I see? (5 min): "I can see something white in the sky." "A cloud!" "I can see something red on the house." "The roof!" Then your child gives the clues.
  5. Baby animals (4 min): "A baby cat is a… kitten! A baby dog is a… puppy!" Your child draws a kitten and a puppy and writes the words.
  6. Tractor game (3 min): your child "drives" a toy tractor (or a hand) to each place you say: "Drive to the field! Drive to the house with the red roof!"
  7. Watch (2 min): Driving In My Tractor. Drive round the room and stop with the song.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this?" "It's a tractor." … "I can see something white in the sky." … "A baby cat is a kitten."
