You Need

  • The story below, written large, and the seven sentences to finish (one word each).
  • Four sets of word cards for "Which one is different?": cow, sheep, shark, goat · kitten, puppy, tractor, rabbit · river, lake, sea, mountain · roof, door, window, cloud.

Grandpa's farm

On Sunday mornings, Jack and his cousin Lily go to their grandpa's farm near the river. They help Grandpa in the field. He drives the tractor and they carry the eggs. Then they feed the ducks and the chickens.

Grandpa has got a new puppy. Its name is Spot and it's very funny! Lily likes the puppy, but Jack's favourite animal is the old grey horse.

1 The children go to the farm on ____ mornings. · 2 The farm is near a ____. · 3 Grandpa drives the ____. · 4 The children carry the ____. · 5 They feed the ducks and the ____. · 6 The puppy's name is ____. · 7 Jack's favourite animal is the ____.

Answers: Sunday · river · tractor · eggs · chickens · Spot · horse. Which one is different: the shark (it lives in the sea) · the tractor (it isn't an animal) · the mountain (it hasn't got water) · the cloud (it isn't part of a house).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Nature Vocabulary Chant. Say each word in the gap.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the story together. Your child writes one word for each sentence, then reads the sentence aloud.
  4. Which one is different? (6 min): show one set of cards; your child says which one and why: "The shark, because it lives in the sea." Sentences
  5. Farm helpers (4 min): "Drive the tractor! Carry the eggs! Feed the chickens!" Your child does it; then your child says and you do it.
  6. Watch (3 min): Driving In My Tractor.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "They help Grandpa in the field." … "Which one is different?" … "The shark, because it lives in the sea."
