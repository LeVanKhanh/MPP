Week 3 · Lesson 3: Where Do Animals Live?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Farm and Countryside and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 3: Fun at the farm

Fun for Movers · Week 3: Fun at the farm | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's on the farm? Lesson 2 · A day at the farm Lesson 3 · Where do animals live?

You Need

  • A big sheet of paper in four parts: farm · jungle · sea · river and lake. The animal cards from Weeks 2 and 3.
  • Coloured pencils for a farm drawing.
  • "There", "their" and "they're" sound alike but mean different things. Do the "Sound twins" step only if your child already reads well; if not, play "Animal homes" for three more minutes.

There's a cow in the field. · The children help their grandpa. · They're on the farm.

1 ____ are two horses near the river. · 2 The farmers love ____ new tractor. · 3 Look at the kittens! ____ very funny!

Answers: There · their · They're.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Amazing Animal Habitats! Say "They live in the…" for each animal.
  3. Animal homes (6 min): your child puts each card in one part of the paper and says: "Dolphins live in the sea. Cows live on a farm. Monkeys live in the jungle." Animal words
  4. Animals and me (5 min): ask: "Which animals are you frightened of? Which animal is the best? Which animal would you like to be? Why?" Your child answers, then writes: "I'd like to be a ____ because ____."
  5. Sound twins (3 min): read the three example sentences together, then your child writes there, their or they're in each gap.
  6. My farm (4 min): your child draws a farm and says three sentences about it: "There's a tractor in the field. There are two cows."
  7. Watch (3 min): Walk Around the Farm, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Where do dolphins live?" … "They live in the sea." … "I'd like to be a horse because I'd like to run."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things on a farm and in the countryside: farmer, field, tractor, roof, cloud, kitten, puppy, river, lake, mountain, jungle.
  • Read a short farm story and write one missing word, and say which one is different and why: "The shark, because it lives in the sea."
  • Say where animals live and which animal they'd like to be: "Dolphins live in the sea." "I'd like to be a horse."
