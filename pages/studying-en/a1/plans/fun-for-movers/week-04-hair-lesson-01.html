Week 4 · Lesson 1: Hair Words

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 4: Your hair looks great!

Fun for Movers · Week 4: Your hair looks great! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Hair words Lesson 2 · Which one is Lily? Lesson 3 · Sally's family photo

You Need

  • Eight faces, drawn or cut from a magazine, with different hair: long straight fair, short curly brown, curly black, straight red, grey, one with a beard, one with a moustache, one with glasses.
  • Word cards: long, short, curly, straight, fair, brown, black, red, grey, beard, moustache, glasses. A mirror.
  • Before the lesson, make a 7 × 7 word search with: curly, long, short, beard, fair, glasses.
  • For light hair, the Movers list has both "blond(e)" and "fair". Describing words go before "hair": long curly brown hair.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Long Hair, Short Hair. Touch your hair when you hear "long" or "short".
  3. Hair words (6 min): show a face and say: "long curly hair". Your child finds the cards and puts them under the face, then says it. Hair words
  4. Mirror talk (4 min): look in the mirror together. Your child says: "I've got short straight black hair." Then your child describes you.
  5. Word search (5 min): your child finds and circles the six words, then reads each one aloud and points to a face with it.
  6. Watch (2 min): Describing People ~ Who is it? Say who it is before the answer.
  7. Listen and draw (4 min): say slowly: "a man with short curly red hair and a beard". Your child draws him. Then your child says one for you to draw.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "She's got long fair hair." … "He's got a beard." … "I've got short straight black hair."
