Week 4 · Lesson 2: Which One Is Lily?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 4: Your hair looks great!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three rows of three faces, A, B and C. Row 1, three girls: A long straight fair hair · B short curly fair hair · C short straight brown hair. Row 2, three men: A brown hair, glasses and a big beard · B a moustache · C grey hair and glasses. Row 3, three boys: A curly black hair · B straight red hair · C short fair hair and glasses.
  • Read each part of the script below twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.

1 Which girl is Lily? – Lily's got short hair. It isn't straight – it's curly. And it's fair.

2 Which man is Uncle Fred? – Uncle Fred has got glasses and a big beard. His hair is brown.

3 Which boy is Charlie? – Charlie's hair isn't curly and it isn't red. He's got glasses.

Answers: 1 B · 2 A · 3 C.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Hair review (3 min): show a face from Lesson 1; your child says the hair words: "long, straight, fair".
  3. Watch (6 min): Describing People's Looks. Say each word with the video.
  4. Listen and tick (6 min): read each part of the script twice; your child ticks A, B or C, then says why: "B, because her hair is curly."
  5. Who is it? (6 min): choose a secret face from the nine. Your child asks: "Has your person got glasses?" "No, he hasn't." "Has he got a beard?" Then swap. Sentences
  6. Funny faces (4 min): your child gives one face a new look: "Now he's got long green hair and a moustache!" Then says: "He looks funny!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which girl is Lily?" … "Has your person got glasses?" … "No, he hasn't."
