You Need

  • The text below, written large, and the six sentences to finish with one, two or three words.
  • A hat, a scarf and some glasses for dressing up, and a mirror.
  • The video says "What does he look like?" – a Flyers question. At Movers your child answers with "He's got…".

Sally's family photo

Look at this photo of my family. That's my grandma. She's got short grey hair and glasses. Next to her is my grandpa. He hasn't got any hair, but he's got a big white moustache!

My dad is very tall and he's got a short black beard. My mum's got long straight brown hair. And that's my little brother, Jim. He's got curly fair hair and a big smile.

And me? I'm the girl with the red hat. You can't see my hair!

1 Grandma's hair is short and ____. · 2 Grandpa has got a big ____. · 3 Dad is very ____. · 4 Mum's hair is long, straight and ____. · 5 Jim has got ____ hair. · 6 Sally is wearing a ____.

Answers: grey · white moustache · tall · brown · curly fair · red hat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): What Does He Look Like? Song. Sing the "He's got…" answers.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the text together. Your child writes one, two or three words for each sentence, then reads it aloud.
  4. Draw the photo (5 min): your child draws Sally's family from the text, then checks each person with you: "Grandma – short grey hair, glasses. Yes!"
  5. My family (5 min): your child describes people in the family (or a favourite cartoon family): "My mum's got long black hair." Then writes two sentences. Keep this page at home. Family words
  6. You look great! (4 min): dress up with the hat, scarf and glasses in front of the mirror: "You look great!" "You look funny!" Make a surprised face: "I look surprised!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Grandma's got short grey hair." … "Has Dad got a beard?" … "You look great!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Describe hair and faces: long / short, curly / straight, fair, brown, grey, a beard, a moustache, glasses.
  • Listen and find the right person, and play "Who is it?" with "Has he got…? – Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't."
  • Read a short description and write one to three words, and say "You look great!" "He looks surprised."
