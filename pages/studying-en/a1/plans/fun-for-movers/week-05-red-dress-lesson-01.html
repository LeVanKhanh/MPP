Week 5 · Lesson 1: What's in the Bag?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 5: The woman in the red dress

Fun for Movers · Week 5: The woman in the red dress | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

You Need

  • Real clothes in a big bag: a scarf, a sweater, a coat, a shirt, a pair of socks, a swimsuit, shorts, boots, a bike helmet, glasses.
  • A body outline drawn on a big sheet of paper, with head, neck, body, legs and feet.
  • We say "a pair of" for socks, shoes, boots, glasses, trousers, shorts and jeans. Many British people say "jumper", but the Movers word is "sweater".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Winter Fashion. Pretend to put on each thing.
  3. What's in the bag? (6 min): your child closes their eyes, puts a hand in the bag and guesses: "It's a scarf!" Then takes it out and checks. "A pair of socks!" Clothes words
  4. Head to feet (5 min): your child puts each thing on the body outline and says where it goes: "A helmet on the head. A scarf round the neck. Boots on the feet."
  5. Hot or cold? (4 min): "It's cold! What do you wear?" "A coat and a scarf!" "It's hot! What do you wear?" "Shorts and a T-shirt!"
  6. Spelling (4 min): your child writes six of the words from memory, then checks each one with the real thing: scarf, coat, shirt, socks, boots, helmet.
  7. Watch (2 min): Today I am Wearing. Sing it with your own clothes.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in the bag?" "It's a scarf!" … "It's cold. I'm wearing a coat and a scarf." … "a pair of socks"
