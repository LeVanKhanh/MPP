Week 5 · Lesson 2: Yes, I Can! No, I Can't!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 5: The woman in the red dress

Fun for Movers · Week 5: The woman in the red dress | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's in the bag? Lesson 2 · Yes, I can! No, I can't! Lesson 3 · People in the park

You Need

  • The six clothes riddles below, on cards.
  • Three teddies or dolls and some doll or baby clothes (or paper clothes to cut out), including a green scarf and a red dress.
  • Say "can" short in questions ("Can you…?") and "can't" long and clear: "No, I can't!"

1 You wear these on your legs when it's hot. They're short.

2 You wear this on your head when you ride a bike.

3 You wear this when it's cold. It's got long arms.

4 You wear these on your feet when there's lots of snow.

5 A girl or a woman can wear this to a party.

6 You wear this on your head when it's sunny.

Answers: shorts · a helmet · a sweater · boots · a dress · a hat (or a baseball cap).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Summer Fashion. Say which clothes you have got.
  3. Clothes riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it. Then your child makes one riddle for you.
  4. Dress the toys (7 min): your child dresses the three toys. Then you ask: "Which teddy is in the green scarf?" "That one!" "Who's the doll in the red dress?" Your child points and answers.
  5. Silly questions (5 min): ask funny questions: "Can you wear boots in the swimming pool?" "No, I can't!" "Can you wear a hat on your feet?" "No, I can't!" "Can you wear a scarf in the snow?" "Yes, I can!" Then your child asks you.
  6. Watch (2 min): Today I am Wearing.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which teddy is in the green scarf?" … "Can you wear boots in the swimming pool?" … "No, I can't!"
