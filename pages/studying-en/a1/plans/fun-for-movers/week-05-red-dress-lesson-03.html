Week 5 · Lesson 3: People in the Park

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Clothes and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 5: The woman in the red dress

Fun for Movers · Week 5: The woman in the red dress | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's in the bag? Lesson 2 · Yes, I can! No, I can't! Lesson 3 · People in the park

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a park with four people. Don't write their names: a girl in a green sweater flying a kite; a boy in a blue helmet on a bike; a woman in a red dress sitting on a seat; a man in a brown coat walking a dog.
  • Read the script below twice; your child draws a line from each name to the right person. Then the five questions (answers: one to five words).

Daisy's the girl in the green sweater. She's flying a kite. · Jim's on his bike. He's wearing a blue helmet. · Vicky is the woman in the red dress. She's sitting on the seat. · Paul's the man in the brown coat. He's walking his dog.

1 The girl in the green sweater is flying a ____. · 2 Jim has got a ____ on his head. · 3 What colour is Vicky's dress? · 4 What's Paul doing? · 5 Now write one sentence about the picture.

Answers: Daisy – green sweater, kite · Jim – blue helmet, bike · Vicky – red dress, seat · Paul – brown coat, dog. Questions: kite · (a) blue helmet · red · walking his dog / walking (with) a dog · any true sentence.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Seasons and Clothes Song. Say what you wear for each kind of weather.
  3. Listen and draw lines (6 min): read the script; your child draws a line from each name to the right person in your picture.
  4. Look, read and write (6 min): your child answers the five questions in one to five words, then reads the sentence they wrote.
  5. Who am I thinking of? (4 min): "I'm thinking of someone in a brown coat." "Paul!" Then use people at home: "I'm thinking of someone with a black sweater." Describing people
  6. My clothes (3 min): ask: "What are you wearing today? What do you wear when it's cold? What's your favourite sweater?"
  7. Watch (3 min): Clothes Vocabulary Chant, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Daisy's the girl in the green sweater." … "Who's the woman in the red dress?" … "What are you wearing today?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and spell more clothes: scarf, sweater, coat, shirt, socks, swimsuit, shorts, boots, helmet, glasses, and say "a pair of socks".
  • Guess clothes from a clue, and answer "Can you…?" clearly: "Yes, I can!" "No, I can't!"
  • Find people by their clothes – "the woman in the red dress" – and answer questions about a picture in one to five words.
