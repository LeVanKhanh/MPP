You Need

  • A big sheet of paper (or a few sheets taped together) for drawing round your child, and a pen.
  • Word cards: head, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, back, stomach, leg, foot, ear, eye, mouth, nose, teeth. A mirror.
  • The five riddles below, on cards. "Touch" is a Flyers word, so say "Point to…" or "Put your hands on…". One tooth, two teeth; one foot, two feet.

1 You have got two of these. Your arms start here.

2 It's behind you. You carry your bag on it.

3 When you're hungry, it says "Grrr!"

4 You can wear a scarf round it.

5 You clean them every morning and every night.

Answers: shoulders · back · stomach · neck · teeth.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): My Body Parts. Point to each part on your own body.
  3. Draw round me (6 min): your child lies on the paper and you draw round them. Your child puts each word card on the right part: "This is my neck. These are my shoulders." Body words
  4. Point to your… (4 min): "Point to your neck! Put your hands on your shoulders! Point to your back!" Faster and faster. Then your child is the teacher.
  5. Body riddles (5 min): your child reads each riddle, points to the part and writes the word.
  6. Watch (3 min): There's A Monster In My Tummy. Rub your stomach.
  7. How many? (3 min): in front of the mirror: "How many shoulders have you got?" "Two!" "How many teeth can you see?" Count them together.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Put your hands on your shoulders!" … "This is my neck." … "How many teeth can you see?"
