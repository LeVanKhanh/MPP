Week 6 · Lesson 2: Bigger and Smaller

You Need

  • A mirror, a piece of string, and toy animals or pictures of different sizes (an elephant, a mouse, a giraffe, a horse, a snake, a lizard).
  • Movers compares with -er + than: tall → taller, short → shorter, long → longer, old → older, young → younger, small → smaller, big → bigger (two g's).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): Comparatives – Monsters Learn English. Say "The ___ is bigger than the ___" with the monsters.
  3. Who's taller? (6 min): stand back to back in front of the mirror: "Who's taller?" "You're taller than me!" Measure hands and feet with the string: "My hand is bigger than your hand."
  4. Toy animals (5 min): your child puts two animals side by side and says: "The elephant is bigger than the mouse. The giraffe's neck is longer than the horse's neck."
  5. Yes or no? (5 min): say sentences about your family; your child says yes or no and makes the "no" ones right: "Grandpa is younger than me." "No! He's older than you!"
  6. Write (3 min): your child writes two sentences: "My dad is taller than me. My cat is smaller than my dog."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's taller?" … "You're taller than me!" … "The elephant is bigger than the mouse."
