Week 6 · Lesson 3: Fizz the Monster

You Need

  • Paper and coloured pencils for two monster drawings.
  • The text below for "Listen and draw", then the five questions (answers: one to three words).

Fizz

My monster is called Fizz. It's got two heads and a very long neck. It's got big shoulders and six arms, but only one leg! Its stomach is round and orange. It's got lots of teeth, but they're very small. Fizz lives on the moon.

1 How many heads has Fizz got? · 2 What's very long? · 3 How many arms has Fizz got? · 4 What colour is its stomach? · 5 Where does Fizz live?

Answers: two · its neck · six · orange · on the moon.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Body Parts Vocabulary. Say each word before the voice does.
  3. Listen and draw (7 min): read the Fizz text slowly, twice. Your child draws Fizz, then checks the drawing with you.
  4. Read and answer (5 min): your child reads the text and writes one, two or three words for each question.
  5. Our monsters (5 min): you and your child each draw a quick monster, then compare: "My monster's neck is longer than your monster's neck." "Your monster is taller!" Bigger and smaller
  6. Answer with your body (5 min): say a sentence. If it's right, your child puts their hands on their shoulders; if it's wrong, on their stomach. "Fizz has got one leg." "Elephants are smaller than mice." Then your child says some for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It's got a very long neck." … "My monster is taller than your monster." … "Hands on your shoulders!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the Movers body words neck, shoulder, back, stomach and tooth / teeth with the Starters ones, and count them.
  • Compare people and things with -er than: "You're taller than me." "The elephant is bigger than the mouse."
  • Listen and draw a monster, answer questions in one to three words, and play a yes/no game with their body.
