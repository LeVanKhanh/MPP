Week 7 · Lesson 1: What's the Weather Like?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 7: What's the weather like?

You Need

  • Weather picture cards (draw them): the sun, clouds, rain, wind, snow, a rainbow, the moon and stars. A word card for each.
  • A weather diary: a sheet with seven boxes, Monday to Sunday. Your child draws the weather in it every day this week.
  • Say "It's raining" and "It's snowing", not "It's rain". "Rainy" is not on the Cambridge lists; use "when it's raining".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Weather – Learn Weather Words. Do an action for each kind of weather.
  3. Weather cards (6 min): your child matches each picture with its word card and says it: "It's sunny. It's raining. It's windy." Weather words
  4. Weather actions (4 min): "It's windy!" (lean and wobble) "It's raining!" (hands over head) "It's cold!" (shiver) "It's hot!" (fan your face). Then your child says one for you.
  5. Weather diary (4 min): look out of the window: "What's the weather like today?" Your child draws it in today's box and writes one word: "sunny".
  6. Spelling (4 min): your child writes six words from memory, then checks them with the cards: sun, rain, snow, wind, cloud, moon.
  7. Watch (3 min): What's The Weather Like Today. Sing the question, then say the weather outside.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's the weather like today?" … "It's sunny and windy." … "It's raining!"
