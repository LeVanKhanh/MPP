Week 7 · Lesson 2: My Favourite Weather

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 7: What's the weather like?

Fun for Movers · Week 7: What's the weather like? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's the weather like? Lesson 2 · My favourite weather Lesson 3 · Ben's kite

You Need

  • The six weather riddles below, on cards.
  • The listening text below and a chart with three columns: Who? · What weather? · Why?

1 It's big, yellow and hot. You can see it in the day. · 2 It's got lots of colours. You can see it in the sky after the rain. · 3 It's white and cold. You can play in it in the garden. · 4 You can't see it, but it moves the trees and your kite. · 5 You wear this when it's cold. It's got long arms. · 6 It's in the sky at night. Sometimes it's round and sometimes it isn't.

Paul likes windy weather because he can fly his kite in the park. Mary likes sunny weather because she can swim in the sea. Uncle Jim likes it when it's raining because he can read his books at home.

Answers: the sun · a rainbow · snow · the wind · a sweater · the moon. Chart: Paul – windy – fly his kite · Mary – sunny – swim in the sea · Uncle Jim – raining – read his books.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The weather for kids. Say the weather word before the voice does.
  3. Weather riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it.
  4. Listen and write (6 min): read the text twice, slowly. Your child fills in the chart: who, what weather, and why.
  5. My favourite weather (5 min): ask: "Do you like windy weather? What's your favourite weather? Why?" Your child says and writes: "I like ____ weather because I can ____." Sentences
  6. Weather diary (2 min): your child draws today's weather in the diary and says it.
  7. Watch (3 min): How's The Weather? It means the same as "What's the weather like?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Do you like windy weather?" … "Yes, I do! I can fly my kite." … "It's in the sky at night. What is it?"
