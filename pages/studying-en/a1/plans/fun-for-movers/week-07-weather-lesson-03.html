Week 7 · Lesson 3: Ben's Kite: Tell the Story

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 7: What's the weather like?

Fun for Movers · Week 7: What's the weather like? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's the weather like? Lesson 2 · My favourite weather Lesson 3 · Ben's kite

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a four-picture story. 1 A sunny, windy day: Ben and his dad go to the park with a new kite. 2 The wind takes the kite up into a tree. Ben is sad. 3 Clouds and rain: Ben is wet and cold. 4 The sun comes out and there's a rainbow. Dad climbs the tree and gets the kite. Ben is happy.
  • In the Movers speaking test, the examiner tells the first picture and your child tells the rest. Short sentences are fine.
  • The four sentences below, for matching to the pictures.

Picture 1 (you say): It's a sunny, windy day. Ben has got a new kite. He's in the park with his dad.

a "Oh no! My kite is in the tree!" · b "I'm wet and I'm cold." · c "Let's go to the park. It's windy today!" · d "Well done, Dad! Thank you!"

Answers: a 2 · b 3 · c 1 · d 4.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): What's The Weather Like? Song. Do the actions.
  3. Look at the pictures (4 min): look at the four pictures together. Your child says the weather in each: "Sunny and windy… raining… sunny, and a rainbow!"
  4. Tell the story (8 min): you tell picture 1. For pictures 2, 3 and 4 ask: "Where's the kite now? What's the weather like? Is Ben happy or sad? Who's saying 'Well done'?" Your child tells each picture. Then your child tells the whole story.
  5. Match (5 min): your child reads sentences a–d and writes the picture number next to each.
  6. Weather diary (2 min): your child draws today's weather, then counts: "How many sunny days?"
  7. Watch (3 min): What's The Weather Like Today, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's the weather like in picture 3?" … "It's raining and Ben is wet." … "Well done, Dad!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the weather and the sky: sun, cloud, rain, snow, wind, rainbow, moon, stars; sunny, cloudy, windy, hot, cold, wet.
  • Ask and answer "What's the weather like?" and say "I like windy weather because I can fly my kite."
  • Tell a four-picture story: where the person is, what the weather is like, and if they are happy or sad.
