Week 8 · Lesson 1: Yesterday and Today

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 8: The hottest and coldest places

You Need

  • The weather diary from Week 7 (or start one now and use last week's memory), and the weather cards.
  • A simple map drawn on paper: mountains, the sea, a town and a park.
  • Past forms for weather: it is → it was; it rains → it rained; it snows → it snowed. "Yesterday" is a Movers word.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): I Was, You Were. Then say: "Yesterday it was ___."
  3. Today and yesterday (6 min): look at the weather diary: "What's the weather like today?" "It's sunny." "What was the weather like yesterday?" "It was cloudy." "It rained on Tuesday!" Sentences
  4. Weather on TV (6 min): your child is the TV weather person. Point at the map and say yesterday's and today's weather: "Yesterday it was cold and it snowed in the mountains. Today it's sunny in the park!" Then swap.
  5. Fix it! (5 min): say wrong sentences; your child makes them right: "Yesterday it is sunny." "No! Yesterday it was sunny!" "Yesterday it rains." "Yesterday it rained!"
  6. My week (4 min): your child writes three sentences from the diary: "On Monday it was sunny. On Wednesday it rained."
  7. Watch (2 min): How was it? (Past tense). Answer about your own yesterday.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What was the weather like yesterday?" … "It was cloudy and it rained." … "Today it's sunny."
