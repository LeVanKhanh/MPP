Week 8 · Lesson 2: The Biggest and the Coldest

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Opposites and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 8: The hottest and coldest places

Fun for Movers · Week 8: The hottest and coldest places | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Yesterday and today Lesson 2 · The biggest and the coldest Lesson 3 · Penguins in the snow

You Need

  • Three toy animals of different sizes (for example a bear, an elephant, a whale), and a globe or a world map if you have one.
  • Five place pictures: a very cold place with ice and penguins, a very hot place, a very wet place, a very windy place, a sunny beach.
  • Movers uses -est for "the most": cold → the coldest, hot → the hottest (two t's), wet → the wettest, dry → the driest, windy → the windiest, sunny → the sunniest, big → the biggest, tall → the tallest. "Desert" is a Flyers word.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Big Bigger Biggest. Show the size with your arms.
  3. Three in a row (5 min): put the three toys in a row: "The bear is big. The elephant is bigger. The whale is the biggest!" Then try three people: "Dad is the tallest."
  4. Places in the world (5 min): show each place picture (and find it on the globe): "This is Antarctica. It's the coldest place in the world." Your child says one for each picture: the hottest, the wettest, the windiest, the sunniest.
  5. Watch (4 min): A Walk in the Arctic. Say the animals you see in the coldest place.
  6. Family records (4 min): ask: "Who's the tallest in our family? Who's the youngest? Who's the oldest?" Your child writes three sentences.
  7. Quick quiz (4 min): "Which is the biggest: a cat, a horse or a mouse?" "Which is the longest: a snake, a lizard or a frog?" "Which is the coldest: ice, water or tea?" "Which is the smallest: a fly, a bird or a dog?" Then your child asks you one.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The elephant is bigger. The whale is the biggest!" … "Antarctica is the coldest place in the world." … "Who's the tallest in our family?"
