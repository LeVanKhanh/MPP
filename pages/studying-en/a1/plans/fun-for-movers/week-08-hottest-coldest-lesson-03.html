You Need

  • The text below, written large, and the five questions (answers: one to three words).
  • A soft ball for the penguin walk.

Penguins

Penguins are birds, but they can't fly. They're very good at swimming. Some penguins live in the coldest place in the world. They walk on the ice and they eat fish.

The dad penguin carries the egg on his feet, and the mum goes to the sea to find fish. When the baby penguin comes out of the egg, the mum and dad give it food.

1 Penguins are birds, but they can't ____. · 2 They're very good at ____. · 3 What do penguins eat? · 4 Who carries the egg? · 5 Where does the mum go?

Answers: fly · swimming · fish · the dad (penguin) · to the sea.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and answer (7 min): read the text together. Your child writes one, two or three words for each question.
  3. Penguin walk (4 min): your child is the dad penguin: walk across the room with the ball on top of their feet without dropping the "egg". Then you try!
  4. Animal records (5 min): ask: "Which is the tallest animal? The biggest? The strongest? The slowest? The cleverest?" Your child answers and writes each animal. For "the cleverest" any answer is fine – ask "Why?" Animal words
  5. Watch (5 min): Animal Superlatives Quiz. Shout your answer before the video shows it.
  6. Silly records (4 min): your child writes two funny sentences: "My teddy is the cleverest teddy in the world!" "Our cat is the strongest animal in our street!" Then reads them to you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Penguins are birds, but they can't fly." … "Which is the tallest animal?" … "The giraffe is the tallest!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Talk about yesterday's weather: "It was windy yesterday." "It rained on Monday."
  • Compare three things with -est: "The whale is the biggest." "Antarctica is the coldest place in the world."
  • Read a short animal text and answer in one to three words, and say which animals are the tallest, strongest and cleverest.
