Week 9 · Lesson 1: Our Family Tree

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 9: Me and my family

Fun for Movers · Week 9: Me and my family | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our family tree Lesson 2 · Who's the loudest? Lesson 3 · A day at Grandma's

You Need

  • Family photos or quick drawings, a big sheet of paper and glue or tape.
  • The example family tree below, drawn on paper (only names, no faces needed).
  • Word cards in pairs: aunt / uncle, son / daughter, grandson / granddaughter, mum / dad, brother / sister, grandma / grandpa. "Wife" and "husband" are Flyers words. Your child's real family tree has real names: keep it at home.

The Hill family

Grandpa Fred and Grandma Mary Hill have got two children: Paul and Sally.

Paul and Lily have got two children: Daisy (10) and Charlie (7).

Aunt Sally and Uncle Jim have got two children too: Zoe (12) and Jack (5).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Family Tree. Point to each person on the example tree.
  3. Our tree (7 min): make your child's family tree with photos or drawings and names. Your child says: "This is my aunt. She's my mum's sister." Family words
  4. Who is it? (5 min): ask about the Hill family: "Who's Daisy's uncle?" "Jim!" "Who are Fred's granddaughters?" "Daisy and Zoe!" "Is Jack Paul's son?" "No! He's Sally's son." Then your child asks you.
  5. Family pairs (5 min): turn the word cards face down and play memory: find a pair (aunt + uncle, son + daughter…) and say a sentence: "Charlie is Paul's son. Daisy is Paul's daughter."
  6. Watch (3 min): Extended Finger Family. Name a real aunt or uncle for each finger.
  7. Write (2 min): your child writes two sentences about the Hill family: "Zoe is Fred's granddaughter. Jim is Charlie's uncle."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is my aunt. She's my mum's sister." … "Who's Daisy's uncle?" … "Charlie is Fred's grandson."
