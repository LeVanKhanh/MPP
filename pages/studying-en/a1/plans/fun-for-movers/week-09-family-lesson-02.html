Week 9 · Lesson 2: Who's the Loudest?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Family and Opposites. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 9: Me and my family

Fun for Movers · Week 9: Me and my family | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our family tree Lesson 2 · Who's the loudest? Lesson 3 · A day at Grandma's

You Need

  • Family photos and the family tree from Lesson 1.
  • Eight question cards: Who's the oldest? Who's the youngest? Who's the tallest? Who's the loudest? Who's the quietest? Who's the busiest? Who's the naughtiest? Who's the funniest?
  • New Movers words: loud, quiet, clever, busy, naughty. busy → the busiest, funny → the funniest (y → iest).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Learn Family Members. Say whose sister or brother each one is.
  3. Show me! (5 min): "Show me loud!" (shout) "Show me quiet!" (whisper) "Show me busy!" (run around) "Show me naughty!" "Show me silly!" Then your child says and you show.
  4. Family interview (7 min): your child asks you the eight questions and writes your answers. Then you ask your child. Compare: "My answer: Dad. Your answer: Grandma!"
  5. Our family book (6 min): your child draws two people from the family and writes a sentence for each: "My brother is the silliest!" "Grandma is the cleverest!" Sentences
  6. Watch (3 min): On The Weekend. Then tell one thing you did last weekend.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's the oldest in your family?" … "Grandpa is the oldest." … "My brother is the silliest!"
