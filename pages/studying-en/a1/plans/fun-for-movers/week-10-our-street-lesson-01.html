Week 10 · Lesson 1: Our Street

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 10: People in our street

Fun for Movers · Week 10: People in our street | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our street Lesson 2 · What's he like? Lesson 3 · Three friends

You Need

  • A big sheet of paper and pens for a street map, and some small toys or paper people.
  • Word cards: road, house, flat, balcony, roof, garden, bus stop, car park, café, shop, park.
  • The street can be your real street or a made-up one. If your child's work is shared, use a made-up street name and house numbers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Around My Town. Say which places are in your street.
  3. Draw our street (7 min): draw the street together: houses with numbers, flats with balconies, a bus stop, a café, a shop, a car park, a park. Your child puts the word cards on the map. Street words
  4. Where is it? (5 min): "Where's the café?" "It's next to the bus stop." "It's opposite the park." Then your child asks you.
  5. Who lives here? (5 min): put the toy people in the houses: "Mr Brown lives at number 5. He's got a dog." Your child asks: "Who lives at number 8?" Sentences
  6. Write (3 min): your child writes three sentences about the map: "There's a café in our street. Daisy lives in a flat with a balcony."
  7. Watch (2 min): Where Are You Going?. Point to the places on your map.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who lives at number 5?" … "Mr Brown lives there." … "The café is opposite the park."
