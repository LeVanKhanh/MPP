Week 10 · Lesson 2: What's He Like?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: My Street and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 10: People in our street

Fun for Movers · Week 10: People in our street | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our street Lesson 2 · What's he like? Lesson 3 · Three friends

You Need

  • The dialogue below on paper. Your child chooses A, B or C for each answer.
  • "What's he like?" asks about the person (nice, funny, kind). "What does he like?" asks about things he likes (ice cream, football). Movers tests often mix these up on purpose. "Neighbour" is not on the Cambridge lists, so the lessons say "lives next to us".

The man at number 9 – Daisy and Jack

1 Jack: Who's that man at number 9? – Daisy: A He lives next to us. B It's a big house. C Yes, he can.

2 Jack: What's he like? – Daisy: A He likes ice cream. B He's really funny! C He's got a car.

3 Jack: Has he got any children? – Daisy: A Yes, two girls. B No, he isn't. C They're at school.

4 Jack: What does he do? – Daisy: A He's a doctor. B He's doing it now. C It's at the hospital.

5 Jack: Shall we say hello? – Daisy: A Yes, he does. B Yes, let's go! C I'm fine, thanks.

6 Jack: Can he come to our party on Saturday? – Daisy: A I don't know. Let's ask him! B He's at number 9. C No, it's Friday.

Answers: 1 A · 2 B · 3 A · 4 A · 5 B · 6 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Personality Song For Kids. Say one word for a person you know.
  3. Like or likes? (5 min): ask: "What's Grandma like?" "She's kind." Then: "What does Grandma like?" "She likes flowers." Mix them up and see if your child hears the difference.
  4. Choose the answer (7 min): your child reads the dialogue and circles A, B or C. Then you read it together with two voices: you are Jack, your child is Daisy.
  5. Knock, knock! (7 min): you are a new person in the street, knocking on the door. Your child asks: "What's your name? Where do you live? Have you got any children? Shall I show you the park?" Then swap.
  6. Watch (3 min): People in Your Neighborhood. Say "He's a…" for each person.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's he like?" … "He's really funny!" … "Shall we say hello?" – "Yes, let's go!"
