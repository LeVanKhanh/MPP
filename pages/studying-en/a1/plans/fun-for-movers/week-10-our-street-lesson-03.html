Week 10 · Lesson 3: Three Friends in Green Street

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: My Street and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 10: People in our street

Fun for Movers · Week 10: People in our street | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our street Lesson 2 · What's he like? Lesson 3 · Three friends

You Need

  • The street map from Lesson 1 and coloured pencils.
  • Before the lesson, draw three rows of three small pictures, A, B and C: 1 a café window with cakes · ice creams · sandwiches; 2 a bus · a lorry · a motorbike; 3 a cat on a roof · under a car · in a tree.
  • In the last step, your child talks about their street. No house number or street name is needed.

Listen and draw: Vicky, Paul and Zoe all live in Green Street. Vicky lives at number 3. She's got long curly red hair and green eyes. Paul lives next to her, at number 5. He's taller than Vicky and his hair is short and black. Zoe lives in the flat above the café. She's the youngest. Her hair is longer than Paul's, but shorter than Vicky's, and it's fair.

Listen and tick: 1 What's in the café window today? – There aren't any cakes today, but there are lots of sandwiches. · 2 What does Paul's dad drive? – He doesn't drive a lorry. He drives a big bus! · 3 Where's the cat? – It isn't in the tree now. Look! It's on the roof!

Answers: Listen and tick: 1 C (sandwiches) · 2 A (a bus) · 3 A (on the roof).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where Are You Going? Places Song. Answer with a place.
  3. Listen and draw (8 min): read the first text slowly, twice. Your child draws Vicky, Paul and Zoe on the street map in the right places, then checks: "Paul is taller than Vicky. Yes!"
  4. Listen and tick (6 min): read each part of the second text twice; your child ticks A, B or C.
  5. About my street (6 min): ask: "Is your street big or small? What can you see in your street? Who lives next to you? What's he or she like?" Short answers are fine; a full sentence is better.
  6. Watch (2 min): Where Are You Going?, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Paul is taller than Vicky." … "Where's the cat?" – "It's on the roof!" … "What can you see in your street?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name places in a street – road, flat, balcony, bus stop, car park, café, market – and say where they are: "The café is opposite the park."
  • Ask "What's he like?" and answer "He's really nice" (not "He likes…"), and choose the best answer in a short dialogue.
  • Listen and draw people from a description, and talk about their own street without giving the address.
