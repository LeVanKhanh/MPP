Week 11 · Lesson 1: Food Groups

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 11: Things we eat and drink

Fun for Movers · Week 11: Things we eat and drink | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Food groups Lesson 2 · At the table Lesson 3 · Rice

You Need

  • Real food from the kitchen (or pictures): some fruit, some vegetables, meat or fish, drinks, and other food like rice, pasta, noodles, cheese and bread.
  • Five plates or boxes with labels: fruit · vegetables · meat and fish · drinks · other food.
  • New Movers words: soup, salad, cheese, pasta, noodles, sauce, pancake, coffee, tea, milkshake.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Foods Vocabulary Chant. Say each food in the gap.
  3. Kitchen hunt (7 min): go to the kitchen together. Your child finds ten things to eat or drink and names each one: "Cheese! Noodles! Tea!" Food words
  4. Sort it (6 min): your child puts each thing on the right plate and says why: "Cheese isn't a vegetable. It's other food." "A lemon is fruit."
  5. Which one is different? (5 min): say four foods; your child says which one is different and why. 1 soup, tea, coffee, ice cream · 2 banana, lemon, carrot, mango · 3 pasta, noodles, rice, milk · 4 sausage, burger, meatballs, apple.
  6. Watch (3 min): Pancake Song. Mix, cook and flip your pancake with the song.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is cheese a vegetable?" … "No, it isn't!" … "Ice cream is different. It's cold."
