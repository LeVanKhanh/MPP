Week 11 · Lesson 2: At the Table

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 11: Things we eat and drink

Fun for Movers · Week 11: Things we eat and drink | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Food groups Lesson 2 · At the table Lesson 3 · Rice

You Need

  • The six food riddles below, on cards.
  • A real snack or meal time with a few foods and drinks, plates, cups and glasses.

1 It's white or yellow. Mice love it! · 2 They're long and thin. People in China and Vietnam love eating them. · 3 It's green and cold. You make it with vegetables. · 4 It's red and you put it on pasta or on chips. · 5 Grandma and Grandpa drink it hot in a cup. It's brown or green. · 6 It's hot. You can make it with chicken or vegetables and lots of water.

Answers: cheese · noodles · salad · (tomato) sauce · tea · soup.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): At the table – Do you want some more? Answer "Yes, please" or "No, thank you".
  3. Food riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it.
  4. Make a riddle (4 min): your child chooses a food and makes a riddle for you: "It's round and you can put lemon on it." "A pancake!" Sentences
  5. At the table (7 min): have a real snack together. Use only English: "Would you like some more pasta?" "Yes, please!" "Can I have some water, please?" "Here you are." "Would you like a cup of tea?" "No, thank you."
  6. My meals (3 min): ask: "What do you eat for breakfast? For lunch? For dinner?" Your child draws one plate and writes the food on it.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Would you like some more pasta?" … "Yes, please!" … "It's white or yellow. Mice love it!"
