You Need

  • The text below, written large, and the five sentences to finish with one word.
  • Paper for a food survey with two columns: "Who?" and "Favourite food".

Rice

Rice grows in wet fields. Farmers put the little rice plants in the water, and then the plants grow tall and green. At the end, the plants are yellow.

Lots of people in Asia eat rice every day – for breakfast, lunch and dinner! You can make lots of things with rice: soup, cake, noodles and drinks.

1 Rice grows in ____ fields. · 2 Farmers put the little plants in the ____. · 3 The plants grow tall and ____. · 4 At the end, the plants are ____. · 5 You can make ____, cake, noodles and drinks with rice.

Answers: wet · water · green · yellow · soup.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Grow Your Own Potatoes! Say "leaves", "plant" and "potato" when you see them.
  3. Read and write (7 min): read the text together. Your child writes one word for each sentence.
  4. Words in words (5 min): write some long food words; your child finds smaller words inside: pancake (cake) · sandwich (sand) · milkshake (milk) · watermelon (water) · pineapple (apple) · meatballs (meat, ball).
  5. Food survey (5 min): your child asks two people at home: "What's your favourite food? What do you drink in the morning?" and writes the answers in the survey.
  6. Picnic list (3 min): your child writes a list of six foods and drinks for a family picnic, then reads it to you: "Sandwiches, cheese, apples…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Rice grows in wet fields." … "What's your favourite food?" … "I can see 'cake' in 'pancake'!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and sort more food and drinks: soup, salad, cheese, pasta, noodles, sauce, pancake, coffee, tea, milkshake.
  • Guess food from a clue, and offer and ask politely: "Would you like some more?" "Yes, please." "Can I have some water, please?"
  • Read a short text about food and write one word, and find small words inside bigger words.
