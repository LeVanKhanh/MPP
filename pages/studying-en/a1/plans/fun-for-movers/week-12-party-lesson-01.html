Week 12 · Lesson 1: A Bottle of Lemonade

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out, Food and Drinks and Shapes. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 12: Party things

Fun for Movers · Week 12: Party things | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · A bottle of lemonade Lesson 2 · Party plans Lesson 3 · Grandpa's birthday

You Need

  • Real containers: a box, a bottle, a bowl, a cup, a glass, and a few things to put in them (water, rice, sweets, small toys).
  • A pretend shop on a table: some bottles, boxes and bowls on top of a box and some on the floor, and a short shopping list.
  • "Round" and "square" are Movers words. "Shelf" is a Flyers word, so in the shop say "on the top" or "on the floor".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Birthday Party Words. Point to each party thing you see.
  3. What's inside? (7 min): put something in each container. Your child looks or shakes and guesses: "What's in the bottle?" "Water!" Then writes: a bottle of water, a box of sweets, a bowl of rice, a cup of tea, a glass of milk. On the table
  4. Round or square? (5 min): hunt for round and square things at home: "The plate is round. The box is square." Shape words
  5. Shop game (7 min): your child is the shopper with the list: "Can I have two bottles of lemonade, please?" You ask: "Where are the bowls?" "On the floor, next to the boxes!" Then swap.
  6. Watch (2 min): Let's Go Shopping Song. Say what you would buy.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in the bottle?" … "A bottle of lemonade!" … "The plate is round. The box is square."
