Week 12 · Lesson 2: Party Plans

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 12: Party things

Fun for Movers · Week 12: Party things | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · A bottle of lemonade Lesson 2 · Party plans Lesson 3 · Grandpa's birthday

You Need

  • The listening text below and Daisy's list with five gaps.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for an invitation card.
  • "Must" has no "to" after it: "I must buy…", not "I must to buy…".

Listen: It's Mum's birthday on Saturday and I'm making a party for her! I must make a chocolate cake. I must invite six friends. I must put balloons on the door. I must clean my bedroom. And I must buy some ice cream – Mum loves it! – Daisy

Daisy's list: 1 make a ____ cake · 2 invite ____ friends · 3 put balloons on the ____ · 4 clean my ____ · 5 buy some ____

Answers: chocolate · six · door · bedroom · ice cream.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Happy Birthday – This is for you. Give a pretend present and say it.
  3. Listen and write (7 min): read Daisy's text twice, slowly. Your child writes the missing word in each line of her list.
  4. My party list (5 min): your child plans a party for a toy or a family member and writes four sentences: "I must make a cake. I must buy some lemonade…" Sentences
  5. Invitation (5 min): your child makes an invitation card and reads it to you: "Would you like to come to my party on Saturday?" You answer: "Yes, please! Thank you!"
  6. Happy birthday! (3 min): give each other a pretend present: "Happy birthday! This is for you." "Thank you! What a great present!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I must make a chocolate cake." … "Would you like to come to my party?" … "Happy birthday! This is for you."
