Week 12 · Lesson 3: Grandpa's Birthday

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Shapes. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 12: Party things

Fun for Movers · Week 12: Party things | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · A bottle of lemonade Lesson 2 · Party plans Lesson 3 · Grandpa's birthday

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a four-picture story. 1 Mum and Lily make a cake and put balloons in the living room. Grandpa is in the garden. 2 Friends and family come in quietly. 3 Grandpa comes in and everyone shouts "Happy birthday!" Grandpa looks very surprised. 4 Everyone dances and eats cake. Grandpa opens his present: a new hat.
  • The drawing instructions below, and paper and coloured pencils.

Picture 1 (you say): It's Grandpa's birthday. Mum and Lily are making a big cake. Grandpa is in the garden.

Read and draw: Draw a long table. · Put three round plates on the table. · Next to the plates, draw a square box with a present inside. · Draw a bottle of orange juice next to the box. · Draw a big cake next to the bottle. · Put five balloons above the table and colour them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Dance Party! Copy every move.
  3. Tell the story (8 min): you tell picture 1. For pictures 2, 3 and 4 ask: "Who's coming in? What are they saying? Is Grandpa happy? What's his present?" Your child tells each picture, then the whole story.
  4. Read and draw (7 min): your child reads each instruction and draws it, then tells you: "There are three round plates and a square box."
  5. Party talk (4 min): ask: "What do you like doing at parties? What food do you like at a party? What's the best present for Grandpa?"
  6. Watch (2 min): Let's Go Shopping Song, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It's Grandpa's birthday." … "Happy birthday, Grandpa!" … "There are three round plates on the table."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name containers and shapes: a bottle of lemonade, a bowl of rice, a glass of milk, a box of sweets; round, square.
  • Say what they must do for a party ("I must make a cake") and invite a friend ("Would you like to come to my party?").
  • Tell a four-picture party story and draw a party table from written instructions.
