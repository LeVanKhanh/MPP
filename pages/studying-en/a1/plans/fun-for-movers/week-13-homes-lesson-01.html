Week 13 · Lesson 1: Parts of a House

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 13: Different homes

Fun for Movers · Week 13: Different homes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Parts of a house Lesson 2 · Upstairs, downstairs Lesson 3 · Find the differences

You Need

  • Sticky notes with the words: door, window, wall, floor, mat, roof, balcony, basement, upstairs, downstairs, lift, grass, tree.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a big house drawing.
  • "Stairs" and "chimney" are in the book but not on the Cambridge lists; "upstairs" and "downstairs" are Movers words.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Parts of the House. Point to each part of your own home.
  3. Sticky note tour (7 min): walk round your home together. Your child puts each sticky note on the right thing and says it: "This is the floor. That's a window." Words for things you haven't got (a basement, a lift) go on the drawing instead. House words
  4. Draw and label (6 min): your child draws a big house with a basement, two floors, a balcony and a roof, then labels each part.
  5. Watch (3 min): All About Houses. Which home would you like to live in?
  6. My home (5 min): ask: "Do you live in a house or a flat? Has it got a balcony? Is there a lift? What can you see from your window?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is the roof." … "Has it got a balcony?" … "I live in a flat. There's a lift!"
