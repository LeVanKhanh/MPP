Week 13 · Lesson 2: Upstairs, Downstairs

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 13: Different homes

Fun for Movers · Week 13: Different homes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Parts of a house Lesson 2 · Upstairs, downstairs Lesson 3 · Find the differences

You Need

  • The house drawing from Lesson 1, and a small toy for hiding.
  • The dialogue below on paper. Your child chooses A, B or C for each answer.

Aunt Mary's new flat – Lily and Fred

1 Lily: I went to my aunt's new flat yesterday! – Fred: A Yes, she can. B What was it like? C I'm fine.

2 Lily: It's on the top floor, and there's a lift! – Fred: A Here you are. B It's not mine. C Wow! That's great!

3 Lily: There's a big balcony too. You can see the sea! – Fred: A Really? Fantastic! B Sorry, I can't. C Shall we?

4 Fred: Has she got any pets? – Lily: A She's in the kitchen. B Yes, a grey cat. C No, she isn't.

5 Lily: Can you come with me on Saturday? – Fred: A Me too! B Well done! C Yes, please! I'd love to.

6 Fred: Is the flat old? – Lily: A No, it's new. B It's my aunt's. C Yes, I am.

Answers: 1 B · 2 C · 3 A · 4 B · 5 C · 6 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Upstairs, Downstairs. Say "upstairs!" or "downstairs!" each time.
  3. Upstairs or downstairs? (5 min): on the house drawing, your child puts the rooms: "The bedrooms are upstairs. The kitchen is downstairs. The car is in the basement." Sentences
  4. Choose the answer (7 min): your child reads the dialogue and circles A, B or C. Then you read it together with two voices.
  5. Hide the toy (5 min): hide the toy; give clues: "It's downstairs, next to the door. It's on the floor." Your child finds it. Then swap.
  6. Write (3 min): your child writes two sentences about the drawing: "My bedroom is upstairs. There's a big balcony."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where's your bedroom?" … "It's upstairs, next to the bathroom." … "Really? Fantastic!"
