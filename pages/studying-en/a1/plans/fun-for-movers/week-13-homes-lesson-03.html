Week 13 · Lesson 3: Find the Differences

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 13: Different homes

Fun for Movers · Week 13: Different homes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Parts of a house Lesson 2 · Upstairs, downstairs Lesson 3 · Find the differences

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two houses that are almost the same, A and B, with six differences. A: three windows, a cat on the roof, a red door, two trees, a man on the balcony, the moon. B: four windows, a bird on the roof, a green door, one tree, a woman on the balcony, the sun.
  • In Movers speaking Part 1, the examiner shows two pictures and says one difference; your child says the others. "But" is the key word.

Answers: windows 3/4 · cat/bird on the roof · door red/green · trees 2/1 · man/woman on the balcony · moon/sun.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Movers Speaking: Find the Differences. See how the test works.
  3. Find the differences (8 min): you say the first one: "In picture A there are three windows, but in picture B there are four." Your child finds and says the other five. Sentences
  4. What's different now? (5 min): your child closes their eyes; you change three things in the room (open a door, move a cup, put a hat on a chair). Your child says: "The door was closed, but now it's open!" Then swap.
  5. My dream home (8 min): your child draws a dream home and writes three or four sentences: "My dream home has got a big balcony and a lift. My bedroom is upstairs. There's a swimming pool in the basement!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "In picture A the door is red, but in picture B it's green." … "The door was closed, but now it's open!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name parts of a house: wall, floor, roof, door, window, balcony, basement, upstairs, downstairs, lift.
  • Choose the best answer in a short dialogue ("Really? Fantastic!") and say where things are: "It's upstairs, next to the bathroom."
  • Find and say differences between two pictures: "In picture A the door is red, but in picture B it's green."
