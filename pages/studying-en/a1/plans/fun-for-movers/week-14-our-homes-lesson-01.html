Week 14 · Lesson 1: Kitchen and Chicken

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 14: Our homes

Fun for Movers · Week 14: Our homes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Kitchen and chicken Lesson 2 · Welcome to my home Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • The five room riddles below, on cards.
  • Two big cards: "ch" (as in chicken) and "k" (as in cake).

1 You cook here. · 2 You sleep here. · 3 You wash here. · 4 You watch TV and sit on the sofa here. · 5 You eat your dinner here, at a big table.

Say it fast: The children are in the kitchen, eating cheese, chips and chicken!

Answers: kitchen · bedroom · bathroom · living room · dining room (or kitchen).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): My House. Point to the same rooms in your home.
  3. Room riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the room and writes it. Room words
  4. Watch (3 min): The CH Sound. Say "ch" with the video.
  5. Ch or k? (6 min): say each word; your child holds up the "ch" card or the "k" card: children, cake, cheese, cook, chicken, cup, chips, kite, chocolate, kitchen (it has both!).
  6. Say it fast (3 min): say the sentence together, slowly first, then faster and faster. Who can say it three times?
  7. How many? (3 min): "How many rooms are there in our home? How many windows? How many doors?" Count them together.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You cook here. It's the kitchen!" … "Chicken – ch!" … "How many rooms are there?"
