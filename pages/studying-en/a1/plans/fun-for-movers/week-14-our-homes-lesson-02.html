Week 14 · Lesson 2: Welcome to My Home

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 14: Our homes

Fun for Movers · Week 14: Our homes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Kitchen and chicken Lesson 2 · Welcome to my home Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • Zoe's text and the word box below, written large.
  • The listening text and the form with five lines.

Read – word box: city · floor · lift · rooms · favourite · near

Hi! My name's Zoe. I live in a flat in a big (1) ____. My flat is on the third (2) ____, so we always take the (3) ____. It's got four (4) ____: a kitchen, a living room, a bathroom and my bedroom. My (5) ____ room is the kitchen because I like cooking with my dad. There's a park (6) ____ our flat. You can play football there!

Listen: This is my grandpa's new house. He lives there with Grandma and their dog, Rex. The house has got two floors. In the garden, there are lots of flowers and a big apple tree. From the bedroom window, you can see the mountains.

Form: 1 Who lives there? Grandpa, Grandma and ____ · 2 The dog's name: ____ · 3 Number of floors: ____ · 4 In the garden: flowers and ____ · 5 From the window you can see: ____

Answers: Read: city · floor · lift · rooms · favourite · near. Listen: the dog · Rex · two · an apple tree · the mountains.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Welcome to my house. Say the sentences with the video.
  3. Read and complete (7 min): your child reads Zoe's text and writes a word from the box in each gap, then reads it aloud.
  4. Listen and write (7 min): read the listening text twice, slowly. Your child fills in the form.
  5. Home tour (6 min): your child shows you round your home in English: "This is the kitchen. We cook here. This is my bedroom. I sleep here." Sentences
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I live in a flat in a big city." … "This is the kitchen. We cook here." … "My favourite room is my bedroom."
