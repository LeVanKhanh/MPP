Week 14 · Lesson 3: My Home

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 14: Our homes

Fun for Movers · Week 14: Our homes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Kitchen and chicken Lesson 2 · Welcome to my home Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • The writing frame and the listen-and-draw text below. Paper and coloured pencils.
  • Your child's writing asks for an address. If it will be shared outside the family, use a made-up street and number (for example "12 Sunny Road").

Write about your home: I live in a ____ (flat / house). I live in a ____ (village / town / city). My address is ____. Our home has got ____ rooms. My favourite room is ____ because ____. There's a ____ near my home. You can ____ there.

Listen and draw: It's a small house with a red roof. It's got two floors and a green door. There's a cat on the roof and a big tree next to the house. A man with a beard is in the garden. He's reading a book.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where do you live? Say "I live in a…"
  3. Village, town or city? (5 min): ask: "Is a village big or small? Are there lots of people in a city? Where would you like to live? Why?" Where I live
  4. Write about my home (8 min): your child fills in the writing frame and reads it to you.
  5. Listen and draw (6 min): read the house text slowly, twice. Your child draws it, then checks each line with you.
  6. Watch (3 min): My House, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I live in a flat in a town." … "My favourite room is the kitchen because I like cooking." … "There's a park near my home."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Talk about rooms and what we do in them, and say the "ch" sound in kitchen, chicken, children and cheese.
  • Read and complete a text about a home, and listen and write details: names, numbers and places.
  • Write about their own home (with a made-up address if it is shared) and draw a house from a description.
