Week 15 · Lesson 1: Classroom Riddles

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 15: At our school

Fun for Movers · Week 15: At our school | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Classroom riddles Lesson 2 · My school week Lesson 3 · A music lesson in English

You Need

  • The six riddles below, on cards.
  • Your child's school bag, or a bag with school things in it.
  • Paper for tick-or-cross answers. "Rubber" (UK) and "eraser" (US) are both Starters words.

1 Your teacher gives you this to do at home. · 2 You look at this to find a street in a new town. · 3 It's big and black, and you play music on it. · 4 This means "No, that's not right!" · 5 You use this to look at websites and play games. · 6 You make this when you write a word wrong. It's OK!

Answers: homework · a map · a piano · a cross · a computer · a mistake.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): School Supplies Song. Hold up each thing from your school bag.
  3. Classroom riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it. School words
  4. Tick or cross? (5 min): say sentences; your child draws a tick (✓) for right and a cross (✗) for wrong: "You write with a rubber." ✗ "A map has got roads and rivers on it." ✓ "We do homework at the cinema." ✗ Then your child says some for you.
  5. My school bag (5 min): your child takes everything out of the bag and names it: "a pencil, a ruler, a rubber, my homework…" Then: "What's on page 5 of your English book?"
  6. Watch (3 min): The Going to School Song. Do each action.
  7. Spelling (3 min): your child writes six words from memory: board, map, page, tick, cross, homework.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Your teacher gives you this to do at home." … "Homework!" … "Tick or cross?"
