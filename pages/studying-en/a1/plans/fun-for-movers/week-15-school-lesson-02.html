Week 15 · Lesson 2: My School Week

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 15: At our school

Fun for Movers · Week 15: At our school | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Classroom riddles Lesson 2 · My school week Lesson 3 · A music lesson in English

You Need

  • Seven day cards: Monday to Sunday (Movers words).
  • A table for your child's school week: days across the top, lessons underneath.
  • A survey sheet: Name · How do you come to school? · Where do you do your homework?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Days of the Week Song. Say which day you have English.
  3. Days of the week (5 min): mix the day cards; your child puts them in order and reads them. Then: "What day is it today? What day was yesterday?"
  4. My school week (6 min): your child fills in the table with real lessons and says: "We have English on Monday and Thursday. We have music on Friday." Sentences
  5. School survey (6 min): your child asks two people: "How do you come to school (or work)?" "By bus." "I walk." "Where do you do your homework?" and writes the answers. Ask Grandma or Grandpa too: "How did you come to school?"
  6. Watch (2 min): How Do You Go To School? Answer "By bus", "By car" or "I walk".
  7. Write (3 min): your child writes three sentences: "I come to school by bike. I do my homework in the kitchen. My favourite day is Friday."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What day is it today?" … "How do you come to school?" – "By bus." … "We have English on Monday."
