Week 15 · Lesson 3: A Music Lesson in English

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 15: At our school

Fun for Movers · Week 15: At our school | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Classroom riddles Lesson 2 · My school week Lesson 3 · A music lesson in English

You Need

  • The story and the word box below, written large.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a classroom drawing.

Word box: music · pointed · board · laughed · played

On Friday morning, the English teacher, Mrs Hall, wasn't at school. Mr Brown, the (1) ____ teacher, came into the classroom with his guitar. He (2) ____ to the (3) ____ and said, "Today we can sing in English!" The children (4) ____. They sang three songs and (5) ____ a word game. It was the best English lesson!

Choose the best name for the story: A Mrs Hall's new guitar · B A music lesson in English · C Mr Brown can't sing

Answers: music · pointed · board · laughed · played. Best name: B.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): How Do You Go To School?
  3. Read and write (7 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box in each gap, then reads the story aloud.
  4. Best name (3 min): your child chooses the best name and says why: "B, because Mr Brown and the children sang in English."
  5. My classroom (6 min): your child draws their classroom (or a dream classroom) and writes three sentences: "There's a big map on the wall. The board is white. I sit next to the window."
  6. Be the teacher (4 min): your child is the teacher and gives you instructions: "Open your book at page 10! Point to the map! Write your name!" Then swap.
  7. Watch (3 min): Days of the Week Song, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "He pointed to the board." … "The children laughed." … "Open your book at page 10!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name more school things: board, map, rubber, page, tick, cross, homework, mistake, internet, website.
  • Say the days of the week and talk about the school week: "We have English on Monday." "I come to school by bus."
  • Read a short school story, write the missing words and choose the best name for it.
