Week 16 · Lesson 1: Play or Go?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Sport. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 16: Let's do some sport!

Fun for Movers · Week 16: Let's do some sport! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Play or go? Lesson 2 · My sports weekend Lesson 3 · Let's go swimming

You Need

  • Sport picture cards (draw or print): football, basketball, baseball, tennis, table tennis, badminton, hockey, swimming, sailing, ice skating, fishing.
  • Two labels: "play" and "go". We play ball games; we go swimming, fishing, sailing, ice skating.
  • Write two long sport words on paper for the word game: BADMINTON and TABLE TENNIS.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Sports & Sports Verbs Chant. Say each sport and do its move.
  3. Play or go? (6 min): your child puts each card under "play" or "go" and says it: "play football", "go swimming". Sport words
  4. Sports charades (5 min): "What am I doing?" Act a sport; your child guesses: "You're playing tennis!" Then swap.
  5. Watch (2 min): Sports with Do, Go and Play. Say "play" or "go" before the song.
  6. Words in a sport (5 min): your child makes small words from the letters of BADMINTON (bad, man, mat, in, not, and…) and TABLE TENNIS (table, ten, net, sit, tell…). Count them!
  7. Sport survey (3 min): ask: "What sports do you play? What's your favourite sport? Can you swim?" Then your child asks you.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I play football." … "I go swimming." … "You're playing tennis!"
