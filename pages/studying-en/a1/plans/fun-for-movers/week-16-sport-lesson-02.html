Week 16 · Lesson 2: My Sports Weekend

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Sport. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 16: Let's do some sport!

Fun for Movers · Week 16: Let's do some sport! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Play or go? Lesson 2 · My sports weekend Lesson 3 · Let's go swimming

You Need

  • Jack's text and the word box below, written large.
  • The -ed ending sounds like "t" in walked, watched, laughed, cooked, jumped and like "d" in played and rained.

Jack's weekend – word box: rained · watched · cooked · played · walked · laughed

On Saturday it (1) ____, so I (2) ____ a film at home with my sister. In the evening, Dad (3) ____ chicken and rice. On Sunday the sun was out! I (4) ____ basketball in the park, and then I (5) ____ home with my friends. We (6) ____ a lot!

Answers: rained · watched · cooked · played · walked · laughed.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Past Tense Verbs #1. Say the -ed word first.
  3. Yesterday I… (5 min): say "Yesterday I jumped!" and your child jumps. Then walked, played football, watched TV, laughed, cooked, kicked a ball. Then your child says and you act.
  4. Read and write (7 min): your child reads Jack's text and writes a word from the box in each gap, then reads it aloud.
  5. My weekend (6 min): ask: "What did you do last weekend?" Your child answers, then writes three sentences: "On Saturday I played badminton. I watched TV. It rained." Sentences
  6. T or d? (3 min): say -ed words; your child claps for a "t" sound (walked, watched, jumped) and taps the table for a "d" sound (played, rained).
  7. Watch (2 min): Hey-O We Want To Play-O. Play each sport with your body.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What did you do last weekend?" … "I played badminton." … "It rained, but we laughed a lot!"
