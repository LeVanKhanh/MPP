Week 16 · Lesson 3: Let's Go Swimming

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Sport. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 16: Let's do some sport!

You Need

  • The text below and the five gaps, each with three choices.
  • The questions and answers for matching, cut into cards.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a sport poster.

Swimming: Lots of people love swimming. You can swim (1) ____ the sea, in a lake or in a swimming pool. Swimming is good (2) ____ your body. It makes your arms and legs (3) ____. Some children (4) ____ swimming lessons when they are four or five. Before you go swimming, you must put on your swimsuit. And take (5) ____ towel!

1 in / on / at · 2 for / with / by · 3 strong / stronger / strongest · 4 start / starts / starting · 5 your / you / yours

Match: What sport did you play? · Where did you play? · Who did you play with? · Did you score a goal? · Was it fun? — Yes, two! · With my cousin. · Yes, it was great! · Football! · In the park.

Answers: in · for · strong · start · your. Match: Football! · In the park. · With my cousin. · Yes, two! · Yes, it was great!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Do You Like Sports? Answer "Yes, I do!" or "No, I don't!"
  3. Read and choose (7 min): your child reads the swimming text and chooses the right word for each gap.
  4. Match (5 min): your child matches each question card with its answer card, then reads the little dialogue with you.
  5. Interview (5 min): your child asks a family member the same questions about a sport they played and writes the answers.
  6. My sport poster (4 min): your child draws a poster of their favourite sport and writes three sentences: "My favourite sport is football. I play it in the park. It's great!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What sport did you play?" … "Football!" … "Was it fun?" – "Yes, it was great!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name sports and say "play" or "go": "I play badminton." "I go swimming."
  • Talk about last weekend with -ed verbs: "I played tennis. It rained. We laughed a lot."
  • Read a short sports text and choose the right word, and ask and answer questions about a game.
