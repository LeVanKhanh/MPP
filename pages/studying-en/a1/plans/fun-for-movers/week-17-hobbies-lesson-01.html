Week 17 · Lesson 1: Hobby Words

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 17: Our hobbies

Fun for Movers · Week 17: Our hobbies | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Hobby words Lesson 2 · Vicky and Jim Lesson 3 · Three-word story

You Need

  • Hobby picture cards: hockey, ice skating, comic books, DVDs, a piano, swimming, a laptop, table tennis, drawing, fishing, a guitar.
  • Six word puzzles on paper, first letter and dashes: d _ _ _ _ _ _ · p _ _ _ _ · c _ _ _ _ · f _ _ _ _ _ _ · l _ _ _ _ _ · g _ _ _ _ _
  • After "like", "love" and "enjoy" we use -ing: "I like drawing." Instruments take "the": "play the piano".

Answers: drawing · piano · comic · fishing · laptop · guitar.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Hobbies and Interests. Act each hobby.
  3. Hobby cards (6 min): your child names each card and sorts them into "I like it" and "I don't like it": "I like ice skating. I don't like fishing." Hobby words
  4. Hobby charades (5 min): "What am I doing?" Act a hobby; your child guesses: "You're playing the piano!" Then swap.
  5. First letters (4 min): your child completes the six word puzzles and reads each word.
  6. Watch (2 min): What Do You Like To Do? Song.
  7. Family hobbies (3 min): ask: "What does Dad like doing? What does Grandma like doing?" Your child answers: "Dad likes fishing."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I like ice skating." … "You're playing the piano!" … "What does Dad like doing?"
