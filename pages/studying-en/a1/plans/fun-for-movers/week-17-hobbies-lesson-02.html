You Need

  • The text below, written large, and the five questions (answers: one to three words).
  • A survey table: Name · What do you like doing? · Can you play the piano / the guitar?

My cousins, Vicky and Jim

Vicky is twelve. She loves music. She plays the guitar every day and she practises the piano on Saturdays. She likes reading comic books too.

Jim is eight. He doesn't like music, but he loves sport. He goes swimming on Mondays and he plays table tennis with Grandpa in the garden. When it's cold, he watches DVDs on his laptop.

1 Vicky plays the ____ every day. · 2 She practises the piano on ____. · 3 Jim goes swimming on ____. · 4 Jim plays table tennis with ____. · 5 When it's cold, Jim watches ____.

Choose the best name: A Two cousins, two hobbies · B Grandpa's garden · C A day at the pool

Answers: guitar · Saturdays · Mondays · Grandpa · DVDs (on his laptop). Best name: A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Hobbies – I like / I love. Say each sentence with the video.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the text together. Your child answers the five questions in one, two or three words.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name for the text and says why.
  5. Hobby survey (6 min): your child asks two people: "What do you like doing? Can you play the piano? Can you play the guitar?" and fills in the table. Sentences
  6. Watch (4 min): The Hobbies Song. Answer with your own hobby.
  7. Write (2 min): your child writes: "I like ____ and ____. I don't like ____."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you like doing?" … "I like reading comic books." … "Can you play the guitar?"
