Week 17 · Lesson 3: Three-Word Story

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 17: Our hobbies

Fun for Movers · Week 17: Our hobbies | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Hobby words Lesson 2 · Vicky and Jim Lesson 3 · Three-word story

You Need

  • The story and the three-word clues below. Read the story to your child twice before they tell it.
  • Paper for your child's own three-word clues.

Lily's music day: One morning, Lily put her guitar in her bag. She took her bag on the bus to school. At school, she had a music lesson with Mr Hall, the music teacher. Mr Hall played the piano and Lily played a song on her guitar. All her friends sang and clapped. In the evening, Lily went home and told Mum about her day at dinner.

Three-word clues: morning, guitar, bag · bag, bus, school · school, music, teacher · piano, guitar, song · friends, sang, clapped · evening, home, Mum

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Can You Play The Piano? Play a pretend instrument.
  3. Listen (4 min): read Lily's story twice. Your child points to each group of three words as you read it.
  4. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the story, one sentence for each group of three words: "One morning, Lily put her guitar in her bag…" Help with a question if they stop: "Where did she go?"
  5. My hobby story (5 min): your child writes four groups of three words about their own favourite hobby day, then tells you the story.
  6. When I'm older (4 min): ask: "What do you want to do when you're older?" Your child answers: "When I'm older, I want to draw comic books." Then you answer too.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "One morning, Lily put her guitar in her bag." … "All her friends sang and clapped." … "When I'm older, I want to…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name hobbies: comic books, DVDs, a laptop, the piano, the guitar, ice skating, table tennis, fishing.
  • Ask and answer "What do you like doing?" "I like playing table tennis." "Can you play the piano?"
  • Read about someone's hobbies and answer in one to three words, and tell a story from three-word clues.
