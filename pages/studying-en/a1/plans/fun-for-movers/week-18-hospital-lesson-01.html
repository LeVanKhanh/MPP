Week 18 · Lesson 1: Doctors and Nurses

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 18: At the hospital

Fun for Movers · Week 18: At the hospital | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Doctors and nurses Lesson 2 · You must go to bed! Lesson 3 · In the hospital

You Need

  • Picture cards: a doctor, a nurse, a dentist, a driver, a hospital, a bus, teeth, a bed.
  • The six riddles below, on cards. Toys or teddies for a pretend hospital.

1 This person looks at your teeth. · 2 This person works in a hospital and helps the doctor. · 3 You go here when you are very ill. · 4 This person drives a bus. · 5 When you've got this, your head hurts. · 6 When you've got this, you say "Atchoo!" and you are cold.

Answers: a dentist · a nurse · a hospital · a driver · a headache · a cold.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Hospital – Doctor & Nurse. Say each word before the voice does.
  3. Who and where? (6 min): your child matches the people with things and places: "A dentist – teeth. A driver – a bus. A nurse – a hospital." Health words
  4. Riddles (6 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it.
  5. Watch (3 min): What's The Matter? Song. Sing the answers.
  6. Teddy hospital (6 min): your child is the doctor; the toys are ill. "What's the matter, Teddy?" "I've got a toothache." "Go to the dentist!" Then you are the doctor.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's the matter, Teddy?" … "I've got a toothache." … "Go to the dentist!"
