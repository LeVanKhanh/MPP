Week 18 · Lesson 2: You Must Go to Bed!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 18: At the hospital

Fun for Movers · Week 18: At the hospital | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Doctors and nurses Lesson 2 · You must go to bed! Lesson 3 · In the hospital

You Need

  • Cards for a must/mustn't game: go to bed · eat ice cream all day · drink lots of water · go swimming · see the doctor · play football in the rain.
  • The four advice sentences and the word box below.
  • "Mustn't" means "don't do it!" "Ambulance" and "medicine" are Flyers words.

Word box: bed · water · tired · careful · toothache

1 When you've got a cold, you must go to (1) ____ and drink lots of (2) ____. · 2 When you're very (3) ____, go to sleep! · 3 Be (4) ____ on your bike! Wear a helmet. · 4 Don't eat lots of sweets, or you can get a (5) ____.

Answers: bed · water · tired · careful · toothache. Must/mustn't for a child with a cold: must go to bed, drink lots of water, see the doctor; mustn't eat ice cream all day, go swimming, play football in the rain.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Health Problems – Ouch, I'm Sick! Act each one.
  3. Show me! (4 min): "I've got a headache!" Your child acts it. "I've got a stomach-ache! A cough! A temperature!" Then your child says and you act.
  4. Must or mustn't? (6 min): "Tom has got a cold." Your child puts each card under "must" or "mustn't" and says it: "He must go to bed. He mustn't go swimming." Sentences
  5. Good advice (6 min): your child reads the sentences and writes a word from the box in each gap.
  6. At the doctor's (4 min): you are ill; your child is the doctor: "What's the matter?" "My ear hurts." "You must go to bed and drink lots of water!" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's the matter?" … "I've got a cold." … "You must go to bed. You mustn't go swimming."
