Week 18 · Lesson 3: In the Hospital

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 18: At the hospital

Fun for Movers · Week 18: At the hospital | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Doctors and nurses Lesson 2 · You must go to bed! Lesson 3 · In the hospital

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a hospital waiting room: a nurse holding a baby, a girl reading a comic book, a man talking on the phone, a woman asleep on a chair, a boy with his arm in a white bag, and a small café with cakes and juice.
  • Words for the silent-letter game, written large: talk, walk, head, friend, stomach, building, listen, write.
  • In the last step, no need to name a real hospital.

1 The nurse is holding a ____. · 2 One girl is reading a ____. · 3 What's the man doing? · 4 Who is asleep? · 5 What can you buy in the café? · 6 Now write one sentence about the picture.

Answers: baby · comic book · talking on the phone · a woman (on the chair) · cakes and juice · any true sentence. Silent letters: ta(l)k, wa(l)k, he(a)d, fr(i)end, stomac(h), b(u)ilding, lis(t)en, (w)rite.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Doctor Doctor Song. Be the doctor.
  3. Look, read and write (8 min): your child looks at your hospital picture and answers each question in one to five words, then writes one sentence.
  4. Silent letters (5 min): say each word slowly. Your child circles the letter we write but don't say: "talk – the l is silent!"
  5. Talk about health (5 min): ask: "When were you ill? What was the matter? What did you do? Is someone in your family a doctor, a nurse or a dentist?"
  6. Watch (3 min): What's The Matter? Song, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The nurse is holding a baby." … "The woman is asleep." … "In talk, the l is silent!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name people and places that help us: doctor, nurse, dentist, driver, hospital.
  • Ask "What's the matter?" and answer "I've got a headache / a cold / a temperature", and give advice with "You must… / You mustn't…".
  • Answer questions about a hospital picture in one to five words, and find silent letters in words like talk, head and friend.
