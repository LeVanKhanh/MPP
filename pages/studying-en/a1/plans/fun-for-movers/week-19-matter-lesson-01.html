Week 19 · Lesson 1: Why Is He Ill?

You Need

  • Eight health words with the vowels missing, written large: h _ _ d _ ch _ · c _ _ gh · t _ mp _ r _ t _ r _ · t _ _ th _ ch _ · st _ m _ ch- _ ch _ · b _ ck · d _ ct _ r · n _ rs _
  • Cards in two colours. Blue (what happened): ate four ice creams · played outside in the cold wind · fell off a bike · carried a big box · watched TV all night. Red (now): stomach-ache · earache · hurt arm · back hurts · tired.

Answers: headache · cough · temperature · toothache · stomach-ache · back · doctor · nurse.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Health Problems – Ouch, I'm Sick! Act each one.
  3. Missing vowels (6 min): your child writes a, e, i, o or u in each gap and reads the word. Health words
  4. Why? (6 min): your child matches each blue card with a red card and says it: "He ate four ice creams. Now he's got a stomach-ache." Sentences
  5. Act and guess (5 min): your child acts a problem; you guess: "You've got a cough!" "Your back hurts!" Then swap.
  6. Write (3 min): your child writes two sentences like the cards: "She played outside in the wind. Now she's got an earache."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "He ate four ice creams." … "Now he's got a stomach-ache." … "You've got a cough!"
