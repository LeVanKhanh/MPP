Week 19 · Lesson 2: Why Weren't You at School?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 19: What is the matter?

Fun for Movers · Week 19: What is the matter? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Why is he ill? Lesson 2 · Why weren't you at school? Lesson 3 · Lily and Jack

You Need

  • The dialogue below on paper. Your child chooses A, B or C for each answer.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a "get well" picture.
  • "Get well soon!" is a fixed phrase; you can say it, but "soon", "dear" and "card" are Flyers words.

Lily and Sam

1 Lily: Why weren't you at school yesterday? – Sam: A It's a big school. B I was ill. C Yes, I was.

2 Lily: What was the matter? – Sam: A I had a bad cough. B It's my cough. C You're right.

3 Lily: Did you go to the doctor? – Sam: A Yes, I do. B Yes, my mum took me. C No, she isn't.

4 Lily: Are you better now? – Sam: A I'm at home. B It was yesterday. C Yes, I'm fine, thanks!

5 Lily: Do you want to play football after school? – Sam: A I'm playing it. B He likes it. C Yes, great idea!

6 Sam: What did we do in class yesterday? – Lily: A We read a story about a whale. B It's in my bag. C We're in class.

Answers: 1 B · 2 A · 3 B · 4 C · 5 C · 6 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What did you do? (Past tense). Answer about yesterday.
  3. Yesterday (5 min): ask: "Where did you go yesterday? What did you eat? What did you play?" Your child answers: "I went to the park. I ate rice."
  4. Choose the answer (7 min): your child reads the dialogue and circles A, B or C. Then you read it together with two voices.
  5. Get well picture (6 min): your child draws a picture for an ill friend or toy and writes: "Paul, I'm sorry you're ill! You must go to bed and drink lots of water. Then you can play with me! Love, ____."
  6. Watch (2 min): Get Well Soon.
  7. Phone call (3 min): pretend to phone a friend who is ill: "Hello, Paul! How are you? What's the matter? Are you better now?" Then swap.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Why weren't you at school yesterday?" … "I was ill. I had a bad cough." … "Are you better now?"
