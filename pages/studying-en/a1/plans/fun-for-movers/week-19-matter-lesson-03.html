Week 19 · Lesson 3: Lily and Jack

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 19: What is the matter?

Fun for Movers · Week 19: What is the matter? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Why is he ill? Lesson 2 · Why weren't you at school? Lesson 3 · Lily and Jack

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two pictures. Lily: on the sofa with a cold, drinking water, a cat next to her, reading a comic book. Jack: in bed with a stomach-ache, asleep, a dog next to him, a TV on.
  • The text about Jack and the five questions below (answers: one to five words).

Jack: Jack didn't go to school today because he had a stomach-ache. Yesterday was his cousin's birthday and he ate lots of cake and six ice creams! Now he's in bed. He's asleep and he's dreaming about football. When he's well again, he wants to play in the park with his dog.

1 Why didn't Jack go to school today? · 2 What did he eat yesterday? · 3 Where is he now? · 4 What is he dreaming about? · 5 What does he want to do when he's well again?

Say it fast: Six children chose chips and chicken for lunch. Then all the children had a stomach-ache!

Answers: Differences: sofa / bed · a cold / a stomach-ache · drinking water / asleep · cat / dog · comic book / TV. Questions: he had a stomach-ache · (lots of) cake and (six) ice creams · in bed · football · play in the park (with his dog).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Doctor Doctor Song. Be the doctor.
  3. Find the differences (6 min): your child finds five differences between Lily and Jack: "Lily is on the sofa, but Jack is in bed."
  4. Read and answer (7 min): your child reads the text about Jack and answers the questions in one to five words.
  5. Say it fast (3 min): say the ch sentence slowly, then faster and faster.
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "When were you ill? What was the matter? Who helped you?"
  7. Watch (2 min): Get Well Soon, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Lily is on the sofa, but Jack is in bed." … "He ate lots of cake." … "He wants to play in the park."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell health words and say why someone is ill: "He ate four ice creams. Now he's got a stomach-ache."
  • Talk about the past – "Why weren't you at school?" "I was ill. I had a bad cough." – and choose the best answer in a dialogue.
  • Find differences between two pictures and answer questions about a short text in one to five words.
