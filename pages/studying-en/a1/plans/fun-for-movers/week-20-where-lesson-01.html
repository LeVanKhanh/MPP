Week 20 · Lesson 1: Where Am I?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: My Street and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 20: Where?

Fun for Movers · Week 20: Where? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where am I? Lesson 2 · Our town map Lesson 3 · A day at the funfair

You Need

  • Place cards (draw or print): shop, supermarket, market, cinema, hospital, car park, playground, farm, funfair, beach, lake, river, mountain, island, lift.
  • Two labels: "in a town" and "in the countryside".
  • The riddles and the four "which one is different?" sets below.

Where am I? 1 I'm watching a film with lots of people. · 2 I'm buying fruit and vegetables outside. · 3 I'm swimming and I can see boats and fish. · 4 There's loud music and I'm on a ride. · 5 The doctors and nurses are here. · 6 I'm going up to the top floor, but not on my feet!

Which one is different? 1 cinema, hospital, supermarket, river · 2 lake, sea, river, car park · 3 mountain, beach, island, lift · 4 market, shop, supermarket, playground

Answers: Riddles: cinema · market · the sea · funfair · hospital · lift. Different: river (it isn't a building) · car park (it hasn't got water) · lift (it's in a building) · playground (you can't buy things there).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Places in the City. Say each place first.
  3. Place cards (6 min): your child names each card and puts it under "in a town" or "in the countryside". Place words
  4. Where am I? (6 min): read each riddle; your child guesses the place and writes it. Then your child makes one for you.
  5. Which one is different? (6 min): your child says which one is different in each set and why: "The river, because it isn't a building."
  6. Watch (3 min): Guess Where in Town? Shout the place before the answer.
  7. Talk (2 min): ask: "Where do you like going at the weekend? What can you do there?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm watching a film. Where am I?" … "At the cinema!" … "The river is different because it isn't a building."
