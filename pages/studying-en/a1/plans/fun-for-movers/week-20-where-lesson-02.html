Week 20 · Lesson 2: Our Town Map

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 20: Where?

Fun for Movers · Week 20: Where? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where am I? Lesson 2 · Our town map Lesson 3 · A day at the funfair

You Need

  • Two sheets of paper and pencils. Before the lesson, draw your own secret town map with eight places on it (don't show it).
  • Words for where: next to, opposite, behind, between, near, in front of.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where Are You Going? Places Song. Sing the answers.
  3. Our town map (7 min): draw a town together with eight places: a cinema, a supermarket, a hospital, a car park, a market, a park, a river and a funfair. Your child writes the names.
  4. Where's the cinema? (5 min): ask: "Where's the cinema?" "It's next to the supermarket." "Where's the car park?" "It's opposite the hospital." Then your child asks you. Sentences
  5. Picture dictation (8 min): describe your secret map: "The hospital is next to the river. The cinema is between the market and the park…" Your child draws it on new paper. Then put the two maps side by side: are they the same?
  6. Watch (2 min): Where Are You Going?
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where's the cinema?" … "It's next to the supermarket." … "The car park is opposite the hospital."
