Week 20 · Lesson 3: A Day at the Funfair

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 20: Where?

Fun for Movers · Week 20: Where? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where am I? Lesson 2 · Our town map Lesson 3 · A day at the funfair

You Need

  • The story and the word box below, written large.
  • A list of words for the "k" sound: car park, market, lake, kite, cake, duck, rock, clock, cinema (careful: c can also sound like "s"!).

Word box: fun · frightened · bought · ice creams · drive · river · hospital · cold

Last Saturday, Lily and her cousin Tom went to the funfair near the river. It was great (1) ____! First, they rode on the horses and then they went on the big boat ride. Tom was (2) ____ of the boat ride, but Lily held his hand. Then they were hungry, so they (3) ____ two big (4) ____ and ate them on a seat near the water. "When I'm older, I want to (5) ____ the little cars at the funfair!" Tom laughed.

Choose the best name: A A great day at the funfair · B Tom's new car · C Swimming in the river

Answers: fun · frightened · bought · ice creams · drive. Best name: A. The "k" sound: c in car, cake, clock; k in park, market, lake, kite; ck in duck, rock, clock. In cinema the c sounds like "s".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where Are You Going? (English Tree). Answer with a place.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box in each gap. Three words are extra.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  5. Find the k sound (5 min): your child underlines the letters that make the "k" sound in each word: c, k or ck. Then: "Does the c in cinema sound like k?"
  6. My favourite place (5 min): your child draws a favourite place and writes two sentences: "My favourite place is the beach. I can swim and play in the sand there."
  7. Watch (2 min): Places in the City, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It was great fun!" … "Tom was frightened of the boat ride." … "My favourite place is the beach."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name places: shop, supermarket, market, cinema, hospital, car park, farm, funfair, beach, lake, river, mountain, island, lift.
  • Say where places are (next to, opposite, behind, between, near) and say which one is different and why.
  • Read a short story, write the missing words and choose the best name, and find the "k" sound in c, k and ck.
