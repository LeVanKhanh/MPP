Week 21 · Lesson 1: Where Can You…?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 21: Here and there in town

Fun for Movers · Week 21: Here and there in town | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where can you…? Lesson 2 · Daisy's day in town Lesson 3 · A day in town

You Need

  • Eight activity cards: watch a film · buy fruit and vegetables · read books · play tennis · have a milkshake · see animals · catch a bus · see a doctor.
  • Eight place cards: cinema · market · library · sports centre · café · zoo · bus station · hospital.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Town Activities and Places. Say "You can… at the…"
  3. Activity cards (7 min): your child puts each activity card next to a place card and says it: "You can watch a film at the cinema." Place words
  4. Where can you…? (5 min): ask: "Where can you see a doctor?" "At the hospital!" Then your child asks you. Sentences
  5. Town mime (5 min): act an activity without words; your child guesses the place: "You're reading. You're at the library!" Then swap.
  6. Watch (3 min): Guess Where in Town?
  7. Write (2 min): your child writes two sentences: "You can play tennis at the sports centre. You can buy fruit at the market."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where can you see a doctor?" … "At the hospital!" … "You can watch a film at the cinema."
