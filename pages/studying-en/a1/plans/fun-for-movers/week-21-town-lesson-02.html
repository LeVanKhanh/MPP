Week 21 · Lesson 2: Daisy's Day in Town

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 21: Here and there in town

Fun for Movers · Week 21: Here and there in town | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where can you…? Lesson 2 · Daisy's day in town Lesson 3 · A day in town

You Need

  • Daisy's email and the six questions below (answers: one to three words).
  • Before the lesson, draw three rows of three small pictures, A, B and C: 1 a hospital · a library · a sports centre; 2 a café · a home kitchen · a market; 3 a boy swimming · playing tennis · reading. Read the listening script twice.
  • "Postcard" is a Flyers word, so Daisy writes an email.

Daisy's email: Hi Grandma! We're on holiday by the sea. Yesterday it rained, so we didn't go to the beach. We took the bus to the town centre. First we went to the library and I chose two comic books. Then we had lunch in a café near the market. Dad had fish and chips and I had a cheese sandwich and a banana milkshake. After lunch, we watched a film at the cinema. It was very funny! Now it's sunny and we're at the beach. Love, Daisy

1 Yesterday it ____. · 2 The family went to the town centre by ____. · 3 Daisy chose two ____ at the library. · 4 Dad had ____ for lunch. · 5 After lunch, they watched ____. · 6 Where are they now?

Listen and tick: 1 Where does Uncle Paul work? – He isn't a doctor. He works with books, because he loves reading. · 2 Where did Mum have a coffee? – She didn't have it at home. She had a coffee at the café next to the cinema. · 3 What is Tom doing? – Tom's at the sports centre. He isn't swimming today. He's playing tennis.

Answers: rained · bus · comic books · fish and chips · a film · at the beach. Listen and tick: 1 B (library) · 2 A (café) · 3 B (playing tennis).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): What did you do? We went to the park. Answer with "We went to…"
  3. Read and answer (8 min): read Daisy's email together. Your child answers the six questions in one, two or three words.
  4. Listen and tick (6 min): read each part of the script twice; your child ticks A, B or C.
  5. Watch (3 min): Where Are You Going? Places Song.
  6. My day in town (5 min): your child writes a short email to a family member about a day in town: "Hi Grandpa! Yesterday I went to… First… Then…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We took the bus to the town centre." … "First we went to the library." … "Then we had lunch in a café."
