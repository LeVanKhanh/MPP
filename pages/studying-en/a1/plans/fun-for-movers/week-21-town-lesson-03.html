Week 21 · Lesson 3: A Day in Town

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 21: Here and there in town

Fun for Movers · Week 21: Here and there in town | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Where can you…? Lesson 2 · Daisy's day in town Lesson 3 · A day in town

You Need

  • The town map from Week 20 (or a new quick map).
  • For the word chain: the last letter of one word is the first letter of the next. Example: cinema → apple → egg → garden → nurse → …

Say it fast: Two blue balloons for you at the zoo, and school food too!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Town Activities and Places.
  3. Say it fast (3 min): say the "oo" sentence slowly, then faster. Who can say it three times?
  4. Word chain (6 min): take turns: you say "cinema", your child says a word starting with "a" ("apple"), you say a word with "e"… Town and food words get a point!
  5. Plan a day in town (7 min): your child plans a day on the map and writes it: "First we go to the market. Then we have lunch at the café. After that we watch a film at the cinema." Sentences
  6. Talk (4 min): ask: "What can you do in your town? Where do you like going at the weekend? Is there a cinema near your home?"
  7. Watch (2 min): Places in the City, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "First we go to the market." … "Then we have lunch at the café." … "After that we watch a film."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say where you can do things in town: "You can watch a film at the cinema." "You can catch a bus at the bus station."
  • Read an email about a day in town and answer in one to three words, and listen for details about places.
  • Plan a day in town with "first", "then" and "after that", and play a word-chain game.
