Week 22 · Lesson 1: Village or City?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Farm and Countryside and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 22: A trip to the city

Fun for Movers · Week 22: A trip to the city | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Village or city? Lesson 2 · The city mouse Lesson 3 · Lily's city trip

You Need

  • Eight sentence cards: There are lots of tall buildings. · You can see cows and sheep near the houses. · There are lots of buses and cars. · Everyone knows everyone. · There's a big hospital and a train station. · There's only one small shop. · It's quiet at night. · There are lots of cinemas and cafés.
  • Two labels, "a village" and "a city", and a big sheet of paper folded in two for drawing.
  • Comparisons: big → bigger, tall → taller, quiet → quieter, quickly → more quickly. "Noisy" and "thousand" are Flyers words.

Answers: City: tall buildings · buses and cars · hospital and train station · cinemas and cafés. Village: cows and sheep · everyone knows everyone · one small shop · quiet at night.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Towns and Cities. Say what is different.
  3. Village or city? (7 min): your child reads each card and puts it under "a village" or "a city". Countryside words
  4. Compare (6 min): your child says and writes three sentences: "A city is bigger than a village. The buildings in a city are taller. A village is quieter." Sentences
  5. Draw (5 min): on the folded paper, your child draws a village on one side and a city on the other, then labels four things on each.
  6. Watch (2 min): Places in the City.
  7. Where would you like to live? (2 min): ask: "Would you like to live in a village or a city? Why?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A city is bigger than a village." … "A village is quieter." … "I'd like to live in a village because it's quiet."
