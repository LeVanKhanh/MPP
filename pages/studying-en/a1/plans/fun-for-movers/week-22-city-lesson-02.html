Week 22 · Lesson 2: The City Mouse and the Country Mouse

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 22: A trip to the city

Fun for Movers · Week 22: A trip to the city | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Village or city? Lesson 2 · The city mouse Lesson 3 · Lily's city trip

You Need

  • Four quick drawings to help retell the story: a small country home with grass and corn; a big city house with lots of food; a cat; the country mouse going home.
  • The lines below for "Who said it?"
  • The video uses an American accent; the story is a very old fable.

Who said it? 1 "My home is small and quiet, and the food comes from the farm." · 2 "In the city there's cheese and cake every day!" · 3 "Look! A big cat! Run!" · 4 "I like my little, quiet home best."

Answers: 1 the country mouse · 2 the city mouse · 3 the city mouse · 4 the country mouse.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): The City Mouse and the Country Mouse. Watch the whole story.
  3. Who said it? (5 min): read each line; your child says which mouse said it: "The country mouse!"
  4. Retell (7 min): your child tells the story with the four drawings, one or two sentences for each: "The country mouse lives in a small, quiet home…"
  5. I think… So do I! (5 min): say opinions; your child agrees ("So do I!") or doesn't ("I don't! I think…"): "I think the city is exciting." "I think the cat is scary." "I think the country mouse is right."
  6. Write (3 min): your child writes: "I think the ____ mouse is right because ____."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I think the country mouse is right." … "So do I!" … "The city is exciting, but there's a big cat!"
