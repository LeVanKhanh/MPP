Week 22 · Lesson 3: Lily's City Trip

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: My Street and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 22: A trip to the city

Fun for Movers · Week 22: A trip to the city | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Village or city? Lesson 2 · The city mouse Lesson 3 · Lily's city trip

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five things on the left (a map, a bottle of water, two tickets, a baseball cap, a book) and six places on the right (a station, a sports centre, a cinema, a park, a library, a swimming pool). Read the script twice; your child draws lines. One place is extra.
  • Any picture of a busy city street (from a picture book or a photo) for "Look, read and write".
  • The sentence pairs below, cut into cards.

Listen: Lily, please take the map to the station. Grandpa is waiting there. Take this bottle of water to the sports centre; your brother is playing basketball there. These two tickets are for the cinema – give them to Aunt Mary. And your baseball cap? Take it to the park. It's really sunny there! Oh, and this book goes back to the library.

Look, read and write (about your street picture): 1 How many buses can you see? · 2 What colour is the biggest building? · 3 What's the man in the red jacket doing? · 4 Write one sentence about the picture.

Match: I'm really thirsty! · I think this park is great! · I know where the cinema is. · Look! Our bus is here! — So do I! · Quick! Let's run! · Let's buy some water. · Great! Let's go!

Answers: map – station · water – sports centre · tickets – cinema · cap – park · book – library (the swimming pool is extra). Match: thirsty → Let's buy some water · park is great → So do I! · where the cinema is → Great! Let's go! · bus is here → Quick! Let's run!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where Are You Going? Places Song.
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice; your child draws a line from each thing to the right place.
  4. Look, read and write (7 min): your child looks at the street picture and answers the questions in one to five words.
  5. What do you say? (6 min): your child matches each sentence with the best answer, then you act the little dialogues. Sentences
  6. Watch (2 min): Towns and Cities, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Take the map to the station." … "I'm really thirsty!" – "Let's buy some water." … "Quick! Let's run!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Compare a village and a city: "A city is bigger. A village is quieter. People in a city walk more quickly."
  • Retell "The City Mouse and the Country Mouse" and agree with "So do I!"
  • Listen and match things to places in a city, and answer questions about a busy street picture.
