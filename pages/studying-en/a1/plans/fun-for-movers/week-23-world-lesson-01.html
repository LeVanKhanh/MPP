Lesson 1 · Our beautiful world Lesson 2 · Mountains Lesson 3 · World quiz

You Need

  • A big landscape picture (from a book or a calendar) with mountains, water and sky, or a window with a good view.
  • Word cards for sorting: mountain, field, forest, rock, island, river, lake, waterfall, sea, cloud, star, moon, sun. Three labels: land · water · sky.
  • The six riddles below.

1 Water falls down from the rocks here. It's very loud! · 2 It's got water all round it. You can go there by boat. · 3 You can see lots of these in the sky at night. · 4 It's green and cows and sheep eat it. · 5 It's a big place with lots and lots of trees. · 6 Water goes from the mountains to the sea in this.

Answers: a waterfall · an island · stars · grass · a forest · a river.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Geography – Exploring Our World. Point and say each place.
  3. Nature hunt (6 min): look at the big picture (or out of the window). Your child points and says: "I can see a mountain. I can see clouds and a river." Nature words
  4. Riddles (7 min): your child reads each riddle, says the answer and writes it.
  5. Land, water or sky? (5 min): your child puts each card under land, water or sky and says it: "A lake – water!"
  6. Watch (2 min): Learn Nature Vocabulary.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I can see a mountain." … "It's got water all round it. What is it?" … "An island!"
