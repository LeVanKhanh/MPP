Week 23 · Lesson 2: Mountains

You Need

  • The text below, written large. In each gap your child crosses out the two wrong words.
  • Paper and coloured pencils.

Mountains: Mountains are very (1) tall / short / small, and it's often (2) cold / hot / sunny at the top. Some mountains have got (3) snow / sand / sweets on them all year. Lots of people like (4) climbing / swimming / sailing up mountains, but it's (5) dangerous / easy / funny when it's very windy. You can see beautiful (6) waterfalls / supermarkets / car parks and lakes in the mountains. Goats can live there because they're very good at (7) jumping / flying / singing on rocks.

Answers: tall · cold · snow · climbing · dangerous · waterfalls · jumping.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Explore the Rainforest! Say "It's hot and wet."
  3. Read and choose (8 min): your child reads the text and crosses out the two wrong words in each gap, then reads it aloud.
  4. Hot or cold? (4 min): ask: "What's the jungle like?" "It's hot and wet." "What's it like at the top of a mountain?" "It's cold and windy." Then an island, a forest, the sea. Sentences
  5. Be careful! (4 min): name a place; your child warns you: "A river!" "Be careful! There are crocodiles!" "The rocks!" "Be careful on the rocks!"
  6. Draw my place (4 min): your child draws a place in nature (an island with a waterfall, a mountain with goats…) and writes two sentences about it.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Mountains are very tall." … "It's cold at the top." … "Be careful! There are crocodiles in the river!"
