Week 23 · Lesson 3: World Quiz

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 23: The world around us

Fun for Movers · Week 23: The world around us | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Our beautiful world Lesson 2 · Mountains Lesson 3 · World quiz

You Need

  • Word cards for the sound game: green, grass, garden, great, giraffe, jungle, jump, juice, jacket, jellyfish.
  • The two sentences and the eight quiz questions below.

Say it fast: A big green frog is jumping in the garden. · The giraffe and the jellyfish are good friends!

World quiz: 1 Do penguins live in a hot place or a cold place? · 2 Which is bigger: a lake or the sea? · 3 What can you see in the sky at night? · 4 Where do monkeys live: in the jungle or in the sea? · 5 What colour are the leaves in the jungle? · 6 What do goats like climbing on? · 7 What's got water all round it? · 8 Where can you see a waterfall?

Answers: "g" sound: green, grass, garden, great. "j" sound: giraffe, jungle, jump, juice, jacket, jellyfish. Quiz: a cold place · the sea · the moon and stars · in the jungle · green · rocks · an island · in the mountains (or in a jungle).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Soft g – Sounds like "j".
  3. G or j? (6 min): your child says each word and sorts it: "g" as in green, or "j" as in jump. Giraffe goes with jump!
  4. Say it fast (3 min): say the two sentences slowly, then faster and faster.
  5. World quiz (8 min): ask the eight quiz questions. Then your child writes two quiz questions for you.
  6. My favourite place (3 min): ask: "Would you like to go to a jungle, a mountain or an island? Why?"
  7. Watch (2 min): Learn Nature Vocabulary, the last time this week.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Green – g! Giraffe – j!" … "Which is bigger: a lake or the sea?" … "I'd like to go to an island."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things in the world around us: mountain, island, jungle, forest, field, river, lake, waterfall, rock, grass, leaf, plant, sky, star, moon.
  • Read a short text about a place and choose the right words, and say "Jungles are hot and wet." "Be careful! There are crocodiles!"
  • Hear the difference between "g" (green) and "j" (giraffe, jungle), and answer a world quiz.
