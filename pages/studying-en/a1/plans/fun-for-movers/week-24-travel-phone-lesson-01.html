Week 24 · Lesson 1: How Did You Travel?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Travel. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 24: Travelling, texting, phoning

Fun for Movers · Week 24: Travelling, texting, phoning | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · How did you travel? Lesson 2 · Texts and emails Lesson 3 · Picture A or B?

You Need

  • Transport cards: car, bus, train, plane, boat, ship, helicopter, motorbike, bike. Three labels: land · water · sky.
  • Before the lesson, draw a picture: a girl on a beach waving at a boat, a plane in the sky, a bike next to a tree, a sunny sky.
  • The five yes/no sentences below.

1 The girl is at the beach. · 2 It's a very cloudy day. · 3 There's a plane in the sky. · 4 The girl came here by bike. · 5 The boat is smaller than the bike.

Answers: yes · no (it's sunny) · yes · yes (her bike is next to the tree) · no (the boat is bigger).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Travel Song. Act each way to travel.
  3. Land, water or sky? (5 min): your child sorts the cards and says: "A helicopter – sky! A ship – water!" Travel words
  4. How did you travel? (5 min): ask about real trips: "How did you travel to Grandma's?" "By car." "How do you go to school?" Then your child asks you. Sentences
  5. Yes or no? (6 min): your child looks at your picture and writes yes or no for each sentence, then makes the "no" sentences right.
  6. Pack your bag (4 min): "I'm going on a trip and I'm taking a map." "…a map and a ticket." Take turns and add one thing each time.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How did you travel?" … "By train." … "I'm going on a trip and I'm taking a map."
