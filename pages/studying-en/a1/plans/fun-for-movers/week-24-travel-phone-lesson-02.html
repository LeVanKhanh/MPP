Week 24 · Lesson 2: Texts and Emails

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Travel. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 24: Travelling, texting, phoning

Fun for Movers · Week 24: Travelling, texting, phoning | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · How did you travel? Lesson 2 · Texts and emails Lesson 3 · Picture A or B?

You Need

  • The alphabet with numbers: a = 1, b = 2 … z = 26.
  • The secret message below, written large.
  • A real or toy phone for the last step.

Secret message: 9 · 1 13 · 1 20 · 20 8 5 · 19 20 1 20 9 15 14 · 3 1 14 · 25 15 21 · 3 15 13 5 ?

Answers: I AM AT THE STATION. CAN YOU COME?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Technology – Mobile phone, tablet… Say the words you know.
  3. Secret message (7 min): your child finds each letter with the number code and writes the secret message. Then your child writes a short coded message for you.
  4. What can you do with it? (5 min): ask: "What can you do with a tablet? A laptop? A phone?" Your child answers: "You can send texts and emails, watch videos and take photos." Keeping in touch
  5. Write a text (5 min): your child writes a short text to a friend about a pretend trip: "Hi Zoe! I'm at the beach. I'm taking lots of photos. I sent you one! Bye!"
  6. Phone call (3 min): pretend phone call: "Hello! This is Lily. Can I talk to Jim, please?" "Yes. Jim! It's Lily on the phone!" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I sent you a text this morning." … "Did you get my email?" … "Can I talk to Jim, please?"
