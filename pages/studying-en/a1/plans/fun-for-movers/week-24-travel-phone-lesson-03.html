Week 24 · Lesson 3: Picture A or B?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 24: Travelling, texting, phoning

Fun for Movers · Week 24: Travelling, texting, phoning | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · How did you travel? Lesson 2 · Texts and emails Lesson 3 · Picture A or B?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two beach pictures. Picture a: a girl with long straight hair, a red kite, a boat with a white sail, three birds, an ice cream, a yellow towel. Picture b: the same girl with short curly hair, a blue kite, a big ship, two birds, a sandwich, a green towel.
  • The eight sentences below.

1 The girl's hair is long. · 2 There's a big ship. · 3 The kite is blue. · 4 There are three birds. · 5 She's eating a sandwich. · 6 The towel is yellow. · 7 Her hair is curly. · 8 There's a small boat with a white sail.

Answers: a · b · b · a · b · a · b · a.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Can I Speak to Sally? At Movers say "Can I talk to…?"
  3. Picture a or b? (7 min): your child reads each sentence and writes a or b.
  4. Complete the sentences (6 min): your child writes three sentences: "In picture a the kite is red, but in picture b it's blue." Picture a / picture b
  5. Phone game (5 min): turn your back. Your child "phones" you and describes one picture: "The girl's hair is short and curly…" You guess: "Picture b!" Then swap.
  6. Watch (4 min): I'm Going On A Trip, the last time this week.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Picture a or b?" … "In picture a the kite is red, but in picture b it's blue." … "Picture b!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say how people travel – by car, by train, by boat, by helicopter – and what to take on a trip.
  • Talk about texts, emails, photos and phone calls: "I sent you a text." "Can I talk to Jim, please?"
  • Say if a sentence is about picture a or b, and describe differences: "In picture a the kite is red, but in picture b it's blue."
