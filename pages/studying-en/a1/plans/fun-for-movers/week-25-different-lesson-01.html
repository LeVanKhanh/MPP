Week 25 · Lesson 1: Things That Go Together

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Opposites and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 25: Which one is different?

You Need

  • Paper with four boxes in each row, two pictures drawn in each row and one box empty: 1 a fish, a duck · 2 a towel, a toothbrush · 3 a car, a bike · 4 a tomato, a red apple.
  • The five "which one is different?" sets below, and twelve word cards for the group game: car, bus, bike · banana, apple, pear · dog, cat, rabbit · sofa, armchair, lamp.

Things that go together: 1 A fish, a duck and a ____ can swim. · 2 You can find a towel, a toothbrush and ____ in a bathroom. · 3 A car, a bike and a ____ go on the road. · 4 A tomato, an apple and a ____ are red.

Which one is different? 1 car, bus, train, banana · 2 dolphin, whale, shark, kangaroo · 3 Lily is laughing, Ben is laughing, Sam is laughing, Mia is crying · 4 sofa, armchair, lamp, bed · 5 kitten, puppy, baby, grandpa

Answers: Any good third thing (for example a dolphin · toothpaste · bus · red pen). Different: banana (you can't travel on it) · kangaroo (it doesn't live in the sea) · Mia (she's crying) · bed (it isn't in the living room) · grandpa (he isn't young).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): Guess the odd one out. Say why before the answer.
  3. Things that go together (7 min): in each row your child draws a third picture in the empty box, then writes the word and reads the sentence.
  4. Which one is different? (6 min): read each set; your child says which one and why: "The banana, because you can't travel on it!" Which one is different?
  5. Make a set (3 min): your child makes a set of four for you, with one different thing.
  6. Groups game (3 min): mix the twelve word cards; your child makes four groups of three and says what each group is: "These are fruit."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which one is different?" … "The banana, because you can't travel on it!" … "A fish, a duck and a dolphin can swim."
