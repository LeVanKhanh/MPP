Week 25 · Lesson 2: A Nice Little Grey Cat

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Opposites. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 25: Which one is different?

Fun for Movers · Week 25: Which one is different? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Things that go together Lesson 2 · A nice little grey cat Lesson 3 · Jack's new pet

You Need

  • Some toys or soft animals of different sizes and colours.
  • A word table on paper with four columns: nice · beautiful · funny · ugly | big · little | black · brown · grey · white · orange | cat · dog · rabbit · horse · parrot.
  • Describing words usually go: what it's like → size → colour → thing: a nice little grey cat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Big, Big, Big – Adjectives Song.
  3. Describe it! (6 min): hold up a toy; your child describes it with three words: "a funny little brown teddy". Sentences
  4. Word table (7 min): your child takes one word from each column and makes four sentences: "Grandpa has got a funny little white rabbit." "I'd like a beautiful big black horse."
  5. Watch (3 min): Order of Adjectives Song. Sing the examples.
  6. Guess my animal (4 min): "I'm thinking of a big grey animal with a very long nose." "An elephant!" Then your child gives clues with "really" or "very".
  7. Draw and write (3 min): your child draws one animal from the word table and writes its sentence under it.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's a nice little grey cat." … "I'd like a beautiful big black horse." … "It's really clever!"
