Week 25 · Lesson 3: Jack's New Pet

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Opposites and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 25: Which one is different?

You Need

  • The story and the word box below, written large. Three words in the box are extra.

Word box: loved · buy · bowl · fish · garden · cloudy · head · downstairs

Jack (1) ____ birds. He wanted a parrot because parrots can talk. "Can we (2) ____ a parrot, Dad?" he asked. "Sorry, Jack," Dad said. "Parrots are very loud, and our flat is small." Jack was sad. Then Grandma came to see them. She had a big glass (3) ____, and in it there was a beautiful orange (4) ____. "This is for you!" she said. "It's quiet and it doesn't need a (5) ____!" Jack laughed. "Thank you, Grandma! It's really sweet. I'm going to call him Goldie!"

Choose the best name: A Jack gets a pet fish · B Dad buys a parrot · C Grandma's garden

Answers: loved · buy · bowl · fish · garden. Best name: A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): Adjectives – Animals. Describe each animal with two words.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box in each gap. Three words are extra.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  5. Retell (5 min): your child tells the story again with "First… Then… At the end…"
  6. My dream pet (4 min): ask: "What pet would you like?" Your child answers with three describing words: "I'd like a clever little black dog."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Jack loved birds." … "Grandma had a big glass bowl." … "I'd like a clever little black dog."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Put things in groups and say which one is different and why.
  • Describe things with two or three words in the right order – "a nice little grey cat" – and use "really" and "very".
  • Read a short story, write the missing words and choose the best name.
