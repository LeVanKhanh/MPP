Week 26 · Lesson 1: Who Lives Above Me?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 26: Guess who lives here?

Fun for Movers · Week 26: Guess who lives here? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Who lives above me? Lesson 2 · Five little bats Lesson 3 · Guess who lives here!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a building with three floors and the stairs in the centre. Each floor has two flats, one on each side of the stairs. Doors: floor 3 (top) red and blue · floor 2 green and yellow · floor 1 (bottom) brown and white.
  • Six name cards: Mr Bear, Miss Parrot, Mrs Duck, the Frogs, Grandpa Mouse, the Rabbits. The six clues below.
  • Flats on the same floor are opposite each other. Count the floors from the bottom: 1, 2, 3. (In British English the bottom floor is often the "ground floor"; at Movers "first, second, third" is enough.)

Who lives here? 1 Miss Parrot lives at the top, behind the red door. · 2 Mr Bear lives opposite Miss Parrot. · 3 Mrs Duck lives below Mr Bear. · 4 The Frogs live opposite Mrs Duck. They play loud music! · 5 Grandpa Mouse lives below the Frogs. He doesn't like the noise. · 6 The Rabbits live at the bottom, behind the white door.

Answers: Floor 3: red – Miss Parrot · blue – Mr Bear. Floor 2: green – the Frogs · yellow – Mrs Duck. Floor 1: brown – Grandpa Mouse · white – the Rabbits.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Prepositions Song. Show each word with your hands.
  3. Above or below? (4 min): hold a toy above, below or near your hand; your child says: "It's above your hand!" Then your child moves the toy and you say. Who lives where?
  4. Who lives here? (8 min): read each clue together. Your child puts the name card on the right door, then writes the names in the building.
  5. Ask and answer (5 min): "Who lives above Grandpa Mouse?" "The Frogs." "Who lives opposite Mrs Duck?" "The Frogs!" Then your child asks you. Sentences
  6. Watch (2 min): Ordinal Numbers. Then ask: "Which floor do the Frogs live on?" "The second floor."
  7. Talk (3 min): ask: "Do you live in a house or a flat? Which floor is your bedroom on? Who lives near you?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who lives above Grandpa Mouse?" … "The Frogs live above him." … "We live on the third floor."
