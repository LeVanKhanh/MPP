Week 26 · Lesson 2: Five Little Bats

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: At Home and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 26: Guess who lives here?

Fun for Movers · Week 26: Guess who lives here? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Who lives above me? Lesson 2 · Five little bats Lesson 3 · Guess who lives here!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a living room: stairs on one side with a cat asleep at the bottom; a big clock on the wall opposite the door; a spider above the window; a girl reading a book on the sofa; a boy playing the guitar next to a lamp; a bowl of apples on the table.
  • Read the bat script below twice. Your child draws each bat in the picture and writes its name.
  • In the "look, read and write" part, answers can be one, two, three, four or five words.

Name the bats: Billy is on top of the clock. · Betty is under the table. · Ben is sleeping on the lamp. · Bella is at the top of the stairs. · Bob is flying above the girl's head.

Example: The cat is at the bottom of the ____. (stairs) · Finish the sentences: 1 There's a spider above the ____. · 2 The big clock is opposite the ____. · Answer the questions: 3 What is the girl doing? · 4 Where is the bowl of apples? · Now write two sentences about the picture: 5 ____ · 6 ____

Answers: Bats: check each one is in the right place. 1 window · 2 door · 3 (She's) reading a book · 4 on the table · 5–6 any true sentences, for example "The boy is playing the guitar." "Bella is at the top of the stairs."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Where is it? (3 min): point to things in your room; your child says where they are: "The lamp is near the window." "The clock is above the door."
  3. Watch (4 min): Upstairs, Downstairs. Say "upstairs!" or "downstairs!" each time.
  4. Name the bats (6 min): read the bat script twice. Your child draws each bat in the right place and writes its name next to it.
  5. Look, read and write (8 min): your child finishes sentences 1–2, answers questions 3–4, then writes two sentences of their own about the picture.
  6. What can I see? (4 min): "I can see something above the window. What is it?" "The spider!" Then your child gives you one from the picture or from your room.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Bella is at the top of the stairs." … "There's a spider above the window." … "The girl is reading a book."
