Week 26 · Lesson 3: Guess Who Lives Here!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: At Home and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 26: Guess who lives here?

Fun for Movers · Week 26: Guess who lives here? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Who lives above me? Lesson 2 · Five little bats Lesson 3 · Guess who lives here!

You Need

  • The animal riddles and the four rooms below.
  • Paper and colour pencils for the alphabet game and the dream building.

Animal homes: 1 I live on a farm and I give you milk. · 2 I live in the sea. I'm very big and I can swim. · 3 I sleep in the day and fly at night. Sometimes I sleep in old houses! · 4 I live in a tree. I can climb and I've got a long tail.

Who lives here? A There are lots of plates and a big table. Someone cooks dinner here every day. · B There's a guitar and lots of loud music. Someone wants to be a star! · C There's a ball, a football shirt and lots of photos of football players. · D There are lots of books and two armchairs. It's very quiet.

Answers: Animals: a cow · a whale · a bat · a monkey. Rooms: A a cook · B a pop star · C a football player · D someone who loves reading.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Who Lives Here? Guess each animal before the answer.
  3. Animal homes (4 min): read each riddle; your child guesses: "A bat lives there!" Then your child makes one riddle for you.
  4. Who lives here? (6 min): read each room; your child guesses who lives there and says why: "A pop star, because there's a guitar!"
  5. Alphabet find and draw (6 min): choose six letters, for example b, c, d, l, s, t. Your child finds something at home for each letter, draws it and writes the word: "b – bed".
  6. From top to bottom (4 min): your child draws a dream building with three floors and describes it from top to bottom: "At the top there's my bedroom. Below it there's a big kitchen…"
  7. Talk (3 min): ask: "Who would you like to live opposite? Who would you like to live above you? Why?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who lives here?" … "A pop star, because there's a guitar!" … "At the top there's my bedroom."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say where people live in a building: above, below, opposite, at the top / bottom of the stairs, on the second floor.
  • Listen and put names in a picture, then look, read and write: finish sentences, answer questions and write two sentences.
  • Guess who lives somewhere from what is in it, and describe a building from top to bottom.
