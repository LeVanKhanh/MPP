Week 27 · Lesson 1: You Can Wear These

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Actions and Plurals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 27: Seeing differences

Fun for Movers · Week 27: Seeing differences | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · You can wear these Lesson 2 · One mouse, two mice Lesson 3 · My silly day

You Need

  • Eight small pictures (draw them): a kite, a horse, a tree, shoes, a cake, ducks, a boat, flowers.
  • The table below on paper, with three columns to join with lines.
  • Before the lesson, make a 7 × 7 word search with: boots, moon, phone, bed, snow, giraffe.

Make sentences: You can … fly · ride · climb · wear · eat · feed · sail on · grow … this / these … at the beach · on a farm · in the park · on your feet · at a party · by the river · on the sea · in the garden.

What is it? 1 You wear these on your feet when it rains. · 2 You can see this in the sky at night. · 3 You can call people and send texts on this. · 4 You sleep in this. · 5 This is white and cold. You can play in it in winter. · 6 This animal has got a very long neck.

Answers: Sentences, for example: You can fly this at the beach (kite) · ride this on a farm (horse) · climb this in the park (tree) · wear these on your feet (shoes) · eat this at a party (cake) · feed these by the river (ducks) · sail on this on the sea (boat) · grow these in the garden (flowers). What is it? boots · the moon · a phone · a bed · snow · a giraffe.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Singular and Plural Song. Show one finger, then lots of fingers.
  3. This or these? (4 min): point to things around the room; your child says "this lamp" or "these books", "this chair" or "these shoes". This / these
  4. Make sentences (8 min): your child takes a picture, joins the right words in the table with lines, then says and writes the sentence: "You can wear these on your feet."
  5. What is it? (6 min): read each sentence; your child says the word, finds it in the word search and writes it.
  6. Your turn (3 min): your child makes a "You can … this …" riddle for you: "You can ride this to school." "A bike!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You can wear these on your feet." … "You can fly this at the beach." … "What is it? – A kite!"
