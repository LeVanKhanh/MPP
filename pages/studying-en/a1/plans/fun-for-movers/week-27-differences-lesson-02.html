Week 27 · Lesson 2: One Mouse, Two Mice

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Plurals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 27: Seeing differences

Fun for Movers · Week 27: Seeing differences | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · You can wear these Lesson 2 · One mouse, two mice Lesson 3 · My silly day

You Need

  • Sixteen word cards: cat, toy, box, bus, watch, dress, baby, family, man, woman, child, foot, tooth, mouse, sheep, leaf.
  • Four labels: -s · -es · -ies · special.
  • The five "which one is different?" sets below.
  • After a, e, o, u, y just takes -s: toy – toys, boy – boys. After a consonant, y becomes -ies: baby – babies.

Which one is different? 1 mice, feet, teeth, cats · 2 boxes, buses, watches, toys · 3 babies, families, stories, boys · 4 shorts, jeans, glasses, hat · 5 leaves, trees, flowers, ducks

Answers: Sorting: -s cat, toy · -es box, bus, watch, dress · -ies baby, family · special man, woman, child, foot, tooth, mouse, sheep, leaf. Different: cats (just add -s) · toys (no -es) · boys (no -ies) · hat (it's one thing; the others are always plural) · ducks (they're animals, not plants).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Plurals -s -es. Clap once for "cats", twice for "box-es".
  3. Sort the cards (6 min): your child reads a card, says the plural and puts it under -s, -es, -ies or special: "One box, two boxes!" Plural words
  4. Watch (4 min): Irregular Plural Nouns. Say the special plural first.
  5. Which one is different? (6 min): read each set; your child says which one and why: "Cats, because you just add -s!"
  6. Plural quiz (5 min): say "A mouse!"; your child says "Two mice!" Then swap. Special plurals get two points. Plural quiz
  7. Count at home (2 min): "How many feet are in this room? How many chairs?" Your child counts and answers: "There are six feet!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "One mouse, two mice!" … "One box, three boxes." … "Cats is different because you just add -s."
