Week 27 · Lesson 3: My Silly Day

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Plurals and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 27: Seeing differences

Fun for Movers · Week 27: Seeing differences | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · You can wear these Lesson 2 · One mouse, two mice Lesson 3 · My silly day

You Need

  • First ask the ten questions below and write your child's answers on a paper. Don't show the story yet!
  • Then read the story, putting each answer in the gap with the same number. For gap 1, your child says and writes the plural.
  • Paper and pencils for two drawings.

Questions: 1 Name an animal. · 2 Say a number from 2 to 20. · 3 Name a food. · 4 Name a person in your family. · 5 Say a colour. · 6 Name something you wear. · 7 Name a part of your body. · 8 Name a sport or game. · 9 Name a place in a town. · 10 Name another animal.

My silly day: Yesterday I went to the (9) ____ with my (4) ____. We saw (2) ____ (1, many) ____! They were (5) ____ and they were eating (3) ____. Then a (10) ____ took my (6) ____ and ran away! We played (8) ____ with the (1, many) ____ all afternoon. I laughed so much that my (7) ____ hurt! At night I had a dream about them. What a silly day!

Answers: Any answers. Check the plural in gap 1 (mice, sheep, fish, not "mouses", "sheeps") and the right form in gap 7: "my foot hurt" or "my feet hurt".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Answer the questions (4 min): ask the ten questions and write your child's answers. Don't say why!
  3. The silly story (5 min): read the story with your child's answers. Your child writes the plural for gap 1, then reads the whole story aloud.
  4. Draw it (5 min): your child draws the silliest part of the story and tells you about it: "Five pink mice are eating cake!"
  5. Watch (3 min): Find The Differences. Say each difference before the answer.
  6. Draw and compare (6 min): you and your child each draw a park with trees, birds and children, without looking. Then compare: "In my picture there are three birds, but in your picture there are two."
  7. Talk (2 min): ask: "Tell me about a funny dream. What did you see?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "We saw ten pink mice!" … "My feet hurt!" … "In my picture there are three birds, but in your picture there are two."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Make sentences with "You can … this / these …" and find a word from what it means.
  • Say plurals: -s, -es, -ies and special ones like mice, feet, teeth, children, sheep.
  • Answer questions, use the answers in a silly story, and say differences between two pictures.
