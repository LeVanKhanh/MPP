Week 28 · Lesson 1: Mia's Busy Week

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 28: Our busy holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 28: Our busy holidays | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Mia's busy week Lesson 2 · Who, what, when, where? Lesson 3 · Lily's holidays

You Need

  • Seven day cards (Monday to Sunday) in a row on the table.
  • Five small pictures (draw them): swimming, painting a picture, tennis, fishing, making a cake. Your child draws a line from each picture to a day. Two days have no picture.
  • Read Mia's script below twice.

Mia's script: Hi! I'm Mia. In the holidays I'm very busy! On Monday I always go swimming with my mum. On Wednesday I often paint pictures with Grandma. On Thursday I play tennis with my cousin Tom, but sometimes it rains. On Saturday I go fishing with my uncle. We never catch big fish! And on Sunday I always make a cake with my aunt.

Complete the sentences: 1 On ____, Mia goes swimming with her ____. · 2 Mia often paints pictures with ____ on Wednesday. · 3 On Thursday she plays tennis with her ____ Tom. · 4 Mia and her uncle never catch ____ fish. · 5 On Sunday Mia always ____ with her aunt.

Answers: Lines: swimming – Monday · painting – Wednesday · tennis – Thursday · fishing – Saturday · cake – Sunday (Tuesday and Friday have no picture). Sentences: 1 Monday, mum · 2 Grandma · 3 cousin · 4 big · 5 makes a cake.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Days Of The Week Song. Point to each day card as you sing.
  3. Days in order (4 min): mix the day cards; your child puts them in order. Ask: "What comes after Tuesday? What day is it today?" Days of the week
  4. Listen and draw lines (6 min): read Mia's script twice. Your child draws a line from each picture to the right day.
  5. Complete the sentences (5 min): your child writes one or two words in each sentence, then reads it aloud.
  6. Watch (2 min): Adverbs of Frequency Song. Show the fingers: always 4, often 3, sometimes 2, never 0.
  7. How often? (6 min): say a thing: "eat ice cream", "go to bed at ten o'clock". Your child shows fingers and says: "I sometimes eat ice cream." Then your child asks you. Sentences
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "On Monday Mia always goes swimming." … "What comes after Tuesday?" … "I never go to bed at ten o'clock!"
