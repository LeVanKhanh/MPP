Week 28 · Lesson 2: Who, What, When, Where?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 28: Our busy holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 28: Our busy holidays | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Mia's busy week Lesson 2 · Who, what, when, where? Lesson 3 · Lily's holidays

You Need

  • The holiday words below on paper, and the word box.
  • Sixteen small cards for the game: four "who" (my dad, a monkey, Grandma, my teacher), four "what" (is dancing, is eating a cake, is flying a kite, is singing a song), four "when" (on Monday, on Saturday morning, at night, at seven o'clock), four "where" (at the zoo, in the kitchen, on a boat, at the beach).

Holiday words: go ____ · ride a ____ · fly a ____ · take a ____ · watch a ____ · drink a ____ · read a ____ · play ____

Word box: swimming · bike · kite · photo · film · milkshake · comic · tennis

Holiday questions: 1 Where do you go in the school holidays? (the park · the beach · Grandma's house) · 2 What do you wear? (shorts and a T-shirt · a dress · a coat and a scarf) · 3 Who do you see? (my family · my friends · my cousins)

Answers: go swimming · ride a bike · fly a kite · take a photo · watch a film · drink a milkshake · read a comic · play tennis. Holiday questions: any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Holiday Time! Say which things you like doing.
  3. Holiday words (6 min): your child writes a word from the box after each action, then acts one out for you to guess: "You're flying a kite!"
  4. Ask four questions (5 min): your child chooses four holiday words and asks you: "Do you often go swimming?" "How often do you read a comic?" Then you ask your child.
  5. Holiday questions (4 min): ask the three questions and give the three choices. Your child chooses and says a full answer: "I go to the beach. I wear shorts and a T-shirt." In the holidays…
  6. Who, what, when, where? (7 min): put the four piles face down. Take one card from each pile and read the silly sentence: "Grandma is flying a kite at night at the zoo!" Your child draws the funniest one.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Do you often go swimming?" … "I sometimes ride my bike." … "My teacher is dancing on a boat on Monday!"
