Week 28 · Lesson 3: Lily's Holidays

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 28: Our busy holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 28: Our busy holidays | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Mia's busy week Lesson 2 · Who, what, when, where? Lesson 3 · Lily's holidays

You Need

  • The story and the word box below, written large. Three words in the box are extra.
  • Paper for your child's holiday sentences.

Word box: animals (example) · rabbits · picnic · swim · mountain · frightened · shopping · tennis · pretty

Hi! My name's Lily and I'm nine. In the school holidays we always go to my grandparents' farm in the countryside. Every morning I help Grandpa feed the animals. I love the (1) ____ best because they're small and white. In the afternoon we often have a (2) ____ by the lake. My little brother Sam always wants to (3) ____ in the lake, but Mum says it's too cold! Sometimes we climb the (4) ____ behind the farm. From the top you can see the village and the river. At night it's very quiet. Sam is (5) ____ at night, so he sleeps with a lamp! I love my holidays on the farm.

Choose the best name: A Lily's holidays on the farm · B Sam's new lamp · C A day at the beach

Answers: 1 rabbits · 2 picnic · 3 swim · 4 mountain · 5 frightened. Extra words: shopping, tennis, pretty. Best name: A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look at the words (3 min): read the word box together. Your child says which words are animals, places, actions and feelings.
  3. Read and choose (8 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box in each gap. Three words are extra.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  5. Watch (3 min): How Often Do You Do the Laundry? Answer each "How often…?" for yourself.
  6. My holidays (6 min): your child writes three sentences about their own holidays with always, often, sometimes or never: "In the holidays I often go to the beach."
  7. Talk (3 min): ask: "What do you like doing in the school holidays? What do you never do?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "In the school holidays we always go to the farm." … "Sam is frightened at night." … "I often go to the beach."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say the days of the week and what someone does on each day, with always, often, sometimes and never.
  • Put words together – go swimming, fly a kite, drink a milkshake – and ask and answer about the holidays.
  • Read a story, choose words from a box, choose the best name and write about your own holidays.
