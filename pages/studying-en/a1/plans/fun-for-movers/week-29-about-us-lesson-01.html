Week 29 · Lesson 1: How Do You Do It?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Actions. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 29: About us

Fun for Movers · Week 29: About us | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

You Need

  • The six sentences and the yes/no list below.
  • Action cards for the mime game: walk, eat, sing, clap, dance, carry a cup, talk. Adverb cards: quickly, slowly, loudly, quietly, carefully.
  • Most -ly words come from a describing word: quick – quickly, loud – loudly, careful – carefully. But good – well.

Complete the sentences: 1 The old man is driving very ____. · 2 The band is playing very ____. · 3 Shh! The baby's asleep. Please talk ____. · 4 Lucy is carrying a big cake ____. · 5 Ben's bus is coming! He's running ____. · 6 Tom can't sing. He sings very ____.

What do you think? Write yes or no: 1 I eat my dinner quickly. · 2 My mum sings well. · 3 I always walk quietly at home. · 4 Our cat runs quickly. · 5 I write carefully at school. · 6 My dad dances badly.

Answers: 1 slowly · 2 loudly · 3 quietly · 4 carefully · 5 quickly · 6 badly. Yes or no: any true answers – count the yes and no answers together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Quiet Loud Quiet Song. Sing quietly, then loudly.
  3. Do it like this! (5 min): take one action card and one adverb card: "Walk slowly!" Your child does it. Then your child gives you one. How do you do it?
  4. Complete the sentences (6 min): your child writes the right -ly word in each sentence and reads it aloud in that way: loudly, quietly, slowly…
  5. Yes or no? (5 min): your child reads each sentence and writes yes or no, then counts the yes and no answers. You do it too and compare.
  6. Watch (2 min): Fast and Slow Actions Song. Move with it and say "quickly!" or "slowly!"
  7. Talk (4 min): ask: "Who in your family talks loudly? Who drives slowly? What do you do well?" Sentences
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "That man sings very loudly." … "Please talk quietly." … "I eat my dinner slowly."
