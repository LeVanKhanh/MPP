Week 29 · Lesson 2: Picnic by the River

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Actions and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 29: About us

Fun for Movers · Week 29: About us | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · How do you do it? Lesson 2 · Picnic by the river Lesson 3 · Tom's day at the beach

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a picnic by a river with eight people: a man flying a kite · a girl taking photos with a camera · a boy playing the guitar · a girl climbing a big rock · a man fishing · a girl feeding ducks · a woman reading a comic · a boy eating a cake. Add a dog, ducks on the river, and a picnic with plates and cups.
  • Write six names above and below the picture: Peter, Anna, Jim, Kim, Paul, Lucy. Read the script below twice; your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  • Six paper slips for the drawing game, each with a silly sentence: A dog is singing loudly. · A cat is eating quickly. · Grandma is dancing well. · A monkey is drawing carefully. · A horse is walking quietly. · Dad is swimming badly.

Script: Look at the picnic! Peter is the man who's flying a kite. He's running quickly! · Anna is taking photos. She's got a camera. · Can you hear the guitar? That's Jim. He plays very badly! · Kim is climbing the big rock. She's doing it carefully. · Paul is the man who's fishing. He's sitting very quietly. · And Lucy? She's feeding the ducks.

Answers: Peter – kite · Anna – camera · Jim – guitar · Kim – rock · Paul – fishing · Lucy – ducks. The woman with the comic and the boy with the cake have no name. c, d, p words: camera, cake, comic, cup · dog, ducks · picnic, photos, plates.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): How Do You Do Things? Act each one.
  3. Picture words (5 min): your child finds things in the picture that start with c, d and p and writes them: camera, dog, picnic…
  4. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  5. Who is it? (4 min): your child describes one person with an -ly word: "She's climbing carefully." You say: "It's Kim!" Then swap.
  6. Draw the sentences (6 min): take turns: pick a paper slip and draw the sentence without words. The other person guesses: "A dog is singing loudly!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Peter is the man who's flying a kite." … "Kim is climbing carefully." … "A dog is singing loudly!"
