Week 29 · Lesson 3: Tom's Day at the Beach

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Actions and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 29: About us

Fun for Movers · Week 29: About us | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · How do you do it? Lesson 2 · Picnic by the river Lesson 3 · Tom's day at the beach

You Need

  • The story below, written large. Your child circles one word in each pair.
  • A table on paper for "Ask and answer" with three columns: Me · Mum · Dad (or another person at home).

Tom's day at the beach: We went to the beach on Saturday. I ran to the sea quickly / slowly because I was very hot. The water was cold, but I (1) was / wasn't frightened. I swam (2) well / badly and Dad said, "Great!" Then I was hungry, so we (3) had / didn't have a picnic on the sand. I (4) ate / didn't eat my sandwich because a big bird came and took it! I (5) laughed / shouted at the bird very loudly, but it flew away. After lunch, my little sister was asleep, so we talked very (6) quietly / loudly. In the afternoon I found a (7) huge / thirsty shell. I carried it home (8) carefully / quickly. It was a great day!

Ask and answer: 1 Can you hop quickly? · 2 Are you good at dancing? · 3 Can you draw a cat well? · 4 Do you walk to school quickly or slowly? · 5 Can you sing quietly? · 6 Are you good at swimming?

Answers: Example: quickly. 1 wasn't · 2 well · 3 had · 4 didn't eat · 5 shouted · 6 quietly · 7 huge · 8 carefully. Ask and answer: any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Adverbs 2 Song. Sing the chorus.
  3. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child circles the right word in each pair and says why: "wasn't, because Tom swam."
  4. Read it again (3 min): your child reads the story aloud: the loud parts loudly, the quiet parts quietly.
  5. Ask and answer (7 min): your child asks you and another person the six questions and writes short answers: "Yes, I can." "No, I'm not." Then answers for "Me".
  6. Talk (4 min): ask: "Tell me about a great day. Where did you go? What did you do well?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I swam well and Dad said, 'Great!'" … "I didn't eat my sandwich." … "Can you hop quickly? – Yes, I can!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say how people do things: quickly, slowly, loudly, quietly, well, badly, carefully.
  • Find words in a picture by their first letter, and listen and draw lines to the right people.
  • Read a story and choose the right words (was / wasn't, ate / didn't eat), then ask and answer "Can you…? Are you good at…?"
