Week 30 · Lesson 1: All About Me

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Questions. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 30: About me

Fun for Movers · Week 30: About me | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · All about me Lesson 2 · Ask your friend Lesson 3 · The same word

You Need

  • The eight questions below on paper with a box next to each, and room to write Lily's answers.
  • Question cards (who, what, where, when, why, how many, how often) and answer cards (Lucy · a banana · at home · on Saturday · because it's funny · three · every day).
  • Read the interview twice. Your child ticks the questions Mr Green asks, then writes Lily's answers.

Questions: 1 How old are you? · 2 Where do you live? · 3 How many brothers and sisters have you got? · 4 What's your favourite colour? · 5 How often do you see your grandparents? · 6 How do you come to school? · 7 Can you swim? · 8 What's your favourite film?

Interview: Mr Green: Hello, Lily. How old are you? – Lily: I'm nine. · Mr Green: How many brothers and sisters have you got? – Lily: One brother. His name's Sam. · Mr Green: How often do you see your grandparents? – Lily: Every Sunday. We have lunch at their house. · Mr Green: How do you come to school? – Lily: I walk with my mum. · Mr Green: And what's your favourite film? – Lily: The Clever Dog. It's really funny!

Answers: Ticked: 1, 3, 5, 6, 8. Lily's answers: nine · one brother (Sam) · every Sunday · she walks with her mum · The Clever Dog.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): The Five W's Song. Point up for each question word.
  3. Question words (4 min): your child matches each question card to an answer card: "Who? – Lucy." "When? – On Saturday." Question words
  4. Listen and tick (7 min): read the interview twice. Your child ticks the questions Mr Green asks, then writes Lily's answers.
  5. Your answers (6 min): ask your child all eight questions. Your child answers aloud, then writes four answers. Sentences
  6. Watch (2 min): WH Questions Song for Kids. Say the answers before the song.
  7. Make a question (4 min): you say an answer; your child makes the question: "Every day." – "How often do you eat bread?" "By car." – "How do you go to Grandma's?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How often do you see your grandparents?" … "Every Sunday." … "Why do you like it? – Because it's really funny!"
