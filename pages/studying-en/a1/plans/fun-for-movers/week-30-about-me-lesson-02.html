Week 30 · Lesson 2: Ask Your Friend

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions, Colours and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 30: About me

Fun for Movers · Week 30: About me | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · All about me Lesson 2 · Ask your friend Lesson 3 · The same word

You Need

  • An interview table on paper with three columns: question · Mum or Dad · a friend or a brother or sister. Use the questions below.
  • Before the lesson, draw a big circle with three parts. In one part, write eight colour words in the wrong colours (write "red" with a green pen, "blue" with an orange pen…). Leave the other two parts empty.

Interview – school: 1 Who do you sit next to? · 2 Where do you do your homework? · 3 Which is your favourite day? Things you like: 4 What's your favourite film? When did you see it? Who did you see it with? Why do you like it? · 5 Can you play the piano or the guitar? · 6 Are you good at singing? · 7 Can you swim?

Look, read and do: 1 How many colour words are there? Say the colour of each word, not the word! · 2 In the top part, write two sports you like playing and one you like watching. · 3 In the bottom part, write two foods you love and draw a star next to them. Then write the juice you like and draw a smiley ☺. Last, write one food you don't like and draw a sad face ☹.

Answers: Interview and look, read and do: any true answers. Check the colour game by pointing: your child says the ink colour, not the word.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): 10 Common English Questions for Kids. Pause after each question and answer it.
  3. Interview (8 min): your child asks you the questions and writes short answers in the table. Then your child asks a friend, a brother or a sister (or a toy, and answers for it).
  4. Colour trick (4 min): point to each colour word; your child says the colour of the pen, not the word: the word says "red", but your child says "green!"
  5. Look, read and do (6 min): your child reads instructions 2 and 3 and does them in the circle.
  6. Tell me (2 min): your child tells you one answer from the interview: "My dad's favourite film is… He likes it because…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who do you sit next to?" … "Why do you like it? – Because it's exciting!" … "I love noodles. I don't like eggs."
