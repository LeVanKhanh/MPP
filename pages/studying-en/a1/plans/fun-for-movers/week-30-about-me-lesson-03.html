Week 30 · Lesson 3: The Same Word

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 30: About me

Fun for Movers · Week 30: About me | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · All about me Lesson 2 · Ask your friend Lesson 3 · The same word

You Need

  • The text below with an empty box in each gap. The same word goes in every box.
  • A toy microphone (or a spoon) for the speaking practice.
  • The book's game uses "on my right / on my left". Left and right (for directions) are Flyers words, so the game here uses next to, in front of and behind.

What is this about? Everyone needs ____ every day. You can drink it, and you can swim in it. Plants and animals need ____ too. When you play sport, drink lots of ____. It helps your body and your head! Doctors say: drink six glasses of ____ every day.

Answers: water.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. The same word (7 min): read the text together. Your child thinks about the clues and writes the same word in every box, then reads the text again.
  3. Your secret word (6 min): your child chooses a secret word (music, ice cream, my dog…) and writes three sentences with a box for it: "You can listen to ____." You guess. Then swap.
  4. Watch (2 min): WH Questions Song for Kids, the last time this week.
  5. Next to me (5 min): put three toys and two people around the table. Ask: "Who's next to you? What's in front of you? What's behind Teddy?" Your child answers, then asks you.
  6. Speaking practice (5 min): you are the teacher with the microphone. Ask six questions from Questions. Your child answers with a little more: "By bus. My mum comes with me."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Everyone needs water." … "Who's next to you?" … "I come to school by bus. My mum comes with me."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen to an interview, tick the questions you hear and write the answers.
  • Ask and answer questions about yourself, your school and the things you like: who, where, when, why, how often.
  • Find the same missing word in a short text, and answer questions like in the Movers speaking test.
