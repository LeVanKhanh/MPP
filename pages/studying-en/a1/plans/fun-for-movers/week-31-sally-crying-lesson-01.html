Week 31 · Lesson 1: At the Swimming Pool

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Feelings. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 31: Why is Sally crying?

Fun for Movers · Week 31: Why is Sally crying? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · At the swimming pool Lesson 2 · Pat needs a bath Lesson 3 · Naughty Max

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a swimming pool with six people: a boy crying next to an ice cream on the ground · a girl laughing at a funny dog · a man sleeping on a chair · a woman with a frog on her towel, looking surprised · a boy drinking water · a girl sitting next to her mum, afraid of the water.
  • Write six names around the picture: Tom, Kate, Mr Brown, Anna, Dan, Lily. Read the script twice; your child draws lines from the names to the people.
  • Then the look, read and write sentences below. Answers can be one to five words.

Script: Look at the swimming pool! Tom is the boy who's crying. He dropped his ice cream! · Kate is laughing. That dog is very funny. · The man who's sleeping is Mr Brown. He's really tired. · Anna is surprised. There's a frog on her towel! · Dan is drinking water because he's thirsty. · And Lily? She's afraid of the water. She's sitting next to her mum.

Example: The boy is crying because he dropped his ____. (ice cream) · Complete the sentences: 1 The girl is laughing at a ____. · 2 The man is sleeping because he's ____. · Answer the questions: 3 Why is the woman surprised? · 4 What is the boy drinking? · Now write two sentences about the picture: 5 ____ · 6 ____

Answers: Lines: Tom – crying boy · Kate – laughing girl · Mr Brown – sleeping man · Anna – woman with the frog · Dan – boy drinking · Lily – girl next to her mum. 1 funny dog · 2 (really) tired · 3 (Because) there's a frog on her towel · 4 water · 5–6 any true sentences, for example "Lily is afraid of the water."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Feelings and Emotion Vocabulary Chant. Make each face.
  3. Face game (4 min): say a feeling: "tired!"; your child makes the face and says: "I'm tired." Then your child says one for you. Feelings
  4. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  5. Look and read and write (8 min): your child finishes sentences 1–2, answers questions 3–4, then writes two sentences of their own.
  6. Why? (4 min): point to each person and ask: "Why is Tom crying?" Your child answers: "Because he dropped his ice cream." Why…? Because…
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Why is Tom crying?" … "Because he dropped his ice cream." … "Lily is afraid of the water."
