Week 31 · Lesson 2: Pat Needs a Bath

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Feelings and At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 31: Why is Sally crying?

Fun for Movers · Week 31: Why is Sally crying? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · At the swimming pool Lesson 2 · Pat needs a bath Lesson 3 · Naughty Max

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 a very dirty dog in a garden and a girl looking angry · 2 a girl and her dad washing the dog in the bath · 3 the dog running out of the bathroom and Dad all wet · 4 a clean dog and everyone laughing.
  • The story and the word box below. Three words in the box are extra.
  • Dad's four sentences (a–d) on small papers.

Word box: dirty · bath · wash · wet · laughing · happy · hungry · socks · kitchen

Kim has got a dog called Pat. One day Pat played in the garden after the rain and now he's very (1) ____. "Pat needs a (2) ____!" says Dad. Kim and Dad put Pat in the bath and (3) ____ him. But then Pat jumps out of the bath and runs into the living room. Now Dad is (4) ____ too! Kim can't stop (5) ____. At the end, Pat is clean and Kim is very (6) ____.

Dad says… a Look! Pat is clean now. Good dog! · b Oh no! Pat is really dirty. He needs a bath! · c Come back, Pat! Now I'm wet! · d Hold him, Kim. I'm washing his ears.

Answers: 1 dirty · 2 bath · 3 wash · 4 wet · 5 laughing · 6 happy. Extra words: hungry, socks, kitchen. Dad says: picture 1 – b · 2 – d · 3 – c · 4 – a.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Feelings and Emotions Song. Sing and act each feeling.
  3. Read and write (7 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box in each gap. Three words are extra.
  4. Dad says… (4 min): your child reads Dad's sentences and puts each one under the right picture.
  5. Tell the story (5 min): your child tells the story with the four pictures: "First Pat is very dirty. Then…" Ask: "Why is Kim laughing?"
  6. Act it out (5 min): act the story with a toy dog. One person is Kim, one is Dad. Use the faces: angry, surprised, happy.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Pat needs a bath!" … "Now Dad is wet too!" … "Kim is happy because Pat is clean."
