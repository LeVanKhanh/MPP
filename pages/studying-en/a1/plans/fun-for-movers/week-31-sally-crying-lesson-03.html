Week 31 · Lesson 3: Naughty Max

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Feelings and Family. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 31: Why is Sally crying?

Fun for Movers · Week 31: Why is Sally crying? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · At the swimming pool Lesson 2 · Pat needs a bath Lesson 3 · Naughty Max

You Need

  • The questions and answers below on paper, in two columns. Your child draws lines to join them.
  • Before the lesson, draw Max's bedroom: a bed, a cat under the bed, a ball, a T-shirt on a chair, a door. Your child colours and writes as you say.
  • Twelve cards for the memory game: six faces (happy, sad, angry, tired, frightened, surprised) and the six words.

Questions: 1 What did Max do this afternoon? · 2 Who did he go with? · 3 When did he come home? · 4 Where is he now? · 5 Why is he having a hot bath? · 6 How did he get wet? · 7 He's a naughty son sometimes!

Answers: A He fell off a tree into the river! · B I know! · C He climbed a tree in the park. · D At six o'clock. · E Because he's wet and cold. · F His cousin Sam. · G He's in the bathroom.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the cat under the bed grey. · Colour the T-shirt on the chair green. · Colour the ball red and blue. · Write MAX on the door.

Answers: 1 C · 2 F · 3 D · 4 G · 5 E · 6 A · 7 B. Colour: grey cat, green T-shirt, red and blue ball, MAX on the door.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Why Are You Crying? Say "Because…" for each child.
  3. Read and draw lines (7 min): your child reads each question and draws a line to the right answer, then reads the pairs aloud.
  4. Ask about my day (4 min): your child asks you about yesterday: "What did you do? Who did you go with? When did you come home?"
  5. Listen and colour and write (6 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or writes in Max's bedroom.
  6. Match the cards (6 min): put the twelve cards face down. Turn over two; if a face and a word match, say a sentence: "He's tired because he played all day!" and keep them.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Why is he having a hot bath?" … "Because he's wet and cold." … "He's a naughty son sometimes! – I know!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say how people are – happy, sad, tired, thirsty, frightened, surprised – and why, with "because".
  • Look, read and write about a picture, listen and draw lines to names, and tell a picture story.
  • Match questions about the past (what, who, when, where, why, how) to their answers.
