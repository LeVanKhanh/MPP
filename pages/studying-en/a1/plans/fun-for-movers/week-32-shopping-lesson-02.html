Week 32 · Lesson 2: Tom Goes to the Market

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Shopping. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 32: Mary goes shopping

Fun for Movers · Week 32: Mary goes shopping | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's in the kitchen? Lesson 2 · Tom goes to the market Lesson 3 · Where does milk come from?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 a boy buying eggs and cheese at a market; a man giving him a bag of bananas · 2 the boy playing football in the park; his bag under a tree · 3 the boy at home with nothing in his hands; his dad looking angry · 4 a girl at the door with the bag.
  • The sentences and the word box below. Two words in the box are extra.
  • Some food and a bag for "Play shops".

Word box: cheese · bananas · football · tree · angry · bag · kitchen · asleep

Picture 1: Tom is at the market. He's buying some eggs and (1) ____ for his dad. A man is giving him a bag of (2) ____. · Picture 2: Tom is in the park. He's playing (3) ____ with his friends. His bag is under a (4) ____. · Picture 3: Tom is at home. Dad is (5) ____ because there's nothing in Tom's hands. · Picture 4: A girl called Zoe is at the door. She's got Tom's (6) ____!

Who says this? Write Tom, Dad or Zoe: 1 Tom, can you go to the market? We need some eggs and some cheese. · 2 Can I have six eggs and some cheese, please? · 3 Oh no! Where's my bag? · 4 Hello! Is this your bag? I found it in the park. · 5 Thank you, Zoe! Now we can make lunch.

Answers: 1 cheese · 2 bananas · 3 football · 4 tree · 5 angry · 6 bag. Extra words: kitchen, asleep. Who says this: 1 Dad · 2 Tom · 3 Tom · 4 Zoe · 5 Dad.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Supermarket. Put things in a pretend bag as you sing.
  3. Tell the pictures (4 min): your child looks at the four pictures and says what is happening: "A boy is buying eggs."
  4. Complete the sentences (7 min): your child writes a word from the box in each gap, then reads the story aloud. Two words are extra.
  5. Who says this? (5 min): read each sentence; your child writes Tom, Dad or Zoe, then says it like that person.
  6. Play shops (7 min): your child is the shopkeeper. You ask: "Can I have some cheese, please? Are there any bananas?" Then swap. Play shops
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Can I have six eggs, please?" … "Oh no! Where's my bag?" … "Is this your bag? I found it in the park."
