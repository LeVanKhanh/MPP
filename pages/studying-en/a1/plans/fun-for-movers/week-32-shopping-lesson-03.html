Week 32 · Lesson 3: Where Does Milk Come From?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Shopping and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 32: Mary goes shopping

Fun for Movers · Week 32: Mary goes shopping | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · What's in the kitchen? Lesson 2 · Tom goes to the market Lesson 3 · Where does milk come from?

You Need

  • The text below, written large, with the three choices next to each gap.
  • Nothing for the memory game – just your voices.

Where does milk come from? Example: (Do / Have / Are) you drink milk every day? Most milk comes from cows. Every morning, the farmer (1 gets / get / getting) the milk from his cows. Then a driver takes it (2 to / at / on) a big building. There, people put the milk in (3 a / some / any) bottles. Then the bottles go to shops (4 and / but / because) supermarkets. Milk is (5 good / better / best) for your teeth!

Answers: Example: Do. 1 gets · 2 to · 3 some · 4 and · 5 good.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each gap and writes it, then reads the text again.
  3. Watch (3 min): How Cheese Is Made! Say the steps with the video.
  4. First, then… (4 min): your child tells you how milk gets to the shop: "First, the farmer gets the milk. Then…"
  5. I went to the market (6 min): "I went to the market and I bought some cheese." "I went to the market and I bought some cheese and two kiwis." Take turns and add one thing each time.
  6. Talk (4 min): ask: "What do you like buying at the market? What did we buy last time?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Most milk comes from cows." … "First, the farmer gets the milk." … "I went to the market and I bought some cheese."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what is in the kitchen with some, any, lots of and only, and decide what to buy.
  • Complete a picture story about shopping, say who says each sentence, and play shops.
  • Read a short text and choose the right word for each gap, and say how milk gets to the shops.
