Week 33 · Lesson 1: Grandpa Bob's Busy Day

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 33: Last weekend, last week

Fun for Movers · Week 33: Last weekend, last week | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Grandpa Bob's busy day Lesson 2 · How was your weekend? Lesson 3 · My weekend diary

You Need

  • Five place cards: library, market, swimming pool, funfair, cinema. Eight thing cards (draw them): books, a shopping bag, a towel, a camera, a scarf, a milkshake, a kite, a skateboard.
  • Read the script twice. Your child puts a thing card under each place. Three things are extra.
  • Word cards for "Now and past": go, see, take, have, eat, buy, come, get.

Script: On Saturday Grandpa Bob was very busy! In the morning he took his books back to the library. Then he went to the market. He took a big shopping bag with him. Before lunch he went to the swimming pool. He took his towel. After lunch he went to the funfair with Lily. He took his camera and took lots of photos. That evening he went to the cinema. It was cold, so he took his scarf.

Complete the sentences: 1 In the morning, Grandpa took his ____ back to the library. · 2 He took a big ____ to the market. · 3 Before lunch he went to the swimming ____. · 4 After lunch he went to the funfair with ____. · 5 That evening he took his ____ to the cinema.

Answers: library – books · market – shopping bag · swimming pool – towel · funfair – camera · cinema – scarf (extra: milkshake, kite, skateboard). Sentences: 1 books · 2 shopping bag · 3 pool · 4 Lily · 5 scarf.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Present – Past Song. Say the past word before the song.
  3. Now and past (5 min): show a word card: "go!" Your child says: "went!" Then your child makes a sentence: "I went to school." Now and in the past
  4. Listen and match (7 min): read the script twice. Your child puts a thing card under each place. Three things are extra.
  5. Complete the sentences (5 min): your child writes one or two words in each sentence, then reads it aloud.
  6. Grandpa's day (6 min): your child tells Grandpa Bob's day with the cards: "First he went to the library. Then… After lunch… That evening…" Sentences
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "He took his towel to the swimming pool." … "After lunch he went to the funfair." … "Go – went, take – took!"
