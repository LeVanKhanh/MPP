Week 33 · Lesson 2: How Was Your Weekend?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 33: Last weekend, last week

Fun for Movers · Week 33: Last weekend, last week | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Grandpa Bob's busy day Lesson 2 · How was your weekend? Lesson 3 · My weekend diary

You Need

  • A word box: boring · busy · cool · different · exciting · fantastic · great · quiet · scary · terrible.
  • The conversation below. Your child chooses A, B or C for each answer.

Let's talk about last weekend: Where were you on Saturday? · Who were you with on Saturday afternoon? · What time did you get up on Sunday? · What did you do that was fun? · What was the weather like on Sunday?

Read and choose the best answer: Example – Kim: Did you enjoy your holiday, Ben? Ben: A Yes, it was fantastic! B On Monday. C What a nice dog! · 1 Kim: Where did you go? A To the mountains. B By car. C Yes, I did. · 2 Kim: Who did you go with? A It was great. B My mum and my sister. C At ten o'clock. · 3 Kim: What was the weather like? A It was sunny every day. B It's my coat. C Well done! · 4 Kim: What did you do there? A I'm fine, thanks. B We walked and climbed. C No, there aren't. · 5 Kim: Were you tired? A Yes, I was! B No, she isn't. C It's here. · 6 Kim: Can I see your photos? A Good morning! B All right! C I'm sorry.

Answers: Example A · 1 A · 2 B · 3 A · 4 B · 5 A · 6 B.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): How was your Weekend? Then say one thing about your weekend.
  3. Let's talk (7 min): ask the five weekend questions. Your child answers in full sentences: "I was at the park." Then your child asks you.
  4. How was it? (3 min): your child chooses a word from the box and says: "Last weekend was great!" and why.
  5. Read and choose (8 min): read Kim's questions together. Your child chooses the best answer, A, B or C, and says why the others are wrong.
  6. Act it out (4 min): you are Kim, your child is Ben. Then your child asks you the same questions about your weekend.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where were you on Saturday?" … "I was at Grandma's house." … "Last weekend was fantastic!"
