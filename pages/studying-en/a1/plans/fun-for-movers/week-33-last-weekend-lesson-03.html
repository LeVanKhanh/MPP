Week 33 · Lesson 3: My Weekend Diary

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 33: Last weekend, last week

Fun for Movers · Week 33: Last weekend, last week | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Grandpa Bob's busy day Lesson 2 · How was your weekend? Lesson 3 · My weekend diary

You Need

  • Before the lesson, make an 8 × 8 word search with eight w words: window, water, walk, weather, white, watch, wet, whale.
  • A page for the weekend diary, with room for a picture.

My weekend diary: Last weekend was ____! · On Saturday I went to ____. · I was with ____. · We ____. · On Sunday the weather was ____.

Answers: Word search: window, water, walk, weather, white, watch, wet, whale. Diary: any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): What did you do? – Role Play. Pause and answer the questions yourself.
  3. Find the w words (6 min): your child finds the eight w words, writes them, and makes a sentence with two of them: "The whale is in the water."
  4. My weekend diary (8 min): your child finishes the five diary sentences about last weekend and draws a picture.
  5. Weekend news (5 min): your child reads the diary to you. Ask two more questions: "What did you eat? Did you enjoy it?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Last weekend was busy!" … "On Saturday I went to the park." … "The whale is in the water."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and say what someone took to each place, and tell their day with first, then, after lunch, that evening.
  • Answer questions about last weekend and say how it was: great, boring, busy, exciting.
  • Choose the best answer in a short conversation, and write a weekend diary.
