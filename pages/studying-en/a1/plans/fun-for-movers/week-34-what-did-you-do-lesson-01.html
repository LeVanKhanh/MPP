Week 34 · Lesson 1: When I Was Young

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 34: What did you do then?

Fun for Movers · Week 34: What did you do then? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · When I was young Lesson 2 · What did your family do? Lesson 3 · A day at the circus

You Need

  • Ten verb cards: jump, call, wait, show, shout, plant, move, play, help, change.
  • The text "Grandpa Joe and Grandma Rose" below, written large, and a pencil for circling.
  • The rule: most verbs add -ed (jump → jumped). Verbs with e at the end add -d (move → moved). Some short verbs double the last letter (drop → dropped, stop → stopped).

Grandpa Joe and Grandma Rose: Grandpa Joe: When I was young, I played football every day. I jumped over the river and I climbed big trees! Now I only watch football on TV. · Grandma Rose: When I was young, I danced at every party and I sailed a boat on the lake. I helped my dad in the garden too. We planted lots of flowers. Now I only dance in the kitchen!

Add -ed or -d: jump → ____ · call → ____ · wait → ____ · show → ____ · shout → ____ · plant → ____ · move → ____ · play → ____ · help → ____ · change → ____

Answers: Circle: played, jumped, climbed, danced, sailed, helped, planted. Add -ed or -d: jumped, called, waited, showed, shouted, planted, moved, played, helped, changed.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (1 min): The -ed Song. Clap when you hear "-ed".
  3. Add -ed or -d (6 min): show a verb card: "jump!" Your child says "jumped!" and writes it. For move and change, just add -d. Add -ed
  4. Read and circle (5 min): read the text together. Your child circles every past verb, then says the "now" word: "played – play".
  5. When I was young (6 min): your child asks you: "What did you do when you were young?" You answer with two or three -ed verbs. Then your child says: "Yesterday I…" Sentences
  6. Mime and guess (7 min): one person does an action and stops. The other guesses: "You climbed a tree!" "You planted a flower!" Take turns.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "When I was young, I played football." … "Move – moved, drop – dropped." … "You jumped!"
