Week 34 · Lesson 2: What Did Your Family Do?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Family. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 34: What did you do then?

Fun for Movers · Week 34: What did you do then? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · When I was young Lesson 2 · What did your family do? Lesson 3 · A day at the circus

You Need

  • The ten sentences below. Before the lesson, draw a very small picture next to each one (a piano, a cake, an ice cream on the floor…).
  • Paper for three sentences about your own family.

Look at the pictures, then complete the sentences: Example: My cousin played the piano at the party. · 1 Grandma c____ a big cake for my birthday. · 2 My little brother d____ his ice cream on the floor. · 3 Dad w____ in the garden all day. · 4 We l____ a lot at the funny clown. · 5 My aunt s____ a boat on the lake. · 6 I c____ a picture of a whale with my crayons. · 7 My sister d____ at the school party. · 8 Mum p____ Grandpa to say happy birthday. · 9 My friends c____ the big tree in the park.

Answers: 1 cooked · 2 dropped · 3 worked · 4 laughed · 5 sailed · 6 coloured · 7 danced · 8 phoned · 9 climbed.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Adding -ed. Say the past word before the video.
  3. Yesterday I… (4 min): say a verb: "cook!" Your child says a sentence: "Yesterday I cooked eggs!" Then swap.
  4. Complete the sentences (8 min): your child looks at each small picture, writes the past verb from the first letter, then reads the sentence aloud.
  5. My family (6 min): your child writes three sentences about your family: "Last Sunday Dad cooked lunch."
  6. Who did it? (4 min): say: "Someone dropped a cup yesterday. Who?" Your child guesses: "Grandpa dropped it!" Then your child asks you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Grandma cooked a big cake." … "My brother dropped his ice cream." … "Who did it?"
