Week 34 · Lesson 3: A Day at the Circus

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 34: What did you do then?

Fun for Movers · Week 34: What did you do then? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · When I was young Lesson 2 · What did your family do? Lesson 3 · A day at the circus

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a circus: a clown with big red shoes riding a bike · a parrot standing on the clown's hat · a lion sitting on a chair · an elephant playing with a ball · a girl and her dad laughing.
  • The look, read and write sentences and the message below. Answers can be one to five words.
  • A hat, a scarf and some glasses for dressing up.

Example: These people are at a ____. (circus) · Complete the sentences: 1 The parrot is standing on ____. · 2 The clown is riding ____. · Answer the questions: 3 Where's the lion? · 4 What's the elephant doing? · Now write two sentences about the picture: 5 ____ · 6 ____

Complete Lucy's message – Word box: party · dressed · pirate · hat · parrot · laughed. Hi Grandma! Yesterday was my birthday party. It was great! My aunt (1) ____ up as a (2) ____. She put on a big black (3) ____ and she had a toy (4) ____ on her arm. We all (5) ____ and ____!

Answers: 1 the clown's hat · 2 a bike · 3 (On) a chair · 4 Playing with a ball · 5–6 any true sentences, for example "The clown has got big red shoes." Message: 1 dressed · 2 pirate · 3 hat · 4 parrot · 5 laughed.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Circus Clown. Do the clown's actions.
  3. Tell the picture (4 min): your child says what everyone is doing: "The lion is sitting on a chair."
  4. Look and read and write (8 min): your child finishes sentences 1–2, answers questions 3–4, then writes two sentences of their own.
  5. Complete the message (6 min): your child writes a word from the box in each gap, then reads Lucy's message aloud like a letter.
  6. Dress up (5 min): your child dresses up with the hat, scarf and glasses, then tells you: "I dressed up as a clown. I put on a big hat."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The parrot is standing on the clown's hat." … "My aunt dressed up as a pirate." … "We all laughed and laughed!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Add -ed or -d to make the past: jump – jumped, move – moved, drop – dropped.
  • Say what people in the family did, from the first letter and a picture.
  • Look at a picture and write about it, and complete a short message with words from a box.
