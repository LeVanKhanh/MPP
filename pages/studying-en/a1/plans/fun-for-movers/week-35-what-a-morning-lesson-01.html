Week 35 · Lesson 1: Guess the Morning Word

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 35: What a morning!

Fun for Movers · Week 35: What a morning! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Guess the morning word Lesson 2 · Kim's terrible morning Lesson 3 · The past verb game

You Need

  • Eight picture cards (draw them): a towel, breakfast, a bus stop, homework, a shower, a toothbrush, a cup, a map.
  • Eight action cards for "My morning": wake up, get up, have a shower, get dressed, have breakfast, clean my teeth, put on my coat, catch the bus.
  • The sentences below. Two picture cards are extra.

Read and write the word: Example: You dry your body with this after a bath. a towel · 1 You clean your teeth with this. ____ · 2 You eat this in the morning before school. ____ · 3 Water comes out of this and you wash under it. ____ · 4 Your teacher gives you this and you do it at home. ____ · 5 Lots of people stand here and look for the bus. ____

Answers: 1 a toothbrush · 2 breakfast · 3 a shower · 4 homework · 5 a bus stop. Extra: a cup, a map.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): This Is The Way – Daily Routines. Do each action with the song.
  3. My morning (5 min): your child puts the action cards in order and says: "First I wake up. Then I get up…" Morning words
  4. Guess the word (7 min): read each sentence. Your child finds the picture card and writes the word. Two cards are extra.
  5. Make a riddle (5 min): your child makes a riddle for a cup or a map: "You put milk in this." You guess. Then you make one.
  6. When do you…? (5 min): ask: "When do you get up?" Your child answers: "At seven o'clock." Do three things and draw a clock for each. Sentences
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "First I wake up. Then I have a shower." … "I get up at seven o'clock." … "You clean your teeth with this!"
