Week 35 · Lesson 2: Kim's Terrible Morning

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 35: What a morning!

Fun for Movers · Week 35: What a morning! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Guess the morning word Lesson 2 · Kim's terrible morning Lesson 3 · The past verb game

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures of Kim: 1 Kim in bed and a clock with eight o'clock · 2 Kim running in the rain with no coat · 3 the school bus going away from the bus stop · 4 Kim in the classroom, very wet, and the teacher looking angry.
  • The two columns below. Your child writes the past in the gaps; some gaps need two words (didn't + verb).

Every day / Last Monday: Example: Every day Kim wakes up at seven o'clock. → Last Monday Kim woke up at eight o'clock! · 1 She has a shower. → She ____ ____ a shower. · 2 She eats an egg for breakfast. → She ____ ____ breakfast. · 3 She cleans her teeth. → She ____ ____ her teeth! · 4 She puts on her coat. → She ____ ____ her coat, and it was raining. · 5 She walks to the bus stop. → She ____ to the bus stop. · 6 She catches the bus. → She ____ ____ the bus. · 7 Her teacher is happy with her. → Her teacher ____ happy.

Answers: 1 didn't have · 2 didn't eat (or didn't have) · 3 didn't clean · 4 didn't put · 5 ran · 6 didn't catch · 7 wasn't.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What do you do in the morning? Sing the answers.
  3. Every day (5 min): your child tells you their morning: "Every day I get up, I have breakfast…" Then you tell yours.
  4. Tell the pictures (5 min): your child looks at the four pictures and tells Kim's story: "Kim woke up at eight o'clock. She ran…"
  5. Every day / Last Monday (8 min): read each "every day" sentence. Your child writes what Kim did or didn't do last Monday, then reads both sentences aloud.
  6. Did you…? (5 min): ask: "Did you have a shower this morning?" Your child answers: "Yes, I did." or "No, I didn't." Then your child asks you. Did you…?
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Kim didn't have breakfast." … "She didn't catch the bus." … "Did you clean your teeth? – Yes, I did!"
