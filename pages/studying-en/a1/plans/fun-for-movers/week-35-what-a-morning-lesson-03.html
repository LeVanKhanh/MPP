Week 35 · Lesson 3: The Past Verb Game

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 35: What a morning!

Fun for Movers · Week 35: What a morning! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Guess the morning word Lesson 2 · Kim's terrible morning Lesson 3 · The past verb game

You Need

  • The story below with seven gaps. Write one word on each line.
  • Before the lesson, make a board with 20 squares and one verb in each: go, eat, have, see, take, get, put, say, catch, wake, run, come, buy, drink, swim, sing, fly, ride, sit, make. A dice and two small toys.

Sam's good morning: This morning I woke up at seven o'clock and I (1) ____ a shower. Then I (2) ____ on my school clothes and went downstairs to the kitchen. I (3) ____ an egg and some bread for breakfast. I (4) ____ my teeth and I (5) ____ goodbye to my mum. I (6) ____ the bus with my friend Jim. At school, my teacher (7) ____ very happy because I had all my homework!

Answers: 1 had · 2 put · 3 ate (or had) · 4 cleaned · 5 said · 6 caught · 7 was. Board: went, ate, had, saw, took, got, put, said, caught, woke, ran, came, bought, drank, swam, sang, flew, rode, sat, made.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Yes, I Did! No, I Didn't! Answer each question with the song.
  3. Read and write (8 min): read Sam's story together. Your child writes one word on each line, then reads the story aloud.
  4. My good morning (3 min): your child changes the story to their own morning: "This morning I woke up at…"
  5. The past verb game (8 min): roll the dice and move. Say the past and a sentence: "swim – swam! I swam in the sea." If it's wrong, go back one square. The first to the end wins.
  6. Morning news (3 min): your child tells you three things they did this morning and one thing they didn't do.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I caught the bus with my friend." … "Swim – swam!" … "I didn't eat an egg this morning."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a short sentence about a morning thing and write the word: a towel, a shower, a bus stop, homework.
  • Say what you do every morning, and what someone did or didn't do on a bad morning.
  • Complete a short story with one word on each line, and say the past of 20 verbs in a game.
