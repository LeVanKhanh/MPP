Week 36 · Lesson 1: Tom's Saturday

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Health. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 36: Could you do it?

You Need

  • Tom's list and the story below, written large.
  • Tom fell off his bike on Friday and hurt his leg. He wanted to do six things on Saturday, but he couldn't do most of them.

Tom's list for Saturday: go swimming with Ben · make a cake for Grandma · wash the car with Dad · text Lily · go to the cinema · read his new book

Tom's Saturday: Tom's leg hurt, so he sat on the sofa all day. Dad washed the car, and Tom watched from the window. Mum made the cake, and Tom helped her. He texted Lily from the sofa and read his new book.

Complete the sentences: Example: Tom couldn't go swimming with Ben. · 1 He ____ ____ the car with Dad. · 2 He ____ ____ Lily. · 3 He ____ ____ to the cinema. · 4 He ____ ____ his new book. · 5 He ____ make the cake, but he helped Mum.

Answers: 1 couldn't wash · 2 could text · 3 couldn't go · 4 could read · 5 couldn't.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Could – Couldn't. Say "could" or "couldn't" with the video.
  3. Tom's list (4 min): read Tom's list and the story together. Your child points to each thing and says: "Yes!" or "No!"
  4. Complete the sentences (7 min): your child writes "could" or "couldn't" and the verb, then reads each sentence aloud. Sentences
  5. When you were three (6 min): ask: "Could you ride a bike when you were three? Could you swim? Could you read?" Your child answers: "No, I couldn't." or "Yes, I could." Then your child asks you.
  6. Why? (6 min): your child says two things they couldn't do last week and why: "I couldn't play outside because it was raining."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Tom couldn't go swimming." … "Could you swim when you were three? – No, I couldn't." … "I couldn't play because it was raining."
