Week 36 · Lesson 2: Listen and Tick

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 36: Could you do it?

Fun for Movers · Week 36: Could you do it? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Tom's Saturday Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · The best excuse

You Need

  • The five questions and the A/B/C answers below. Before the lesson, draw a very small picture for each answer.
  • Read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C for each question.

Script: Mum: Hello, Lily! Where did you go this morning? · Lily: I went to the library with Dad, Mum. I wanted to go to the zoo, but it was raining. · Mum: Did you buy something? · Lily: Yes! I bought a kite at the market after that. · Mum: Great! Where's your bike? It isn't in the garden. · Lily: I put it in the hall, next to the door. · Mum: Good girl. Let's go to Grandma's party now. · Lily: No! I can't find my bag. Oh, here it is, under the table! · Mum: Grandma lives at number 33 now, not number 13. · Lily: Thirty-three. OK!

Listen and tick (✓) the box: 1 Where did Lily go this morning? A the zoo B the library C the park · 2 What did Lily buy? A a book B a T-shirt C a kite · 3 Where's Lily's bike? A in the garden B in the hall C next to the car · 4 What couldn't Lily find? A her bag B her pen C her glasses · 5 What number is Grandma's flat? A 13 B 30 C 33

Answers: 1 B · 2 C · 3 B · 4 A · 5 C.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Could – Couldn't Song. Clap for "could", stamp for "couldn't".
  3. Look first (4 min): your child looks at the small pictures and names them: "a kite, a library, number thirty…"
  4. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice, as Mum and Lily. Your child ticks A, B or C. Check together.
  5. Ask Lily (5 min): your child is Lily. You ask the five questions; your child answers without the paper: "I went to the library."
  6. Your turn (5 min): your child asks you: "Where did you go this morning? What did you buy? What couldn't you find?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I wanted to go to the zoo, but it was raining." … "I couldn't find my bag." … "Grandma lives at number thirty-three."
