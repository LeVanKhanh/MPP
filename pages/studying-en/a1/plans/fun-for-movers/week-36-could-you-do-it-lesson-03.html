Week 36 · Lesson 3: The Best Excuse

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 36: Could you do it?

Fun for Movers · Week 36: Could you do it? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Tom's Saturday Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · The best excuse

You Need

  • Eight picture cards (draw them): glasses, a goat, wind, a book in a bath, a girl looking for her bag, a cat under the sofa, rain, a dinner.
  • Eight word cards: glasses, goat, wind, dropped, lost, hid, rain, dinner. Your child matches each word to a picture.
  • Some real clothes (a sock, a T-shirt, a skirt, a sweater, shorts, a scarf), a string between two chairs and some clothes pegs.

I couldn't do my homework because… Example: I couldn't see the board because I lost my glasses. · 1 a ____ ate my homework at the farm. · 2 the ____ took my paper out of the window. · 3 I ____ my book in the bath. · 4 my cat ____ my pencil under the sofa. · 5 the ____ made my homework very wet.

Answers: 1 goat · 2 wind · 3 dropped · 4 hid · 5 rain. Extra words: glasses, dinner.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): I Couldn't Get It – The Fox and the Stork. Say what the fox and the stork couldn't do.
  3. Words and pictures (4 min): your child matches each word card to a picture card and says the word.
  4. Funny excuses (7 min): your child chooses a word for each sentence and writes it, then reads the excuse in a sad voice: "I couldn't do my homework because a goat ate it!"
  5. My best excuse (3 min): your child makes up one more funny excuse. You make one too. Whose is the funniest?
  6. Put out the washing (7 min): say: "Put the sock next to the scarf. Put the sweater between the T-shirt and the skirt." Your child puts the clothes on the string. Then swap. Clothes
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I couldn't do my homework because a goat ate it!" … "My cat hid my pencil." … "Put the sock next to the scarf."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what someone could and couldn't do last weekend, and why.
  • Listen to a short talk and tick the right answer, A, B or C.
  • Match words to pictures, finish funny excuses with "I couldn't… because…", and put out the washing.
