Week 37 · Lesson 1: I Like… I Have to…

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Jobs and Daily Routines. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 37: Mr Must changes his job

Fun for Movers · Week 37: Mr Must changes his job | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · I like… I have to… Lesson 2 · Kate's letter from the sea Lesson 3 · Who am I?

You Need

  • A big paper with two circles that cross in the middle. Write "I like…" on one and "I have to…" on the other.
  • Twelve cards: have a shower · eat breakfast · go to school · clean my teeth · sleep · get dressed · watch TV · do homework · play computer games · feed the cat · help in the kitchen · go swimming.
  • If your child likes it and has to do it, the card goes in the middle.

Say it: I like ___ing. · I have to ___. · I like ___ing, and I have to do it too! · I don't have to ___ at the weekend.

Answers: Any true answers. For example: I like watching TV. I have to clean my teeth. I like eating breakfast, and I have to do it too!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Have To Song. Say "I have to…" with the song.
  3. Sort the cards (7 min): your child puts each card in a circle, or in the middle. Ask: "Do you like it? Do you have to do it?"
  4. Say it (5 min): your child reads the circles: "I like watching TV. I have to clean my teeth." Sentences
  5. What about you? (5 min): your child asks you: "Do you have to go to work? Do you like cooking?" You answer in full sentences.
  6. Then and now (5 min): say: "When you were three, you had to sleep after lunch. Now you don't have to." Your child says two more: "When I was three, I couldn't…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I like swimming." … "I have to do my homework." … "I don't have to go to school on Sunday!"
