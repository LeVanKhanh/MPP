Week 37 · Lesson 2: Kate's Letter from the Sea

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Jobs and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 37: Mr Must changes his job

Fun for Movers · Week 37: Mr Must changes his job | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · I like… I have to… Lesson 2 · Kate's letter from the sea Lesson 3 · Who am I?

You Need

  • The story below, written large, in two parts.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two or three words from the story.

Part 1: Kate Bell was a cook in a big café in the city. Every day she had to get up at five o'clock and catch the bus to work. She had to cook eggs and pancakes for lots of people. Kate didn't like the city. It was very busy and there weren't many trees. She wanted to live by the sea.

Part 2: One evening, a letter came for Kate. It was from her brother Sam. "I've got a little café on the beach," he wrote. "Can you come and cook with me?" Kate laughed. "Yes, please!" Now Kate gets up at seven o'clock and walks to work. She cooks pancakes every day, but now she can see the sea from her kitchen window!

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: Kate was a cook in the city. · 1 Every morning she had to catch the ____ to work. · 2 Kate didn't like the city because it was very ____. · 3 Kate wanted to live by the ____. · 4 ____ wrote a letter to Kate. · 5 Sam has got a café on the ____. · 6 Now Kate can see the sea from her ____.

Answers: 1 bus · 2 busy · 3 sea · 4 (Her brother) Sam · 5 beach · 6 kitchen window.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Jobs Song – What Do You Do? Say each job before the singer.
  3. Read the story (6 min): read Part 1 together. Ask: "Did Kate like the city?" Then read Part 2. Ask: "Is Kate happy now?"
  4. Write the words (7 min): your child writes one, two or three words from the story in each sentence.
  5. Kate then and now (4 min): your child says: "Before, Kate had to get up at five o'clock. Now she gets up at seven o'clock."
  6. Act the letter (5 min): you are Sam and read the letter aloud. Your child is Kate and answers: "Yes, please! I want to live by the sea!" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Kate had to catch the bus." … "She wanted to live by the sea." … "Now she can see the sea from her window!"
