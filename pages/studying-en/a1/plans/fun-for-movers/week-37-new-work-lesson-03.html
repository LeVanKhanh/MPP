Week 37 · Lesson 3: Who Am I?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Jobs and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 37: Mr Must changes his job

Fun for Movers · Week 37: Mr Must changes his job | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · I like… I have to… Lesson 2 · Kate's letter from the sea Lesson 3 · Who am I?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four rows of four pictures: 1 a doctor, a nurse, a dentist, a pop star · 2 a bus, a lorry, a car, a horse · 3 a cup, a bowl, a plate, a balloon · 4 a cow, a goat, a sheep, a parrot.
  • The five "Who am I?" clues below.
  • Paper and a pencil for the family interview.

Who am I? 1 I work on a farm. I give the cows and sheep their food every morning. · 2 I work in a kitchen. I make food for lots of people. · 3 I look at your teeth. You have to open your mouth! · 4 I drive a bus or a lorry all day. · 5 I work in a school. I help children read and write.

Family interview: What's your work? · Where do you work? · What do you have to do every day? · Do you like it? Why?

Answers: Which is different (other good reasons are fine): 1 the pop star – the others help sick people · 2 the horse – it's an animal, you drive the others · 3 the balloon – you don't put food in it · 4 the parrot – it can fly. Who am I?: 1 a farmer · 2 a cook · 3 a dentist · 4 a driver · 5 a teacher.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Hello, What Do You Do? Do an action for each job.
  3. Which one is different? (7 min): your child looks at each row and says which picture is different and why: "The parrot, because it can fly." Any good reason is fine.
  4. Who am I? (6 min): read each clue. Your child guesses: "You're a farmer!" Then your child makes a clue for you. Jobs words
  5. Family interview (7 min): your child asks a family member the four questions, writes short answers and draws a picture of them at work.
  6. What do you want to be? (3 min): ask: "What do you want to be?" Your child answers: "I want to be a doctor, because…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The parrot is different because it can fly." … "You're a dentist!" … "What's your work? Do you like it?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what you like doing and what you have to do: "I like swimming. I have to clean my teeth."
  • Read a story about someone's work and write words to complete the sentences.
  • Say which picture is different and why, guess people's work from clues, and ask a family member about their work.
