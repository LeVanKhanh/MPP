Week 38 · Lesson 1: Grandpa's Garden

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 Grandpa and Grandma in the garden with some flowers, a boy and a girl next to them · 2 the girl putting water on the flowers, the boy carrying a big bag, a rabbit looking at the flowers · 3 Grandma bringing glasses of lemonade, the rabbit eating a flower · 4 rain, everyone running into the house, the rabbit sitting in the flowers, happy.
  • The sentences below. Your child chooses the words – there isn't one right answer for names.

Let's plant some flowers! 1 Grandpa and Grandma ____ want to plant some flowers in their garden. Their grandson ____ and their granddaughter ____ are helping them. · 2 ____ is putting water on the flowers. ____ is carrying a big bag. A rabbit called ____ is looking at the flowers. · 3 Grandma is bringing everyone some ____ to drink. Oh no! The rabbit is ____ a flower! · 4 The family is going ____ now because it's starting to ____. Look at the rabbit! It's ____ in the flowers. It's very happy!

Answers: Names: any (for example Smith, Tom, Lucy, Fluffy). Other gaps: 3 lemonade · eating · 4 inside (or into the house) · rain · sitting.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Gardening Song. Do the actions: dig, plant, water.
  3. Look at the pictures (4 min): your child says what is in each picture: "There's a rabbit. Grandma has got some lemonade."
  4. You choose the words (8 min): your child writes a word in each gap. Names can be anything! Then your child reads the story aloud.
  5. Tell the story (5 min): your child tells the story with the pictures and no paper. Ask: "Why is the rabbit happy?"
  6. Helping at home (4 min): ask: "Who do you help at home? How do you help?" Your child answers: "I help Dad. I wash the plates." Helping at home
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Lucy is putting water on the flowers." … "Oh no! The rabbit is eating a flower!" … "It's starting to rain."
