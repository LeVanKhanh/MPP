Week 38 · Lesson 2: Alex's Busy Week

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 38: Playing and working

Fun for Movers · Week 38: Playing and working | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Grandpa's garden Lesson 2 · Alex's busy week Lesson 3 · Dr Lee works at night

You Need

  • A paper with three boxes: Wednesday · Thursday · Friday.
  • Read the script twice. Your child draws what Alex did in each box.

Script: Hi, I'm Alex. Last week was very busy! On Wednesday it was sunny, so after school I washed my bike in the garden. My dog Rex wanted to help, but he only got very wet! On Thursday I helped my mum in the kitchen. We made pancakes for dinner. They were great! On Friday it rained all day, so I played a board game with my sister at home. It was fun!

Let's talk: 1 What was your best day last week? Why? · 2 What was the weather like on Monday? · 3 Who did you help last weekend? What did you do? · 4 When did you play with your friends? Where were you?

Answers: Wednesday – Alex washing his bike (and a wet dog) · Thursday – Alex and Mum making pancakes · Friday – Alex and his sister playing a board game at home, rain outside.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Days Of The Week Song. Stand up for Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.
  3. Listen and draw (9 min): read the script twice. Your child draws what Alex did on each day. Simple pictures are fine.
  4. Tell Alex's week (4 min): your child points to the pictures and says: "On Wednesday Alex washed his bike."
  5. Let's talk (6 min): ask the four questions. Your child answers in full sentences. Then your child asks you two of them.
  6. My week (4 min): your child tells you what they did last Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Days sentences
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "On Wednesday Alex washed his bike." … "On Friday it rained all day." … "I helped my mum in the kitchen."
