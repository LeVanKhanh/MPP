Week 38 · Lesson 3: Dr Lee Works at Night

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Jobs and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 38: Playing and working

Fun for Movers · Week 38: Playing and working | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Grandpa's garden Lesson 2 · Alex's busy week Lesson 3 · Dr Lee works at night

You Need

  • Word cards with a small picture on each: hospital (example), doctor, night, soup, drove, hungry, sunny, shoes, kitchen. Three words are extra.
  • Before the lesson, draw two pictures of Dr Lee at the table. Picture A: a clock with seven o'clock, a cup of coffee, an apple, a cat on the chair, the sun in the window. Picture B: a clock with eight o'clock, a glass of milk, a banana, a dog on the chair, the moon in the window.

Read the story. Choose a word from the box. Dr Lee works at a big hospital in the town. She's a (1) ____. Some weeks she works in the day, and some weeks she works at (2) ____. Last Monday, Dr Lee slept in the day. In the evening she made some (3) ____ for her family's dinner. Then she got into her car and (4) ____ to the hospital. She helped lots of sick people. On Tuesday morning, Dr Lee came home. She was very tired and (5) ____! She ate some bread and cheese and went to bed.

Choose the best name for this story: A night at work! · A new car! · Dr Lee's holiday!

Answers: 1 doctor · 2 night · 3 soup · 4 drove · 5 hungry. Extra: sunny, shoes, kitchen. Best name: A night at work! Differences: seven/eight o'clock · coffee/milk · apple/banana · cat/dog · sun/moon.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Daytime and Nighttime. Point up for the sun, down for the moon.
  3. Read and choose (8 min): read the story together. Your child chooses a word card for each number and writes it. Three words are extra.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why: "Because she works at night."
  5. Find the differences (6 min): your child finds five differences: "In picture A there's a cat, but in picture B there's a dog."
  6. Day or night? (6 min): say a word: sun, moon, stars, breakfast, school, bed, lunch, sleep. Your child stands up for "day" and sits down for "night". Then your child says the words.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Dr Lee works at night." … "She drove to the hospital." … "In picture A there's a cat, but in picture B there's a dog."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Tell a picture story about helping in the garden, choosing your own names and words.
  • Listen and draw what someone did on three days, and talk about last week.
  • Read a story about night work, choose words from a box, and find the differences between two pictures.
