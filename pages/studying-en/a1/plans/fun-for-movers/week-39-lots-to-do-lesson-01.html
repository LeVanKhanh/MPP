Week 39 · Lesson 1: When Do You Do It?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 39: We've got lots of things to do

Fun for Movers · Week 39: We've got lots of things to do | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · When do you do it? Lesson 2 · Grandma Rose's farm Lesson 3 · Do you make your bed?

You Need

  • Four clock cards (draw them): 1 seven o'clock, a girl washing her face · 2 four o'clock, a boy doing his homework · 3 seven o'clock, a family having dinner · 4 ten o'clock, a man sleeping.
  • Six question cards: Do you ride your bike? · Do you read a book? · Do you play outside? · Do you help in the kitchen? · Do you have a bath? · Do you watch TV?
  • Paper with three empty clocks for "My day".

When do you do these things? 1 seven o'clock – the girl washes her face in the ____. · 2 four o'clock – the boy does his homework in the ____. · 3 seven o'clock – the family has dinner in the ____. · 4 ten o'clock – the man is sleeping at ____.

Answers: 1 morning · 2 afternoon · 3 evening · 4 night. Question cards: any true answers, for example "Yes, I do. I play outside in the afternoon."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Evening Routines Song. Do the evening actions with the song.
  3. Clocks (5 min): your child looks at each clock card and writes morning, afternoon, evening or night.
  4. Do you…? When do you…? (8 min): your child takes a question card and asks you: "Do you ride your bike?" You answer. Then your child asks: "When do you ride your bike?" Swap. Sentences
  5. My day (6 min): your child draws the time on three clocks and says what they do then: "At four o'clock, in the afternoon, I do my homework."
  6. Clock game (4 min): say a time: "eight o'clock in the evening!" Your child acts what they do then. You guess.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I play outside in the afternoon." … "When do you have a bath? – In the evening." … "At ten o'clock I'm sleeping!"
