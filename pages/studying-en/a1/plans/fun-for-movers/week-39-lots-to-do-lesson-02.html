Week 39 · Lesson 2: Grandma Rose's Farm

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Farm and Countryside and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 39: We've got lots of things to do

Fun for Movers · Week 39: We've got lots of things to do | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

You Need

  • The story below, written large, with the three words next to each number.
  • A word box: easy · difficult · boring · exciting.

Grandma Rose's farm: Grandma Rose is 85 now. She likes telling her family about the farm where she lived when she (Example: was / is / were) young. "Every morning I got up at five o'clock. I gave the chickens and the ducks their food, then I (1 helps / helped / help) my father with the cows. We worked (2 in / on / at) the fields all day. When (3 they / it / there) was a party in the village, we danced all night! I worked hard, but I was happy (4 every / some / many) day. And I (5 had / have / has) a horse called Star!"

Is it easy, difficult, boring or exciting to be a… farmer? · doctor? · teacher? · clown? · driver?

Answers: Example: was · 1 helped · 2 in · 3 there · 4 every · 5 had. Easy/difficult: any answer with a reason.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): A Day in the Life – Farmer. Say what the farmer does first, then.
  3. Read and choose (8 min): read the story together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and writes it, then reads the story aloud.
  4. Grandma Rose's day (4 min): your child tells Grandma Rose's day: "She got up at five o'clock. She helped her father…"
  5. Easy or difficult? (6 min): ask: "Is it easy to be a farmer?" Your child chooses a word from the box and says why: "I think it's difficult because you work all day." Jobs words
  6. When you were young (4 min): your child asks you or a grandparent: "What did you do when you were young? Did you work hard?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "She helped her father with the cows." … "There was a party in the village." … "I think it's exciting to be a farmer!"
