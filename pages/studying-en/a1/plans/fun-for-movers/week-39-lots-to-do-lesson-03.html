Week 39 · Lesson 3: Do You Make Your Bed?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Jobs and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 39: We've got lots of things to do

Fun for Movers · Week 39: We've got lots of things to do | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · When do you do it? Lesson 2 · Grandma Rose's farm Lesson 3 · Do you make your bed?

You Need

  • Four action pictures (draw them): wash the plates · cook the dinner · make your bed · feed the fish.
  • Five answer cards: Yes, every day! · I always do that! · I often do that. · Sometimes. · No, never! Your child chooses true answers – there isn't one right answer.
  • The form below for Listen and write. Read the script twice.

Script: Boy: Hi, Lucy! Who's your swimming teacher? · Lucy: Her name's Anna Brown. · Boy: How do you spell Brown? · Lucy: B-R-O-W-N. · Boy: How old is she? · Lucy: She's twenty-four. · Boy: When are the lessons? On Friday? · Lucy: No, on Saturday. In the morning, at ten o'clock. · Boy: How many children are there in your class? · Lucy: There are eight. · Boy: Does Anna only teach swimming? · Lucy: Yes, but she loves dancing. She wants to teach dancing, too!

Park Road Swimming Pool – Swimming class: Teacher's name: Anna Brown · 1 Age: ____ · 2 Day: on ____ · 3 When: in the ____ · 4 Number of children in the class: ____ · 5 Anna wants to teach ____, too.

What about you? 1 On Saturday morning, I always ____. · 2 After school, I often ____. · 3 In the evening, I sometimes ____. · 4 I never ____ on Monday morning.

Answers: 1 24 · 2 Saturday · 3 morning · 4 8 (eight) · 5 dancing. Answer cards and "What about you?": any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): How often do you exercise? Show always, often, sometimes, never with your fingers.
  3. Draw lines (6 min): ask: "Do you make your bed?" Your child points to a true answer card: "I often do that." Do all four pictures.
  4. Make questions (4 min): your child finishes these and asks you: "Do you carry ___? Do you clean ___? Do you paint ___?"
  5. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number for each line on the form.
  6. What about you? (5 min): your child finishes the four sentences about their own week, then reads them to you. How often
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Do you make your bed? – Yes, every day!" … "I never cook the dinner." … "The lessons are on Saturday morning."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say when you do things: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night.
  • Read a short story and choose the right word from three, and say if work is easy, difficult, boring or exciting.
  • Say how often you help at home, and listen and write details about a class.
