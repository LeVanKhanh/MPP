Week 40 · Lesson 1: Lost by the River

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Animals and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 40: People who help us

Fun for Movers · Week 40: People who help us | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Lost by the river Lesson 2 · Lost pet! Lesson 3 · Look! A good book!

You Need

  • The story below in three parts, written large.
  • Answers are one, two or three words from the story.

Part 1: On Saturday, Lucy and Sam went to the river with their grandpa. They took their puppy, Spot. The children played with a red ball in a field next to the river. Sam kicked the ball and Spot ran after it, but he didn't come back! · Example: Lucy and Sam went to the river with their grandpa. Spot is the children's puppy. · 1 The children played with Spot in a ____ next to the river.

Part 2: The children looked for Spot by the river, but they couldn't find him. "We must ask someone to help us," said Lucy. Then they saw a farmer on a big green tractor. "What's the matter?" she asked. Grandpa told her about Spot. "I can help!" said the farmer. "Let's look behind my house. There are lots of animals there." · 2 A ____ on a tractor came to help. · 3 They looked for Spot ____ the farmer's house.

Part 3: Behind the house there were some sheep. "Listen! What's that noise?" said Sam. Something brown was under a tree, between two sheep. It was Spot! He was asleep, with the red ball under his head. Lucy picked Spot up, but he jumped out of her arms and ran to the sheep. "I think he wants a new friend!" laughed the farmer. · 4 Spot was under a tree between ____. · 5 Spot jumped out of Lucy's ____. · 6 The farmer thinks Spot wants a new ____.

Answers: 1 field · 2 farmer · 3 behind · 4 two sheep · 5 arms · 6 friend.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): I Lost my Pet. Where is the pet? Shout it before the song does.
  3. Part 1 (6 min): read Part 1 together. Ask: "Where did Spot go?" Your child writes the answer to sentence 1.
  4. Part 2 (5 min): read Part 2. Your child writes the answers to sentences 2 and 3.
  5. Part 3 (6 min): read Part 3. Your child writes the answers to sentences 4, 5 and 6, then reads all six sentences.
  6. Tell the story (4 min): your child tells the story with no paper: "Sam kicked the ball. Spot ran after it…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Spot ran after the ball." … "What's the matter? – We can't find our puppy!" … "He was asleep between two sheep."
