Week 40 · Lesson 2: Lost Pet!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Animals and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 40: People who help us

Fun for Movers · Week 40: People who help us | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  • Paper and crayons for a "Lost pet" poster.

Script: Woman: Hello. What's the matter? · Boy: I can't find my kitten! · Woman: Oh no. When did you lose her? · Boy: This morning, at about ten o'clock. · Woman: What's your name? · Boy: My name's Ben Kay. K-A-Y. · Woman: And where do you live, Ben? · Boy: At 19 Hill Street. · Woman: Where were you when you lost her? · Boy: Near the library. She jumped out of my bag! · Woman: What colour is she? · Boy: She's grey and white, with a black tail. · Woman: OK, Ben. Let's go and look for her.

Lost pet: Example: Pet: kitten · 1 When lost: this ____ · 2 Boy's name: Ben ____ · 3 Boy's address: ____ Hill Street · 4 Where pet lost: near the ____ · 5 Colour of pet: grey and ____

Talk about your pet (a real pet or a pretend one): What's the matter? – I can't find my ____. · When did you lose your ____? – I lost it ____. · What's your name? – It's ____. · Where were you when you lost your ____? – I was ____. · Tell me more about your ____. – OK. It's ____.

Answers: 1 morning · 2 Kay · 3 19 · 4 library · 5 white. Talk about your pet: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Community Helpers. Who helps us? Say each one.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number on each line of the form.
  4. Read it back (3 min): your child tells you about the lost kitten: "Ben lost his kitten this morning near the library."
  5. My lost pet (7 min): you ask the five questions; your child answers about a real or pretend pet. Then swap. Sentences
  6. Lost pet poster (4 min): your child draws the pet and writes: "LOST! A ___ kitten. Near ___. Please call ___." (A made-up number is fine.)
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I can't find my kitten!" … "I lost her near the library." … "She's grey and white."
