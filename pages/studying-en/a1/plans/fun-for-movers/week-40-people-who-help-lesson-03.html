Week 40 · Lesson 3: Look! A Good Book!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Jobs. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 40: People who help us

Fun for Movers · Week 40: People who help us | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Lost by the river Lesson 2 · Lost pet! Lesson 3 · Look! A good book!

You Need

  • Word cards: look · book · cook · good · foot · took · put.
  • Big paper and crayons for "My work day".

Say it: Look! The cook took a good book. · Put your foot on the book! · The boys looked for their puppy.

My work day: I'm a ____. · I work in / on a ____. · In the morning, I ____. · In the afternoon, I ____. · I like my work because ____.

Answers: All the words have the same short sound as "look". My work day: any answers, for example "I'm a farmer. I work on a farm. In the morning, I give the cows their food."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Say it (4 min): read the three sentences slowly, then fast. Your child says them with you. Who can say "Look! The cook took a good book" three times?
  3. Sound hunt (4 min): your child reads each word card and finds the same sound in Lesson 1's story: looked, took.
  4. Who am I? (6 min): you act a helper: doctor, nurse, farmer, driver, cook, teacher or dentist. Your child asks: "Are you a cook?" Then swap. Jobs words
  5. My work day (8 min): your child chooses a helper, draws them at work and writes the five sentences.
  6. Show and tell (3 min): your child shows the picture and reads the sentences to you like a teacher.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Look! The cook took a good book." … "I'm a farmer. I work on a farm." … "Are you a dentist?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a story about a lost puppy and write one, two or three words to complete sentences.
  • Listen and write details about a lost pet, and answer questions about your own pet.
  • Say the sound in look, book, cook and good, and tell about a helper's work day.
