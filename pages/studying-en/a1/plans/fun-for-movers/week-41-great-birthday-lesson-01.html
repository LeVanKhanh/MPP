Week 41 · Lesson 1: At the Party

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 41: I had a great birthday!

Fun for Movers · Week 41: I had a great birthday! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · At the party Lesson 2 · Mia's birthday at the lake Lesson 3 · Find the presents

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a party: two children in a band playing guitars · a girl in a yellow dress with no shoes · a woman by the door with a big cake · a man taking photos with a camera · a girl sitting next to the presents · balloons next to the presents · a table with lots of sandwiches and lemonade.
  • Draw five short lines around the picture for names. Read the script twice; your child writes each name and draws a line to the person.

Script: Look at Kim's party! Kim is the girl in the yellow dress. She isn't wearing any shoes! · Ben and Tom are in the band. Ben has got a red guitar. · Mrs Day is the woman by the door. She's bringing the cake. · Mr Day has got a camera. He's taking photos. · Lily is sitting next to the presents.

Complete the sentences: 1 Two children in the band are ____. · 2 The girl in the yellow dress isn't wearing ____. · 3 The woman by the door is bringing ____. · 4 The man with the camera is ____. · 5 Next to the presents, there are some ____. · 6 On the table, there are lots of ____.

Read and choose the best answer: 1 Ben: Happy birthday, Kim! Kim: A Me too! B Thank you! C Don't be sad! · 2 Ben: How old are you now? Kim: A It's a cake. B At four o'clock. C I'm eight! · 3 Ben: Did you get lots of presents? Kim: A Yes, I did! B Yes, I am. C Yes, there is. · 4 Ben: Who made your cake? Kim: A It's chocolate. B My grandma did. C On the table. · 5 Ben: Can I have some lemonade? Kim: A It was great. B I don't know. C Here you are.

Answers: Names: Kim – girl in the yellow dress · Ben and Tom – the band · Mrs Day – woman with the cake · Mr Day – man with the camera · Lily – girl next to the presents. Sentences: 1 playing guitars · 2 any shoes · 3 a (big) cake · 4 taking photos · 5 balloons · 6 sandwiches (and lemonade). Choose: 1 B · 2 C · 3 A · 4 B · 5 C.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Birthday Vocabulary. Point to each party thing in your picture.
  3. Look and say (4 min): your child says what everyone is doing: "A man is taking photos."
  4. Listen and write names (6 min): read the script twice. Your child writes each name and draws a line to the person.
  5. Complete the sentences (6 min): your child writes one, two or three words in each sentence, then reads it aloud.
  6. Read and choose (6 min): you are Ben, your child is Kim. Your child chooses the best answer, A, B or C, and says it. Party sentences
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Happy birthday! – Thank you!" … "The man with the camera is taking photos." … "Can I have some lemonade? – Here you are."
