Week 41 · Lesson 2: Mia's Birthday at the Lake

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 41: I had a great birthday!

Fun for Movers · Week 41: I had a great birthday! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · At the party Lesson 2 · Mia's birthday at the lake Lesson 3 · Find the presents

You Need

  • Word cards with a small picture on each: birthday (example), sunny, boat, picnic, opened, tired, bought, hid, cold. Three words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

Mia's story: It was my birthday last Sunday. My aunt and uncle took me and my cousin Sam to Blue Lake. It was a beautiful (1) ____ day. First we went on a (2) ____ and we saw lots of ducks and fish. Then we had a big (3) ____ under a tree. My aunt made chicken sandwiches and a chocolate cake – my favourite! After lunch my uncle said, "Close your eyes, Mia!" I did. When I (4) ____ them, there was a new kite in front of me! We played with the kite all afternoon. In the evening I was really (5) ____, but very happy!

Choose the best name for the story: Sam's new bike · A great day at the lake · Uncle's boat

Answers: 1 sunny · 2 boat · 3 picnic · 4 opened · 5 tired. Extra: bought, hid, cold. Best name: A great day at the lake.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Bing Plays Musical Statues. Then play it: dance, and stop when the music stops!
  3. Read and choose (8 min): read the story together. Your child chooses a word card for each number and writes it. Three words are extra.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why: "Because they went to a lake."
  5. Mia's day (5 min): your child tells Mia's day: "First they went on a boat. Then they had a picnic…"
  6. My best birthday (7 min): ask: "Where were you on your best birthday? Who was there? What did you eat? What presents did you get?" Your child answers, then asks you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It was a beautiful sunny day." … "When I opened my eyes, there was a kite!" … "I was tired but very happy."
