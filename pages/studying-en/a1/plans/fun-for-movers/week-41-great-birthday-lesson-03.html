Week 41 · Lesson 3: Find the Presents

You Need

  • Before the lesson, write these letters in a big circle with no spaces: kitecomicscarfteddyskateboardwatch. Your child finds the six presents and draws lines to the clues.
  • Paper and crayons for the party plan.

Find the presents: 1 You can play with me on a windy day. · 2 I've got lots of pictures and stories in me. · 3 You wear me round your neck when it's cold. · 4 I'm brown, I've got a big smile, and I sleep in your bed. · 5 You stand on me and ride me in the park. · 6 You wear me on your arm. Look at me – it's three o'clock!

Plan your party: Where? · When? · Who can come? · What food and drinks? · What games? · What music? · Invitation: Please come to my party! It's on ____ at ____ o'clock at ____.

Answers: 1 kite · 2 comic · 3 scarf · 4 teddy · 5 skateboard · 6 watch. Party plan: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (1 min): Birthday Presents Song. What presents can you hear?
  3. Find the presents (7 min): your child finds the six words in the circle, then reads each clue and draws a line to the right present.
  4. Make a riddle (4 min): your child makes a clue for a present: "I'm round and you kick me." You guess. Then swap.
  5. Birthday presents game (6 min): "For my birthday I got a kite." "For my birthday I got a kite and a comic." Take turns and add one present each time.
  6. Plan your party (7 min): your child plans a party with the questions and writes a short invitation. Party ideas
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "You wear me round your neck. – A scarf!" … "For my birthday I got a kite and a comic." … "Please come to my party!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and write names in a party picture, complete sentences about it, and choose the best answer in a birthday talk.
  • Read a birthday story, choose words from a box, and tell about your own best birthday.
  • Find presents from clues, play a memory game, and plan a party.
