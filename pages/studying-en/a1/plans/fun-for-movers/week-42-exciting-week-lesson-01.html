Week 42 · Lesson 1: Which Was the Best Day?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Opposites and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 42: An exciting week for Jane!

You Need

  • Five day cards: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.
  • Tom's four sentences below, written in speech bubbles.
  • Your child puts the day cards in order, from the best day to the worst day.

Tom's school week: "Monday was better than Friday." · "Wednesday was worse than Friday." · "Tuesday was more exciting than Monday." · "Thursday was the worst day!"

Which was Tom's best school day? Put the days in order: 1 (best) ____ · 2 ____ · 3 ____ · 4 ____ · 5 (worst) ____

Answers: 1 Tuesday (best) · 2 Monday · 3 Friday · 4 Wednesday · 5 Thursday (worst).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Comparative Superlative Song. Say "better" and "best" with the song.
  3. Good, better, best (4 min): say: "good!" Your child says: "better, best!" Then "bad!" – "worse, worst!" Then "exciting!" – "more exciting, the most exciting!" Words
  4. Tom's week (9 min): read Tom's sentences one by one. Your child moves the day cards after each sentence and finds Tom's best day.
  5. Check it (4 min): your child reads the order and says why: "Tuesday was better than Monday, and Monday was better than Friday."
  6. My week (6 min): your child talks about their own last week: "Monday was better than Tuesday because we played football." Sentences
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Monday was better than Friday." … "Thursday was the worst day!" … "Tuesday was more exciting than Monday."
