Week 42 · Lesson 2: Where Did Lucy Go?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: My Street and Family. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 42: An exciting week for Jane!

Fun for Movers · Week 42: An exciting week for Jane! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Which was the best day? Lesson 2 · Where did Lucy go? Lesson 3 · Off to the funfair!

You Need

  • Six people cards: her aunt, her uncle, her classmates, her cousin, her dad, her mum.
  • Eight place cards with letters (draw them): A a boat on the river · B the funfair · C the sports centre · D the market · E the cinema · F the train station · G the zoo · H the swimming pool. Two places are extra.
  • Read the script twice. Your child writes a letter under each person.

Script: Grandpa: Hello, Lucy! Did you have a good week in the city? · Lucy: Yes, it was great, Grandpa! On Monday, my mum took me to the market. We bought lots of fruit. · Grandpa: Did you see your cousin? · Lucy: Yes! On Tuesday, my cousin Ben and I went to the swimming pool. · Grandpa: And your aunt? · Lucy: On Wednesday, my aunt took me to the cinema. The film was very funny! · Grandpa: What about your classmates? · Lucy: On Thursday, we went to the zoo with our teacher. I saw a baby elephant! · Grandpa: Wow! And your uncle? · Lucy: On Friday, my uncle took me on a boat on the river. · Grandpa: And your dad? · Lucy: On Saturday, we went to the funfair. That was the best day!

A day in the city: Where do you want to go? · Who do you want to go with? · What do you want to see there? · How can you get there?

Answers: her aunt – E (cinema) · her uncle – A (boat on the river) · her classmates – G (zoo) · her cousin – H (swimming pool) · her dad – B (funfair) · her mum – D (market). Extra: C, F.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (4 min): your child names the eight places: "A is a boat on the river." Places in town
  3. Listen and write (9 min): read the script twice, as Grandpa and Lucy. Your child writes a letter under each person. Two places are extra.
  4. Lucy's week (4 min): your child tells Lucy's week: "On Monday, Lucy went to the market with her mum."
  5. A day in the city (8 min): your child plans a day in the city with the four questions, draws the places and tells you the plan.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "On Friday, my uncle took me on a boat." … "Saturday was the best day!" … "I want to go to the zoo with my cousin."
