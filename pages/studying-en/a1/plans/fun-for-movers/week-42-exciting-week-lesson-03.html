Week 42 · Lesson 3: Off to the Funfair!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Hello and Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 42: An exciting week for Jane!

Fun for Movers · Week 42: An exciting week for Jane! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Which was the best day? Lesson 2 · Where did Lucy go? Lesson 3 · Off to the funfair!

You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child circles the right answer, A or B.
  • Before the lesson, draw two pictures of a park. Picture A: a clown riding a bike, three balloons, a cat under a tree, a boy with a kite, the sun. Picture B: a clown walking, two balloons, a dog under a tree, a girl with a kite, rain.
  • Some small things to give: a pencil, an apple, a book, a toy.

Ben is going to the funfair. Circle his correct answers. 1 Mum: Are you hungry, Ben? Ben: A Yes, I am! B Yes, it is! · 2 Mum: Here are some cheese sandwiches and an apple. Ben: A Sorry, Mum! B Thanks, Mum! · 3 Mum: Shall we take some water, too? Ben: A Well done! B Good idea! · 4 Mum: Can you carry the bag? Ben: A Yes, I can. B Yes, I do. · 5 Mum: Would you like an ice cream at the funfair? Ben: A How are you? B No, thanks! · 6 Mum: Where's your jacket? Ben: A Here it is! B Here they are!

Answers: 1 A · 2 B · 3 B · 4 A · 5 B · 6 A. Differences: riding/walking · three/two balloons · cat/dog · boy/girl with the kite · sun/rain.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Please and Thank You Song. Say "please" and "thank you" with the song.
  3. Read and circle (7 min): you are Mum, your child is Ben. Your child reads both answers and circles the right one, then says it.
  4. Find the differences (7 min): your child finds five differences: "In picture A the clown is riding a bike, but in picture B he's walking."
  5. Say thanks (5 min): give your child a small thing: "This is for you." Your child says: "Thank you!" "Thanks a lot!" "Thank you very much!" Then swap.
  6. Act it out (3 min): act the conversation again with no paper. Change the food and the place.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Shall we take some water? – Good idea!" … "Would you like an ice cream? – No, thanks!" … "Thank you very much!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Compare days with better, worse, more exciting than, and find the best day.
  • Listen and match people to the places they went to in the city.
  • Choose the right answer in a short talk, find differences, and say thank you in different ways.
