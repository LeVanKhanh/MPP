Week 43 · Lesson 1: Let's Talk About Holidays

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 43: My holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 43: My holidays | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Let's talk about holidays Lesson 2 · Kate's island holiday Lesson 3 · My dream holiday

You Need

  • The five questions and five answers below, on small papers. Your child matches them.
  • The conversation below. Your child chooses A, B or C.

Questions: 1 Where did you go on your last holiday? · 2 How did you travel there? · 3 Who did you go with? · 4 What did you eat there? · 5 What did you like doing? · Answers: a My aunt and my cousins. · b We went by train. · c Swimming in the sea! · d To the mountains. · e Lots of fish and rice.

Read and choose the best answer: 1 Ms Hill: Did you have a good holiday, Sam? Sam: A Last week. B Yes, it was great! C Here you are. · 2 Ms Hill: Where did you sleep? Sam: A In a little house by the sea. B At ten o'clock. C Yes, I did. · 3 Ms Hill: How did you get there? Sam: A It's my bag. B Let's go! C By boat! · 4 Ms Hill: What did you do there? Sam: A We climbed a mountain. B It's a big mountain. C I don't like it. · 5 Sam: Look! Here are my photos. Ms Hill: A Excuse me! B Wow! They're great! C On my tablet.

Answers: Match: 1 d · 2 b · 3 a · 4 e · 5 c. Choose: 1 B · 2 A · 3 C · 4 A · 5 B.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Summer Vacation. Say the past words with the song: went, swam, saw.
  3. Match (6 min): your child reads each question and finds the answer, then reads the pairs aloud.
  4. Ask me (6 min): your child asks you the five questions about your last holiday. You answer in full sentences. Sentences
  5. Read and choose (6 min): you are Ms Hill, your child is Sam. Your child chooses A, B or C and says the answer.
  6. My last holiday (4 min): your child answers the five questions about their own last holiday.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where did you go on your last holiday?" … "We went by train." … "Wow! They're great!"
