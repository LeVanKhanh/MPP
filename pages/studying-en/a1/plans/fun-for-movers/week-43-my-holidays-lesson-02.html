Week 43 · Lesson 2: Kate's Island Holiday

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 43: My holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 43: My holidays | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Let's talk about holidays Lesson 2 · Kate's island holiday Lesson 3 · My dream holiday

You Need

  • Word cards: love (example), boat, sunny, pineapples, photos, grandpa, cold, train, clever. Three words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.
  • Paper and crayons to draw Kate's island.

My holiday by Kate: My name is Kate and I love going on holiday. Last year we went to an island. We travelled there by (1) ____. It was very exciting! It was hot and (2) ____ every day, but one evening it rained. The food was great. We ate lots of (3) ____ and fish. We walked in the jungle and saw a big waterfall. In the sea, I saw a dolphin! My dad took lots of (4) ____ on his phone. I found some pretty shells on the beach. I gave the best one to my (5) ____. He loved it!

Answers: 1 boat · 2 sunny · 3 pineapples · 4 photos · 5 grandpa. Extra: cold, train, clever.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): read the story together. Your child chooses a word card for each number and writes it. Three words are extra.
  3. Questions (5 min): ask: "How did Kate travel? What was the weather like? What did she see in the sea? Who got the best shell?"
  4. Draw the island (6 min): your child draws Kate's island and writes labels: beach, sea, jungle, waterfall, dolphin. Holiday words
  5. Hello from the island! (5 min): your child writes a short message from the island: "Hello Grandma! I'm on an island. It's hot and sunny. I saw a dolphin!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We travelled there by boat." … "In the sea, I saw a dolphin!" … "I gave the best shell to my grandpa."
