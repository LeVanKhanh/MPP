Week 43 · Lesson 3: My Dream Holiday

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 43: My holidays

Fun for Movers · Week 43: My holidays | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Let's talk about holidays Lesson 2 · Kate's island holiday Lesson 3 · My dream holiday

You Need

  • A paper with two columns: "mine" and "yours", and the four sentences below.
  • Before the lesson, draw two jungle pictures. Picture A: red parrots in a tree, a monkey eating a banana, a boy with a camera, a frog next to the water, the sun. Picture B: green parrots, a monkey sleeping, a girl with a camera, a lizard next to the water, clouds.
  • Big paper and crayons for the island project.

Two dream holidays: I'd love to go to ____. · I'd love to travel there by ____. · I'd love to go there with ____. · On my dream holiday, I'd love to ____ and ____ and ____!

An island project: Draw your dream island. Give it a name. Write five things on it: a beach, a waterfall, a jungle… What animals live there? What can you do there?

Answers: Dream holidays: any answers. Differences: red/green parrots · monkey eating/sleeping · boy/girl with the camera · frog/lizard · sun/clouds.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Where do you want to go on holiday? Answer the question with the song.
  3. My dream holiday (7 min): your child finishes the "mine" column, then asks you: "Where would you love to go?" and writes your answers in "yours".
  4. Find the differences (6 min): your child finds five differences: "In picture A the parrots are red, but in picture B they're green."
  5. Island project (6 min): your child draws a dream island, gives it a name and writes five labels.
  6. Tell me (3 min): your child tells you about the island: "This is Monkey Island. There's a big waterfall. You can swim with dolphins!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I'd love to go to an island." … "I'd love to travel there by plane." … "In picture A the monkey is eating, but in picture B it's sleeping."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Ask and answer questions about a holiday, and choose the best answer in a short talk.
  • Read a holiday story and write the right word from a box next to each number.
  • Say where you'd love to go on a dream holiday, and find the differences between two pictures.
