Week 44 · Lesson 1: The Big Wave

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 44: On the sand and by the sea

Fun for Movers · Week 44: On the sand and by the sea | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · The big wave Lesson 2 · Beach words Lesson 3 · Same sound, different word

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 a girl, a boy and their dad on the beach, towels and a bag next to a big rock · 2 the children swimming, the boy holding a pink shell, Dad asleep on his towel with a book · 3 a big wave, a book and a bag in the water, Dad very wet · 4 a dog sitting on the bag on the rocks.
  • The story below in three parts. Answers are one, two or three words.

Part 1: Last Saturday, Amy and her brother Ben went to the beach with their dad. They put their towels and their bags next to a big rock. Then they played with a ball on the sand. · Example: Amy has a brother called Ben. · 1 Amy and Ben put their towels next to ____.

Part 2: After that, the children swam in the sea. They looked for fish and shells. Ben found a beautiful pink shell. Dad sat on his towel and read a book. Then Dad was asleep! · 2 The children looked for ____ in the sea. · 3 Ben found a ____ shell. · 4 Dad read a book and then he was ____.

Part 3: Then a big wave came. It took Dad's book and Amy's bag! "Quick, Dad! Wake up!" shouted Amy. Dad jumped up, but he was very wet. The children laughed and laughed. They found the book in the water, and the bag was on the rocks. A dog was sitting on it! "Good dog!" said Ben. · 5 A big wave took Dad's book and Amy's ____. · 6 When Dad jumped up, he was very ____. · 7 They found the bag on the rocks with a ____ on it.

Answers: 1 a big rock · 2 fish and shells · 3 (beautiful) pink · 4 asleep · 5 bag · 6 wet · 7 dog.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): On The Beach Song. Point to the beach things you know.
  3. Tell the pictures (4 min): your child looks at the four pictures and tells the story first: "They're at the beach. Dad is asleep…"
  4. Part 1 (4 min): read Part 1 together. Your child writes the answer to sentence 1.
  5. Part 2 (5 min): read Part 2. Your child writes the answers to sentences 2, 3 and 4.
  6. Part 3 (6 min): read Part 3. Your child writes the answers to sentences 5, 6 and 7, then reads all seven sentences.
  7. Tell it again (4 min): your child tells the story with no paper. Ask: "Why did the children laugh?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "They put their towels next to a big rock." … "Quick, Dad! Wake up!" … "A dog was sitting on the bag!"
