Lesson 1 · The big wave Lesson 2 · Beach words Lesson 3 · Same sound, different word

You Need

  • The six clues below. The answers are words from "The Big Wave".
  • The six "say it another way" sentences below, with the first letters.

Find the answers in the story: Example: You sit on this on the beach, and you dry your body with it. a towel · 1 You can find these in the sea and on the beach. Some are pink. · 2 This is big and grey, and you can sit on it. · 3 These are big and they come from the sea to the beach. · 4 You read this. It's got lots of words and pictures. · 5 Your mum and dad's son, but not you!

Say it another way: 1 take something from the floor = p____ something u__ · 2 go and swim = g_ f__ a swim · 3 get on a bus and go = c____ a bus · 4 write an email to someone = s___ an email · 5 put on your clothes = g__ d______ · 6 go to the shops = g_ s_______

Answers: 1 shells · 2 a rock · 3 waves · 4 a book · 5 a brother. Another way: 1 pick something up · 2 go for a swim · 3 catch a bus · 4 send an email · 5 get dressed · 6 go shopping.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find the answers (8 min): read each clue. Your child finds the word in "The Big Wave" and writes it.
  3. Say it another way (8 min): your child reads each sentence and finishes the words from the first letters.
  4. Act it out (5 min): say: "Go for a swim!" "Catch a bus!" "Get dressed!" Your child acts it. Then your child says one for you.
  5. Make a clue (4 min): your child makes a clue for a beach word: "It's yellow and hot and it's in the sky." You guess: "The sun!" Holiday words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You can find these on the beach. – Shells!" … "Let's go for a swim!" … "I get dressed every morning."
