Week 44 · Lesson 3: Same Sound, Different Word

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Travel. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 44: On the sand and by the sea

Fun for Movers · Week 44: On the sand and by the sea | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · The big wave Lesson 2 · Beach words Lesson 3 · Same sound, different word

You Need

  • Twenty word cards, mixed up: sea, see, sun, son, eye, I, four, for, eight, ate, right, write, red, read, no, know, pair, pear, where, wear.
  • Before the lesson, draw two beach pictures. Picture A: a dog with a ball, a girl reading, three birds, a boat, an orange towel. Picture B: a cat with a ball, a boy reading, two birds, no boat, a blue towel.

Find ten pairs: The words in each pair sound the same, but they mean different things: sea – see · sun – son · eye – I · four – for · eight – ate · right – write · red – read · no – know · pair – pear · where – wear.

Make a sentence with both words: I can see the sea. · I ate eight apples! · Where do you wear your hat? · Look at my pair of pears!

Answers: Ten pairs as above. Differences: dog/cat · girl/boy reading · three/two birds · boat/no boat · orange/blue towel.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): I Spy… Homophones! Same sound, different word. Say each pair.
  3. Find the pairs (7 min): put the cards face up. Your child finds the ten pairs and reads each pair aloud.
  4. Make a sentence (4 min): read the four funny sentences. Your child makes one more: "My son is in the sun!"
  5. Find the differences (6 min): your child finds five differences: "In picture A there's a dog, but in picture B there's a cat."
  6. A to Z (6 min): say a letter: "B!" Your child says a word: "beach!" Then the next letter. How far can you go? The alphabet
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I can see the sea." … "I ate eight apples!" … "In picture A there are three birds, but in picture B there are two."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Tell a picture story about a day at the beach and write one, two or three words to complete sentences.
  • Find beach words from clues, and say things another way: go for a swim, catch a bus, get dressed.
  • Find words that sound the same (sea – see, eight – ate), and find the differences between two pictures.
