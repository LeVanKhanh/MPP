Week 45 · Lesson 1: Pirate Words

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Travel. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 45: Treasure!

Fun for Movers · Week 45: Treasure! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Pirate words Lesson 2 · The pirate film Lesson 3 · Pirate poems

You Need

  • Eight picture cards (draw them): a pirate, a ticket, the sky, treasure, a ship, an island, a map, a bee. A bee and one more card are extra.
  • The clues below.
  • Twelve small word cards for "Words in a name": tree, pear, star, seat, sea, tea, pet, ear, dog, cake, fish, bike.

Read and write the word: Example: This person lives on a ship and looks for treasure. a pirate · 1 You buy this when you want to go on a bus or a train. · 2 This is blue in the day and black at night. You can see stars in it. · 3 Pirates look for this. It's often in a big box. · 4 This is a very big boat. Lots of people can travel on it. · 5 This has water all round it. · 6 You look at this when you don't know where to go.

Words in a name: The film is called PIRATE TREASURE. Which words can you make with these letters? Colour them: tree · pear · star · seat · sea · tea · pet · ear · dog · cake · fish · bike

Answers: 1 a ticket · 2 the sky · 3 treasure · 4 a ship · 5 an island · 6 a map. Extra: a bee. Words in a name: tree, pear, star, seat, sea, tea, pet, ear (not dog, cake, fish, bike).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Pirate Song for Kids. Do the pirate actions with the song.
  3. Name the pictures (3 min): your child names each picture card. Say the new ones together: treasure, island, ship.
  4. Read and write (8 min): read each clue. Your child finds the picture and writes the word. Pirate words
  5. Words in a name (7 min): write PIRATE TREASURE on paper. Your child checks each word card letter by letter and colours the words you can make.
  6. Make a clue (5 min): your child makes a clue for a pirate word: "It's a bird and it can talk." You guess: "A parrot!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Pirates look for treasure." … "An island has water all round it." … "I can make 'star' with those letters!"
