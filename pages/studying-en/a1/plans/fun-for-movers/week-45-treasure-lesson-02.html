Week 45 · Lesson 2: The Pirate Film

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 45: Treasure!

Fun for Movers · Week 45: Treasure! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Pirate words Lesson 2 · The pirate film Lesson 3 · Pirate poems

You Need

  • The story below in three parts. Answers are one, two or three words.
  • Before the lesson, draw two pictures for Lesson 3 too: Picture 2 – the pirates eating fruit under a tree on a hot day · Picture 3 – the pirates singing on the beach at night, with the treasure.

Part 1: Last weekend, Mia and her grandpa went to see a film at the Sun Cinema. The film was about a pirate called Rosa. She had long red hair and a big black hat. She had a monkey too. His name was Coco, and he always sat on Rosa's hat. · Example: Mia went to the cinema with her grandpa. · 1 The film was about a ____ called Rosa. · 2 Rosa's monkey always sat on her ____.

Part 2: Rosa and her pirates sailed to a small island. It was very hot, and they were hungry. "Let's find some fruit!" said Rosa. The pirates found lots of mangoes and bananas. They ate them under a big tree, and then they went to sleep. · 3 The pirates sailed to a ____. · 4 They found mangoes and ____ to eat. · 5 After they ate, the pirates ____.

Part 3: Then Coco jumped up and down and shouted. He was next to a big rock. The pirates woke up. "Coco wants to show us something!" said Rosa. Under the rock there was a box. Inside, there was lots of treasure! That night, the pirates danced and sang on the beach under the stars. Mia loved the film! · 6 Coco was next to a big ____. · 7 That night, the pirates danced and ____ on the beach.

Answers: 1 pirate · 2 hat · 3 small island · 4 bananas · 5 went to sleep · 6 rock · 7 sang.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Part 1 (6 min): read Part 1 together. Ask: "What did Rosa look like?" Your child writes the answers to sentences 1 and 2.
  3. Part 2 (6 min): read Part 2. Your child writes the answers to sentences 3, 4 and 5.
  4. Part 3 (6 min): read Part 3. Your child writes the answers to sentences 6 and 7, then reads all the sentences.
  5. Tell the film (4 min): your child tells you the film like Mia: "Last weekend I saw a film about a pirate called Rosa…"
  6. My favourite film (3 min): ask: "What's your favourite film? Who is in it? What do they do?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Rosa had a monkey called Coco." … "They sailed to a small island." … "Inside, there was lots of treasure!"
