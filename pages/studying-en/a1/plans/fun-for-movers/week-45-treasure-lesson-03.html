Week 45 · Lesson 3: Pirate Poems

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson page: Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 45: Treasure!

Fun for Movers · Week 45: Treasure! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Pirate words Lesson 2 · The pirate film Lesson 3 · Pirate poems

You Need

  • The two pictures you drew for Lesson 2.
  • Paper for poems, and crayons.

Talk about the differences: In picture 2 it's day, but in picture 3 it's night. · In picture 2 the pirates are eating, but in picture 3 they're ____. · In picture 2 there's the sun, but in picture 3 there are ____.

A five-line poem: Line 1 – one thing: Pirates · Line 2 – two describing words: Strong and funny · Line 3 – three -ing words: Sailing, singing, looking · Line 4 – a short sentence: They love treasure! · Line 5 – one word: Hooray!

Answers: Differences: day/night · eating/singing · sun/stars and the moon · no treasure/treasure. Poems: any answers that follow the five lines.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Over The Deep Blue Sea. Sing and do the actions.
  3. Talk about the differences (6 min): your child compares the two pictures: "In picture 2 it's hot and sunny, but in picture 3 it's night."
  4. Read a poem (3 min): read the pirate poem together. Clap one time for each line.
  5. Write a poem (9 min): your child writes a five-line poem about a pirate, the sea, a pet or a monkey, and draws a picture.
  6. Poem show (4 min): your child reads the poem to you like a pop star. Then you read one you wrote.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "In picture 2 it's day, but in picture 3 it's night." … "Sailing, singing, looking." … "They love treasure! Hooray!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a clue and write the word: a ticket, the sky, treasure, a ship, an island, a map.
  • Read a story about a pirate film and write one, two or three words to complete sentences.
  • Talk about the differences between two pictures, and write a five-line poem.
