Week 46 · Lesson 1: On the Island

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Travel and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 46: A day on the island

Fun for Movers · Week 46: A day on the island | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · On the island Lesson 2 · Which one is different? Lesson 3 · Guess my picture

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw an island: a pirate on a ship with a red scarf · a boy in the sea swimming with a dolphin · a woman in a green dress flying a kite · a monkey at the top of a tree · a man in blue shorts holding a big fish · a girl hiding behind the tree · a boy sleeping on a towel.
  • Write six names around the picture: Pat, Dan, Ella, Mr Fox, Zoe, Sam. Read the script twice; your child draws lines from the names to the people.

Script: Look at the people on the island! Pat is the pirate on the ship. He's wearing a red scarf. · Dan is the boy in the sea. He's swimming with a dolphin! · Ella is the woman in the green dress. She's flying a kite. · Mr Fox is wearing blue shorts. He's holding a big fish. · Zoe is hiding behind the tree. · And Sam is sleeping on his towel. He's very tired!

Examples: The pirate on the ship is wearing a red scarf. What is the boy in the sea doing? swimming with a dolphin · Complete the sentences: 1 The woman in the green dress is flying ____. · 2 A monkey is sitting at the top of ____. · Answer the questions: 3 What is the man in the blue shorts holding? · 4 Where is the girl hiding? · Now write two sentences about the picture: 5 ____ · 6 ____

Say it: Pat the pirate has got a big black bag on his back!

Answers: Lines: Pat – pirate · Dan – boy with the dolphin · Ella – woman with the kite · Mr Fox – man with the fish · Zoe – girl behind the tree · Sam – boy on the towel. 1 a kite · 2 the tree · 3 a (big) fish · 4 behind the tree · 5–6 any true sentences.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (4 min): your child says what everyone is doing: "A monkey is sitting in the tree."
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  4. Look and read and write (9 min): your child finishes sentences 1–2, answers questions 3–4, then writes two sentences of their own.
  5. Say it (5 min): say the tongue twister slowly, then fast: "Pat the pirate has got a big black bag on his back!" Who can say it three times?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Ella is flying a kite." … "Zoe is hiding behind the tree." … "Pat the pirate has got a big black bag on his back!"
