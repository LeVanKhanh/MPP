Week 46 · Lesson 2: Which One Is Different?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions and Opposites. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 46: A day on the island

Fun for Movers · Week 46: A day on the island | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · On the island Lesson 2 · Which one is different? Lesson 3 · Guess my picture

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four rows of four pictures: 1 a lake, a river, the sea, a mountain · 2 a plane, a helicopter, a bird, a bus · 3 a sandwich, a banana, a cake, a towel · 4 a boy swimming, a girl swimming, a fish, a boy reading.
  • Paper for your child's own row.

Say which picture is different and why: 1 a lake · a river · the sea · a mountain · 2 a plane · a helicopter · a bird · a bus · 3 a sandwich · a banana · a cake · a towel · 4 a boy swimming · a girl swimming · a fish · a boy reading

Answers: Other good reasons are fine: 1 the mountain – there's no water · 2 the bus – it can't fly · 3 the towel – you can't eat it · 4 the boy reading – he isn't in the water.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Is it? Yes, it is! Answer each question with the song.
  3. Which one is different? (9 min): your child looks at each row and says: "The bus is different because it can't fly." Any good reason is fine.
  4. Make a row (6 min): your child draws four pictures with one different. You find it and say why. Then swap.
  5. Is it…? (4 min): point to a picture and ask: "Is it a river?" Your child answers: "Yes, it is." or "No, it isn't. It's a lake." Questions
  6. What can I see? (4 min): say: "I can see something in this room. It's blue and you can read it." Your child guesses: "Is it a book?" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The bus is different because it can't fly." … "Is it a river? – No, it isn't. It's a lake." … "Is it a book? – Yes, it is!"
