Week 46 · Lesson 3: Guess My Picture

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 46: A day on the island

Fun for Movers · Week 46: A day on the island | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · On the island Lesson 2 · Which one is different? Lesson 3 · Guess my picture

You Need

  • Paper with four boxes for words and four lines for questions.
  • Ten picture cards for the guessing game (for example a parrot, a dolphin, a ship, a towel, a monkey, a kite, a helicopter, a fish, an island, a shell).

Listen and write: Words (spell each one): island · parrot · towel · dolphin. Questions: Is it hot today? · Can you swim? · Have you got a pet? · Do you like pirates?

Write your own question and a yes or no answer: Question: ____? Answer: Yes, ____. / No, ____.

Answers: Words and questions as read. Answers: any true answers, for example "Yes, it is." "Yes, I can." "No, I haven't." "Yes, I do."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Animals – Yes or No Game. Shout "Yes!" or "No!" before the answer.
  3. Listen and write (9 min): say and spell the four words; your child writes them. Then say the four questions slowly; your child writes them and writes a true answer.
  4. My question (4 min): your child writes one more yes/no question for you and you answer it.
  5. Guess my picture (9 min): take a card and hide it. Your child asks: "Is it an animal? Can it fly? Is it big?" You answer only "Yes" or "No". Ten questions! Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is it an animal? – Yes, it is." … "Can it fly? – No, it can't." … "Is it a dolphin? – Yes!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and draw lines from names to people in a picture, then look, read and write about it.
  • Say which picture is different and why, and say a tongue twister.
  • Listen and write words and questions, and guess a picture with yes/no questions.
