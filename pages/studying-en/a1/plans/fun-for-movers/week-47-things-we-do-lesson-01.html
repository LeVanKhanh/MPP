You Need

  • The two columns below on paper. Your child draws lines to join them.
  • Nothing for the mime game – just your bodies.

Draw lines: 1 answer · 2 wait for · 3 invite · 4 water · 5 build · 6 dress up · 7 clap · 8 go for · 9 count — a the flowers in the garden · b my friends to a picnic · c a question · d a model ship · e your hands · f the stars in the sky · g my friend at the bus stop · h as a pirate · i a swim

Answers: 1 c · 2 g · 3 b · 4 a · 5 d · 6 h · 7 e · 8 i · 9 f.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What are you doing? Answer with the song: "I'm…ing."
  3. Draw lines (7 min): your child joins each action word to its words, then reads each pair aloud: "Water the flowers in the garden." Action words
  4. Do it! (5 min): say one pair: "Clap your hands!" "Count the stars!" Your child does it. Then your child says one for you.
  5. What am I doing? (6 min): do an action and stop. Your child guesses: "You're watering the flowers!" Take turns.
  6. My day (5 min): your child says three of these things they did this week: "I answered a question at school."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Water the flowers in the garden." … "What am I doing? – You're building a model ship!" … "Clap your hands!"
