Week 47 · Lesson 2: What Are They Doing Now?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Actions and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 47: The different things we do

Fun for Movers · Week 47: The different things we do | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Things I do Lesson 2 · What are they doing now? Lesson 3 · In the park

You Need

  • Four questions with three small pictures each (draw them, label A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.
  • The "same thing" rows below.

Script: 1 Man: Where's Lily? Woman: She's in her bedroom. She isn't skipping now – she's reading a comic. · 2 Woman: Is Ben playing the piano? Man: No, he's in the garden. He's washing the car with Dad. · 3 Boy: What's Grandma doing? Is she sleeping? Girl: No, she isn't. She's watering the plants. · 4 Girl: Is Max swimming? Boy: Not now. He's building a model plane in the kitchen.

Listen and tick (✓) the box: 1 What is Lily doing now? A skipping B reading a comic C riding a horse · 2 What is Ben doing now? A playing the piano B taking a photo C washing the car · 3 What is Grandma doing now? A watering the plants B sleeping C cooking · 4 What is Max doing now? A swimming B building a model plane C dressing up

Circle the word that means the same thing: film – (movie / money / map) · shop – (sport / store / star) · flat – (apartment / armchair / animal) · football – (soup / soccer / sock) · sweets – (candy / cake / centre) · lift – (elephant / elevator / eraser) · Missing letters: mo_ie · s_ore · apa_tment · so_cer · ca_dy · ele_ator

Answers: 1 B · 2 C · 3 A · 4 B. Same thing: movie, store, apartment, soccer, candy, elevator. Missing letters: v, t, r, c, n, v.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names each small picture: "skipping, reading a comic…"
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C for each question.
  4. Same thing, two words (6 min): your child circles the word that means the same. Tell them: people in the UK say "film", people in the USA say "movie". Two words
  5. Watch (2 min): British English and American English. Say both words.
  6. Missing letters (6 min): your child writes the missing letters, then makes a sentence: "Let's watch a movie!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "She isn't skipping now – she's reading a comic." … "Film and movie mean the same thing." … "Let's watch a movie!"
