Week 47 · Lesson 3: In the Park

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Actions and My Street. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 47: The different things we do

Fun for Movers · Week 47: The different things we do | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Things I do Lesson 2 · What are they doing now? Lesson 3 · In the park

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a park: a boy on a bike · a girl skipping · a woman on a seat with a laptop · a cat on a wall · a dog jumping into the water · a man in a swimming pool · a ball under a tree · a sign with no words on it.
  • Crayons. Say each instruction twice; your child colours or writes.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the boy's bike red. · Colour the cat on the wall grey. · Write PARK on the sign. · Colour the ball under the tree yellow and blue. · Write ZOE on the girl's T-shirt.

Who is doing what? Complete the sentences: 1 ____ is s____ing. (the girl) · 2 ____ is s____ on a seat. (the woman) · 3 ____ is j____ing into the water. (the dog) · 4 ____ is r____ a bike. (the boy) · 5 ____ is s____ in the pool. (the man)

Answers: Colour: red bike, grey cat, PARK on the sign, yellow and blue ball, ZOE on the girl's T-shirt. Sentences: 1 Zoe / The girl is skipping · 2 The woman is sitting · 3 The dog is jumping · 4 The boy is riding · 5 The man is swimming.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour and write (8 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or writes in the picture.
  3. Who is doing what? (6 min): your child completes the five sentences with names or people and the -ing words.
  4. Right or wrong? (5 min): say sentences about the picture: "The cat is on the bike." Your child says: "No, that's wrong! It's on the wall." or "Yes, that's right!"
  5. Mime the words (6 min): whisper an action to your child: "skipping". Your child does it and you guess: "You're skipping!" Then swap. What's she doing?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The dog is jumping into the water." … "No, that's wrong! The cat is on the wall." … "Yes, that's right!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Match action words to the right words: answer a question, water the flowers, clap your hands.
  • Listen and tick what people are doing now, and find two words that mean the same thing: film – movie.
  • Listen and colour and write in a picture, and say "Yes, that's right" or "No, that's wrong".
