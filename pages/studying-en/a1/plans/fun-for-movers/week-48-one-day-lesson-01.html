You Need

  • Eight picture cards (draw them): 1 swim with dolphins · 2 sail round the world · 3 go to the moon · 4 sleep in the jungle · 5 drive a big lorry · 6 be in a film · 7 see a whale · 8 cook for a pop star.
  • The conversation below. Your child chooses A, B or C.

One day, would you like to…? Yes, I'd like to… / No, I wouldn't. It's too…

Read and choose the best answer: Example: Ben: Hi, Ana. What's your book about? Ana: A On the table. B A brave girl and her horse. C I can't swim today. · 1 Ben: What did the girl do? Ana: A She rode her horse in the mountains. B She's my sister. C It's a big horse. · 2 Ben: When did you get this book? Ana: A Every day. B On my birthday. C In the kitchen. · 3 Ben: Who is the book by? Ana: A It's mine. B A woman called Kate Green. C On page ten. · 4 Ben: Do you like it? Ana: A Yes, it is. B Yes, you can. C Yes, I do! · 5 Ben: Is it a very long book? Ana: A No, there are only fifty pages. B I'm sorry. C There aren't any. · 6 Ben: Can I read it after you? Ana: A Don't be sad. B Yes, OK! C Me too!

Answers: Would you like to…: any answers. Choose: Example B · 1 A · 2 B · 3 B · 4 C · 5 A · 6 B.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Dream Big. What would you like to do one day?
  3. One day… (7 min): show each card and ask: "Would you like to swim with dolphins?" Your child answers: "Yes, I'd like to!" or "No, I wouldn't. It's too scary!" Sentences
  4. Your turn (4 min): your child asks you about four cards. Then you both choose your favourite card.
  5. Read and choose (8 min): you are Ben, your child is Ana. Your child chooses A, B or C and says the answer.
  6. My book (3 min): ask: "What's your favourite book about? Who is in it?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Would you like to go to the moon? – Yes, I'd like to!" … "No, I wouldn't. It's too scary!" … "It's about a brave girl and her horse."
