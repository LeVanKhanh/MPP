Week 48 · Lesson 2: Tim's Book About Dan Sky

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 48: We want to do this one day

Fun for Movers · Week 48: We want to do this one day | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Would you like to…? Lesson 2 · Tim's book about Dan Sky Lesson 3 · How well do you know me?

You Need

  • Word cards with small pictures: man (example), dolphins, frightened, stars, website, sail, hungry, upstairs, laptop. Three words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

The story: Last Saturday, Grandma gave Tim a present – a book about a man called Dan Sky. When Dan was young, he swam in the sea every day. One day, he swam with (1) ____ next to his boat! Then Dan sailed round the world in a very small boat. Sometimes it was very windy and the waves were big, but Dan was never (2) ____. At night, he looked at the (3) ____ and the moon. After his trip, Dan made a (4) ____ with lots of photos. Tim loved the book. "Can I learn to (5) ____, Grandma?" he asked. "Yes, but first you must learn to swim well!" Grandma laughed.

Choose the best name for the story: Grandma's new boat · Tim's book about Dan Sky · A day at the swimming pool

Answers: 1 dolphins · 2 frightened · 3 stars · 4 website · 5 sail. Extra: hungry, upstairs, laptop. Best name: Tim's book about Dan Sky.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child chooses a word card for each number and writes it. Three words are extra.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  4. Ask about Dan (5 min): ask: "What did Dan see next to his boat? Was he frightened? What did he look at at night?"
  5. Watch (3 min): Would you like…? Answer each question with the song.
  6. Brave things (6 min): your child says one brave thing they did: "I swam in the sea." and one brave thing they'd like to do one day.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Dan swam with dolphins next to his boat!" … "He was never frightened." … "Can I learn to sail?"
