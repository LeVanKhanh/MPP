Week 48 · Lesson 3: How Well Do You Know Me?

You Need

  • Four small word boxes for "My story" (below). Your child chooses one word or phrase from each box.
  • Paper for the quiz and a pencil.

Write a story: Who? a girl · a boy · a pirate · a monkey · Where? on an island · in the jungle · on the moon · on a ship · What did they do? found some treasure · swam with dolphins · climbed a mountain · met an alien · How were they? happy · frightened · surprised · tired · Start: One day, ____ …

How well do you know me? 1 What's my favourite food? · 2 What's my favourite animal? · 3 What would I like to do one day? · 4 Would I like to go to the moon? · 5 What did I do last weekend?

Answers: Story and quiz: any answers. One point for each right guess.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Choose the words (4 min): your child chooses one thing from each box: "A monkey – on the moon – met an alien – surprised!" Story words
  3. Write the story (9 min): your child writes four or five sentences: "One day, a monkey went to the moon. He met an alien…" and draws a picture.
  4. Read it to me (3 min): your child reads the story to you. Ask one question about it.
  5. How well do you know me? (9 min): your child writes a guess for each question about you. Then you say the right answers. Count the points! Then swap: you guess your child's answers.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "One day, a monkey went to the moon." … "I think your favourite food is noodles." … "Would you like to go to the moon? – No, I wouldn't!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Answer "Would you like to…?" about exciting things, and choose the best answer in a talk about a book.
  • Read a story and write the right word from a box next to each number, and choose the best name.
  • Write a short story from chosen words, and play "How well do you know me?".
