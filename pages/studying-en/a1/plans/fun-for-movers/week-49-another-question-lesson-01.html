Week 49 · Lesson 1: Can You Say That Again?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions and School. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 49: Ask me another question

Fun for Movers · Week 49: Ask me another question | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Can you say that again? Lesson 2 · Where is it? Lesson 3 · Ask me another question

You Need

  • The five word groups below on small papers. Your child puts the words in order.
  • Six picture cards for the spelling game (for example a kangaroo, a helicopter, an island, a parrot, a rainbow, a penguin).

Make sentences with these words: 1 this? / What's · 2 know / I / Sorry, / don't · 3 again, / please? / you / say / Can / that · 4 spell / you / How / do / "kangaroo"? · 5 mean / What / does / "island"?

Act it out: Mr Lee: What's this? · Kim: Sorry, I don't know. · Mr Lee: It's a kangaroo. · Kim: Can you say that again, please? · Mr Lee: It's a kangaroo. · Kim: How do you spell "kangaroo"? · Mr Lee: K-A-N-G-A-R-O-O. · Kim: K-A-N-G-A-R-O-O. OK! Thank you.

Answers: 1 What's this? · 2 Sorry, I don't know. · 3 Can you say that again, please? · 4 How do you spell "kangaroo"? · 5 What does "island" mean?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Classroom Language Song. Say the class sentences with the song.
  3. Make sentences (7 min): your child puts each word group in order and reads the sentence. Helpful sentences
  4. Act it out (5 min): you are Mr Lee, your child is Kim. Then swap and use a different animal.
  5. Spelling game (6 min): show a picture card. Your child asks: "What's this? How do you spell it?" You spell it and your child writes it. Then swap.
  6. Sorry, I don't know! (5 min): ask some silly questions: "What's the name of the moon's cat?" Your child says: "Sorry, I don't know!" Then ask real ones too.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Sorry, I don't know." … "Can you say that again, please?" … "How do you spell 'kangaroo'?"
