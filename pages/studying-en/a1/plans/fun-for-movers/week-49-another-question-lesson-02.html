Week 49 · Lesson 2: Where Is It?

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and At Home. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 49: Ask me another question

Fun for Movers · Week 49: Ask me another question | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Can you say that again? Lesson 2 · Where is it? Lesson 3 · Ask me another question

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a living room: a clock above the window · a photo below the clock · a cat behind the sofa · a ball under the table · a fish in a bowl · a hat on the lamp · a map of the world next to the door.
  • The two columns below. Your child draws lines to join them.
  • A small toy to hide.

Where are these things? Draw lines. The clock is · The photo is · The cat is · The ball is · The fish is · The hat is · The map of the world is — in the bowl. · on the lamp. · next to the door. · above the window. · behind the sofa. · below the clock. · under the table.

Answers: The clock is above the window · the photo is below the clock · the cat is behind the sofa · the ball is under the table · the fish is in the bowl · the hat is on the lamp · the map of the world is next to the door.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Where Is My Dinosaur? Point and say where the dinosaur is.
  3. Talk about the picture (5 min): your child says where things are: "There's a cat behind the sofa." Where is it?
  4. Draw lines (6 min): your child joins each sentence start to its ending, then reads the seven sentences.
  5. Hide and find (6 min): hide the toy in the room. Your child asks: "Is it above the cupboard? Is it behind the door?" You answer. Then swap.
  6. My room (4 min): your child draws three things in their room and says: "My bag is next to my bed."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The clock is above the window." … "The photo is below the clock." … "Is it behind the door? – Yes, it is!"
