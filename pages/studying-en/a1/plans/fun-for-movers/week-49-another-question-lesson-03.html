Week 49 · Lesson 3: Ask Me Another Question

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Questions and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 49: Ask me another question

Fun for Movers · Week 49: Ask me another question | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Can you say that again? Lesson 2 · Where is it? Lesson 3 · Ask me another question

You Need

  • Paper with two boxes for drawing: "Yesterday and today" and "My bag".
  • The conversation below. Your child chooses A, B or C.

Answer the questions and draw: What was the weather like yesterday? It was ____. · What's the weather like today? It's ____ and ____. · What's your school bag like? It's ____ and it's got ____ on it.

Read and choose the best answer: 1 Mr Lee: How old are you, Kim? Kim: A Fine, thanks. B Kim Brown. C I'm nine. · 2 Mr Lee: Who do you sit next to in class? Kim: A My friend Lucy. B At the end of the day. C In the playground. · 3 Mr Lee: What do you do after school? Kim: A My bike is red. B I play football in the park. C Yes, I do. · 4 Mr Lee: Have you got any brothers or sisters? Kim: A Her name's Kim. B Brothers are funny. C Yes, I've got one sister. · 5 Mr Lee: What's your favourite food? Kim: A I drew a cake. B Pancakes, I think. C They aren't apples.

Find the question: "It's blue and white." · "At seven o'clock." · "My grandma." · "Yes, I can!"

Listen and answer: What's your name? · How old are you? · Who is in your family? · What do you like doing at the weekend? · What did you do yesterday? · What would you like to do one day?

Answers: Weather and bag: any true answers. Choose: 1 C · 2 A · 3 B · 4 C · 5 B. Find the question, for example: What's your bag like? · When do you get up? · Who made this cake? · Can you swim?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Answer and draw (7 min): your child answers the three questions in full sentences and draws the weather and their bag.
  3. Read and choose (6 min): you are Mr Lee, your child is Kim. Your child chooses A, B or C and says it.
  4. Find the question (5 min): read an answer: "At seven o'clock." Your child makes a question for it: "When do you get up?"
  5. Listen and answer (7 min): ask the six questions like a teacher. Your child answers in full sentences. If they don't understand, they say: "Can you say that again, please?" Questions
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It rained yesterday." … "My bag is red and it's got a rainbow on it." … "I'd like to sail round the world one day!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say helpful class sentences: "Sorry, I don't know." "Can you say that again, please?" "How do you spell…?"
  • Say where things are with above, below, behind, next to, in and on.
  • Answer questions about the weather and your things, choose the best answer, and answer questions about yourself.
