Week 50 · Lesson 1: Words That Rhyme

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Animals. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 50: Well done!

Fun for Movers · Week 50: Well done! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Words that rhyme Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Well done!

You Need

  • Ten picture cards (draw them): a cake, a whale, a frog, a fly, a bear, a star, a boat, a bee, a mouse, a hat.
  • Before the lesson, write these letters in a big circle with no spaces: laketaildogskychaircargoattreehousecat.

Find words that sound the same at the end: Look at each picture. Find a word in the circle that rhymes with it. Example: cake – lake.

Silly rhymes: A cat in a hat. · A goat in a boat. · A bee in a tree. · A mouse in a house.

Answers: cake – lake · whale – tail · frog – dog · fly – sky · bear – chair · star – car · boat – goat · bee – tree · mouse – house · hat – cat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Does It Rhyme? Say "Yes!" or "No!" before the video.
  3. Name the pictures (3 min): your child names each picture card.
  4. Find the rhymes (8 min): your child finds a word in the circle for each picture and writes the pair: "frog – dog". Rhyming words
  5. Rhyme or not? (5 min): say two words: "boat – goat?" Your child says: "Yes, they rhyme!" "car – cat?" "No!"
  6. Silly rhymes (7 min): read the silly rhymes. Your child makes two more and draws one: "A bear on a chair!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Cake and lake rhyme!" … "A goat in a boat." … "A bear on a chair!"
