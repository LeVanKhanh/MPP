Week 50 · Lesson 2: Listen and Tick

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: School and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 50: Well done!

Fun for Movers · Week 50: Well done! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A1 Movers

Lesson 1 · Words that rhyme Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Well done!

You Need

  • Six questions with three small pictures each (draw them, label A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.

Script: Example – Boy: Which girl is Lucy? Girl: Lucy is the girl who's reading a book. · 1 Mum: Have you got your crayons, Mia? Mia: Yes, but I need my glasses! I can't see the board! · 2 Dad: Is Tom in the kitchen? Mum: No, he's upstairs in his bedroom. · 3 Girl: Is that a football game on your computer, Ben? Ben: No, it's a photo of my grandpa's farm. · 4 Man: Did you play the piano at the school party, Sam? Sam: No, I played the guitar, and my friend sang. · 5 Mum: Do you want to do your homework now, Jim? Jim: No, I'm thirsty. Can I have some water, please?

Listen and tick (✓) the box: Example: Which girl is Lucy? A swimming B reading C sailing · 1 What does Mia need? A crayons B a skirt C glasses · 2 Where is Tom now? A the living room B his bedroom C the kitchen · 3 What is on Ben's computer? A a farm B a plant C a football · 4 What did Sam play at the party? A the piano B the guitar C a song · 5 What does Jim want to do now? A write B watch TV C drink some water

My favourite things: What was your favourite story this year? · Your favourite song or video? · Your favourite game? · What can you do in English now?

Answers: 1 C · 2 B · 3 A · 4 B · 5 C.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (4 min): your child names the small pictures for each question.
  3. Listen and tick (9 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C for each question.
  4. Be the teacher (6 min): your child reads the script to you, and you tick the boxes. Your child checks your answers!
  5. My favourite things (6 min): ask the four questions. Your child answers in full sentences. Look back at the menu of weeks together if you like.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I need my glasses!" … "He's upstairs in his bedroom." … "My favourite story was the pirate film!"
