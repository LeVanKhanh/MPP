Week 50 · Lesson 3: Well Done!

30 minutes for young learners (7–10). Lesson pages: Opposites and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Movers – Unit 50: Well done!

Lesson 1 · Words that rhyme Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Well done!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw twelve stepping stones on paper, from START to END. Write one letter on each: b, c, d, f, h, k, m, p, r, s, t, w. A dice and two small toys.
  • Paper and crayons for a "Well done!" card.

Opposites race: hot · loud · strong · first · upstairs · top · inside · straight · clean · before · better · boring

Stepping stones: Roll the dice and move. Say a word with the letter on your stone. The other player chooses: an animal, a food, a place or an action. Example: m – an animal – monkey!

Well done! Well done, ____! In English, I can… 1 ____ 2 ____ 3 ____

Answers: Opposites: cold · quiet · weak · last · downstairs · bottom · outside · curly · dirty · after · worse · exciting. Stepping stones and card: any good answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): I Love to Rhyme. Sing and clap the rhymes.
  3. Opposites race (6 min): say a word: "loud!" Your child says the opposite fast: "quiet!" Then swap. Opposites
  4. Stepping stones (9 min): play the game. If your child can't think of a word, they can say "Sorry, I don't know" and stay on the stone.
  5. Well done! (7 min): your child makes a "Well done!" card and writes three things they can do in English now. Say: "Well done! I'm very happy!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Loud – quiet! Strong – weak!" … "M – an animal – monkey!" … "In English, I can tell a story!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find words that rhyme: cake – lake, frog – dog, bee – tree.
  • Listen and tick the right picture, and talk about your favourite things from the book.
  • Say opposites fast, play a word game from A to Z, and say what you can do now.
