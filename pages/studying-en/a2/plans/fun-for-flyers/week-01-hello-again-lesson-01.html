Week 1 · Lesson 1: Our School

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: School and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 1: Hello again

Fun for Flyers · Week 1: Hello again | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school Lesson 2 · My favourite game Lesson 3 · All about me

You Need

  • A photo of your child's school, or paper for your child to draw it: the building, the sky, children in the playground.
  • Ten word cards: the sky, the roof, a seat, a backpack, a bicycle, the grass, sunglasses, a skateboard, a gate, a flag.
  • Some small things to move around on the picture for the memory game (a coin, a paper backpack, a paper skateboard).

Write the missing words about your picture: The children in my picture are about ____ years old. · A girl in a ____ sweater is standing on a ____. · A boy is sitting on the ____. He's interested in ____. · A backpack is next to the ____. It's ____.

Answers: Any answers that match your child's picture.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): School Supplies Vocabulary Chant. Say the words you know before the chant.
  3. Label the picture (7 min): your child draws the school (or uses the photo) and puts the word cards in the right places: "The flag is on the roof." School words
  4. Write the missing words (6 min): your child completes the six sentences about their own picture.
  5. What's different? (6 min): your child closes their eyes. Move three or four things on the picture. Your child says what is different: "Before, the backpack was on the seat. Now it's under the seat."
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "What's the best thing about your school? What would you like to change?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The flag is on the roof." … "He's interested in skateboarding." … "Before, the backpack was on the seat. Now it's under the seat."
