Week 1 · Lesson 2: My Favourite Game

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 1: Hello again

Fun for Flyers · Week 1: Hello again | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school Lesson 2 · My favourite game Lesson 3 · All about me

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  • The same form, empty, for your child's own favourite game.

Script: Boy: Hi, Mia. What's your favourite game? · Mia: It's called Ocean Star. It's a game on my mum's tablet. · Boy: When did you get it? · Mia: Last birthday. My aunt gave it to me. · Boy: Who do you play it with? · Mia: With my cousin. We play it every Saturday. · Boy: What's it about? · Mia: There's a dolphin. His name is Bubbles – B-U-B-B-L-E-S. · Boy: What colour is he? · Mia: He's blue, but his tail is purple! · Boy: And what does he do? · Mia: He swims round the islands and he likes finding shells. You get a star for every shell!

Mia's favourite game: Example: Name of game: Ocean Star · 1 When got this game: last ____ · 2 Plays this game with: her ____ · 3 Name of dolphin in game: ____ · 4 Colour of dolphin's tail: ____ · 5 Dolphin likes finding: ____

Answers: 1 birthday · 2 cousin · 3 Bubbles · 4 purple · 5 shells.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): read the form together. Ask: "Is the answer a word, a name or a colour?"
  3. Listen and write (9 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word on each line. Check the spelling of Bubbles together.
  4. My favourite game (7 min): your child fills in the empty form for their own favourite game – a video game, an app or a board game – and tells you about it. Hobbies
  5. Talk about games (6 min): ask: "How often do you play games? Who do you play with? Are games better on a screen or on a table? Why?" Questions
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's called Ocean Star." … "I play it with my cousin every Saturday." … "I think board games are better because…"
