Week 1 · Lesson 3: All About Me

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Questions and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 1: Hello again

Fun for Flyers · Week 1: Hello again | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school Lesson 2 · My favourite game Lesson 3 · All about me

You Need

  • The six questions and seven answers below on small papers. One answer is extra.
  • Big paper and coloured pencils for an "All about me" poster.

Find the answer to each question: 1 How do you get to school in the morning? · 2 What do you and your friends like talking about? · 3 Where do you like going at the weekend? · 4 Which clothes do you like wearing most? · 5 What's your favourite app or game? · 6 Who do you sit next to in class? — a We talk about films and football. · b By bus, but sometimes my dad drives me. · c My best friend, Lily. · d To the swimming pool with my cousins. · e Jeans and a warm sweater. · f A drawing app on my mum's tablet. · g Yes, every day.

Say it: A loud brown mouse is running round the house!

Answers: 1 b · 2 a · 3 d · 4 e · 5 f · 6 c. Extra: g.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Sound of ou/ow. Say each ou/ow word with the song.
  3. Read and match (6 min): your child reads each question and finds the answer. One answer is extra – which one, and why doesn't it fit?
  4. Ask me (5 min): your child asks you the six questions. You answer, then your child answers them too. Questions
  5. Say it (3 min): your child says "A loud brown mouse is running round the house!" slowly, then fast. Find more words with the same sound: cloud, cow, now, out.
  6. All about me poster (8 min): your child makes a poster for a new class: their name, a picture, and five sentences about school, friends, clothes and games.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I get to school by bus." … "My favourite app is a drawing app." … "A loud brown mouse is running round the house!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things around a school (roof, seat, backpack, sunglasses) and say what is different in a picture.
  • Listen and write details about someone's favourite game, and describe your own.
  • Match questions and answers about yourself, say the /aʊ/ sound in brown, cloud and mouse, and make an "All about me" poster.
