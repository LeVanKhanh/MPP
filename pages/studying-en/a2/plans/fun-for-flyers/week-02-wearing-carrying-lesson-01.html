Week 2 · Lesson 1: What Is It?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Clothes and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 2: Wearing and carrying

Fun for Flyers · Week 2: Wearing and carrying | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · At the castle Lesson 3 · Why are you together?

You Need

  • A word box on paper: a backpack · gloves · a belt · pockets · an umbrella · suitcases · trainers · a crown · a ring · a watch · a necklace · a coat. Two words are extra.
  • Some real things from home: a belt, gloves, a ring or a watch, an umbrella.

Read and write the correct words from the box: Example: You carry this on your back when you go to school or walk in the mountains. a backpack · 1 You put these on your hands when it's snowing. · 2 You wear this round your trousers. · 3 You can put money or keys in these on your jeans. · 4 You open this when it's raining. · 5 You put your clothes in these when you go on holiday. · 6 People wear these on their feet when they run or do sport. · 7 A king or a queen wears this on their head. · 8 You wear this on your finger. · 9 You wear this on your arm and it tells you the time.

Answers: 1 gloves · 2 a belt · 3 pockets · 4 an umbrella · 5 suitcases · 6 trainers · 7 a crown · 8 a ring · 9 a watch. Extra: a necklace, a coat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Accessories – Kids Vocab. Say each word before the voice does.
  3. Real things (4 min): show the real things. Your child names them and puts them on: "I'm wearing gloves." Clothes
  4. Read and write (9 min): your child reads each clue and writes the word from the box. Two words are extra.
  5. Make a clue (4 min): your child makes a clue for a necklace or a coat. You guess. Then you make one.
  6. Spell it (3 min): say a word from the box. Your child spells it without looking: S-U-I-T-C-A-S-E.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You open this when it's raining. – An umbrella!" … "I'm wearing my dad's gloves." … "You wear a ring on your finger."
