Week 2 · Lesson 2: At the Castle

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Clothes and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 2: Wearing and carrying

Fun for Flyers · Week 2: Wearing and carrying | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · At the castle Lesson 3 · Why are you together?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a castle garden: a king with a ring and a gold crown at a round table · a queen with a crown · a boy with a scarf playing the guitar · a girl carrying apples · three dogs under the table · a woman giving a letter to the queen · a man with a drum.
  • Write six names around the picture: Henry, Anna, Tom, Grace, Robert, Lucy. Read the script twice; your child draws lines.

Look and read. Circle the correct word. 1 The king is wearing a (ring / glove) on his finger. · 2 There are (two / three) dogs under the table. · 3 The girl is carrying some (apples / eggs). · 4 The boy with the guitar is wearing a (scarf / belt). · 5 The table in the garden is (round / square). · 6 The queen has a (crown / umbrella) on her head.

Script: Welcome to Blue River Castle! Can you see the king at the table? His name is Henry. · The queen is next to him. That's Anna. · Tom is the boy with the guitar. He plays music for the king every evening. · Grace is the girl with the apples. She's the queen's best friend. · Robert is the man with the drum. · And Lucy is the woman with the letter. It's a very important letter – it's for the queen!

Listen again and answer: 1 What is the name of the castle? · 2 Who plays music for the king? · 3 Who is the queen's best friend? · 4 Who is the important letter for?

Answers: Circle: 1 ring · 2 three · 3 apples · 4 scarf · 5 round · 6 crown. Lines: Henry – king · Anna – queen · Tom – boy with the guitar · Grace – girl with the apples · Robert – man with the drum · Lucy – woman with the letter. Answers: 1 Blue River Castle · 2 Tom · 3 Grace · 4 the queen.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look and read (6 min): your child reads each sentence, looks at the picture and circles the correct word.
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  4. Listen again (5 min): read the script once more. Your child answers the four questions.
  5. Watch (2 min): Kings and Queens Dress Up. Dress up like a king or a queen with the song.
  6. Who am I? (5 min): your child is someone in the picture: "I'm wearing a gold crown and I'm sitting at the table." You guess: "You're Henry!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The king is wearing a ring on his finger." … "Tom plays music for the king." … "The letter is for the queen!"
