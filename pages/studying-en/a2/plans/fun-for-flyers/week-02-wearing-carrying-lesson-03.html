Week 2 · Lesson 3: Why Are You Together?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Clothes and My Body. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 2: Wearing and carrying

Fun for Flyers · Week 2: Wearing and carrying | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · At the castle Lesson 3 · Why are you together?

You Need

  • Twelve picture cards: a helmet, a ring, a scarf, trainers, shorts, sunglasses, gloves, a belt, a necklace, an umbrella, a suitcase, a backpack.
  • Body word cards: head, neck, finger, hands, feet, legs, eyes.

Where do we wear these? a helmet · a ring · a scarf · trainers · shorts · sunglasses · gloves · a necklace — head · neck · finger · hands · feet · legs · eyes

Why are you together? Example: gloves and a scarf – "You wear them when it's cold." · a suitcase and a backpack · sunglasses and shorts · a ring and a necklace · a helmet and trainers

Answers: helmet – head · ring – finger · scarf – neck · trainers – feet · shorts – legs · sunglasses – eyes · gloves – hands · necklace – neck. Together: any good reason, for example "You carry them when you travel." "You wear them when it's hot and sunny."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Where do we wear these? (6 min): your child puts each picture card next to a body card and says: "You wear a helmet on your head." My body
  3. Why are you together? (8 min): your child takes two cards and says why they go together: "A ring and a necklace – they're both gold!" Any good reason wins a point. Take turns.
  4. Pack your suitcase (6 min): "I'm going on holiday. In my suitcase I've got sunglasses." "…sunglasses and a scarf." Take turns and add one thing each time.
  5. Draw and write (5 min): your child draws themself ready for a cold day or a hot day and writes three sentences about what they are wearing and carrying.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "You wear a helmet on your head." … "Gloves and a scarf – you wear them when it's cold." … "In my suitcase I've got sunglasses and a scarf."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things we wear and carry (belt, gloves, ring, suitcase, umbrella) and read a clue to find the right word.
  • Look at a castle picture, choose the right word, and listen for names and details.
  • Say where we wear things and why two things go together.
