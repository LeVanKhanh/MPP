Week 3 · Lesson 1: Flags With Spots and Stripes

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Colours and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 3: Spots and stripes

Fun for Flyers · Week 3: Spots and stripes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Flags with spots and stripes Lesson 2 · At the airport Lesson 3 · Find the picture

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw six flags and number them: 1 blue with white spots · 2 three green stripes · 3 yellow with a red star · 4 black and white stripes with a small ship · 5 orange and purple stripes · 6 white with one big red spot.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for your child's own flag.

Complete the sentences. Use 1, 2 or 3 words. Example: Flag 1 is blue with white spots. · 1 Flag 2 has got three ____. · 2 There's a red ____ on flag 3. · 3 The flag with a small ship on it has got ____ stripes. · 4 Flag 5 is striped. The stripes are orange and ____. · 5 Flag 6 has got only one ____ on it.

Answers: 1 green stripes · 2 star · 3 black and white · 4 purple · 5 (big) red spot.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Patterns in English. Find something with spots or stripes at home.
  3. Spots or stripes? (4 min): point to clothes at home: "Is it spotted or striped?" Your child answers: "It's striped. It's got blue and white stripes." Patterns
  4. Complete the sentences (7 min): your child looks at the six flags and writes one, two or three words in each sentence.
  5. Which flag? (4 min): say: "This flag has got a star on it." Your child points: "Flag 3!" Then your child says one for you.
  6. My flag (6 min): your child designs a flag for their family or class with spots, stripes or stars, and writes two sentences about it.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Flag 2 has got three green stripes." … "My T-shirt is striped." … "My flag is red with yellow spots."
