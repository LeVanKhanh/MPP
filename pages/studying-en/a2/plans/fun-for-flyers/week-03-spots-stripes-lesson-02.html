Week 3 · Lesson 2: At the Airport

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 3: Spots and stripes

Fun for Flyers · Week 3: Spots and stripes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Flags with spots and stripes Lesson 2 · At the airport Lesson 3 · Find the picture

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw an airport: a big clock · four children · a girl crying next to her grandmother · a man reading a newspaper · a pile of suitcases · a boy in a jacket listening to music · a plane through the window · passengers waiting.
  • Coloured pencils. Read the instructions twice; your child colours and writes.

Write your own answers (there are no wrong answers – use your ideas!): 1 How old are the four children? · 2 Why is the girl crying? · 3 What's in the suitcases? · 4 What's the man reading about? · 5 What song is the boy in the jacket listening to? · 6 Now write one sentence about the passengers or the airport.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the boy's jacket green with black stripes. · Colour the biggest suitcase red with white spots. · Write LONDON on the plane. · Colour the man's newspaper yellow. · Draw a big smile on the grandmother.

Answers: Questions 1–6: any answers that make sense. Colour and write: green striped jacket, red spotted suitcase, LONDON on the plane, yellow newspaper, smiling grandmother.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Airport Vocabulary. Say the airport words you know.
  3. Talk about the picture (4 min): your child says what the people are doing: "A man is reading a newspaper." Travel words
  4. Your own answers (9 min): your child writes an answer to questions 1–5 with their own ideas, then writes one sentence of their own.
  5. Listen and colour and write (6 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or writes.
  6. Tell me (2 min): your child reads their funniest answer to you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The girl is crying because she can't find her teddy." … "There are presents in the suitcases." … "The jacket is green with black stripes."
