Week 3 · Lesson 3: Find the Picture

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Colours and Describing People. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 3: Spots and stripes

Fun for Flyers · Week 3: Spots and stripes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Flags with spots and stripes Lesson 2 · At the airport Lesson 3 · Find the picture

You Need

  • Paper, pencils and coloured pencils for two people. A book to stand up between you so you can't see each other's paper.
  • The six flags from Lesson 1.

Can you find the picture I've written about? a This flag isn't striped. It's got lots of small spots on it. · b This flag has got stripes and a small ship. · c This flag hasn't got any stripes or spots, but it's got a star. · d This flag has got only one spot, and it's very big.

Answers: a flag 1 · b flag 4 · c flag 3 · d flag 6. Picture dictation: any differences you find.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find the flag (5 min): read each sentence a–d. Your child finds the flag and writes its number.
  3. Write about a flag (4 min): your child writes two sentences about flag 2 or flag 5. You find the flag.
  4. Draw what I say (8 min): stand the book between you. You draw a person in spotted or striped clothes and tell your child: "She's wearing a striped dress. It's got pink and blue stripes." Your child draws it. Then swap.
  5. Find the differences (5 min): look at the two pictures together. Your child says the differences: "In my picture, the dress has got spots, but in yours it's got stripes."
  6. Spot the stripes (3 min): your child finds five striped or spotted things in the house in one minute and names them.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "This flag has got only one spot." … "She's wearing a striped dress." … "In my picture the dress has got spots, but in yours it's got stripes."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what patterns things have got: spots, stripes, spotted, striped.
  • Write your own answers about a busy airport picture, and listen and colour and write.
  • Draw from a friend's words, find the differences, and find the picture someone has written about.
