Week 4 · Lesson 1: Leo's Friends

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Family and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 4: My friends and my pets

Fun for Flyers · Week 4: My friends and my pets | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

You Need

  • The talk questions below.
  • A paper with six names (Max, Zoe, Ben, Emma, Sam, Kate) on the left and six sentences (a–f) on the right. Read the script twice; your child draws lines.

Let's talk: Who lives in your house? Who's the oldest person? · Has anyone in your family got the same name as you? · Who sits next to you in class? · What do you do with your friends at the weekend? · Tell me about your best friend. What does he or she look like?

Script: Hi, I'm Leo. These are my friends at my birthday party. Max is my best friend. We do everything together! · Zoe is my cousin. She's really funny. · Ben has guitar lessons with me on Fridays. · Emma likes the same music as me. · Sam is a very loud singer! · And Kate goes sailing with my family in the summer.

Draw lines: a is Leo's best friend. · b is Leo's cousin. · c also has guitar lessons. · d likes the same music. · e is a loud singer. · f goes sailing with Leo's family.

Now write the names of people you know: 1 ____ likes the same music as me. · 2 ____ is the funniest person I know. · 3 ____ is a really good singer. · 4 ____ plays football very well.

Answers: Max – a · Zoe – b · Ben – c · Emma – d · Sam – e · Kate – f. People you know: any names.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Let's talk (6 min): ask the five questions. Your child answers in full sentences, then asks you two of them. Family sentences
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to a sentence.
  4. People I know (5 min): your child writes the names of real people in sentences 1–4 and tells you why. Family and friends
  5. Watch (3 min): A Friend Like You. What makes a good friend?
  6. A good friend (4 min): your child finishes: "A good friend is… A good friend always…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Max is Leo's best friend." … "My cousin is the funniest person I know." … "A good friend is kind and friendly."
