Week 4 · Lesson 2: Come and See Our New Flat!

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 4: My friends and my pets

Fun for Flyers · Week 4: My friends and my pets | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Leo's friends Lesson 2 · Come and see our new flat! Lesson 3 · Where are the h's?

You Need

  • The email below, written large. Your child writes one word on each line.
  • The form below for Listen and write.

Read the email and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Hi Ella! Come and see our new flat on Saturday. Saturday is the best day for us. It's easy to find us. Get (1) ____ the bus at the stop next to the park. You can also meet our new pet. It's a tortoise! He's very old, (2) ____ he's really friendly. He eats (3) ____ carrots than our rabbit! (4) ____ it's sunny, we can play in the garden. (5) ____ is your favourite game? See you! Leo

Script: Ella: Hi, Leo! I'm coming on Saturday. Where's the bus stop? · Leo: It's in Lake Street – L-A-K-E. · Ella: And your flat? · Leo: It's in Green Road, next to the park. · Ella: What number is it? · Leo: Number 17. · Ella: And what's your tortoise called? · Leo: Rocky! · Ella: Great name! And your rabbit? · Leo: She's called Snow, because she's white.

Saturday – go to Leo's flat! Example: Friend's name: Leo · 1 Bus stop is in: ____ Street · 2 Leo's flat is in: ____ Road · 3 Number of flat: ____ · 4 Tortoise's name: ____ · 5 Rabbit's name: ____

Answers: Email: 1 off · 2 but · 3 more · 4 If · 5 What. Form: 1 Lake · 2 Green · 3 17 · 4 Rocky · 5 Snow.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): read the email together. Your child writes one word on each line, then reads the email aloud.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number on each line of the form.
  4. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant. Which pet would you like?
  5. Write back (5 min): your child writes a short answer to Leo: "Hi Leo! Thanks! I'd love to come. My favourite game is…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Get off the bus next to the park." … "He's very old, but he's really friendly." … "If it's sunny, we can play in the garden."
