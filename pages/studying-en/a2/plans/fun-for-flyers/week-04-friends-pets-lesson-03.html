Week 4 · Lesson 3: Where Are the h's?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 4: My friends and my pets

Fun for Flyers · Week 4: My friends and my pets | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Leo's friends Lesson 2 · Come and see our new flat! Lesson 3 · Where are the h's?

You Need

  • Twelve word cards: hello, hat, horse, honey, white, who, what, hour, school, shoe, phone, three.
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a pet card.

Where are the h's? Read each word. Can you hear h? Put the card in one of two groups: "I can hear h" or "I can't hear h". hello · hat · horse · honey · white · who · what · hour · school · shoe · phone · three

My pet card (a real pet or a dream pet): Name: ____ · Animal: ____ · Age: ____ · Colour: ____ · It eats: ____ · It's funny / naughty because ____.

Answers: I can hear h: hello, hat, horse, honey, who. I can't hear h by itself: white, what, hour, school, shoe, phone, three (it makes a new sound with another letter, or it is quiet).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Silent Letters for Kids. Which letters are quiet?
  3. Where are the h's? (8 min): your child reads each card and puts it in a group. Then say the "can't hear" words together: sh, ch, th, ph, wh.
  4. h sentence (3 min): your child says: "Who has got a horse in a white house?" slowly, then fast.
  5. My pet card (7 min): your child makes a card for a real pet or a dream pet and draws it. Animals
  6. Show and tell (3 min): your child tells you about the pet. Ask: "What does it eat? Is it naughty?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I can hear h in horse, but I can't hear it in hour." … "Who has got a horse in a white house?" … "My dream pet is a parrot called Kiwi."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Talk about friends and family, and listen for who is who.
  • Write one missing word in each line of an email, and listen and write names and places.
  • Find the letter h in words and say if you can hear it, and write about a best friend or a pet.
