Week 5 · Lesson 1: How Do They Move?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 5: About animals

Fun for Flyers · Week 5: About animals | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · How do they move? Lesson 2 · Dinosaur days Lesson 3 · Dolphins or bats?

You Need

  • A table with five columns: run · fly · jump · swim · hop. Ten animal cards: kangaroo, eagle, crocodile, camel, dolphin, butterfly, frog, penguin, octopus, beetle.
  • The clues below and a word box: a zebra · a bee · a camel · a swan · an octopus · a butterfly · a polar bear · a tortoise · a beetle · a donkey · a parrot. Two words are extra.

Look and read. Write the animal. Example: This animal has got black and white stripes and it looks like a horse. a zebra · 1 This small insect makes honey. · 2 This animal lives in hot, dry places. It can walk for a long time without water. · 3 This big white bird swims on lakes and rivers. · 4 This sea animal has got eight long arms. · 5 This insect has got beautiful wings and flies from flower to flower. · 6 This animal is white and furry and lives where it's very cold. · 7 This animal moves very slowly and carries its home on its back. · 8 This small insect has got a hard shell and six legs.

Answers: How they move (some can go in two groups): run – camel, crocodile · fly – eagle, butterfly · jump – frog · swim – dolphin, octopus, penguin, crocodile · hop – kangaroo, frog · beetle – run or fly. Clues: 1 a bee · 2 a camel · 3 a swan · 4 an octopus · 5 a butterfly · 6 a polar bear · 7 a tortoise · 8 a beetle. Extra: a donkey, a parrot.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Insects Song. Move like each insect.
  3. How do they move? (6 min): your child puts each animal card in a column and says: "An eagle can fly." Some animals go in two columns! Animals
  4. Read and write (9 min): your child reads each clue and writes the animal from the box. Two words are extra.
  5. Animal mime (4 min): move like an animal. Your child guesses: "You're a tortoise! You're moving very slowly!" Then swap.
  6. Make a clue (3 min): your child writes a clue for a donkey or a parrot.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "An eagle can fly." … "A tortoise carries its home on its back." … "A beetle has got six legs and a hard shell."
