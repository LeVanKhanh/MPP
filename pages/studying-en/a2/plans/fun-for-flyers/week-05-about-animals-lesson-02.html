Week 5 · Lesson 2: Dinosaur Days

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 5: About animals

Fun for Flyers · Week 5: About animals | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.
  • Paper for a dinosaur fact card.

Dinosaurs: Dinosaurs lived (living / lives / lived) on Earth a very long time ago. Some dinosaurs were (1 small / smaller / smallest) than a chicken, but others were bigger than a bus! Many dinosaurs only ate plants, (2 but / because / than) some of them ate meat. Baby dinosaurs came out of eggs (3 in / on / at) nests. Today, people sometimes (4 finds / finding / find) dinosaur teeth in rocks. You can learn about (5 they / their / them) in museums and films.

Answers: 1 smaller · 2 but · 3 in · 4 find · 5 them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): What Happened to the Dinosaurs? Why aren't there any dinosaurs now?
  3. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and says why the other two are wrong.
  4. Bigger or smaller? (5 min): your child compares animals: "A dinosaur was bigger than a bus. A beetle is smaller than a mouse."
  5. Dinosaur card (8 min): your child makes a fact card about a dinosaur: name (a real one or a new one), how big, what it ate, where it lived, and a drawing.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Some dinosaurs were smaller than a chicken." … "Many dinosaurs only ate plants." … "My dinosaur was bigger than a house!"
