Week 5 · Lesson 3: Dolphins or Bats?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 5: About animals

Fun for Flyers · Week 5: About animals | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · How do they move? Lesson 2 · Dinosaur days Lesson 3 · Dolphins or bats?

You Need

  • The phrases below. Your child writes "of" or "about".
  • Two big cards: DOLPHINS and BATS.

Of or about? lots ____ dinosaurs · a story ____ a dolphin · 1 all kinds ____ insects · 2 a book ____ the jungle · 3 a pair ____ wings · 4 this part ____ the film · 5 a cartoon ____ wild animals · 6 a song ____ the sea

Dolphins or bats? It can fly. · It lives in the sea. · It sleeps in the day. · It can swim very fast. · It has got big ears. · It's grey. · It's very clever. · It eats fish.

Answers: lots of · a story about · 1 of · 2 about · 3 of · 4 of · 5 about · 6 about. Dolphins or bats: fly – bats · sea – dolphins · sleeps in the day – bats · swims fast – dolphins · big ears – bats · grey – both · clever – both · eats fish – dolphins (and some bats!).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Of or about? (7 min): your child writes "of" or "about" in each phrase. Tip: a book, a film or a song is "about" something.
  3. Make phrases (4 min): your child makes two more: "a film about…", "lots of…"
  4. Dolphins or bats? (7 min): read each sentence. Your child holds up DOLPHINS, BATS or both cards and says why.
  5. Animal fact file (7 min): your child chooses a wild animal and writes five sentences: where it lives, what it eats, how it moves, what it looks like, and one thing that is a surprise.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "A book about the jungle." … "A pair of wings." … "Bats sleep in the day, but dolphins don't."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say how animals move, and read a clue to find the animal: beetle, camel, swan, octopus, tortoise.
  • Read a short text about dinosaurs and choose the right word from three.
  • Choose "of" or "about" (a book about the jungle, a pair of wings), and tell dolphins and bats apart.
