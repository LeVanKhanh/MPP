Week 6 · Lesson 1: What's on It?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Toys and Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 6: My things

Fun for Flyers · Week 6: My things | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's on it? Lesson 2 · Who gave you that? Lesson 3 · Animal quiz

You Need

  • Six picture cards with numbers (draw them): 1 a scarf · 2 a diary · 3 a torch · 4 a comb · 5 a backpack · 6 a snowboard.
  • Eight small animal pictures with letters: A a bat · B a beetle · C a butterfly · D a swan · E a dolphin · F a shark · G an octopus · H an eagle. Two animals are extra.

Script: Look at my things! My scarf is my favourite. It's got butterflies on it. · On my diary there's a big eagle. · My torch has got a bat on it – it's good for the dark! · There's a swan on my comb. · My backpack has got an octopus on it. Its arms go all round the bag! · And my new snowboard has got a shark on it. Cool!

Answers: Words: 1 a scarf · 2 a diary · 3 a torch · 4 a comb · 5 a backpack · 6 a snowboard. Animals: scarf – C · diary – H · torch – A · comb – D · backpack – G · snowboard – F. Extra: B, E.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Kids Show and Tell: Favorite Toy. What did the children bring?
  3. What are these? (5 min): your child writes the word under each thing card and spells it to you. My things
  4. Listen (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter (A–H) next to each thing. Two animals are extra.
  5. Design it (6 min): your child draws one of their own things with an animal on it and says: "My ruler has got a beetle on it."
  6. Find it (3 min): say: "Find something with an animal on it!" Your child finds one at home and describes it.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "My torch has got a bat on it." … "There's a swan on my comb." … "My new snowboard has got a shark on it!"
