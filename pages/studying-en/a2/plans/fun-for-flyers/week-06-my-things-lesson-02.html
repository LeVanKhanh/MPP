Week 6 · Lesson 2: Who Gave You That?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Toys and Family. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 6: My things

Fun for Flyers · Week 6: My things | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's on it? Lesson 2 · Who gave you that? Lesson 3 · Animal quiz

You Need

  • The two halves of the sentences below on separate papers. Your child joins them.
  • Two cards for Ask and answer (below).

Join the two parts: 1 Leo's aunt bought this scarf for him · 2 His friend Kim chose this diary · 3 His dad got him this snowboard · 4 His grandmother gave him this torch · 5 Leo carries his books in this backpack · 6 His cousin found this comb — a last January. · b every day to school. · c at a shop in the mountains. · d on the beach in the summer. · e because Leo likes writing stories. · f because he's frightened of the dark.

Ask and answer: Mia's guitar – Colour? red · When / get? last summer · New / old? new · Who / give? her uncle · Where / now? in her bedroom. · Ben's drum – Colour? dark blue · When / get? last birthday · New / old? old · Who / give? his grandmother · Where / now? downstairs.

Answers: 1 c · 2 e · 3 a · 4 f · 5 b · 6 d. Questions, for example: What colour is Mia's guitar? When did she get it? Is it new or old? Who gave it to her? Where is it now?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Join the parts (8 min): your child joins each first part to its end and reads the six sentences aloud.
  3. Make questions (5 min): from the card words, your child makes full questions: "When did Mia get her guitar?"
  4. Ask and answer (7 min): you have Mia's card, your child has Ben's. Ask each other the five questions and answer in full sentences. Hobbies sentences
  5. Watch (5 min): Musical Instruments. Which instrument would you like to play?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who gave it to her? – Her uncle gave it to her." … "Leo carries his books in this backpack." … "His grandmother gave him this torch because he's frightened of the dark."
