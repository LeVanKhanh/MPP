Week 6 · Lesson 3: Animal Quiz

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and Toys. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 6: My things

Fun for Flyers · Week 6: My things | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's on it? Lesson 2 · Who gave you that? Lesson 3 · Animal quiz

You Need

  • Paper and pencils for two quizzes.
  • One special thing your child owns (a toy, a book, a present).

Example quiz questions: 1 Which animal can fly and sleeps in the day? · 2 Which insect makes honey? · 3 Which sea animal has got eight arms? · 4 Which bird is white and swims on lakes? · 5 Which animal carries its home on its back?

My special thing: What is it? · Who gave it to you? · When did you get it? · Is it new or old? · Where is it now? · Why is it special?

Answers: 1 a bat · 2 a bee · 3 an octopus · 4 a swan · 5 a tortoise (or a snail). My special thing: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Answer my quiz (5 min): read the five example questions. Your child answers quickly.
  3. Write a quiz (8 min): your child writes five animal questions for you, with the answers on the back. Animals
  4. Quiz time (5 min): you answer your child's quiz. Your child gives you points – and you can ask for a clue!
  5. My special thing (7 min): your child shows their special thing and answers the six questions in full sentences, like a show and tell at school.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Which insect makes honey? – A bee!" … "My grandfather gave it to me last year." … "It's special because…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name everyday things (a diary, a torch, a comb, a snowboard) and listen for the animal on each one.
  • Join two halves of a sentence about who gave something and why, and ask and answer about someone's things.
  • Make an animal quiz, and talk about a special thing of your own.
