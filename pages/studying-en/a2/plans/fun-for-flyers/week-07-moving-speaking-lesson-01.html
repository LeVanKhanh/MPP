Week 7 · Lesson 1: Eyes, Ears, Mouth, Nose or Hands?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Actions and My Body. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 7: Moving and speaking

Fun for Flyers · Week 7: Moving and speaking | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Eyes, ears, mouth, nose or hands? Lesson 2 · Whisper or shout? Lesson 3 · Max finds the goats

You Need

  • A table with five columns: eyes · ears · mouth · nose · hands.
  • Twenty-four action cards: bounce, smell, catch, shout, see, cook, whistle, carry, throw, hear, whisper, speak, push, pull, laugh, hold, build, clap, chat, taste, watch, sing, call, listen.

Which part do we use? Put each action in a column. Some actions can go in two columns!

Answers: eyes – see, watch · ears – hear, listen · mouth – shout, whistle, whisper, speak, laugh, chat, taste, sing, call · nose – smell · hands – bounce, catch, cook, carry, throw, push, pull, hold, build, clap. (Taste can be mouth; cook can be hands and nose – any good reason is fine.)

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): I Have Five Senses. Point to each part of your body.
  3. Sort the actions (8 min): your child puts each card in a column and says: "We whistle with our mouth." Actions
  4. Act it out (5 min): take a card and act it without words. Your child guesses: "You're pulling something!" Then swap.
  5. Simon says (5 min): "Simon says whisper your name! Simon says hold your nose!" If you don't say "Simon says", your child doesn't do it.
  6. My senses (4 min): your child finishes: "I love the smell of… I love the taste of… I love the sound of…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We whistle with our mouth." … "You're pulling a heavy box!" … "I love the smell of pancakes."
