Week 7 · Lesson 2: Whisper or Shout?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Actions and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 7: Moving and speaking

Fun for Flyers · Week 7: Moving and speaking | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Eyes, ears, mouth, nose or hands? Lesson 2 · Whisper or shout? Lesson 3 · Max finds the goats

You Need

  • A word box: hear · whisper · decide · guess · believe · remember · smell. One word is extra.
  • Ten short sentences on cards for the voices game (for example: "I'm hungry!" "Where's my sock?" "It's a secret.").

Complete each sentence with a word from the box: 1 The music is too loud! I can't ____ what you're saying. · 2 Don't tell anyone! Come here and I'll ____ it in your ear. · 3 I can't ____ which film to watch – they all look good! · 4 Can you ____ what's in this box? It's round and it's a present. · 5 My brother says he saw a dinosaur in the garden, but I don't ____ him! · 6 I can't ____ where I put my keys.

Answers: 1 hear · 2 whisper · 3 decide · 4 guess · 5 believe · 6 remember. Extra: smell.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Sing, Speak, Whisper, Shout. Change your voice with the song.
  3. Complete the sentences (8 min): your child reads each sentence and writes a word from the box. One word is extra.
  4. Voices game (6 min): your child takes a sentence card. You say: "Whisper it!" "Shout it!" "Sing it!" "Say it very slowly!" Your child says it that way. Then swap.
  5. Guess what it is (5 min): put something in a bag. Your child can touch and smell but not look: "I guess it's a… because…"
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "When do you whisper? When do you shout? Who do you believe?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I can't hear what you're saying!" … "I can't decide which film to watch." … "I guess it's an orange because I can smell it."
