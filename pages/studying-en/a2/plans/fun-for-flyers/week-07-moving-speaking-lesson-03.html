Week 7 · Lesson 3: Max Finds the Goats

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Farm and Countryside and Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 7: Moving and speaking

Fun for Flyers · Week 7: Moving and speaking | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Eyes, ears, mouth, nose or hands? Lesson 2 · Whisper or shout? Lesson 3 · Max finds the goats

You Need

  • A word box: town (example) · windy · followed · hill · laughed · biscuits · actor · engine · cloudy · excited. Four words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–5. My name is Sam. I live in a town, but last October I visited my grandparents' farm. One very (1) ____ morning, Grandpa came into the kitchen. "The goats aren't in their field!" he said. We all ran outside. Grandpa's clever dog, Max, (2) ____ us up the (3) ____ behind the farm. There we found the goats. They were eating Grandma's flowers! Grandma (4) ____ and said, "Naughty goats!" That evening, Max was very tired, but he wasn't too tired to eat his favourite dog (5) ____!

Choose the best name for this story: Max finds the goats · Grandma's new flowers · A day in town

Write words to complete the sentences: 1 Sam visited the farm in ____. · 2 The goats weren't in their ____. · 3 The goats were eating ____.

Answers: 1 windy · 2 followed · 3 hill · 4 laughed · 5 biscuits. Extra: actor, engine, cloudy, excited. Best name: Max finds the goats. Sentences: 1 October · 2 field · 3 Grandma's flowers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number. Four words are extra.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why the other two are wrong.
  4. Complete the sentences (4 min): your child writes one, two or three words in each sentence.
  5. Tell it from Max (6 min): your child tells the story as Max the dog: "One windy morning, I heard Grandpa shout…"
  6. Draw it (4 min): your child draws the best part of the story and writes one sentence under it.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Max followed us up the hill." … "The goats were eating Grandma's flowers!" … "He wasn't too tired to eat his biscuits."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say which part of the body we use for actions: whistle, whisper, taste, smell, push, pull.
  • Complete sentences with hear, whisper, decide, guess, believe and remember, and use different voices.
  • Read a story, choose a word from a box for each gap, and choose the best name.
