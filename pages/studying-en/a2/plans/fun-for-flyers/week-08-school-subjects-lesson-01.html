You Need

  • Eight word cards with the vowels missing: _rt · g__gr_phy · h_st_ry · sp_rt · l_ng__g_s · m_ths · m_s_c · sc__nc_. Your child writes a, e, i, o or u.
  • The clues below.

Which subject is it? 1 In this subject you learn about countries, mountains, rivers and maps. · 2 In this lesson you learn about people who lived a long time ago. · 3 You learn about plants and animals and find out how things work. · 4 You count and add numbers. · 5 You learn to speak English or another language. · 6 You learn songs and play instruments. · 7 You run, jump and play team games.

Answers: Vowels: art, geography, history, sport, languages, maths, music, science. Clues: 1 geography · 2 history · 3 science · 4 maths · 5 languages · 6 music · 7 sport.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): School Subjects Song. Say each subject before the singer.
  3. Missing letters (5 min): your child writes the missing vowels and reads each subject. School subjects
  4. Which subject? (7 min): read each clue. Your child writes the subject.
  5. My timetable (6 min): your child writes their school subjects for Monday and Tuesday and says: "On Monday I've got maths and art."
  6. Talk (4 min): ask: "What's your favourite subject? Why? Which subject is the most difficult?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "In geography we learn about countries." … "On Monday I've got maths and art." … "My favourite subject is science because…"
