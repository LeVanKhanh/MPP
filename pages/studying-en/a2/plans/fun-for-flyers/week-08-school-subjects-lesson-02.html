Week 8 · Lesson 2: A School Trip

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: School and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 8: School subjects

Fun for Flyers · Week 8: School subjects | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What do you learn? Lesson 2 · A school trip Lesson 3 · An email from school

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  • A word box for the art sentences: artists · draw · paintings · drawing · paints.

Script: Teacher: Listen, everyone! On Wednesday we're going to the museum in the city. We meet at the school gate at nine o'clock. · Boy: What can we see there? · Teacher: There are some beautiful paintings by Lucy Brown. That's B-R-O-W-N. · Girl: How do we get there? · Teacher: By bus. It's bus number 25. · Boy: Is there anything about animals? · Teacher: Yes! In the museum shop you can buy a book about eagles. · Girl: When do our parents come? · Teacher: At half past three. · Boy: And lunch? · Teacher: Bring some cheese sandwiches and a drink.

Wednesday – school trip: Example: Meet at: the school gate · 1 See paintings by: Lucy ____ · 2 Bus number: ____ · 3 Book about: ____ · 4 Time parents should come: ____ · 5 For lunch, bring: ____ and a drink

Complete the sentences about art: 1 In art, our teacher shows us how to ____ with pencils. · 2 Picasso and Monet were famous ____. · 3 I'm very good at ____ animals. · 4 You need brushes, water and ____ for this lesson. · 5 In the museum we looked at lots of ____.

Answers: Form: 1 Brown · 2 25 · 3 eagles · 4 half past three (3.30) · 5 cheese sandwiches. Art: 1 draw · 2 artists · 3 drawing · 4 paints · 5 paintings.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): read the form together. Ask: "Is the answer a name, a number or a time?"
  3. Listen and write (9 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  4. Art sentences (6 min): your child completes the five art sentences with words from the box.
  5. Draw and tell (7 min): your child draws a picture in five minutes, then tells you about it like an artist: "In my painting there's…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We meet at the school gate." … "Parents should come at half past three." … "I'm good at drawing animals."
