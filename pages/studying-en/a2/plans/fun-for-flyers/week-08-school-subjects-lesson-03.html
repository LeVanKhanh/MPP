Week 8 · Lesson 3: An Email From School

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: School and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 8: School subjects

Fun for Flyers · Week 8: School subjects | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What do you learn? Lesson 2 · A school trip Lesson 3 · An email from school

You Need

  • The email below. Your child writes one word on each line.
  • The conversation below. Your child chooses A, B or C.

Read the email and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Hi Ben, I'm writing to you because you weren't at school today. In history we learned (1) ____ castles. It was really interesting! Some castles are more (2) ____ 900 years old. For homework, we (3) ____ to draw a castle and write five sentences about it. You can find (4) ____ more on the school website. Please bring your homework (5) ____ Friday. See you tomorrow! Kim

Choose the best answer: 1 What's your favourite subject? A On Mondays. B Science, because I like finding out how things work. C My teacher's name is Mrs Lee. · 2 Which subject don't you like? A I don't like maths very much. B Yes, I do. C It's in my bag. · 3 What did you do in art today? A We painted pictures of the sea. B It's a pencil. C Every Tuesday. · 4 How long is your lunch break? A A sandwich, please. B At school. C About an hour. · 5 What would you like to learn about? A Space and the planets! B I'm fine, thanks. C I learned it yesterday.

Answers: Email: 1 about · 2 than · 3 have · 4 out · 5 on (or by). Choose: 1 B · 2 A · 3 A · 4 C · 5 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): read the email together. Your child writes one word on each line, then reads it aloud.
  3. Choose the best answer (6 min): you ask the questions, your child chooses A, B or C and says it.
  4. Your answers (5 min): ask the five questions again. Your child gives their own real answers. Talk About Your Favourite Subject
  5. Write an email (5 min): your child writes a short email to a friend about their school day: "Today in science we…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Some castles are more than 900 years old." … "You can find out more on the website." … "I don't like maths very much."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell and name school subjects – art, geography, history, maths, science – and say what you learn in each.
  • Listen and write details about a school trip, and complete sentences about art.
  • Write one missing word in each line of an email, and choose the best answers in a talk about school.
