Week 9 · Lesson 1: What Is It For?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 9: In my classroom

Fun for Flyers · Week 9: In my classroom | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What is it for? Lesson 2 · Two classes Lesson 3 · ee or ea?

You Need

  • Real classroom things if you have them: glue, scissors, a dictionary, a calendar, a file, a ruler.
  • The first and second halves of the sentences below on separate papers.

Find the two halves: 1 Glue: you can use it · 2 Scissors: you use them · 3 A dictionary: you look in it · 4 A bin: you put things in it · 5 A calendar: you look at it · 6 A file: you keep your work in it · 7 A shelf: you put books on it — a when you want to find the date. · b when you don't want them any more. · c to put two pieces of paper together. · d so they don't get lost. · e to cut paper or card. · f when you don't know what a word means. · g so the desks aren't too full.

Answers: 1 c · 2 e · 3 f · 4 b · 5 a · 6 d · 7 g.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): School Supplies – What's in My Bag? Say each thing before the video does.
  3. Real things (4 min): show the real things. Your child names them and says what they are for: "You use scissors to cut paper." Classroom words
  4. Find the two halves (8 min): your child joins each first half to its second half and reads the seven sentences.
  5. What am I? (5 min): your child gives a clue for another classroom thing: "You use me to draw straight lines." You guess: "A ruler!" Then swap.
  6. My dream classroom (4 min): your child says three things they would like in their classroom: "I'd like a big shelf full of comics."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You use scissors to cut paper." … "You look in a dictionary when you don't know what a word means." … "You put things in a bin when you don't want them."
