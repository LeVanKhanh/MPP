Week 9 · Lesson 2: Two Classes

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: School and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 9: In my classroom

Fun for Flyers · Week 9: In my classroom | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What is it for? Lesson 2 · Two classes Lesson 3 · ee or ea?

You Need

  • Five questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.
  • Two class cards for Ask and answer (below).

Script: 1 Mum: What's your first lesson today, Lily? Lily: Not maths – that's after lunch. First we've got history. · 2 Lily: For art we need scissors and paints… Oh, and glue! Mrs Lee said glue is the most important. · 3 Lily: Oh no! I forgot my dictionary. I've got my ruler and my glasses, but not my dictionary! · 4 Teacher: Put your files on the shelf, please, not in the cupboard. · 5 Lily: There's a competition at school next week. It isn't for drawing or music. It's for spelling!

Listen and tick: 1 What's the first lesson today? A maths B history C science · 2 What does Mrs Lee say is most important for art? A scissors B glue C paints · 3 What did Lily forget? A her dictionary B her ruler C her glasses · 4 Where should the students put their files? A in the cupboard B on the desk C on the shelf · 5 What kind of competition is it? A drawing B music C spelling

Ask and answer: Mr Green's class – Teacher: Mr Green · Desks: 12 · Studying: geography · On the wall: a map of the world · Lesson: easy. · Ms Hill's class – Teacher: Ms Hill · Desks: 20 · Studying: music · On the wall: a calendar · Lesson: difficult.

Answers: 1 B · 2 B · 3 A · 4 C · 5 C. Questions, for example: What's the teacher's name? How many desks are there? What are the children studying? What's on the wall? Is the lesson easy or difficult?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the small pictures for each question.
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  4. Make questions (4 min): your child makes full questions from the card: "How many desks are there?"
  5. Ask and answer (6 min): you have Mr Green's card, your child has Ms Hill's. Ask and answer the five questions.
  6. My class (4 min): your child answers the same five questions about their own class.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "First we've got history." … "How many desks are there? – There are twenty." … "There's a map of the world on the wall."
