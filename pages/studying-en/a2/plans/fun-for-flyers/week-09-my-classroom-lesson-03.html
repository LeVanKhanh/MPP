You Need

  • The six sentences below with the missing letters.
  • Paper and pencils for two people for the pair dictation.

Write ee or ea: 1 The qu__n is sitting under a gr__n tr__. · 2 Pl__se cl__n your desk this w__k. · 3 I had a dr__m about the s__ and the b__ch. · 4 Our t__cher r__ds us a story every day. · 5 This bag is very h__vy! · 6 Can I have a s__t n__r the window?

Pair dictation – Text A (you read): Our classroom is on the first floor. There are twenty desks and a big shelf with dictionaries. · Text B (your child reads): On the wall there's a calendar and a map. Our teacher's name is Mr Green.

Answers: 1 queen, green, tree · 2 Please, clean, week · 3 dream, sea, beach · 4 teacher, reads · 5 heavy · 6 seat, near.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Long Vowel e – ee/ea. Say each word with the song.
  3. ee or ea? (8 min): your child writes ee or ea in each word, then reads the sentences. Which ea word sounds different? (heavy)
  4. Word hunt (4 min): your child finds three more ee words and three more ea words in a book.
  5. Pair dictation (8 min): read Text A slowly; your child writes it. Then your child reads Text B and you write it. Check each other's spelling.
  6. Well done (2 min): count the right words together and choose one word to practise again.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The queen is sitting under a green tree." … "Please clean your desk this week." … "This bag is very heavy!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what classroom things are for: glue, scissors, a dictionary, a bin, a calendar, a file.
  • Listen and tick the right picture, and ask and answer about two different classes.
  • Spell words with ee and ea, and write what a partner reads in a dictation.
