Week 10 · Lesson 1: Our School Trip

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 10: Clothes, animals and school

Fun for Flyers · Week 10: Clothes, animals and school | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school trip Lesson 2 · Same first letter Lesson 3 · What's my word?

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  • Photos or memories of a trip your child went on (with school or family).

Script: Grandma: How was your school trip, Jack? · Jack: It was great! We went to the Rainbow Insect House. · Grandma: When did you go? · Jack: On Thursday. · Grandma: How did you get there? · Jack: We went by train. We left school at eight o'clock. · Grandma: What was the most unusual animal? · Jack: A blue beetle! It was as big as my hand. · Grandma: Wow! And where did you have lunch? · Jack: We had a picnic next to the lake.

Our school trip: Example: Place: the Rainbow Insect House · 1 Day of trip: ____ · 2 Went there by: ____ · 3 Left school at: ____ o'clock · 4 Most unusual animal: a blue ____ · 5 Had a picnic lunch by: the ____

Talk about your trip: Where did you go? · Who did you go with? · How did you get there? · What was the most interesting thing you saw? · What did you eat?

Answers: 1 Thursday · 2 train · 3 eight · 4 beetle · 5 lake. Talk: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): read the form together. Ask: "Is the answer a day, a number or an animal?"
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  4. Ask Jack (4 min): your child is Jack. You ask the questions; your child answers without the paper.
  5. My trip (6 min): ask the five questions about a real trip. Your child answers in full sentences. Animals
  6. Trip poster (4 min): your child draws the best part of the trip and writes two sentences.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We went by train." … "The most unusual animal was a blue beetle." … "We had a picnic next to the lake."
