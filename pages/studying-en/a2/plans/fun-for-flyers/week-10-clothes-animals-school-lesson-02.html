Week 10 · Lesson 2: Same First Letter

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Clothes and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 10: Clothes, animals and school

Fun for Flyers · Week 10: Clothes, animals and school | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school trip Lesson 2 · Same first letter Lesson 3 · What's my word?

You Need

  • The letter list below on paper. Your child writes two words for each letter.
  • The lesson pages from Units 1–9 to look back at (Clothes, School, Animals).

Find two words with the same first letter: a – apple and ____ · b – backpack and ____ · c – camel and ____ · d – dictionary and ____ · f – fur and ____ · g – glue and ____ · h – history and ____ · i – insect and ____ · l – language and ____ · m – maths and ____ · n – necklace and ____ · p – pocket and ____ · r – ring and ____ · s – scissors and ____ · t – torch and ____ · u – umbrella and ____ · w – wings and ____

Answers: Any real words, for example: animal, beetle, calendar, diary, flag, gloves, helmet, information, lizard, music, newspaper, parrot, roof, swan, tortoise, uniform, whistle.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Accessories – Kids Vocab. Say each word you know.
  3. Same first letter (10 min): your child writes a second word for each letter. They can look at the lesson pages for help. School words
  4. Check and spell (4 min): your child reads the pairs. Choose three words for your child to spell without looking.
  5. Silly sentences (6 min): your child makes a sentence with two words from the same letter: "A camel is reading a calendar!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "B – backpack and beetle." … "A camel is reading a calendar!" … "W – wings and whistle."
