Week 10 · Lesson 3: What's My Word?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 10: Clothes, animals and school

Fun for Flyers · Week 10: Clothes, animals and school | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Our school trip Lesson 2 · Same first letter Lesson 3 · What's my word?

You Need

  • A word box: a dictionary · instruments · swimsuits · wings · languages · a camel · science · fur · a pocket · a crown · sunglasses · a bracelet · a snail · college. Four words are extra.
  • The clues below.

Look and read. Write the correct words. Example: You can find out what words mean in this. a dictionary · 1 In music lessons, you learn to play these. · 2 People wear these when they go swimming. · 3 Birds and butterflies use these to fly. · 4 People in different countries speak different ones. · 5 This animal can walk a long way across a hot, dry desert. · 6 In this subject, you find out how things work. · 7 This is the soft coat on rabbits, cats and bears. · 8 You can put your keys or your phone in this when you're wearing jeans. · 9 A king or a queen wears this on their head.

Answers: 1 instruments · 2 swimsuits · 3 wings · 4 languages · 5 a camel · 6 science · 7 fur · 8 a pocket · 9 a crown. Extra: sunglasses, a bracelet, a snail, college.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): your child reads each clue and writes a word from the box. Four words are extra.
  3. Clues for the extras (5 min): your child writes a clue for two of the extra words.
  4. What's my word? (8 min): think of a word from this unit. Your child asks: "Is it an animal? Do you wear it? Is it in a classroom?" You answer "Yes" or "No". Then swap.
  5. Well done (3 min): your child says three new words they learned in Units 1–10 and uses each one in a sentence. Animals
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Birds and butterflies use these to fly. – Wings!" … "Is it an animal? – No, it isn't." … "Do you wear it? – Yes, you do!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and write details about a school trip, and talk about a trip of your own.
  • Find two words with the same first letter for each letter, using words from Units 1–9.
  • Read a clue and find the word (dictionary, wings, fur, pocket, crown), and play "What's my word?".
