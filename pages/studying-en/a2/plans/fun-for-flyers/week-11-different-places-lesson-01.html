Week 11 · Lesson 1: The S Puzzle

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: The World Around Us and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 11: Visiting different places

Fun for Flyers · Week 11: Visiting different places | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The S puzzle Lesson 2 · A call from the mountains Lesson 3 · Pack your backpack

You Need

  • The ten clues below. Every answer ends with the letter s. Write ten rows of boxes so your child can count the letters.
  • Coloured pencils for the picnic picture (Lesson 1, last step): draw two children having a picnic on a hill, a kite in a tree, a sign, a pie on a plate, a dog, a bird.

Every word ends with s! 1 These are smaller than mountains. You can walk up them. · 2 Farmers grow vegetables in these. · 3 These are big places with lots of tall trees. · 4 These have water all round them. · 5 You find these on the beach. Some are very pretty. · 6 The sea makes these. They come to the beach. · 7 These are big and grey. You can climb on them by the sea. · 8 These are holes in mountains. Bats sometimes live in them. · 9 These are very hot, dry places with lots of sand. · 10 You can see these in the sky at night.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the kite in the tree red and yellow. · Write HILL on the sign. · Colour the pie on the plate brown. · Colour the dog next to the boy black. · Draw a sun in the sky.

Answers: 1 hills · 2 fields · 3 forests · 4 islands · 5 shells · 6 waves · 7 rocks · 8 caves · 9 deserts · 10 stars. Colour and write as said.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Landforms Rap. Say the places you know.
  3. The S puzzle (10 min): read each clue. Your child writes the word in the boxes. Nature words
  4. Which is your favourite? (4 min): your child chooses three places and says why: "I'd like to visit a desert because…"
  5. Listen and colour and write (6 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or writes in the picnic picture.
  6. Make a clue (3 min): your child writes a clue for another s-word: rivers, lakes, mountains.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Hills are smaller than mountains." … "Bats sometimes live in caves." … "I'd like to visit a desert because I'd like to see a camel."
