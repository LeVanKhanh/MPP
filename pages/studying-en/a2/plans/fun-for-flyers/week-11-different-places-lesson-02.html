Week 11 · Lesson 2: A Call From the Mountains

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 11: Visiting different places

You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Ben's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.

What is Ben saying to his grandma? Grandma: Hello, Ben! How's your holiday? Ben: B · 1 Grandma: Are you staying in a hotel? · 2 Grandma: Who's there with you? · 3 Grandma: What do you do all day? · 4 Grandma: Have you seen any wild animals? · 5 Grandma: Is it very cold at night?

Ben's answers: A Yes, but we've got warm blankets. · B We're having a wonderful time, thanks! · C No, we're camping next to a river! · D I saw an eagle yesterday, and a big brown bear! · E My parents and my cousin Sam. · F We walk in the forest and swim in the lake. · G I'd like some pasta, please. · H Yes, it's on Tuesday.

Answers: 1 C · 2 E · 3 F · 4 D · 5 A. Extra: G, H.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the questions (4 min): read Grandma's questions together. Your child says what kind of answer each one needs: a place, people, an animal…
  3. Choose the answers (9 min): your child chooses Ben's answer for each question. Then you read it together as a phone call.
  4. Your holiday (6 min): you are Grandma. Your child answers the five questions about a real or dream holiday.
  5. Watch (4 min): Camping – Kids Vocab. Which camping things have you used?
  6. Write a postcard (2 min): your child writes two lines: "Dear Grandma, We're camping next to a river…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We're having a wonderful time, thanks!" … "We're camping next to a river." … "I saw an eagle yesterday!"
