Week 11 · Lesson 3: Pack Your Backpack

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 11: Visiting different places

Fun for Flyers · Week 11: Visiting different places | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The S puzzle Lesson 2 · A call from the mountains Lesson 3 · Pack your backpack

You Need

  • Sixteen word cards: gloves, sweater, map, hat, T-shirt, camera, dictionary, chocolate, chess game, torch, sunglasses, phone, umbrella, cold water, blanket, swimsuit.
  • Two backpack pictures: a mountain trip in winter and a beach trip in summer.
  • Five short sentences on papers around the room for the moving dictation (for example: "We put the tent next to the lake.").

What's in each backpack? Put each card in the mountain backpack, the beach backpack or both. Then say why.

Moving dictation: 1 We put the tent next to the lake. · 2 My cousin found a cave in the hill. · 3 There were lots of shells on the beach. · 4 It was very cold at night in the desert. · 5 We took a torch and two warm blankets.

Answers: Any good reasons, for example: mountain – gloves, sweater, map, torch, blanket · beach – sunglasses, swimsuit, T-shirt, hat, cold water · both – camera, phone, chocolate.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Pack the backpacks (8 min): your child puts each card in a backpack and says why: "I need gloves because it's cold in the mountains."
  3. What did I forget? (4 min): take one card away while your child closes their eyes. Your child says what is missing.
  4. Moving dictation (9 min): your child walks to a sentence, reads it, comes back and writes it from memory. Then check the spelling together.
  5. My perfect trip (4 min): your child says where they would like to go and three things they would take.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I need gloves because it's cold in the mountains." … "You forgot the torch!" … "We took a torch and two warm blankets."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a clue and write places and things in nature: hills, fields, caves, deserts, waves.
  • Choose the best answer in a phone call from a holiday, and listen and colour and write.
  • Decide what to pack for two different trips and say why, and play a moving dictation.
