Week 12 · Lesson 1: On a Strange Planet

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 12: A journey into space

Fun for Flyers · Week 12: A journey into space | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · On a strange planet Lesson 2 · Our planet Lesson 3 · Planet fact cards

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a strange planet: a rocket with square windows · two astronauts playing basketball · an astronaut taking photos · an astronaut looking at a map · a big planet with rings in the sky · a small moon · purple plants that look like umbrellas · a green alien watching the astronauts.
  • A word box: basketball · square · photos · rings · umbrellas · map · smaller · alien · round. One word is extra.

Complete the sentences with words from the box: 1 Two of the astronauts are playing ____. · 2 The rocket has got ____ windows. · 3 One astronaut is taking ____. · 4 The big planet in the sky has got ____. · 5 The plants look like purple ____. · 6 One astronaut is looking at a ____. · 7 The moon is ____ than the big planet. · 8 A green ____ is watching the astronauts.

Answers: 1 basketball · 2 square · 3 photos · 4 rings · 5 umbrellas · 6 map · 7 smaller · 8 alien. Extra: round.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Planets Song. Say each planet with the song.
  3. Talk about the picture (4 min): your child says what everyone is doing: "An astronaut is taking photos." Space words
  4. Complete the sentences (8 min): your child writes a word from the box in each sentence. One word is extra.
  5. Draw what I say (6 min): say: "Draw a second rocket. It's got round windows. Draw a yellow alien on the steps." Your child draws it. Then your child tells you what to draw.
  6. Strange planet (4 min): your child gives the planet a name and says two strange things about it: "On Planet Zog, the trees are blue."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Two astronauts are playing basketball." … "The big planet has got rings." … "On Planet Zog, the trees are blue!"
