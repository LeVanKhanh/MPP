Week 12 · Lesson 2: Our Planet

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: The World Around Us and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 12: A journey into space

Fun for Flyers · Week 12: A journey into space | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · On a strange planet Lesson 2 · Our planet Lesson 3 · Planet fact cards

You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.
  • The Earth card below for Ask and answer.

Our planet: We live on a planet called (called / calling / calls) Earth. It is one of eight planets (1 that / what / who) go round the sun. From space, Earth (2 looking / looks / look) like a big blue and white ball, because (3 every / all / most) of it is water. A hundred years ago, people didn't have (4 no / some / any) photos of Earth from space. Now, cameras on spaceships take pictures of it every day. These photos help us (5 learn / learning / learned) about the weather. Would you like to be an astronaut one day?

Earth card: What colour? blue and white · What temperature? about 14°C · Has it got rings? no · How long to go round the sun? 365 days · How many moons? 1

Answers: 1 that · 2 looks · 3 most · 4 any · 5 learn. Questions, for example: What colour is Earth? What's the temperature? Has it got rings? How long does it take to go round the sun? How many moons has it got?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and says why.
  3. Make questions (5 min): from the card, your child makes full questions: "How many moons has Earth got?"
  4. Ask and answer (5 min): your child asks you the questions and you answer from the card. Then swap.
  5. Watch (4 min): Life in Space – Kids Ask an Astronaut. What would you ask an astronaut?
  6. My question (2 min): your child writes one question they would like to ask an astronaut.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Earth looks like a big blue and white ball." … "How many moons has Earth got? – Only one." … "I'd like to ask an astronaut what they eat in space."
