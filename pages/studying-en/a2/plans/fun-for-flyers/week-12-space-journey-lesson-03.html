Week 12 · Lesson 3: Planet Fact Cards

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 12: A journey into space

Fun for Flyers · Week 12: A journey into space | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · On a strange planet Lesson 2 · Our planet Lesson 3 · Planet fact cards

You Need

  • A row of eight circles for the planets, from the sun out. Write Earth and Neptune in; leave the others empty.
  • Coloured pencils, and paper for a fact card.

Script: Next to the sun there's a small grey planet. That's Mercury – M-E-R-C-U-R-Y. Colour it grey. · Next there's Venus. It's very hot! Colour it yellow. · Then Earth – that's where we live! · After Earth there's Mars. People call it the red planet. Colour it red. · Then the biggest planet: Jupiter. Colour it orange and white. · Next is Saturn. It's got big rings. Colour it yellow and draw the rings. · Then Uranus. Colour it light blue. · And the last one is Neptune. Colour it dark blue.

My planet fact card: Name · Colour · Hot or cold? · Has it got rings? · How many moons? · One thing that is a surprise

Answers: Order from the sun: Mercury (grey), Venus (yellow), Earth, Mars (red), Jupiter (orange and white), Saturn (yellow with rings), Uranus (light blue), Neptune (dark blue).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (9 min): read the script twice. Your child writes each planet's name in its circle and colours it.
  3. Check the order (3 min): your child says the planets in order: "Mercury, Venus, Earth…"
  4. Fact card (9 min): your child chooses one planet (you can look it up together) and makes a fact card with a drawing. Space words
  5. Planet talk (4 min): your child tells you about the planet: "Saturn is very big and it's got rings…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Mars is the red planet." … "Saturn has got big rings." … "Jupiter is the biggest planet."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Talk about a space picture with planet, rocket, astronaut and alien, and complete sentences with words from a box.
  • Read a short text about Earth, choose the right word from three, and ask and answer questions about our planet.
  • Listen and write the names of the planets, and make a fact card about one planet.
