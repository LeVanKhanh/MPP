Week 13 · Lesson 1: Weather Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 13: What horrible weather!

You Need

  • The word snake below, written large on paper.
  • The six sentences below. Read each one aloud with a gap; your child writes the word.

Find the weather words: sunnystormfoggyicewarmwetwindydryrainbowcloudsnowtemperature

Listen and complete the sentences: 1 Mia can't go swimming today because it's very ____. · 2 Grandpa heard a noisy ____ last night. · 3 Look! There's a beautiful ____ over the hill. · 4 Be careful – there's ____ on the road this morning. · 5 It's dangerous to drive quickly when it's ____. · 6 Sam wants to fly his kite because it's ____. · And you? If it snows, I'd like to ____.

Answers: Words: sunny, storm, foggy, ice, warm, wet, windy, dry, rainbow, cloud, snow, temperature. Sentences: 1 cold · 2 storm · 3 rainbow · 4 ice · 5 foggy · 6 windy. If it snows: any answer.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Learn Weather Vocabulary. Do an action for each weather word.
  3. Find the words (5 min): your child draws a line between the twelve words in the snake. Weather words
  4. Listen and complete (7 min): read each sentence with a gap. Your child writes a word from the snake.
  5. If it snows… (4 min): your child finishes: "If it snows, I'd like to… If there's a storm, I…"
  6. Weather report (6 min): your child gives a weather report for tomorrow like on TV: "Good evening! Tomorrow it will be foggy in the morning…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Grandpa heard a noisy storm last night." … "Be careful – there's ice on the road!" … "If it snows, I'd like to build a snowman."
