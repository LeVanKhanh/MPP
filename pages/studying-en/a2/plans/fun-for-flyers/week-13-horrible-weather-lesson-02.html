Week 13 · Lesson 2: Sunny Park, Rainy Park

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 13: What horrible weather!

Fun for Flyers · Week 13: What horrible weather! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Weather words Lesson 2 · Sunny park, rainy park Lesson 3 · Caught in the storm

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a park on a sunny day: a girl on a swing · a boy flying a kite · a girl reading on a seat · a boy listening to music · a girl with a dog · a boy eating an ice cream · a girl climbing a tree.
  • Write seven names around the picture: Holly, Harry, Sarah, George, Emma, Mark, Zoe. Read the script twice; your child draws lines.

Script: It's a lovely warm day in the park! Holly is on the swing. · Harry is flying his kite – it's windy today. · Sarah is reading a book on the seat. · George is listening to music. · Emma is walking her dog. · Mark is eating an ice cream. It's chocolate! · And Zoe? She's climbing the tree. Be careful, Zoe!

Now the weather changes! It's raining and windy. What's different? The kite is in the tree. · Sarah's book is wet. · Mark's ice cream is on the ground. · Emma's dog is dirty. · Everyone is running.

Answers: Holly – swing · Harry – kite · Sarah – reading on the seat · George – music · Emma – dog · Mark – ice cream · Zoe – climbing the tree.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child says what everyone is doing: "A girl is on the swing."
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  4. The weather changes (6 min): read the "now the weather changes" sentences. Your child adds rain, clouds and the changes to the picture and says what is different: "Before, Sarah was reading. Now her book is wet."
  5. Watch (6 min): Rainy Day – The Storytellers. What do the children do on a rainy day?
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "What do you do on a sunny day? On a rainy day? Which is better?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Harry is flying his kite." … "Before, Sarah was reading. Now her book is wet." … "On a rainy day I play board games."
