Week 13 · Lesson 3: Caught in the Storm

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 13: What horrible weather!

Fun for Flyers · Week 13: What horrible weather! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Weather words Lesson 2 · Sunny park, rainy park Lesson 3 · Caught in the storm

You Need

  • The first half of the story below. Read it aloud; your child answers the four questions.
  • Paper to draw the end of the story.

The first half: Lily, Ben, Kim and Sam were in the park. It was warm and sunny. Then, suddenly, big dark clouds came and it started to rain. "Oh no! I'm getting wet!" said Lily. Ben looked at the sky. "I don't like storms. I'm frightened," he said. Kim had only a T-shirt. "I'm really cold!" she said. "It's OK," said Sam. "My house is near here. Let's go there!"

What did you hear? 1 Who says they're getting wet? · 2 Who is frightened of storms? · 3 Who is cold? · 4 Whose house can the friends go to?

What happens next? Picture A: The friends run to Sam's house. Sam's mum gives them dry towels. · Picture B: The friends stand under a big tree and wait. Which is better? Why?

Answers: 1 Lily · 2 Ben · 3 Kim · 4 Sam's. Next: A is better – standing under a tree in a storm is dangerous. The end: any good ending.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen (6 min): read the first half twice. Your child answers the four questions: Lily, Ben, Kim or Sam?
  3. What happens next? (5 min): read pictures A and B. Your child chooses and says why: "A, because a tree in a storm is dangerous."
  4. Tell the end (7 min): your child tells the end of the story: what they do at Sam's house and what happens when the sun comes out.
  5. Draw the end (5 min): your child draws the last picture of the story and writes one sentence under it.
  6. Talk (2 min): ask: "Are you frightened of storms? What do you do?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Oh no! I'm getting wet!" … "A is better, because a tree in a storm is dangerous." … "At Sam's house they played games and the sun came out."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find and use weather words: storm, foggy, ice, warm, wet, dry, temperature.
  • Listen and draw lines to people in a park, and say how the park changes when the weather changes.
  • Listen to the start of a story, choose what happens next, and tell the end.
