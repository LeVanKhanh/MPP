Week 14 · Lesson 1: What Am I?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 14: Are you hungry? Thirsty?

Fun for Flyers · Week 14: Are you hungry? Thirsty? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What am I? Lesson 2 · How do you make it? Lesson 3 · How often do you…?

You Need

  • Two word wheels (letters in a circle, no spaces): honeysaltjambutter and sugarcerealyoghurtflour.
  • Real food from your kitchen if you have it: honey, salt, sugar, butter, flour, cereal.

What am I? Find the answers in the word wheels. 1 Bees make me. I'm sweet and yellow. · 2 I'm white. People put me on chips and meat, but not on ice cream! · 3 I'm made of fruit and sugar. You put me on bread. · 4 I'm yellow and soft. You put me on bread before the jam. · 5 I'm white and sweet. Some people put me in tea or coffee. · 6 You eat me with milk for breakfast, in a bowl. · 7 I'm made from milk, and sometimes I taste of strawberries. · 8 You need me to make bread, cakes and pancakes.

Answers: 1 honey · 2 salt · 3 jam · 4 butter · 5 sugar · 6 cereal · 7 yoghurt · 8 flour.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Breakfast Time. What do you have for breakfast?
  3. Find the words (4 min): your child finds the eight foods in the two word wheels. Food words
  4. What am I? (8 min): read each clue. Your child finds the answer and writes it.
  5. Taste test (5 min): if you can, your child closes their eyes and tastes a little sugar, salt or honey: "I think it's honey because it's sweet."
  6. Make a clue (4 min): your child writes a "What am I?" clue for another food. You guess.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Bees make me. – Honey!" … "I eat cereal with milk for breakfast." … "I think it's salt because it isn't sweet."
