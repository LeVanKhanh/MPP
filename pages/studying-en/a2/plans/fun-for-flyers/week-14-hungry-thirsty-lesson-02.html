Week 14 · Lesson 2: How Do You Make It?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Shopping. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 14: Are you hungry? Thirsty?

Fun for Flyers · Week 14: Are you hungry? Thirsty? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What am I? Lesson 2 · How do you make it? Lesson 3 · How often do you…?

You Need

  • The three descriptions below.
  • Four questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.

Guess the food: 1 You need flour, eggs and milk. Mix them and cook them on the cooker. Eat them with honey or jam. · 2 You need two pieces of bread. Put cheese, meat or salad between them. · 3 You cut potatoes into thin pieces and cook them until they're hot and brown. People eat them with burgers.

Script: 1 Mum: There isn't any fish tonight, Lily, and there isn't any soup. But you can have pasta with meatballs. · 2 Dad: What did you have for lunch, Sam? A burger? Sam: No, I had a cheese sandwich and some orange juice. · 3 Grandma: Do you want eggs for breakfast, Ella? Ella: No, thanks. Can I have cereal and yoghurt, please? · 4 Mum: What did you eat at the party, Tom? Tom: Lots of chocolate cake! I didn't have any sausages.

Listen and tick: 1 What can Lily have for dinner? A fish and rice B soup C pasta with meatballs · 2 What did Sam have for lunch? A a burger B a cheese sandwich C chicken · 3 What does Ella want for breakfast? A eggs B cereal and yoghurt C pancakes · 4 What did Tom eat at the party? A chocolate cake B sausages C sandwiches

Answers: Guess: 1 pancakes · 2 a sandwich · 3 chips (fries). Tick: 1 C · 2 B · 3 B · 4 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Guess the food (6 min): read each description. Your child guesses the food and says which words helped.
  3. Tell me how (5 min): your child tells you how to make their favourite snack: "First you need… Then you…"
  4. Look first (3 min): your child names the small pictures for each question.
  5. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "What did you have for lunch yesterday? What do you want for dinner tonight?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You need flour, eggs and milk." … "Put cheese between two pieces of bread." … "I had a cheese sandwich for lunch."
