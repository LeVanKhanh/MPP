Week 14 · Lesson 3: How Often Do You…?

Lesson 1 · What am I? Lesson 2 · How do you make it? Lesson 3 · How often do you…?

You Need

  • The quiz below. Your child writes one word on each line, then circles a, b or c.
  • Paper for a healthy day plan.

How often do you…? (a never · b sometimes · c every day) Example: How often do you do sport? · 1 How often do you go ____ a walk? · 2 How often do you eat lots ____ fruit and vegetables? · 3 How often do you go to bed ____ ten o'clock? · 4 How often do you drink water ____ you're thirsty? · 5 How often do you eat sweets ____ chocolate? · 6 How often do you walk ____ school? · 7 How often do you have breakfast ____ the morning? · 8 How often do you play outside ____ your friends?

Points: questions 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8: a = 0, b = 1, c = 2 · questions 3 and 5: a = 2, b = 1, c = 0.

Answers: 1 for · 2 of · 3 after · 4 when · 5 or · 6 to · 7 in · 8 with. Points: any – 12 or more is very healthy!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Write the words (7 min): your child writes one missing word on each line of the quiz.
  3. Do the quiz (5 min): your child answers the quiz with a, b or c and counts the points.
  4. Ask me (4 min): your child asks you the quiz and counts your points. Who is healthier?
  5. Watch (6 min): Healthy Eating for Kids. Which foods give us energy? (For older children.)
  6. My healthy day (3 min): your child writes a healthy breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack for one day.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How often do you go for a walk? – Sometimes." … "I eat lots of fruit every day." … "For breakfast I'd like cereal and yoghurt."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Guess foods from "What am I?" clues: honey, salt, jam, butter, sugar, cereal, yoghurt, flour.
  • Say how to make some foods, and listen and tick what people eat.
  • Write one missing word in each line of a quiz, answer it, and plan a healthy day of meals.
