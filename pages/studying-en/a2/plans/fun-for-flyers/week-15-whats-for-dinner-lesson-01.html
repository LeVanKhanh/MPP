Week 15 · Lesson 1: Mia's Lemon Biscuits

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 15: What's for dinner?

Fun for Flyers · Week 15: What's for dinner? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Mia's lemon biscuits Lesson 2 · Penguins Lesson 3 · Funny food sentences

You Need

  • A "You need" list with boxes to tick: eggs · flour · sugar · lemon · honey · milk · butter · salt.
  • Six step pictures (draw them small, a–f): a cutting a lemon · b putting butter and sugar in a bowl · c adding an egg · d adding flour · e making small round biscuits · f putting them in the oven.

Script: Hi, I'm Mia! Today I'm making my lemon biscuits. You need butter, sugar, one egg, flour and a lemon. You don't need milk or honey. First, put the butter and sugar in a bowl and mix them. Then add the egg. Next, cut the lemon and add the lemon juice. After that, add the flour and mix again. Then make small round biscuits with your hands. Last, put them in the oven for twelve minutes. Mmm!

Answers: You need: butter, sugar, an egg, flour, a lemon (not milk, honey or salt). Order: b, c, a, d, e, f.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Making Cookies. What do they do first?
  3. Look first (3 min): your child says what is happening in each step picture.
  4. Listen and tick (5 min): read the script once. Your child ticks what Mia needs.
  5. Listen and order (7 min): read the script again. Your child numbers the step pictures 1–6.
  6. Tell the recipe (5 min): your child tells you the recipe with First, Then, Next, After that, Last. Food words
  7. My recipe (3 min): your child says what they would add to make the biscuits better.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "First, put the butter and sugar in a bowl." … "Next, cut the lemon." … "Last, put them in the oven for twelve minutes."
