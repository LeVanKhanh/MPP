You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.

Penguins: Penguins are birds, but (but / and / so) they can't fly. Most penguins live (1 in / at / on) very cold places, but some live where it's warm. They are (2 good / better / best) swimmers than most other birds. Penguins eat fish (3 which / what / who) they catch in the sea. Baby penguins stay with their parents (4 until / before / during) they can swim. Some penguins in zoos live (5 for / since / at) more than thirty years!

Answers: 1 in · 2 better · 3 which · 4 until · 5 for.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Gentoo Penguin. Where does this penguin live?
  3. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and says why.
  4. Right or wrong? (5 min): say sentences: "Penguins can fly." "Penguins eat fish." Your child says "Yes, that's right!" or "No, that's wrong!" and corrects the wrong ones.
  5. Animal text (9 min): your child writes five sentences about another animal with a gap in each, and three words to choose from. You do it!
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Penguins are birds, but they can't fly." … "They're better swimmers than most birds." … "Baby penguins stay with their parents until they can swim."
