Week 15 · Lesson 3: Funny Food Sentences

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 15: What's for dinner?

Fun for Flyers · Week 15: What's for dinner? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Mia's lemon biscuits Lesson 2 · Penguins Lesson 3 · Funny food sentences

You Need

  • Paper and coloured pencils.
  • The examples below.

Funny food sentences (the name, the food, the time and the day start with the same letter!): Sam saw seven sausages at six on Saturday! · Ben bought bananas and butter at breakfast. · Fred found fish and fries at five on Friday! · Tom took two tomatoes to his teacher on Thursday!

Let's talk about food: What's your favourite food? · Who cooks most of the food in your home? · Tell me about the food you ate for dinner yesterday. · Where do you eat at home? · What food don't you like?

Answers: Any funny sentences that follow the rule. Talk: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the examples (4 min): read the four funny sentences together and colour the letters that are the same.
  3. Write funny sentences (9 min): your child writes funny food sentences for three people in your family or friends: "Mum made milkshakes at midnight on Monday!"
  4. Read them aloud (3 min): your child reads them fast. Can you say them without laughing?
  5. Let's talk about food (9 min): ask the five questions. Your child answers in full sentences, then asks you.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Sam saw seven sausages at six on Saturday!" … "My mum cooks most of the food in our home." … "Yesterday I ate rice and fish for dinner."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen to a recipe, tick what you need and put the steps in order.
  • Read a short text about penguins and choose the right word from three.
  • Write funny food sentences with the same first letter, and ask and answer questions about food at home.
