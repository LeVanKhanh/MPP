Week 16 · Lesson 1: Picnic Things

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 16: Let's have a picnic!

You Need

  • Real things from your kitchen: a plate, a glass, a bowl, a knife, a fork, a spoon, chopsticks, a cushion, a blanket.
  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 a family having a picnic on a blanket in a field · 2 a goat looking over the gate · 3 the goat eating the sandwiches · 4 everyone laughing and the goat with a napkin on its head.

Write one word in each sentence: Example: You eat soup with this. a spoon · 1 You put your food on this before you eat it. It's often round. · 2 You drink lemonade from this. · 3 You can put salad or soup in this. · 4 This is black. Put a little on your food to make it hot. · 5 Some people use these two long pieces of wood to eat noodles. · 6 You use this with a knife to eat meat. · 7 You sit on this on the grass at a picnic. · 8 You can put this soft thing on a chair to sit on.

Answers: 1 a plate · 2 a glass · 3 a bowl · 4 pepper · 5 chopsticks · 6 a fork · 7 a blanket · 8 a cushion.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): I'm a Knife, Fork, Spoon. Do the actions with the song.
  3. Real things (4 min): show the real things. Your child names them and says what they are made of: "The fork is made of metal." On the table
  4. Write the words (7 min): your child reads each clue and writes the word.
  5. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the four-picture story: "A family was having a picnic. Suddenly a goat…" Ask: "How did they feel?"
  6. Set the table (4 min): your child sets the table for a picnic and says where everything goes: "The fork is next to the plate."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You eat soup with a spoon." … "The goat ate all the sandwiches!" … "The fork is next to the plate."
