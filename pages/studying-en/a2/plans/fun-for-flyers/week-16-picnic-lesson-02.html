You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two, three or four words from the story.

A picnic in the living room: Last Saturday, Sam and his sister Ella wanted to have a picnic at the beach with their dad. But in the morning it started to rain and it was very windy. "We can't go to the beach now," said Dad. Ella had a wonderful idea. "Let's have a picnic in the living room!" They moved the sofa and put a big blue blanket and four cushions on the floor. Sam fetched plates, glasses, knives and forks from the kitchen. Dad made cheese sandwiches and a big bowl of salad, and Ella brought a bottle of cold lemonade and some strawberries. Then Dad turned off the lights and turned on a film about the sea. "Now we're at the beach!" laughed Sam. At the end of the day, the sun came out, but they didn't want to go outside.

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: Sam's sister is called Ella. · 1 The children wanted to go to the ____ with their dad. · 2 In the morning it started to ____ and it was very windy. · 3 They put a blanket and ____ on the floor. · 4 Sam fetched plates, glasses, knives and forks ____. · 5 Dad made ____ and a big bowl of salad. · 6 Ella brought a bottle of ____ and some strawberries. · 7 They watched a film about ____.

Answers: 1 beach · 2 rain · 3 four cushions · 4 from the kitchen · 5 cheese sandwiches · 6 cold lemonade · 7 the sea.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "Whose idea was it? Was it a good idea?"
  3. Write the words (9 min): your child completes the seven sentences with one to four words from the story.
  4. Watch (2 min): We're Going on a Picnic! What would you take?
  5. Act it out (7 min): put a blanket on the floor and act the story together. Your child is Ella and says her lines.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Let's have a picnic in the living room!" … "Sam fetched plates and glasses from the kitchen." … "Now we're at the beach!"
