Week 16 · Lesson 3: Guess What's in the Bag

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 16: Let's have a picnic!

Fun for Flyers · Week 16: Let's have a picnic! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Picnic things Lesson 2 · A picnic in the living room Lesson 3 · Guess what's in the bag

You Need

  • A bag or a box with six picnic things inside (for example a spoon, an apple, a cushion, a cup, a banana, a bottle).
  • Paper for a picnic list and an invitation.

Guessing game: Is it something to eat? · Is it made of metal? · Is it round? · Can you drink from it? · Is it soft?

Plan a picnic: Where? · When? · Who can come? · Food and drinks · Things to take (blanket, plates…) · Games to play · Invitation: Please come to our picnic in ____ on ____ at ____ o'clock!

Answers: Any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Guessing game (8 min): put one thing in the bag. Your child asks yes/no questions: "Is it something to eat? Is it made of metal?" Then your child guesses. Take turns.
  3. Feel and say (4 min): your child puts a hand in the bag without looking: "It's round and soft. I think it's an orange."
  4. Plan a picnic (9 min): your child plans a picnic with the questions and writes the list. Party plans
  5. Invitation (4 min): your child writes a short invitation to a friend or grandparent.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is it made of metal? – Yes, it is." … "I think it's an orange." … "Please come to our picnic in the park on Sunday!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things for eating and drinking – knife, fork, spoon, chopsticks, bowl – and tell a picture story.
  • Read a longer story and complete sentences with one, two, three or four words.
  • Play a guessing game, and plan a picnic with a list and an invitation.
