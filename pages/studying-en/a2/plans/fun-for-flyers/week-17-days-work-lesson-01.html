Week 17 · Lesson 1: Who Does This Job?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 17: A day's work

Fun for Flyers · Week 17: A day's work | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who does this job? Lesson 2 · Best job, worst job Lesson 3 · Lucy the journalist

You Need

  • Ten job cards: photographer, journalist, manager, cook, mechanic, doctor, actor, designer, pilot, waiter.
  • The clues below.

Read and write the job: Example: This person takes photos for magazines and websites. a photographer · 1 This person finds out about things that happen and writes about them for newspapers. · 2 This person works in an office or a shop and tells other people what to do. · 3 This person makes delicious food in a restaurant. · 4 You take your car to this person when there's a problem with its engine. · 5 You go to this person when you are ill. · 6 This person acts in films and on a theatre stage. · 7 This person draws new clothes, cars or buildings. · 8 This person flies planes. · 9 This person brings food to your table in a restaurant.

Answers: 1 a journalist · 2 a manager · 3 a cook · 4 a mechanic · 5 a doctor · 6 an actor · 7 a designer · 8 a pilot · 9 a waiter.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Jobs – Kids Vocab. Say each job before the voice does.
  3. Read and write (8 min): your child reads each clue and writes the job. Jobs
  4. Where do they work? (5 min): your child says where each person works: "A waiter works in a restaurant. A pilot works on a plane."
  5. Act a job (5 min): act a job without words. Your child guesses: "You're a mechanic!" Then swap.
  6. Make a clue (3 min): your child writes a clue for another job: a dentist, an engineer, a police officer.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A journalist writes for newspapers." … "You take your car to a mechanic." … "A waiter works in a restaurant."
