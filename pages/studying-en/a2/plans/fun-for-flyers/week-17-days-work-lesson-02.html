Week 17 · Lesson 2: Best Job, Worst Job

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 17: A day's work

Fun for Flyers · Week 17: A day's work | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who does this job? Lesson 2 · Best job, worst job Lesson 3 · Lucy the journalist

You Need

  • Eight job cards: engineer, designer, police officer, manager, actor, dentist, fire fighter, pop star.
  • The script below. Read each person's words; your child guesses the job.

Who am I? a "I work in a hospital, but I'm not a doctor. I look at people's teeth." · b "I'm often on TV. I sing on a big stage, and lots of people come to listen." · c "I wear a uniform and help people when there's a fire." · d "I design bridges and buildings. Maths is very important in my job."

My list: Put the eight jobs in order. 1 = the worst job for me · 8 = the best job for me.

Answers: a a dentist · b a pop star · c a fire fighter · d an engineer. List: any order with reasons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and guess (6 min): read a–d. Your child guesses the job and writes it.
  3. My list (7 min): your child puts the eight cards in order and says why: "Pop star is the best job for me because I love singing. Dentist is the worst because…"
  4. Your list (4 min): you make your own list. Compare: which jobs are in the same place?
  5. Watch (4 min): 100 Kids Tell Us What They Want to Be. Which job do most children choose?
  6. My dream job (4 min): your child writes three sentences: "I'd like to be a… because… I'd need to…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I think pop star is the best job because I love singing." … "A dentist looks at people's teeth." … "I'd like to be an engineer because I like maths."
