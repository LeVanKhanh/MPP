Week 17 · Lesson 3: Lucy the Journalist

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 17: A day's work

Fun for Flyers · Week 17: A day's work | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who does this job? Lesson 2 · Best job, worst job Lesson 3 · Lucy the journalist

You Need

  • Six thing cards: a camera, a map, a scarf, an umbrella, a tablet, a diary. Eight place cards with letters: A a castle · B an office · C a funfair · D a shop · E a museum · F a hotel · G a café · H a car park. Two places are extra.
  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 Lucy waiting outside a hotel with lots of photographers · 2 a famous actor running past them into a café · 3 Lucy in the café, sitting at the next table · 4 Lucy and the actor talking and laughing; next day, Lucy's story in the newspaper.

Script: Lucy is a journalist. Last week she was very busy! On Monday she went to the castle. She took her camera to take photos of the old walls. · On Tuesday she was in the office. She wrote her stories on her tablet. · On Wednesday she went to the museum. She took a map because it's very big. · On Thursday it was raining, so she took her umbrella to the car park. · On Friday she went to the hotel. It was cold, so she took her scarf.

What did Lucy take to each place? camera · tablet · map · umbrella · scarf

Answers: camera – A (castle) · tablet – B (office) · map – E (museum) · umbrella – H (car park) · scarf – F (hotel). Extra: C, D, G. Story: any good story.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the things and the places.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter under each thing.
  4. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the four-picture story: "Lucy was waiting outside a hotel. Suddenly a famous actor…"
  5. Interview (6 min): your child is a journalist and asks you five questions, like a famous person: "What's your name? What do you do? What's your favourite…?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "She took her camera to the castle." … "Suddenly a famous actor ran into the café!" … "What's your favourite film?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name Flyers jobs – mechanic, photographer, journalist, manager, designer, waiter – and say what they do.
  • Put jobs in order from best to worst and say why, and guess jobs from what people say.
  • Listen and match things to places, and tell a picture story about a journalist.
