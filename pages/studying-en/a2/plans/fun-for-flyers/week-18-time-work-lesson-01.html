Week 18 · Lesson 1: What's the Time?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 18: Time and work

Fun for Flyers · Week 18: Time and work | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's the time? Lesson 2 · A family in three cities Lesson 3 · Kim's job

You Need

  • Six clocks (draw them, a–f): a 7:00 · b 8:30 · c 4:15 · d 19:15 · e 5:45 · f 12:00 at night.
  • Four short conversations below. Read each one; your child writes its number next to the right clock.

Listen and find the clock: 1 Boy: When's lunch today? Teacher: At half past eight? No! Breakfast is at half past eight. Lunch is at twelve o'clock. Oh, sorry – today it's at quarter past four, after the school trip! · 2 Girl: When does the film start? Dad: At quarter past seven in the evening – that's 19:15. · 3 Mum: What time do you have to get up tomorrow, Zoe? Zoe: At seven o'clock. · 4 Boy: What time is it now? Girl: It's quarter to six.

Answers: a 7:00 – seven o'clock (3) · b 8:30 – half past eight · c 4:15 – quarter past four (1) · d 19:15 – quarter past seven in the evening (2) · e 5:45 – quarter to six (4) · f midnight.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Telling the Time for Kids. Show quarter past, half past and quarter to with your arms.
  3. Say the times (6 min): your child writes each clock time in words: "It's half past eight." Time words
  4. Listen and find (7 min): read the four conversations. Your child writes the number next to the right clock.
  5. My day in times (5 min): your child says four times in their day: "I get up at quarter to seven. School starts at…"
  6. Clock race (4 min): say a time; your child moves the hands on a real clock or draws it as fast as possible.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's quarter past four." … "The film starts at quarter past seven in the evening." … "I get up at quarter to seven."
