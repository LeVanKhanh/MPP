Week 18 · Lesson 2: A Family in Three Cities

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 18: Time and work

Fun for Flyers · Week 18: Time and work | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's the time? Lesson 2 · A family in three cities Lesson 3 · Kim's job

You Need

  • A word box: classmates (example) · visiting · pilot · hours · later · online · borrowing · college · factory · skiing. Four words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–6. Lily is in London with some of her classmates. They're (1) ____ museums and learning about art. Her dad is a (2) ____, so he's often in different countries. This week he's in New York. Her grandma lives in Sydney. Lily wants to talk to them every day, but it isn't easy because the time is different in each city. When it's twelve o'clock in New York, it's five o'clock in London – five (3) ____ (4) ____! And in Sydney it's three o'clock in the morning. So Lily talks to Dad at five o'clock in the afternoon, and to Grandma at eight o'clock in the morning. She goes (5) ____ on her teacher's tablet – she's (6) ____ it.

Choose the best name: Lily's day at the airport · Talking to a family in three cities · Grandma's new tablet

Answers: 1 visiting · 2 pilot · 3 hours · 4 later · 5 online · 6 borrowing. Extra: college, factory, skiing (and classmates is the example). Best name: Talking to a family in three cities.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (10 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  4. Watch (3 min): Time Zones for Kids. Why is it night in one country and day in another?
  5. What time is it there? (6 min): ask: "It's nine o'clock in the morning here. What time is it in London?" Look it up together on a phone or a clock app.
  6. Message (4 min): your child writes a short message to someone in another country: "Hi! Here it's… What time is it where you are?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "When it's twelve o'clock in New York, it's five o'clock in London." … "She's borrowing her teacher's tablet." … "What time is it where you are?"
