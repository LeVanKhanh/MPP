Week 18 · Lesson 3: Kim's Job

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 18: Time and work

Fun for Flyers · Week 18: Time and work | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's the time? Lesson 2 · A family in three cities Lesson 3 · Kim's job

You Need

  • Six questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.
  • Paper and pencils for the drawing game.

Script: Boy: What do you do, Kim? Kim: I'm a photographer. I take photos for a magazine. · Boy: How do you get to work? By car? Kim: No, I go by bike. It's quicker! · Boy: What time do you start work? Kim: At half past eight. · Boy: Where do you have lunch? Kim: When it's sunny, I eat my sandwiches in the park. · Boy: What was your first job? Kim: I was a waiter in a café. · Boy: What do you like most about your job? Kim: Meeting lots of interesting people!

Listen and tick: Example: What's Kim's job? A photographer B pilot C artist · 1 How does Kim go to work? A car B bike C bus · 2 What time does Kim start work? A 8:00 B 8:30 C 9:15 · 3 Where does Kim have lunch on sunny days? A café B office C park · 4 What was Kim's first job? A police officer B cook C waiter · 5 What does Kim like most? A the photos B meeting people C her camera

Answers: Example A · 1 B · 2 B · 3 C · 4 C · 5 B.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the pictures for each question.
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  4. Interview me (5 min): your child asks you the same six questions about your work.
  5. Which job have I drawn? (9 min): draw a job in one minute (no words!). Your child guesses: "Is it a mechanic?" Then your child draws one. Jobs
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I go to work by bike. It's quicker!" … "I start work at half past eight." … "Is it a mechanic? – Yes!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Tell the time with o'clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to, and on 24-hour clocks.
  • Read a story about a family in different cities, choose words from a box, and choose the best name.
  • Listen and tick details about someone's job, and guess a job from a drawing.
