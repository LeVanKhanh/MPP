Week 19 · Lesson 1: Mia's Questions

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Questions and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 19: Answer my questions

Fun for Flyers · Week 19: Answer my questions | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Mia's questions Lesson 2 · Question words Lesson 3 · Questions for every place

You Need

  • A word box: museum (example) · asleep · enormous · hear · hurry · explore · project · scary · candy · building. Four words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–6. Sam had a little sister called Mia. She was only five, but she never stopped asking questions. "Why is the sky blue, Sam? Why do cats sleep all day? How many stars are there?" She sometimes asked questions when Sam was (1) ____! One Saturday, Mum took them to the science museum. In the first room there was an (2) ____ dinosaur. "Can it (3) ____ us?" asked Mia. "Come on, children, (4) ____! We want to (5) ____ the space room before lunch," said Mum. In the space room, Mia found the answers to lots of her questions. Back at home she said, "Now I want to do a (6) ____ about stars!"

Choose the best name: Mia's favourite pet · Questions and answers at the museum · Mum's new car

Answers: 1 asleep · 2 enormous · 3 hear · 4 hurry · 5 explore · 6 project. Extra: scary, candy, building. Best name: Questions and answers at the museum.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (10 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  4. Watch (3 min): Why Is the Sky Blue? Can you answer Mia's first question now?
  5. My big questions (6 min): your child writes three "big questions" they would like to ask, like Mia: "Why do…? How many…?"
  6. Find an answer (4 min): choose one of the questions and find the answer together in a book or online.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "She never stopped asking questions." … "Can it hear us?" … "Why is the sky blue?"
