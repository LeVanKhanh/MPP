Week 19 · Lesson 2: Question Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 19: Answer my questions

Fun for Flyers · Week 19: Answer my questions | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Mia's questions Lesson 2 · Question words Lesson 3 · Questions for every place

You Need

  • A box of question words: How many · How much · How often · How old · What · What time · When · Where · Which · Who · Whose · Why · How.
  • The twelve questions below. Your child writes the question word, then asks family members.

Write the question word: Example: Where do you live? · 1 ____ is your surname? · 2 ____ sits next to you in class? · 3 ____ did you come to school today? By bus? · 4 ____ is your birthday? · 5 ____ are you? Ten? Eleven? · 6 ____ did you get up this morning? · 7 ____ do you use a computer? Every day? · 8 ____ people live in your house? · 9 ____ is better, a museum or a funfair? · 10 ____ bag is this? · 11 ____ are you learning English? · 12 ____ water do you drink every day?

Answers: 1 What · 2 Who · 3 How · 4 When · 5 How old · 6 What time · 7 How often · 8 How many · 9 Which · 10 Whose · 11 Why · 12 How much.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): The Five W's Song. Point up for each question word.
  3. Write the words (8 min): your child writes a question word in each question. Question words
  4. Ask me (5 min): your child asks you the twelve questions and writes your short answers.
  5. Questions mingle (7 min): your child asks two more people at home (or on the phone) four of the questions and writes their answers.
  6. Report (3 min): your child tells you one thing they found out: "Grandma drinks a lot of water every day!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How often do you use a computer?" … "Whose bag is this?" … "Which is better, a museum or a funfair?"
