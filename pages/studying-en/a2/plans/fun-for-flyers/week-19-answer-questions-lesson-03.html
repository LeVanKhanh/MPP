Week 19 · Lesson 3: Questions for Every Place

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Questions and Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 19: Answer my questions

Fun for Flyers · Week 19: Answer my questions | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Mia's questions Lesson 2 · Question words Lesson 3 · Questions for every place

You Need

  • Four place cards: on the beach · in a museum · in class · at a party.
  • Paper and pencils.

Write questions and answers: For each place, write a question you might ask there and its answer. Example: at a party – "Whose birthday is it?" "It's Kim's!" · on the beach – ____? · in a museum – ____? · in class – ____? · at a party – ____?

Interview: You are a famous person. Your child is a journalist and asks you eight questions with eight different question words.

Answers: Any good questions, for example: on the beach – "How cold is the water?" · in a museum – "How old is this dinosaur?" · in class – "Can you help me, please?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Questions for places (8 min): your child writes one question and one answer for each place card.
  3. Guess the place (4 min): your child reads a question only. You guess the place: "How old is this dinosaur? – In a museum!"
  4. Interview (9 min): your child interviews you with eight different question words. You answer like a famous person.
  5. Swap (4 min): you ask your child five quick questions. Your child answers in full sentences.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How old is this dinosaur?" … "Whose birthday is it?" … "Why did you want to be an actor?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a story about a child who asks lots of questions, choose words from a box, and choose the best name.
  • Use all the question words – how many, how much, how often, what time, which, whose, why – and ask and answer.
  • Write questions for different places, and ask and answer in an interview game.
