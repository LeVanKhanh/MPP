Week 20 · Lesson 1: Where's My Phone?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 20: Calling and sending

Fun for Flyers · Week 20: Calling and sending | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where's my phone? Lesson 2 · Phones then and now Lesson 3 · Tell me about your phone

You Need

  • A word box: sure (example) · missing · call · online · strange · sounds · website · repeats · wifi · instrument. Four words are extra.
  • Name parts on cards for Lesson 1, last step: Kim / Grandma / Dad · finds / loses / repairs · her / his · phone / violin / bag.

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–5. When Kim got home after her violin lesson, she emptied her backpack on her bed. "Where's my phone?" she said. "I'm sure it was in here." She looked everywhere, but her phone was (1) ____. "Oh no! I can't (2) ____ Grandma tonight, and I can't go (3) ____ to do my homework!" Then she heard a (4) ____ noise. Some music was playing in her violin bag. "That (5) ____ like my phone!" she said. And it was! "Hooray!" said Kim. "Now I can call Grandma."

Answers: 1 missing · 2 call · 3 online · 4 strange · 5 sounds. Extra: website, repeats, wifi, instrument. Best name: Kim finds her phone.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (10 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  3. Make the best name (3 min): your child puts three name cards together to make the best name for the story.
  4. Lost and found (7 min): hide your phone (on quiet) or a small toy somewhere. Your child asks: "Is it in the kitchen? Is it under the sofa?" You answer. Then swap.
  5. Watch (2 min): May I Speak to Kate? Say the phone sentences with the video.
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "Have you ever lost something important? How did you feel? Did you find it?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm sure it was in here!" … "That sounds like my phone!" … "Is it under the sofa?"
