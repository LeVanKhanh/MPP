Week 20 · Lesson 2: Phones Then and Now

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 20: Calling and sending

Fun for Flyers · Week 20: Calling and sending | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where's my phone? Lesson 2 · Phones then and now Lesson 3 · Tell me about your phone

You Need

  • Six fact cards (below) and six year cards: 1876 · 1973 · 1992 · 2000 · 2007 · today. Your child matches them.
  • An old phone and a new phone, or pictures of them.

When did these things happen? a The first phone call ever – on a phone that stayed in one place. · b The first call on a mobile phone. It was very big and heavy! · c Someone sent the first text message. · d People could buy the first phone with a camera. · e Phones with big screens you touch with your fingers came to the shops. · f Lots of children use phones to learn, play and talk to friends.

Answers: a 1876 · b 1973 · c 1992 · d 2000 · e 2007 · f today.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The History of Phones for Kids. Which phone is the oldest?
  3. Match the years (8 min): your child reads each fact and puts it next to a year. Then read them in order.
  4. Then and now (6 min): your child compares an old and a new phone: "The old phone was bigger. The new phone has got a camera and a screen."
  5. Phones in 2050 (5 min): your child imagines a phone in the year 2050 and writes three sentences: "It will…"
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "What's the best thing about phones? What's the worst?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The first mobile phone call was in 1973." … "The old phone was bigger and heavier." … "In 2050, phones will…"
