Week 20 · Lesson 3: Tell Me About Your Phone

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 20: Calling and sending

Fun for Flyers · Week 20: Calling and sending | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where's my phone? Lesson 2 · Phones then and now Lesson 3 · Tell me about your phone

You Need

  • Two phone cards for Ask and answer (below).
  • Nothing for the chain game – just your voices.

Ask and answer: Ben's phone – When / get? last month · Colour? silver · Big or small screen? big · Who / often call? his cousin · Where / keep? in his pocket · How many messages / each day? about ten · Eva's phone – When / get? on her birthday · Colour? purple · Big or small screen? small · Who / often call? her grandma · Where / keep? in her bag · How many messages / each day? two or three

Verb–noun chain: send → a message · message → read · read → a book · book → write… Each person says a word that goes with the last one. Example: send a message → read a message → read a book → write a book → write an email…

Answers: Questions, for example: When did Ben get his phone? What colour is it? Has it got a big or a small screen? Who does he often call? Where does he keep it? How many messages does he send each day?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Make questions (6 min): your child makes full questions from the card words: "When did Ben get his phone?"
  3. Ask and answer (7 min): you have Ben's card, your child has Eva's. Ask and answer all six questions.
  4. Our phones (5 min): your child answers the same questions about a family phone or tablet. Keeping in touch
  5. Verb–noun chain (7 min): play the chain game. If you can't think of a word, start a new chain!
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "When did Ben get his phone? – Last month." … "He keeps it in his pocket." … "Send a message – read a message – read a book!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a story about a missing phone, choose words from a box, and make the best name for it.
  • Put facts about phones in order with years, and talk about phones then and now.
  • Ask and answer questions about someone's phone, and play a verb–noun chain game.
