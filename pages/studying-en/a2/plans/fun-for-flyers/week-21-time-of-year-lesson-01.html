You Need

  • Eight time cards: a minute · an hour · a day · a weekend · a week · a month · a year · a century.
  • A word box: fall · summer · midday · minutes · days · the weekend · months · a century · winter · midnight · at the moment · a diary.

Read and write the word: Example: There are usually 365 days in this. a year · 1 In the USA, this is another word for autumn. · 2 This is usually the hottest time of the year. · 3 This is another way to say 12 o'clock in the day. · 4 There are sixty of these in an hour. · 5 There are seven of these in a week. · 6 This is Saturday and Sunday. · 7 There are twelve of these in a year. · 8 A hundred years. · 9 This is usually the coldest time of the year. · 10 One day finishes and the next one begins at this time. · 11 Another way to say "now". · 12 You can write about your day in this.

Answers: Order: a minute, an hour, a day, a weekend, a week, a month, a year, a century. Clues: 1 fall · 2 summer · 3 midday · 4 minutes · 5 days · 6 the weekend · 7 months · 8 a century · 9 winter · 10 midnight · 11 at the moment · 12 a diary.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Shortest to longest (4 min): your child puts the eight time cards in order: "A minute is shorter than an hour." Time words
  3. Read and write (10 min): your child reads each clue and writes a word from the box.
  4. How long? (5 min): ask: "How long is a football match? A school day? A summer holiday?" Your child answers with time words.
  5. At the moment (3 min): your child says what each family member is doing at the moment: "At the moment Dad is cooking."
  6. My diary (3 min): your child writes three lines in a diary about today.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A minute is shorter than an hour." … "A century is a hundred years." … "At the moment I'm learning English!"
