Week 21 · Lesson 2: Four Seasons

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 21: The time of the year

Fun for Flyers · Week 21: The time of the year | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Minutes to centuries Lesson 2 · Four seasons Lesson 3 · A day in the forest

You Need

  • Four big boxes on paper: spring · summer · autumn (fall) · winter.
  • Cards: making a snowman · going to the beach · leaves falling · first flowers · picnics · skiing · baby animals · hot soup · swimming in the sea · birds making nests · ice skating · camping · and the twelve months, January to December.

Put the cards in the spring, summer, autumn or winter boxes. Then put the months in the boxes for where you live.

Did you know? In some countries, it's summer in December! And in some hot countries there are only two seasons: a wet one and a dry one.

Answers: Any good answers, for example: spring – first flowers, baby animals, birds making nests · summer – going to the beach, picnics, swimming, camping · autumn – leaves falling, hot soup · winter – making a snowman, skiing, ice skating. Months depend on where you live.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Months of the Year Song. Stand up when you hear your birthday month.
  3. Seasons boxes (8 min): your child puts the cards in the four boxes and says why: "You make a snowman in winter because it's snowy." Seasons
  4. Months (5 min): your child puts the months in the boxes for your country. Is it the same everywhere?
  5. Watch (3 min): Seasons – Months of the Year. Why are the seasons different?
  6. My favourite season (5 min): your child says and writes: "My favourite season is… because… In this season I like…"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Birds make nests in spring." … "My birthday is in July, in the summer." … "My favourite season is winter because I love snow."
