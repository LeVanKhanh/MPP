Week 21 · Lesson 3: A Day in the Forest

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: The World Around Us and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 21: The time of the year

Fun for Flyers · Week 21: The time of the year | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Minutes to centuries Lesson 2 · Four seasons Lesson 3 · A day in the forest

You Need

  • The message below. Your child writes one word on each line.
  • A watch or a phone with a timer for the time games.

Read the message and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Aunt Sue is brilliant! This morning she took us to Green Hill Forest, where we met (1) ____ of her friends. We walked (2) ____ the river and looked for birds. Then it started to rain, (3) ____ we had our lunch in a cave. After that, we made a fire (4) ____ cook some sausages. When we got home, we (5) ____ very tired, but happy!

Time games: 1 One minute: close your eyes and say "Now!" when you think a minute has finished. · 2 How many? How many animals can you say in thirty seconds? · 3 Before and after: "What month is after May? What day is before Friday?"

Answers: 1 some · 2 along · 3 so · 4 to · 5 were.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (8 min): read the message together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Ask about it (4 min): ask: "Where did they go? Why did they have lunch in a cave? What did they cook?"
  4. Time games (9 min): play the three time games. Take turns and keep points.
  5. My message (4 min): your child writes a short message about a great day out: "Grandpa is brilliant! Last Sunday he took us to…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "We walked along the river." … "It started to rain, so we had our lunch in a cave." … "What month is after May? – June!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Put time words in order from the shortest to the longest, and read clues about time (midday, century, at the moment).
  • Say the months and seasons, and what people do in each season.
  • Write one missing word in each line of a message, and play time games.
