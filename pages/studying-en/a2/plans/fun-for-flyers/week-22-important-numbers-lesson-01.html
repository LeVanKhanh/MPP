Week 22 · Lesson 1: How Long? How Far?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 22: Important numbers

Fun for Flyers · Week 22: Important numbers | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · How long? How far? Lesson 2 · Our book of records Lesson 3 · My numbers

You Need

  • Five picture cards: a giraffe · a whale · a very high mountain · the moon · a pencil. Five number cards: 18 cm · 5 m · 25 m · 8,849 m · 384,400 km. Your child matches them.
  • A tape measure (or a ruler) to measure things at home.

Match the pictures and the numbers: How tall is the giraffe? · How long is the whale? · How high is the mountain? · How far away is the moon? · How long is the pencil?

Big numbers: 100 a hundred · 1,000 a thousand · 1,000,000 a million · 8,849 eight thousand, eight hundred and forty-nine · 384,400 three hundred and eighty-four thousand, four hundred.

Answers: giraffe – 5 m tall · whale – 25 m long · mountain – 8,849 m high (Mount Everest) · moon – 384,400 km away · pencil – 18 cm long.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Big Numbers Song. Count up with the song.
  3. Match (5 min): your child matches each picture to a number and says it: "The moon is three hundred and eighty-four thousand, four hundred kilometres away!"
  4. Say big numbers (5 min): write five big numbers. Your child reads them aloud. Numbers
  5. Measure it (8 min): your child measures four things at home and says: "The table is one metre forty long. I'm one metre thirty-five tall."
  6. How far? (4 min): ask: "How far is it to school? To Grandma's house? To the beach?" Your child guesses, then you check together on a map.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The giraffe is five metres tall." … "The moon is very far away." … "I'm one metre thirty-five tall."
