Week 22 · Lesson 2: Our Book of Records

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Family. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 22: Important numbers

Fun for Flyers · Week 22: Important numbers | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · How long? How far? Lesson 2 · Our book of records Lesson 3 · My numbers

You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.
  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes on each line.

Class 5's book of records: Did you know (know / knew / knowing) that our class has got its own book of records? The tallest person in our class (1 is / are / be) Sam. He's one metre fifty-eight! Lucy can say the alphabet very quickly – she only needs nine seconds (2 to / for / at) do it. Ben has visited (3 more / most / much) countries than anyone else: seven! The youngest person in the class is Mia. Her birthday is (4 in / on / at) the 30th of December. And our teacher, Mr Hill, has got the (5 long / longer / longest) name: Jonathan Alexander Hill!

Script: Girl: Grandma, tell me about your mountain again! · Grandma: Well, I climbed Snow Mountain for the first time when I was sixty. · Girl: Sixty! And the second time? · Grandma: On the 14th of June, 2019. We got to the top at eleven a.m. · Girl: How high is it? · Grandma: Three thousand, two hundred metres.

Grandma's mountain: Example: Climbed: Snow Mountain · 1 Age the first time: ____ · 2 Date of second time: ____ June 2019 · 3 Time they got to the top: ____ a.m. · 4 Mountain is: ____ metres high

Answers: Text: 1 is · 2 to · 3 more · 4 on · 5 longest. Form: 1 60 · 2 14th · 3 11 · 4 3,200.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number.
  3. Listen and write (7 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a number on each line.
  4. Watch (3 min): Kids Breaking World Records. Which record would you like to try?
  5. Our family records (7 min): your child writes four records for your family: "The tallest person is Dad. The oldest person is Grandpa…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The tallest person in our class is Sam." … "Her birthday is on the 30th of December." … "Grandma climbed a mountain when she was sixty!"
