Week 22 · Lesson 3: My Numbers

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 22: Important numbers

Fun for Flyers · Week 22: Important numbers | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · How long? How far? Lesson 2 · Our book of records Lesson 3 · My numbers

You Need

  • The sentences below on paper.
  • A calendar for the birthday puzzle.

Complete the sentences with your numbers: I'm ____ metres tall. · My lucky number is ____. · My birthday is on ____. · I think I know ____ English words! · It takes me ____ minutes to come to school. · My school is ____ kilometres from my house. · About ____ people live in my town or city.

The birthday puzzle: Today, I am ____ years, ____ months and ____ days old!

Guess my number: Think of a number between 1 and 1,000. The other person guesses; you say "Bigger!" or "Smaller!"

Answers: Any true answers. Birthday puzzle: count from the last birthday on the calendar.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. My numbers (8 min): your child completes the seven sentences, finding out the numbers they don't know.
  3. Birthday puzzle (7 min): with the calendar, your child works out their age in years, months and days.
  4. Guess my number (6 min): play Guess my number. Who needs fewer guesses?
  5. Number facts (4 min): your child tells you three number facts about themself: "My lucky number is seven. My birthday is on…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "It takes me fifteen minutes to come to school." … "Today I'm ten years, four months and six days old!" … "Is it bigger than five hundred?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say how long, how tall, how high and how far away things are, with big numbers.
  • Read a short text about records and choose the right word from three, and listen and write numbers and dates.
  • Complete sentences with your own numbers, and work out how old you are in years, months and days.
