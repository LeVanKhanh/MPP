Week 23 · Lesson 1: World, Weather, Work Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 23: World, weather, work

Fun for Flyers · Week 23: World, weather, work | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · World, weather, work words Lesson 2 · Ms Green's email Lesson 3 · A windy day story

You Need

  • A word box: an office (example) · websites · a dentist · a cave · a storm · a pop star · fog · a desert · a screen · the sky · a wave · ice · a designer · wifi. Four words are extra.
  • The clues below.

Look and read. Write the correct words. Example: Businessmen and businesswomen work and have meetings in this place. an office · 1 You can find all kinds of information on these when you're online. · 2 This person can help you when you've got a bad tooth. · 3 This is a dark place in a hill or a mountain. Bats often live here. · 4 This weather is very loud, wet and windy. · 5 You might see this person singing on TV. · 6 In this weather, it's difficult to see things that are far away. · 7 This is a very hot, dry place with lots of sand. · 8 You look at words and pictures on this part of a computer. · 9 When you look up in the day, you always see this. · 10 This is sea water that moves up and down and comes to the beach.

Answers: 1 websites · 2 a dentist · 3 a cave · 4 a storm · 5 a pop star · 6 fog · 7 a desert · 8 a screen · 9 the sky · 10 a wave. Extra: ice, a designer, wifi (and the example).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (10 min): your child reads each clue and writes a word from the box. Four words are extra.
  3. Clues for the extras (5 min): your child writes a clue for ice, a designer and wifi.
  4. Three groups (5 min): your child sorts all fourteen words into three groups: world, weather and work. Some can go in two! Jobs
  5. Quick quiz (5 min): ask fast questions: "What do you need in a storm? Who works in an office?" Your child answers in full sentences. Weather
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Bats often live in caves." … "In fog it's difficult to see." … "A designer works in an office."
