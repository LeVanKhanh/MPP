Week 23 · Lesson 2: Ms Green's Email

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 23: World, weather, work

Fun for Flyers · Week 23: World, weather, work | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · World, weather, work words Lesson 2 · Ms Green's email Lesson 3 · A windy day story

You Need

  • The email below. There are no wrong answers – your child chooses words that make sense.

Add information to Ms Green's email. You choose! Good morning, Tom! Thanks for your message. I've got a meeting at 9.30 with the designers, and after that I need to (1) ____. Can you meet me at (2) ____? I want to hear all about your trip to (3) ____. I'd also like to show you some photos of the (4) ____ for our new website. Please bring the (5) ____ to the meeting. My car is at the mechanic's, so I came to work by (6) ____ today. It's going to (7) ____ later, so can you drive me home? I live in (8) ____ Street. See you later! Mary Green

Answers: Any answers that make sense, for example: 1 answer some emails · 2 half past twelve · 3 London · 4 new office · 5 tickets · 6 bus · 7 rain · 8 Hill.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read it first (4 min): read the email together. Ask: "Who is Ms Green? What is her problem today?"
  3. You choose (9 min): your child writes their own words in each gap. Then reads the whole email aloud – does it make sense?
  4. Swap and check (5 min): you fill the gaps too, then compare. Whose email is funnier?
  5. Write back (7 min): your child writes Tom's answer: "Hi Mary, Yes, I can meet you at… I can drive you home. See you!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I've got a meeting at half past nine." … "Can you meet me at twelve o'clock?" … "It's going to rain later."
