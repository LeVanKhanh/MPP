Week 24 · Lesson 1: Journey Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 24: Leaving and arriving

Fun for Flyers · Week 24: Leaving and arriving | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Journey words Lesson 2 · Aunt Rosa's busy week Lesson 3 · My dream journey

You Need

  • A word box: platform · station · pilot · passengers · taxi · city · wheels · ticket · airport · astronauts · journey · rocket. Use two words in each sentence.
  • The "say it another way" pairs below on separate papers.

Choose two words to complete each sentence: 1 You wait on the ____ at a railway ____ for your train. · 2 The ____ flies the plane and the ____ sit and read or sleep. · 3 A ____ driver takes you from one part of the ____ to another. · 4 You usually need a ____ before you can get on a train, and you catch a plane at an ____. · 5 ____ travel into space in a ____. · 6 A bicycle has got two ____, and a long ____ on it makes your legs tired!

Draw lines between words that mean the same: 1 Travelling to the beach takes an hour. · 2 Dad drove me to school. · 3 We arrived at the station at six. · 4 I rode my bike to the park. · 5 We left the house early. · 6 We went on foot. — a Dad gave me a lift. · b We walked. · c The journey takes an hour. · d We got to the station at six. · e I cycled. · f We went out of the house early.

Answers: 1 platform, station · 2 pilot, passengers · 3 taxi, city · 4 ticket, airport · 5 Astronauts, rocket · 6 wheels, journey. Lines: 1 c · 2 a · 3 d · 4 e · 5 f · 6 b.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): Means of Transport for Children. Land, water or air? Say each one.
  3. Two words (8 min): your child writes two words from the box in each sentence. Travel words
  4. Another way (6 min): your child joins each sentence to the one that means the same.
  5. My journey to school (5 min): your child describes their journey to school: "I usually go by bus. The journey takes twenty minutes."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You wait on the platform for your train." … "Dad gave me a lift to school." … "The journey takes twenty minutes."
