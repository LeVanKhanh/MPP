Week 24 · Lesson 2: Aunt Rosa's Busy Week

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 24: Leaving and arriving

Fun for Flyers · Week 24: Leaving and arriving | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Journey words Lesson 2 · Aunt Rosa's busy week Lesson 3 · My dream journey

You Need

  • Six place cards: the airport (example) · a castle · a museum · an island · the mountains · a farm. Eight transport cards with letters: A helicopter · B boat · C taxi · D bus · E train · F bike · G car · H lorry. Two are extra.
  • The questions below for Lesson 2, last step.

Script: Aunt Rosa had a very busy week! On Monday she took a taxi to the airport. · On Tuesday she visited an old castle. She went there by train. · On Wednesday she went to the museum by bus. · On Thursday she went to a small island. She took a boat. · On Friday she flew over the mountains in a helicopter! · And on Saturday she went to her friend's farm on her bike.

Answer the questions. Write 1, 2, 3 or 4 words. 1 How did Aunt Rosa get to the airport? · 2 Which day did she visit the castle? · 3 How did she get to the island? · 4 What did she fly over on Friday? · 5 Whose farm did she go to on Saturday?

Answers: airport – C (taxi) · castle – E (train) · museum – D (bus) · island – B (boat) · mountains – A (helicopter) · farm – F (bike). Extra: G, H. Questions: 1 By taxi · 2 On Tuesday · 3 By boat · 4 The mountains · 5 Her friend's (farm).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the places and the transport.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter for each place.
  4. Answer the questions (7 min): your child writes one to four words for each answer.
  5. Watch (4 min): How Do Hot Air Balloons Work? Would you like to go in a balloon?
  6. Fastest or slowest? (3 min): your child puts the transport cards in order from the slowest to the fastest and says why.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "She took a taxi to the airport." … "She flew over the mountains in a helicopter!" … "A bike is slower than a train."
