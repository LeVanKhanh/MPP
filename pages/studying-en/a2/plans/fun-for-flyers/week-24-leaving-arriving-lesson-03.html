Week 24 · Lesson 3: My Dream Journey

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 24: Leaving and arriving

Fun for Flyers · Week 24: Leaving and arriving | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Journey words Lesson 2 · Aunt Rosa's busy week Lesson 3 · My dream journey

You Need

  • A big paper or a world map, and coloured pencils.

Plan your dream journey: Where do you leave from? · Where do you go first? How do you travel? · Where do you go next? · How long is the journey? · Where do you arrive at the end? · Who goes with you?

Write about your journey (40 words or more): "On my dream journey, I left from… First I went to… by… Then…"

Answers: Any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Plan it (8 min): your child plans a journey with four places and four different kinds of transport, and draws it on the map.
  3. Tell me (5 min): your child tells you about the journey using leave, arrive, journey and get to.
  4. Write it (9 min): your child writes 40 words or more about the journey.
  5. Questions (3 min): you ask: "What was the best part? Was anything difficult?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I left from the airport by plane." … "Then I went to an island by boat." … "At the end I arrived in the mountains."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use travel words – platform, passenger, journey, taxi, wheels – and say things another way: arrive = get to.
  • Listen and match places to ways of travelling, and answer questions with one to four words.
  • Plan a dream journey with different kinds of transport and write about it.
