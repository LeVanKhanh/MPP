Week 25 · Lesson 1: Imagine the Picture

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 25: What shall we do next?

Fun for Flyers · Week 25: What shall we do next? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Imagine the picture Lesson 2 · What shall we take? Lesson 3 · Kim's secret plan

You Need

  • The text below. Your child doesn't see a picture – they imagine it!
  • Paper and coloured pencils.

Leo and Kim at the zoo: Leo and Kim are on their school trip. They're at the zoo, in a big building where the penguins live. They're standing next to a huge window, and they can see the penguins swimming under the water. Their teacher is taking photos. Lots of other children are in the same room. Some of them are eating their snacks.

Imagine the picture and answer: How old are Leo and Kim? · What are they wearing? · Is their teacher a man or a woman? What's he or she wearing? · How many penguins can they see? · Is the room noisy or quiet? · What snacks are the other children eating?

Ask and answer about two school trips: Where did you go on your last school trip? · How did you get there? · What did you see? · What did you eat? · What was the best part?

Answers: Any answers – they come from your child's imagination.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and imagine (4 min): read the text twice, slowly. Your child closes their eyes and imagines the picture.
  3. Answer the questions (6 min): your child answers the six questions with their own ideas.
  4. Draw it (7 min): your child draws the picture they imagined. Then you compare it with the text: is everything there?
  5. Watch (2 min): Gentoo Penguin. Is it like the penguins you imagined?
  6. Two school trips (6 min): ask about your child's last school trip with the five questions. Then your child asks about a trip you went on as a child.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "In my picture, Kim is wearing a red sweater." … "The room is very noisy." … "On my last school trip we went to a farm."
