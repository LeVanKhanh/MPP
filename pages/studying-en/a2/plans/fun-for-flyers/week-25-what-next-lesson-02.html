Week 25 · Lesson 2: What Shall We Take?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 25: What shall we do next?

You Need

  • Paper with ten empty lines. Read the script twice; your child writes ten things.
  • Eight short sentences below for Yes or no.

Script: Teacher: Listen, everyone. Tomorrow we're going to the mountains. You need to take a rucksack with these things in it: a warm sweater, gloves and a scarf, because it's going to be cold. Bring some sandwiches and a bottle of water. Take a map and a torch. Don't forget your umbrella, because it might rain. And bring a pencil and your diary, because we're going to write about the trip!

Yes or no? 1 They're going to the beach. · 2 It's going to be cold. · 3 They need to bring sandwiches. · 4 They don't need a map. · 5 They should bring an umbrella. · 6 They need a camera. · 7 They're going to write about the trip. · 8 They need a torch.

Answers: Ten things: rucksack, sweater, gloves, scarf, sandwiches, (bottle of) water, map, torch, umbrella, pencil (and diary). Yes or no: 1 no · 2 yes · 3 yes · 4 no · 5 yes · 6 no · 7 yes · 8 yes.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes ten things to take.
  3. Yes or no? (6 min): read the eight sentences. Your child writes yes or no.
  4. Watch (3 min): What Are You Going to Do? Answer with "I'm going to…"
  5. Our trip (8 min): plan a real family day out. Your child says: "We're going to… We're going to take… because…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We're going to the mountains." … "Take an umbrella because it might rain." … "We're going to take a map and a torch."
