You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two, three or four words.

Kim's secret plan: Kim was too excited to sleep. She got out of bed and phoned her grandpa. "I'm going on a school camping trip tomorrow!" she said. "We're going to sleep in tents next to a lake." Grandpa was surprised. "That sounds fantastic! What are you going to do there?" "We're going to look for bats at night with our torches, and we're going to cook sausages on a fire. And my friend Ben and I have got a plan. We're going to make a big cake in secret, because it's our teacher's birthday on Saturday!" "What a good idea," said Grandpa. "Does your teacher know?" "No!" laughed Kim. "It's a surprise!"

Complete the sentences: Example: Kim was too excited to sleep. · 1 Kim phoned her ____. · 2 The children are going to sleep in tents next to ____. · 3 They're going to look for bats with ____. · 4 They're going to cook ____ on a fire. · 5 Kim and Ben are going to make ____. · 6 It's their teacher's ____ on Saturday. · 7 The teacher doesn't know because it's a ____.

Answers: 1 grandpa · 2 a lake · 3 their torches · 4 sausages · 5 a (big) cake · 6 birthday · 7 surprise.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (6 min): read the story together. Ask: "Why couldn't Kim sleep? What's the secret plan?"
  3. Complete the sentences (8 min): your child writes one to four words in each sentence.
  4. Going to (5 min): your child finds all the "going to" sentences in the story and reads them.
  5. Topic words game (6 min): say a topic: "camping!" Each person says a word for that topic. If you can't think of one, you're out. Then a new topic: "school", "the zoo"…
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "We're going to sleep in tents next to a lake." … "We're going to make a cake in secret." … "It's a surprise!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a short text, imagine the picture, and answer questions about it.
  • Listen and write things to take on a trip, and say if sentences are right or wrong.
  • Read a story about plans with "going to" and complete sentences with one to four words.
