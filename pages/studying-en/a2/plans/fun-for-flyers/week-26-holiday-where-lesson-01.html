Week 26 · Lesson 1: Hotel or Tent?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 26: Where can we go on holiday?

You Need

  • Four word circles (letters in a circle, no spaces): tentlakeforestfire · hotelcitymuseumtaxi · boatislandbeachsail · mountainsnowclimbcave. Four pictures: a tent by a lake · a hotel in a city · a boat near an island · a snowy mountain.
  • The holiday diary below.

Complete the holiday diary. Write one word in each space. Our hotel is next to the beach. Every morning we have breakfast (1) ____ the garden. (2) ____ breakfast, my brother and I go swimming in the sea. Yesterday we (3) ____ some dolphins! In the evening, there's music (4) ____ dancing. My dad dances very badly, (5) ____ he's funny! Tomorrow we're going (6) ____ visit an island. I think staying in a hotel is (7) ____ fun than camping, because the beds are soft!

Answers: Circles match: tent/lake – tent picture · hotel/city – hotel picture · boat/island – boat picture · mountain/snow – mountain picture. Diary: 1 in · 2 After · 3 saw · 4 and · 5 but · 6 to · 7 more.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Camping – Kids Vocab. Which camping things would you take?
  3. Word circles (6 min): your child finds the words in each circle and matches the circle to a picture. Holiday words
  4. Holiday diary (8 min): your child writes one word in each space, then reads the diary aloud.
  5. Hotel or tent? (4 min): your child says which is better and why: "I prefer camping because you can sleep under the stars."
  6. My diary (3 min): your child writes two lines of a holiday diary: a real holiday or a dream one.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We have breakfast in the garden." … "Staying in a hotel is more fun than camping." … "I prefer camping because…"
