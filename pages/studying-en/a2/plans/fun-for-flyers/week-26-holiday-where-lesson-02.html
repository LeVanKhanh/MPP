Week 26 · Lesson 2: Holiday Chat

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 26: Where can we go on holiday?

Fun for Flyers · Week 26: Where can we go on holiday? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Hotel or tent? Lesson 2 · Holiday chat Lesson 3 · The day we lost the map

You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Lily's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.

Read the conversation and choose the best answer: Example: Sam: The holidays start next week! Lily: C · 1 Sam: Where do you usually go on holiday? · 2 Sam: Do you stay in a hotel? · 3 Sam: What do you take with you? · 4 Sam: What do you do every day there? · 5 Sam: We're going to visit London this year. Have you been there?

Lily's answers: A No, we sleep in a tent. We prefer that! · B To a lake in the mountains. · C I know! It's brilliant! · D Lots of things, like warm blankets and torches. · E Yes, two years ago. You're going to love it! · F We swim, we walk and we look for wild animals. · G What a big building! · H It's on Tuesday.

Answers: 1 B · 2 A · 3 D · 4 F · 5 E. Extra: G, H.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read Sam's questions together. Your child chooses Lily's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (4 min): you are Sam, your child is Lily. Read the conversation with feeling.
  4. Your answers (7 min): ask Sam's five questions again. Your child gives their own true answers.
  5. Ask me (5 min): your child asks you about a holiday you had as a child.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "We prefer sleeping in a tent!" … "We look for wild animals." … "Have you been to London? – Yes, two years ago."
