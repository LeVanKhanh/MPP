Week 26 · Lesson 3: The Day We Lost the Map

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 26: Where can we go on holiday?

Fun for Flyers · Week 26: Where can we go on holiday? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Hotel or tent? Lesson 2 · Holiday chat Lesson 3 · The day we lost the map

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five pictures: 1 a family walking in a forest, Dad holding a map · 2 the wind blowing the map into a river · 3 the family standing at three paths, looking worried · 4 a farmer on a tractor pointing the way · 5 the family at a lake with their tent, laughing.
  • Coloured pencils. Read the instructions twice for picture 5.

Look at the pictures and tell the story: Start: "One windy day, Helen, Robert and their dad went for a long walk in the forest…"

Listen and colour and write (picture 5): Colour the tent orange. · Colour the lake blue and draw two ducks on it. · Write CAMP on the flag next to the tent. · Colour Robert's sweater green with yellow stripes. · Draw the sun going down behind the hill.

Answers: Story: any good story that matches the pictures. Colour and write as said.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (4 min): your child says what is happening in each picture: "In picture 2, the map is flying into the river!"
  3. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the whole story, starting with the first line. Use: first, then, suddenly, after that, in the end.
  4. Write the end (5 min): your child writes three sentences about picture 5.
  5. Listen and colour and write (6 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours, draws or writes in picture 5.
  6. Talk (2 min): ask: "Have you ever got lost? What did you do?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Suddenly the wind took the map!" … "A farmer showed them the way." … "In the end they found the lake."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find holiday words in word circles, and write one word in each gap of a holiday diary.
  • Choose the best answer in a conversation about holidays.
  • Tell a picture story about a holiday that went wrong, and listen and colour and write.
