Week 27 · Lesson 1: An Adventure in the Forest

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 27: It's the holidays! Bye!

Fun for Flyers · Week 27: It's the holidays! Bye! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · An adventure in the forest Lesson 2 · Leo's holiday plans Lesson 3 · My first holiday day

You Need

  • Seven picture cards (draw them): a picnic · a beach · a cave · a dog · an old map · a girl · a boat.
  • A word box: lake (example) · stream · strawberries · cave · touch · wonderful · missing · hid · unkind · collecting. Four words are extra.

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–5. Every summer, Lucy goes to the lake with her grandparents and her cousin Tom. One afternoon, they walked along a small (1) ____ into the forest and had a picnic. They ate cheese sandwiches and (2) ____. Then Tom found a dark (3) ____ behind some trees. "Perhaps a bear lives in there!" he whispered. Lucy wasn't frightened. She went in with her torch. Inside, there were hundreds of bats! "Don't (4) ____ them!" said Grandpa. "They're sleeping." When they came out, Grandma said, "What a (5) ____ day!"

Choose the best name: Lucy's new torch · An adventure in the forest · Grandpa's lake house

Answers: 1 stream · 2 strawberries · 3 cave · 4 touch · 5 wonderful. Extra: missing, hid, unkind, collecting. Best name: An adventure in the forest.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Seven-picture story (8 min): your child puts the seven cards in an order and tells a story with all of them: "One day, a girl found an old map on the beach…"
  3. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  5. Would you go in? (6 min): ask: "Would you go into the cave? Why? What would you take?" Your child answers with three reasons.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Perhaps a bear lives in there!" … "Don't touch them – they're sleeping." … "What a wonderful day!"
