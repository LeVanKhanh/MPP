Week 27 · Lesson 2: Leo's Holiday Plans

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 27: It's the holidays! Bye!

Fun for Flyers · Week 27: It's the holidays! Bye! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · An adventure in the forest Lesson 2 · Leo's holiday plans Lesson 3 · My first holiday day

You Need

  • Ten plan cards with tick boxes: play chess · eat lots of biscuits · watch a TV programme · go on a journey · repair his bike · write messages · join a club · play video games · be really lazy · read an adventure story.

Script: Grandma: It's the holidays, Leo! What are you going to do today? · Leo: Well, first I'm going to be really lazy and stay in bed until ten! · Grandma: And then? · Leo: Then I'm going to repair my bike. The wheel's broken. · Grandma: Are you going to play video games? · Leo: No, not today. I'm going to play chess with Dad after lunch. · Grandma: Good idea! · Leo: And I'm going to join the swimming club at the sports centre. · Grandma: Wonderful! And this evening? · Leo: I'm going to read my new adventure story. It's about pirates!

Answers: Tick: be really lazy, repair his bike, play chess, join a club, read an adventure story. Not: biscuits, TV programme, journey, messages, video games.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the cards (3 min): your child reads the ten plan cards aloud.
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks five things Leo is going to do.
  4. Leo's day (4 min): your child tells Leo's day: "First he's going to stay in bed until ten. Then…"
  5. Watch (4 min): What Are You Going to Do? Answer the song with your own plans.
  6. Which five? (6 min): your child chooses five cards for their own perfect holiday day and explains: "I'm going to join a club because…" Hobbies
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm going to be really lazy!" … "I'm going to repair my bike." … "I'm going to join the swimming club."
