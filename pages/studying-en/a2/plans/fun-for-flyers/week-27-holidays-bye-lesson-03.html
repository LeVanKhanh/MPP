Week 27 · Lesson 3: My First Holiday Day

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Daily Routines. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 27: It's the holidays! Bye!

Fun for Flyers · Week 27: It's the holidays! Bye! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · An adventure in the forest Lesson 2 · Leo's holiday plans Lesson 3 · My first holiday day

You Need

  • The sentences below on paper.
  • Nothing for the game – just your voices.

Tomorrow is the first day of my holidays! I'm not going to get up until ____. · I'm going to have ____ for breakfast. · Then I'm going to put on my ____ and call my friend ____, because ____. · While I'm having lunch, I'm going to ____. · Then I'm going to go to ____, and then I'm going to ____. · After dinner, I'm going to ____. · And I'm not going to go to bed until ____! See you! Bye for now.

Really? Say a sentence about your plans. It can be true or not true: "Tomorrow I'm going to fly to the moon." The other person says "Really?" if they think it's not true.

Answers: Any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Write my plans (10 min): your child completes all the sentences about their first holiday day.
  3. Read it (3 min): your child reads the plan to you like a message.
  4. Ask me (4 min): your child asks: "What are you going to do tomorrow?" You answer with three plans.
  5. Really? (8 min): play "Really?" Take turns. One point for each right "Really?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I'm not going to get up until nine!" … "While I'm having lunch, I'm going to watch a film." … "Really? I don't believe you!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Make a story from seven pictures, and read a story and choose words from a box.
  • Listen and tick what someone is going to do in the holidays.
  • Write about your plans for the first day of the holidays, and play "Really?".
