Week 28 · Lesson 1: What Were They Doing?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 28: I want to win!

Fun for Flyers · Week 28: I want to win! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What were they doing? Lesson 2 · I won! Lesson 3 · Ben's cooking competition

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a school quiz competition with a clock showing quarter past three: a boy (Sam) writing an answer · a girl (Kate) looking at the clock, worried · a boy (Ben) eating a snack · a girl (Lily) taking a photo · a man (Mr Hill) explaining the game · a girl (Mia) holding the prize cup. Write the names under the people.

At quarter past three, who… Example: was writing an answer? Sam · 1 was feeling worried about the time? · 2 was eating something? · 3 was explaining the game? · 4 was taking a photo? · 5 was holding the prize?

Answer the questions, then write the story: Are all these children in the same class? · What were they doing before the quiz? · What time is it? · Is it a competition? · Who won? · What happened at the end?

Answers: 1 Kate · 2 Ben · 3 Mr Hill · 4 Lily · 5 Mia. Story: any story that matches the picture.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Past Continuous Song. Say the "was …ing" sentences with the song.
  3. Who was…? (6 min): your child answers the five questions with names from the picture.
  4. Was or were? (5 min): your child says what everyone was doing: "Sam was writing. Kate and Ben were…"
  5. Write the story (9 min): your child answers the six questions, then writes a short story about the quiz (40 words or more). Story sentences
  6. Yesterday at six (3 min): ask: "What were you doing yesterday at six o'clock? And at eight?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "At quarter past three, Sam was writing an answer." … "Kate was feeling worried." … "Yesterday at six I was eating dinner."
