Week 28 · Lesson 2: I Won!

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 28: I want to win!

Fun for Flyers · Week 28: I want to win! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What were they doing? Lesson 2 · I won! Lesson 3 · Ben's cooking competition

You Need

  • The message below. Your child writes one word on each line.

Read Ella's message and write the missing words. Hi Sam! I can't believe it! I won the drawing competition! I drew a picture of my grandma's garden with lots (1) ____ butterflies. The prize is four tickets (2) ____ the new swimming pool. It's (3) ____ to the sports centre. We're going on Sunday. (4) ____ you like to come with us? My mum can drive us all there in her car. They say it's really big (5) ____ exciting! Ask your parents and tell me tomorrow. See you! Ella

Talk: Have you ever won a prize? · What competition would you like to enter? · Is it more important to win or to have fun?

Answers: 1 of · 2 for · 3 next · 4 Would · 5 and.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): read the message together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Write back (6 min): your child writes Sam's answer: "Hi Ella! Well done! I'd love to come…"
  4. Talk (6 min): ask the three questions. Your child answers with reasons. Sport words
  5. Make a prize (4 min): your child designs a prize cup or a certificate for a family competition and writes what it's for.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I won the drawing competition!" … "The prize is four tickets for the swimming pool." … "I think having fun is more important than winning."
