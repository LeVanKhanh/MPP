Week 28 · Lesson 3: Ben's Cooking Competition

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 28: I want to win!

Fun for Flyers · Week 28: I want to win! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What were they doing? Lesson 2 · I won! Lesson 3 · Ben's cooking competition

You Need

  • Six questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.
  • Paper for the word game.

Script: Girl: Hi, Ben! What are you doing? · Ben: I'm getting ready for a cooking competition. · Girl: Who's helping you? · Ben: My grandpa. He's a brilliant cook. · Girl: When is it? · Ben: On the 12th of May. · Girl: How did you find out about it? · Ben: I saw it on the school website. · Girl: What's the first prize? · Ben: A new bike! · Girl: Where are you going now? · Ben: To the supermarket. I need some lemons and some flour.

Listen and tick: Example: What kind of competition is it? A drawing B music C cooking · 1 Who is helping Ben? A his grandpa B his aunt C his teacher · 2 What's the date of the competition? A 12th May B 12th June C 20th May · 3 How did Ben find out? A in a newspaper B on the website C from a friend · 4 What's the first prize? A a cup B a bike C money · 5 Where is Ben going now? A the supermarket B the park C the sports centre

Word game: How many words can you make from these letters? A WONDERFUL COOKING COMPETITION

Answers: Example C · 1 A · 2 A · 3 B · 4 B · 5 A. Word game: any real words, for example cook, cool, wood, win, tent, ring, point, kite, moon, net.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the pictures for each question.
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  4. Word game (9 min): you both make words from the title in five minutes. Who has more? Check the spelling together.
  5. Our competition (5 min): plan a small family competition: "We're going to have a… competition. The prize is…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I'm getting ready for a cooking competition." … "The first prize is a new bike!" … "I can make cook, wood and win!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what people were doing at a time in the past: "At quarter past three, Sam was writing."
  • Write one missing word in each line of a message about winning a competition.
  • Listen and tick details about a competition, and make lots of words from a long title.
