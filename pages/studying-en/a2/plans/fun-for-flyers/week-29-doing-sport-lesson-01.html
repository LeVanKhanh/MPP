Week 29 · Lesson 1: A Snowy Day

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Sport and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 29: Doing sport! Having fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 29: Doing sport! Having fun! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A snowy day Lesson 2 · Sports quiz Lesson 3 · My sport

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a snowy hill: a boy on a sledge · a girl skiing · a man carrying a snowboard · two children making a snowman · a girl ice skating on a lake · a dog playing in the snow · a woman throwing a snowball · a bird on the roof of a house.
  • Write seven names around the picture: Kim, Max, Lucy, Dan, Ella, Sam, Zoe. Read the script twice; your child draws lines.

Script: What a cold, snowy day! Can you see Max? He's the boy on the sledge. He's going really fast! · Lucy is skiing. She's wearing a purple jacket. · Dan is carrying his snowboard up the hill. · Kim and Sam are making a snowman. It's got a carrot for a nose! · Zoe is ice skating on the lake. · And Ella is throwing a snowball at her dad!

Write answers about Zoe: Where is she? · How old is she? · What is she wearing? · What is she doing? · How is she feeling? · Is she having fun?

Answers: Lines: Max – sledge · Lucy – skiing · Dan – snowboard · Kim and Sam – snowman · Zoe – ice skating · Ella – snowball. Twelve things, for example: snow, a sledge, skis, a snowboard, a snowman, a lake, a dog, a snowball, a roof, a bird, a house, a hill. Zoe: any answers that match the picture.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Winter Sports! Do each winter sport with your body.
  3. Twelve things (6 min): your child writes twelve things they can see in the picture.
  4. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  5. Write about Zoe (6 min): your child writes answers to the six questions about Zoe.
  6. Snow or sun? (4 min): ask: "Have you ever played in the snow? What would you like to try – a sledge or skis? Why?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Max is the boy on the sledge." … "Lucy is skiing in a purple jacket." … "I'd like to try a sledge because it looks fast!"
