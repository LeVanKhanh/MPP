Week 29 · Lesson 2: Sports Quiz

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 29: Doing sport! Having fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 29: Doing sport! Having fun! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A snowy day Lesson 2 · Sports quiz Lesson 3 · My sport

You Need

  • The ten clues below and a word box: fishing · volleyball · skiing · table tennis · swimming · golf · ice skating · basketball · sailing · hockey.

Write the answers to the sports quiz: 1 People sit next to a river or a lake and wait for something to eat their food. Later, they might eat it! · 2 Five players in each team bounce the ball and throw it through a high net. · 3 You wear long, thin boots on your feet and move quickly down the snow. · 4 Two or four people hit a small, light ball across a table. · 5 You use your arms and legs to move through the water. · 6 You hit a very small ball and try to make it go into a hole with a flag in it. · 7 People do this sport on ice. It sometimes looks like dancing. · 8 Players hit the ball over a net with their hands. It can't touch the ground! · 9 People race boats in the wind on the sea or a lake. · 10 Players hit a hard ball across grass or ice, and try to score goals.

Answers: 1 fishing · 2 basketball · 3 skiing · 4 table tennis · 5 swimming · 6 golf · 7 ice skating · 8 volleyball · 9 sailing · 10 hockey.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (10 min): your child reads each clue and writes the sport. Sport words
  3. Which is the most…? (5 min): ask: "Which sport is the most dangerous? The most exciting? The easiest?" Your child answers with reasons.
  4. Watch (4 min): Sports & Sports Verbs Chant. Do the actions.
  5. Make a clue (6 min): your child writes two clues for sports that aren't in the quiz (football, tennis, running) and you guess.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "In golf you hit a very small ball." … "I think skiing is the most exciting sport." … "In volleyball the ball can't touch the ground!"
