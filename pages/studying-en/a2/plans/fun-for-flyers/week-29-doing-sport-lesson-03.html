Week 29 · Lesson 3: My Sport

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Sport and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 29: Doing sport! Having fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 29: Doing sport! Having fun! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A snowy day Lesson 2 · Sports quiz Lesson 3 · My sport

You Need

  • The snowy-day picture from Lesson 1. Before the lesson, draw a second picture with five differences: the dog is now a cat · Max's sledge is red, not green · there are two snowmen · Ella is throwing the snowball at a tree · it's snowing.
  • The questions below for Listen and write.

Listen and write your answers: 1 What's your favourite sport? · 2 When do you do it? · 3 Who do you do it with? · 4 Where do you do it? · 5 What do you need for it? · 6 What sport would you like to try one day?

Answers: Differences: dog/cat · green/red sledge · one/two snowmen · snowball at Dad/at a tree · not snowing/snowing. Questions: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find the differences (7 min): your child finds five differences: "In the first picture there's a dog, but in the second there's a cat."
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read each question twice. Your child writes a short answer.
  4. Tell me (5 min): your child uses their answers to tell you about their favourite sport in one minute.
  5. Sports poster (5 min): your child makes a small poster: "Try volleyball!" with three reasons.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "In the first picture there's a dog, but in the second there's a cat." … "My favourite sport is swimming." … "I'd like to try golf one day."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things in a snowy picture (sledge, ski, snowboard, snowman) and listen and draw lines to names.
  • Read clues and find sports: golf, hockey, volleyball, table tennis, skiing, sailing.
  • Find the differences between two pictures, and answer questions about your favourite sport.
