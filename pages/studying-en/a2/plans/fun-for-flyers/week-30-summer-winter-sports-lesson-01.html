Week 30 · Lesson 1: Who Won the Race?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Sport and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 30: Summer and winter sports

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five skiers (A–E): A green helmet, jacket with spots, gloves, yellow skis · B green helmet, jacket with spots, no gloves, red skis · C green helmet, jacket with spots, no gloves, yellow skis · D blue helmet, jacket with spots, no gloves, yellow skis · E green helmet, striped jacket, no gloves, yellow skis.
  • The eight sentences below. Your child writes to, because, while or so.

Which skier won the race? The person who won wore a green helmet… · and their jacket had spots on it… · but they forgot their gloves… · and their skis were yellow.

Write to, because, while or so: Example: Holly and I went to the mountains because we love snow sports. · 1 Holly couldn't ski, ____ she took her sledge instead. · 2 It was funny! Holly fell in the snow ____ she was talking on her phone. · 3 We took our skates ____ we could skate on the lake. · 4 We went to the café ____ have some hot chocolate. · 5 I skated round the lake ____ Holly was making a snowman. · 6 It was very cold, ____ we went home at four o'clock. · 7 I wanted to ski better, ____ I practised every day. · 8 Mum took lots of photos ____ she wanted to remember the holiday.

Answers: Winner: C. Sentences: 1 so · 2 while · 3 because · 4 to · 5 while · 6 so · 7 so · 8 because.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Winter Sports! Which winter sport would you choose?
  3. Who won? (7 min): read the clues one by one. After each clue, your child crosses out the skiers who can't be the winner.
  4. to, because, while, so (9 min): your child writes a joining word in each sentence and reads it aloud.
  5. My sentences (4 min): your child makes one sentence with each word: "I went to the park to play football."
  6. Summer or winter? (3 min): ask: "Do you prefer summer sports or winter sports? Why?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The winner wore a green helmet." … "Holly fell in the snow while she was talking on her phone." … "I practised every day, so I got better."
