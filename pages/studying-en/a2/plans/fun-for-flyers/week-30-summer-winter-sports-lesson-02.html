Week 30 · Lesson 2: The Wrong Backpack

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 30: Summer and winter sports

Fun for Flyers · Week 30: Summer and winter sports | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who won the race? Lesson 2 · The wrong backpack Lesson 3 · Why?

You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two, three or four words.

The wrong backpack! Every Saturday, Lily and her grandpa play tennis at the sports centre. Last Saturday, they took the train there. Lily put her tennis racket and her trainers in her green backpack. On the train, there were lots of people. A girl with a green backpack was sitting next to Lily. When they got to the station, Grandpa picked up a backpack and they hurried to the sports centre. At the sports centre, Lily opened the backpack. Inside there wasn't a racket. There was a violin! "Oh no! It's the wrong backpack!" said Lily. Grandpa phoned the station. Half an hour later, the girl from the train arrived with Lily's backpack. "I've got your racket and you've got my violin!" she laughed. "I've got a music lesson in ten minutes!"

Write answers: Example: Lily and Grandpa play tennis every Saturday. · 1 Last Saturday, they went to the sports centre by ____. · 2 Lily's backpack was ____. · 3 Who picked up the wrong backpack? · 4 What was inside the wrong backpack? · 5 Who did Grandpa phone? · 6 The girl had a ____ in ten minutes.

Answers: 1 train · 2 green · 3 Grandpa · 4 a violin · 5 the station · 6 music lesson.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "Why did Grandpa take the wrong backpack?"
  3. Write answers (8 min): your child writes one to four words for each answer.
  4. Act it out (6 min): act the story: one person is Lily, one is the girl with the violin. Change the end if you like!
  5. Talk (4 min): ask: "Have you ever taken the wrong thing by mistake? What happened?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's the wrong backpack!" … "There was a violin inside!" … "I've got your racket and you've got my violin!"
