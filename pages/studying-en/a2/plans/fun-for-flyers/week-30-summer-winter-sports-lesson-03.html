Week 30 · Lesson 3: Why?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 30: Summer and winter sports

Fun for Flyers · Week 30: Summer and winter sports | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who won the race? Lesson 2 · The wrong backpack Lesson 3 · Why?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures: 1 a boy and his sister pulling a sledge up a snowy hill · 2 the sledge going very fast down the hill · 3 the sledge landing in a big pile of snow, only their boots showing · 4 the children and their mum laughing, with snow everywhere.

Look at the pictures and tell the story: Start: "One cold winter morning, Tom and his sister…"

The "Why?" game: Example: "I'm going to the park." – "Why?" – "Because I want to play football." – "Why?" – "Because it's my favourite sport." – "Why?" – …

Answers: Story: any good story that matches the pictures.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the story with the four pictures. Use: first, then, suddenly, in the end.
  3. Write the story (8 min): your child writes the story with 40 words or more. Story words
  4. The "Why?" game (7 min): your child says a sentence. You keep asking "Why?" and your child answers with because. How many times can they answer? Then swap.
  5. Read it (2 min): your child reads their story aloud.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Suddenly the sledge went really fast!" … "Why? – Because it's my favourite sport!" … "In the end, there was snow everywhere!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read clues to find the winner of a race, and join ideas with to, because, while and so.
  • Read a story about a mistake and write answers with one to four words.
  • Tell a picture story, and play the "Why?" game with because.
