You Need

  • Six place cards with numbers (draw them): 1 a stadium · 2 a theatre · 3 a museum · 4 a post office · 5 a restaurant · 6 a castle.
  • The six sentences below on separate papers, with letters A–F.

Match the pictures with the sentences: A Mia sent a birthday present to her cousin from here. · B My aunt sang in a concert at this place last week. · C We saw dinosaur teeth and a very old ship here. · D Ben watched his favourite football team win here. · E People come here to choose something delicious to eat. · F A king and a queen lived here hundreds of years ago.

Answers: 1 stadium – D · 2 theatre – B · 3 museum – C · 4 post office – A · 5 restaurant – E · 6 castle – F.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Parts of the City. Say the places you know before the video.
  3. Match (6 min): your child reads each sentence and puts it under the right place card. Places
  4. Here and there (6 min): your child describes a place without saying its name: "You can see lots of paintings here." You guess. Then swap.
  5. My town (5 min): your child says which of these places are in your town and where: "There's a stadium near the river."
  6. Best place (3 min): ask: "Which place would you most like to visit this weekend? Why?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Mia sent a present from the post office." … "A king and a queen lived in this castle." … "There's a stadium near the river."
