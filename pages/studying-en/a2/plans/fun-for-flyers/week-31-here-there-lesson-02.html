Week 31 · Lesson 2: The Hungry Dog

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: My Street and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 31: Here and there

Fun for Flyers · Week 31: Here and there | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where did it happen? Lesson 2 · The hungry dog Lesson 3 · At the museum café

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures: 1 a family eating in a restaurant, their dog sitting outside · 2 a waiter carrying a plate of sausages, the dog jumping up and taking one · 3 the waiter and the family running after the dog in the park; the dog giving the sausage to some ducks.
  • Draw picture 3 again with five differences: two ducks not three · the waiter has no hat · it's raining · the dog is white, not brown · there's a girl on a bike.

Look at the three pictures. Write about this story. Write 20 or more words. Start: "Last Saturday, the Green family went to a restaurant…"

Answers: Story: any story of 20 or more words that matches the pictures. Differences: three/two ducks · hat/no hat · sunny/raining · brown/white dog · no girl/a girl on a bike.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tell the pictures (5 min): your child tells you what happens in each picture.
  3. Write the story (10 min): your child writes the story with 20 words or more. Count the words together!
  4. Find five differences (7 min): your child compares the two picture 3s: "In this picture the dog is brown, but in that one it's white."
  5. Read it (3 min): your child reads the story aloud with feeling.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The dog jumped up and took a sausage!" … "Everyone ran after the dog." … "In the end, the dog gave the sausage to the ducks!"
