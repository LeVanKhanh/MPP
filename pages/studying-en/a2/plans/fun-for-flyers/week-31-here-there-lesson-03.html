Week 31 · Lesson 3: At the Museum Café

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 31: Here and there

Fun for Flyers · Week 31: Here and there | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where did it happen? Lesson 2 · The hungry dog Lesson 3 · At the museum café

You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Kim's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.

Uncle Tom is talking to Kim in a café in the museum. What does Kim say? Example: Uncle Tom: What a nice café! Kim: B · 1 Uncle Tom: Shall we sit over there, next to the door? · 2 Uncle Tom: What would you like to eat and drink? · 3 Uncle Tom: Have you been to this museum before? · 4 Uncle Tom: Which part of the museum did you like most? · 5 Uncle Tom: What would you like to do next?

Kim's answers: A The room with the dinosaurs. They were enormous! · B Yes! And I really enjoyed the museum too. · C I think by the window is better. · D A pancake and an orange juice, please. · E Can we go to the museum shop? · F Only once, on a school trip. · G No, it's on Friday. · H It's my favourite colour.

Answers: 1 C · 2 D · 3 F · 4 A · 5 E. Extra: G, H.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read Uncle Tom's questions together. Your child chooses Kim's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (4 min): you are Uncle Tom, your child is Kim. Act the conversation.
  4. Watch (2 min): May I Take Your Order? Say the café sentences with the video.
  5. Play café (7 min): your child is the waiter at a café; you order: "Can I have…, please?" Then swap.
  6. My answers (3 min): ask questions 3, 4 and 5 about a real museum or place your child visited.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I think by the window is better." … "A pancake and an orange juice, please." … "Can we go to the museum shop?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name places in a town – stadium, theatre, museum, post office, restaurant, castle – and match sentences to them.
  • Write a story of 20 words or more from three pictures, and find five differences.
  • Choose the best answers in a conversation in a café.
