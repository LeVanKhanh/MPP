Week 32 · Lesson 1: Where Did Leo Take It?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: My Street and Toys. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 32: Where?

Fun for Flyers · Week 32: Where? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where did Leo take it? Lesson 2 · Homes around the world Lesson 3 · My dream home

You Need

  • Six thing cards (draw them, 1–6): some stamps · a birthday card · a board game · a box of chocolates · a magazine · an envelope.
  • Eight place cards with letters: A a stadium · B a museum · C a railway station · D a university · E a hotel · F a factory · G a post office · H a library. Two are extra.

Script: Leo was very busy on Saturday. First he took the envelope to the post office. · Then he took his magazine to the railway station to read on the train. · He went to see his sister at the university and gave her the birthday card. · After that, he took the box of chocolates to his grandma at the hotel where she works. · He took the stamps to the museum because there's a stamp room there. · And in the evening he took the board game to the library, because there's a games club there on Saturdays.

Answers: envelope – G · magazine – C · birthday card – D · chocolates – E · stamps – B · board game – H. Extra: A, F.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Name the things (4 min): your child writes the name under each thing card.
  3. Listen and match (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter next to each thing.
  4. Leo's day (4 min): your child tells Leo's day: "First he took the envelope to the post office…"
  5. Where would you take it? (5 min): your child chooses three things from home and says where they would take them and why. Places
  6. Write a card (4 min): your child writes a short birthday card to someone in the family.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "He took the envelope to the post office." … "There's a games club at the library." … "I'd take my football to the stadium!"
