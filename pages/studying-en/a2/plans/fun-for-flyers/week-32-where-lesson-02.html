Week 32 · Lesson 2: Homes Around the World

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: At Home and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 32: Where?

You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.

Homes around the world: A long time ago (since / for / ago), many people lived in caves. Today, homes are very different. Some people live (1 in / on / at) boats on rivers. In very cold places, some people make homes (2 from / of / with) ice and snow. In big cities, lots of people live in tall buildings (3 calls / called / calling) skyscrapers. Some skyscrapers have more (4 that / then / than) a hundred floors! Some new buildings have gardens on (5 there / their / they) roofs. Plants and trees on buildings help to make the air (6 clean / cleaner / cleanest).

Answers: 1 on · 2 from · 3 called · 4 than · 5 their · 6 cleaner.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and says why.
  3. Watch (4 min): Different Homes Around the World. Which home would you like to live in?
  4. Compare (6 min): your child compares two homes: "A skyscraper is taller than a house. A boat home moves, but a cave doesn't."
  5. Green roofs (6 min): ask: "Why are green roofs a good idea?" Your child gives two reasons, then draws a building with a green roof.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Some people live on boats." … "Some skyscrapers have more than a hundred floors!" … "Plants on buildings make the air cleaner."
