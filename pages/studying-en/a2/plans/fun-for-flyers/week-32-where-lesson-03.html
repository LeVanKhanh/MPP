Week 32 · Lesson 3: My Dream Home

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 32: Where?

Fun for Flyers · Week 32: Where? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Where did Leo take it? Lesson 2 · Homes around the world Lesson 3 · My dream home

You Need

  • Four picture cards (draw them): a house in a big tree · a round house made of glass · a home on a boat · a house with grass on the roof.
  • Big paper and coloured pencils.

What is unusual about these homes? Talk about each one: Where is it? What's it made of? What's good about it? What's difficult about living there?

My dream home (40 words or more): Where is it? · What's it made of? · How many rooms has it got? · What's on the roof? · What's the best thing about it?

Answers: Any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Unusual homes (7 min): your child talks about each picture: "This home is unusual because it's in a tree. It's good because you can see the birds, but it's difficult when it's windy."
  3. Design it (8 min): your child draws a dream home and labels five parts. Home words
  4. Write it (8 min): your child writes 40 words or more about the dream home.
  5. Show and tell (2 min): your child shows the picture and reads the text.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "This home is unusual because it's made of glass." … "My dream home is on an island." … "It's got a swimming pool on the roof!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things like stamps, an envelope and a magazine, and listen and match them to places.
  • Read a text about homes around the world and choose the right word from three.
  • Describe unusual homes, and design and write about your dream home.
