Week 33 · Lesson 1: Hospital Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Health and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 33: At the hospital

Fun for Flyers · Week 33: At the hospital | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Hospital words Lesson 2 · In the waiting room Lesson 3 · At the doctor's

You Need

  • Before the lesson, make a word search (10 × 10 letters) with these words hidden: cold, ambulance, dentist, medicine, chemist, nurses, temperature, stomachache.
  • The clues below.

Read the sentences and find the answers in the word search: Example: When you've got this, you cough and your nose goes red. a cold · 1 This takes people who are very ill to hospital very quickly. · 2 You see this person when you've got a toothache. · 3 The doctor gives you this to help you get better. · 4 You can buy medicine, soap and shampoo in this shop. · 5 These people look after people who are ill in hospital. · 6 When you're ill, the nurse takes this to see if you're too hot. · 7 If you eat too many sweets, you might get this!

Answers: 1 an ambulance · 2 a dentist · 3 medicine · 4 a chemist('s) · 5 nurses · 6 your temperature · 7 a stomach-ache.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Hospital – Doctor & Nurse. Say the hospital words you know.
  3. Read and find (10 min): your child reads each clue, finds the word in the word search and writes it. Health words
  4. Who helps? (5 min): your child says who helps when: "If you break your arm, a doctor helps you. If you've got a toothache, a dentist helps you."
  5. Make a clue (6 min): your child writes clues for three more words: soap, shampoo, headache.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "An ambulance takes very ill people to hospital." … "You can buy shampoo at the chemist's." … "The nurse took my temperature."
