Week 33 · Lesson 2: In the Waiting Room

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Health and My Body. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 33: At the hospital

Fun for Flyers · Week 33: At the hospital | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Hospital words Lesson 2 · In the waiting room Lesson 3 · At the doctor's

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a hospital waiting room: a boy with a broken leg · a girl holding her arm · a boy with a red nose · a girl with her hand on her head · a boy holding his stomach · a little girl holding her face.
  • Write six names around the picture: Tom, Lucy, Ben, Kate, Sam, Mia. Read the script twice; your child draws lines.

Script: It's very busy in the waiting room today! Tom fell off his bike. He's got a broken leg. · Lucy was playing volleyball and she hurt her arm. · Ben has got a terrible cold. Look at his red nose! · Kate has got a headache. She was reading in the sun all afternoon. · Sam ate too much chocolate cake. He's got a stomach-ache! · And little Mia has got a toothache. She's waiting for the dentist.

Complete the sentences with names and words: 1 ____, the boy who fell off his bike, has got a ____ leg. · 2 ____, the girl who was playing volleyball, has hurt her ____. · 3 ____, the boy with the red nose, has got a ____. · 4 ____ has got a headache because she was reading in the ____. · 5 ____ ate too much cake and has got a ____.

Answers: Lines: Tom – broken leg · Lucy – arm · Ben – red nose · Kate – head · Sam – stomach · Mia – face (toothache). Sentences: 1 Tom, broken · 2 Lucy, arm · 3 Ben, cold · 4 Kate, sun · 5 Sam, stomach-ache.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child says what is wrong with each person: "This boy has got a broken leg."
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  4. Complete the sentences (8 min): your child writes a name and a word in each sentence.
  5. What should they do? (7 min): your child gives advice: "Ben should stay in bed and drink lots of water. Sam shouldn't eat so much cake!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Tom has got a broken leg." … "Sam ate too much cake." … "Ben should stay in bed and drink lots of water."
