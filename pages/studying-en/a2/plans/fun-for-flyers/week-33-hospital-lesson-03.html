You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Ella's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.
  • Two job cards for Ask and answer (below).

Read the conversation and choose the best answer: Example: Dr Brown: Good morning, Ella. What's the matter? Ella: H · 1 Dr Brown: When did you start to feel ill? · 2 Dr Brown: Tell me what you ate yesterday evening. · 3 Dr Brown: Did the nurse take your temperature? · 4 Dr Brown: Take this medicine twice a day until you feel better. · 5 Dr Brown: No, it doesn't. Now, don't eat four burgers again, OK?

Ella's answers: A Four burgers and a big bowl of ice cream. · B It really hurt when I woke up this morning. · C Yes. She said it was fine. · D OK. Does it taste nice? · E I won't. I'll eat more fruit! · F My brother has got a cold. · G I'm going to the cinema. · H I've got a terrible stomach-ache.

Ask and answer: Dr Brown – Job? doctor · When / work? every day · Name / hospital? City Hospital · Where / hospital? Park Road · New / old? new. · Mr Smith – Job? ambulance driver · When / work? at night · Name / hospital? River Hospital · Where / hospital? Hill Street · New / old? old.

Answers: 1 B · 2 A · 3 C · 4 D · 5 E. Extra: F, G.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read Dr Brown's questions together. Your child chooses Ella's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (4 min): you are Dr Brown, your child is Ella. Act the conversation.
  4. Watch (2 min): Doctor's Appointment. What's the matter with the man?
  5. Ask and answer (7 min): you have Dr Brown's card, your child has Mr Smith's. Ask and answer the five questions. Jobs
  6. Doctor game (4 min): your child is the doctor and asks you what's the matter. You choose a problem.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I've got a terrible stomach-ache." … "Take this medicine twice a day." … "Mr Smith is an ambulance driver. He works at night."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read clues and find hospital words: ambulance, medicine, chemist, temperature, nurse.
  • Listen and draw lines to people in a waiting room, and say what's wrong with them.
  • Choose the best answers in a talk with a doctor, and ask and answer about hospital jobs.
