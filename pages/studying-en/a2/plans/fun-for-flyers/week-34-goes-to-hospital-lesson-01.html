Week 34 · Lesson 1: Lily Breaks Her Arm

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Health and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 34: Oliver goes to hospital

Fun for Flyers · Week 34: Oliver goes to hospital | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Lily breaks her arm Lesson 2 · Lily's hospital diary Lesson 3 · A message from the teacher

You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two, three or four words.

Lily breaks her arm: Last July, Lily and her cousins were skating in the park. Lily was going very fast when she hit a stone. She fell and hurt her arm. It was broken! Her cousins phoned her mum, and an ambulance took Lily to hospital. Lily stayed in hospital for three days. The nurses were really friendly, and she watched lots of films on her laptop. But after two days, she felt bored. On the third morning, there was a surprise. Lily's favourite singer, Kim Star, came to visit the children in the hospital! She sang three songs and gave Lily a photo with her name on it. That afternoon, Lily's teacher came too. She brought Lily a big box of chocolates – and some maths homework!

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: Lily hurt her arm last July. · 1 Lily was skating with ____ in the park. · 2 Lily fell because she hit ____. · 3 Lily went to hospital in ____. · 4 The nurses at the hospital were ____. · 5 After two days, Lily felt ____. · 6 Kim Star gave Lily ____. · 7 Lily's teacher brought chocolates and some ____.

Answers: 1 her cousins · 2 a stone · 3 an ambulance · 4 (really) friendly · 5 bored · 6 a photo (with her name on it) · 7 maths homework.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Hospital – Doctor & Nurse. What happens in a hospital?
  3. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "What was the best surprise? What was the worst?"
  4. Write the words (9 min): your child completes the seven sentences with one to four words.
  5. Tell it as Lily (5 min): your child tells the story as Lily: "Last July I was skating in the park…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Lily fell and broke her arm." … "An ambulance took her to hospital." … "The teacher brought chocolates – and some maths homework!"
