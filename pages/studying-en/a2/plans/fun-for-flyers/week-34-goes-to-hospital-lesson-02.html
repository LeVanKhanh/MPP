Week 34 · Lesson 2: Lily's Hospital Diary

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Health and Daily Routines. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 34: Oliver goes to hospital

Fun for Flyers · Week 34: Oliver goes to hospital | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Lily breaks her arm Lesson 2 · Lily's hospital diary Lesson 3 · A message from the teacher

You Need

  • The diary below. Your child writes one word on each line.

Read and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Friday: I didn't have to go to school again today because I'm still in hospital. But I've made (1) ____ of new friends here, and the nurses make us laugh. At about half (2) ____ four, a nurse came into my room and turned (3) ____ the TV because my favourite programme was starting. Dinner was OK too. We (4) ____ fish, rice and carrots. Mum brought me a new board game, and I played it (5) ____ evening with the girl in the next bed.

Talk: Have you ever been in hospital? · What would you do if you had to stay in bed for a week? · What would you like your friends to bring you?

Answers: 1 lots · 2 past · 3 on · 4 had · 5 all.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (8 min): read the diary together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Talk (6 min): ask the three questions. Your child answers with reasons.
  4. Get-well card (7 min): your child makes a "Get better soon!" card for Lily with a short message.
  5. A day in bed (4 min): your child writes three things they would do: "I'd read comics, I'd…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I've made lots of new friends." … "The nurse turned on the TV." … "Get better soon, Lily!"
