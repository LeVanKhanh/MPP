Week 34 · Lesson 3: A Message From the Teacher

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: School and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 34: Oliver goes to hospital

Fun for Flyers · Week 34: Oliver goes to hospital | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Lily breaks her arm Lesson 2 · Lily's hospital diary Lesson 3 · A message from the teacher

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  • Twenty word cards for compound words: foot, ball, sun, glasses, bed, room, rain, bow, snow, man, back, pack, home, work, birth, day, sky, scraper, under, stand… (see the text).

Script: Teacher: Hello, Lily's mum! Can you give Lily her history book, please? Tell her that the class is learning about castles. She needs to read page 45. We're going to visit the City Museum – that's C-I-T-Y. Lily can use the purple answer book. And the trip to the museum is on Thursday. I hope she feels better soon!

Tell Lily: Example: Give Lily: her history book · 1 Class is learning about: ____ · 2 Read page number: ____ · 3 Name of museum: ____ Museum · 4 Colour of answer book: ____ · 5 Day of trip: ____

Put words together: foot + ball · sun + glasses · bed + room · rain + bow · snow + man · back + pack · home + work · birth + day · sky + scraper · tooth + brush

Answers: 1 castles · 2 45 · 3 City · 4 purple · 5 Thursday. Compound words: football, sunglasses, bedroom, rainbow, snowman, backpack, homework, birthday, skyscraper, toothbrush.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a word or a number on each line.
  3. Watch (3 min): Compound Word Workout! Put the words together with the song.
  4. Put words together (8 min): mix the cards. Your child makes ten new words from two cards each and writes them.
  5. Make your own (6 min): your child invents three new funny compound words and says what they mean: "A sockbag is a bag for socks!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The class is learning about castles." … "Foot and ball make football." … "A sockbag is a bag for socks!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a longer story about a stay in hospital and complete sentences with one to four words.
  • Write one missing word in each line of a hospital diary.
  • Listen and write details from a teacher's message, and make compound words: foot + ball, sun + glasses.
