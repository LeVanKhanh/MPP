Week 35 · Lesson 1: What's It Made Of?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Materials and At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 35: What's it made of?

Lesson 1 · What's it made of? Lesson 2 · Secret things Lesson 3 · From sand to glass

You Need

  • Seven boxes or papers with labels: wood · metal · glass · plastic · paper · wool · gold.
  • Twelve real things from home (a spoon, a pencil, a glass, a toy, a book, a sweater, a ring, a bottle, a key, a comb…).

What's this? Write the words. Example: Spoons, forks and cars are made of this. It's strong. metal · 1 This comes from trees. Tables and chairs are often made of it. · 2 This comes from sheep. Warm sweaters and scarves are made of it. · 3 Books and newspapers are made of this. · 4 This is yellow and very expensive. Some rings and crowns are made of it. · 5 You can see through it, so windows are made of it. · 6 Lots of toys and bottles are made of this. It can be any colour.

Answers: 1 wood · 2 wool · 3 paper · 4 gold · 5 glass · 6 plastic.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Everyday Materials. Point to something at home for each material.
  3. Read and write (7 min): your child reads each clue and writes the material. Materials
  4. Put them in the boxes (7 min): your child puts each real thing in the right box and says: "This key is made of metal."
  5. Two materials (4 min): your child finds things made of two materials: "This pencil is made of wood and metal."
  6. Find it in this room! (3 min): say "Glass!" Your child has ten seconds to touch something made of glass. Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This key is made of metal." … "My sweater is made of wool." … "This pencil is made of wood and metal."
