Week 35 · Lesson 2: Secret Things

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Materials and At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 35: What's it made of?

Fun for Flyers · Week 35: What's it made of? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What's it made of? Lesson 2 · Secret things Lesson 3 · From sand to glass

You Need

  • A bag. Each person puts one secret thing in it without the other seeing.
  • Before the lesson, draw a messy room: a wooden box · a bike · a glass bottle · a wool hat · a paper map on the wall · a plastic duck · a metal key · an umbrella · a lamp.

Secret things – ask questions: Is it made of wood? · Is it hard or soft? · Is it heavy? · Can you eat with it? · Is it bigger than a book?

Listen and colour and write: Colour the wool hat red and white. · Colour the plastic duck yellow. · Write TOYS on the wooden box. · Draw a big star on the paper map. · Colour the umbrella green.

Answers: Secret things: any. Colour and write as said.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Secret things (9 min): take turns. Ask questions about the secret thing: "Is it made of plastic? Is it heavy?" Guess in ten questions!
  3. Look first (3 min): your child names the things in the room and says what they're made of.
  4. Listen and colour and write (7 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours, writes or draws.
  5. My secret thing (6 min): your child writes a description of a secret thing at home with four clues. You guess: "It's made of glass. It's round…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it made of plastic? – No, it isn't." … "Colour the wool hat red and white." … "It's made of glass and it's round."
