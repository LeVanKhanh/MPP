Week 35 · Lesson 3: From Sand to Glass

You Need

  • The text below with three words next to each number.
  • A glass, a bottle and a little sand if you have it.

Glass: Glass isn't new. People were making glass bottles more than (then / than / when) four thousand years ago. We don't know who made glass (1 first / after / already). One story says that some people from a ship made a fire on a beach (2 to / for / with) cook their dinner. The fire was very hot, and some of the sand (3 change / changed / changing) into glass! Today, glass is made (4 from / for / off) sand in very hot factories. So next time you look through a window, think: "Wow! This glass (5 was / is / were) sand!"

Answers: 1 first · 2 to · 3 changed · 4 from · 5 was.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number and says why.
  3. Watch (2 min): How Does Sand Turn into Glass? Say the steps with the video.
  4. From… to… (6 min): your child says how things are made: "We make glass from sand. We make paper from wood. We make wool sweaters from…"
  5. Design a thing (8 min): your child designs a new thing (a bag, a toy, a house) and writes what each part is made of and why.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Glass is made from sand." … "Some sand changed into glass!" … "My bag is made of plastic because it's strong and light."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what things are made of – wood, metal, glass, plastic, paper, wool, gold – and read clues about materials.
  • Ask yes/no questions to guess a secret thing, and listen and colour and write.
  • Read a short text about glass and choose the right word from three.
