You Need

  • The game instructions below, and the six clues. Your child finds each missing word from its clue.

Clues: 1 not outside (6 letters) · 2 to go up, for example a tree or a mountain (5) · 3 the one after the second (5) · 4 the opposite of "push" (4) · 5 the opposite of "quickly" (6) · 6 the opposite of "forget" (8)

Hi Ben! Here's how you play Crown Castle. Go (1) ____ the castle and find the metal key under the plastic chair. (2) ____ the stairs to the (3) ____ floor. Use the key to open the glass door. Then (4) ____ the big wooden door – don't push it! Walk very (5) ____ past the sleeping lion. (6) ____ to take the silver torch, because it's dark. The gold crown is in a box behind the mirror!

Answers: 1 inside · 2 Climb · 3 third · 4 pull · 5 slowly · 6 Remember.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Clues (6 min): your child reads each clue and writes the word with the right number of letters.
  3. Read the instructions (6 min): your child puts the six words in the instructions and reads them aloud.
  4. Made of what? (5 min): your child finds all the materials in the instructions: "The key is made of metal. The chair is made of plastic…" Materials
  5. Draw the castle (8 min): your child draws the way through Crown Castle and the things the player finds.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Climb the stairs to the third floor." … "Pull the wooden door – don't push it!" … "The key is made of metal."
