Week 36 · Lesson 2: What Has Happened?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 36: Silver, plastic, glass, gold

Fun for Flyers · Week 36: Silver, plastic, glass, gold | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Crown Castle Lesson 2 · What has happened? Lesson 3 · Shall we play again?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a castle room twice. Picture 1: a closed glass door, the light off, a wooden box on the floor, a cat on the bed, no crown. Picture 2: the glass door open, the light on, the wooden box on the shelf, the cat under the bed, a player holding a gold crown, a broken window.

Say what has happened in picture 2. Complete the sentences. Example: The player has opened the glass door. · 1 Someone ____ ____ on the light. · 2 The player ____ ____ the wooden box on the shelf. · 3 The cat ____ ____ under the bed. · 4 The player ____ ____ the gold crown! · 5 Someone ____ ____ the window.

Answers: 1 has turned · 2 has put · 3 has run (gone) · 4 has found · 5 has broken. Six differences: door open · light on · box on the shelf · cat under the bed · player with the crown · broken window.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find six differences (6 min): your child compares the pictures: "In picture 1 the door is closed, but in picture 2 it's open."
  3. What has happened? (8 min): your child completes the five sentences with has + a past word.
  4. Watch (3 min): Present Perfect Song. Say the "has / have" sentences with the song.
  5. Has it happened? (5 min): change three things in the room while your child closes their eyes. Your child says: "You've opened the window! You've moved the chair!"
  6. My week (3 min): your child says three things they have done this week: "I've read a book. I've played football."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The player has opened the glass door." … "Someone has broken the window!" … "You've moved the chair!"
