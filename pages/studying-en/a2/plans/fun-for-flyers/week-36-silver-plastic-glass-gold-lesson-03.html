Week 36 · Lesson 3: Shall We Play Again?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Toys and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 36: Silver, plastic, glass, gold

Fun for Flyers · Week 36: Silver, plastic, glass, gold | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Crown Castle Lesson 2 · What has happened? Lesson 3 · Shall we play again?

You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Dan's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.
  • Paper for game instructions.

Find Dan's answers: 1 Kim: It's a good game, but I think it's for younger children. · 2 Kim: How long did it take you to find the crown? · 3 Kim: Which part of the game did you like most? · 4 Kim: Shall we play it one more time?

Dan's answers: A Well, I enjoyed it and I'm eleven! · B I don't know. I didn't look at my watch. · C When the lion woke up! · D If you want, or we could play my new game. · E It's made of wood. · F Yes, I've got two brothers.

What must the second player do? Write instructions for a game you know (a board game, a ball game or a game you make up). Use: First, … Then, … Don't … Remember to …

Answers: 1 A · 2 B · 3 C · 4 D. Extra: E, F. Instructions: any clear game instructions.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (7 min): read Kim's sentences together. Your child chooses Dan's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (3 min): you are Kim, your child is Dan.
  4. Write instructions (9 min): your child writes instructions for a game with five steps.
  5. Play it (6 min): play the game together from your child's instructions. Are they clear? What must you change?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I enjoyed it and I'm eleven!" … "Shall we play it one more time?" … "First, choose a card. Don't show it to anyone!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read game instructions, find missing words from clues, and say what things are made of.
  • Say what has happened with the present perfect: "The player has opened the door."
  • Choose the best answers in a talk about a game, and write instructions for a game.
