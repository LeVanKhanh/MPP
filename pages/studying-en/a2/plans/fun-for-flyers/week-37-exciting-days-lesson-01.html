Week 37 · Lesson 1: A Police Officer's Day

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 37: Exciting days!

Fun for Flyers · Week 37: Exciting days! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A police officer's day Lesson 2 · My job card Lesson 3 · An afternoon at the fire station

You Need

  • Word cards: police officer · uniform · traffic · car park · missing · helps · city centre · dangerous · exciting · information · jacket · station.
  • The text below. Your child writes one word on each line.

Make sentences about police officers with two or three of the word cards. Example: "A police officer wears a blue uniform."

Read the text and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Anna is a police officer. She works (1) ____ the police station in the city centre. Every morning she gets up at six o'clock (2) ____ puts on her uniform. When she arrives at work, she reads the information (3) ____ things that happened during the night. Every day is (4) ____. She might help people find a missing dog or show people the way to the museum. She also stops people who drive (5) ____ fast. It's an exciting job, but it can (6) ____ dangerous.

Answers: Sentences: any good ones. Text: 1 at · 2 and · 3 about · 4 different · 5 too · 6 be.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Make sentences (7 min): your child makes five sentences with the word cards. Jobs
  3. Read and write (8 min): read the text together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  4. Exciting or dangerous? (5 min): ask: "Which jobs are exciting? Which are dangerous? Would you like to be a police officer? Why?"
  5. Act it out (5 min): your child is a police officer and helps you find a missing pet: "What does it look like? Where did you see it last?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A police officer wears a blue uniform." … "She stops people who drive too fast." … "It's an exciting job, but it can be dangerous."
