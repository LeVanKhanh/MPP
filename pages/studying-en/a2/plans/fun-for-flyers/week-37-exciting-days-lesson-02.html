Week 37 · Lesson 2: My Job Card

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Daily Routines. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 37: Exciting days!

Fun for Flyers · Week 37: Exciting days! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A police officer's day Lesson 2 · My job card Lesson 3 · An afternoon at the fire station

You Need

  • Two job cards with these lines: Name · What job? · When / get up? · What / wear? · Where / work? · Age?

Choose a job and complete the boxes: Card 1 – a person you know (Mum, Dad, an aunt, a neighbour). Card 2 – a job you would like one day, with a name you invent.

Interview questions: What's your job? · When do you get up? · What do you wear at work? · Where do you work? · What do you like about your job? · What's difficult about it?

Answers: Any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Card 1 (8 min): your child interviews a family member (in person or by phone) with the questions and completes card 1.
  3. Card 2 (6 min): your child completes card 2 for a dream job and invents the details.
  4. Tell me (5 min): your child tells you about both people: "My aunt is a nurse. She gets up at five o'clock…"
  5. Watch (6 min): Let's Visit the Fire Station. What do fire fighters wear?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "My aunt is a nurse. She works in a hospital." … "She wears a uniform at work." … "One day, I'd like to be a pilot."
