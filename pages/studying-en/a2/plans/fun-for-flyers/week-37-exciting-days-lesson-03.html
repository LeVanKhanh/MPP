Week 37 · Lesson 3: An Afternoon at the Fire Station

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 37: Exciting days!

Fun for Flyers · Week 37: Exciting days! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A police officer's day Lesson 2 · My job card Lesson 3 · An afternoon at the fire station

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four pictures (not in order): a children trying on fire fighters' helmets and boots · b the class waiting outside the fire station with their teacher · c a boy sitting high up in the fire engine, waving · d the children getting wet next to the fire engine.
  • A word box: class · students · entrance · fire engine · helmets · boots · wet · laughing · water · teacher.

Script: Hi, I'm Jim! Yesterday my class visited the fire station. First, we waited outside the entrance with our teacher, Miss Night. We were really excited! Then we went inside and tried on the fire fighters' helmets and boots. The boots were enormous! After that, a fire fighter let me sit in the fire engine. It was so high! At the end, we played with the water from the fire engine. Everyone got really wet, and we couldn't stop laughing!

Complete the sentences under the pictures with words from the box: b This is Miss Night and our ____. We're waiting outside the ____. · a We're wearing the fire fighters' ____ and ____. · c This is me in the fire engine. I can see everything! · d We're getting really ____ because we're playing with the water from the ____! We can't stop ____!

Answers: Order: 1 b · 2 a · 3 c · 4 d. Words: b class, entrance · a helmets, boots · d wet, fire engine, laughing.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child says what is happening in each picture.
  3. Listen and order (7 min): read the script twice. Your child writes 1–4 next to the pictures.
  4. Complete the sentences (7 min): your child writes words from the box under the pictures.
  5. Our class trip (8 min): your child writes about a real or dream class visit (40 words): "Last month my class visited…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "We waited outside the entrance." … "The boots were enormous!" … "We couldn't stop laughing!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Make sentences about what police officers do, and write one missing word in each line of a text.
  • Complete a job card and interview someone about their work.
  • Listen and put pictures of a fire station visit in order, and complete sentences with words from a box.
