Week 38 · Lesson 1: Tom Meets Leo Diaz

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 38: Famous people

Fun for Flyers · Week 38: Famous people | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Tom meets Leo Diaz Lesson 2 · Leo's diary Lesson 3 · Kim goes to a concert

You Need

  • The story below, written large. Five words in it are wrong.
  • Five word cards: friends · believe · tickets · seats · scored.

Find five words which are wrong: Tom loves football, and he dreams of meeting his favourite player, Leo Diaz. Last Saturday, Tom was playing in the park with his bananas. Suddenly, a big white car stopped next to the gate. A tall man got out and asked, "Excuse me, where's the stadium?" "It's Leo Diaz!" Tom whispered, but his friends didn't sing him. Tom showed the man the way. "Thank you," said Leo. "Would you like two umbrellas for the match tonight?" That evening, Tom and his dad watched the match from the best beds in the stadium, and Leo cooked two goals!

Choose the best name: A famous footballer visits our town · Tom's new football · A trip to the beach with Dad

Write 1, 2, 3 or 4 words: 1 Tom dreams of meeting ____. · 2 The big white car stopped next to ____. · 3 Leo wanted to find ____. · 4 Tom watched the match with ____. · 5 Leo scored ____.

Answers: Wrong words: bananas → friends · sing → believe · umbrellas → tickets · beds → seats · cooked → scored. Best name: A famous footballer visits our town. Sentences: 1 Leo Diaz · 2 the gate · 3 the stadium · 4 his dad · 5 two goals.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find the wrong words (8 min): read the story together. Your child finds the five wrong words and puts a word card on each one.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  4. Write the words (7 min): your child completes the five sentences.
  5. Tell it as Tom (5 min): your child tells the story as Tom: "Last Saturday I was playing in the park…"
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "Which footballer or sports star would you like to meet?" Sport words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "His friends didn't believe him!" … "Would you like two tickets for the match?" … "Leo scored two goals!"
