Week 38 · Lesson 2: Leo's Diary

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 38: Famous people

Fun for Flyers · Week 38: Famous people | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Tom meets Leo Diaz Lesson 2 · Leo's diary Lesson 3 · Kim goes to a concert

You Need

  • Leo Diaz's diary below. Your child writes one word on each line.

Read Leo's diary. Write one word on each line. Saturday: Today I went to the new stadium in a town about 100 kilometres away. My driver took me (1) ____ in a big white car. When we arrived in the town, we couldn't find the stadium, (2) ____ I asked some children in the park. A boy called Tom showed us the way. He was very kind, so I (3) ____ him and his dad two tickets for the match. I scored two goals (4) ____ we won! After the match, I gave Tom my football shirt. Tomorrow I'm going to send him a letter (5) ____ thank him.

Answers: 1 there · 2 so · 3 gave · 4 and · 5 to.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): read the diary together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Two stories (5 min): your child compares the diary with Tom's story: "In Tom's story… In Leo's diary…"
  4. Leo's letter (8 min): your child writes Leo's thank-you letter to Tom: "Dear Tom, Thank you for…"
  5. Read it (3 min): your child reads the letter aloud like Leo.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "My driver took me there in a big white car." … "I scored two goals and we won!" … "Dear Tom, Thank you for showing us the way."
