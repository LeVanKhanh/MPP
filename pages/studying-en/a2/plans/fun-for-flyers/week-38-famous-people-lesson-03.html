Week 38 · Lesson 3: Kim Goes to a Concert

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 38: Famous people

Fun for Flyers · Week 38: Famous people | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Tom meets Leo Diaz Lesson 2 · Leo's diary Lesson 3 · Kim goes to a concert

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five pictures: 1 Kim and Dan looking at a concert poster · 2 Dan buying two tickets · 3 Kim and Dan sitting at the back, very far from the stage · 4 the singer pointing at Kim and asking her to come up · 5 Kim singing on the stage with the famous singer.

Look at the pictures. Tell the story. Start: "One day, Kim and her brother Dan saw a poster for a concert…"

Write your answers: 1 Which famous person would you like to meet? · 2 Why is this person famous? · 3 Why do you like this person so much? · 4 What does this person look like? · 5 Would you like to be famous one day? Why? Why not?

Answers: Story: any good story that matches the pictures. Answers: any.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the story with the five pictures. Use: one day, then, suddenly, in the end.
  3. Write your answers (9 min): your child writes an answer to each question in a full sentence. Jobs
  4. Interview (6 min): you are your child's favourite famous person. Your child asks you five questions like a journalist.
  5. Famous one day? (2 min): your child finishes: "If I was famous, I would…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "They were very far from the stage." … "Suddenly the singer pointed at Kim!" … "I'd like to meet a famous footballer because…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find the wrong words in a story about meeting a famous footballer, and complete sentences with one to four words.
  • Write one missing word in each line of a famous person's diary.
  • Tell a picture story about a concert, and write about a famous person you'd like to meet.
