Week 39 · Lesson 1: The Village Café

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: My Street and Farm and Countryside. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 39: In villages and towns

Fun for Flyers · Week 39: In villages and towns | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The village café Lesson 2 · Grandpa's shopping Lesson 3 · Word wheels

You Need

  • A word box: full (example) · popular · huge · sold · frightening · thanks · lovely · motorbike · tent · stamps. Four words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

You're walking through a village. What can you see? Talk about it: a café? a post office? a farm? fields? animals?

Read the story. Write a word from the box next to numbers 1–5. Mr Hill has a small café in the village. It's always full of people because he makes the best cakes in the village. His café is the most (1) ____ place in the village! Last Saturday, a man on a big red (2) ____ stopped outside the café. He was wearing a black helmet and a black jacket. Some children thought he looked (3) ____. The man came in and asked for a (4) ____ piece of chocolate cake and a cup of tea. When he took off his helmet, everyone saw it was Mr Hill's brother! "What a (5) ____ surprise!" said Mr Hill.

Answers: 1 popular · 2 motorbike · 3 frightening · 4 huge · 5 lovely. Extra: sold, thanks, tent, stamps.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Types of Stores for Kids. Which shops are in your town?
  3. In the village (5 min): your child says what they can see in a village: "There's a small café and a post office." Places
  4. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  5. Village or town? (4 min): ask: "Would you like to live in a village or a town? Why?"
  6. Best name (3 min): your child gives the story a name and says why it's a good one.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "His café is the most popular place in the village." … "Some children thought he looked frightening." … "What a lovely surprise!"
