Week 39 · Lesson 2: Grandpa's Shopping

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Shopping and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 39: In villages and towns

Fun for Flyers · Week 39: In villages and towns | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The village café Lesson 2 · Grandpa's shopping Lesson 3 · Word wheels

You Need

  • Six place cards: chemist's · bus station · shopping centre · sports shop · clothes shop · post office. Eight thing cards with letters: A soap · B a magazine · C a phone · D a football · E a scarf · F stamps · G sunglasses · H a toothbrush. Two are extra.

Script: Girl: What did you buy in town, Grandpa? · Grandpa: Well, first I went to the chemist's. I bought some soap. · Girl: And then? · Grandpa: At the bus station, I bought a magazine to read on the bus. · Girl: Did you go to the shopping centre? · Grandpa: Yes. I bought a new phone there – my old one is broken! · Girl: Did you buy anything for me? · Grandpa: Yes! I got you a football at the sports shop. · Girl: Thanks, Grandpa! · Grandpa: And I bought a warm scarf at the clothes shop. Oh, and stamps at the post office.

Answers: chemist's – A · bus station – B · shopping centre – C · sports shop – D · clothes shop – E · post office – F. Extra: G, H.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the places and the things.
  3. Listen and match (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter under each place.
  4. Where can you buy it? (6 min): say a thing: "medicine!" Your child says where: "At the chemist's." Then swap.
  5. My town map (8 min): your child draws a map of a small town with six shops and places and tells you where they are. Places
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "He bought some soap at the chemist's." … "Where can you buy stamps? – At the post office." … "The sports shop is next to the bank."
