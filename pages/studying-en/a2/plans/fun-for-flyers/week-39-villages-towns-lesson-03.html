Week 39 · Lesson 3: Word Wheels

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: My Street and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 39: In villages and towns

Fun for Flyers · Week 39: In villages and towns | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The village café Lesson 2 · Grandpa's shopping Lesson 3 · Word wheels

You Need

  • Three word wheels (letters in a circle, no spaces): nursedoctorambulance · teamgoalsplayersball · booksdictionaryquiet. Your child finds the words and says the place for each wheel.
  • A word box: a restaurant · a factory · a bicycle · a sledge · a concert · traffic · a torch · an entrance · a journey · a seat. Two are extra.

What's this? 1 People make things like computers or cars in this kind of place. · 2 This has got two wheels and you can ride it to school. · 3 You can sit on this and go down a snowy hill very fast. · 4 People go to this to hear music. · 5 These are all the cars, buses and lorries on a busy road. · 6 You can turn this on and see in dark places. · 7 You walk through this to go inside a building. · 8 This means travelling from one place to another.

Guess the describing word: Say a sentence in a special way – loudly, quietly, quickly, slowly, badly. The other person guesses the word.

Answers: Wheels: hospital (nurse, doctor, ambulance) · stadium (team, goals, players, ball) · library (books, dictionary, quiet). Clues: 1 a factory · 2 a bicycle · 3 a sledge · 4 a concert · 5 traffic · 6 a torch · 7 an entrance · 8 a journey. Extra: a restaurant, a seat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Word wheels (7 min): your child finds the words in each wheel and says which place they belong to.
  3. What's this? (9 min): your child reads each clue and writes the word from the box.
  4. Guess the describing word (6 min): say "I'm going to the shops" loudly, quietly, slowly… Your child guesses: "Loudly!" Then swap.
  5. Make a wheel (3 min): your child makes a word wheel for another place (a school, a farm) for you to solve.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "People make cars in a factory." … "You walk through an entrance." … "You said it loudly!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what you can see in a village, and read a story and choose words from a box.
  • Listen and match what someone bought to the shops and places in a town.
  • Find words in word wheels, read clues about places and things in town, and guess a describing word.
