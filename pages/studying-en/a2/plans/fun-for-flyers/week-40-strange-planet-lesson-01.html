You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • The sentences below. Answers can be one, two, three or four words.

Morning on the island: Hi, I'm Ella. I live in a big, noisy city, but last August I went to a small island for the first time. I went with my aunt and my two cousins, who are a year younger than me. We arrived late, so it was already dark, and we couldn't see anything. When I woke up the next morning, I could hear lots of wonderful sounds. Birds were singing in the trees, and the waves were coming to the beach. I opened the window very quietly because my cousins were still asleep. In front of me was the most beautiful view I've ever seen: white sand, blue sea and, far away, three dolphins jumping out of the water! "I wish I could see this every morning," I whispered.

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: Ella lives in a big, noisy city. · 1 Last August, Ella went to ____. · 2 Her cousins are ____ than Ella. · 3 When they arrived, it was already ____. · 4 In the morning, Ella could hear birds and ____. · 5 Ella opened the window quietly because her cousins ____. · 6 Far away, Ella saw ____ jumping out of the water.

Answers: 1 a small island · 2 (a year) younger · 3 dark · 4 the waves · 5 were (still) asleep · 6 three dolphins.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "Why couldn't they see anything when they arrived?"
  3. Write the words (9 min): your child completes the six sentences.
  4. Sounds around us (5 min): close your eyes for one minute and listen. Your child says every sound they heard: "I heard a car, a bird and the fridge!"
  5. I wish… (4 min): your child finishes three wishes: "I wish I could see… I wish I could hear… I wish I could visit…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I could hear lots of wonderful sounds." … "It was the most beautiful view I've ever seen!" … "I wish I could see this every morning."
