Week 40 · Lesson 2: A Strange Planet

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 40: What a strange planet!

Fun for Flyers · Week 40: What a strange planet! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Wonderful sounds Lesson 2 · A strange planet Lesson 3 · World quiz

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a strange planet with unusual animals: a fish with legs walking on the beach · an elephant with wings · a giraffe with a very short neck · a cat with six legs · a frog as big as a car · a bird swimming under the water.
  • The competition questions below.

What's unusual about the animals in this picture? Example: "The fish has got legs and it's walking on the beach!"

Answer the competition questions: 1 Which place would you like to visit? Why? · 2 What's the most beautiful sound you've ever heard? · 3 What's the best photo you've ever seen of an animal or a place? · 4 What's the strangest animal you know?

Answers: Unusual: fish with legs · elephant with wings · giraffe with a short neck · cat with six legs · frog as big as a car · bird swimming under the water. Competition: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. What's unusual? (7 min): your child describes each unusual animal: "The elephant has got wings and it's flying!" Animals
  3. Add an animal (5 min): your child draws one more strange animal and describes it.
  4. Competition questions (8 min): your child answers the four questions in full sentences with reasons.
  5. Watch (3 min): The 7 Continents and 5 Oceans. Which ocean is the biggest?
  6. Ask me (2 min): your child asks you the competition questions.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The elephant has got wings!" … "The most beautiful sound I've ever heard is the sea." … "I'd like to visit the jungle because…"
