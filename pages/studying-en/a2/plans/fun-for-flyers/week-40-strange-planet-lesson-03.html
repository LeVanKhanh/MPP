Week 40 · Lesson 3: World Quiz

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: The World Around Us and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 40: What a strange planet!

Fun for Flyers · Week 40: What a strange planet! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Wonderful sounds Lesson 2 · A strange planet Lesson 3 · World quiz

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures: 1 a sad dog in a garden on a rainy day · 2 the sun comes out; the dog sees children playing football in the park · 3 the dog playing football with the children, everyone happy.
  • The world quiz below. A world map if you have one.

Look at the three pictures. Write about this story. Write 20 or more words.

World quiz: 1 Which is the biggest ocean? · 2 Which is the highest mountain in the world? · 3 Which is the coldest place on Earth? · 4 Which is the biggest hot desert? · 5 Which is the tallest animal? · 6 Which is the biggest animal? · 7 Which is the longest river? · 8 Which is the biggest country?

Answers: Story: any story of 20+ words. Quiz: 1 the Pacific Ocean · 2 Mount Everest · 3 Antarctica · 4 the Sahara · 5 the giraffe · 6 the blue whale · 7 the Nile (some people say the Amazon!) · 8 Russia.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tell and write (10 min): your child tells the story first, then writes 20 words or more.
  3. World quiz (8 min): read the quiz questions. Your child answers – it's fine to guess! Check the answers together on the map.
  4. Make a quiz question (4 min): your child writes two more world questions for you.
  5. Read the story (3 min): your child reads their story aloud.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The dog was sad because it was raining." … "The highest mountain in the world is Everest." … "The biggest ocean is the Pacific."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a story about wonderful sounds and views, and complete sentences with one to four words.
  • Describe unusual animals in a picture, and answer questions about places, sounds and photos.
  • Write a story of 20 words or more from three pictures, and do a world quiz.
