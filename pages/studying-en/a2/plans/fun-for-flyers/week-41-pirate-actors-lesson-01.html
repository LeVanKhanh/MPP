Week 41 · Lesson 1: The Pirate Family

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a pirate ship with six actors: a boy in striped shorts cleaning the ship · an old man with a long white beard and a wooden leg, fishing · a girl with a parrot and no shoes · a woman cooking in a big pot · a tall man counting gold money · a woman with a spotted hat and a silver necklace at the wheel of the ship.
  • A word box: shorts (example) · wooden · parrot · costume · lazy · money · dangerous · moon · wheel.

Write the correct words and names: Example: In the film, Max is never lazy. He cleans the ship and the kitchen. He's wearing striped shorts. · 1 Max's grandfather is called Bill. He spends lots of time fishing. One of his legs is made of ____. · 2 Max's sister, Rosa, has got a noisy pet ____ and she doesn't wear any shoes in the film! · 3 Max's grandmother, Sally, does the cooking. Her pirate ____ is a long purple dress. · 4 Max's father, Jack, is busy all day. He counts his ____ and tells everyone what to do. · 5 Max's mother, Lucy, never takes off her spotted hat. She sails the ship at night by the light of the ____. Sometimes the sea is very ____!

Answers: 1 wood · 2 parrot · 3 costume · 4 money · 5 moon, dangerous. Extra: lazy, wheel (and the example).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Pirate Song for Kids. Do the pirate actions.
  3. Look and say (5 min): your child describes the six actors: "The man with the beard has got a wooden leg."
  4. Read and write (9 min): your child writes the correct words from the box and the names on the picture. Pirate words
  5. Dress up (5 min): your child makes a pirate costume from things at home and describes it: "I'm wearing a striped T-shirt and a hat."
  6. Act a scene (4 min): act a short pirate scene: one person is Grandpa Bill, one is Rosa.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "His leg is made of wood." … "She never takes off her spotted hat." … "I'm wearing a pirate costume!"
