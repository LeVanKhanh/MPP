Week 41 · Lesson 2: Under the Sea

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Sport and Animals. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 41: Meet the pirate actors

Fun for Flyers · Week 41: Meet the pirate actors | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The pirate family Lesson 2 · Under the sea Lesson 3 · Click! What were they doing?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw an underwater picture: two children swimming · a box of treasure on the sand · a shark · an octopus on a rock · some small fish · a boat on top of the water · a butterfly in the sky.
  • Coloured pencils. Read each instruction twice.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the octopus on the rock orange. · Colour the biggest fish yellow with blue stripes. · Write GOLD on the treasure box. · Colour the shark grey. · Draw a flag on the boat and colour it red.

Let's talk about swimming: When did you learn to swim? · Where can you go for a swim near your home? · Who taught you to swim? · Tell me about a beach or a swimming pool you know.

Answers: Colour and write as said. Talk: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names everything in the picture: "There's an octopus on a rock."
  3. Listen and colour and write (8 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours, writes or draws.
  4. Let's talk (7 min): ask the four swimming questions. Your child answers in full sentences, then asks you.
  5. Watch (4 min): Sports & Sports Verbs Chant. Swim with your arms!
  6. Draw more (3 min): your child adds two more sea animals to the picture and says where they are.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "There's an octopus on a rock." … "I learned to swim when I was six." … "My dad taught me to swim."
