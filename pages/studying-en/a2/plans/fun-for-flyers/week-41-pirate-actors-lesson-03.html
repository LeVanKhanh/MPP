Week 41 · Lesson 3: Click! What Were They Doing?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 41: Meet the pirate actors

Fun for Flyers · Week 41: Meet the pirate actors | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · The pirate family Lesson 2 · Under the sea Lesson 3 · Click! What were they doing?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three "photos": 1 a girl riding a horse on a beach · 2 a man eating an ice cream in front of a castle · 3 a boy hiding behind a big tree in a forest.
  • A word box: riding a horse · eating an ice cream · hiding behind a tree · climbing a rock · collecting shells · sleeping in the sun.

What was each person doing when the photographer took these photos? 1 The girl ____. · 2 The man ____. · 3 The boy ____.

Think hard! How much can you remember? (look at Lesson 1's pirate picture for one minute, then turn it over) 1 What was the boy wearing? · 2 What was the old man doing? · 3 What colour was the woman's hat? · 4 How many people were on the ship? · 5 Who had a parrot?

Answers: 1 was riding a horse · 2 was eating an ice cream · 3 was hiding behind a tree. Memory: striped shorts · fishing · (your colour) with spots · six · the girl (Rosa).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Click! (6 min): your child writes what each person was doing, with was + -ing.
  3. Family photos (6 min): look at real family photos together. Your child says: "When you took this photo, I was eating cake!"
  4. Watch (2 min): Past Continuous Song. Say the "was …ing" sentences.
  5. Think hard! (7 min): your child looks at the pirate picture for one minute, turns it over and answers the five questions.
  6. Your turn (4 min): your child makes three memory questions about a picture for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "When the photographer took the photo, the girl was riding a horse." … "I was eating cake!" … "The boy was wearing striped shorts."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read about actors in a pirate film and write the correct words and names.
  • Listen and colour and write in an underwater picture, and talk about swimming.
  • Say what people were doing when a photo was taken, and remember details from a picture.
