Week 42 · Lesson 1: Kim's Holiday Call

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 42: Holiday news

Fun for Flyers · Week 42: Holiday news | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Kim's holiday call Lesson 2 · At the lake camp Lesson 3 · A postcard from the pyramids

You Need

  • Eight small picture cards with tick boxes: swim in the sea · eat fish · visit the castle · buy presents · send postcards · ride a camel · see dolphins · climb the hill.
  • Kim's email below.

Script: Mum: Hi, Kim! How's your holiday? · Kim: It's great, Mum! I've already swum in the sea – the water is warm! · Mum: Have you eaten any fish? · Kim: Yes, I've eaten fish every day! And we've already visited the castle. · Mum: Have you bought any presents yet? · Kim: Not yet. We're going to the market tomorrow. And I haven't sent any postcards yet – sorry! · Mum: Have you seen any dolphins? · Kim: Yes! I saw three this morning! But we haven't climbed the hill yet. It's too hot!

Kim's email: Dear Grandma, I'm sorry I haven't written yet, but I'm fine and we're having a great time. I've already learned to sail a small boat! We're going to visit an island tomorrow. I'll send you some photos soon. Lots of love, Kim

Answers: Already done (tick): swim in the sea, eat fish, visit the castle, see dolphins. Not yet: buy presents, send postcards, climb the hill. Not in the call: ride a camel.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the eight picture cards.
  3. Listen and tick (8 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks the things Kim has already done.
  4. Already or yet? (6 min): your child says: "She has already swum in the sea. She hasn't bought any presents yet."
  5. Watch (3 min): Present Perfect Song. Say the "has / have" sentences.
  6. Read the email (5 min): read Kim's email together. Ask: "What has Kim already learned? What is she going to do tomorrow?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I've already swum in the sea!" … "I haven't sent any postcards yet." … "Have you bought any presents yet? – Not yet."
