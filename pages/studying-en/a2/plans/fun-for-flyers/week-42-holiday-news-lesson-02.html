Week 42 · Lesson 2: At the Lake Camp

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 42: Holiday news

Fun for Flyers · Week 42: Holiday news | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Kim's holiday call Lesson 2 · At the lake camp Lesson 3 · A postcard from the pyramids

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a camp next to a lake: a girl putting up a tent · a boy cooking sausages on a fire · a girl reading a map · a boy taking photos of a duck · a girl swimming in the lake · a boy carrying water.
  • Write six names around the picture: Matt, Sarah, Hugo, Katy, Betty, Alex. Then draw the picture again with five differences.

Script: This is our camp by the lake! Sarah is the girl who's putting up our tent. · Hugo is cooking sausages on the fire – they smell great! · Katy is reading the map. She knows the way to everywhere! · Matt has got a new camera. He's taking photos of a duck. · Betty is swimming in the lake. It's really cold! · And Alex is carrying water from the lake.

Answers: Sarah – tent · Hugo – sausages · Katy – map · Matt – camera · Betty – swimming · Alex – water. Differences: any five you drew.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child says what each person is doing.
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  4. Find five differences (7 min): your child compares the two pictures: "In this picture Hugo is cooking sausages, but in that one he's cooking fish."
  5. What have they done? (5 min): your child says what everyone has done by the evening: "Sarah has put up the tent. Hugo has cooked dinner."
  6. Camping or hotel? (3 min): ask: "Would you like to go camping by a lake? Why? Why not?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Sarah is the girl who's putting up the tent." … "Sarah has put up the tent." … "I'd like to go camping because…"
