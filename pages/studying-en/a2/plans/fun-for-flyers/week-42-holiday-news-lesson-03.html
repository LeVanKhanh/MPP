Week 42 · Lesson 3: A Postcard From the Pyramids

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and Days and Time. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 42: Holiday news

Fun for Flyers · Week 42: Holiday news | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Kim's holiday call Lesson 2 · At the lake camp Lesson 3 · A postcard from the pyramids

You Need

  • The postcard below. Your child writes one word on each line.
  • A real or homemade postcard for your child to write.

Read the postcard and write the missing words. Write one word on each line. Dear Nick, We've already been here for five days! There (1) ____ so many amazing things to see. We've already visited a museum (2) ____ had old gold toys and clothes in it. Yesterday we went to the theatre. The story was (3) ____ a famous queen who lived 4,000 years ago. This evening, our teacher is going to take (4) ____ to a restaurant next to the pyramids. I must go now (5) ____ I have to wash my hair! See you next week at school. Mary

Make sentences about the things you've done today: Today I've already… · I haven't… yet.

Answers: 1 are · 2 which (that) · 3 about · 4 us · 5 because.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (8 min): read the postcard together. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Things I've done today (6 min): your child makes five sentences: "Today I've already had breakfast. I haven't done my homework yet."
  4. Write a postcard (9 min): your child writes a postcard from a real or dream holiday with already and yet. Holiday words
  5. Read it (2 min): your child reads the postcard aloud.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "We've already been here for five days!" … "I haven't done my homework yet." … "We've already visited the museum."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what someone has already done and hasn't done yet on holiday.
  • Listen and draw lines to people at a camp, and find the differences between two pictures.
  • Write one missing word in each line of a postcard, and say what you've done today.
