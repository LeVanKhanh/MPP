Week 43 · Lesson 1: Have You Ever…?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 43: Have you ever ...?

You Need

  • Eight picture cards: a plane · a tent · chopsticks · a prize cup · a whale · a broken arm · a famous person · a horse.
  • A table for a family survey: eight questions and four names.

Write the words to complete the questions: 1 Have you ever been on a ____? · 2 Have you ever slept in a ____? · 3 Have you ever eaten with ____? · 4 Have you ever won a ____? · 5 Have you ever seen a ____? · 6 Have you ever broken your ____? · 7 Have you ever met a ____ person? · 8 Have you ever ridden a ____?

Make sentences about your group: Everyone has… · Most of us have… · About half of us have… · Only a few of us have… · No-one has…

Answers: 1 plane · 2 tent · 3 chopsticks · 4 prize · 5 whale · 6 arm · 7 famous · 8 horse. Group sentences: any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Have You Ever – Present Perfect Song. Answer each question with the song.
  3. Complete the questions (5 min): your child writes the missing word in each question, using the picture cards.
  4. Family survey (10 min): your child asks four people the eight questions and ticks the table: "Have you ever slept in a tent? – Yes, I have."
  5. Group sentences (6 min): your child writes sentences from the survey: "Everyone has eaten with chopsticks. No-one has met a famous person."
  6. Tell me more (2 min): ask one follow-up: "When did you sleep in a tent? Where?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Have you ever slept in a tent? – Yes, I have." … "No, I haven't, but I'd like to!" … "Everyone has eaten with chopsticks."
