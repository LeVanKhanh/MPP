Week 43 · Lesson 3: The Kite Competition

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Telling Stories and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 43: Have you ever ...?

Fun for Flyers · Week 43: Have you ever ...? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Have you ever…? Lesson 2 · Kites Lesson 3 · The kite competition

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures: 1 a girl and her grandpa looking at a poster for a kite competition · 2 the girl and her grandpa making a big kite that looks like a bird · 3 the girl's kite flying higher than all the others; she's holding a prize cup.

Look at the three pictures. Write about this story. Write 20 or more words.

Let's talk about things we've all done: What's the most exciting thing you've ever done? · What's the best present you've ever had? · What's the most delicious food you've ever eaten? · Where's the most beautiful place you've ever been?

Answers: Story: any story of 20+ words that matches the pictures. Talk: any answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tell the story (6 min): your child tells the story with the pictures.
  3. Write it (9 min): your child writes 20 words or more. Count the words together! Story words
  4. Let's talk (8 min): ask the four questions. Your child answers with the present perfect, then asks you.
  5. Read it (2 min): your child reads the story aloud.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "They saw a poster for a kite competition." … "Her kite flew higher than all the others!" … "The most exciting thing I've ever done is…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Ask and answer "Have you ever…?" questions, and say how many people in a group have done something.
  • Read a short text about kites and choose the right word from three.
  • Tell and write a picture story, and talk about things you've all done.
