Week 44 · Lesson 1: Two Party Invitations

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 44: What has just happened?

Fun for Flyers · Week 44: What has just happened? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Two party invitations Lesson 2 · It's your birthday! Lesson 3 · What has just happened?

You Need

  • The two invitations below and two empty tables: Date? · Time / start? · Place? · What / eat? · What / wear?

Invitation 1: Hi! Please come to my party on Saturday, 3rd May, at the Sunshine Café. It starts at 4 p.m. You can choose pancakes or sandwiches. Bring your swimsuit, because we're going to the swimming pool after lunch! Love, Lily

Invitation 2: Hi! This year my party is at my house – 12 River Street – on 21st June. Write it on your calendar! It starts at half past five. Wear your favourite costume! We'll have Dad's famous pizza, a quiz and a dancing competition. Ben

Answers: Lily: 3rd May · 4 p.m. · Sunshine Café · pancakes or sandwiches · a swimsuit. Ben: 21st June · half past five · his house, 12 River Street · pizza · a costume.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the invitations (6 min): read both invitations together. Ask: "Which party would you like to go to? Why?"
  3. Complete the boxes (8 min): your child completes the two tables with the details.
  4. Ask and answer (5 min): you ask about one party: "When does Ben's party start? What should you wear?" Your child answers.
  5. My invitation (6 min): your child writes an invitation to their own dream party with all five details. Party words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Lily's party starts at four p.m." … "You should wear your favourite costume to Ben's party." … "Please come to my party on Saturday!"
