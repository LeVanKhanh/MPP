You Need

  • The conversation below. Your child chooses Ben's answer, A–H, for each question. Two answers are extra.

Read the conversation and choose the best answer: Example: Kim: It's your birthday today, isn't it, Ben? Ben: F · 1 Kim: Are you going to have a party? · 2 Kim: Have you invited everyone in our class? · 3 Kim: What time does it start? · 4 Kim: Has your dad made a lot of food? · 5 Kim: Have a great time!

Ben's answers: A At half past five, I think. · B Yes, he's just made his famous pizza! · C Yes, I'm really excited. · D Happy birthday! · E Thank you. I will! · F That's right. I'm eleven. How did you know? · G Only about ten of them. · H She's here today.

The alphabet game: Find something in your house for each letter: a (armchair), b (bookcase), c…

Answers: 1 C · 2 G · 3 A · 4 B · 5 E. Extra: D, H. Alphabet game: any real things.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read Kim's questions together. Your child chooses Ben's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (3 min): you are Kim, your child is Ben.
  4. The alphabet game (10 min): your child walks around the house and writes something for as many letters as possible in ten minutes.
  5. Hard letters (4 min): talk about the hard letters: q, x, z. Can you find anything?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Are you going to have a party? – Yes, I'm really excited." … "He's just made his famous pizza!" … "A is for armchair, B is for bookcase…"
