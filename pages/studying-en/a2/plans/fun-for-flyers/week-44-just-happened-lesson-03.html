Week 44 · Lesson 3: What Has Just Happened?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 44: What has just happened?

Fun for Flyers · Week 44: What has just happened? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Two party invitations Lesson 2 · It's your birthday! Lesson 3 · What has just happened?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a messy party: a boy who has just dropped the cake · a girl who has just opened a present (a puppy!) · a man who has just fallen asleep in an armchair · a girl who has just spilled lemonade on the floor · a boy who has just popped a balloon · a woman who has just turned on the music.
  • Write six names around the picture: Clare, Tom, Helen, Fred, Alice, Mark. Read the script twice.

Script: What a party! Look at Tom – he's just dropped the birthday cake! · Clare has just opened her present. It's a puppy! · Fred was so tired. He's just fallen asleep in the armchair. · Alice has just put lemonade all over the floor. · Mark has just popped a balloon! · And Helen has just turned on the music. Let's dance!

Read Mark's diary and write the missing words: Saturday: I was eleven years old today. Twelve (1) ____ my friends came to my party. I got some great presents. My aunt gave (2) ____ a book about space, and my sister bought me a T-shirt. (3) ____ has got a rocket on the front! But the best present was from Grandma. She's just given me a puppy! I'm going (4) ____ call him Rocket. He's playing with a ball (5) ____ the moment. It's brilliant!

Answers: Lines: Tom – cake · Clare – puppy · Fred – armchair · Alice – lemonade · Mark – balloon · Helen – music. Diary: 1 of · 2 me · 3 It · 4 to · 5 at.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  3. What has just happened? (5 min): your child says what each person has just done: "Tom has just dropped the cake!"
  4. Read and write (8 min): your child writes one word on each line of Mark's diary.
  5. Find your partner (5 min): say half a sentence: "Tom has just…" Your child finishes it: "…dropped the cake!" Then make up new ones together.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Tom has just dropped the cake!" … "Clare has just opened her present." … "I'm going to call him Rocket."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read two party invitations and complete boxes with the details.
  • Choose the best answers in a talk about a party, and find something for each letter of the alphabet.
  • Say what has just happened at a party, and write one missing word in each line of a diary.
