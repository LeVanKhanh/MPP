Week 45 · Lesson 1: My Calendar Year

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Weather and Seasons. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 45: Talking about the time

Fun for Flyers · Week 45: Talking about the time | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · My calendar year Lesson 2 · Time facts Lesson 3 · The train timetable

You Need

  • A big calendar page with twelve boxes. Some months are written in: March, May, June, September, October, November. Six boxes are empty.
  • Coloured pencils.

Look at the boxes and do these things: 1 Write the names of the six missing months. · 2 How long is each month? Write the number of days. · 3 Write today's date in the right month. · 4 Write your birthday in the right month. · 5 Colour the summer months bright yellow and the spring months light green. · 6 Choose colours for the autumn and winter months. · 7 Draw a star in your favourite month.

An old rhyme: Thirty days has September, April, June and November. All the rest have thirty-one, except February alone, which has twenty-eight days clear, and twenty-nine in each leap year.

Answers: Missing months: January, February, April, July, August, December. Days: 31 – January, March, May, July, August, October, December · 30 – April, June, September, November · 28 or 29 – February.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Months of the Year Song. Stand up for your birthday month.
  3. Missing months (5 min): your child writes the six missing months in order. Time words
  4. Days in each month (6 min): read the old rhyme together. Your child writes the number of days in each box.
  5. Colour and write (7 min): your child does tasks 3–7 on the calendar.
  6. Ask me (3 min): your child asks: "When's your birthday? Which is your favourite month? Why?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "There are thirty days in September." … "My birthday is on the 12th of July." … "My favourite month is December because…"
