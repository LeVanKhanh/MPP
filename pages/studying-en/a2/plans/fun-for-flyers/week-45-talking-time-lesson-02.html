Week 45 · Lesson 2: Time Facts

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Days and Time and The World Around Us. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 45: Talking about the time

Fun for Flyers · Week 45: Talking about the time | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · My calendar year Lesson 2 · Time facts Lesson 3 · The train timetable

You Need

  • Five questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice; your child ticks a box.
  • The text below with three words next to each number.

Script: Grandma: How was your class trip, Sam? Where did you go – the mountains? · Sam: No, Grandma, we went to an island! · Grandma: And when does your school holiday begin? · Sam: On the 18th of July. · Grandma: What did you do on the island? · Sam: We went sailing! And I brought home a big shell for you. · Grandma: Thank you! I'm going to watch your holiday film tonight at eight o'clock. · Sam: Great! I'm going to wear my new striped T-shirt to come and see it!

Listen and tick: 1 Where did Sam's class go? A the mountains B the beach C an island · 2 When does the school holiday begin? A 8th July B 18th July C 28th July · 3 What did Sam do? A sailing B climbing C skiing · 4 What did Sam bring home? A a stone B a shell C a fish · 5 When will Grandma watch the film? A 6 o'clock B 7 o'clock C 8 o'clock

Time: The Earth takes (takes / taking / took) 365 and a quarter days to go round the sun. So every four years, (1 there / they / their) are 366 days in the year. We add one more day (2 at / to / on) the end of February. Most months have 31 days, but four of (3 they / their / them) have only 30. February is the shortest month of (4 all / every / each). In some countries, people change the time (5 two / twice / second) a year, so they can have more light in the evening.

Answers: Tick: 1 C · 2 B · 3 A · 4 B · 5 C. Text: 1 there · 2 to · 3 them · 4 all · 5 twice.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the pictures for each question.
  3. Listen and tick (7 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  4. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child chooses the right word for each number.
  5. Did you know? (6 min): your child tells you two new facts from the text: "There are 366 days in a leap year!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Our school holiday begins on the 18th of July." … "Every four years there are 366 days." … "February is the shortest month."
