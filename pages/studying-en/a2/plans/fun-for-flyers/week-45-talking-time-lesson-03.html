You Need

  • The timetable below, written large.

Train timetable: Hilltown 08:30 · 09:15 · 11:10 · 12:30 | Greenford 09:05 · 10:15 · 11:45 · 13:30 | Lakeside 09:55 · 10:55 · 12:20 · 14:20 | Riverton 10:10 · 11:25 · 12:45 · 14:45 | Seaview 11:15 · 12:25 · 13:40 · 14:55

Timetable quiz: 1 How many stations does the train stop at after Hilltown and before Riverton? · 2 How long is the journey from Greenford to Riverton on the 11:45 train? · 3 It's five past two. How long must I wait at Lakeside for the next train? · 4 I must arrive in Seaview by twenty past one. Which train must I catch from Lakeside? · 5 I want to go from Hilltown to Seaview on the fastest train. What time does it leave? · 6 I leave Hilltown at quarter past nine, stay at Riverton for an hour, then catch the next train. What time do I arrive in Seaview?

Answers: 1 two (Greenford and Lakeside) · 2 one hour (11:45 → 12:45) · 3 fifteen minutes (14:20) · 4 the 10:55 (it arrives at 12:25; the 12:20 arrives at 13:40 – too late) · 5 the 12:30 (it takes 2 hours 25 minutes; the others take longer) · 6 the 09:15 arrives at Riverton at 11:25, wait an hour → 12:25, next train 12:45 → Seaview at 13:40.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the timetable (5 min): your child reads the times aloud: "The first train leaves Hilltown at half past eight."
  3. Timetable quiz (12 min): your child answers the six questions. Use a clock to help with the hard ones.
  4. Make a question (5 min): your child writes one more timetable question for you.
  5. Our journey (3 min): plan a real journey with a real timetable (bus or train) if you can.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The first train leaves Hilltown at half past eight." … "The journey takes one hour." … "I must catch the 10:55 train."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Write the months, say how many days each one has, and colour the seasons.
  • Listen and tick details about a school holiday, and read a text about time and choose the right words.
  • Read a train timetable and answer questions about times and journeys.
