Week 46 · Lesson 1: Who's Talking?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: At Home and Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 46: We're all at home today

Fun for Flyers · Week 46: We're all at home today | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who's talking? Lesson 2 · Where has Dad put my things? Lesson 3 · Bored at home

You Need

  • Six questions and seven answers on separate papers. One answer is extra.

Match the questions and the answers: 1 Are you going swimming today? · 2 Is Oliver at home? · 3 Did you repair your bike? · 4 Were there lots of people at the concert? · 5 Can your dad give me a lift into town later? · 6 Have you seen my pet tortoise? — a He isn't now, but he will be later. · b No, I didn't have time. · c Yes, there were thousands! · d We are, but not until this afternoon. · e He can't today. Sorry! · f Yes, I have. It's in your garden! · g It's really cool.

Answers: 1 d · 2 a · 3 b · 4 c · 5 e · 6 f. Extra: g.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and match (8 min): your child matches each question to an answer and reads the pairs aloud. One answer is extra.
  3. Why not g? (3 min): your child says why g doesn't answer any question.
  4. Act it out (5 min): you and your child are neighbours talking from your windows. Act the six mini conversations.
  5. New answers (6 min): your child gives a different answer to each question: "Are you going swimming today? – No, it's too cold!"
  6. Ask me (3 min): your child asks you three questions about your weekend.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Have you seen my pet tortoise? – Yes, I have. It's in your garden!" … "Can your dad give me a lift? – He can't today. Sorry!" … "No, I didn't have time."
