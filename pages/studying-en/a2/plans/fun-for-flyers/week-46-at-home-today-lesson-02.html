Week 46 · Lesson 2: Where Has Dad Put My Things?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: At Home and Toys. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 46: We're all at home today

Fun for Flyers · Week 46: We're all at home today | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Who's talking? Lesson 2 · Where has Dad put my things? Lesson 3 · Bored at home

You Need

  • Six thing cards: a torch · a diary · sunglasses · a comb · keys · a phone. Eight place cards with letters: A in the bin · B in a box · C in a bag · D in the backpack · E on the shelf · F in the cupboard · G under the bed · H on the desk. Two are extra.

Script: Boy: Dad, where have you put all my things? I can't find anything! · Dad: Well, I tidied your room. Your torch is in your backpack, ready for the camping trip. · Boy: And my diary? · Dad: It's on the shelf, next to your books. · Boy: Where are my sunglasses? · Dad: I put them in your sports bag. · Boy: And my comb? · Dad: It's on your desk. · Boy: Have you seen my keys? · Dad: Yes – they were under the bed! Now they're in the box by the door. · Boy: And my phone? · Dad: It's in the cupboard. You can have it after your homework!

Answers: torch – D · diary – E · sunglasses – C · comb – H · keys – B · phone – F. Extra: A, G.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child names the things and the places.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes a letter under each thing.
  4. Hide and say (7 min): your child hides five things in a room and writes where. You look for them and say: "You've put the comb under the cushion!"
  5. Tidy room (4 min): your child says where they keep five things in their own room. Home words
  6. Talk (3 min): ask: "Who tidies your room? Is it easy to find your things?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where have you put my torch?" … "It's in your backpack." … "You've put the comb under the cushion!"
