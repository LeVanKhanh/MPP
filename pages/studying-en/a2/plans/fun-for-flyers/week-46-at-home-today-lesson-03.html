You Need

  • The story below, written large.
  • Six sentences for the too / to / two game (below).

Bored at home: Last Sunday, our family wanted to go to the beach, but it was very cold and windy, so we stayed at home. After lunch, my little brother Sam was really bored. He came into my bedroom and said, "Ella, play chess with me!" "Sorry, Sam," I said. "I have to finish my history project." Sam went to the kitchen. "Will you play with me, Mum?" "Not now, Sam. I'm cooking dinner." Dad was busy too. He was repairing the light in the hall. "No-one wants to play with me," thought Sam. Later, when I finished my homework, I couldn't find Sam anywhere. Then I heard him laughing in the living room. He was inside a huge castle made of chairs, blankets and cushions! "Come in!" he said. "There's room for two!"

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: The family didn't go to the beach because it was very cold and windy. · 1 Sam wanted Ella to play ____ with him. · 2 Ella had to finish her ____. · 3 Mum couldn't play because she was ____. · 4 Dad was repairing ____. · 5 Ella heard Sam laughing in ____. · 6 Sam's castle was made of chairs, ____.

too, to or two? 1 Can I come ____? · 2 I'm going ____ the park. · 3 I've got ____ brothers. · 4 It's ____ cold to swim! · 5 She gave her ____ cakes. · 6 I want ____ play chess.

Answers: 1 chess · 2 history project · 3 cooking dinner · 4 the light in the hall · 5 the living room · 6 blankets and cushions. too/to/two: 1 too · 2 to · 3 two · 4 too · 5 two · 6 to.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "Why was Sam bored? Was his idea good?"
  3. Write the words (8 min): your child completes the six sentences with one to four words.
  4. too, to or two? (6 min): your child writes too, to or two in each sentence. Same sound words
  5. Rainy-day ideas (4 min): your child writes three ideas for a bored day at home.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "No-one wants to play with me!" … "He was inside a huge castle made of chairs!" … "Can I come too?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Match questions and answers in short conversations between neighbours.
  • Listen and write where someone has put things in a room.
  • Read a story about a bored day at home, complete sentences with one to four words, and use too, to and two.
