Week 47 · Lesson 1: What Might Lily Be?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 47: I will or perhaps I won't

Fun for Flyers · Week 47: I will or perhaps I won't | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What might Lily be? Lesson 2 · In ten years Lesson 3 · Yes, I will! No, I won't!

You Need

  • The four sentences below. Read the script twice; your child writes two words in each sentence.

Script: Dad: What do you want to be one day, Lily? · Lily: Well, I won't be a pilot. I think that's a boring job – you sit down all day! · Dad: What about a nurse? · Lily: I might be a nurse, but that's a really difficult job. · Dad: An engineer? · Lily: Perhaps! I may be an engineer, because I think that's an exciting job. You can design bridges! · Dad: Or a pop star? · Lily: Yes! I'll be a pop star. That's a really fun job!

Listen and write: Example: Lily won't be a pilot because she thinks that's a boring job. · 1 Lily might be a ____, but that's a ____ job. · 2 Lily may be an ____ because she thinks that's an ____ job. · 3 Lily says she will be a ____. That's a really ____ job!

What are you thinking? I'll be a… · I might be a… · I won't be a…

Answers: 1 nurse, difficult · 2 engineer, exciting · 3 pop star, fun.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Future Simple Song – Will and Won't. Say "will" and "won't" with the song.
  3. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes two words in each sentence.
  4. Will, might or won't? (6 min): your child says three sentences about jobs: "I'll be a…, I might be a…, I won't be a…" and why.
  5. Draw my future (6 min): your child draws themself in a thinking bubble doing a job one day and writes one sentence.
  6. Ask me (3 min): your child asks you: "When you were young, what did you want to be?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I won't be a pilot because it's boring." … "I might be a nurse." … "I'll be a pop star!"
