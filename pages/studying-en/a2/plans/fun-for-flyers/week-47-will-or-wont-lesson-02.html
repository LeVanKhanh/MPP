Week 47 · Lesson 2: In Ten Years

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Travel. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 47: I will or perhaps I won't

Fun for Flyers · Week 47: I will or perhaps I won't | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What might Lily be? Lesson 2 · In ten years Lesson 3 · Yes, I will! No, I won't!

You Need

  • The "In ten years" list below with tick boxes.
  • Six people cards (Grace, George, Alex, Michael, Sarah, Robert) and eight present cards with letters: A spoons · B a backpack · C some honey · D a computer mouse · E gloves · F a watch · G a bowl of soup · H golf balls. Two are extra.

Choose your answers. Tick the boxes. In ten years… 1 I'll be: at school / at university / at work · 2 I'll live: in this city / in another country / on another planet · 3 I won't have: any homework / any money / any problems · 4 I might have: a huge castle / a spaceship / a racing car / a gold violin · 5 I might be: a designer / a doctor / an engineer / a farmer / a pop star / a sports player

Script: Mrs Kind: I've got a present for each of my friends! For Grace, a backpack – she loves walking. · George is always cold, so I'll give him some warm gloves. · Alex can't get up in the morning, so she'll get a watch. · Michael loves sweet things. I'll give him some honey. · Sarah's computer is new, so I'll give her a mouse. · And Robert plays golf every Sunday. Golf balls for him!

Answers: Ten years: any answers. Presents: Grace – B · George – E · Alex – F · Michael – C · Sarah – D · Robert – H. Extra: A, G.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. In ten years (7 min): your child ticks their answers and reads them: "In ten years I'll be at university. I might have a racing car!"
  3. Compare (4 min): you tick the list for your child too. Compare: what's the same? What's different?
  4. Listen and write (8 min): read Mrs Kind's script twice. Your child writes a letter under each person.
  5. My presents (6 min): your child says what they will give three family members and why: "I'll give Grandpa a new watch because his is broken."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "In ten years I'll be at university." … "I might have a spaceship!" … "I'll give Grandpa a watch because…"
