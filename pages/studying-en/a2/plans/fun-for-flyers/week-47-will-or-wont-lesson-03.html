Week 47 · Lesson 3: Yes, I Will! No, I Won't!

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: The World Around Us and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 47: I will or perhaps I won't

Fun for Flyers · Week 47: I will or perhaps I won't | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · What might Lily be? Lesson 2 · In ten years Lesson 3 · Yes, I will! No, I won't!

You Need

  • The questions below. Your child answers with Yes, I will · I might · I may · No, I won't.
  • Paper for "What will happen?".

Answer me! 1 Will you travel to other countries in the future? · 2 Do you think you will go to the moon one day? · 3 Do you think you will have a conversation with an alien? · 4 Will people live on other planets in the future? · 5 Will you have a pet one day? · 6 Will you learn another language?

What will happen? Write three things you think will happen in the world in fifty years: cars, schools, phones, holidays…

Answers: Any answers with reasons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Answer me! (7 min): ask the six questions. Your child answers and says why: "I might go to the moon, because…"
  3. Ask me (4 min): your child asks you the same questions.
  4. What will happen? (9 min): your child writes three predictions: "In fifty years, cars will fly. Schools will…"
  5. Our time box (5 min): your child writes one sentence to read again in five years: "In five years, I think I'll…" Keep it somewhere safe!
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Yes, I will!" … "I might go to the moon one day." … "In fifty years, cars will fly!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and write what someone might do one day, and say what you will, might or won't be.
  • Choose answers about your life in ten years, and listen and match presents to people.
  • Answer questions about the future with "Yes, I will", "I might" or "No, I won't".
