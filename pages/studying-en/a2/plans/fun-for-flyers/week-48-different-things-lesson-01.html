Week 48 · Lesson 1: Max and His Animal Films

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Family and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 48: Doing different things

Fun for Flyers · Week 48: Doing different things | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Max and his animal films Lesson 2 · Zoe's busy week Lesson 3 · A famous visitor

You Need

  • Word cards: wife · husband · king · queen · manager · grown-up · kid · son · daughter · aunt · uncle · actor. Three columns: man · woman · man or woman.
  • A word box: called (example) · interested · future · touch · waterfall · swim · important · movie · time.

Read about Max. Choose a word from the box. Hi! Many people think I'm called Mr Wild, but that's only the name I use on TV. My real name is Max Short. I'm 30 and I make films about animals. I've always been (1) ____ in wild animals. My next film is about the ocean. I sometimes have to (2) ____ with sharks or climb next to a huge (3) ____ to film birds. My work is usually safe, but I never (4) ____ the animals – that's very (5) ____! When I'm not working, I like spending (6) ____ at home with my wife and our two kids.

Choose the best name: Max and his animal films · Max's new car · A day at the zoo

Answers: Sort: man – husband, king, son, uncle · woman – wife, queen, daughter, aunt · man or woman – manager, grown-up, kid, actor. Text: 1 interested · 2 swim · 3 waterfall · 4 touch · 5 important · 6 time. Extra: future, movie. Best name: Max and his animal films.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Sort the words (5 min): your child puts each word card in a column. Family words
  3. Read and choose (9 min): read the text together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  4. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  5. Interview Max (6 min): you are Max. Your child asks you five questions about your work.
  6. Would you like it? (3 min): ask: "Would you like Max's job? Why? Why not?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Max has always been interested in wild animals." … "He never touches the animals." … "I'd like his job because I love sharks!"
