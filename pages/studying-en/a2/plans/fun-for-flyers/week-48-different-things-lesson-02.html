Week 48 · Lesson 2: Zoe's Busy Week

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Days and Time and Sport. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 48: Doing different things

Fun for Flyers · Week 48: Doing different things | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Max and his animal films Lesson 2 · Zoe's busy week Lesson 3 · A famous visitor

You Need

  • The message below and five speech bubbles for Zoe.

Read the message and complete Zoe's speech bubbles. Write 1, 2, 3 or 4 words. Hi Zoe! We can't film the boat trip on Monday next week, so you'll do the horse ride that day. The walk with the wild animals (the one you wanted to do on Monday) will be on Thursday now. Sorry! On Tuesday, you won't go sailing in the mountains – you'll have a climbing lesson. You'll spend Wednesday snowboarding, and on Friday you'll have an unusual train journey. Have a great week! George

Zoe's speech bubbles: 1 "Monday will be exciting. I'm going on a ____!" · 2 "It's the ____ on Tuesday." · 3 "On Wednesday, I'm going ____." · 4 "And I'm doing the ____ on Thursday now." · 5 "But I'm going to go on ____ on Friday."

Answers: 1 horse ride · 2 climbing lesson · 3 snowboarding · 4 walk with the wild animals · 5 an (unusual) train journey.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the message (6 min): read the message together. Ask: "What has changed? Why do you think it changed?"
  3. Speech bubbles (8 min): your child completes Zoe's five speech bubbles.
  4. Zoe's diary (5 min): your child writes Zoe's week as a list: Monday – horse ride…
  5. My dream week (6 min): your child plans their own exciting week: "On Monday I'm going to…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "On Monday I'm going on a horse ride!" … "I'm going snowboarding on Wednesday." … "In my dream week, I'm going to…"
