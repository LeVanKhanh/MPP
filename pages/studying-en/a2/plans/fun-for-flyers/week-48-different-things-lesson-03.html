Week 48 · Lesson 3: A Famous Visitor

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Family. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 48: Doing different things

Fun for Flyers · Week 48: Doing different things | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Max and his animal films Lesson 2 · Zoe's busy week Lesson 3 · A famous visitor

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes on each line.
  • Two people cards for Ask and answer (below).

Script: Boy: Mum, is Nick Star, the singer, in our town? · Mum: Yes! He's visiting his son, Michael. Michael lives at 23 Green Street. · Boy: Has Nick got any grandchildren? · Mum: Yes, he's got four grandsons. · Boy: When did he start singing? · Mum: When he was nine. · Boy: Does he play an instrument? · Mum: Yes, the guitar. He plays it really well. · Boy: What's his favourite song? · Mum: "My Blue Kite". He sang it at his first concert!

Nick Star visits our town: Example: Nick's son's name: Michael · 1 Son's address: 23 ____ Street · 2 Number of grandsons: ____ · 3 Age he started singing: ____ · 4 Instrument he plays best: ____ · 5 Song he likes most: My ____

Ask and answer: Jack – Job? pilot · Age? 35 · Lives? in a flat by the airport · Hobby? chess · Favourite food? noodles. · Lily – Job? designer · Age? 28 · Lives? in a village · Hobby? horse riding · Favourite food? pizza.

Answers: 1 Green · 2 four (4) · 3 nine (9) · 4 the guitar · 5 Blue Kite. Ask and answer: any full questions and answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes on each line.
  3. Ask and answer (7 min): you have Jack's card, your child has Lily's. Ask and answer five questions. Jobs
  4. Tell me about another person (6 min): your child chooses a real person they know and tells you five things about them.
  5. Write it (4 min): your child writes three sentences about that person.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Nick Star has got four grandsons." … "He started singing when he was nine." … "My uncle is a designer. He lives in a village."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Sort people words (wife, king, manager, grown-up) and read about an actor, choosing words from a box.
  • Read a message about changed plans and complete speech bubbles with one to four words.
  • Listen and write details about a famous visitor, and ask and answer about two people.
