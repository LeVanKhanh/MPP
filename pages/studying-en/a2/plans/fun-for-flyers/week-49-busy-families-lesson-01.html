Week 49 · Lesson 1: A Busy Kitchen

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Family and At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 49: Busy families

Fun for Flyers · Week 49: Busy families | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A busy kitchen Lesson 2 · Too or enough? Lesson 3 · Wake up, Sam!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a busy kitchen: a girl with a big brush tidying the floor · a woman cutting bread to make sandwiches · a boy writing his homework · two girls in warm clothes going out to make a snowman · a boy repairing a toy rocket · a girl with glue making a card · a man doing the washing up.
  • Write seven names around the picture: Vicky, Sally, David, Lucy, Nick, Jane, Jack. Read the script twice.

Script: It's Saturday and everyone in our family is busy! Vicky is the girl with the big brush. She's helping to tidy the kitchen. · My mum, Sally, is cutting some bread to make more sandwiches. · David is doing his homework at the table. · Lucy and Jane are wearing warm clothes. They're going outside to make a snowman. · Nick is trying to repair his rocket. · And Jack? He's washing the plates. What a good boy!

Find the second half of each sentence: 1 The girl with the big brush is helping · 2 The woman is cutting some bread · 3 The boy at the table is trying · 4 Two girls in warm clothes are going · 5 The boy with the rocket is trying — a to finish his homework. · b to repair it. · c to tidy the kitchen. · d to make more sandwiches. · e outside to make a snowman.

Answers: Vicky – brush · Sally – bread · David – homework · Lucy and Jane – warm clothes · Nick – rocket · Jack – washing plates. Halves: 1 c · 2 d · 3 a · 4 e · 5 b.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look first (3 min): your child says what everyone is doing.
  3. Listen and draw lines (7 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the person.
  4. Second halves (6 min): your child joins the two halves of each sentence.
  5. Our busy Saturday (6 min): your child says what everyone in your family does on a busy Saturday. Family
  6. Guess my four things (3 min): think of four things you did today. Your child asks yes/no questions to guess them: "Did you cook?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Vicky is helping to tidy the kitchen." … "Nick is trying to repair his rocket." … "On Saturday, my dad cooks lunch."
