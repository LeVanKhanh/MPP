Week 49 · Lesson 2: Too or Enough?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Opposites. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 49: Busy families

Fun for Flyers · Week 49: Busy families | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A busy kitchen Lesson 2 · Too or enough? Lesson 3 · Wake up, Sam!

You Need

  • A word snake: boringbigdifficultdirtythinshortnoisysmalltallold. Your child finds the describing words.
  • The five questions and half answers below.

Finish the answers with too or enough and a word from the snake: Example: Why don't you go outside, Vicky? – Because it's too cold and my coat isn't warm enough. · 1 Why didn't you finish your book, Lucy? – Because the story is ____. · 2 Why don't you wear your old trainers, Jack? – Because they're too small and they're ____. · 3 Why can't you get the book from the top shelf, Nick? – Because I'm not ____. · 4 Why can't you make the rocket stronger, David? – Because the paper is ____. · 5 Why aren't you doing your homework in the kitchen? – Because it's ____ and the table isn't big enough.

Answers: Snake: boring, big, difficult, dirty, thin, short, noisy, small, tall, old. Answers (other good ones are fine): 1 too boring · 2 too dirty (too old) · 3 tall enough · 4 too thin · 5 too noisy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (1 min): Too vs Enough. What's the difference?
  3. Word snake (4 min): your child finds the ten describing words in the snake. Opposites
  4. Too or enough? (9 min): your child finishes each answer with too or enough and a word.
  5. Real reasons (6 min): ask real questions: "Why don't you go to bed at seven?" Your child answers with too or enough: "Because I'm not tired enough!"
  6. Make sentences (5 min): your child makes three more sentences: "This tea is too hot to drink."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's too cold to go outside." … "I'm not tall enough to get the book." … "This tea is too hot to drink!"
