Week 49 · Lesson 3: Wake Up, Sam!

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 49: Busy families

Fun for Flyers · Week 49: Busy families | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A busy kitchen Lesson 2 · Too or enough? Lesson 3 · Wake up, Sam!

You Need

  • The conversation below. Read it twice; your child writes one missing word on each line.
  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures: 1 a boy sleeping, his mum at the door · 2 the boy looking out of the window: it's sunny · 3 the boy running to the beach with his family.

Listen and write the missing words: Mum: Wake up, Sam! It's time to (1) ____ to school! · Sam: But I'm too tired. My head (2) ____. I want to stay (3) ____ bed. · Mum: No, Sam. You must get (4) ____ now. It's late! · Sam: It isn't. It's too (5) ____. And listen – it's raining really hard. · Mum: No, it isn't! It's sunny and warm. And it's a holiday today – no school! We're going to the beach. You were having a (6) ____!

Look at the three pictures. Write about this story. Write 20 or more words.

Answers: 1 go · 2 hurts · 3 in · 4 up · 5 early · 6 dream. Story: any 20+ word story that matches the pictures.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the conversation twice. Your child writes one word on each line.
  3. Act it out (4 min): you are Mum, your child is Sam. Act it with lots of feeling!
  4. Write the story (9 min): your child writes the story with 20 words or more.
  5. Read it (4 min): your child reads the story aloud, then tells you about a funny dream they had.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Wake up, Sam! It's time to go to school!" … "I'm too tired!" … "You were having a dream!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and draw lines to people in a busy kitchen, and finish sentences about what they are doing.
  • Use too and enough: "It's too cold." "It isn't big enough."
  • Listen and write missing words in a conversation, and write a story of 20 words or more.
