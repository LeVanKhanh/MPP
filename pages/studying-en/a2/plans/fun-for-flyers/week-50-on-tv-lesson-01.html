Week 50 · Lesson 1: Anna Makes Models

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Jobs and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 50: On TV

Fun for Flyers · Week 50: On TV | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Anna makes models Lesson 2 · On the sofa with Kim Green Lesson 3 · Ella and the elephants

You Need

  • The form below. Read the script twice; your child writes on each line.
  • Four people cards for Ask and answer (below).

Script: Boy: Hi! What's your name? · Woman: I'm Anna Brook – B-R-O-O-K. · Boy: What's your job? · Anna: I make models for TV programmes and films. · Boy: What do you make them with? · Anna: Wood and plastic, and sometimes paper. · Boy: When do you start work? · Anna: At quarter past eight every morning. · Boy: Where do you work? · Anna: In a big room at the top of my house. · Boy: What are you making now? · Anna: A castle for a film about a king!

Listen and write: Example: Name: Anna Brook · 1 Job: makes ____ for TV · 2 Makes things with: wood and ____ · 3 Starts work at: ____ · 4 Works in: a big room at the top of her ____ · 5 What she's making now: a ____

Ask and answer: Tony – pilot · flies to Paris · gets up at 5 a.m. · Vicky – cook · works in a hotel · loves making cakes · Alex – mechanic · repairs cars · works in town · Kim – journalist · writes for a newspaper · talks to famous people

Answers: 1 models · 2 plastic · 3 quarter past eight (8.15) · 4 house · 5 castle.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes on each line.
  3. Ask and answer (7 min): your child chooses a person card. You ask: "What's his job? Where does he work?" Then swap. Jobs
  4. Make a model (7 min): your child makes a quick model from paper or card and describes it: "It's made of paper. It's a…"
  5. Talk (3 min): ask: "Would you like to make models for films? What would you make?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Anna makes models for TV." … "She makes them with wood and plastic." … "My model is made of paper."
