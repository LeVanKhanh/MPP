Week 50 · Lesson 2: On the Sofa With Kim Green

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 50: On TV

Fun for Flyers · Week 50: On TV | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

You Need

  • The interview below. Your child chooses Grace's answer, A–H. Two answers are extra.
  • A word box for the TV text: millions (example) · should · channel · turn · screen · singers · gate · film.

Kim Green is talking to Grace Star on TV. What does Grace say? 1 Kim: Hello Grace! How are you? · 2 Kim: Have you just arrived in our city? · 3 Kim: Why are you here? · 4 Kim: What's your new song about? · 5 Kim: How long will you stay? · 6 Kim: When will you make your next film? — A That's right. I got here at midday. · B A girl who sails round the world. · C I'm fine, thanks. Hello everyone! · D Only a week. · E To sing at the big concert on Saturday. · F Next spring, I hope. · G It's made of glass. · H Yes, I've got two sisters.

Read the text and choose words from the box: Every Saturday, millions of people (1) ____ on their TVs at 7:30 to watch "Sofa Time" on (2) ____ Five. If you enjoy hearing about famous people, you (3) ____ watch it too! Every week, Kim Green talks to (4) ____, actors and sports stars. You can see them on the big (5) ____ behind her sofa. Don't miss it!

Answers: Interview: 1 C · 2 A · 3 E · 4 B · 5 D · 6 F. Extra: G, H. Text: 1 turn · 2 Channel · 3 should · 4 singers · 5 screen. Extra: gate, film.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read Kim's questions together. Your child chooses Grace's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (4 min): you are Kim, your child is Grace. Act it like a real TV programme!
  4. Read the TV text (7 min): your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  5. Watch (6 min): Invention of Television. What were the first TVs like?
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I got here at midday." … "I'm here to sing at the big concert." … "You should watch it too!"
