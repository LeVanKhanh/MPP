Week 50 · Lesson 3: Ella and the Elephants

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Animals and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 50: On TV

Fun for Flyers · Week 50: On TV | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Anna makes models Lesson 2 · On the sofa with Kim Green Lesson 3 · Ella and the elephants

You Need

  • The email below.
  • Before the lesson, draw five pictures: 1 a TV team arriving at a zoo with cameras · 2 Ella, who looks after the elephants, showing them a baby elephant · 3 the baby elephant taking a camera with its nose · 4 everyone running after the baby elephant · 5 the baby elephant giving the camera back; the photo it took is of Ella laughing.

Read Sam's email to Kim Green and answer the questions: Hi Kim, I went to City Zoo today. A woman who works there is called Ella Smith. She isn't famous like other people on your programme, but she's very interesting and funny, and so are the elephants she looks after! But there's a problem. Ella can come to your TV programme, but the elephants can't! So you'll have to take your cameras to the zoo and film her there. Do you agree? Sam

Questions: 1 Where did Sam go today? · 2 Who is Ella Smith? · 3 What does Ella do? · 4 What's the problem? · 5 What does Sam want Kim to do?

Answers: 1 to City Zoo · 2 a woman who works at the zoo · 3 she looks after the elephants · 4 the elephants can't come to the TV programme · 5 take her cameras to the zoo and film Ella there. Story: any good story that matches the pictures.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and answer (7 min): read the email together. Your child answers the five questions.
  3. Tell the story (8 min): your child tells the five-picture story. Use: one day, then, suddenly, after that, in the end.
  4. Write the end (6 min): your child writes the end of the story in three sentences.
  5. My TV programme (4 min): your child invents a TV programme: "It's called… It's on Channel… Every week…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Ella looks after the elephants." … "Suddenly, the baby elephant took the camera!" … "My TV programme is called…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and write details about someone who makes models for TV, and ask and answer about people's work.
  • Choose the best answers in a TV interview, and choose words from a box for a text about a TV programme.
  • Read an email to a TV presenter, answer questions, and tell a picture story.
