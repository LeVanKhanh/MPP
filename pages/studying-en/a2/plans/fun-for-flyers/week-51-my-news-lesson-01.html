Week 51 · Lesson 1: Around Our School

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 51: Here's my news

Fun for Flyers · Week 51: Here's my news | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Around our school Lesson 2 · Two hotels Lesson 3 · Two emails

You Need

  • Seven place cards with letters (draw them): A the office · B the sports hall · C the library · D the computer room · E the playground · F the dining room · G the entrance.
  • Read the script twice. Your child writes the letters in order.

Script: Nick had a busy day at school today. First, he went into the school through the entrance. · Then he had to take a letter to the office. · At ten o'clock he had his sport lesson in the sports hall. · At lunchtime he ate in the dining room. · After lunch, he played football in the playground. · And at the end of the day, he found a book about planets in the library.

Answers: G (entrance) · A (office) · B (sports hall) · F (dining room) · E (playground) · C (library). Not used: D (computer room).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Name the places (4 min): your child writes the name under each place card. School words
  3. Listen and order (8 min): read the script twice. Your child writes the letters in the order Nick went.
  4. Nick's day (4 min): your child tells Nick's day: "First he went in through the entrance…"
  5. My school map (6 min): your child draws a simple map of their own school and labels five places.
  6. Favourite place (3 min): ask: "Where's your favourite place at school? Why?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "He took a letter to the office." … "At lunchtime he ate in the dining room." … "My favourite place is the library because…"
