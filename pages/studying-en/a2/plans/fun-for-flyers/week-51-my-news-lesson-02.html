Week 51 · Lesson 2: Two Hotels

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and At Home. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 51: Here's my news

Fun for Flyers · Week 51: Here's my news | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Around our school Lesson 2 · Two hotels Lesson 3 · Two emails

You Need

  • Two hotel cards for Ask and answer (below).
  • Paper and coloured pencils for a hotel advert.

Ask and answer about the hotels: Sea View Hotel – Noisy or quiet? quiet · How many rooms? 35 · Sport? sailing · Internet in the bedrooms? yes · What can you see from the balcony? the beach and the island. · City Star Hotel – Noisy or quiet? noisy · How many rooms? 420 · Sport? tennis · Internet in the bedrooms? no · What can you see from the balcony? a big factory.

Answers: Questions, for example: Is the hotel noisy or quiet? How many rooms has it got? What sport can you do there? Is there internet in the bedrooms? What can you see from the balcony?

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Make questions (5 min): your child makes full questions from the card words: "How many rooms has it got?"
  3. Ask and answer (7 min): you have one card, your child has the other. Ask and answer all five questions.
  4. Which hotel? (4 min): your child chooses a hotel for a holiday and says why: "I'd choose Sea View because it's quiet and you can go sailing."
  5. Hotel advert (9 min): your child designs an advert for a dream hotel: name, place, rooms, sports, view. Holiday words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many rooms has it got? – Thirty-five." … "What can you see from the balcony?" … "I'd choose Sea View because it's quiet."
