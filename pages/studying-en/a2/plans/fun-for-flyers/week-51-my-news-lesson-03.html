Week 51 · Lesson 3: Two Emails

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Travel and School. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 51: Here's my news

Fun for Flyers · Week 51: Here's my news | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Around our school Lesson 2 · Two hotels Lesson 3 · Two emails

You Need

  • The two email starts and the five pairs of sentences below. Your child decides who wrote each sentence.

Email 1: Dear Grandma, Here's a photo of the school where I'm studying English. Mia · Email 2: Dear Grandma, Here's a photo of the beach next to our camp. Leo

Who wrote each sentence – Mia or Leo? 1a We've been here since Monday, and I can see the sea from our tent. · 1b Everyone in my class is friendly, but I wish I was better at speaking English! · 2a I'm making new friends because we often do projects together. · 2b I met some children on the beach and we played volleyball all afternoon. · 3a Later today, we're going to sail a boat round the island. · 3b We usually have lunch in a café that's only ten minutes from the school. · 4a My listening has really improved because we watch lots of films in English. · 4b In the evening, we sit round a fire and sing songs. · 5a The teachers let us choose between sport and art at the end of the day. · 5b The grown-ups are always busy cooking, so we play all day!

Answers: Mia: 1b, 2a, 3b, 4a, 5a. Leo: 1a, 2b, 3a, 4b, 5b.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and decide (9 min): read each pair together. Your child writes Mia or Leo next to each sentence and says why.
  3. Listen to a day (5 min): tell your child about your day in five sentences. Your child asks you two questions about it.
  4. Write my news (9 min): your child writes an email with their own news: school, friends, weekend plans (50 words or more).
  5. Read it (2 min): your child reads the email aloud.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Mia wrote that, because she's at a school." … "We've been here since Monday." … "Here's my news!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name parts of a school (office, library, playground, dining room, entrance) and listen for where someone is.
  • Ask and answer questions about two hotels.
  • Decide who wrote sentences in two emails, and write your own news.
