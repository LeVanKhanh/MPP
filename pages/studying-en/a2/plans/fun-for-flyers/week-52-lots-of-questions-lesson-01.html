Week 52 · Lesson 1: Question Words

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson page: Questions. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 52: What a lot of questions!

Fun for Flyers · Week 52: What a lot of questions! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

You Need

  • A box: Who · How often · How long · How many · Which · Whose · What kind · Have you ever · How · What.
  • Three columns: Questions · Me · My partner.

Complete each question with words from the box: Example: Who is your best friend? · 1 ____ do you go online? · 2 ____ have you lived in your home? · 3 ____ people are there in your class? · 4 ____ been on a stage? · 5 ____ instrument do you like best – the piano, the guitar or the violin? · 6 ____ bag is the biggest in your family? · 7 ____ of food do you like best? · 8 ____ do you get to school? · 9 ____ is the date today?

Answers: 1 How often · 2 How long · 3 How many · 4 Have you ever · 5 Which · 6 Whose · 7 What kind · 8 How · 9 What.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Complete the questions (8 min): your child writes the right words from the box in each question. Question words
  3. Me (6 min): your child writes short answers about themself in the "Me" column.
  4. My partner (7 min): your child asks you all nine questions and writes your answers.
  5. Compare (4 min): your child says what is the same and what is different: "We both go online every day, but…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How often do you go online?" … "Have you ever been on a stage?" … "What kind of food do you like best?"
