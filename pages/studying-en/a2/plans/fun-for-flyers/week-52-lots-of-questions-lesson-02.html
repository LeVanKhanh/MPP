You Need

Ella and the quiz: Ella Bright loved learning. She had lots of books about animals, space and history, and she often found out about things online. One day, Ella's teacher said, "There's a quiz on Channel 4 for the cleverest children in the country. Would you like to be on it?" "Yes, please!" said Ella. The next week, a woman called Ms Gold came to the school. "Before the quiz, I have some questions for you," she said. "Are you ready?" Ella felt quite frightened, but she said yes. "What's the biggest planet?" "Jupiter!" "Where do penguins live?" "In very cold places!" "Well done! You're clever enough for our quiz! A taxi will bring you to the TV building next Friday. Now, just one more question – what's your address?" Ella was so excited that she couldn't remember!

Write words to complete the sentences: Example: Ella's surname was Bright. · 1 Ella had lots of books about animals, ____. · 2 The quiz was on ____. · 3 The woman who came to the school was called ____. · 4 Ella said the biggest planet was ____. · 5 A ____ will take Ella to the quiz. · 6 Ella couldn't remember her ____!

Answers: 1 space and history · 2 Channel 4 · 3 Ms Gold · 4 Jupiter · 5 taxi · 6 address.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read the story (7 min): read the story together. Ask: "Why couldn't Ella remember her address?"
  3. Write the words (8 min): your child completes the six sentences.
  4. Quick quiz (6 min): ask your child five quick quiz questions about animals, space and your country.
  5. Would you like to? (4 min): ask: "Would you like to be on a TV quiz? Which subject would you choose?"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "There's a quiz for the cleverest children in the country." … "You're clever enough for our quiz!" … "She was so excited that she couldn't remember!"
