Week 52 · Lesson 3: Quiz Night

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Questions and Parties and Going Out. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 52: What a lot of questions!

Fun for Flyers · Week 52: What a lot of questions! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Question words Lesson 2 · Ella and the quiz Lesson 3 · Quiz night

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a TV quiz: a woman asking questions · two children at desks with numbers 136 and 142 · a camera · a big screen with a planet on it · a cup for the winner.
  • Paper for a family quiz with five questions for each of three people.

Listen and colour: Colour the girl's sweater red. · Colour the planet on the screen orange with brown stripes. · The boy's number is 142. Colour it green. · Colour the cup for the winner yellow. · Draw a big star on the woman's book and colour it blue.

Our family quiz: Make five questions for each person: one about animals, one about food, one about places, one about sport and one about the family.

Answers: Colour as said. Quiz: any questions.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour (7 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or draws.
  3. Write the quiz (8 min): your child writes the family quiz questions and the answers.
  4. Quiz night (8 min): play the quiz with your family. Your child asks the questions and counts the points!
  5. The winner (2 min): your child says who won and gives them a paper cup.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Colour the planet orange with brown stripes." … "Question one: Which animal can fly and sleeps in the day?" … "The winner is…!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Complete questions with the right question words and ask a partner.
  • Read a story about a quiz and complete sentences with one to four words.
  • Listen and colour, and make and play a family quiz.
