Week 53 · Lesson 1: A Map of Rivertown

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 53: Finding your way

Fun for Flyers · Week 53: Finding your way | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A map of Rivertown Lesson 2 · How do I get there? Lesson 3 · Word review and shop actors

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a simple town map: two long streets (High Street and Station Road) and a river with a bridge. Draw empty boxes for eight places. Your child writes the places in the boxes as they read.

Read about Rivertown and find each place: 1 The station is at the end of Station Road. The hotel is across the street from the station. · 2 The library is next to the hotel. · 3 The bank is opposite the library. · 4 The supermarket is on the corner of High Street and Station Road. · 5 The chemist's is next to the supermarket, in High Street. · 6 The post office is across the street from the chemist's. · 7 There's a café between the post office and the bridge. · 8 A path goes from the café down to the river.

Answers: Any map that follows all eight sentences. Check together: the library is next to the hotel and opposite the bank; the café is between the post office and the bridge.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Giving Directions Song. Turn left and right with the song.
  3. Find the places (10 min): read each sentence. Your child writes the place in the right box on the map. Where is it?
  4. Check it (4 min): ask questions: "What's opposite the library? What's next to the supermarket?"
  5. Our street (6 min): your child describes your own street or town: "The café is on the corner. The school is opposite the park."
  6. Where am I? (3 min): your child says where they are on the map without saying the place: "I'm next to the hotel." You guess: "The library!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The bank is opposite the library." … "The supermarket is on the corner of High Street." … "I'm next to the hotel. – You're at the library!"
