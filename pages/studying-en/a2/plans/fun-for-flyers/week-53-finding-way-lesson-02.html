Week 53 · Lesson 2: How Do I Get There?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and My Street. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 53: Finding your way

Fun for Flyers · Week 53: Finding your way | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A map of Rivertown Lesson 2 · How do I get there? Lesson 3 · Word review and shop actors

You Need

  • The Rivertown map from Lesson 1 and a small toy to move.
  • Five questions with three small pictures each (draw them, A, B, C). Read the script twice.

Script: Boy: Mum, I need some toothpaste and shampoo. · Mum: OK, we can go to the chemist's. · Boy: Is it next to the bank? · Mum: No, the bank's opposite the library now. The chemist's is next to the supermarket. · Boy: How do we get there? · Mum: Turn left out of the station, go straight on, then turn right at the corner. · Boy: Can we have lunch at the café? · Mum: Yes, they have great fish and chips. · Boy: Oh no! I've lost my ticket for the train home! · Mum: It's OK, I've got it.

Listen and tick: 1 What does the boy need? A toothpaste and shampoo B a T-shirt C a book · 2 What is opposite the library? A the hotel B the bank C the café · 3 Where is the chemist's? A next to the bank B next to the supermarket C on the bridge · 4 What will they have for lunch? A soup B pasta C fish and chips · 5 What has the boy lost? A his ticket B his phone C his map

Answers: 1 A · 2 B · 3 B · 4 C · 5 A.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (7 min): read the script twice. Your child ticks A, B or C.
  3. Directions game (9 min): say directions on the map: "Start at the station. Turn left, go straight on, cross the bridge. Where are you?" Your child moves the toy. Then swap.
  4. Real directions (6 min): your child gives you directions from your home to a real place nearby (the school, a shop).
  5. Write them (3 min): your child writes the directions in three sentences.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Turn left out of the station." … "Go straight on and cross the bridge." … "The chemist's is on the corner."
