Week 53 · Lesson 3: Word Review and Shop Actors

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: My Street and Shopping. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 53: Finding your way

Fun for Flyers · Week 53: Finding your way | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · A map of Rivertown Lesson 2 · How do I get there? Lesson 3 · Word review and shop actors

You Need

  • A word box: months · the news · buildings · autumn · a path · minutes · a bridge · cartoons · a channel · a calendar · a city. One word is extra.
  • Some things from home for a pretend shop.

Choose the correct words: Example: There are twelve of these in every year. months · 1 This is information about what is happening in the world on TV or in a newspaper. · 2 Homes, schools, shops and skyscrapers are all kinds of these. · 3 In this season, it gets colder and leaves fall from trees. · 4 You can walk or ride your bike along this in a park or forest. · 5 There are sixty of these in an hour. · 6 You walk or drive over this to cross a river. · 7 These are funny drawings that you can find in comics or watch on TV. · 8 You change this to watch a different TV programme. · 9 You look at this to see what the date is.

Answers: 1 the news · 2 buildings · 3 autumn · 4 a path · 5 minutes · 6 a bridge · 7 cartoons · 8 a channel · 9 a calendar. Extra: a city.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Choose the words (9 min): your child reads each clue and writes a word from the box.
  3. Make a clue (3 min): your child writes a clue for the extra word.
  4. Be actors in a shop (10 min): set up a pretend shop. One person works in the shop, one is shopping and asks for things and for directions: "Excuse me, where's the bank?" Places
  5. Swap (3 min): swap roles and play again.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "There are sixty minutes in an hour." … "You walk over a bridge to cross a river." … "Excuse me, where's the bank? – It's opposite the library."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read about places on a town map and find them: opposite, across from, next to, on the corner of.
  • Give and follow directions: turn left, turn right, go straight on, cross the bridge.
  • Read clues and find words from Units 1–52, and act a scene in a shop or a town.
