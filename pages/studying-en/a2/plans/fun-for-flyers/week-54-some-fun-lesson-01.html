Week 54 · Lesson 1: Shall We…?

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 54: Let's have some fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 54: Let's have some fun! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Shall we…? Lesson 2 · I'm feeling bored Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • Seven starts and seven endings on separate papers. Your child joins them.

Join the starts and the endings to make suggestions: 1 Would you like · 2 Why don't we · 3 Let's · 4 What about · 5 Shall we · 6 How about · 7 We could — a going to the funfair this evening? · b to join the new chess club? · c ask Dad to give us a lift into town? · d make a cake for Grandma. · e playing my new board game? · f dress up in some old costumes? · g invent a new game!

Answers: Some starts go with more than one ending. A good answer: 1 b · 2 c · 3 d · 4 a · 5 f · 6 e · 7 g. (Would you like to… · Why don't we + verb · Let's + verb · What about / How about + -ing · Shall we + verb · We could + verb.)

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Join them (8 min): your child joins each start to an ending and reads the suggestion. Some can go together in more than one way! Party sentences
  3. The rules (4 min): your child sorts the starts: which ones need -ing? (What about, How about)
  4. Plan the weekend (8 min): make suggestions for the weekend together. Your child uses five different starts: "How about going to the park?" You answer: "Good idea!" or "No, it's too cold."
  5. Write them (5 min): your child writes three suggestions for a rainy day.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Shall we dress up in some old costumes?" … "How about playing my new board game?" … "Why don't we ask Dad to give us a lift?"
