Week 54 · Lesson 2: I'm Feeling Bored

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 54: Let's have some fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 54: Let's have some fun! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Shall we…? Lesson 2 · I'm feeling bored Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • The phone call below. Your child chooses Sam's answer, A–H, for each line. Two answers are extra.

Read the conversation and choose the best answer: Example: Lily: Hello! Is that you, Sam? Sam: B · 1 Lily: I'm feeling quite bored today. · 2 Lily: We could go to the sports centre. · 3 Lily: How about seeing the new "Space Cats" film? · 4 Lily: What was it like? · 5 Lily: Well, I haven't got any other ideas. Have you?

Sam's answers: A I've already seen it. · B Yes, it's me! · C Me too! · D Yes! Come to the park and we can play football! · E But it's closed on Sundays. · F Quite good, but too long. · G It's made of wood. · H I'm going to bed.

Answers: 1 C · 2 E · 3 A · 4 F · 5 D. Extra: G, H.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read Lily's lines together. Your child chooses Sam's answer for each one.
  3. Act it out (4 min): act the phone call with pretend phones.
  4. Our phone call (8 min): make a new phone call: your child is bored and you make suggestions. Your child says yes or no and why.
  5. Bored? Try this! (5 min): your child writes a list of five things to do when you feel bored.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm feeling quite bored." … "How about seeing a film? – I've already seen it." … "Come to the park and we can play football!"
