Week 54 · Lesson 3: Dress-Up Day

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Clothes and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 54: Let's have some fun!

Fun for Flyers · Week 54: Let's have some fun! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · Shall we…? Lesson 2 · I'm feeling bored Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a school play on a stage: a pirate with a parrot · a girl with big paper wings, flying · a queen reading a piece of paper · a man holding an envelope · a whale made of card · a sign that says "This afternoon".
  • Coloured pencils. Read each instruction twice.

Listen and colour and write: Colour the pirate's parrot green. · Colour the girl's wings blue and silver. · Write DANCE under "This afternoon" on the sign. · Colour the queen's crown gold. · Write "Happy birthday!" on the man's envelope.

Write a story. Start like this: "It was raining, and Ben and Kim were bored. Then they found an old box under Grandma's bed…" What was in the box? What did they do? What happened when Grandma came home? (50 words or more)

Answers: Colour and write as said. Story: any good story.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour and write (7 min): say each instruction twice. Your child colours or writes.
  3. Costumes (4 min): ask: "What's the pirate wearing? What's the queen wearing?" Your child answers. Clothes
  4. Plan the story (4 min): your child says three ideas: what's in the box, what they do, and the surprise at the end.
  5. Write the story (10 min): your child writes the story with 50 words or more.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The pirate is wearing a big black hat." … "They found an old box under Grandma's bed." … "When Grandma came home, she was very surprised!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Make suggestions: Shall we…? Why don't we…? How about…? We could…
  • Choose the best answers in a phone call about what to do.
  • Listen and colour and write in a costume picture, and write a story from a start.
