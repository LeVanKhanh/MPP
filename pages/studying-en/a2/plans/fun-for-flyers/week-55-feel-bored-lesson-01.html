Week 55 · Lesson 1: When I Feel…

You Need

  • The halves of the sentences below on separate papers.
  • The "about me" sentences below.

Draw lines between the two halves: 1 When I need to talk about something, · 2 If I'm thirsty, · 3 When I'm really excited, · 4 If I feel too hot, · 5 When I'm ill, — a I drink some water. · b I phone my best friend. · c I open the window. · d my mum gives me some medicine. · e I can't stop laughing and singing!

Finish these sentences about yourself: 1 When I'm tired, I ____. · 2 I laugh when ____. · 3 I feel afraid when ____. · 4 If I'm bored, I usually ____. · 5 ____ makes me angry. · 6 On days when I feel lazy, I ____.

Answers: 1 b · 2 a · 3 e · 4 c · 5 d. About yourself: any true answers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Feelings and Emotion Vocabulary Chant. Make each face.
  3. Draw lines (6 min): your child joins the halves and reads the sentences aloud.
  4. About me (8 min): your child finishes the six sentences with true answers. Feeling sentences
  5. Ask me (5 min): your child asks you the same six questions: "What do you do when you're tired?"
  6. Feelings game (4 min): say a situation: "Your friend is ill." Your child says how they feel and what they do: "I feel sad. I phone her."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "If I'm thirsty, I drink some water." … "When I'm bored, I usually read a comic." … "I feel afraid when there's a storm."
