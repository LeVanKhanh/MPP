Week 55 · Lesson 2: Sam's Bicycle Race

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Sport and Feelings. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 55: If I feel bored

Fun for Flyers · Week 55: If I feel bored | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · When I feel… Lesson 2 · Sam's bicycle race Lesson 3 · Getting ready

You Need

  • A word box: early (example) · race · suddenly · blanket · knee · disappear · lazy · excited · unhappy. Three words are extra.
  • The story below, written large.

Read the story. Choose a word from the box. Sam and his grandpa often go to the park together early in the morning. They're both going to be in a long bicycle (1) ____ next month, so they practise every day. Last Monday, they were riding near the lake when (2) ____ a big dog ran in front of Grandpa. Grandpa went off the path and fell into the lake! He was wet and cold, so they went home. Grandma gave him a (3) ____ to put round his shoulders. "We can't practise tomorrow, Sam," said Grandpa. Sam felt (4) ____. Grandpa could see that Sam was sad. "Don't be sad!" he laughed. "It's only for one day. My (5) ____ hurts a little, that's all!"

Choose the best name: Be careful, Grandpa! · The bicycle prize · Grandma's new blanket

Answers: 1 race · 2 suddenly · 3 blanket · 4 unhappy · 5 knee. Extra: disappear, lazy, excited. Best name: Be careful, Grandpa!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the story together. Your child writes a word from the box next to each number.
  3. Best name (2 min): your child chooses the best name and says why.
  4. How did they feel? (6 min): ask: "How did Grandpa feel in the lake? How did Sam feel? How did Grandma feel?" Your child answers with reasons.
  5. What happened next? (8 min): your child writes three sentences about the day of the race.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Suddenly a big dog ran in front of Grandpa!" … "Sam felt unhappy." … "Don't be sad – it's only for one day!"
