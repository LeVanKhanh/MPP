Week 55 · Lesson 3: Getting Ready

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Daily Routines and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 55: If I feel bored

Fun for Flyers · Week 55: If I feel bored | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · When I feel… Lesson 2 · Sam's bicycle race Lesson 3 · Getting ready

You Need

  • The sentences below.
  • Twenty word cards for "Which word?": nouns and verbs from this unit and earlier units.

Complete the sentences about getting ready: Before Grandpa and Sam went to the park, they needed to get ready. Grandpa put on his (1) ____ and his helmet. Then he fetched the bikes from the (2) ____. Sam brushed his (3) ____ and put on his clean (4) ____ and his trainers. Grandma gave them a bottle of (5) ____ for the journey.

Which word? One person chooses a word card and explains it without saying the word. The other person guesses. Example: "You put this round your shoulders when you're cold." – "A blanket!"

Answers: Any words that make sense, for example: 1 jacket · 2 garden · 3 hair · 4 T-shirt · 5 water.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Complete the sentences (6 min): your child writes a word that makes sense in each gap. Daily routines
  3. My getting-ready (5 min): your child tells you how they get ready for school: "First I wash my face, then I…"
  4. Tell the story (6 min): your child tells the whole story of Sam and Grandpa, from getting ready to the race.
  5. Which word? (8 min): play "Which word?" with the cards. Take turns. One point for each right guess.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Grandpa put on his helmet." … "First I wash my face, then I get dressed." … "You put this round your shoulders. – A blanket!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Join sentences with if and when, and finish sentences about your own feelings.
  • Read a story, choose words from a box, and choose the best name.
  • Complete sentences about getting ready, tell a story, and play "Which word?".
