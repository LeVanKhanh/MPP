Week 56 · Lesson 1: We Had So Much Fun!

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Parties and Going Out and Hobbies. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 56: Fun and games

Fun for Flyers · Week 56: Fun and games | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · We had so much fun! Lesson 2 · Tom learns the guitar Lesson 3 · Word games

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a party with seven people: a girl carrying a big birthday cake · a boy with a plate of pancakes · a girl playing the guitar · a boy with a crown, dancing · a girl with a camera · a boy on the sofa reading a comic · a man putting up balloons.
  • Write seven names round the picture: Sophie · Daniel · Emma · Harry · Katie · Fred · Peter. One name is extra. Read the script twice.

Script: We had so much fun at our party at the end of the year! · Look at Sophie. She's carrying the big birthday cake. (example) · Daniel is the boy with the plate of pancakes. He cooked them with his dad. · Can you see Emma? She's playing the guitar. She's been practising for weeks! · Harry is wearing a gold crown. He dances better than everyone in our class! · Katie is the girl with the camera. She took photos of everyone. · And Fred is sitting on the sofa. He's reading a comic because he's tired of dancing!

Answers: Sophie – the cake (example) · Daniel – the pancakes · Emma – the guitar · Harry – the crown, dancing · Katie – the camera · Fred – the sofa and the comic. Extra name: Peter (and nobody talks about the man with the balloons).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw lines (8 min): read the script twice. Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  3. Who's who? (4 min): ask: "What's Emma doing? Who's wearing a crown?" Your child answers with full sentences.
  4. Our best party (6 min): your child tells you about the best party they have been to: where, who, what they ate, what they did. Party sentences
  5. Plan a party (7 min): plan a party for the end of the course together: food, music, games. Your child writes the list: "We're going to…"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Emma is playing the guitar." … "Harry is the boy who is wearing a crown." … "At our party, we're going to play board games!"
