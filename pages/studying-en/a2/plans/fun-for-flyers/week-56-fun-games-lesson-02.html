Week 56 · Lesson 2: Tom Learns the Guitar

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Hobbies and Telling Stories. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 56: Fun and games

Fun for Flyers · Week 56: Fun and games | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · We had so much fun! Lesson 2 · Tom learns the guitar Lesson 3 · Word games

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five pictures (simple drawings are fine).
  • 1 Tom watches a band on TV and has an idea: he wants to play the guitar. · 2 Tom borrows a book about the guitar from the library. · 3 Tom watches guitar lessons online on the computer. · 4 Tom practises in his bedroom. His sister isn't happy because it's very noisy! · 5 Tom plays the guitar on the stage at the school concert. Everyone claps.

Look at the pictures and tell the story: Tom learns the guitar. Start like this: "One evening, Tom was watching a band on TV…"

Something new: What would you like to learn? How could you learn it – from a book, online, from a teacher, or from a friend?

Answers: Any good story, for example: One evening, Tom was watching a band on TV, and he had an idea: he wanted to play the guitar! The next day, he borrowed a book about the guitar from the library. Then he watched some lessons online. He practised every day in his bedroom, but his sister didn't like the noise. A month later, Tom played on the stage at the school concert, and everyone clapped!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Musical Instruments. Point to the guitar.
  3. Tell the story (7 min): your child tells the story, one picture at a time. Help with words like borrow, online and practise.
  4. Write the story (9 min): your child writes the story with 50 words or more.
  5. Something new (6 min): talk about something new your child would like to learn and how. Hobby sentences
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Tom borrowed a book from the library." … "He watched lessons online and practised every day." … "I'd like to learn the piano. I could watch lessons online."
