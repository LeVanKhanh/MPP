Week 56 · Lesson 3: Word Games

30 minutes for young learners (9–12). Lesson pages: Flyers Wordlist and Jobs. Goes with Fun for Flyers – Unit 56: Fun and games

Fun for Flyers · Week 56: Fun and games | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: A2 Flyers

Lesson 1 · We had so much fun! Lesson 2 · Tom learns the guitar Lesson 3 · Word games

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a big grid with five rows and five boxes in each row. Write these words in the boxes (mix them up): dentist · mechanic · journalist · pilot · businessman · eagle · butterfly · tortoise · octopus · camel · sandwich · honey · jam · salt · pepper · January · weekend · century · yesterday · afternoon · plastic · wool · metal · paper · wood.
  • Five coloured pencils and some small paper squares to cover words.

Game 1 – Find the groups: Colour the five jobs blue, the five animals green, the five kinds of food yellow, the five time words red and the five materials orange.

Game 2 – Clue bingo: Example clues: This person helps you when you've got a toothache. · You put this on your food. It isn't salt. · This animal lives in the sea and has eight legs. · You can make a sweater from this. · There are a hundred years in one of these. · This person writes for a newspaper. · Bees make this. · This is the first month of the year.

Answers: Jobs: dentist, mechanic, journalist, pilot, businessman · Animals: eagle, butterfly, tortoise, octopus, camel · Food: sandwich, honey, jam, salt, pepper · Time: January, weekend, century, yesterday, afternoon · Materials: plastic, wool, metal, paper, wood. Clues: dentist · pepper · octopus · wool · century · journalist · honey · January.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Find the groups (5 min): your child colours the five groups in the grid.
  3. Clue bingo (8 min): read a clue. Your child finds the word and covers it. Five in a line is bingo!
  4. My clues (7 min): your child writes clues for five more words in the grid and reads them to you. You find the words. Flyers wordlist
  5. Well done! (5 min): talk about the course: "What was your favourite unit? What new things can you do in English?" Celebrate – your child has finished Fun for Flyers!
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "This animal lives in the sea and has eight legs. – An octopus!" … "My favourite unit was…" … "Now I can tell stories in English!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen to someone talk about a party and draw lines to the right people.
  • Tell and write a story from five pictures about learning something new.
  • Play word games with words from the whole Flyers course.
