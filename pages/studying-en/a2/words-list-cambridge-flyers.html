Learners should also know the numbers 101–1,000, the ordinals 21st–31st and these names: Betty, David, Emma, Frank, George, Harry, Helen, Holly, Katy, Michael, Oliver, Richard, Robert, Sarah, Sophia, William.

Study tips

  • Learn the Starters and Movers words first: Flyers adds about 500 new words on top of them.
  • Pick one topic a week. Tap each word, then use it in a short story about your day.
  • Watch for UK and US pairs – torch / flashlight, biscuit / cookie. Both can appear in the test.
