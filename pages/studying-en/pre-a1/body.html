Basic sentences Sentences

This is my ___. / These are my ___.

  • This is my nose.
  • This is my mouth.
  • These are my eyes.
  • These are my ears.

How many ___? – Two!

  • How many eyes?
  • Two eyes!
  • How many noses?
  • One nose!

Point to your ___!

  • Point to your nose!
  • Point to your ears!
  • Close your eyes!
  • Clap your hands!

I'm happy! / I'm sad.

  • Look at my smile! I'm happy!
  • Are you happy?
  • Yes, I am!
  • Oh dear. Teddy is sad.

💬 Let's talk

  • How many eyes have you got?
  • Where's your nose?
  • Are you happy or sad today?

Parent's Corner

Game: Teddy says

  • "Teddy says: point to your nose!" Your child points. Go faster and slower, then let your child be Teddy and give the orders. Many classes say "Touch your nose!"; that is fine too.
  • Common mistake: "Two eye." → "Yes! Two eyes!" Say the -s a little longer. One nose, two eyes, two ears.
  • Common mistake: "This is my eyes." → "These are my eyes!" Just say it back; at this age they learn it by hearing it many times.
  • Tip: draw a big circle, then let your child add two eyes, one nose and one mouth. Count each one as it goes on.
