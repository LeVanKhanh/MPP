Basic sentences Sentences

The ___ is big. The ___ is small.

  • The elephant is big.
  • The mouse is small.
  • The snake is long.
  • The pencil is short.

Is it long or short? – It's ___.

  • Is the snake long or short?
  • It's long!
  • Is your hair long or short?
  • It's short.

Old, new, young

  • Grandpa is old.
  • The baby is young.
  • My bag is new.
  • My hat is old.

Clean or dirty? Open or closed?

  • My hands are dirty!
  • Now they're clean.
  • The door is open.
  • The window is closed.

Parent's Corner

Game: Opposite actions

  • Say a word and do the action together: "Big!" (stretch up), "Small!" (crouch down), "Long!" (arms wide), "Short!" (hands close). Then say a word and your child does the opposite.
  • About the words: the first group is all Cambridge Starters. Little, slow, hot, cold and bad are Movers words, and fast is a Flyers word, but preschool books use them early. The grey tag on each word says which.
  • Common mistake: "It's big snake." → "It's a big snake!" The describing word goes before the thing, with a in front.
  • Tip: compare real things: two pencils, two shoes, Mum's hand and your child's hand. Opposites are easiest when you can see both.

Review Next Time

Ask these with real things. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show two pencils) Which is long?
  2. Is your hair long or short?
  3. Is an elephant big or small?
  4. Is the door open or closed?
  5. Are your hands clean or dirty?
