Week 1 · Lesson 3: How Old Are You?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello and goodbye Lesson 2 · What's your name? Lesson 3 · How old are you?

You Need

  • Teddy, a few crayons, a sheet of paper.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello Song for Kids (The Singing Walrus). Wave and clap.
  2. Remember (4 min): Teddy asks "What's your name?" Your child answers. Then your child asks Teddy, and Teddy answers "My name is Tom!"
  3. How old? (5 min): hold up fingers. "How old are you?" "I'm three!" Count the fingers together: "One, two, three!" Teddy is two: "One, two!"
  4. Watch and move (2 min): Hello Hello! Can You Clap Your Hands? Do every action.
  5. You are the teacher (6 min): your child gives the orders: "Stand up! Sit down! Clap your hands!" You do them, sometimes wrong on purpose. Your child laughs and says "No!"
  6. Please and thank you (4 min): pass the crayons. "Can I have a crayon, please?" "Here you are." "Thank you!" Swap roles.
  7. Show Teddy (4 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How old are you? I'm three! One, two, three!" … "Can I have a crayon, please? Thank you!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Wave and say "Hello!" and "Bye-bye!" at the start and end of a lesson.
  • Answer "What's your name?" with their name, and "How old are you?" with fingers or a number.
  • Stand up, sit down, clap and wave when you say so.
