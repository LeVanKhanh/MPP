Week 2 · Lesson 1: My School Bag

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: School. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 1: School.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 2: School | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My school bag Lesson 2 · Red and yellow Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • Your child's real school bag with a book, a crayon and a pencil inside, a sheet of paper.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired.
  • About 6 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs). Wave, then "What's your name?" to warm up from last week.
  2. What's in my bag? (5 min): take out the book, the crayon and the pencil one by one. "Look! It's a book!" Say each word three times; your child points. Then hold up the bag: "It's a bag!" Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): School Supplies – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the real thing when it comes on screen.
  4. Run to it (4 min): put the four things in different corners. "Run to the book!" "Run to the bag!" Then your child says, and you run.
  5. What's missing? (5 min): line up bag, book, crayon, pencil. Your child closes their eyes; take one away. "What's missing?" Pointing at the gap is a good answer.
  6. Watch (2 min): What's this? – It's a pencil. Clap on "pencil".
  7. Draw (5 min): "Draw a big book!" with the crayon. Then "Draw a pencil!" Name each drawing together.
  8. Pack and goodbye (4 min): "Put the book in the bag! Put the crayon in the bag!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in my bag? Look! It's a book!" … "Put the pencil in the bag!"
