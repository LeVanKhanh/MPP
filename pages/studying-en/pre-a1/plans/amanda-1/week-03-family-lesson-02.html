Week 3 · Lesson 2: Brother, Sister, One

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 2: Family.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 3: Family | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Mummy and Daddy Lesson 2 · Brother, sister, one Lesson 3 · Who's this?

You Need

  • Family photos, a boy doll and a girl doll (or two teddies), and last week's book, crayon and pencil.
  • No brother or sister at home? Use cousins, friends or the dolls: "This is Tom. He's a brother."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photos again (3 min): "Who's this?" for Mummy and Daddy. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Brother and sister (5 min): the boy doll is the brother, the girl doll is the sister. "Who's this? He's my brother!" "She's my sister!" Point to real photos if you have them.
  4. Watch (3 min): Meet My Family! (English Singsing). Point when you hear "brother" and "sister".
  5. Number one (5 min): hold up one finger: "One!" Then "One teddy! One car! One book!" Ask "Give me one crayon!" and count it together: "One!"
  6. Watch (2 min): She's My Mother. Point to Mummy's photo on "mother".
  7. Give it to Mummy (6 min): last week's school things come back. "Give the book to Mummy! Give the pencil to Daddy!" Your child carries each one; everyone says "Thank you!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, and wave to every doll.

Say this: "Who's this? He's my brother! She's my sister!" … "Give me one crayon, please. One!"
