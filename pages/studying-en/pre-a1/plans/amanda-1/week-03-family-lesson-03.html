Week 3 · Lesson 3: Who's This?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 2: Family.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 3: Family | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Mummy and Daddy Lesson 2 · Brother, sister, one Lesson 3 · Who's this?

You Need

  • Family photos, a sheet of paper to cover them, the dolls, a soft ball, crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photo peekaboo (5 min): cover a photo and pull the paper away slowly. "Who's this? Is it Daddy?" When the face shows: "Yes! It's Daddy!"
  3. Watch and fingers (2 min): Finger Family. Your child shows each finger now.
  4. Family bowling (4 min): stand the dolls up and roll the ball. Knock one down: "Oh dear! Sorry, Mummy!" Stand it up again.
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): The People In My Family. Hold up a family photo as you sing.
  6. Draw my family (6 min): your child draws or colours the family. Write each name and say it: "This is my mum. This is my brother."
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who's this? Is it Daddy? Yes! It's Daddy!" … "Oh dear! Sorry, Mummy!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to Mummy, Daddy, a brother and a sister in photos, and say at least two of them.
  • Answer "Who's this?" with a word or "This is my…".
  • Show one finger, or give you one thing, when you say "one".
