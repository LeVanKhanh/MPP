Week 4 · Lesson 1: My Toys

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 3: Toys.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 4: Toys | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · Blue and green Lesson 3 · Teddy's birthday

You Need

  • A box with four toys: a ball, a car, a doll and a teddy.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Toy box (5 min): take the toys out one by one. "Look! It's a car!" Say each word three times; your child points. Listen → point → say. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Toys Vocabulary Chant. Hold up the real toy when you hear it.
  4. Be a toy (4 min): "Drive like a car! Bounce like a ball!" Then the teddy and the doll dance. Your child calls out a toy and you do it.
  5. What's missing? (5 min): line up the four toys. Your child closes their eyes; take one away. "What's missing?" Pointing at the gap is a good answer.
  6. Watch (2 min): I Have A Toy. Show your own toy when the song asks.
  7. Car race (5 min): push the car across the floor. "Go, car, go! … Stop!" Then your child says "Go!" and "Stop!" and you drive.
  8. Toys to bed (3 min): "Put the teddy in the box! Put the car in the box!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a car! Go, car, go! Stop!" … "Put the teddy in the box!"
