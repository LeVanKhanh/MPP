Week 4 · Lesson 2: Blue and Green

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 3: Toys.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 4: Toys | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · Blue and green Lesson 3 · Teddy's birthday

You Need

  • The toy box from Lesson 1, red, yellow, blue and green crayons, a bag, and a sheet of paper with a big ball and a big car drawn on it.
  • Red and yellow came in week 2. Today adds blue and green, so your child knows four colours.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Toy box again (3 min): "What's this?" for the ball, car, doll and teddy. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Blue and green (5 min): hold up the blue crayon: "It's blue!" Then the green one: "It's green!" Then put the four crayons out and sort toys by colour: red, yellow, blue, green. Colour words
  4. Watch (2 min): Red Yellow Green Blue. Hold up each crayon as you hear its colour.
  5. Is this a…? (5 min): put a toy in the bag. Your child feels it: "Is this a car?" Nod and say "Yes, it is!" or shake your head: "No, it isn't!"
  6. Colour hunt (5 min): "Find a blue thing! … Find a green thing!" Then one red and one yellow thing from week 2.
  7. Colour the toys (5 min): "Colour the car blue. Colour the ball green." Name the colours as your child colours.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's blue! It's green!" … "Is this a car? Yes, it is!"
