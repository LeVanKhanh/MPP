Week 4 · Lesson 3: Teddy's Birthday

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys and Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 3: Toys.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 4: Toys | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · Blue and green Lesson 3 · Teddy's birthday

You Need

  • Teddy, the other toys, the dolls from the family week (Mummy, Daddy, brother, sister), a bag, a soft ball.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): a toy in the bag. "Is this a ball? Is it a car?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a car!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Can You Please Help Me? Toys, colours and family together. Point to the right toy each time.
  4. Teddy's birthday (6 min): "Happy birthday, Teddy!" The family dolls come to the party. Your child gives Teddy a toy: "This is for you!" Teddy says "Thank you!" Ask "What colour is it?" for each one.
  5. Sing (2 min): sing "Happy Birthday" to Teddy together and clap.
  6. Ball games (5 min): "Throw the ball! Catch the ball! Kick the ball!" A little football: "Kick the ball to Teddy! Hooray!"
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Happy birthday, Teddy! This is for you!" … "Is this a ball? Yes, it is!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to the ball, car, doll and teddy when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Say blue and green, and find a red, yellow, blue or green thing.
  • Answer "Is this a…?" with a nod, a shake of the head, "Yes!" or "No!".
