Week 5 · Lesson 1: My Face

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 4: Body.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 5: Body | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Two eyes, one nose Lesson 3 · Happy face, sad face

You Need

  • A mirror, Teddy, paper and crayons.
  • Many classes say "Touch your nose!"; the Cambridge Starters list says "Point to your nose!". Use both.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Mirror, mirror (5 min): sit in front of the mirror together. "Look! My nose! … My eyes!" Say each word three times while you point; your child points to their own. Listen → point → say. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): My Face – Talking Flashcards. Point to your own face with the video.
  4. Touch your nose! (4 min): "Touch your nose! Touch your ears! Point to your mouth!" Faster, slower, then your child gives the orders.
  5. Teddy's face (5 min): "Where's Teddy's nose?" Your child points. Then "Where's Teddy's mouth? … Teddy's eyes?"
  6. Watch (3 min): This Is My Face. Point as you sing.
  7. Draw a face (5 min): draw a big circle: "Look! A circle! It's a face!" Your child adds the eyes, nose and mouth as you say them.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): wave to the face in the mirror: "Bye-bye!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! My nose! Where's your nose?" … "Touch your ears! Point to your mouth!"
