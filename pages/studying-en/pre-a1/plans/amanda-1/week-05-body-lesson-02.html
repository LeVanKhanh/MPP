Week 5 · Lesson 2: Two Eyes, One Nose

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 4: Body.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 5: Body | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Two eyes, one nose Lesson 3 · Happy face, sad face

You Need

  • Two toy cars, a few crayons (blue and green among them), and a sheet of paper with a big face drawn on it.
  • Number one came in week 3. Today adds two: count real things, touching each one.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Face check (3 min): point to your own face: "What's this?" Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. How many? (6 min): "How many eyes? One, two. Two eyes!" Touch each eye as you count. "How many noses? One nose!" Then ears, hands and feet. Sentences
  4. Watch (2 min): I Have Two Eyes. Hold up one finger or two fingers with the song. Watch it twice.
  5. One or two? (5 min): "Give me one car! … Give me two cars!" Then crayons: "Give me two crayons, please." Count them together each time.
  6. Watch and move (2 min): Head Shoulders Knees & Toes (Learn It). Copy the actions; the new words can wait.
  7. Colour the face (6 min): "Colour the eyes blue! Colour the hair green!" Silly colours are fun. Count the eyes again: "Two blue eyes!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, waving with two hands.

Say this: "How many eyes? One, two. Two eyes!" … "Give me two cars, please!"
