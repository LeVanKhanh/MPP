Week 5 · Lesson 3: Happy Face, Sad Face

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 4: Body.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 5: Body | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Two eyes, one nose Lesson 3 · Happy face, sad face

You Need

  • Teddy, paper and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Teddy says (5 min): "Teddy says: touch your nose! Teddy says: point to your ears!" Then your child is Teddy and gives the orders.
  3. Watch (4 min): Me! (Noodle & Pals). Point to each part on yourself.
  4. Happy and sad (5 min): make a big smile: "Look at my smile! I'm happy!" Then a sad face: "Oh dear. I'm sad." Your child copies, then asks you "Happy or sad?" Teddy is sad, so give Teddy a smile.
  5. Mummy's face (4 min): "Where's Mummy's nose? … Mummy's eyes?" Your child points on your face. Then you point on theirs.
  6. Draw two faces (4 min): two circles: one happy face and one sad face. "Is it happy or sad?"
  7. Show Teddy (3 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Look at my smile! I'm happy!" … "Are you happy or sad?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to their eyes, ears, nose and mouth when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Count two things, touching one thing for each number ("two eyes, one nose").
  • Follow "Touch your…!" or "Point to your…!", and show a happy face and a sad face.
