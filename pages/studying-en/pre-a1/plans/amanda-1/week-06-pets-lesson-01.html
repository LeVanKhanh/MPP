Week 6 · Lesson 1: Pets

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 5: Pets.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big and little Lesson 3 · Count and play

You Need

  • Toys or pictures of a cat, a dog, a fish and a mouse, a bag to hide them in, paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired.
  • About 8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs), the same hello song as every week.
  2. Meet the pets (5 min): take the cat, dog, fish and mouse out of the bag one by one. Say each word three times and make the sound; your child points. Listen → point → say. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant. Point at the screen when you hear "cat" or "dog".
  4. Move (4 min): "Walk like a cat! Swim like a fish! Run like a mouse!" Do every action together.
  5. Where's the dog? (5 min): hide the four pets around the room. "Where's the dog?" When your child finds it: "Yes! It's a dog!"
  6. Song (2 min): It's a Dog, with the actions.
  7. Draw (5 min): draw a big cat together; your child colours. "A cat! A brown cat!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, then "Bye-bye, cat! Bye-bye, dog!"

Say this: "Look! It's a cat! Can you say cat?" … "Where's the dog?"
