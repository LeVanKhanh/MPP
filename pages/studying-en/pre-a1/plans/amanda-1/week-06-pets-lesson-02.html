Week 6 · Lesson 2: Big and Little

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 5: Pets.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big and little Lesson 3 · Count and play

You Need

  • The four pets from Lesson 1, an elephant (toy or picture), two balls, a big box and a little box.
  • Little and small mean the same. Use little if your child's class uses it; the Cambridge Starters list uses small, so say both: "It's little. It's small!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Bag again (3 min): the four pets. "What's this?" Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Big elephant, little mouse (4 min): "It's an elephant! It's big!" Stretch up tall. "It's a mouse. It's little!" Crouch down small. Sentences
  4. Watch and move (4 min): Let's Go To The Zoo. Be a big elephant, then a little mouse.
  5. Big or little? (5 min): hold up a toy: "Is it big or little?" Your child puts it in the big box or the little box.
  6. Count to two (4 min): "How many balls? One, two. Two balls!" Touch each one as you count. Then two dogs, two cars.
  7. Feed the pets (5 min): pretend food. "Give the cat some fish! Give the dog a ball! Give the mouse some cake!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it big or little?" … "How many balls? One, two. Two balls!"
