Week 6 · Lesson 3: Count and Play

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 5: Pets.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big and little Lesson 3 · Count and play

You Need

  • The five animals from this week, a ball, and five small toy cars.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Bag of five (3 min): cat, dog, fish, mouse, elephant. "What's this?" Cheer for every one.
  3. Watch (3 min): Five Spotted Dogs. Hold up five fingers and put one down each time.
  4. Count real things (5 min): "How many cars? One, two. Two cars!" Touch each one, then say the last number again. Easy? Go on to three, four, five.
  5. Watch and move (4 min): Yes, I Can! Do every action.
  6. Silly questions (4 min): "A fish can swim!" (act it) … "Can a cat fly?" "Nooo!" Silly is fun at this age.
  7. Animal football (5 min): stand the animals up as targets. "Kick the ball to the dog!" Knock one down? "Bye-bye, dog!"
  8. My favourite and goodbye (3 min): "My favourite pet is the dog! And you?" Your child picks one and says its name. Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many cars? Let's count! One, two. Two cars!" … "Can a fish swim? Yes, it can!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to the cat, dog, fish, mouse and elephant when you say the word.
  • Say "big" or "little" about a toy.
  • Count two real things, touching one thing for each number.
