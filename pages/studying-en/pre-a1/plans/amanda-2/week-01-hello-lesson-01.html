Week 1 · Lesson 1: Hello Again

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello again Lesson 2 · Colours and 1, 2, 3 Lesson 3 · Who's this? What's this?

You Need

  • A bag with an apple, a ball and a book. Teddy. Crayons: blue, green, orange, red, yellow. Paper.
  • This week brings back last year's English: hello, colours, numbers 1–3, and "What's this?". If your child is new to the course, it is a gentle start too.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello Song for Kids (The Singing Walrus). Wave and sing.
  2. Hello, everyone (4 min): say hello to everybody in the room and every toy: "Hello, Mummy! Hello, Teddy!" Then "What's your name?" "My name is…"
  3. What's in the bag? (5 min): take out the apple, the ball and the book. "What's this? It's an apple!" Your child answers; one word is perfect. Sentences
  4. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name? (Super Simple Puppets). Say your own name in the gaps.
  5. Colour ball (5 min): roll the ball and hold up a crayon. Whoever catches it says the colour: "Red!" "Blue!"
  6. Watch and move (2 min): Hello Hello! Can You Clap Your Hands? Do every action.
  7. Draw (6 min): draw an apple, a ball and a book. "Colour the apple red. Colour the ball blue. Colour the book yellow."
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, and wave to every toy.

Say this: "Hello! What's your name?" … "What's this? It's an apple!" … "What colour is it?"
