Week 1 · Lesson 3: Who's This? What's This?

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Colours and Hello. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello again Lesson 2 · Colours and 1, 2, 3 Lesson 3 · Who's this? What's this?

You Need

  • Family photos and a sheet of paper to cover them, the apple, ball and book from Lesson 1, crayons, Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photo peekaboo (5 min): cover a family photo and pull the paper away slowly. "Who's this? Is it Daddy?" "Yes! It's Daddy!"
  3. Watch and fingers (2 min): Finger Family. Your child shows each finger.
  4. Show me (5 min): mix colours and numbers: "Show me a red crayon! … Show me two books! … Show me an apple!"
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): What's Your Favourite Colour? Sing your own colour in the answer.
  6. You are the teacher (5 min): your child asks you "What's this?" "What colour is it?" "How many?" You answer, sometimes wrong on purpose, and your child says "No!"
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions from the Hello page, then "What colour is it?" and "How many?" with the toys. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who's this? Is it Daddy?" … "Show me two books!" … "What's this? What colour is it?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say hello and goodbye, and answer "What's your name?".
  • Name five colours (blue, green, orange, red, yellow) and say what colour a thing is.
  • Say "one, two, three", count three things, and answer "What's this?" and "Who's this?".
