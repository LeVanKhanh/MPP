Week 2 · Lesson 1: Chair, Table, Door, Window

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: School. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 1: School.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 2: School | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Chair, table, door, window Lesson 2 · Brown, pink, purple Lesson 3 · What colour is the dog?

You Need

  • A room with a door, a window, a table and a chair. Teddy and a small toy chair or box for Teddy to sit on.
  • A school bag with a book, a crayon and a pencil (last year's words).
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Room tour (5 min): walk around and point: "Look! It's a door! … It's a window! … It's a table! … It's a chair!" Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Around The House – Talking Flashcards. Run and point to the real door, window, table and chair.
  4. Run to it (4 min): "Run to the door! … Sit on the chair! … Look at the window!" Then your child gives the orders.
  5. Teddy's school (5 min): Teddy sits on a chair at the table. "Where's Teddy? On the chair!" Move Teddy to the door, the window, under the table, and ask again.
  6. Watch (3 min): My Classroom (Super Simple Songs). Point to the same things in your room.
  7. What's in the bag? (5 min): last year's words come back. "Is this a pencil? … Is this a book?" "Yes, it is!" / "No, it isn't!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Bye-bye, door! Bye-bye, window!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a door!" … "Sit on the chair!" … "Where's Teddy? On the chair!"
