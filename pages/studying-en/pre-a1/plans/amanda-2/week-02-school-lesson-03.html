Week 2 · Lesson 3: What Colour Is the Dog?

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 1: School.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 2: School | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Chair, table, door, window Lesson 2 · Brown, pink, purple Lesson 3 · What colour is the dog?

You Need

  • Teddy and a toy dog, the school bag with a book, a crayon and a pencil, crayons, the flower drawings from Lesson 2, a box (Teddy's door) and a sheet of paper (Teddy's window).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): a school thing in the bag. "Is this a crayon? Is it a pencil?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a pencil!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Do You Have A Crayon? Hold up a crayon every time you hear "crayon".
  4. What colour is it? (5 min): "What colour is the dog? It's brown!" Then the flowers, the crayons, the chair: "What colour is it?"
  5. Sing (2 min): If It's Yellow and You Know It. Hold up the right crayon.
  6. Teddy's classroom (6 min): build a little classroom: a table, a chair, a box door and a paper window. Teddy and the dog come to school. "Open the door! … Sit on the chair! … Look at the window!" Your child moves the toys.
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions, then "What's this?" for the door, window, table and chair. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What colour is the dog? It's brown!" … "Open the door! Sit on the chair!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a chair, a table, a door and a window when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Say brown, pink and purple, and the five colours from last week.
  • Answer "Is this a…?" and "What colour is it?" with a word or a short sentence.
