Week 3 · Lesson 1: Granny and Grandad

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 2: Family.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 3: Family | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Granny and Grandad Lesson 2 · Auntie, cousin, 4 and 5 Lesson 3 · Family hands

You Need

  • Family photos with the grandparents, a few dolls or teddies, paper and crayons.
  • Granny and Grandad are the course words; the Cambridge Starters list says grandma and grandpa. Use both: "Granny! My grandma!"
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Family photos (5 min): "Who's this? It's Granny! My grandma!" "It's Grandad! My grandpa!" Then Mummy, Daddy, brother and sister from last year. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Family – Talking Flashcards. Point to a photo for each word.
  4. Walk like Grandad (4 min): "Walk like Grandad! … Jump like my brother! … Sit like Granny!" Then your child calls out a person and you do it.
  5. Is this Granny? (5 min): point to photos or dolls: "Is this Granny?" "Yes, it is!" / "No, it isn't. It's Mummy!" Then your child asks you.
  6. Watch and fingers (2 min): Finger Family. Hold up each finger.
  7. Draw (5 min): draw Granny and Grandad. Write "Granny" and "Grandad" under them and read the words together.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Bye-bye, Granny! Bye-bye, Grandad!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's this? It's Granny! My grandma!" … "Is this Grandad? Yes, it is!"
