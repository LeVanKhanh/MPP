Week 3 · Lesson 2: Auntie, Cousin, 4 and 5

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 2: Family.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 3: Family | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Granny and Grandad Lesson 2 · Auntie, cousin, 4 and 5 Lesson 3 · Family hands

You Need

  • Family photos, five dolls or teddies, and five number cards (big 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 on paper).
  • No auntie or cousin nearby? Use the dolls: "This is Auntie. This is my cousin, Tom."
  • Numbers 1–3 came last year. Today adds 4 and 5: always touch one thing for each number.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photos again (3 min): "Who's this?" for Granny, Grandad, Mummy and Daddy. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Auntie and cousin (5 min): "This is my auntie. … This is my cousin." Point to photos or dolls; your child says the word.
  4. Watch (3 min): Five Little Ducks. Hold up five fingers and put one down each time.
  5. Count to five (6 min): line up the five dolls. "How many? One, two, three, four, five. Five!" Touch each one. Then count the people in a family photo. Sentences
  6. What number is it? (4 min): show a number card: "What number is it?" "Four!" Your child puts that many dolls next to the card.
  7. Take one away (4 min): five dolls. One goes away: "How many? Four!" Then three, two, one. "Oh dear! No dolls!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, waving with five fingers.

Say this: "This is my auntie. This is my cousin." … "How many? One, two, three, four, five. Five!"
