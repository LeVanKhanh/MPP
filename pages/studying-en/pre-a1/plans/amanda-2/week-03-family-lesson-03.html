Week 3 · Lesson 3: Family Hands

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 2: Family.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 3: Family | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Granny and Grandad Lesson 2 · Auntie, cousin, 4 and 5 Lesson 3 · Family hands

You Need

  • Family photos and a sheet of paper to cover them, the number cards 1–5, a toy phone, paper and crayons, Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photo peekaboo (4 min): cover a photo and pull the paper away slowly. "Who's this? Is it Granny?" "Yes! It's Granny!"
  3. Watch and sing (3 min): The People In My Family. Hold up a photo as you sing.
  4. Family hands (5 min): draw around your child's hand. Each finger is a person: "This is Mummy, this is Daddy, this is Granny…" Then count the fingers: "One, two, three, four, five. Five!"
  5. Watch and count (2 min): How Many Fingers? Show the number before the singer says it.
  6. Family phone (5 min): phone the family: "Hello, Granny! … Hello, Auntie! … Bye-bye, Grandad!" Then your child phones someone.
  7. Show Teddy (6 min): ask the five Review questions, then hold up number cards: "What number is it?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "This is Mummy, this is Granny… How many? Five!" … "Hello, Granny! Bye-bye, Grandad!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to Granny, Grandad, an auntie and a cousin in photos, and say at least two of them.
  • Count five things, touching one thing for each number, and say "four" and "five".
  • Answer "Who's this?" and "What number is it?" with a word.
