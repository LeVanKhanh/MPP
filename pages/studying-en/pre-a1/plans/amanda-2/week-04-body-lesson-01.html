Week 4 · Lesson 1: Head, Arms, Hands, Legs

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Head, arms, hands, legs Lesson 2 · Black, white and square Lesson 3 · Shape people

You Need

  • Space to move, Teddy, paper and crayons.
  • Last year: ears, eyes, nose, mouth. This year: head, arms, hands and legs.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 5 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Face check (3 min): "Touch your nose! … your ears! … your mouth!" Last year's words, quickly.
  3. New words (5 min): point and say: "This is my head! These are my arms! These are my hands! These are my legs!" Say each word three times; your child points to their own. Word list
  4. Watch and move (2 min): Head Shoulders Knees & Toes (Learn It). Touch each part with the song.
  5. One or two? (4 min): "Do we have one or two arms?" "Two!" "One or two heads?" "One!" Hold up the fingers each time. Sentences
  6. Action time (4 min): "Wave your arms! Clap your hands! Kick your legs!" Then your child gives the orders and you do them.
  7. Draw a person (6 min): draw a person together and count: "One head. Two arms. Two hands. Two legs!" Your child adds each part as you say it.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): wave your arms: "Bye-bye!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "These are my arms! One arm, two arms." … "Wave your arms! Kick your legs!"
