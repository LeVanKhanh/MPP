Week 4 · Lesson 2: Black, White and Square

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Colours and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Head, arms, hands, legs Lesson 2 · Black, white and square Lesson 3 · Shape people

You Need

  • Black and white crayons (and a dark sheet of paper for the white crayon), a picture of a zebra, a paper circle and a paper square.
  • A circle came last year; the square is new. Both are course words, a level above Starters.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Teddy says (3 min): "Teddy says: wave your arms! … clap your hands!" Lesson 1's words.
  3. Black and white (5 min): hold up the crayons: "It's black! … It's white!" Show the zebra: "What colour is the zebra? Black and white!" Then find black and white things in the room. Colour words
  4. Watch (2 min): Learn Shapes – Talking Flashcards. Draw each shape in the air.
  5. Circle or square? (5 min): hold up the paper circle: "It's a circle!" Then the square: "It's a square!" Swap them behind your back: "Square or circle?" Sentences
  6. Watch (2 min): Shapes Song (Dream English). Make each shape with your hands.
  7. Shape hunt (5 min): "Find a square! … The window is a square!" "Find a circle! … The clock is a circle!"
  8. Draw and goodbye (5 min): "Draw a black square! Draw a white circle!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour is the zebra? Black and white!" … "Square or circle? It's a square!"
