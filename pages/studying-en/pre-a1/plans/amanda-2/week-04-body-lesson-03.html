Week 4 · Lesson 3: Shape People

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Head, arms, hands, legs Lesson 2 · Black, white and square Lesson 3 · Shape people

You Need

  • Paper shapes: a few circles, a few squares and some long strips (for arms and legs). Glue or tape, crayons, Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Teddy says (4 min): "Teddy says: touch your head! … wave your arms! … kick your legs!" Then your child is Teddy.
  3. Watch and draw (3 min): I Like To Draw. Draw each shape on paper as you sing.
  4. Make a shape person (6 min): build a person on paper: "A circle head! A square body! Two arms, two legs, two hands!" Count each part as it goes on.
  5. Big and small (4 min): put your hands together: "My hands are big. Your hands are small!" Then feet, arms and legs.
  6. Watch and clap (3 min): Triangle, Circle, Circle, Square! Clap on every "circle" and jump on every "square".
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions, then "Do we have one or two legs?" and "Is it a circle or a square?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "A circle head! A square body! Two arms, two legs!" … "My hands are big. Your hands are small!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to their head, arms, hands and legs when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Say "black" and "white", and answer "Do we have one or two…?".
  • Tell a circle from a square, and find one of each in the room.
