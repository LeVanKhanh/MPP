Week 5 · Lesson 1: My Clothes

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Hot or cold? Lesson 3 · Dress-up show

You Need

  • A box of real clothes: a coat, a dress, a hat, a jumper, shoes and trousers. Teddy and some doll clothes.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Clothes box (5 min): take the clothes out one by one. "Look! It's a hat! … They're shoes!" Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Learn Clothes Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the real thing when it comes on screen.
  4. Dress Teddy (5 min): "Put on the hat! … Put on the shoes!" Your child dresses Teddy, then tells you what to put on.
  5. Watch (3 min): Put On Your Shoes. Act out putting on each thing.
  6. Clothes race (4 min): "Run and find your shoes! … your hat!" Your child brings each one back.
  7. What colour? (5 min): "What colour is the hat? It's red!" "What colour are the shoes? They're white!" Use the colours from earlier weeks.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a hat! They're shoes!" … "What colour are they? They're white!"
