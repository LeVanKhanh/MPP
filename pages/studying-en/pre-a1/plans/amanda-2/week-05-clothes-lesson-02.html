Week 5 · Lesson 2: Hot or Cold?

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Hot or cold? Lesson 3 · Dress-up show

You Need

  • The clothes box, a cup of warm (not hot) water and an ice cube, pictures of the sun, a fire, snow and an ice cream, two boxes.
  • Hot and cold are Movers words, a level above Starters. Act them out: fan your face for hot, shiver for cold.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Clothes box again (3 min): "What's this?" for each piece of clothing. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Hot and cold (5 min): touch the warm cup: "It's hot!" (fan your face). Touch the ice: "It's cold!" (shiver). Then the pictures: "The sun is hot. The ice cream is cold." Sentences
  4. Watch (3 min): Hot and Cold Song. Fan or shiver with the song.
  5. Hot or cold? (5 min): hold up a picture: "Is it hot or cold?" Your child puts it in the hot box or the cold box: sun, fire, snow, ice cream.
  6. What do I wear? (5 min): "It's cold! Put on your coat! Put on your jumper!" … "It's hot! Put on your T-shirt and your hat!" Your child picks from the box.
  7. Watch (2 min): Put on your coat (English Singsing). Pretend to put on each thing.
  8. Goodbye (4 min): put everything back: "Coat in the box! Hat in the box!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's hot! … It's cold!" … "Is it hot or cold?" … "It's cold! Put on your coat!"
