Week 5 · Lesson 3: Dress-up Show

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Clothes and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Hot or cold? Lesson 3 · Dress-up show

You Need

  • Big clothes to dress up in (yours are perfect), a bag, crayons, a sheet of paper with a dress, a hat and trousers drawn on it, Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): clothes in the bag. "Is it a sock? … Is it a hat?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a hat!"
  3. Watch (3 min): What Are You Wearing? Answer with your own clothes.
  4. Dress-up show (6 min): your child dresses up and walks like a model. You say: "She's wearing a pink dress and a black hat!" Then your child says "I'm wearing…" and you take a turn.
  5. Colour the clothes (5 min): "Colour the dress purple! Colour the trousers brown! Colour the hat yellow!" Then "What colour are the trousers?"
  6. Hot or cold game (3 min): "It's cold!" Your child shivers and pretends to put on a coat. "It's hot!" Your child fans and puts on a hat. Faster and faster.
  7. Show Teddy (4 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I'm wearing a pink dress!" … "What colour are the trousers? They're brown!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a coat, a dress, a hat, a jumper, shoes and trousers, and say at least three of them.
  • Say "hot" and "cold", and put the sun, snow, a fire and an ice cream in the right group.
  • Answer "What colour is it?" and "What colour are they?" for their own clothes.
