Week 6 · Lesson 1: Farm Animals

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Farm animals Lesson 2 · Six and triangles Lesson 3 · Our farm

You Need

  • Toys or pictures of a cow, a horse, a pig and a sheep (and last year's cat, dog and fish), paper and crayons.
  • Animal noises: cow "moo", horse "neigh", pig "oink", sheep "baa", cat "meow", dog "woof". Young children love making them.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Farm animals (5 min): one by one: "Look! It's a cow! Moo!" Then the horse, the pig and the sheep. Say each word three times and make the noise; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (4 min): The Animals On The Farm. Make every animal noise together.
  4. Animal walk (4 min): "Walk like a horse! … Walk like a sheep! … Walk like a pig!" Then your child calls out an animal and you walk.
  5. Who says moo? (5 min): make a noise: "Moo! Is it a horse?" "No! It's a cow!" Then your child makes a noise and you guess.
  6. Last year's animals (2 min): "Meow! … Woof! … What's this? It's a fish!" Quickly, for fun.
  7. Draw (4 min): draw a pink pig and a white sheep. "What colour is the pig? It's pink!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Bye-bye, cow! Moo!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a cow! Moo!" … "Moo! Is it a horse? No! It's a cow!"
