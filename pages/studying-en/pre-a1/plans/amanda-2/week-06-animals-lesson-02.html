Week 6 · Lesson 2: Six and Triangles

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Farm animals Lesson 2 · Six and triangles Lesson 3 · Our farm

You Need

  • The farm animals, six small toys, number cards 1–6, and paper shapes: a circle, a square and six triangles in different colours (write a number 1–6 on each triangle).
  • Numbers 1–5 came earlier; today adds six. Circle and square came in week 4; today adds the triangle.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Farm again (3 min): hold up an animal: "Is this a cow?" "Yes, it is!" / "No, it isn't! It's a pig!"
  3. Number six (5 min): line up six toys. "One, two, three, four, five, six. Six!" Touch each one. Then show the card: "What number is it? It's six!" Numbers
  4. Watch and move (2 min): Count And Move. One action for each number.
  5. A new shape (5 min): hold up the triangle: "It's a triangle!" Then the old ones: "Circle, square or triangle?" Your child points to the right one. Shapes
  6. Watch (3 min): Triangle, Circle, Circle, Square! Clap on every "triangle".
  7. How many triangles? (6 min): spread out the six triangles. "How many triangles? Six!" Then "What colour is number five?" Your child finds the triangle with a 5 and says its colour.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, waving six times.

Say this: "One, two, three, four, five, six. Six!" … "How many triangles?" … "What colour is number five?"
