Week 6 · Lesson 3: Our Farm

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Farm animals Lesson 2 · Six and triangles Lesson 3 · Our farm

You Need

  • The animal toys, a bag, blocks or books to build a farm, paper shapes (circles and triangles), glue or tape, Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): an animal in the bag. "Is this a pig? … Is it a sheep?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a sheep! Baa!"
  3. Watch and move (4 min): Yes, I Can! Move like each animal.
  4. Build our farm (6 min): build a farm with blocks or books and put the animals in. Count them: "How many animals? Six!" Then "How many cows? … How many sheep?"
  5. What does a cow say? (4 min): "What does a cow say?" "Moo!" Then the horse, the pig, the sheep, the cat and the dog.
  6. Shape animals (4 min): make a cat from shapes: "A circle head! Two triangle ears!" Then a pig with a circle face.
  7. Show Teddy (3 min): ask a few Review questions, then "Is it a circle, a square or a triangle?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many animals? Six!" … "What does a cow say? Moo!" … "Two triangle ears!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a cow, a horse, a pig and a sheep, say at least two of them, and make their noises.
  • Count six things, touching one thing for each number, and say "six".
  • Find a triangle and tell it from a circle and a square.
