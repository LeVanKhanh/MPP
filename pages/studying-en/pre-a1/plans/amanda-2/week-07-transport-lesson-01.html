Week 7 · Lesson 1: Cars, Buses and Trains

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 6: Transport.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 7: Transport | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Cars, buses and trains Lesson 2 · What number is the car? Lesson 3 · Happy or sad?

You Need

  • Toys or pictures of a bike, a boat, a bus, a car, a plane and a train. A blue sheet of paper (water) and a grey one (the street).
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Transport toys (5 min): one by one: "Look! It's a bus!" Then the car, the bike, the train, the boat and the plane. Say each word three times and make its sound; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Vehicles – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the toy when it comes on screen.
  4. Be a vehicle (4 min): "Drive a car! … Ride a bike! … Fly a plane!" Do each action together, then your child calls them out.
  5. Water or street? (5 min): put down the blue and grey papers. "The boat is on the water. The car is on the street." Your child puts each toy in the right place. The plane flies over both!
  6. Watch (3 min): The Wheels On The Bus. Do every action.
  7. Car race (5 min): push the cars across the floor: "Go! … Stop!" Then your child says "Go!" and "Stop!" and you drive.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Bye-bye, bus!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a bus!" … "Ride a bike! Fly a plane!" … "Go! Stop!"
