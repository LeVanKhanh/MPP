Week 7 · Lesson 2: What Number Is the Car?

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Street, Numbers and ABC and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 6: Transport.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 7: Transport | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Cars, buses and trains Lesson 2 · What number is the car? Lesson 3 · Happy or sad?

You Need

  • Six toy vehicles, number cards 1–6, paper shapes (circles, squares, triangles, a star), glue or tape.
  • Numbers 1–6 and the four shapes all came earlier this year; today they come back together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Transport again (3 min): "What's this?" for each toy. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Number line-up (6 min): put a number card next to each vehicle. "What number is the car? It's number three!" Then the other way: "What is number two?" "The bus!" Sentences
  4. Watch (2 min): Count And Move. One action for each number.
  5. Shape vehicles (6 min): make a car from shapes: "A square! Two circles!" Then a boat with a triangle, and a plane with a star. Name every shape as it goes on. Shapes
  6. Watch (3 min): Driving In My Car. Hold a pretend wheel and drive.
  7. How many? (4 min): "How many cars? … How many boats?" Count them, touching each one. Then all of them: "Six!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What number is the car? It's number three!" … "A square and two circles! It's a car!"
