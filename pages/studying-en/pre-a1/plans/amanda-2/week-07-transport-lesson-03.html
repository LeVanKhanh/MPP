Week 7 · Lesson 3: Happy or Sad?

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Street and My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 6: Transport.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 7: Transport | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Cars, buses and trains Lesson 2 · What number is the car? Lesson 3 · Happy or sad?

You Need

  • Teddy, the toy vehicles, a few chairs in a line (a train), paper with a happy face and a sad face drawn on it.
  • This is the last unit of the year. Make it a little party: cheer for everything your child can say.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. What's Teddy in? (5 min): put Teddy in the car: "What's Teddy in?" "A car!" Then the boat, the train, the plane. Your child moves Teddy and asks you.
  3. Watch (2 min): If You're Happy And You Know It. Clap and stamp with the song.
  4. Happy or sad? (5 min): Teddy's car goes: "Teddy is happy!" (big smile). The car stops: "Oh dear. Teddy is sad." (sad face). Hold up the faces: "Happy or sad?" Sentences
  5. Watch (2 min): Feelings Song – I'm Happy. Make each face.
  6. Chair train (5 min): line up the chairs. Teddy, the toys and your child get on the train. "Go! … Stop! … Go!" At every stop: "Are you happy or sad?"
  7. Show Teddy (6 min): ask the five Review questions, then "Is Teddy happy or sad?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a big sticker: well done!

Say this: "What's Teddy in? A car!" … "Teddy is happy! … Oh dear, Teddy is sad." … "Happy or sad?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a bike, a boat, a bus, a car, a plane and a train, and say at least three of them.
  • Answer "What number is the car?" and "What's Teddy in?" with a word.
  • Say "happy" and "sad", and show a happy face and a sad face.
