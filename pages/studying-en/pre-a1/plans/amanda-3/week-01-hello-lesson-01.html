Week 1 · Lesson 1: Colours and Shapes

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Colours, Shapes and Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Colours and shapes Lesson 2 · What colour is number 3? Lesson 3 · Treasure hunt

You Need

  • Crayons in ten colours (black, blue, brown, green, orange, pink, purple, red, white, yellow), paper shapes (circles, squares, triangles) in different colours, family photos, paper.
  • This week brings back the last two years: colours, shapes, numbers 1–6 and "Who's this?". New learners can join here too.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs). "Hello! What's your name? How old are you?"
  2. Who's this? (4 min): family photos. "Who's this?" "It's my granny!" Your child answers, then asks you.
  3. Colour parade (5 min): hold up each crayon: "What colour is it?" Then lay them in a line and ask your child to find one: "Where's purple? … Where's brown?" Colour words
  4. Watch (2 min): If It's Yellow and You Know It. Hold up the right crayon.
  5. Shape and colour (5 min): hold up a paper shape: "What shape is it?" "A triangle!" "What colour is it?" "It's green!" Then: "It's a green triangle!" Shape sentences
  6. Watch and draw (3 min): I Like To Draw. Draw each shape as you sing.
  7. Draw for me (5 min): "Draw a red circle! … a blue square! … a yellow triangle!" Then your child gives you the orders.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What shape is it? What colour is it?" … "It's a green triangle!" … "Who's this?"
