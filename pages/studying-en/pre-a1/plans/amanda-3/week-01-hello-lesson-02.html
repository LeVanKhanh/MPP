Week 1 · Lesson 2: What Colour Is Number 3?

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Colours and shapes Lesson 2 · What colour is number 3? Lesson 3 · Treasure hunt

You Need

  • Six number cards (1–6), each written in a different colour, six small toys, and the paper shapes from Lesson 1.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Count to six (4 min): "One, two, three, four, five, six!" on your fingers, then with six toys, touching each one. Numbers
  3. Watch and move (2 min): Count And Move. One action for each number.
  4. Coloured numbers (6 min): lay out the cards. "What colour is number three?" "It's red!" Then the other way: "What number is blue?" "Five!" Take turns asking. Number sentences
  5. Watch (3 min): Five Little Ducks. Put a finger down each time a duck goes away.
  6. Number jump (5 min): put the cards on the floor. "Jump to number four! … Jump to green!" Faster and faster.
  7. How many? (5 min): put out shapes: "How many triangles?" "Four!" "How many red circles?" "Two!" Count by touching each one.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour is number three? It's red!" … "What number is blue? Five!" … "How many triangles?"
