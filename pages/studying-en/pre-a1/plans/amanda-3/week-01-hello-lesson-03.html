Week 1 · Lesson 3: Treasure Hunt

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Family, Colours and Shapes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Colours and shapes Lesson 2 · What colour is number 3? Lesson 3 · Treasure hunt

You Need

  • Family photos and a sheet of paper to cover them. Coloured paper shapes with a number 1–6 written on each, hidden around the room before the lesson. Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Photo peekaboo (4 min): cover a family photo and pull the paper away slowly. "Who's this? Is it Grandad?" "Yes! It's Grandad!"
  3. Watch and fingers (2 min): Finger Family.
  4. Treasure hunt (6 min): your child finds the hidden shapes. For each one: "What shape is it? What colour is it? What number is it?" "A blue square! Number four!"
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): What's Your Favourite Colour? Sing your own colour.
  6. You are the teacher (5 min): your child holds up the treasures and asks you "What colour is it? What shape is it? How many?" You answer, sometimes wrong on purpose.
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): a few colour and shape review questions, then "Who's this?" with one photo. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What shape is it? What colour is it? What number is it?" … "A blue square! Number four!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name at least six of the ten colours and the circle, square and triangle.
  • Count to six and answer "What colour is number three?" and "What number is red?".
  • Answer "Who's this?" about family photos.
