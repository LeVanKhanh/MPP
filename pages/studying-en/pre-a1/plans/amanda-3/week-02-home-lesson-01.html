Week 2 · Lesson 1: Rooms of the House

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 1: Home.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 2: Home | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms of the house Lesson 2 · Seven, eight, nine, ten Lesson 3 · Goldilocks

You Need

  • Your home, Teddy, paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. House tour (5 min): walk from room to room: "This is the kitchen! … This is the bathroom! … the bedroom! … the living room!" Your child says the name at the door. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Learn Rooms of the House Song. Do the actions for each room.
  4. Where's Teddy? (5 min): hide Teddy in a room. "Where's Teddy? Is he in the kitchen?" Your child finds him: "He's in the bedroom!" Then swap. Sentences
  5. Watch (2 min): Where are you? In the kitchen. Sing the answers.
  6. What do we do here? (5 min): act it out: "I sleep in the bedroom. I eat in the kitchen. I watch TV in the living room. I have a bath in the bathroom." Your child guesses the room from your action.
  7. Draw a house (4 min): draw a house with four rooms. "Draw a bed in the bedroom! … a bath in the bathroom!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is the kitchen!" … "Where's Teddy? He's in the bedroom!" … "I sleep in the bedroom."
