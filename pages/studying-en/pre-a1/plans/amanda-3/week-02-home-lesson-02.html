Week 2 · Lesson 2: Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and At Home. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 1: Home.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 2: Home | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms of the house Lesson 2 · Seven, eight, nine, ten Lesson 3 · Goldilocks

You Need

  • Ten number cards (1–10), each written in a different colour, and ten small toys.
  • Numbers 1–6 came last year; today adds 7, 8, 9 and 10. Always touch one thing for each number.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Rooms again (3 min): "Where's the sofa?" "In the living room!" "Where's the bath?" "In the bathroom!"
  3. Count to ten (5 min): on your fingers, then with ten toys, touching each one: "…seven, eight, nine, ten. Ten!" Show the new cards: "What number is it? It's eight!" Numbers
  4. Watch (3 min): Counting 1–10 Song. Hold up a finger for every number.
  5. Count the house (5 min): walk around: "How many chairs? … How many windows? … How many doors?" Count together, touching each one.
  6. Coloured numbers (5 min): lay out the cards. "What colour is number ten?" "It's purple!" "What number is blue?" "Seven!" Take turns.
  7. Watch and move (2 min): Count And Move. One action for each number.
  8. Number jump and goodbye (4 min): cards on the floor: "Jump to number nine!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Seven, eight, nine, ten. Ten!" … "How many windows?" … "What colour is number ten?"
