Week 2 · Lesson 3: Goldilocks

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 1: Home.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 2: Home | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms of the house Lesson 2 · Seven, eight, nine, ten Lesson 3 · Goldilocks

You Need

  • Three teddies (big, small and a baby), three cups, three cushions for chairs, three towels for beds, and a doll for Goldilocks.
  • Goldilocks is a very old story that everyone can tell. Tell it in your own simple words with the toys; the English lines below are enough.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch the story (6 min): Goldilocks And The Three Bears. Pause and ask "Where's Goldilocks? Is she in the kitchen?"
  3. Tell it with toys (7 min): the three bears: "A big bear, a small bear and a baby bear." In the kitchen, Goldilocks tries the cups: "This is hot! … This is cold! … Mmm, this is good!" In the living room, the chairs. In the bedroom, the beds: "Where's Goldilocks? She's in the bedroom!" Then your child moves the toys and you say the lines together.
  4. Big or little? (4 min): hold up two teddies, two cups, two shoes: "Is it big or little?" (Little is the course word; the Starters list says small. Say both.)
  5. Hide and seek (5 min): your child hides in a room. "Where are you? Are you in the kitchen? … Are you in the bathroom?" Then swap.
  6. Show Teddy (3 min): ask a few Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "This is hot! This is cold! Mmm, this is good!" … "Where's Goldilocks? She's in the bedroom!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and living room, and answer "Where's Teddy?" with "He's in the…".
  • Count to ten, touching one thing for each number, and say 7, 8, 9 and 10.
  • Join in the Goldilocks story with "hot", "cold", "big" and "little".
