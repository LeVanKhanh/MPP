Week 3 · Lesson 1: At the Park

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 2: Toys.

You Need

  • Best of all: a real playground. At home: Teddy, a doll, books or a cushion for a slide, a ruler on a block for a seesaw, and a scarf tied to a chair for a swing.
  • Slide, seesaw, swing and springer are course words; up and down are Movers words. All are easy to learn by doing them.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Park words (5 min): at the playground or with pictures: "Look! It's a slide! … It's a swing! … It's a seesaw!" Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Playground – Let's Play! (English Singsing). Point when you hear "slide" and "swing".
  4. Up and down (4 min): "Up!" (stretch up high) "Down!" (crouch down low). Faster and slower, then your child calls it.
  5. What's Teddy on? (5 min): put Teddy on the slide, the swing, the seesaw. "What's Teddy on?" "The slide!" Then "He's on the slide!" Sentences
  6. Watch and move (2 min): Up and Down. Move with the song.
  7. Draw a playground (5 min): "Draw a slide! Colour it red. Draw a swing! Colour it blue."
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Bye-bye, slide! Bye-bye, swing!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a slide!" … "What's Teddy on? He's on the swing!" … "Up! Down!"
