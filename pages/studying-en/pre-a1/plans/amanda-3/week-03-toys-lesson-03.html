Week 3 · Lesson 3: Happy at the Park

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Toys and My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 2: Toys.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 3: Toys | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · At the park Lesson 2 · Up or down? Lesson 3 · Happy at the park

You Need

  • The toy park (or a trip to a real one), the toys in a bag, Teddy, paper with a happy face and a sad face.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): a toy in the bag. "Is this a ball? … Is it a car?" Then take it out and say its colour.
  3. Watch (3 min): Let's Take Turns. Who is happy? Who is sad?
  4. Park play (6 min): at the playground, or with the toys: "Go up the slide! … Down you go!" "Swing up! Swing down!" Your child says the words as they play.
  5. Happy or sad? (4 min): Teddy wants the swing but the doll is on it: "Oh dear. Teddy is sad." Then it's Teddy's turn: "Teddy is happy!" Hold up the faces: "Happy or sad?"
  6. I like…! Me too! (3 min): "I like the slide!" Your child answers "Me too!" or "I like the swing!" Take turns.
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask a few Review questions, then "What's Teddy on?" and "Is Teddy up or down?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is Teddy happy or sad?" … "I like the slide! – Me too!" … "What's Teddy on?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a slide, a swing and a seesaw, and say at least two of them.
  • Answer "What's Teddy on?" and "Is Teddy up or down?".
  • Say if a toy or a person is happy or sad, and answer "I like…" with "Me too!".
