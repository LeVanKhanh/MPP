Week 4 · Lesson 1: Feet, Fingers, Hair, Teeth

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Feet, fingers, hair, teeth Lesson 2 · Long or short? Lesson 3 · What big eyes!

You Need

  • A mirror, paper and crayons, Teddy.
  • Feet and hair are Starters words; teeth is a Movers word and fingers a Flyers word. All are easy to learn by pointing.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Teddy says (3 min): "Teddy says: touch your head! … your arms! … your legs! … your nose!" Words from earlier years.
  3. New words (5 min): in front of the mirror: "These are my fingers! These are my feet! This is my hair! These are my teeth!" Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  4. Watch (2 min): Ten Little Fingers. Count your fingers with the song.
  5. How many? (5 min): "How many fingers? … Ten!" "How many feet? Two!" "How many legs has a dog? Four!" Count by touching each one.
  6. Watch (2 min): Brush Your Teeth. Show your teeth and pretend to brush.
  7. Hand and foot prints (6 min): draw around a hand and a foot. "Count the fingers! One, two… five!" Colour them.
  8. Goodbye (4 min): wave your fingers: "Bye-bye!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "These are my fingers! These are my teeth!" … "How many fingers? Ten!"
