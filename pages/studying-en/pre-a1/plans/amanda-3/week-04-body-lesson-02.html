Week 4 · Lesson 2: Long or Short?

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Opposites and My Body. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Feet, fingers, hair, teeth Lesson 2 · Long or short? Lesson 3 · What big eyes!

You Need

  • A long pencil and a short pencil, a long and a short ribbon or string, play dough or paper strips, family photos, crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Body again (3 min): "Show me your fingers! … your feet! … your teeth!"
  3. Long and short (5 min): hold up the pencils: "This pencil is long. This pencil is short." Arms wide for long, hands close for short. Then hair in the photos: "Mummy's hair is long! Daddy's hair is short!" Opposites
  4. Watch (2 min): Long Short Opposites Song. Do the arm actions.
  5. Long or short? (5 min): make snakes from play dough or paper: "Is it long or short?" Your child sorts them into a long group and a short group. Sentences
  6. Big and small (4 min): compare hands and feet: "My hands are big. Your hands are small!" (Preschool books also say little.)
  7. Watch (3 min): Adjectives and Opposites Song. Sing the opposite.
  8. Draw and goodbye (5 min): "Draw a girl with long hair! Draw a boy with short hair!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This pencil is long. This pencil is short." … "Is it long or short?"
