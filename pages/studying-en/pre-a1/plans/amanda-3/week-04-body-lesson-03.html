Week 4 · Lesson 3: What Big Eyes!

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: My Body, Fruit and Vegetables and At Home. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 3: Body.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 4: Body | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Feet, fingers, hair, teeth Lesson 2 · Long or short? Lesson 3 · What big eyes!

You Need

  • A red hat or scarf, a bag with an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear, a doll (Grandma) and a toy wolf or dog, Teddy.
  • Little Red Riding Hood is a very old story that everyone can tell. Tell it in your own simple words with the toys; the English lines below are enough.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch the story (6 min): Little Red Riding Hood. Point to your own eyes, ears and teeth when the girl talks about Grandma's.
  3. What big eyes! (6 min): you are "Grandma" in bed. Your child says the famous lines: "Oh, Grandma! What big eyes! … What big ears! … What big teeth!" and points to each one. Then swap.
  4. Fruit for Grandma (4 min): put the fruit in the bag together: "An apple, a banana… How many? Four!" Then take them out: "What's this?"
  5. Red hat, red dress (4 min): your child puts on the red hat: "She's wearing a red hat!" Then "What colour is your dress / T-shirt?"
  6. Where's Grandma? (3 min): hide the doll in a room. "Where's Grandma? Is she in the bedroom? … in the bathroom?"
  7. Show Teddy (2 min): "How many fingers?" "Is your hair long or short?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Oh, Grandma! What big eyes! What big ears! What big teeth!" … "Where's Grandma? Is she in the bedroom?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to their feet, fingers, hair and teeth, and say at least two of them.
  • Answer "How many fingers?" and "Is it long or short?".
  • Join in "What big eyes! What big ears! What big teeth!" in the story.
