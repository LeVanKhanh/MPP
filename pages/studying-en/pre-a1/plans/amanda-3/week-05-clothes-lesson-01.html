Week 5 · Lesson 1: Can You See the Skirt?

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Can you see the skirt? Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • A box of clothes: boots, a scarf, shorts, a skirt, socks, a T-shirt, plus last year's dress, hat, jumper, shoes and trousers. Teddy.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Clothes box (5 min): take out the new things: "Look! Boots! … A scarf! … A skirt!" Then last year's: "What's this?" "A jumper!" Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Learn Clothes Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards. Hold up each thing.
  4. Can you see…? (5 min): put the clothes around the room. "Can you see the skirt?" "Yes, I can!" "Can you see the boots?" "No, I can't!" Then your child asks you. Sentences
  5. What colour? (5 min): "What colour is the scarf? It's red!" "What colour are the socks? They're white!"
  6. Watch (3 min): This Is The Way We Get Dressed. Act out each line.
  7. Dress Teddy (4 min): "Put on Teddy's scarf! … Put on his boots!" Then your child gives the orders.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Can you see the skirt? Yes, I can!" … "What colour is the scarf?"
