Week 5 · Lesson 2: In, On, Under

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and Clothes. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Can you see the skirt? Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • A box, a toy mouse (or any small toy), a table, the clothes box, four socks.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Clothes again (3 min): "What's this?" for the boots, scarf, skirt and socks. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. In, on, under (5 min): move the mouse: "The mouse is in the box! … on the box! … under the box!" Your child moves it as you say the word. Word list
  4. Watch (2 min): On, In, Under, By. Move the mouse with the song.
  5. Where's the mouse? (5 min): hide the mouse while your child closes their eyes. "Where's the mouse? Is it under the table?" Your child finds it: "It's on the chair!" Then swap. Sentences
  6. Watch (2 min): Where is it? Answer before the singer.
  7. Where are the socks? (5 min): hide four socks: one in the box, one on the chair, one under the table, one under the bed. Your child finds them: "It's under the table!" "How many socks? Four!"
  8. Goodbye (5 min): put everything back: "Socks in the box!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "The mouse is in the box! On the box! Under the box!" … "Where's the mouse?"
