Week 5 · Lesson 3: Dress-up Day

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Clothes and In, On, Under. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 4: Clothes.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 5: Clothes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Can you see the skirt? Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Dress-up day

You Need

  • Dress-up things you already have: a hat (pirate), a dress or crown (princess), a scarf as a cape (superhero), a white T-shirt (snowman), a toy bag (doctor). A toy car, a table and a box, Teddy.
  • Doctor and pirate are Movers words, snowman is a Flyers word, and princess and superhero are course words. At a dress-up party they are easy to learn.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Let's Get Dressed Song. Point to your own clothes.
  3. Dress-up show (7 min): your child dresses up and says who they are: "I'm a pirate! … I'm a superhero! … I'm a princess!" You clap and say "Wow! You're a doctor!" Then you dress up too.
  4. Who are you? (4 min): you dress up behind a door. Your child guesses: "Are you a pirate?" "No! I'm a snowman!"
  5. Where's Teddy? (4 min): Teddy dresses up too and hides: in the car, on the table, under the box. "Where's Teddy? He's in the car!" Count the boxes: "One, two, three, four!"
  6. Thank you! (3 min): give each other dress-up things: "Here you are!" "Thank you!"
  7. Show Teddy (4 min): a few clothes and in, on, under review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I'm a pirate! … Are you a superhero?" … "Where's Teddy? He's in the car!" … "Here you are! – Thank you!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name boots, a scarf, shorts, a skirt, socks and a T-shirt, and answer "Can you see the…?".
  • Put a toy in, on or under something and answer "Where's the…?".
  • Dress up and say "I'm a pirate / a doctor / a superhero!".
