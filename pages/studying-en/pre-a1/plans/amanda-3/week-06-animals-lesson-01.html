Week 6 · Lesson 1: Wild Animals

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Wild animals Lesson 2 · Fast or slow? Lesson 3 · The lion and the mouse

You Need

  • Toys or pictures of an elephant, a lion, a monkey and a snake, plus the farm animals and pets from earlier years. Paper and crayons.
  • Elephant, monkey and snake are Starters words; lion is a Movers word.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Wild animals (5 min): one by one: "Look! It's a lion!" Then the elephant, the monkey and the snake. Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (5 min): Wild Animals at the Zoo (English Singsing). Point before the voice says the word.
  4. Animal walk (4 min): "Walk like an elephant! … Jump like a monkey! … Be a snake! Sss!" Then your child calls out an animal.
  5. How many? (5 min): put out the animals in groups. "How many elephants are there? Three!" Count up to ten with all of them. Sentences
  6. Old friends (3 min): the cat, cow, dog, fish, horse, mouse, pig and sheep: "What's this?" and make its noise.
  7. Draw (3 min): draw a long snake and colour it: "Is it long or short? Long!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a lion!" … "How many elephants are there?" … "Jump like a monkey!"
