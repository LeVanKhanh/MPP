Week 6 · Lesson 2: Fast or Slow?

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Animals and Opposites. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Wild animals Lesson 2 · Fast or slow? Lesson 3 · The lion and the mouse

You Need

  • The animal toys, a toy car, pictures of a snail and a fly.
  • Slow, snail and fly (the insect) are Movers words; fast is a Flyers word. Show them with your body and they are easy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Animals again (3 min): "What's this?" for the lion, elephant, monkey and snake.
  3. Fast and slow (5 min): "A snail is slow." (walk very slowly) "A horse is fast!" (run on the spot) Then the fly: "A fly is fast!" Opposites
  4. Watch and move (4 min): Fast Slow Action Song. Move fast and slow with the song.
  5. Race! (5 min): race two toys across the floor: "The car is fast! The snail is slow!" Then hold up animals: "Is it fast or slow?"
  6. Big and little (4 min): "The elephant is big. The mouse is little." Your child finds a big animal and a little animal. (Little is the course word; the Starters list says small.)
  7. Guess the animal (4 min): Wild Animals – What Is It? Say "Is it a lion?" before the picture shows.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, very slowly, then very fast!

Say this: "A snail is slow. A horse is fast!" … "Is it fast or slow?"
