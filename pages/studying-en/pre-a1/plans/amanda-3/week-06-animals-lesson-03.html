Week 6 · Lesson 3: The Lion and the Mouse

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Animals and Toys. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 5: Animals.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 6: Animals | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Wild animals Lesson 2 · Fast or slow? Lesson 3 · The lion and the mouse

You Need

  • A big toy (the lion) and a small toy (the mouse), a scarf or string (the net), a ball and a few toys, Teddy.
  • The Lion and the Mouse is a very old fable that everyone can tell. Tell it in your own simple words with the toys; the English lines below are enough. Help is a Movers word, and it is the heart of this story.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch the story (5 min): The Lion and The Mouse. Pause and ask: "Who is big? Who is little?"
  3. Tell it with toys (7 min): the lion sleeps; the mouse runs on him. "Grrr!" "Sorry, Lion! I can help you one day!" Later the lion is in the net: "Oh dear!" The mouse comes: "I can help you!" Your child moves the mouse and says the lines.
  4. Big and little (4 min): "The lion is big. The mouse is little." Then other toys: "Is it big or little?"
  5. Happy or sad? (3 min): "The lion is in the net. Is he happy or sad?" "Sad!" The mouse helps him. "Look! Is he happy or sad now?" "Happy!"
  6. What's Teddy playing with? (4 min): give Teddy a toy: "What's Teddy playing with?" "A ball!" Then a car, a doll, a book.
  7. Show Teddy (2 min): a few Review questions, then "Is a snail fast or slow?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The lion is big. The mouse is little." … "I can help you!" … "What's Teddy playing with?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name an elephant, a lion, a monkey and a snake, and answer "How many elephants are there?".
  • Say if an animal is fast or slow, big or little.
  • Join in the lion and mouse story with "I can help you!" and say if the lion is happy or sad.
