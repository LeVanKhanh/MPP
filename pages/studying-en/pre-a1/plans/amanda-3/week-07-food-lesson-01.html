Week 7 · Lesson 1: Eat or Drink?

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 6: Food.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 7: Food | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Eat or drink? Lesson 2 · Hungry, thirsty, good for you Lesson 3 · Teddy's café

You Need

  • Real food and drinks, or pictures: a sandwich, a piece of cake, a cup of water, a cup of juice, some fruit, a few sweets. Paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops. About 7 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Food table (5 min): one by one: "Look! It's a sandwich! … It's cake! … It's water! … It's juice!" Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Learn Food Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards. Say each food first.
  4. Eat or drink? (5 min): hold up each thing: "Do we eat it or drink it?" Your child mimes eating or drinking. Put them in two groups: "We eat cake. We drink juice."
  5. Do you like…? (5 min): "Do you like sandwiches?" "Yes, I do!" "Do you like water?" Take turns asking. Sentences
  6. Watch (3 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream? Shout the answers.
  7. Draw a table (4 min): draw a table with food on it: "Draw a big cake! … two sandwiches! … a cup of juice!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Do we eat it or drink it?" … "We eat cake. We drink juice." … "Do you like sandwiches?"
