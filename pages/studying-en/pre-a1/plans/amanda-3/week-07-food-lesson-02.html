Week 7 · Lesson 2: Hungry, Thirsty, Good for You

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 6: Food.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 7: Food | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Eat or drink? Lesson 2 · Hungry, thirsty, good for you Lesson 3 · Teddy's café

You Need

  • The food from Lesson 1, pictures of vegetables and crisps, eight sweets (or eight small toys), two boxes, Teddy.
  • Hungry, thirsty, vegetables and bad are Movers words. Act them out: rub your tummy for hungry, touch your throat for thirsty.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Food again (3 min): "What's this?" for each food and drink.
  3. Hungry and thirsty (5 min): rub your tummy: "I'm hungry!" Touch your throat: "I'm thirsty!" Then Teddy: "Is Teddy hungry or thirsty?" Your child decides and gives Teddy a sandwich or water.
  4. Watch (2 min): Are You Hungry? Rub your tummy and sing.
  5. Good for you? (5 min): two boxes. "Fruit is good for you!" "Lots of sweets are bad for you." Your child sorts the pictures: fruit, vegetables, water, crisps, sweets, cake.
  6. Watch (3 min): Healthy Food Vs Junk Food Song. Thumbs up or thumbs down.
  7. Count the sweets (5 min): "How many sweets? One, two… eight! Eight sweets! Oh dear, too many!" Then fruit: "How many apples?" Numbers
  8. Goodbye (4 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm hungry! … I'm thirsty!" … "Is Teddy hungry or thirsty?" … "Fruit is good for you!"
