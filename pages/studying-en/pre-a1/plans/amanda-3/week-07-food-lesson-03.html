Week 7 · Lesson 3: Teddy's Café

30 minutes for young learners (4–6). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Amanda and Friends 3 – Unit 6: Food.

Amanda and Friends 3 · Week 7: Food | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Eat or drink? Lesson 2 · Hungry, thirsty, good for you Lesson 3 · Teddy's café

You Need

  • Real or toy food and drinks on a table (the café), an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear in a bag, Teddy and a few dolls as customers.
  • This is the last unit of the year. Make it a little party: cheer for everything your child can say.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): fruit in the bag. "Is it an apple? … Is it a pear?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a banana!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Do you like…? Yes or No (English Singsing). Say the answers with the children in the video.
  4. Teddy's café (7 min): your child is the waiter. Teddy comes in: "I'm hungry! Can I have a sandwich, please?" "Here you are!" "Thank you!" The dolls ask for juice, cake and fruit. Then you are the waiter. When someone says "I like cake!", the others say "Me too!"
  5. Fast or slow? (3 min): eat a pretend sandwich slowly like a snail, then fast like a horse. "Is Teddy fast or slow?"
  6. Happy or sad? (3 min): "Oh dear! Teddy's cake is on the floor! Is he happy or sad?" "Sad!" "Here's some fruit!" "Happy!"
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a big sticker: well done!

Say this: "I'm hungry! Can I have a sandwich, please?" … "Here you are!" … "I like cake! – Me too!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name cake, juice, a sandwich and water, and say if we eat it or drink it.
  • Say "I'm hungry!" or "I'm thirsty!" and ask "Can I have…, please?".
  • Answer "Do you like…?" and say if a food is good for you.
