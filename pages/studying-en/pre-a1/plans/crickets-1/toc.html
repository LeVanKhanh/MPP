Crickets 1

Home plans that go with the 8 units of Richmond Crickets 1, a preschool English course built on big questions about the child's world. One unit a week, three short lessons each.

Course plan | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters | Ages about 3–5

How These Plans Work

  • One unit, one week. Each unit asks a big question ("Why is school important?") and four smaller ones. At home they become three 30-minute lessons: first words, then words with a sentence pattern, then a review with a mini check.
  • Core words first. The book uses many words. Each week picks the ones your child can see, hold or act out, most of them from the Pre A1 Starters list; the rest are for listening only.
  • Learn with real things. Toys, food, clothes and walks outside come before screens. Video is at most about 10 minutes a lesson, watched and sung together.
  • Listening first. Pointing, doing the action or saying one word is success. "Yes, I can" and "I like…" come slowly; never turn the lesson into a test.
  • Use the book in class. These lessons add songs, videos and games with the same topic; they do not copy the book's pages, stories or characters.
  • Every lesson links to a shared lesson page (like School or Jobs) where you can tap a word to hear it and watch checked videos.
  • Before this book: Crickets Nursery. After it: Amanda and Friends 2–3 or Fun for Starters.
