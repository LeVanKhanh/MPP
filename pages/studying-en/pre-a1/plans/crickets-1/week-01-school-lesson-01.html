Week 1 · Lesson 1: Hello, Friends!

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, In, On, Under and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 1: Why is school important?

Lesson 1 · Hello, friends Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Classroom rules

You Need

  • Two teddies or puppets, family photos, paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Wave on every "hello".
  2. Teddies meet (4 min): Teddy and a puppet meet: "Hello! I'm Teddy. What's your name?" – "My name is Bunny." Then Teddy asks your child. Hello sentences
  3. Watch (2 min): What Is Your Name? – Greetings Song. Say your name with the song.
  4. Boy or girl? (4 min): Point to photos: "He's a boy. She's a girl." Then "Are you a boy?" – "No, I'm a girl!" or "Yes!"
  5. How are you? (4 min): "How are you?" – "Fine, thank you!" Then show faces: "I'm happy! I'm sad." "Are you happy?" Feelings words
  6. Watch and move (3 min): Feelings and Emotions Song for Kids. Make each face.
  7. Say hello around the world (5 min): Try different hellos with the teddies: shake hands, bow, wave, rub noses. Each time: "Hello!" Then draw a friend with a happy face.
  8. Goodbye (5 min): Each teddy says "Goodbye!", shakes hands and waves. Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Hello! What's your name?" – "My name is…" … "How are you?" – "Fine, thank you!"
