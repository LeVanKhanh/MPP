Week 1 · Lesson 2: In, On, Under the Box

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, In, On, Under and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 1: Why is school important?

Crickets 1 · Week 1: School | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello, friends Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Classroom rules

You Need

  • A school bag or backpack with a book, crayons and pencils, a box, a table.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What's your name?" to warm up.
  2. What's in my bag? (5 min): Take things out: "It's a book. It's a crayon. It's a pencil." Then point: "This is the door. This is the window." Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): School Supplies [Kids Vocab] What's in My Bag?. Find each thing in your own bag.
  4. Open and closed (3 min): "Open the door! Close the door!" "The window is open. Now it's closed."
  5. Where's the crayon? (5 min): Put the crayon in, on, then under the box: "Where's the crayon? It's under the box!" Your child moves it as you say. Position words
  6. Watch (2 min): Where is it?. Point to where the thing is.
  7. What's it for? (5 min): Use each thing: "A crayon is for drawing. Scissors are for cutting." Draw with the crayon, then cut paper together (child-safe scissors). Red things: "What is red? An apple! A strawberry!"
  8. Goodbye (4 min): "Put the pencil in the bag!" Pack up, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where's the crayon?" – "It's under the box!" … "Is it a pencil? Yes, it is."
