Week 1 · Lesson 3: Stand Up, Sit Down

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, In, On, Under and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 1: Why is school important?

Crickets 1 · Week 1: School | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello, friends Lesson 2 · In, on, under Lesson 3 · Classroom rules

You Need

  • The school things, a chair, three crayons, three books, Teddy, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Where's Teddy?" – hide him in, on or under something.
  2. Teacher says (5 min): You are the teacher: "Stand up! Sit down! Line up! Raise your hand!" Then your child is the teacher and you follow.
  3. Watch and move (3 min): Stand Up, Sit Down – Actions Song. Do every action.
  4. Tidy the classroom (5 min): "Pick up the crayon. Put away the crayon. Close your book. Push in your chair." Your child does each job.
  5. How many? (4 min): "How many crayons are there? One, two, three!" Then books and pencils. "Please pass the crayon. Thank you!"
  6. Watch (2 min): Do You Have A Crayon? – School Supplies Song. Hold up a crayon when you hear it.
  7. Mini check (5 min): Ask the Review questions with the real things. Teddy gets a turn too. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Stand up! Sit down! Raise your hand, please." … "How many crayons are there?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say "Hello!", answer "What's your name?" and say "I'm a boy" or "I'm a girl".
  • Point to and name a book, a crayon, a pencil, a box, the door and the window.
  • Put a crayon in, on or under the box, and follow "Stand up", "Sit down" and "Open your book".
