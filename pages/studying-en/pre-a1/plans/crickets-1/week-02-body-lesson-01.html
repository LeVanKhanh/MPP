Week 2 · Lesson 1: My Face

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body, Feelings and Actions. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 2: What makes me special?

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · My body Lesson 3 · I can…

You Need

  • A mirror, paper plates or paper, crayons in black, brown, blue and green.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Stand up! Sit down!" from last week.
  2. In the mirror (5 min): "This is my face. This is my nose. This is my hair. It's black." Your child copies in the mirror. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Face [Kids Vocab] My Face Parts. Touch each part.
  4. Touch your… (4 min): "Touch your nose! Touch your ears! Open your mouth! Show me your face!"
  5. My eyes are… (4 min): Look in the mirror: "My eyes are brown. My hair is black." Find the same colour crayon. Colour words
  6. Watch and sing (3 min): This Is My Face – Parts of the Face Song. Point with the song.
  7. Silly puppet face (6 min): Make a silly face on a paper plate: "He has two mouths! He has three green eyes!" Your child tells you what to draw.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is my nose." … "My eyes are brown. What colour are your eyes?"
