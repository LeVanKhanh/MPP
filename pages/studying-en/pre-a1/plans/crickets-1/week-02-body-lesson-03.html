Week 2 · Lesson 3: I Can Smile, I Can Jump

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body, Feelings and Actions. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 2: What makes me special?

Crickets 1 · Week 2: My Body | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · My body Lesson 3 · I can…

You Need

  • Feelings faces drawn on paper (happy, sad, angry), Teddy, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Point: "What's this? My arm!"
  2. Happy, sad, angry (4 min): Show the faces: "I'm happy. I can smile. I'm sad. I can cry. I'm angry!" Your child copies each face. Feelings words
  3. Watch (3 min): Feelings Song – I'm Happy. Make each face with the song.
  4. Can you…? (5 min): "Can you jump? Can you clap? Can you wave?" Your child does it: "Yes, I can!" Ask a silly one: "Can you fly?" – "No, I can't!" Action words
  5. Watch and move (3 min): I Can Jump! | Actions Song for Kids. Do every action.
  6. Can an elephant jump? (4 min): "Can an elephant jump? No, it can't! Can a kangaroo jump? Yes, it can!" Act each animal.
  7. Mini check (5 min): Ask the Review questions, then "Can you run to the door?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker on a hand.

Say this: "Can you jump?" – "Yes, I can!" … "Can you fly?" – "No, I can't!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to and name parts of the face and body, and say "My eyes are…" with a colour.
  • Show happy, sad and angry faces and say "I'm happy".
  • Answer "Can you jump?" with "Yes, I can" or "No, I can't", and do the action.
