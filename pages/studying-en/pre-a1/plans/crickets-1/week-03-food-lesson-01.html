Week 3 · Lesson 1: What Food Is This?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks, Fruit and Vegetables and Shapes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 3: Why is healthy food good for my body?

Crickets 1 · Week 3: Healthy Food | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Food and drink Lesson 2 · Colour and shape Lesson 3 · Good food, I like…

You Need

  • Real food from the kitchen: an apple, a banana, an orange, a carrot, peas in a pod if possible, bread, a cup of milk, juice and water.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Can you jump?" from last week.
  2. Kitchen visit (5 min): In the kitchen: "This is an apple. This is a carrot. This is milk." Your child holds each one. Food words
  3. Watch (3 min): Learn Food Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards. Hold up your real food when it comes.
  4. Food or drink? (4 min): "A sandwich is food. Milk is a drink." Put food on one plate and drinks on the other. "Can you eat juice? No!"
  5. Where does it grow? (4 min): "Oranges are on a tree. Carrots are in the ground. Peas are in a pod." Open a pea pod together if you have one.
  6. Watch (2 min): Drinks [Kids Vocab] Thirsty!. Drink some water with the video.
  7. Shopping bag (6 min): "A banana and water, please." Your child puts them in a bag. Swap. "Thank you!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this? It's a carrot." … "Is milk food or a drink?" – "A drink!"
