Week 3 · Lesson 2: Colours and Shapes of Food

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks, Fruit and Vegetables and Shapes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 3: Why is healthy food good for my body?

Crickets 1 · Week 3: Healthy Food | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Food and drink Lesson 2 · Colour and shape Lesson 3 · Good food, I like…

You Need

  • A lunch box, a plate, fruit (bananas, an orange, a watermelon slice if possible), peas or grapes, a sandwich or bread cut in a square and triangles.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Is it food or a drink?" with a cup.
  2. Food colours (4 min): "What colour are the peas? They're green. The banana is yellow. The watermelon is pink and green!" Colour words
  3. Watch (3 min): Fruit Song for Kids. Say the colour of each fruit.
  4. Food shapes (4 min): "The orange is round, like a circle. The bread is a square. Now it's a triangle!" Shape words
  5. Count the peas (4 min): "How many peas can you see? One, two, three, four, five!" Touch each one.
  6. Lunch box (5 min): Pack a lunch box together: "What is in your lunch box?" – "A sandwich, two yellow bananas and juice!"
  7. Watch (2 min): [Counting] How many apples? – Sing Along. Count with the song.
  8. Goodbye (5 min): Eat a little of the lunch box together: "Yum!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour are the peas? They're green." … "What is in your lunch box?"
