Week 3 · Lesson 3: I'm Hungry! I Like Eggs

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks, Fruit and Vegetables and Shapes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 3: Why is healthy food good for my body?

Crickets 1 · Week 3: Healthy Food | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Food and drink Lesson 2 · Colour and shape Lesson 3 · Good food, I like…

You Need

  • Food pictures or toy food including sweets/candy, an egg, cheese, yoghurt, bread; two plates; a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "How many peas?" with a few real peas.
  2. Good food (5 min): Two plates: good food (watermelon, peas, carrots, milk, water) and "sometimes" food (candy, ice cream). "Watermelon is good food! Candy isn't good for you."
  3. Watch (3 min): Healthy Food Vs Junk Food Song. Thumbs up for good food.
  4. Do you like…? (4 min): "Do you like eggs? Do you like cheese?" Thumbs up or "Yes!" Then "I like bread. I don't like…"
  5. Watch (2 min): Are You Hungry? – Super Simple Puppets. Rub your tummy on "hungry".
  6. Hungry, thirsty (4 min): Rub your tummy: "I'm hungry! I want a big sandwich, please." Hold your throat: "I'm thirsty. I want juice." Your child asks you for food.
  7. Mini check (6 min): Ask the Review questions with the real food. Then "What food do you like?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker.

Say this: "I'm hungry. I want a sandwich, please." … "Do you like eggs?" – "Yes!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name at least five foods and drinks and say if they are food or a drink.
  • Say the colour of a food and count up to five peas or pieces of fruit.
  • Say "I like…", answer "Do you like…?" and ask "I want…, please".
