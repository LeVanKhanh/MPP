Week 4 · Lesson 1: This Is My Family

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Animals and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 4: How are families alike and different?

Crickets 1 · Week 4: Family | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My family Lesson 2 · Happy with my family Lesson 3 · Pets and fun

You Need

  • Family photos, pictures of other families (books or magazines), paper and crayons.
  • In class the children say "Mom". The British word is "Mum"; use the class word at home.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Do you like eggs?" from last week.
  2. Our family (5 min): "This is my family. This is Mom. This is Dad. This is my brother." Your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Family [Kids Vocab] Meet My Family!. Point to the same person in your photo.
  4. Who is this? (4 min): Cover the photo and show one face: "Who is this?" – "Grandma!"
  5. Different families (4 min): Look at other family pictures: "This family is big. This family is small. This girl has two brothers. This boy has no brothers or sisters." Every family is a family.
  6. Watch and sing (2 min): Finger Family Song. Wiggle each finger.
  7. Draw my family (6 min): Your child draws or you draw together. "Who is this? How many people? Let's count!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is my family. This is Mom." … "Who is this?" – "Grandpa!"
