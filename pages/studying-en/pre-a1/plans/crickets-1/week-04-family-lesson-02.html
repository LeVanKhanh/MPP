Week 4 · Lesson 2: I'm Happy When…

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Animals and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 4: How are families alike and different?

Crickets 1 · Week 4: Family | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My family Lesson 2 · Happy with my family Lesson 3 · Pets and fun

You Need

  • A picture book to read together, music for dancing, the family drawing.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Who is this?" with the family drawing.
  2. I'm happy when… (5 min): Read a little of a picture book together: "Mom is reading to me. I'm happy!" "Grandma says goodbye. I'm sad." Make the faces. Feelings words
  3. Watch (3 min): The People In My Family. Point to your photo.
  4. Who can dance? (5 min): "My dad can dance. My mom can read. I can run! The baby can't read, but the baby can dance!" Act each one.
  5. Dance time (3 min): Put on music and dance together: "We can dance!"
  6. I love you (3 min): Give a hug: "I love you, Grandma!" Your child says it to a family member or a teddy.
  7. Picture (5 min): Draw one thing the family does together: reading, dancing, eating. "What are we doing? We're dancing!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm happy when Mom reads to me." … "My dad can dance. Can you dance?"
