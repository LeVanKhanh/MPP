Week 4 · Lesson 3: Pets and Fun Together

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Animals and Feelings. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 4: How are families alike and different?

Crickets 1 · Week 4: Family | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My family Lesson 2 · Happy with my family Lesson 3 · Pets and fun

You Need

  • Toy pets or pet pictures, a ball, books, a puzzle or a toy truck, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Can you dance?"
  2. Family pets (5 min): "This family has a dog. This family has a cat." "We love our pets. We play with our pets." Animal words
  3. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant for Children. Say the pet first.
  4. Look after the pet (4 min): With a toy pet: "Give it food. Give it water. Give it a hug!" Your child does each one.
  5. Fun together (5 min): Play together with a ball, a puzzle or books: "We all have fun together! What can you do with the books? We can read the books!"
  6. How many? (3 min): Count the toys: "How many balls are there? One, two, three!" Count up to six.
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the photos. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a family sticker.

Say this: "Come and play with me!" … "We all have fun together."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to people in a family photo and say "This is my…".
  • Say "I'm happy" about something they do with the family and say what someone can do ("Dad can dance").
  • Talk about a family pet or a game they play together, with a word or a short sentence.
