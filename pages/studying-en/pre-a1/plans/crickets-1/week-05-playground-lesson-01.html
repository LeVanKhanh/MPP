Week 5 · Lesson 1: In the Playground

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, Actions and Sport. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 5: How do I have fun?

Crickets 1 · Week 5: Playground | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The playground Lesson 2 · Playing games Lesson 3 · Being a friend

You Need

  • A trip to a playground if you can (or a playground picture), a ball.
  • Slide, swing and seesaw are not in the Starters list: fine to use, they are words your child sees.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "This is my family!" from last week.
  2. Playground words (5 min): At the playground or with a picture: "This is the slide. This is the swing. This is the seesaw." Toys and playground words
  3. Watch (3 min): Fun on the Playground – Slide, Swing, Seesaw. Point to each one.
  4. I like the slide (4 min): "I like the slide! What do you like?" Your child points or says "The swing!"
  5. I can… (5 min): With the ball: "I can kick a ball. I can throw. I can catch. I can run!" Your child does each one. Action words
  6. Watch and move (2 min): Action Verbs (v1) – Let's Move! Jump, Run, Dance. Do every action.
  7. Play time (5 min): Play on the playground (or with the ball). Talk as you play: "Up the ladder! Down the slide!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I like the slide!" … "I can kick a ball. What can you do?"
