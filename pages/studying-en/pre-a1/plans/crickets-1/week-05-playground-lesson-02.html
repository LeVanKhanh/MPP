Week 5 · Lesson 2: Are You Playing Soccer?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, Actions and Sport. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 5: How do I have fun?

Crickets 1 · Week 5: Playground | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The playground Lesson 2 · Playing games Lesson 3 · Being a friend

You Need

  • A ball, a toy car, a toy train and a toy plane, chalk or tape for hopscotch, a rope.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What can you do?" – "I can jump!"
  2. Games (4 min): "Let's play soccer! Let's play hide-and-seek!" Name each game as you start it. (In British English soccer is "football".) Sport words
  3. Soccer (4 min): Kick the ball to each other: "Are you playing soccer?" – "Yes!" "I'm playing soccer!"
  4. Watch (3 min): Sports Song – Do You Like Sports?. Act each sport.
  5. Hide-and-seek (5 min): Count "One, two, three, four, five, six!" Then "Where are you?" Swap.
  6. Big and small toys (3 min): "The plane is big. The car is small." Line up the toys from big to small.
  7. Hopscotch or jump the rope (5 min): Draw a hopscotch with chalk or jump over a rope: "I can jump high!" "Are you playing hopscotch?"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Are you playing soccer?" – "Yes!" … "I can jump high!"
