Week 5 · Lesson 3: I Can Help

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, Actions and Sport. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 5: How do I have fun?

Crickets 1 · Week 5: Playground | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The playground Lesson 2 · Playing games Lesson 3 · Being a friend

You Need

  • A ball, two teddies, the playground picture or toys for a pretend slide (a board or a book), a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Are you playing soccer?" with a kick.
  2. Up and down (4 min): Teddy goes up the slide and down: "Up! Down!" Your child moves Teddy as you say. Up and Down song if there is time.
  3. Teddy is sad (5 min): Teddy is scared at the top. "Teddy is scared. Bunny is sad. I can help!" Your child helps Teddy down. "They are happy!"
  4. Watch (3 min): Let's Take Turns – Play Together Song. Pass a ball on each "turn".
  5. Throw and catch (5 min): "I can throw the ball high. I can throw it slowly… quickly!" Catch it: "Can you catch? Yes, I can!"
  6. Share (3 min): One ball, two players: "Let's share! My turn. Your turn."
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions, then "Can you kick a ball?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker.

Say this: "Teddy is sad. I can help!" … "My turn. Your turn."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things in a playground and say "I like the…".
  • Say "I can kick a ball", "I can jump" and do the action.
  • Answer "Are you playing…?" with "Yes" or "No", and take turns and share in a game.
