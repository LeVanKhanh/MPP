Week 6 · Lesson 1: Do You Have a Pet?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals, Opposites and Actions. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 6: How do people and animals interact?

Crickets 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Do you have a pet? Lesson 2 · What pets do Lesson 3 · Caring for a pet

You Need

  • Toy animals or pictures: dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, turtle, frog, snake, spider.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Can you kick a ball?" from last week.
  2. Pet show (5 min): "What's this? It's a dog. What colour is it? It's black. Is it big or small?" Go through the pets. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant for Children. Say the pet before the voice.
  4. Do you have a pet? (4 min): Teddy asks: "Do you have a pet?" – "Yes, I do. I have a fish!" or "No, I don't." "What do you want?" – "I want a dog!"
  5. Whose pet? (4 min): Give pets to Teddy and Bunny. "Is the rabbit Teddy's rabbit?" – "No! It's Bunny's rabbit!"
  6. Watch (2 min): Five Spotted Dogs – Counting Song. Count on your fingers.
  7. Count the pets (6 min): "How many dogs can you see? Let's count!" Count toy pets or pets in a book, up to seven.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Do you have a pet?" – "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't." … "What do you want?" – "I want a fish."
