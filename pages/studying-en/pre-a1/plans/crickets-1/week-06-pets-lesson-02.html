Week 6 · Lesson 2: What Does Your Dog Eat?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals, Opposites and Actions. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 6: How do people and animals interact?

Crickets 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Do you have a pet? Lesson 2 · What pets do Lesson 3 · Caring for a pet

You Need

  • The toy animals, a toy bone or paper one, a carrot, lettuce if you have it, string for a spider web.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Do you have a pet?"
  2. What do pets do? (5 min): Act each one: "The bird flies. The frog jumps. The snake sleeps all day. The spider spins a web." Your child acts too. Action words
  3. Watch and move (3 min): Yes, I Can! – Animal Song. Move like each animal.
  4. What does it eat? (4 min): "What does your dog eat? My dog eats dog food. A rabbit eats carrots and lettuce." Feed each toy pet.
  5. Who eats who? (4 min): Line up toys for a simple food chain: "The snake eats a frog. The frog eats a spider." Point to each one.
  6. Watch (2 min): It's a Dog – Kids Song with Actions. Do the dog actions.
  7. Spider web (6 min): Make a web with string between two chairs. A toy spider "spins" it. "What does the spider spin? A web!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What does the dog eat?" – "Dog food!" … "The snake sleeps all day."
