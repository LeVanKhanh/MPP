Week 6 · Lesson 3: I Feed My Fish

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals, Opposites and Actions. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 6: How do people and animals interact?

Crickets 1 · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Do you have a pet? Lesson 2 · What pets do Lesson 3 · Caring for a pet

You Need

  • A toy pet and a box for its cage, a small bowl of water, a brush, a cloth, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What does the bird do?" – flap your arms.
  2. Look after a pet (5 min): "This is my pet. I love my pet. I feed my pet. I give it water. I brush it." Your child does each job with a toy pet.
  3. Watch (3 min): I Lost my Pet – Kids Songs. Find the pet in each picture.
  4. Clean the cage (4 min): "Clean the cage! Wash the bowl!" Use the box and the cloth. "Good job!"
  5. Who feeds the bird? (4 min): "Who feeds the bird? Mom feeds the bird. Does she wash her cat? No, she doesn't!" Make silly questions.
  6. Pets that help (4 min): "Some dogs work. They help people." Act a dog helping: catching a ball, sitting, bringing things. Your child is the dog: "Sit! Catch the ball!"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the toys. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and an animal sticker.

Say this: "I feed my fish. I give it water." … "Sit! Catch the ball!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Answer "Do you have a pet?" with "Yes, I do" or "No, I don't", and name some pets.
  • Say what a pet does: "The dog runs", "The bird flies", "The snake sleeps".
  • Show how to care for a pet: give it food and water, clean its home, play with it.
