Week 7 · Lesson 1: I'm a Teacher

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Jobs, Health and Clothes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 7: What do people in my community do?

Crickets 1 · Week 7: Jobs | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Jobs and places Lesson 2 · What they do Lesson 3 · What they use and wear

You Need

  • Pictures of a teacher, a doctor, a chef, a pilot, a bus driver, a firefighter (from books or the lesson page videos), toy people if you have them.
  • Most job words are above the Starters list (doctor and nurse are Movers). That is fine: they are words your child sees every day.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "I feed my fish!" from last week.
  2. Who is this? (5 min): "She's a teacher. He's a doctor. She's a chef. He's a pilot." Act each job: write on the board, listen to a heart, stir a pot, fly a plane. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Community Helpers – Kids Songs. Point to the job you know.
  4. Where do they work? (4 min): "I'm a teacher. I work at a school. A doctor works at a hospital. A pilot works at an airport." Put the pictures by the place.
  5. Guess the job (4 min): Act a job without talking. "Who am I?" – "A chef!" Swap.
  6. Watch (2 min): Jobs Song for Kids – What Do You Do?. Act each job.
  7. My family (6 min): "My mom is a… My grandpa is a…" Draw a family member at work.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I'm a teacher. I work at a school." … "A doctor works at a hospital."
