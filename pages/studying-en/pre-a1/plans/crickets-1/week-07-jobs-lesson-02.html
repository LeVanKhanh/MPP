Week 7 · Lesson 2: What's the Pilot Doing?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Jobs, Health and Clothes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 7: What do people in my community do?

Crickets 1 · Week 7: Jobs | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Jobs and places Lesson 2 · What they do Lesson 3 · What they use and wear

You Need

  • The job pictures, a toy bus and a toy plane, a box for a house, a toy cat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Who is this?" with a job picture.
  2. What do they do? (5 min): "A teacher teaches. A bus driver drives a bus. A firefighter puts out fires. A nurse helps sick people." Act each one.
  3. Watch (3 min): Nurse Song – Community Helpers. Act what the nurse does.
  4. What's he doing? (4 min): Act a job: "What's he doing? He's flying a plane! What's she doing? She's driving a bus!" Your child acts, you guess.
  5. Bus ride (4 min): Line up chairs as a bus. Your child is the bus driver: "Beep beep!" Pick up Teddy and the dolls.
  6. Watch (2 min): Let's Visit The Fire Station – Caitie's Classroom. Watch the first two minutes.
  7. Firefighter rescue (6 min): A toy cat is up on a box: "Help! Help!" Your child is the firefighter and rescues it. "A firefighter rescues cats!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's he doing?" – "He's driving a bus." … "A firefighter helps people."
