Week 7 · Lesson 3: Doctor, Doctor!

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Jobs, Health and Clothes. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 7: What do people in my community do?

Crickets 1 · Week 7: Jobs | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Jobs and places Lesson 2 · What they do Lesson 3 · What they use and wear

You Need

  • Dress-up things: a hat, a coat or a big shirt, an apron, a wooden spoon, a toy stethoscope (or string), a toy hose (a skipping rope), a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What does a teacher do?"
  2. What do they use? (5 min): "A doctor uses a stethoscope. A firefighter uses a hose. A chef uses a wooden spoon." Match each thing to a job picture.
  3. Listen to your heart (4 min): Put two fingers on the wrist or ear on the chest: "Listen! Boom boom!" Run for a minute, then listen again: "Fast!"
  4. Watch (3 min): Doctor Doctor Song – Feel Better Soon. Be the doctor for Teddy.
  5. What do they wear? (4 min): "The firefighter wears a helmet. The doctor wears a coat. The chef wears an apron." Dress up in each one. Clothes words
  6. Job dress-up show (4 min): Your child dresses up and you ask: "What are you?" – "A doctor!" "What are you wearing?" – "A coat!"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the pictures. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker.

Say this: "A doctor uses a stethoscope." … "What are you wearing?" – "A helmet!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name four or five jobs and act each one.
  • Say where people work: "A teacher works at a school. A doctor works at a hospital."
  • Match a job to the thing it uses or wears (a stethoscope, a hose, a helmet, a hat).
