Week 8 · Lesson 1: I Can See Birds in the Sky

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons, Days and Time and Animals. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 8: What do I know about the earth and the sky?

Crickets 1 · Week 8: Earth and Sky | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The daytime sky Lesson 2 · The night sky Lesson 3 · Sun, shadows, rainbow

You Need

  • A walk outside or a window with a view of the sky, a kite or a paper plane if you have one, paper and crayons.
  • Never look straight at the sun. Point to it and look away.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "What does a doctor use?" from last week.
  2. Look up (5 min): Outside or at the window: "Look! The sun is shining. I can see clouds. I can see a bird." Your child says "I can see…" with one word. Weather words
  3. Watch (3 min): Daytime and Nighttime. Watch the daytime part.
  4. In the sky (4 min): Act each one: "The birds are singing. The bees are buzzing. The planes are flying. The clouds are floating." Your child copies the sound or move.
  5. Fly a kite (4 min): Fly a kite or throw a paper plane: "It's flying! It's high! Now it's low!"
  6. Watch (2 min): What's The Weather Like Today. Point to the sky.
  7. Sky picture (6 min): Draw the sky: a yellow sun, white clouds, birds and a red plane. "What can you see in the sky?" Count the birds up to ten.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What can you see in the sky?" – "I can see a bird." … "The plane is flying."
