Week 8 · Lesson 2: The Moon and the Stars

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons, Days and Time and Animals. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 8: What do I know about the earth and the sky?

Crickets 1 · Week 8: Earth and Sky | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The daytime sky Lesson 2 · The night sky Lesson 3 · Sun, shadows, rainbow

You Need

  • Paper, a yellow crayon, star stickers if you have them, toy or picture animals: cat, dog, mouse, bat, owl. A torch (flashlight).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What can you see in the sky?"
  2. Day and night (4 min): Open the curtains: "It's day. I can see the sun." Close them and turn off the light: "It's night. I can see the moon and the stars."
  3. Watch (3 min): Daytime and Nighttime. Watch the night part.
  4. Night animals (5 min): In the dark room with the torch: "I can see an owl. I can see a bat, a mouse and a cat!" Shine the torch on each toy. "The dog is sleeping."
  5. Moon shapes (4 min): Draw the moon: "The moon is a circle, big and bright. Now it's half a circle. Now it's thin." Shape words
  6. Watch (2 min): WHAT AM I? Animal Guessing Game. Guess the animal.
  7. Night sky picture (6 min): Make a night picture: a dark page, a yellow moon and stars. "How many stars? Let's count to ten!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Say "Goodnight, moon!" then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it day or night?" – "Night!" … "I can see the moon and the stars."
