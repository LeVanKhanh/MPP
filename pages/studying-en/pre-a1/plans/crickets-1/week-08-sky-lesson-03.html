Week 8 · Lesson 3: What Does the Sun Do?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons, Days and Time and Animals. Goes with Crickets 1 – Unit 8: What do I know about the earth and the sky?

Crickets 1 · Week 8: Earth and Sky | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · The daytime sky Lesson 2 · The night sky Lesson 3 · Sun, shadows, rainbow

You Need

  • A sunny spot outside or a torch, chalk, a wet T-shirt, a glass of water or a hose spray for a rainbow (or a rainbow picture), a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Is it day or night?"
  2. The sun gives light (4 min): "The sun is hot and bright. The sun gives us light. The sun helps plants grow." Look at a plant by the window.
  3. Shadows (5 min): In the sun (or with a torch): "Look! My shadow!" Draw round your child's shadow with chalk. "Make a big shadow! A small shadow!"
  4. Watch (3 min): Rainy Day – The Storytellers. Watch for the sun at the end.
  5. Dry the T-shirt (3 min): Hang a wet T-shirt in the sun: "The sun makes the T-shirt dry." Check it at the end of the lesson.
  6. Rainbow (5 min): Make a rainbow with a glass of water in the sun or look at a picture: "What colour is it? Red, yellow, green, blue!" Colour words
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions, then "What can you see at night?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Check the T-shirt: "It's dry!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye and a star sticker for finishing Crickets 1!

Say this: "The sun is hot and bright." … "Look! It's my shadow!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say "I can see… in the sky" about the sun, birds, a plane or clouds.
  • Say if it is day or night, and name the moon and stars.
  • Show what the sun does: it gives light and makes shadows.
