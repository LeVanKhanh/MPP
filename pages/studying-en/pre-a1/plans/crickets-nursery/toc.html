Crickets Nursery

Home plans that go with the 8 units of Richmond Crickets Nursery, a preschool English course. One unit a week, three short lessons each, with songs, real objects and games.

Course plan | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters | Ages about 3–5

How These Plans Work

  • One unit, one week. Each unit becomes three 30-minute lessons: first words, then words with a short sentence, then a review with counting and a mini check. If the class stays on a unit longer, repeat the week with new toys and the same songs.
  • Learn with real things. Toys, food, clothes and the family photo album come before pictures and screens. Video is at most about 10 minutes a lesson, watched and sung together.
  • Listening first. Pointing, doing the action or saying one word is success at this age. Never turn the lesson into a test.
  • Use the book in class. These lessons add songs, videos and games with the same topic; they do not copy the book's pages, stories or characters.
  • Every lesson links to a shared lesson page (like School or My Body) where you can tap a word to hear it and watch checked videos. The words are checked against the Pre A1 Starters wordlist.
  • Next book: Crickets 1. For the next step after it, see Amanda and Friends 2 or Fun for Starters.
