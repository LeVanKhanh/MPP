Week 1 · Lesson 1: Hello! My Name Is…

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, Colours and Feelings. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 1: What can I see in my school?

Crickets Nursery · Week 1: School | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello, my name is… Lesson 2 · Book, crayon, chair, table Lesson 3 · Put it on the table

You Need

  • A teddy or a puppet, a sheet of paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Wave on every "hello".
  2. Teddy says hello (4 min): Teddy waves: "Hello! My name's Teddy." Then Teddy asks your child: "What's your name?" Help with "My name's…" and clap.
  3. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name? – Super Simple Puppets. Wave at the puppets and say your name with them.
  4. Boy or girl? (4 min): Point to family photos or toys: "He's a boy. She's a girl." Then point to your child: "Are you a boy or a girl?" Pointing at themselves is a good answer.
  5. Happy, sad (5 min): Make a big smile: "I'm happy!" Make a sad face: "I'm sad." Your child copies. Then Teddy is happy or sad and your child shows the same face. Feelings words
  6. Watch and move (3 min): If You're Happy And You Know It. Clap and stamp with the song.
  7. Draw (5 min): Draw a big circle face together. "Is it happy or sad?" Add a smile or a sad mouth. Say "Happy!" or "Sad!" for each face.
  8. Goodbye (4 min): Teddy waves "Goodbye!" to everyone in the room, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Hello! My name's Teddy. What's your name?" … "Are you happy? I'm happy!"
