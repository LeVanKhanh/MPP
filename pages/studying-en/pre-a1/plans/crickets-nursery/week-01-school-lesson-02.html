Week 1 · Lesson 2: Book, Crayon, Chair, Table

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, Colours and Feelings. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 1: What can I see in my school?

Crickets Nursery · Week 1: School | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello, my name is… Lesson 2 · Book, crayon, chair, table Lesson 3 · Put it on the table

You Need

  • A book, red and blue crayons, a small chair and a table (real ones at home), Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "What's your name?" to Teddy and to your child.
  2. Show and say (5 min): Touch each thing: "Look! It's a book. It's a crayon. It's a chair. It's a table." Say each word three times; your child points. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): My Classroom. Point to the same things in your room.
  4. Run and touch (4 min): "Touch the table! Touch the chair! Touch the book!" Then swap: your child says, you run.
  5. Red or blue? (5 min): Hold up the red and the blue crayon: "It's red. It's blue." Then "Give me the blue crayon, please." … "Thank you!" Colour words
  6. Watch (2 min): Red Yellow Green Blue – The Bumble Nums. Hold up the red or blue crayon when that colour comes.
  7. Colour (5 min): Draw a book and a chair. "What colour is the book?" Your child chooses red or blue and colours it.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Put the crayons in the box together, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's a crayon. It's red." … "Give me the blue crayon, please. Thank you!"
