Week 1 · Lesson 3: Put the Book on the Table

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School, Hello, Colours and Feelings. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 1: What can I see in my school?

Crickets Nursery · Week 1: School | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello, my name is… Lesson 2 · Book, crayon, chair, table Lesson 3 · Put it on the table

You Need

  • The book, crayons, chair and table again, Teddy, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Teddy says "Hello! I'm happy!"
  2. What's this? (4 min): Point to each thing: "What's this?" Pointing or one word is a good answer. Then "Is this a book?" – "Yes!" / "No!"
  3. Teddy's classroom (5 min): Teddy is the pupil. "Put the book on the table." "Put the crayon on the chair." "Put Teddy on the chair." Your child does each one.
  4. Watch (3 min): What's this? – It's a pencil. (Stationery Rap). Say "It's a…" with the singer.
  5. Stand up, sit down (4 min): "Stand up! Sit down!" on the real chair, faster and slower. Then Stand Up, Sit Down – Actions Song if there is energy left.
  6. Count (4 min): Put crayons on the table and count them together, touching each one: "One, two, three crayons!"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the real things. Clap after every answer, even a pointing one.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's this? Is it a book? Yes!" … "Put the book on the table, please."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Wave and say "Hello!" and "Goodbye!", and answer "What's your name?" with their name.
  • Point to a book, a crayon, a chair and a table when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Find something red and something blue, and show a happy face and a sad face.
