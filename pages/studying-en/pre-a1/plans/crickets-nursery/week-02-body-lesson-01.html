Week 2 · Lesson 1: My Face

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 2: What are the parts of my body?

Crickets Nursery · Week 2: My Body | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Head, hands, feet Lesson 3 · Two eyes

You Need

  • A mirror, a teddy or doll, paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Are you happy?" from last week.
  2. In the mirror (5 min): Sit in front of the mirror. Touch and say: "My eyes. My ears. My nose. My mouth." Your child copies. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): My Face – Talking Flashcards. Touch each part on your own face.
  4. Teddy's face (4 min): "Where are Teddy's eyes?" Your child points. "Where is Teddy's nose?" Then swap: your child asks, you point to the wrong part and they laugh and fix it.
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): This Is My Face – Parts of the Face Song. Point with the song.
  6. What's missing? (5 min): Draw a face with no mouth. "What's missing?" Your child points or says "Mouth!" and draws it. Then a face with no eyes.
  7. Touch your… (4 min): "Touch your nose! Touch your ears!" Faster and faster, then very slowly.
  8. Goodbye (4 min): Wave goodbye with a big smile, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is my nose. Where is your nose?" … "Touch your ears!"
