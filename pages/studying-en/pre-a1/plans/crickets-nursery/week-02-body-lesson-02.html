Week 2 · Lesson 2: Head, Hands and Feet

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 2: What are the parts of my body?

Crickets Nursery · Week 2: My Body | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Head, hands, feet Lesson 3 · Two eyes

You Need

  • The mirror, a pink and a yellow crayon (or toys), a big sheet of paper.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Touch your nose!" to warm up.
  2. Body words (5 min): Touch and say: "My head. My hands. My arms. My legs. My feet." Wiggle each one. Word list
  3. Watch and move (3 min): Head Shoulders Knees & Toes (Learn It). Touch head and feet; the other words can wait.
  4. Simon says (4 min): "Show me your hands! Show me your feet!" Clap hands, stamp feet, shake arms.
  5. Pink and yellow (4 min): Find a pink thing and a yellow thing in the room. "It's pink! It's yellow!" Colour words
  6. Watch (2 min): If It's Yellow and You Know It. Clap when you hear "yellow".
  7. Hand and foot prints (6 min): Draw round your child's hand in yellow and foot in pink. "What colour is the hand? Yellow!" Name each one.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Wave your hands, stamp your feet, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Show me your hands!" … "What colour is the hand? It's yellow!"
