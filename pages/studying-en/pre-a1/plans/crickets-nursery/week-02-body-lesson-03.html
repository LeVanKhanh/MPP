Week 2 · Lesson 3: I Have Two Eyes

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: My Body and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 2: What are the parts of my body?

Crickets Nursery · Week 2: My Body | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My face Lesson 2 · Head, hands, feet Lesson 3 · Two eyes

You Need

  • The hand and foot pictures from lesson 2, Teddy, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Show the hand picture: "What's this? A hand!"
  2. One or two? (5 min): Touch and count together: "One nose. One mouth. Two eyes! Two ears! Two hands!" Hold up fingers for one and two.
  3. Watch (2 min): I Have Two Eyes. Point and hold up two fingers.
  4. How many? (4 min): "How many eyes has Teddy got? One, two – two eyes!" Then legs, arms, ears. Real counting, finger on each one.
  5. Watch and move (3 min): Me! – Body Parts Song. Move each part with the song.
  6. Funny face (5 min): Draw a funny face with your child telling you what to add: "Two eyes! One nose! A big mouth!" Then colour the hair yellow or pink.
  7. Mini check (5 min): Ask the Review questions face to face. Pointing is a good answer. Then your child asks you one.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker on a hand.

Say this: "How many eyes? One, two. Two eyes!" … "I have two hands."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to their eyes, ears, nose and mouth when you say the word.
  • Touch their head, hands, arms, legs and feet in an action song or "Touch your…" game.
  • Count two eyes, two ears and one nose, and find something pink and something yellow.
