Week 3 · Lesson 1: Apple, Banana, Orange, Pear

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables, Food and Drinks and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 3: What do I eat?

Crickets Nursery · Week 3: Food | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit Lesson 2 · I like… Lesson 3 · Count the fruit

You Need

  • A real apple, banana, orange and pear (or toy fruit), a lunch box or bag.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Touch your nose!" from last week.
  2. What's in the lunch box? (5 min): Take out the fruit one by one: "What's this? This is an apple!" Smell and touch each one. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Fruit – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the real fruit when it comes.
  4. Fruit hunt (4 min): Hide the fruit around the room. "Find the banana!" Your child brings it: "Thank you!"
  5. What colour is it? (5 min): "What colour is the banana? Yellow!" "The pear is green." "The orange is… orange!" Colour words
  6. Watch and sing (3 min): Fruit Song for Kids. Dance with the fruit you hear.
  7. Fruit picture (5 min): Draw and colour one fruit each. "I have a banana! What have you got?"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Put the fruit back in the lunch box, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this? This is an apple." … "What colour is the banana? It's yellow."
