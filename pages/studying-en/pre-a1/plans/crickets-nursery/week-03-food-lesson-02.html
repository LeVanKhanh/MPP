Week 3 · Lesson 2: I Like Bananas

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables, Food and Drinks and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 3: What do I eat?

Crickets Nursery · Week 3: Food | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit Lesson 2 · I like… Lesson 3 · Count the fruit

You Need

  • The fruit, a cup of milk or juice, a cookie, a picture of a sandwich and a pizza (or toy food).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Show a fruit: "What's this?"
  2. Food and drink (4 min): Add milk, juice, a cookie, a sandwich and a pizza picture: "This is milk. This is a sandwich." Food words
  3. Watch (3 min): [Liking] Do you like…? Yes or No – Easy Dialogue. Thumbs up or down with the children in the video.
  4. Thumbs up, thumbs down (5 min): Hold up each food: "I like bananas! 👍 I don't like pizza! 👎" Then "Do you like apples?" Your child shows thumbs up or down, or says "Yes!" or "No!"
  5. Fruit shop (6 min): You are the shop. "Hello! A banana, please." Your child gives it. Swap: your child asks, you give. "Thank you!"
  6. Watch (2 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream?. Shout "No!" or "Yes!" with the song.
  7. Snack time (4 min): Share a real snack: "I have a banana. Do you like bananas?" "Yum!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Do you like bananas?" – "Yes!" … "I like apples. I don't like pizza."
