Week 3 · Lesson 3: Three Bananas!

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables, Food and Drinks and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 3: What do I eat?

Crickets Nursery · Week 3: Food | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit Lesson 2 · I like… Lesson 3 · Count the fruit

You Need

  • Five apples (or toy fruit), five bananas or five orange segments, a basket, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Do you like apples?" to warm up.
  2. Count to three (4 min): Put out bananas one by one: "One, two, three. Three bananas!" Your child touches each one as you count.
  3. Watch (3 min): [Counting] How many apples? – Sing Along. Hold up fingers with the song.
  4. Basket game (5 min): "Put two apples in the basket!" "Put four oranges in the basket!" Count together as they go in.
  5. Watch and move (2 min): I Want It! – Bananas & Apples Counting Song. Count on your fingers.
  6. How many? (4 min): Make little piles of 1–5 fruits. "How many apples? Let's count!" Counting each one is more important than the answer.
  7. Mini check (6 min): Ask the Review questions with real fruit. Then "What colour is the pear?" and "Do you like oranges?" Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a fruit sticker.

Say this: "How many bananas? One, two, three. Three bananas!" … "Put two apples in the basket."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear, and say at least two of them.
  • Show "I like…" and "I don't like…" with a word, a thumbs up or a thumbs down.
  • Count up to five real fruits, touching each one.
