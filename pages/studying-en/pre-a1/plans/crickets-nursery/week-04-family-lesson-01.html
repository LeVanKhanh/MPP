Week 4 · Lesson 1: This Is Mom

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Shapes and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 4: Who is in my family?

Crickets Nursery · Week 4: Family | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · This is Mom Lesson 2 · Grandma, Grandpa Lesson 3 · My family

You Need

  • A family photo or photo album, paper and crayons.
  • In class the children say "Mom". The British word is "Mum"; use the class word at home.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "What's this?" with a fruit from last week.
  2. Our family photo (5 min): Point to each person: "This is Mom. This is Dad. This is the baby." Your child points as you say each word. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Family – Talking Flashcards. Point to the same person in your photo.
  4. Who's this? (4 min): Cover the photo with your hand and show one face: "Who's this?" Pointing, the name or "Dad!" are all good.
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): Finger Family Song. Wiggle each finger.
  6. Finger family (4 min): Draw faces on your fingers: thumb Dad, finger Mom, little finger the baby. "Where is Dad? Here I am!"
  7. Draw (6 min): Draw your family as stick people. "Who's this? It's Mom!" Your child adds hair or a smile to each one.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Wave goodbye to the family photo, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's this?" – "Mom!" … "Yes! This is Mom."
