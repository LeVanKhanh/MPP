Week 4 · Lesson 2: Grandma and Grandpa

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Shapes and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 4: Who is in my family?

Crickets Nursery · Week 4: Family | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · This is Mom Lesson 2 · Grandma, Grandpa Lesson 3 · My family

You Need

  • The photo album, paper shapes: a circle and a square cut out of card.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Hold up the family drawing: "Who's this?"
  2. More family (5 min): Find Grandma, Grandpa, a brother or a sister in the photos: "This is Grandma. This is Grandpa." Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Family [Kids Vocab] Meet My Family!. Wave at each family member.
  4. Is this Grandpa? (4 min): Point to a photo: "Is this Grandpa?" – "Yes!" / "No!" Sometimes say the wrong name so your child can shout "No!"
  5. Circle and square (5 min): Hold up the shapes: "It's a circle. It's a square." Find circles and squares at home: a plate, a window, a clock. Shape words
  6. Watch (2 min): Shapes Song – Circle, Triangle, Square, Heart. Draw the shape in the air.
  7. Shape faces (5 min): Glue or draw a family: a circle face for Grandma, a square face for Grandpa. "What is it? It's a circle!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is this Grandpa?" – "Yes!" … "What is it? It's a circle."
