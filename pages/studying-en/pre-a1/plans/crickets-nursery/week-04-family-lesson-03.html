Week 4 · Lesson 3: This Is My Family

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Family, Shapes and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 4: Who is in my family?

Crickets Nursery · Week 4: Family | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · This is Mom Lesson 2 · Grandma, Grandpa Lesson 3 · My family

You Need

  • The photos and drawings, a toy house or a box, five small toy people or clothes pegs, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Who's this?" with one photo.
  2. How many people? (5 min): Look at a family photo: "How many people can you see? One, two, three, four!" Touch each face as you count.
  3. Watch (3 min): The People In My Family. Point to the people in your photo.
  4. Who lives in the house? (5 min): Put toy people in the box house one by one: "Here is Dad. Here is Grandma." Then "Who lives in the house?" Your child names or points.
  5. Shape hunt (3 min): "Find a circle! Find a square!" Run and touch.
  6. My family picture (5 min): Draw the house as a big square and the people inside. "This is my house. This is my family."
  7. Mini check (3 min): Ask the Review questions with the photos. Clap for every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a family sticker.

Say this: "How many people can you see? Let's count!" … "This is my house. This is my family."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to Mom, Dad, Grandma and Grandpa in a family photo, and say at least two of them.
  • Say "This is my…" or name a person in answer to "Who's this?".
  • Count the people in a picture up to five, and find a circle and a square.
