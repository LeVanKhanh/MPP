Week 5 · Lesson 1: My Toys

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, In, On, Under and Shapes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 5: What toys do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 5: Toys | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · In and on Lesson 3 · Shapes and toys

You Need

  • Your child's own ball, car, doll, teddy bear and a few blocks, a box.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Who's this?" with the family photo.
  2. Toy box (5 min): Take toys out of the box one by one: "What's this? It's a car! It's a ball!" Your child points and copies. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Toy [Kids Vocab] My Favourite Toys. Hold up your real toy when it comes.
  4. Fetch it (4 min): Put the toys around the room. "Bring me the teddy bear!" "Bring me the ball!" Then swap.
  5. What colour is the car? (4 min): "What colour is the car? It's red!" "The ball is blue." Name the colour of each toy. Colour words
  6. Watch (2 min): What Is It? Song – Toys Guessing Game. Shout the toy before the picture shows.
  7. Play and talk (6 min): Play cars or dolls together and talk as you play: "The car goes vroom! The teddy bear is happy!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): "Put the ball in the box! Put the car in the box!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this? It's a car. What colour is the car? It's red."
