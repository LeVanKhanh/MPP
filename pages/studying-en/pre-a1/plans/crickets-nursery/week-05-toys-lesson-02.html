Week 5 · Lesson 2: In the Box, on the Table

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, In, On, Under and Shapes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 5: What toys do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 5: Toys | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · In and on Lesson 3 · Shapes and toys

You Need

  • The toys, a big box, a table, blocks.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Hold up a toy: "What's this?"
  2. In or on? (5 min): Show it slowly: "The doll is in the box. The ball is on the table." Your child copies with another toy. Position words
  3. Watch (3 min): On In Under Song For Kids. Put the teddy in or on the box with the song.
  4. Teddy hides (4 min): Your child closes their eyes; hide Teddy in the box or on the chair. "Where is Teddy?" Pointing and "In!" or "On!" are good answers.
  5. Blocks (5 min): "What are these? They're blocks!" Count them: "How many blocks? One, two, three!" Build a tower of three.
  6. Watch (2 min): I Have A Toy. Hold up your favourite toy.
  7. Tidy up game (5 min): "Put the car in the box. Put the ball on the table. Put the doll in the box." Your child tidies, you cheer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where is Teddy? Teddy is in the box!" … "Put the ball on the table, please."
