Week 5 · Lesson 3: What Shape Do You Like?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Toys, In, On, Under and Shapes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 5: What toys do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 5: Toys | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My toys Lesson 2 · In and on Lesson 3 · Shapes and toys

You Need

  • Blocks in different shapes and colours (or paper shapes: circle, square, triangle, rectangle), a toy train or boat if you have one, a purple crayon, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Where is Teddy?" – hide him before the song.
  2. Shape blocks (5 min): Hold up blocks: "It's a circle. It's a square. It's a triangle. It's a rectangle." Your child finds the same one. Shape words
  3. Watch (3 min): Triangle, Circle, Circle, Square!. Draw each shape in the air.
  4. Build a train (5 min): Make a train or a boat with the blocks: "A rectangle, a square, two circles!" "What colour is it? It's purple!"
  5. Shape hop (3 min): Put paper shapes on the floor. "Jump on the triangle! Jump on the circle!"
  6. My favourite toy (4 min): "What do you like? I like my teddy bear!" Your child picks a toy and shows it. "What shape do you like?"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the real toys. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker on the favourite toy.

Say this: "What shape is it? It's a triangle." … "What do you like?" – "I like my teddy bear."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a ball, a car, a doll, a teddy bear and blocks, and say at least three of them.
  • Put a toy in the box or on the table when you ask.
  • Find a circle, a square and a triangle among the blocks, and say "I like…" about a toy.
