Week 6 · Lesson 1: Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals and Opposites. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 6: What pets do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big or small Lesson 3 · Pet food

You Need

  • Toy animals or pictures: a dog, a cat, a bird, a fish (a rabbit and a turtle too if you have them).
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "What's this?" with a toy from last week.
  2. Pet show (5 min): Show each animal with its sound: "It's a dog! Woof! It's a cat! Meow!" Your child copies the sound. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant for Children. Say the pet before the voice does.
  4. Move like a pet (4 min): "Fly like a bird! Swim like a fish! Jump like a rabbit!" Then your child says, you move.
  5. What's this? (4 min): Hide one animal behind your back and make its sound: "What's this?" "It's a cat!"
  6. Watch and sing (2 min): It's a Dog – Kids Song with Actions. Do the actions.
  7. Draw a pet (6 min): Draw a pet together. "What colour is the fish? It's orange!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Every pet waves goodbye, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this? It's a dog! Woof!" … "Fly like a bird!"
