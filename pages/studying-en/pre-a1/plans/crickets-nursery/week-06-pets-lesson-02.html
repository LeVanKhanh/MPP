Week 6 · Lesson 2: Big or Small?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals and Opposites. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 6: What pets do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big or small Lesson 3 · Pet food

You Need

  • The toy pets, two of one animal in different sizes if you can (big dog, small dog), a box for a dog house, a cloth for a bed.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Make a pet sound: "What's this?"
  2. Big and small (5 min): Hands far apart: "Big!" Hands close: "Small!" Show two toys: "This dog is big. This dog is small." Opposite words
  3. Watch (3 min): Opposite Words (v1) [Kids Vocab] Big & Small, Open & Close. Show big and small with your hands.
  4. Is it big or small? (4 min): Hold up toys and things: "Is it big or small?" Your child shows with hands or says the word.
  5. Pet homes (5 min): Make a dog house from a box and a bed from a cloth. "It's a house for the dog. It's a bed for the cat." Your child puts each pet to bed.
  6. Watch (2 min): Five Spotted Dogs – Counting Song. Count the dogs on your fingers.
  7. Big and small pictures (5 min): Draw a big fish and a small fish. "Which fish is big?"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Say "Goodnight!" to the pets in their beds, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it big or small?" – "Small!" … "It's a bed for the cat."
