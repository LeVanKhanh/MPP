Week 6 · Lesson 3: A Dog Eats Bones

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Animals and Opposites. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 6: What pets do I like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 6: Pets | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Pets Lesson 2 · Big or small Lesson 3 · Pet food

You Need

  • The toy pets, a real carrot, a toy bone or a paper one, fish food or a picture, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Is it big or small?" with two toys.
  2. Pet food (5 min): Show the food: "It's a bone. It's a carrot." Then "A dog eats bones. A rabbit eats carrots." Your child feeds each pet.
  3. Watch (3 min): I Lost my Pet – Kids Songs. Point to the pet you see.
  4. Feed the pet (4 min): "Give the dog a bone. Give the rabbit a carrot." Your child chooses the right food. Silly mistakes are funny: "A fish eats bones? No!"
  5. Count the pets (4 min): Line up toy pets: "How many turtles? One, two, three!" Count up to five, touching each one.
  6. Pet shapes (4 min): Make a pet out of paper shapes: a circle head, triangle ears. "What shape is this? It's a triangle!"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions with the toys. Clap for every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and an animal sticker.

Say this: "What's this? It's a bone. A dog eats bones." … "How many turtles can you see?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a dog, a cat, a bird and a fish, and make each animal's sound or move.
  • Show "big" and "small" with their hands and with two toys.
  • Match a pet to its food or bed, and count up to five toy animals.
