Week 7 · Lesson 1: My T-shirt, My Shoes

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Clothes and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 7: What clothes do I wear?

Crickets Nursery · Week 7: Clothes | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Colours of clothes Lesson 3 · I like the T-shirt

You Need

  • Your child's real clothes: a T-shirt, shorts, a skirt or pants, socks, shoes, a sweater. A bag.
  • In class: "pants" and "sweater". British English says "trousers" and "jumper".
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "Woof! What's this?" from last week.
  2. Clothes bag (5 min): Take clothes out one by one: "What's this? It's a T-shirt! What are these? They're shoes!" Your child holds each one. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Learn Clothes Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the real thing.
  4. Clothes race (4 min): "Put on your socks! Put on your shoes!" over their own clothes. Cheer when they finish.
  5. Touch and say (4 min): "Touch your T-shirt! Touch your shoes!" Then on Teddy or a doll.
  6. Watch and sing (2 min): Put On Your Shoes. Do the actions.
  7. Dress the doll (6 min): Dress a doll or teddy in your child's socks or a hat. "It's a sock! Put on the sock!" Lots of laughing.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Fold the clothes together, then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's this? It's a T-shirt." … "What are these? They're shoes. Put on your shoes!"
