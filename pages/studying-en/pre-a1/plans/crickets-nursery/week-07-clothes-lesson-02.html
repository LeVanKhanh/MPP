Week 7 · Lesson 2: My Socks Are Pink

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Clothes and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 7: What clothes do I wear?

Crickets Nursery · Week 7: Clothes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Colours of clothes Lesson 3 · I like the T-shirt

You Need

  • Clothes in different colours, a washing line or string and pegs (or a chair to hang them on).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Hold up a sock: "What's this?"
  2. What colour is it? (5 min): "What colour is the skirt? It's green. What colour are the shoes? They're yellow." Your child names or points to the colour.
  3. Watch (3 min): What Are You Wearing? – Clothes Song. Point to your own clothes.
  4. Washing line (5 min): "Give me the blue T-shirt, please!" Your child hangs it. "Thank you!" Count the clothes on the line: "One, two, three, four, five!"
  5. Colour hunt (4 min): "Find something pink to wear!" "Find a green sock!"
  6. Watch (2 min): What Colour Is It? Song. Answer with the singer.
  7. Colour the clothes (5 min): Draw a T-shirt and shorts. Your child chooses the colours: "My T-shirt is red!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour are the socks? They're pink." … "Give me the blue T-shirt, please."
