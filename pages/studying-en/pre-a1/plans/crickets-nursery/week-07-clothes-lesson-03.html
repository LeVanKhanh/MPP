Week 7 · Lesson 3: I Like the Yellow T-shirt

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Clothes and Colours. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 7: What clothes do I wear?

Crickets Nursery · Week 7: Clothes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My clothes Lesson 2 · Colours of clothes Lesson 3 · I like the T-shirt

You Need

  • Two or three T-shirts or sweaters in different colours, the dress-up clothes, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "What colour is your T-shirt?"
  2. Do you like it? (5 min): Hold up a T-shirt: "Do you like the yellow T-shirt?" Thumbs up or down, "Yes!" or "No!" Then you say "I like the sweater!"
  3. Watch (3 min): This Is The Way We Get Dressed. Act getting dressed.
  4. Dress-up show (5 min): Your child picks clothes to wear and walks like a model. You say: "The girl is wearing pants and shoes!" or "The boy is wearing a T-shirt!"
  5. Is this a blouse? (3 min): Hold up clothes and ask: "Is this a blouse?" Sometimes the wrong name: your child shouts "No!"
  6. Count the clothes (4 min): "How many socks? Let's count!" Pair them up: "One sock, two socks!"
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker on the T-shirt.

Say this: "Do you like the yellow T-shirt?" – "Yes!" … "I like the sweater."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to a T-shirt, shorts, a skirt, socks and shoes, and say at least three of them.
  • Say the colour of their own clothes ("My socks are pink").
  • Say "Yes" or "No" to "Do you like the yellow T-shirt?" and choose clothes they like.
