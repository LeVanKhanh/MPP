Week 8 · Lesson 1: It's Sunny!

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and Clothes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 8: What's the weather like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 8: Weather | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Sunny, rainy, windy Lesson 2 · Leaves and clouds Lesson 3 · Weather clothes

You Need

  • A window to look out of, paper and yellow, blue and grey crayons, a scarf or a sheet of paper to wave.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 6–8 minutes of video, watched and sung together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Then "What colour is your T-shirt?" from last week.
  2. Look outside (4 min): At the window: "Look! It's sunny!" (or rainy, or windy). Point to the sky. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Weather – Learn Weather Words in 5 Minutes. Watch the first part; do an action for each word.
  4. Weather actions (5 min): Sunny: arms up in a big circle. Rainy: fingers fall like rain. Windy: wave the scarf and blow. "It's windy!" Your child does the action you say.
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): How's The Weather?. Do the weather actions with the song.
  6. The sun (4 min): Draw a big sun. "What's this? It's the sun. What colour is the sun? It's yellow!" "It's hot!" – fan your face.
  7. Clouds (5 min): Draw clouds round the sun. "It's a cloud!" Blow the clouds away: "It's windy!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look! It's sunny. What colour is the sun?" – "Yellow!" … "It's windy!"
