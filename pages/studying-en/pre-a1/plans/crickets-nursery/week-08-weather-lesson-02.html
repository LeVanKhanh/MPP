Week 8 · Lesson 2: Leaves on the Tree

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and Clothes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 8: What's the weather like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 8: Weather | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Sunny, rainy, windy Lesson 2 · Leaves and clouds Lesson 3 · Weather clothes

You Need

  • A walk outside or a window with a tree, five real leaves (or paper ones), cotton wool or paper clouds.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. "Is it sunny today?"
  2. Tree and leaves (5 min): Outside or at the window: "Look! It's a tree. These are leaves. What colour are the leaves? Green!" Collect five leaves if you can.
  3. Count the leaves (4 min): "How many leaves? One, two, three, four, five!" Touch each one.
  4. Watch (3 min): What's The Weather Like Today. Point to the window when they ask.
  5. Windy game (4 min): Put the leaves on the table and blow: "It's windy! The leaves are moving!" Then count them again.
  6. Count five clouds (4 min): Make five clouds with cotton wool or paper. "What number is this? Five! Count five clouds."
  7. Picture (4 min): Glue the leaves on a tree drawing. Add the sun or clouds: "It's sunny!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour are the leaves? Green!" … "How many leaves? Let's count!"
