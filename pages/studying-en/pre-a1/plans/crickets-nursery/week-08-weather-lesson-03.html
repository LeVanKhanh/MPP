Week 8 · Lesson 3: I Need an Umbrella

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Weather and Seasons and Clothes. Goes with Crickets Nursery – Unit 8: What's the weather like?

Crickets Nursery · Week 8: Weather | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Sunny, rainy, windy Lesson 2 · Leaves and clouds Lesson 3 · Weather clothes

You Need

  • An umbrella, a raincoat, boots, sandals, shorts, a T-shirt, a sweater, a bag, a sticker.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! – Kids Greeting Song. Look out of the window: "What's the weather like?"
  2. Weather clothes (5 min): Take things out of the bag: "What's this? It's an umbrella. It's rainy!" "These are shorts. It's sunny!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Put on your coat, hat, socks… (Weather Rap). Put on the clothes you have.
  4. Weather dress-up (5 min): Call out the weather: "It's rainy!" Your child picks the umbrella or the raincoat. "It's sunny!" – shorts and a T-shirt.
  5. Rain dance (3 min): Open the umbrella and walk under it: "It's rainy! I'm wearing a raincoat!" Then "It's sunny!" – close it and dance.
  6. Weather chart (4 min): Draw a sun, a cloud with rain and a windy tree. Each morning this week, your child points to today's weather.
  7. Mini check (4 min): Ask the Review questions at the window. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sun sticker for finishing Crickets Nursery!

Say this: "It's rainy! I need an umbrella." … "It's sunny. I wear shorts."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Look out of the window and say or show "sunny", "rainy" or "windy".
  • Point to the sun, a cloud, a tree and leaves in a picture or outside.
  • Choose an umbrella for rain and shorts for a sunny, hot day.
