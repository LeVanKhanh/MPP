Fun for Starters

Home plans that go with the 45 units of Cambridge Fun for Starters, the practice book for the Pre A1 Starters exam. One unit a week, three short lessons each.

Course plan | Level: Pre A1 Starters | Ages about 5–8

How These Plans Work

  • One unit, one week. Each unit becomes three 30-minute lessons at home: words, then sentences and games, then a review.
  • Use the book in class or at home for its own pages. The home lessons add songs, videos and games with the same words; they do not copy the book's exercises.
  • Every lesson links to a lesson page (like Hello or Animals) where you can tap a word to hear it and watch checked videos.
  • All the words are checked against the Pre A1 Starters wordlist.
