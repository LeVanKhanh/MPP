Week 1 · Lesson 1: What's Your Name?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Hello and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 1: Say hello!

Fun for Starters · Week 1: Say hello! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's your name? Lesson 2 · Know your letters Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • Paper and pencils, a set of letter cards (a–z, one letter per small card), and name cards with names from the Starters list: Tom, Sam, Kim, Ben, Ann, Lucy, Nick, May.
  • In the Starters exam, children spell names letter by letter and write them. Names from the Cambridge list are the ones they will hear.
  • About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs). Wave and sing.
  2. Hello, what's your name? (5 min): "Hello! My name is Mum. What's your name?" "My name is…" Then pick a name card each and play that person: "Hello! My name is Tom." Sentences
  3. Watch (3 min): The Alphabet Song. Point to each letter on the screen.
  4. How do you spell it? (5 min): "How do you spell Tom?" "T – O – M." Lay out the letter cards for each name card, then say the letters together.
  5. Spell my name (5 min): your child finds the letter cards for their own name and puts them in order. "How do you spell your name?" Your child spells it out loud.
  6. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name? (Super Simple Puppets). Say your own name in the gaps.
  7. Write it (4 min): your child writes "My name is …" and their name. Then one name from the cards: "Write Kim!"
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Hello! What's your name?" … "How do you spell Tom? T – O – M." … "Write your name, please."
