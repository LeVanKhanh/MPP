Week 1 · Lesson 2: Know Your Letters

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 1: Say hello!

Fun for Starters · Week 1: Say hello! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's your name? Lesson 2 · Know your letters Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • The letter cards (a–z), a few extra cards to make short words, pictures of a cat, a dog, a fish, a frog and a goat. Paper and a red pencil.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. ABC song (3 min): The Alphabet Is So Much Fun. Clap on every letter. Tap the letters
  3. Letter path (5 min): spread the letter cards on the floor. Your child steps from a to z in order, saying each letter. Then time it: can you do it faster?
  4. Listen and find (5 min): say a letter: "Find g! … Find s!" Your child finds the card and holds it up. Then your child says the letters and you find them.
  5. First letters (5 min): show an animal picture: "Cat. C, c, cat! What letter is it?" Then dog, fish, frog, goat. Your child finds the first letter for each one.
  6. Watch (3 min): The Alphabet Song. Sing it slowly together.
  7. Make a word (4 min): make short words with the cards: c-a-t, d-o-g, s-u-n. "What's this word? Cat!" Then your child makes one for you to read.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Find g!" … "Cat. What letter is it? C!" … "What's this word?"
