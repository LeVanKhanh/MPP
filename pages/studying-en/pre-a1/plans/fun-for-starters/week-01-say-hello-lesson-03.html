Week 1 · Lesson 3: What's This?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 1: Say hello!

Fun for Starters · Week 1: Say hello! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's your name? Lesson 2 · Know your letters Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • Pictures or toys: a fish, a goat, a spider, a frog, a sheep, a duck, a cat, a dog and a snake. The letter cards. Paper and pencils.
  • Before the lesson, write a few animal words with one or two letters missing (g _ a t, f r _ g, d _ c k) and a few with the letters mixed up (s i h f, c u d k).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. What's this? (5 min): hold up each animal: "What's this?" "It's a goat!" Then "How do you spell goat?" Spell the short ones together. Word list
  3. Watch (4 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant. Say each animal in the gap.
  4. Mixed-up letters (5 min): show "s i h f". "What's the word?" Your child moves the letter cards to make "fish". Then "c u d k" → "duck".
  5. Missing letters (5 min): show "g _ a t". "What's the letter?" Your child writes it in: "o! Goat!" Then frog and duck.
  6. Name the pets (4 min): give the toy animals names from the Starters list: "This cat's name is Kim. K – I – M." Your child names the dog and the snake and spells the names.
  7. Make a word game (2 min): take three letter cards each. Can you make a word? (c-a-t, s-u-n, d-o-g…)
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's this? It's a goat!" … "How do you spell goat?" … "This cat's name is Kim. K – I – M."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say "My name is…", ask "What's your name?" and spell their own name.
  • Say the alphabet from a to z and find a letter when you say it.
  • Name and spell short animal words: cat, dog, fish, frog, goat, duck.
