Week 2 · Lesson 1: One to Twenty

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 2: Numbers, numbers, numbers.

Fun for Starters · Week 2: Numbers | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · One to twenty Lesson 2 · How many are there? Lesson 3 · Listen and write

You Need

  • Two sets of cards: numbers 1–20 (1, 2, 3…) and number words (one, two, three…). Twenty small things to count (blocks, buttons, toy cars). Paper and pencils.
  • In the Starters exam, children read and write numbers 1–20. Saying them is easy; spelling words like eight, twelve, fifteen takes practice.
  • About 6 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Count to twenty (5 min): count the twenty things together, touching each one: "…eighteen, nineteen, twenty!" Then count back from ten. Tap the numbers
  3. Watch and move (3 min): Count & Move – Count Up To Twenty. Move with every number.
  4. Number and word (5 min): lay out the number cards. Your child finds the word card for each one: "7 … seven!" Start with 1–10, then 11–20.
  5. Number jump (5 min): number cards on the floor. "Jump to fifteen! … Jump to eight!" Then your child calls a number for you.
  6. Watch (2 min): Let's Count 1–10 (Dream English). Count with real things.
  7. Write it (5 min): say a number; your child writes the word: two, five, ten, twelve, twenty. Check together with the word cards.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Let's count to twenty!" … "Jump to fifteen!" … "How do you spell twelve?"
