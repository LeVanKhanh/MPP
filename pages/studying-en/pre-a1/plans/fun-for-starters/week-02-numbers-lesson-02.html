Week 2 · Lesson 2: How Many Are There?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC, At Home and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 2: Numbers, numbers, numbers.

Fun for Starters · Week 2: Numbers | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · One to twenty Lesson 2 · How many are there? Lesson 3 · Listen and write

You Need

  • Your child's bedroom (the messier, the better!), a box, letter cards to make mixed-up words, paper and pencils.
  • Before the lesson, write a few bedroom words with the letters mixed up: r a c, d e b, k o o b, t a c, h o e s, o c k s.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. What's in my room? (4 min): in the bedroom: "What's this?" "It's a bed! … a car! … a book! … a sock!" At Home words
  3. Mixed-up words (5 min): show "r a c". "What's the word?" Your child moves the letters: "car!" Then bed, book, cat, shoe, sock. Your child writes each word.
  4. How many are there? (6 min): "How many cars are there?" Count them together: "There are six cars!" Then balls, books, shoes. Your child asks you one too. Number sentences
  5. Watch (3 min): How Many Fingers? Show the number before the singer says it.
  6. Tidy and count (5 min): put things in the box one by one and count: "One ball, two balls, three balls… How many balls? Five!"
  7. Goodbye (4 min): "How many toys are in the box now?" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many cars are there?" … "There are six cars!" … "What's the word? Car!"
