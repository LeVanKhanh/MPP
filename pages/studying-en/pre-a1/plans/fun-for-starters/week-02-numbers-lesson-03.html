Week 2 · Lesson 3: Listen and Write

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 2: Numbers, numbers, numbers.

Fun for Starters · Week 2: Numbers | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · One to twenty Lesson 2 · How many are there? Lesson 3 · Listen and write

You Need

  • Teddy and a few toys, paper and pencils, brown, green and yellow crayons.
  • Before the lesson, draw a simple dot-to-dot picture (a house, a star or a fish) with dots numbered 1–20.
  • In the Starters listening test, children hear about a person and write a name or a number. This lesson practises that in a friendly way.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (5 min): tell your child about Teddy, slowly: "This is Tom. T – O – M. He's nine. He's got three cars and two books." Ask: "What's his name? How old is he? How many cars?" Your child writes the name and the numbers.
  3. Watch and move (2 min): Count And Move.
  4. Your turn (4 min): your child tells you about a toy: "Her name is Ann. She's six." You write it, and your child checks your spelling.
  5. Dot to dot (6 min): your child joins the dots from 1 to 20, saying each number out loud. "What is it? It's a house!"
  6. Colour and draw (5 min): "Colour the door brown. Colour the tree green. Draw a big yellow sun!" Colour words
  7. Number games (3 min): "Clap seven times! … Jump twelve times!" Then your child gives you a number.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's his name? How old is he?" … "How many cars has he got?" … "Draw a big yellow sun!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say, read and count numbers 1–20, and write some of them as words.
  • Ask and answer "How many … are there?" about things in a room.
  • Listen to a short description and write a name and a number.
