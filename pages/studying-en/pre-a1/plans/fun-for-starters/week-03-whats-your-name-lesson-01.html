Week 3 · Lesson 1: Boy or Girl?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 3: What's your name?

Fun for Starters · Week 3: What's your name? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Boy or girl? Lesson 2 · Names I know Lesson 3 · A card for a friend

You Need

  • Name cards with Starters names: boys Ben, Bill, Dan, Mark, Matt, Nick, Tom; girls Alice, Ann, Grace, Jill, Kim, Lucy, May, Sue; and Alex, Pat, Sam, which can be a boy or a girl.
  • Letter cards to make mixed-up names, three hoops or boxes (boy, girl, boy and girl), two toys, paper and crayons.
  • Starters tests use only names from the official list, so knowing them helps your child read and spell them quickly.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name? Sing the answer with your child's own name.
  3. Mixed-up names (5 min): show "n e B". "What's the name?" Your child moves the letters: "Ben!" Then Sam, Lucy, Nick, Kim, Bill. Your child writes each name with a big first letter.
  4. Boy, girl or both? (6 min): your child reads each name card and puts it in a hoop: "Tom is a boy. Grace is a girl." Alex, Pat and Sam go in the "boy and girl" hoop.
  5. Name hop (4 min): say a name; your child jumps into the right hoop. Then your child says a name for you.
  6. My favourite names (4 min): "What are your favourite names?" Your child picks two, writes them in big frames and colours them.
  7. Mr and Mrs (4 min): two toys: "This is Mr Brown. This is Mrs Green." Mr is for a man, Mrs is for a woman. Your child writes "Mr" and "Mrs" labels and gives the toys names.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's the name? Ben!" … "Is Lucy a boy or a girl?" … "This is Mr Brown."
