Week 3 · Lesson 2: Names I Know

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Hello, Family and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 3: What's your name?

Fun for Starters · Week 3: What's your name? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Boy or girl? Lesson 2 · Names I know Lesson 3 · A card for a friend

You Need

  • A big paper circle folded into four parts, a family photo, toy animals, letter cards, paper and pencils.
  • Spell names slowly, one letter at a time, the way the Starters listening test does: "Grace. G – R – A – C – E."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Is Your Name? Ask the question back after the song.
  3. Name circle (6 min): ask four questions and your child writes one name in each part of the circle: "What's your name? What's your friend's name? What's your grandmother's name? What's a good name for a dog?"
  4. Who's this? (5 min): look at the family photo: "Who's this?" "It's my grandma!" "What's her name?" Family words
  5. Listen and write (5 min): say and spell three names: "This is Mrs Green's dog. His name is Ben. B – E – N." Your child writes the names; check the spelling together.
  6. Spell and run (4 min): letter cards on the floor. Say a name; your child runs to each letter in order: "S – U – E!"
  7. Pet names (3 min): your child names the toy animals and tells you: "The cat's name is Kim. The fish's name is Tom."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's your friend's name?" … "How do you spell it?" … "What's a good name for a cat?"
