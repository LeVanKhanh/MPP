Week 3 · Lesson 3: A Card for a Friend

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and the Birthday words. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 3: What's your name?

Fun for Starters · Week 3: What's your name? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Boy or girl? Lesson 2 · Names I know Lesson 3 · A card for a friend

You Need

  • Folded paper for a birthday card and an envelope, crayons, a 3 × 3 bingo card for each player with nine Starters names, and small things to cover the names.
  • Before the lesson, write the hidden-name sentences from activity 6 on a piece of paper.
  • In Starters, children write a house number and a street name. "Address" is a Movers word, so ask "Where do you live?" instead.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Where do you live? (3 min): "What's the number of our house? What's the name of our street?" Your child says them and copies the street name. Street words
  3. Listen and write (4 min): say slowly: "This is Lucy. She lives at number twelve, Park Street." Your child writes Lucy, 12 and Park Street. Then one more friend.
  4. Watch (5 min): Happy Birthday – This is for you. Thanks.
  5. Make a birthday card (6 min): your child draws a cake and writes "Happy birthday, ____!" for a friend. On the envelope: the friend's name, house number and street.
  6. Hidden names (4 min): find a name hiding in each sentence: "Come to my room!" (Tom) · "Jump at the door!" (Pat) · "Is a mouse here?" (Sam) · "My dad and mum" (Dan) · "I'm a yellow fish!" (May).
  7. Names bingo (3 min): call names one by one; your child covers them. Three in a line: "Bingo!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Where do you live?" … "Happy birthday, Lucy! This is for you." … "Can you find the name?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read, spell and write the Starters names, and say whether a name is for a boy, a girl or both.
  • Answer "What's your / your friend's name?" and use Mr and Mrs.
  • Listen and write a name, a house number and a street name.
