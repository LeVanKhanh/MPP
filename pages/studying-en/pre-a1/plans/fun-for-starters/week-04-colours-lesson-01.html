Week 4 · Lesson 1: Paint and See

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 4: Red, blue and yellow.

Fun for Starters · Week 4: Red, blue and yellow | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Paint and see Lesson 2 · Me and my friend Lesson 3 · Colour it in!

You Need

  • Paint in red, blue, yellow, white and black, a brush, water, a plate to mix on, and paper. Crayons work too if you don't want paint today.
  • "Mix" is a Flyers word. With Starters children, say "Blue and red make purple."
  • All the colours today are Starters words: red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, grey, white, black, brown.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Red Yellow Green Blue. Point to something of each colour.
  3. Colour hunt (4 min): "Find something purple! … something grey! … something pink!" Your child runs, finds and names it: "My bag is purple!" Colour words
  4. Paint and see (7 min): put two colours together on the plate. "Blue and red… what colour is it now?" "Purple!" Then white and black (grey), blue and yellow (green), yellow and red (orange), red and white (pink).
  5. Colour sums (5 min): write "blue + yellow = ______". Your child reads it out and writes the colour word, then paints a dot in that colour next to it.
  6. Watch and move (2 min): If It's Yellow and You Know It.
  7. Rainbow picture (5 min): your child paints a picture with the new colours and tells you: "This is a green tree. This is an orange sun."
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Blue and red make… purple!" … "What colour is it now?" … "Find something pink!"
