Week 4 · Lesson 2: Me and My Friend

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours, My Body and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 4: Red, blue and yellow.

Fun for Starters · Week 4: Red, blue and yellow | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Paint and see Lesson 2 · Me and my friend Lesson 3 · Colour it in!

You Need

  • A mirror, crayons, and a table drawn on paper with two columns, "Me" and "My friend" (a parent, a brother or sister, or a toy), and five rows.
  • Word cards in pairs that look or sound alike: goat / boat, cat / hat, kite / white, boy / toy, and the toys or pictures that go with them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What's Your Favorite Color? Answer the song with your own colour.
  3. Me and my friend (6 min): ask five questions: "What colour are your eyes? … your T-shirt? … your bedroom? … your hair? What's your favourite colour?" Your child colours a box for each answer, then asks the friend and colours that column too.
  4. In the mirror (4 min): your child looks and says: "My eyes are brown. My hair is black. My T-shirt is blue." Body words · Clothes words
  5. Which word? (6 min): show a toy or picture and two word cards: "Is this a goat or a boat?" Your child reads both and picks one: "This is a goat." Then cat / hat, kite / white, boy / toy.
  6. Watch (3 min): I See Something Blue.
  7. I spy (4 min): "I spy something pink!" Your child looks and guesses. Then your child chooses a colour for you.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour are your eyes?" … "My eyes are brown." … "Is this a goat or a boat?"
