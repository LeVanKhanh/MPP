Week 4 · Lesson 3: Colour It In!

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours, My Street and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 4: Red, blue and yellow.

Fun for Starters · Week 4: Red, blue and yellow | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Paint and see Lesson 2 · Me and my friend Lesson 3 · Colour it in!

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a simple park picture in pencil: a tree, three birds (one in the tree, one under the tree, one on a boy's head), a kite and a ball.
  • Small drawings of a car, a pair of shoes, a bike and an ice cream to colour, and crayons.
  • In the Starters listening test, children hear where things are and colour them. Today's first game practises this.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour (8 min): slowly: "Can you see the bird in the tree? Colour it yellow." "Now the bird on the boy's head. Colour it blue." Then the third bird, the kite and the ball. Check together: "What colour is the kite?"
  3. Watch (3 min): Learn Colors – Preschool Chant.
  4. Great colours (6 min): "What's a good colour for a car?" "Red!" Your child colours the car, shoes, bike and ice cream, then asks you: "What colour do you like for a bike?" Street words
  5. Ice cream shop (5 min): your child sells you coloured paper ice creams. "Can I have a pink ice cream, please?" "Here you are!" Then swap. Food words
  6. Colour race (3 min): "Touch something green! … something white!" Your child runs to it. Then your child calls a colour for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Colour the bird in the tree yellow." … "What's a good colour for a car?" … "Can I have a pink ice cream, please?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and write all the Starters colours, including purple, grey, pink and orange.
  • Ask and answer "What colour is / are…?" and "What's your favourite colour?"
  • Listen to where something is and colour it the right colour.
