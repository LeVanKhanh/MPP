Week 5 · Lesson 1: Tick, Cross and Write

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 5: Answering questions.

Fun for Starters · Week 5: Answering questions | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Tick, cross and write Lesson 2 · Yes or no? Lesson 3 · Who's who?

You Need

  • Word cards: alphabet, letter, word, sentence, question, number, line, box, tick ✓, cross ✗. Three paper strips, paper with six empty boxes, and pencils.
  • This unit is about the words in Starters test instructions. A child who knows "tick", "cross", "line" and "sentence" can focus on the question, not on the instructions.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Test words (6 min): show each word card with an example: a ✓ for "tick", an ✗ for "cross", a – for "line", A for "letter", 7 for "number". Your child reads the card and points to the example.
  3. Pencil says (6 min): on the paper with six boxes: "Draw a line! … Tick the box! … Put a cross in the box! … Write 'yes'! … Write a number! … Write one word!" Your child does one in each box.
  4. Watch (3 min): The Alphabet Is So Much Fun. "How many letters are in the alphabet?"
  5. Word, sentence, question (5 min): three strips: "cat" · "I like cats." · "Do you like cats?" Your child says which is a word, a sentence and a question, and counts the words in each.
  6. Mini crossword (5 min): draw a small crossword with three of today's words (box, line, tick) and a picture clue for each. Your child writes one letter in each square.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Tick the box!" … "Put a cross in the box!" … "Is this a word, a sentence or a question?"
