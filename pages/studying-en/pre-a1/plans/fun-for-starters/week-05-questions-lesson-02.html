Week 5 · Lesson 2: Yes or No?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 5: Answering questions.

Fun for Starters · Week 5: Answering questions | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Tick, cross and write Lesson 2 · Yes or no? Lesson 3 · Who's who?

You Need

  • A busy picture from any picture book (a park, a garden or a classroom), paper and pencils, and a few toys.
  • Before the lesson, write five short sentences about the picture. Make some true and some not: "The dog is brown and white." "The ball is purple." "The frog is on a book."
  • In the Starters reading test, children look at a picture and write "yes" or "no". Every word in the sentence matters: "a brown dog" is not "a brown and white dog".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Thumbs up, thumbs down (5 min): say sentences about the room: "The door is open." "The cat is on the bed." "Daddy's T-shirt is green." Your child shows thumbs up for yes, thumbs down for no.
  3. Watch (3 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream? Shout "Yes!" or "No!" with the song.
  4. Look and read (8 min): your child reads your five sentences one by one, looks carefully at the picture and writes "yes" or "no" next to each one.
  5. Make it yes (4 min): for each "no", your child says what is true: "No! The ball is red." in, on, under
  6. Toy tricks (5 min): your child puts toys around the room and writes one sentence: "The bear is under the chair." You say yes or no. Then you try to trick your child!
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Look at the picture. Is it yes or no?" … "No! The ball is red." … "The bear is under the chair."
