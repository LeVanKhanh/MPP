Week 5 · Lesson 3: Who's Who?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 5: Answering questions.

Fun for Starters · Week 5: Answering questions | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Tick, cross and write Lesson 2 · Yes or no? Lesson 3 · Who's who?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a simple garden with five children doing different things (one with a kite, one reading, one painting, one sleeping, one playing with a dog). Write five Starters names around the edge: Ben, Kim, Hugo, Eva, Sam.
  • Sentence cards for the acting game: "The boy is sleeping." "I'm eating an apple." "The girl is painting." "I'm reading a book." "The dog is running."
  • In the Starters listening test, children hear about people in a picture and draw a line from each name to the right person.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw lines (8 min): say slowly: "Kim is the girl with the kite." "Hugo is sleeping." "Eva is reading a book." Your child draws a line from each name to the right person. Check together: "Who's painting? Ben!"
  3. Watch and move (4 min): Yes, I Can! Do every action with the song.
  4. Act the sentence (6 min): your child reads a sentence card and acts it without words; you guess: "You're eating an apple!" Then swap.
  5. My favourite word (3 min): "What's your favourite English word?" Your child writes it in a big star and draws it.
  6. Five words, four words (4 min): your child writes a sentence with five words ("I like my red bike.") and a question with four words ("Do you like cats?"), counting the words on their fingers.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who's sleeping?" … "Draw a line from Kim to the girl with the kite." … "What's your favourite English word?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read and follow test instructions: draw a line, tick, put a cross, write a word or a number.
  • Look at a picture, read a sentence and write "yes" or "no".
  • Listen and draw lines from names to people, and write a short sentence and a question.
