Week 6 · Lesson 1: A or An?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 6: Animals and aliens.

Fun for Starters · Week 6: Animals and aliens | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A or an? Lesson 2 · Draw an alien Lesson 3 · Fantastic animals

You Need

  • Word cards: arm, body, ear, eye, face, foot, hand, head, leg, mouth, nose, tail, elephant, mouse. Two big cards, "a" and "an". A bag with a few toy animals inside.
  • The rule, simply: say "an" before a word that starts with a, e, i, o or u (an arm, an ear, an eye, an elephant), and "a" before the others (a hand, a mouse).
  • Watch for one irregular plural: one foot, two feet.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): My Face Parts. Touch each part with the song.
  3. Touch your… (4 min): "Touch your ear! … your foot! … your face!" Faster and faster. Then your child gives the orders. Body words
  4. A or an? (6 min): your child reads each word card and puts it under "a" or "an", saying it out loud: "an arm, a hand, an eye, a leg, an elephant, a mouse."
  5. This is, these are (5 min): point to one, then two: "This is an eye. These are eyes." "This is a hand. These are hands." "This is a foot. These are… feet!"
  6. Watch and move (2 min): Head Shoulders Knees & Toes (Learn It).
  7. Mystery bag (5 min): your child feels a toy animal in the bag without looking: "It's got a long tail… It's a mouse!" or "It's an elephant!" Animal words
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it a or an?" … "This is an eye. These are eyes." … "One foot, two feet!"
