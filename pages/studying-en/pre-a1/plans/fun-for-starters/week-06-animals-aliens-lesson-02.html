Week 6 · Lesson 2: Draw an Alien

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 6: Animals and aliens.

Fun for Starters · Week 6: Animals and aliens | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A or an? Lesson 2 · Draw an alien Lesson 3 · Fantastic animals

You Need

  • Paper, pencils and crayons. Before the lesson, draw your own funny alien and write nine short sentences next to it, each with an empty box: "This is a hand. ☐" "These are arms. ☐" Make some true and some not.
  • "Alien" is a Starters word, and children love it. It's a great way to practise body words with silly numbers: three eyes, five legs!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw (7 min): describe an alien slowly; your child draws it: "My alien has got a big head and three eyes. It's got four arms, two long legs and a green tail." Then compare it with yours.
  3. Tick or cross (6 min): show your own alien and the nine sentences. Your child reads each one, looks at the picture and puts a tick or a cross in the box.
  4. Watch (4 min): Me! Point to each part of your body.
  5. Your alien (5 min): now your child describes an alien and you draw it. Your child checks: "No! It's got five eyes!"
  6. Make an animal (3 min): draw a funny animal together: the head of an elephant, the body of a fish and the tail of a lizard. "What's its name?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "My alien has got three eyes." … "These are arms. Tick or cross?" … "It's got the tail of a lizard!"
