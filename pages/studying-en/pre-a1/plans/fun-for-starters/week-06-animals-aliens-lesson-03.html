Week 6 · Lesson 3: Fantastic Animals

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 6: Animals and aliens.

Fun for Starters · Week 6: Animals and aliens | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A or an? Lesson 2 · Draw an alien Lesson 3 · Fantastic animals

You Need

  • Pictures or toys of an elephant, a giraffe and a horse. Paper tails for five animals (cat, dog, horse, mouse, lizard), hidden around the room.
  • Copy the short story below onto paper, with a box of word pictures under it: zoo, legs, ears, fruit, water, and two extra words, shoe and hand.
  • This is like the Starters reading task where children choose a word from pictures and write it in the story. The two extra words are there to make your child read carefully.

Elephants: You can see elephants at the (1) ______. Elephants are very big. They have got four big (2) ______ and two very big (3) ______. They like eating (4) ______, and they love (5) ______. They swim in it and play with it!

Answers: 1 zoo, 2 legs, 3 ears, 4 fruit, 5 water.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Animal talk (4 min): look at the elephant, giraffe and horse. "What has the giraffe got?" "It's got long legs… and a long tail!" "Can you ride a horse?" Animal words
  3. Watch (3 min): Wild Animals What Is It? Guess the animal before the song says it.
  4. Read and choose (8 min): read the elephant story together. Your child chooses a word from the picture box for each gap and writes it. Shoe and hand are not in the story!
  5. One word on the line (5 min): write about one of your child's toy animals: "This is a ______. It's got a long ______. It likes eating ______." Your child writes one word on each line.
  6. Find the tail (5 min): your child hunts for the paper tails. Ask: "Have you got the lizard's tail?" "Yes, I have!" or "No, I haven't." Then hide them for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Elephants are very big." … "Have you got the lizard's tail?" … "Yes, I have! / No, I haven't."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the parts of the body and use "a" or "an" before them.
  • Say "This is…" for one thing and "These are…" for more, including feet.
  • Read a short animal story and choose the right word for each gap.
