Week 7 · Lesson 1: What's Missing?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: My Body. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 7: Look, listen, smile, draw.

Fun for Starters · Week 7: Look, listen, smile, draw | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's missing? Lesson 2 · I can smile, I can kick Lesson 3 · Robots and monkeys

You Need

  • Letter cards for mixed-up words, paper and pencils. Before the lesson, draw five simple faces, each with one part missing (no ears, no hair, no eyes, no nose, no mouth).
  • A big paper face with no eyes, nose or mouth, plus paper eyes, a nose and a mouth to put on it.
  • Use "his" for a boy and "her" for a girl: "his ears", "her hair".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): This Is My Face. Point along with the song.
  3. Mixed-up words (5 min): "r e a… ear!" Then face, hand, nose, feet, mouth. Your child moves the letters and writes each word. Body words
  4. What's not there? (7 min): show the five faces. "Look at the boy. What's not there?" "His ears!" Your child writes "his ears" or "her hair" under each face, then draws the missing part.
  5. Touch race (3 min): "Touch your nose with your hand! … Touch your ear with your foot!" Silly ones are the best.
  6. Watch (2 min): Ten Little Fingers.
  7. Funny face (5 min): your child closes their eyes and puts the paper eyes, nose and mouth on the big face. Then: "Look! The nose is on the ear!" Swap and laugh.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's not there?" … "His ears!" … "Touch your nose with your hand!"
