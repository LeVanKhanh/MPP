Week 7 · Lesson 2: I Can Smile, I Can Kick

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 7: Look, listen, smile, draw.

Fun for Starters · Week 7: Look, listen, smile, draw | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's missing? Lesson 2 · I can smile, I can kick Lesson 3 · Robots and monkeys

You Need

  • Action cards: smile, kick, wave, see, look, listen, run, throw. Body cards: mouth, foot, hand, eyes, ears, legs. A ball and some space (a garden or a park is great).
  • The pattern for today: "I listen with my ears." One action word, one body word.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Action words (5 min): show each action card and do it together: smile, kick, wave, look, listen, run. Then you do one and your child finds the card.
  3. Watch (2 min): If You're Happy And You Know It.
  4. What do you do with…? (6 min): "What do you do with your ears?" "I listen with my ears!" Your child matches a body card to an action card and writes one or two sentences.
  5. Simon says (4 min): "Simon says wave! … Simon says smile! … Kick!" (Don't kick if Simon didn't say it!)
  6. Ball time (5 min): "Kick the ball! … Throw the ball! … Run!" Then your child says: "I can kick a ball with my foot."
  7. Watch and move (3 min): Stand Up, Sit Down.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): wave goodbye with Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you do with your eyes?" … "I look with my eyes." … "I can kick a ball with my foot!"
