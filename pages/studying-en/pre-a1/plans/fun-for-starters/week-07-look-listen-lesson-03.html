Week 7 · Lesson 3: Robots and Monkeys

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and My Body. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 7: Look, listen, smile, draw.

Fun for Starters · Week 7: Look, listen, smile, draw | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What's missing? Lesson 2 · I can smile, I can kick Lesson 3 · Robots and monkeys

You Need

  • For the robot: a big box, a small box, two socks, a paper clock and tape. For the monkey game: one dice, paper and a pencil for each player.
  • A family photo or a busy picture-book page with people doing things (smiling, waving, sitting).
  • "Dice" is not a Starters word, so say "Throw it!" and "What number is it?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Make a robot (8 min): build a robot together. Then your child writes one word on each line: "The small box is the robot's ______ (head). The ______ (clock) is the robot's face. The ______ (socks) are the robot's arms." Draw its eyes, ears and nose.
  3. Robot walk (3 min): "Walk like a robot! … Wave like a robot! … Smile like a robot!"
  4. Watch (2 min): Up and Down.
  5. What are they doing? (6 min): look at the photo or picture: "What are they doing?" "They're smiling! The baby is waving." Write three short sentences with a gap and a word box; your child writes the missing words.
  6. Draw the monkey (6 min): throw the dice and draw: 1 the body, 2 the head, 3 an eye, 4 an ear, 5 an arm or a leg, 6 the tail. Say what you draw: "I've got the monkey's tail!" First to finish the monkey wins.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The clock is the robot's face." … "What are they doing? They're smiling!" … "Throw it! What number is it?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell the face and body words and say what is missing with "his" and "her".
  • Say what we do with each part: "I listen with my ears. I kick with my foot."
  • Use "the robot's face" and say what people in a picture are doing.
