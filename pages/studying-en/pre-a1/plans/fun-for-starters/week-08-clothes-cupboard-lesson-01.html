Week 8 · Lesson 1: In My Cupboard

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 8: In my clothes cupboard.

Fun for Starters · Week 8: In my clothes cupboard | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In my cupboard Lesson 2 · Which one is Tom? Lesson 3 · There is, there are

You Need

  • A clothes cupboard (or a pile of clothes) with as many of these as you can find: bag, shirt, T-shirt, jeans, glasses, socks, dress, hat, skirt, shoes, trousers, shorts, jacket. A bag, sticky notes and a pencil.
  • Jeans, trousers, shorts and glasses are always "these are": "These are my jeans." Socks and shoes come in pairs, so they are usually "these are" too.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Clothes Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Cupboard hunt (6 min): open the cupboard. Your child takes things out one by one and names them: "This is a skirt." "These are jeans!" Clothes words
  4. Label it (6 min): your child writes the word on a sticky note and sticks it on each thing: hat, dress, jacket, shorts… Check the spelling together.
  5. What's in the bag? (3 min): put one thing in the bag. Your child feels it without looking: "It's a sock!"
  6. Watch (3 min): Let's Get Dressed Song.
  7. Dress-up race (5 min): "Put on a hat! … Put on Daddy's jacket! … Put on Mummy's glasses!" Take a funny photo at the end.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in the cupboard?" … "This is a skirt. These are jeans." … "Put on a hat!"
