Week 8 · Lesson 2: Which One Is Tom?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Clothes, At Home and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 8: In my clothes cupboard.

Fun for Starters · Week 8: In my clothes cupboard | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In my cupboard Lesson 2 · Which one is Tom? Lesson 3 · There is, there are

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three boys, A, B and C, in different clothes (one in a green T-shirt and black trousers, one in a green T-shirt and shorts, one in a red shirt and jeans). Do the same with three women: one has glasses.
  • A T-shirt to hide, and a picture of a living room (a picture book or a photo of your own room).
  • In the Starters listening test, children hear about a person and tick the right picture. The pictures are almost the same, so every word counts.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (7 min): "Which boy is Tom? Tom's got a green T-shirt and black trousers." Your child ticks A, B or C. Then: "Which woman is Dan's teacher? She's got glasses and a long skirt."
  3. Watch (3 min): What Are You Wearing?
  4. What are you wearing? (4 min): take turns: "I'm wearing a blue T-shirt and grey shorts." "Daddy is wearing…"
  5. Where's the T-shirt? (5 min): hide the T-shirt: on the bed, under the chair, in the bag. Your child looks and says where it is: "It's under the chair!" in, on, under
  6. One-word answers (6 min): look at the living room picture. "How many sofas are there?" "What colour is the lamp?" "Where is the jacket?" Your child writes one or two words for each answer. Home words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which boy is Tom?" … "What are you wearing?" … "Where's the T-shirt? It's under the chair!"
