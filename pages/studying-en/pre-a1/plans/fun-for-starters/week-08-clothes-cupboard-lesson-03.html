Week 8 · Lesson 3: There Is, There Are

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 8: In my clothes cupboard.

Fun for Starters · Week 8: In my clothes cupboard | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In my cupboard Lesson 2 · Which one is Tom? Lesson 3 · There is, there are

You Need

  • Word cards to make sentences, for example: four · There are · green · chairs, and one · There is · happy · baby. Some empty cards and a pencil.
  • The rule: "There is" for one thing (There is one lamp), "There are" for two or more (There are two sofas).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Count the room (5 min): "How many chairs are there?" Count together: "There are four chairs!" "How many lamps?" "There is one lamp." Home words
  3. Word blocks (7 min): your child puts the word cards in order: "There are four green chairs." "There is one happy baby." Then they count something in the room and write their own sentence on empty cards.
  4. Watch (4 min): Clothes Vocabulary Chant.
  5. The long clothes sentence (6 min): take turns and add one thing each time: "In my cupboard there's a hat." "In my cupboard there's a hat and a red dress." "…a hat, a red dress and two socks!" Who can remember the longest sentence?
  6. Tidy up (3 min): put everything back in the cupboard, naming each thing: "Jeans in the cupboard! Shoes under the bed!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many chairs are there?" … "There are four chairs. There is one lamp." … "In my cupboard there's a hat and…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and spell the Starters clothes words, with "this is" for one and "these are" for jeans, shorts and glasses.
  • Listen to what someone is wearing and find the right person.
  • Count things and say "There is one…" or "There are four…"
