Week 9 · Lesson 1: Read and Draw a Monster

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body, Colours and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 9: Funny monsters.

Fun for Starters · Week 9: Funny monsters | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Read and draw a monster Lesson 2 · Big, small, silly, scary Lesson 3 · Which monster am I?

You Need

  • Paper, pencils and crayons. Copy the monster story below onto paper.
  • "It's got…" and "It hasn't got…" are the key patterns this week. "Its" (no apostrophe) means "belonging to it": its arms, its feet.

My monster: My monster has got three heads and five eyes. It's got two noses. It hasn't got a mouth! It's got four arms and one long tail. Its feet are very big.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Go Away! (Super Simple Songs). Shout "Go away!" with the song.
  3. Read and draw (8 min): your child reads the monster story, writes the number next to each part (3 heads, 5 eyes…) and draws the monster. "Has it got a mouth?" "No, it hasn't!"
  4. Read and colour (5 min): "Its arms are green. Its feet are orange. Colour its eyes pink." Your child colours the monster. Colour words
  5. Watch (2 min): Go Away Monster (Dream English). Count the monster's eyes and arms.
  6. Dress the monster (5 min): "Today it's wearing a baseball cap, boots and a big T-shirt." Your child draws the clothes and colours them. Clothes words
  7. Monster moves (3 min): "Walk like a monster with big feet! … Wave your four arms! … Make a scary face!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's got three heads!" … "Has it got a mouth? No, it hasn't." … "Its feet are very big."
