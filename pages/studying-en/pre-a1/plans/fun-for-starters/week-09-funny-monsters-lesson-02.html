Week 9 · Lesson 2: Big, Small, Silly, Scary

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Opposites and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 9: Funny monsters.

Fun for Starters · Week 9: Funny monsters | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Read and draw a monster Lesson 2 · Big, small, silly, scary Lesson 3 · Which monster am I?

You Need

  • Play dough or paper, scissors and glue to make a monster. Word cards: big, small, happy, sad, beautiful, ugly, scary, silly, funny.
  • All these describing words are Starters words. "Very" and "really" make them stronger: "It's very big and really funny!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Opposite pairs (5 min): match the word cards in pairs: big – small, happy – sad, beautiful – ugly, scary – silly. Show each one with your body or your face. Opposite words
  3. Watch (2 min): Opposites Song for Kids.
  4. Make a monster (8 min): your child makes a monster from play dough or paper. How many heads? How many eyes? Any tails?
  5. Describe your monster (6 min): your child chooses words and writes about it: "My monster is very big and happy. It's really funny! It drinks milk and juice. Ice cream is its favourite food." Food words
  6. Monster faces (4 min): "Make a scary face! … a silly face! … a sad face! … a happy face!" Then your child calls the faces for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is your monster big or small?" … "It's very big and really funny!" … "What's its favourite food?"
