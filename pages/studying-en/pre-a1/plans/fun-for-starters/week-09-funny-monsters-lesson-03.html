Week 9 · Lesson 3: Which Monster Am I?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body, Colours and Opposites. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 9: Funny monsters.

Fun for Starters · Week 9: Funny monsters | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Read and draw a monster Lesson 2 · Big, small, silly, scary Lesson 3 · Which monster am I?

You Need

  • Paper and crayons, tape, and the monsters from Lessons 1 and 2.
  • Before the lesson, draw a wall of ten boxes on paper and write a body or colour word in each box (eyes, arms, tail, pink, green…).
  • A table with four columns: monster, body, hair, eyes.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Monster wall (5 min): draw two more monsters together, so you have four: one with a flower in its hair, one holding a baby, one with a robot in its hand, one with glasses. Number them 1–4 and tape them on the wall.
  3. Which monster am I? (5 min): "I've got a flower in my hair. Which monster am I?" Your child says and writes the number. Then your child gives the clues.
  4. Colour table (4 min): your child looks at each monster and writes colours in the table: "Monster 1: body blue, hair black, eyes red." Colour words
  5. Watch (3 min): Adjectives and Opposites Song.
  6. Name them (5 min): give each monster a name. Then say slowly: "Hi! My name's Bounce. I love reading funny stories." Your child writes the name and one missing word.
  7. Colour the wall (3 min): say a word from the wall; your child finds it and colours the box. "What's that?" "It's a funny monster!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Which monster am I?" … "I've got a flower in my hair." … "Hi! My name's Bounce."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read about a monster and draw it: "It's got three heads. It hasn't got a mouth."
  • Describe something with big, small, scary, silly, beautiful, ugly and funny.
  • Listen to clues and find the right picture.
