Week 10 · Lesson 1: Which Family?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 10: Our families.

Fun for Starters · Week 10: Our families | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which family? Lesson 2 · Our pets Lesson 3 · My family, my home

You Need

  • Family word cards: mum, dad, brother, sister, grandma, grandpa, cousin, baby. A family photo.
  • A list of twelve words, some about family and some not: mum, cat, dad, ball, sister, tree, grandpa, cake, brother, bike, cousin, hat.
  • Before the lesson, copy the story below and draw three quick families with stick people: one that matches the story and two that don't (for example, one with two babies, one with no grandma).

Kim's family: My name's Kim. I live in a small flat with my mum, my dad and my baby brother. My grandma lives in the flat next to us. We love animals. We've got a cat and two fish. Our cat's name is Sue.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Meet My Family!
  3. Family words (4 min): your child reads each card and puts it next to the right person in the family photo: "This is my grandpa!" Family words
  4. Circle the family words (3 min): your child reads the list of twelve words and draws a circle round each family word.
  5. Read and choose (8 min): read Kim's story together. "Which picture is Kim's family?" Your child checks each picture: "Not this one. Kim's got one brother, not two!"
  6. Who's who? (5 min): point to people in your family photo: "Who's that?" "That's my cousin!" Then your child asks you.
  7. Watch (2 min): Finger Family Song.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which picture is Kim's family?" … "Who's that? That's my grandma." … "She lives next to us."
