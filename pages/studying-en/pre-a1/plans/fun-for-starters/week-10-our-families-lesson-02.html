Week 10 · Lesson 2: Our Pets

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 10: Our families.

Fun for Starters · Week 10: Our families | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which family? Lesson 2 · Our pets Lesson 3 · My family, my home

You Need

  • Toy animals (one cat, one dog, one donkey, two or three fish), paper and pencils. Before the lesson, draw four speech bubbles: "This is my ____." "These are our ____." "This is our ____." "These are my ____."
  • Two small pairs: "this" for one, "these" for more than one; "my" for me, "our" for my family and me.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant. Animal words
  3. This or these? (6 min): hold up one toy: "This is our cat." Hold up the fish: "These are our fish." Your child copies, then chooses the toys and says the sentences.
  4. Speech bubbles (5 min): your child puts a toy next to each speech bubble and writes the missing word: "This is my donkey." "These are our fish."
  5. Pet names (4 min): give each toy pet a name: "Our dog's name is Ben. Our cat's name is May." Your child writes one name sentence.
  6. Where does it sleep? (4 min): "Where does the dog sleep?" Your child puts it somewhere: "It sleeps in the garden!" "The cat sleeps on the bed." Home words
  7. Pet show (2 min): your child acts a pet without words; you guess: "Are you a donkey?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is our cat. These are our fish." … "What's your dog's name?" … "Where does it sleep?"
