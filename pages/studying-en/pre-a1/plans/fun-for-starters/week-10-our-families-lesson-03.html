Week 10 · Lesson 3: My Family, My Home

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Family, At Home and My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 10: Our families.

Fun for Starters · Week 10: Our families | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which family? Lesson 2 · Our pets Lesson 3 · My family, my home

You Need

  • A big sheet of paper for an "All about me" poster, crayons, and family photos (or quick drawings) for the guessing game.
  • Write the poster frame before the lesson, with choices for your child to circle: "My name's ____. I live in a big / small house / flat with my ____. My ____ lives / live next to us. We love ____, but we haven't got a ____!"
  • This poster is for home. If you share it, leave out your street name and anything that shows where you live.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Answer the questions (6 min): "Is our street long or short? How many people live in our house? Do your grandma and grandpa live with us? Have you got a lot of cousins? Has our family got a pet?" Your child writes a short answer for each. Street words
  3. Watch (3 min): The People In My Family.
  4. All about me (8 min): your child draws their face in the middle of the poster, then circles the right words and writes names: "I live in a small flat with my mum, my dad and my sister." Home words
  5. Watch (2 min): She's My Mother. Point to the people in your photos.
  6. Who's that? (6 min): cover a photo with paper and show a little at a time. Your child guesses: "Is it my cousin?" "Who's that? It's Grandpa!" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many people live in our house?" … "I live in a small flat with my…" … "Who's that?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a short text about a family and find the right picture.
  • Use "this / these" and "my / our" to talk about pets and things.
  • Answer simple questions about their family and home, and write a few sentences about themselves.
