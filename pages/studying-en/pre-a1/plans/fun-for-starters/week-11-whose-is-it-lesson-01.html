Week 11 · Lesson 1: Whose Is It?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and Family. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 11: Whose is it?

Fun for Starters · Week 11: Whose is it? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Whose is it? Lesson 2 · Follow the lines Lesson 3 · His or hers?

You Need

  • Six real things and a sentence card for each, some true and some not: a robot ("This is a robot."), socks ("These are socks."), a ball ("This is a cake."), a keyboard, a tablet, a teddy ("This is a tablet.").
  • Things from different people at home: Daddy's watch, Mummy's glasses, a brother's or sister's kite, your child's own toys. A box.
  • The patterns: "Whose watch is it? It's Daddy's. It's his." "Whose glasses are they? They're Mummy's. They're hers." "Is this yours? Yes, it's mine!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tick or cross (5 min): put each sentence card next to its thing. Your child reads it and puts a tick or a cross: "This is a cake. No! It's a ball. Cross!"
  3. Whose is it? (7 min): hold up each thing from the family: "Whose watch is it?" "It's Daddy's!" "Yes, it's his." Then glasses: "They're Mummy's. They're hers." Family words
  4. Watch (6 min): Yours? Mine! – Whose bike is this?
  5. Mine or yours? (4 min): point to things: "Is this yours?" "Yes, it's mine!" or "No, it's yours!" Then your child asks you. Toy words
  6. Lost and found (3 min): put everything in the box. Your child takes each thing back to its owner: "This is yours, Daddy!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Whose watch is it?" … "It's Daddy's. It's his." … "Is this yours? Yes, it's mine!"
