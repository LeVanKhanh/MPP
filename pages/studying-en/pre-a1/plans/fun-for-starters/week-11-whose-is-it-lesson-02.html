Week 11 · Lesson 2: Follow the Lines

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 11: Whose is it?

Fun for Starters · Week 11: Whose is it? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Whose is it? Lesson 2 · Follow the lines Lesson 3 · His or hers?

You Need

  • Tangled lines: before the lesson, write six names across the top of a page (Kim, Nick, Sue, Ben, Grace, Tom) and draw six things along the bottom (a mouse, a watch, a tennis ball, a clock, a kite, some paints). Join each name to one thing with long wiggly lines that cross each other.
  • Five friends: on another page, draw five friends with their names on their T-shirts (Lucy, Ann, Bill, Tom, Nick) and six things under them: a camera, glasses, a ruler, a toy car, a radio, some pens.
  • A few small things to hide behind your back.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tangled lines (8 min): your child follows each line with a crayon, then answers and writes: "Whose mouse is it?" "It's Nick's. It's his." "Whose paints are they?" "They're Grace's. They're hers."
  3. Watch (3 min): Do You Have A Crayon?
  4. Listen and draw lines (8 min): say slowly: "Lucy has got a camera. She likes taking photos." "Bill has got the ruler." "Nick's got glasses." Your child draws a line from each thing to its owner. One thing has no owner! School words
  5. What have I got? (4 min): hide something behind your back. "Have you got a ruler?" "No, I haven't." "Have you got a pen?" "Yes, I have!" Then swap. Toy words
  6. Find it fast (2 min): "Find something that's Mummy's! … something that's yours!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Whose kite is it? It's Ben's. It's his." … "Have you got a ruler?" … "Yes, I have! / No, I haven't."
