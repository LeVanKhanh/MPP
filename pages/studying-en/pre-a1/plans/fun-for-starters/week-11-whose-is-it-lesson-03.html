Week 11 · Lesson 3: His or Hers?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 11: Whose is it?

Fun for Starters · Week 11: Whose is it? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Whose is it? Lesson 2 · Follow the lines Lesson 3 · His or hers?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a cupboard with nine things on its shelves: a football, a green T-shirt, crayons, a green pen, a robot (Ben's things) and a radio, a guitar, a beach ball, a hat (Sue's things). Copy the short text below.
  • Two bags with four secret things in each, and the family's shoes in a pile.

Ben and Sue: Ben has got five things in this cupboard. His favourite colour is green. He likes football, drawing and robots. Sue has got four things. She likes music and going to the beach.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and sort (8 min): read about Ben and Sue together. Your child looks at the cupboard and decides: "The guitar is hers. The football is his." Then writes: "The football, the crayons… are Ben's things."
  3. Watch (3 min): I Have A Toy.
  4. What have you got? (6 min): each player has a secret bag. Take turns: "Have you got a ball?" "Yes, I have!" The first to guess everything in the other bag wins. Toy words
  5. Whose shoes? (5 min): pick shoes from the pile: "Whose shoes are these?" "They're Grandma's. They're hers!" Your child puts them next to the right person. Clothes words
  6. My things (3 min): your child draws three favourite things and writes under one: "This is my robot. It's mine!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is the guitar his or hers?" … "Whose shoes are these? They're Grandma's." … "It's mine!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Ask "Whose … is it? / Whose … are they?" and answer with a name: "It's Daddy's."
  • Use his, hers, mine and yours.
  • Ask and answer "Have you got…?" – "Yes, I have. / No, I haven't."
