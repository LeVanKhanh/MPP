Week 12 · Lesson 1: Men, Women, Children

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Family and My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 12: Who's got the red balloon?

You Need

  • A word snake on paper, with no spaces: manbabygirlwomenkidboychildrenwomanmen. Cards in pairs: man / men, woman / women, child / children, baby / babies, boy / boys, kid / kids.
  • A window onto the street or a park, or a busy picture book page with lots of people.
  • Three people words don't just add "s": one man, two men; one woman, two women; one child, two children. "Kid" is another word for "child".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Word snake (6 min): your child finds the words in the snake, draws a line between them (man | baby | girl…) and writes each word under it.
  3. One, two (6 min): match the pairs of cards: "One man, two men!" "One child, two children!" Your child reads each pair aloud and claps on the tricky ones.
  4. Watch (3 min): The People In My Family.
  5. People spotting (7 min): look out of the window or at the picture: "How many men can you see? How many children?" Your child counts and answers: "There are three women and two babies!" Street words
  6. How many people? (3 min): "How many people are there in our family?" "How many children are there in your class?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "One man, two men." … "How many children can you see?" … "How many people are there in our family?"
