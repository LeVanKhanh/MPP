Week 12 · Lesson 2: Who's Got the Red Balloon?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours, Toys and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 12: Who's got the red balloon?

Fun for Starters · Week 12: Who's got the red balloon? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Men, women, children Lesson 2 · Who's got the red balloon? Lesson 3 · What are they doing?

You Need

  • A red balloon (or a red ball) and some music.
  • Before the lesson, draw a park with people holding things: a man with a dog, a girl with a kite, a woman with a handbag, two boys with a ball, a baby with a red balloon, two women with flowers. Write six sentences under it, some true and some not: "A man has got a dog." "Two girls have got a duck."
  • The book asks about a "best friend"; "best" is a Movers word, so ask "Is your friend a boy or a girl?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Balloon Song. Name each balloon's colour. Colour words
  3. Yes or no? (8 min): your child reads each sentence, looks carefully at your park picture and writes "yes" or "no". Watch for one and two: "Two women have got flowers" is not "a woman"!
  4. Who's got the balloon? (5 min): someone hides the balloon behind their back. Your child asks: "Have you got the red balloon?" "No, I haven't!" … "Who's got it? Daddy's got it!" Toy words
  5. Ask and answer (6 min): "Is your friend a boy or a girl? Is your English teacher a man or a woman? How many children are there in your class?" Your child answers, then asks you. School words
  6. Balloon pass (3 min): pass the balloon while the music plays. When it stops: "Who's got the balloon?" "I've got it!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's got the red balloon?" … "A man has got a dog. Yes or no?" … "Is your teacher a man or a woman?"
