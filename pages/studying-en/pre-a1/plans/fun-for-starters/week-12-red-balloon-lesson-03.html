Week 12 · Lesson 3: What Are They Doing?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 12: Who's got the red balloon?

Fun for Starters · Week 12: Who's got the red balloon? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Men, women, children Lesson 2 · Who's got the red balloon? Lesson 3 · What are they doing?

You Need

  • A picture story of three scenes (from a picture book, or draw it quickly): two kids watching TV; a boy wanting a ball that's behind the sofa; the kids in the garden, one with a phone.
  • A toy cat to hide, paper and a pencil for the word game.
  • Answers in the Starters test are often one or two words: "the television", "behind the sofa", "in the garden".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Are You Doing? (Dream English). Do every action.
  3. Freeze! (5 min): everyone does something and freezes. Your child says what each person is doing: "Daddy is reading. Mummy is watching TV. I'm drawing!"
  4. Look and answer (7 min): look at the three scenes. "How many kids are there? What is the boy watching? What does the boy want? Where are the kids now? Who has got the phone?" Your child writes one or two words for each answer. Home words
  5. Watch (2 min): In Front Of, Behind, Between.
  6. Where's the cat? (4 min): hide the toy cat: "Where's the cat?" "It's behind the sofa!" "It's in the garden!" Then your child hides it. Position words
  7. Nine lives (5 min): draw nine hearts. Think of a people word (children, woman, baby…) and draw a line for each letter. Your child guesses letters; each wrong letter crosses out one heart. Then swap.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What is the boy doing?" … "He's watching TV." … "Where's the cat? It's behind the sofa!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use the people words, including men, women and children.
  • Read "A man has got a dog" and check it against a picture.
  • Answer "What is he doing?", "Where is…?" and "Who has got…?" with a short answer.
