Week 13 · Lesson 1: I Can, I Can't

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and Hello. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 13: Who can do this?

Fun for Starters · Week 13: Who can do this? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · I can, I can't Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · My classmates

You Need

  • Paper, pencils and crayons. Before the lesson, draw a table: four animals down the side (fish, bird, dog, frog) and five actions across the top (fly, swim, run, jump, sing).
  • "Can" never changes: I can, he can, it can. "Can't" is short for "cannot".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Yes, I Can! Do every action with the animals.
  3. Can you…? (5 min): "Can you jump?" "Yes, I can!" (jump) "Can you fly?" "No, I can't!" Then your child asks you.
  4. Ticks and crosses (8 min): fill in the table together with ✓ and ✗. Then riddles: "It can swim, but it can't fly or run. Which animal is it?" Your child writes the name. Then your child makes a riddle for you. Animal words
  5. Watch and move (2 min): Hello Hello! Can You Clap Your Hands?
  6. My can-do page (6 min): your child draws themselves and writes two sentences: "I can ride a bike. I can't fly!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Can you jump? Yes, I can!" … "It can swim, but it can't fly." … "Which animal is it?"
