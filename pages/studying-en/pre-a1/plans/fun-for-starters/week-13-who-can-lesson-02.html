Week 13 · Lesson 2: Listen and Tick

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 13: Who can do this?

Fun for Starters · Week 13: Who can do this? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · I can, I can't Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · My classmates

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five rows of three tiny pictures (A, B, C) with a box under each:
    1. a cat sleeping · a cat reading a book · a cat swimming
    2. a dog in a park · a dog at the beach · a dog in a garden
    3. a frog singing in bed · in front of the TV · in the bath
    4. a book about balloons · about spiders · about cars
    5. a baby who can swim · ride a bike · play the guitar
  • This is the Starters listening task: children hear a short talk and tick one of three pictures. Read each one twice, slowly.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Are You Doing? (Dream English).
  3. Listen and tick (10 min): for each row, say the question and the answer: "What is the cat doing now? Look! It's reading a book." "Where is the frog singing? In the bath!" "What can the baby do? He can ride a bike!" Your child ticks one box in each row.
  4. What am I doing? (5 min): act something out: "What am I doing?" "You're swimming!" "You're reading!" Then swap.
  5. Watch and move (3 min): Fast Slow Action Song.
  6. Draw and say (5 min): your child draws an animal and tells you what it can do: "This is my horse. It can run and jump, but it can't sing!" Animal words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What is the cat doing now?" … "Where is the frog singing?" … "What can the baby do?"
