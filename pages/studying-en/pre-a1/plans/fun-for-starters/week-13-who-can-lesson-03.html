Week 13 · Lesson 3: My Classmates

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 13: Who can do this?

Fun for Starters · Week 13: Who can do this? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · I can, I can't Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · My classmates

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw six children with stick figures, numbered 1–6, each holding one thing: a badminton bat, a horse, a paintbrush, a camera, a guitar, a fishing boat. Copy the six sentences below.
  • Word cards: badminton, tennis, guitar, camera, horse, bike, boat.
  • "Swimming" and "good at" are Movers words; at Starters say "I can swim" and "She can draw very well."

Kim has got a big garden. She plays badminton there with her dad.

Ben can ride a horse. He loves horses!

Lucy draws and paints fantastic pictures.

Tom has got a new camera. He can take really good photos.

May can play the guitar very well.

Nick has got a boat. He catches lots of fish.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write the names (9 min): your child reads each sentence, finds the right child in the picture and writes the name under the number.
  3. Can you play…? (5 min): hold up each word card: "Can you play the guitar?" "No, I can't." "Can you ride a bike?" "Yes, I can!" Toy words
  4. Watch and move (3 min): Stand Up, Sit Down.
  5. Stand up, sit down (5 min): "Stand up if you can swim! Sit down if you can't ride a horse! Stand up if you can sing!" Then your child gives the orders.
  6. My friend (3 min): your child writes one sentence about a real friend or classmate: "Ann can draw very well." School words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who can ride a horse? Ben!" … "Can you play the guitar?" … "Stand up if you can swim!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Ask and answer "Can you…? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't."
  • Say what animals and people can and can't do.
  • Listen and choose the right picture, and read short sentences to find who's who.
