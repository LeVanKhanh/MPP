Week 14 · Lesson 1: Old or New?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Opposites and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 14: Big, small, happy or sad?

Fun for Starters · Week 14: Big, small, happy or sad? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Old or new? Lesson 2 · Silly sentences Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • Real things: a computer or tablet, a pair of socks, a phone, a pair of trousers. A clean sock and a dirty sock, an old shoe and a new shoe, a long pencil and a short pencil.
  • Before the lesson, draw five quick pairs: a clean car and a dirty car, a young woman and an old woman, a beautiful spider and an ugly spider, a long skirt and a short skirt, an old shoe and a new shoe.
  • Watch "a" and "an": a new shoe, an old shoe; a beautiful spider, an ugly spider.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Opposites Song for Kids.
  3. What's this? (9 min): hold up each real thing and ask three questions: "What's this? Have you got a computer? Is it big or small?" "What are these? Are you wearing socks today? Are they long or short?" "Is this phone old or new?" "Are these trousers clean or dirty?" Clothes words
  4. Find the pairs (6 min): your child finds the two socks, two shoes and two pencils and says: "This sock is clean. This sock is dirty!" Opposite words
  5. Watch (2 min): Long Short Opposites Song.
  6. Write the word (6 min): show your five pairs of drawings. Your child writes the right word under each: "a clean car", "an old woman", "an ugly spider".
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it big or small?" … "Are they long or short?" … "This sock is clean. This sock is dirty!"
