Week 14 · Lesson 2: Silly Sentences

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Opposites, Animals and My Body. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 14: Big, small, happy or sad?

Fun for Starters · Week 14: Big, small, happy or sad? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Old or new? Lesson 2 · Silly sentences Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • Five columns of words on a big sheet, numbered 1–6 down the side (a dice makes it more fun):
    1. big · sad · funny · small · happy · short
    2. boy · girl · zebra · cat · elephant · mouse
    3. is drawing a · is painting a · is holding a · is playing with a · is riding a · is watching a
    4. red · blue · yellow · green · black · white
    5. snake · computer · kite · fish · alien · balloon
  • Paper and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Adjectives Words for Kids.
  3. Read the columns (4 min): read each column together. Act out the first column: make yourself big, small, funny, sad… Animal words
  4. Silly sentences (8 min): your child picks one word from each column (or throws the dice five times): "A funny elephant is riding a yellow snake!" Your child writes the sentence and draws it. Make two.
  5. Your turn to draw (5 min): your child makes a silly sentence for you, and you draw it. Did you get everything right?
  6. Make yourself… (4 min): "Make yourself big! … small! … happy! … sad! … old! … young!" Then your child calls them out.
  7. Watch (2 min): If You're Happy And You Know It.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A funny elephant is riding a yellow snake!" … "Make yourself small!" … "Draw a sad mouse!"
