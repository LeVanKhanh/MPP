Week 14 · Lesson 3: Look and Read

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Opposites and Clothes. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 14: Big, small, happy or sad?

Fun for Starters · Week 14: Big, small, happy or sad? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Old or new? Lesson 2 · Silly sentences Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four children in a park with a small dog, and write a name next to each: Pat (jeans, a blue and white T-shirt, dirty hands, smiling), Ann (long brown hair, a blue dress, happy), Eva (eating a chocolate ice cream, a pink skirt), Hugo (playing with Pat, red shoes).
  • Six sentences for yes or no: "The small dog is happy." "The girl in the blue dress is sad." "One boy is wearing red shoes." "One of the girls is eating an ice cream." "The boy with dirty hands is smiling." "These children are in a house."
  • Two bingo cards with nine opposite words each (big, happy, old, clean, long, beautiful, young, new, short…) and small things to cover them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look and read (8 min): your child reads each sentence, looks carefully at the picture and writes "yes" or "no". "The girl in the blue dress is sad. No! She's happy."
  3. Read, write and draw lines (7 min): say or write: "Pat's wearing ____ and a blue and white T-shirt." "Ann's got long brown ____." Your child writes the missing word, then draws a line from each name to the right child. Clothes words
  4. Watch (3 min): Adjectives and Opposites Song.
  5. Opposites bingo (7 min): say a word; your child covers its opposite: you say "small", your child covers "big". Three in a line: "Bingo!" Then swap. Opposite words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The small dog is happy. Yes or no?" … "What's Pat wearing?" … "Old!" "New!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use the Starters opposites: big / small, happy / sad, old / new, old / young, clean / dirty, long / short, beautiful / ugly.
  • Build a sentence like "A funny elephant is riding a yellow snake."
  • Read about people in a picture and say yes or no, and write a missing clothes word.
