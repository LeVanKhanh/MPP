Week 15 · Lesson 1: Animal Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 15: One, two, three animals.

Fun for Starters · Week 15: One, two, three animals | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Animal words Lesson 2 · Where are the pets? Lesson 3 · At the zoo

You Need

  • Toy animals or quick drawings, numbered 1–15: chicken, donkey, snake, frog, hippo, polar bear, spider, sheep, jellyfish, cow, crocodile, tiger, zebra, lizard, goat. A word card for each.
  • Three columns of describing words: big, long, beautiful, short, ugly, small · the colours · fly, jump, run, swim, walk.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): The Animals On The Farm. Make each animal's sound.
  3. Animal numbers (9 min): your child reads each word card, finds the animal and writes its number next to the word: "Jellyfish… number 3!" Animal words
  4. Describe them (6 min): your child chooses three words for some animals: "chicken – small, brown, walk." Then says it: "The chicken is small and brown. It can walk."
  5. Animal moves (4 min): "Walk like a chicken! … Swim like a jellyfish! … Jump like a frog!" Then your child calls one out.
  6. Watch (2 min): It's a Dog – Song with Actions.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which number is the hippo?" … "The chicken is small and brown." … "It can walk."
