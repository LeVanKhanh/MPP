Week 15 · Lesson 2: Where Are the Pets?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 15: One, two, three animals.

Fun for Starters · Week 15: One, two, three animals | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Animal words Lesson 2 · Where are the pets? Lesson 3 · At the zoo

You Need

  • Toy pets (a mouse, a spider, a frog, a fish, a lizard, a cat) and six places: a box, a ball, a shoe, a kite (or paper kite), a baseball cap, a bowl of water.
  • Before the lesson, draw the six pets on one side of a page and the six places on the other.
  • "Cap" on its own is not a Starters word; say "baseball cap".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): On In Under By Song.
  3. Pet places (5 min): put each toy pet somewhere: "Where's the mouse?" "It's in the box!" "Where's the frog?" "It's on the baseball cap!" in, on, under
  4. Listen and draw lines (8 min): say slowly: "Tom's mouse is in the shoe. The spider is on the ball. The lizard is under the kite. The fish is in the water." Your child draws a line from each pet to its place. Animal words
  5. Hide the pet (5 min): your child hides a pet while you close your eyes. "Is it under the bed?" "No, it isn't!" "Is it in the cupboard?" "Yes!" Then swap.
  6. Watch (2 min): Where is it?
  7. A home for a pet (3 min): your child draws a pet in a funny place and writes: "My frog is on the cake!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where's the mouse?" … "It's in the shoe!" … "Is it under the bed?"
