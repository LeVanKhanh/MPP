Week 15 · Lesson 3: At the Zoo

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 15: One, two, three animals.

Fun for Starters · Week 15: One, two, three animals | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Animal words Lesson 2 · Where are the pets? Lesson 3 · At the zoo

You Need

  • A busy zoo picture: a picture book page, or your own photo from a zoo trip.
  • The animal word cards from Lesson 1.
  • In the Starters speaking test, the examiner asks about a picture: "Where's…? What's this? How many…? What colour…? What's he doing?" Short answers are fine.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Let's Go To The Zoo.
  3. Talk about the picture (9 min): ask questions with each question word: "Where's the monkey?" "What's that?" "How many birds can you see?" "What colour is the snake?" "What's the man doing?" Your child answers, then asks you two questions.
  4. Guess the animal (6 min): "It's big and grey. It can swim. It lives at the zoo. What is it?" "It's a hippo!" Then your child gives the clues. Animal words
  5. Animal sort (6 min): your child sorts the word cards into two groups, "big" and "small", then writes three animals from each group.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many birds can you see?" … "What's the man doing?" … "It's big and grey. What is it?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read and write fifteen Starters animal words.
  • Describe an animal: "The chicken is small and brown. It can walk."
  • Say where something is and answer questions about a picture.
