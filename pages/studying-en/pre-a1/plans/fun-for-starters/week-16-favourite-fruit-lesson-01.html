Week 16 · Lesson 1: Fruit and Vegetables

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 16: What's your favourite fruit?

Fun for Starters · Week 16: What's your favourite fruit? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit and vegetables Lesson 2 · Tomatoes and potatoes Lesson 3 · Talk about the picture

You Need

  • Real fruit and vegetables, or pictures: apple, banana, beans, carrot, coconut, grapes, lemon, lime, mango, onion, orange, peas, pear, pineapple, potato, tomato, watermelon, kiwi. Two bowls.
  • Before the lesson, draw a small crossword with five of the words (pear, lime, onion, mango, kiwi) and a picture clue for each.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Fruit – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Fruit or vegetable? (6 min): your child names each one and puts it in the fruit bowl or the vegetable bowl: "A mango is a fruit. An onion is a vegetable." Fruit and vegetable words
  4. Mini crossword (6 min): your child looks at each picture clue and writes the word, one letter in each square.
  5. Watch (4 min): Fruit Song for Kids.
  6. What is it? (7 min): read each riddle; your child guesses and writes the word: "It's a very big fruit. It's green and red." (watermelon) "It's a small brown fruit, but you eat the green part." (kiwi) "It's long and orange. Horses love it." (carrot) "It's very small. It can be green or purple." (grape) "It's brown and white. You can find it on a tree at the beach." (coconut) Then your child makes one for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is a mango a fruit or a vegetable?" … "It's long and orange. What is it?" … "How do you spell lime?"
