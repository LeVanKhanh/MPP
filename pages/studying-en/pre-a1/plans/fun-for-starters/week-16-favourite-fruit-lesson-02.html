Week 16 · Lesson 2: Tomatoes and Potatoes

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 16: What's your favourite fruit?

Fun for Starters · Week 16: What's your favourite fruit? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit and vegetables Lesson 2 · Tomatoes and potatoes Lesson 3 · Talk about the picture

You Need

  • A few tomatoes, potatoes and other fruit, a jug or a bowl, cups. (A real fruit drink or fruit salad is the most fun, with grown-up help for cutting.)
  • A list of nine fruit and vegetables with a box next to each, for the funny drink game.
  • Words ending in -o add -es: one tomato, two tomatoes; one potato, two potatoes; one mango, two mangoes.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Spell and say (5 min): count real things: "One tomato, two tomatoes! One potato, two potatoes!" Your child writes "tomatoes" and "potatoes" and circles the "es".
  3. A funny drink (8 min): say slowly: "In my funny drink there are pears, a carrot, grapes, a banana and a lemon!" Your child ticks the five things in the list. Then: "What's in your drink?" Your child writes five things. Food and drink words
  4. Watch (3 min): Apples & Bananas.
  5. Make it (7 min): make a real fruit drink or fruit salad together. Your child names each thing as it goes in: "Two bananas, three grapes and an orange!"
  6. Say it three times (2 min): say it fast, three times: "Peas, pears and pineapples!" Then your child makes one.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "One tomato, two tomatoes." … "What's in your drink?" … "Peas, pears and pineapples!"
