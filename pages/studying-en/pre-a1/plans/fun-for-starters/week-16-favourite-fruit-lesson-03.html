Week 16 · Lesson 3: Talk About the Picture

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 16: What's your favourite fruit?

Fun for Starters · Week 16: What's your favourite fruit? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit and vegetables Lesson 2 · Tomatoes and potatoes Lesson 3 · Talk about the picture

You Need

  • A picture with fruit and animals in it (a picture book page, or draw a beach with a coconut, a bird, two lizards, a plate of grapes and some shells).
  • Some real fruit for a shop game and a tasting game, and a scarf for closing eyes.
  • In the Starters speaking test, the examiner asks short questions about a picture. One or two words is a good answer.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): I Want It! Bananas & Apples Counting Song.
  3. Talk about the picture (8 min): ask: "Where's the coconut? Where are the lizards? What's this? What colour is the bird? How many shells are there? What's the bird standing on?" Your child answers, then asks you two questions. Animal words
  4. Fruit shop (6 min): your child sells fruit. "Can I have three bananas, please?" "Here you are!" Then swap.
  5. My favourite fruit (5 min): your child draws it and writes: "My favourite fruit is mango. It's yellow and orange."
  6. Taste and guess (4 min): your child closes their eyes, tastes a small piece and guesses: "It's an orange!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Where's the coconut?" … "Can I have three bananas, please?" … "What's your favourite fruit?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name and spell the Starters fruit and vegetable words.
  • Say plurals like tomatoes and potatoes.
  • Guess a fruit from a short description and talk about a picture.
