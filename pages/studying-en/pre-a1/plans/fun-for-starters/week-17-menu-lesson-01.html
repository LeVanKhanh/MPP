Week 17 · Lesson 1: Today's Lunch

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 17: What's on the menu?

Fun for Starters · Week 17: What's on the menu? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Today's lunch Lesson 2 · What would you like? Lesson 3 · In the kitchen

You Need

  • A big sheet for a "Today's lunch" board, toy food or pictures, paper plates.
  • Letter cards for mixed-up drinks: w a t e r, j u i c e, m i l k, l e m o n a d e, h o t c h o c o l a t e.
  • "Menu" is not a Starters word, so the board says "Today's lunch".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Food Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Today's lunch board (7 min): make a board for a home café. Your child writes: fish, chicken, eggs, sausages, rice, meatballs, burger and chips, and adds their favourite lunch. Food words
  4. Mixed-up drinks (5 min): your child moves the letter cards to make each drink and writes it.
  5. Watch (5 min): Drinks – Thirsty!
  6. What's on the plate? (6 min): put toy food on plates: "What's on this plate?" "Fish, potatoes and peas!" Then your child makes a plate for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's on this plate?" … "Fish, potatoes and peas!" … "Can you spell milk?"
