Week 17 · Lesson 2: What Would You Like?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 17: What's on the menu?

Fun for Starters · Week 17: What's on the menu? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Today's lunch Lesson 2 · What would you like? Lesson 3 · In the kitchen

You Need

  • The lunch board from Lesson 1, toy food, a notepad for the waiter.
  • Two lists of the lunch board food with boxes, one for Kim and one for Ben.
  • The pattern: "What would you like for lunch?" "I'd like fish and chips today. And can I have some peas too?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Are You Hungry?
  3. Listen and tick (7 min): say slowly: "Kim would like chicken and rice today. And she'd like some carrots too." Your child ticks Kim's lunch. Then: "Ben would like sausages and potatoes, and an ice cream." Your child ticks Ben's.
  4. Home café (8 min): your child is the waiter: "What would you like for lunch?" You answer; your child writes the order. Then swap. Food words
  5. Write your words (5 min): your child draws a speech bubble and writes their own answer: "I'd like meatballs and rice today. And can I have some juice too?"
  6. Watch (3 min): Healthy Food Vs Junk Food Song.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What would you like for lunch?" … "I'd like fish and chips today." … "And can I have some peas too?"
