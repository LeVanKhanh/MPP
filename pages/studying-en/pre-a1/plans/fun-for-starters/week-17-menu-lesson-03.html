Week 17 · Lesson 3: In the Kitchen

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 17: What's on the menu?

Lesson 1 · Today's lunch Lesson 2 · What would you like? Lesson 3 · In the kitchen

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a kitchen with six people doing different things: one taking a cake out of a cupboard, one drinking milk, one eating fruit, one reading a book on the floor, one listening to music, one sitting at the table. Write six names around it: Sam, Alice, Hugo, Eva, Bill, Grace.
  • This is the Starters "listen and draw lines" task. One name has no person – it's there to make your child listen carefully.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Where are you? In the kitchen.
  3. Listen and draw lines (8 min): say slowly: "Sam is getting a cake from the cupboard. Eva is listening to music. Bill is reading a book on the floor." Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  4. Talk about the picture (6 min): "What's Hugo drinking? How many people are there? Where's the cake?" Your child answers with one or two words. Home words
  5. Kitchen hunt (5 min): in your kitchen: "Find something you can drink! … something red you can eat! … something long!"
  6. The long lunch sentence (4 min): take turns adding food: "For lunch I'd like fish." "For lunch I'd like fish and rice." Who can remember the longest sentence? Food words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What is Sam doing?" … "He's getting a cake." … "For lunch I'd like fish and…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read and write food and drink words, including sausages, meatballs, lemonade and hot chocolate.
  • Ask and answer "What would you like for lunch?" – "I'd like… And can I have some… too?"
  • Listen to a description of a kitchen and draw lines from names to people.
