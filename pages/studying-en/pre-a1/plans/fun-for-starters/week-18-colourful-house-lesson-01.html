Week 18 · Lesson 1: Rooms and Colours

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 18: A colourful house.

Fun for Starters · Week 18: A colourful house | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms and colours Lesson 2 · Where's the clock? Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • Paper and crayons, and your own home to walk round.
  • Starters room words: bedroom, bathroom, hall, dining room, living room, kitchen.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Rooms of the House Song.
  3. House tour (7 min): walk round your home. In each room: "What room is this?" "It's the kitchen!" "What colour is it?" Your child writes the room names on a list. Home words
  4. A colourful house (8 min): your child draws a house with six rooms. You say: "Colour the bathroom red. The bedroom is yellow. The hall is green…" Your child colours and labels each room. Colour words
  5. Watch (2 min): Where Are You? – Rooms In A House.
  6. Where are you? (6 min): hide in a room and call: "Where am I?" Your child guesses, then calls: "Where are you?" "I'm in the bathroom!" Then swap.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What room is this?" … "What colour is the kitchen?" … "Where are you? I'm in the bedroom!"
