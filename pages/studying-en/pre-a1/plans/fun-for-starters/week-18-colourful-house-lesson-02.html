Week 18 · Lesson 2: Where's the Clock?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 18: A colourful house.

Fun for Starters · Week 18: A colourful house | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms and colours Lesson 2 · Where's the clock? Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • The colourful house from Lesson 1, or a new one with six rooms.
  • A small mirror. Before the lesson, write five home words backwards so they can be read in a mirror: wall, window, door, table, mirror.
  • A toy to hide.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw (8 min): say: "There's a clock in the bedroom. Draw it! There's a lamp in the living room. The computer is in the hall." Your child draws each thing in the right room: clock, computer, photo, lamp, radio, mat.
  3. One-word answers (6 min): "Where's the photo?" "It's in the hall!" "Where's the mat?" "In the bathroom!" Your child writes the room for each thing. Home words
  4. Mirror words (6 min): your child holds the mirror next to each backwards word, reads it and writes it the right way round.
  5. Watch (3 min): Hide & Seek (Around The House).
  6. Hide the toy (2 min): hide a toy: "Is it in the kitchen?" "No!" "Is it under the bed?" "Yes!" in, on, under
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where's the clock?" … "It's in the bedroom." … "Can you read this word in the mirror?"
