Week 18 · Lesson 3: My Home

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 18: A colourful house.

Fun for Starters · Week 18: A colourful house | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Rooms and colours Lesson 2 · Where's the clock? Lesson 3 · My home

You Need

  • Copy the short text below, with a box of word pictures: legs, horse, stories, computer, and two extra words, ear and water.
  • A frame for writing: "My home is a ____ (house / flat). It's got ____ rooms. They are… My favourite room is the ____. In my favourite room, you can find ____."
  • If you share your child's writing, leave out your address.

A chair: A chair has got four (1) ______, but it isn't an animal like a (2) ______! Children sit on chairs at their desk. They read (3) ______ or play games on the (4) ______.

Answers: 1 legs, 2 horse, 3 stories, 4 computer.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read the chair text together. Your child chooses a word from the pictures for each gap and writes it. Ear and water are not in the text!
  3. Write about your home (7 min): your child fills in the frame and draws their favourite room. Home words
  4. Watch (2 min): Where are you? In the kitchen.
  5. The long home sentence (6 min): take turns: "In my house there's a red sofa." "In my house there's a red sofa and a big clock." Who can remember the longest sentence?
  6. Room race (2 min): "Run to the kitchen! … Walk to the bathroom! … Jump to the hall!" School words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many rooms has your home got?" … "My favourite room is the kitchen." … "In my house there's…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the rooms in a house and say what colour they are.
  • Say where things are: "The clock is in the bedroom."
  • Read a short text, choose words for the gaps, and write about their home.
