Week 19 · Lesson 1: My Bedroom

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 19: What's in your bedroom?

Fun for Starters · Week 19: What's in your bedroom? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My bedroom Lesson 2 · Two bedrooms Lesson 3 · Guess what I'm drawing

You Need

  • Your child's bedroom, sticky notes and a pencil.
  • Starters bedroom words: armchair, bed, bookcase, clock, computer, cupboard, desk, door, phone, picture, piano, poster, rug, television, window.
  • Some home words are two words together: bed + room = bedroom, book + case = bookcase, arm + chair = armchair, bath + room = bathroom.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Rooms of the House Song.
  3. Label the bedroom (6 min): in the bedroom, your child writes a sticky note for each thing and sticks it on: bed, desk, rug, window, door, clock… Home words
  4. Two words make one (4 min): write "bed" and "room" on two cards and put them together: "bedroom!" Then book + case, arm + chair, bath + room.
  5. What's in your bedroom? (6 min): your child writes: "In my bedroom, there's a bed, a lamp, a desk and a poster."
  6. Watch (3 min): I See Something Blue.
  7. I spy (4 min): "I spy something in my bedroom… It's red!" Your child guesses. Then swap. Colour words
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in your bedroom?" … "There's a bed and a desk." … "Bed and room make… bedroom!"
