Week 19 · Lesson 2: Two Bedrooms

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 19: What's in your bedroom?

Fun for Starters · Week 19: What's in your bedroom? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My bedroom Lesson 2 · Two bedrooms Lesson 3 · Guess what I'm drawing

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a simple bedroom to colour: a bed, a rug, an armchair, a clock, a desk, a cupboard, a window.
  • Then draw two small bedrooms side by side, almost the same. In Nick's room there's a poster of a fish, the rug is orange, the armchair is red and the cupboard is big. In Ann's room the rug is blue, the armchair is green and the cupboard is small.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour (8 min): say: "Colour the rug orange. The armchair is red. The clock is yellow…" Your child colours. Check together: "What colour is the rug?" Colour words
  3. Whose bedroom? (8 min): say: "Nick loves fish." Your child finds Nick's room and writes "Nick" under it. Then your child completes: "In Nick's bedroom, the rug is ____. The ____ is red. The cupboard is ____, but in Ann's room it's small."
  4. Watch (3 min): Hide & Seek (Around The House).
  5. What's different? (6 min): your child changes three things in a room while you close your eyes (moves a toy, opens the door…). You find them: "The teddy is on the bed now!" Then swap. Home words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Colour the rug orange." … "Whose bedroom is this? It's Nick's." … "In Ann's room the cupboard is small."
