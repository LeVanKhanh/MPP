Week 19 · Lesson 3: Guess What I'm Drawing

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 19: What's in your bedroom?

Fun for Starters · Week 19: What's in your bedroom? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My bedroom Lesson 2 · Two bedrooms Lesson 3 · Guess what I'm drawing

You Need

  • Paper and crayons.
  • A short description to read: "This is Nick. He's eight. He loves fish. His bedroom is blue. He's got a computer and a piano in his room, and he can play the piano."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and answer (7 min): read the description twice. Then ask: "How old is Nick? What does he love? What colour is his bedroom? What has he got in his room?" Your child writes one or two words for each answer.
  3. Watch (2 min): Where Are You? – Rooms In A House.
  4. Guess what I'm drawing (7 min): draw a bedroom thing very slowly, one line at a time. Your child guesses: "Is it a clock? Is it a bookcase?" Then swap. Home words
  5. My dream bedroom (9 min): your child draws a dream bedroom and writes three sentences: "My bedroom is purple. There's a big bed and a piano. There are posters of horses on the walls."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How old is Nick?" … "Is it a bookcase?" … "My bedroom is purple."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name the things in a bedroom: armchair, bookcase, desk, poster, rug, piano…
  • Say "In my bedroom, there's a… and a…" and compare two rooms.
  • Listen to a description and answer questions about a person.
