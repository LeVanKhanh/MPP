Week 20 · Lesson 1: Look and Read

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Family. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 20: Ben and Kim live here!

Fun for Starters · Week 20: Ben and Kim live here! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Look and read Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the street Lesson 3 · Ben's street or Kim's street?

You Need

  • Five real things or pictures with a sentence card each, some true and some not: a kitchen picture ("This is a kitchen."), a camera ("This is a phone."), a family photo, a pair of shorts, a toy car ("These are cats.").
  • Copy the kitchen text below, with word pictures: house, table, chairs, lunch, and two extra words, bed and television.

A kitchen: A kitchen is part of a (1) ______ or a flat. In the kitchen there's a big (2) ______ and some (3) ______. Families eat (4) ______ there.

Answers: 1 house, 2 table, 3 chairs, 4 lunch.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Tick or cross (5 min): your child reads each card and ticks or crosses: "This is a phone. No, it's a camera. Cross!"
  3. Watch (2 min): Where Are You? – Rooms In A House.
  4. Read and choose (8 min): read the kitchen text together. Your child chooses a word from the pictures for each gap. Bed and television are not in the text! Home words
  5. Make up a family (8 min): your child chooses and writes: "The family lives in ____ Street. The number of their house is ____. The boy's name is ____ and the girl's name is ____. ____ animals live with them." Then draws the family. Family words
  6. Watch (2 min): Learn Rooms of the House Song.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is a phone. Tick or cross?" … "Families eat lunch in the kitchen." … "The boy's name is…"
