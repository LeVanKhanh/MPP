Week 20 · Lesson 2: Listen and Colour the Street

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 20: Ben and Kim live here!

Fun for Starters · Week 20: Ben and Kim live here! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Look and read Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the street Lesson 3 · Ben's street or Kim's street?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a simple street with pencil: two houses, some flats, a shop, a bookshop, a park with a tree, a car, a lorry, a bus and a man on a bike.
  • Crayons, and a window onto a street if you have one.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Vehicles – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Street words (5 min): point to each thing in your drawing: "What's this?" "It's a bookshop!" "What are these?" "They're flats!" Street words
  4. Listen and colour (9 min): say slowly: "Colour the bookshop blue. Can you see the lorry? Colour it red. There's a man on a bike. Colour his bike yellow." Check together at the end.
  5. Watch (3 min): The Wheels On The Bus.
  6. Window watch (6 min): look out of the window or remember your street: "What can you see? There's a red car next to the tree. There are two lorries!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Colour the bookshop blue." … "What can you see?" … "There's a red car next to the tree."
