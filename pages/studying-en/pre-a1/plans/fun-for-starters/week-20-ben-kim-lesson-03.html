Week 20 · Lesson 3: Ben's Street or Kim's Street?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 20: Ben and Kim live here!

Fun for Starters · Week 20: Ben and Kim live here! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Look and read Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the street Lesson 3 · Ben's street or Kim's street?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two streets. Ben's street: small houses, a bookshop, a park behind the houses, lots of people, no cars. Kim's street: tall flats, cars and lorries, no trees.
  • Seven sentences: "In ____'s street, many houses are small." "There are no cars or lorries in ____'s street." "There's a bookshop in ____'s street." "In ____'s street, the flats are very tall." "There's a park behind ____'s houses." "There are no trees in ____'s street." "You can see lots of people in ____'s street."
  • When your child draws their own street, there's no need to write its name.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Ben or Kim? (9 min): your child reads each sentence, looks at both streets and writes Ben or Kim.
  3. Write five questions (7 min): your child writes questions to ask someone at home: "Is your garden big or small? How many trees are there in your street? Is there a shop next to your house?" Then asks them!
  4. Watch (2 min): In Front Of, Behind, Between.
  5. My street (7 min): your child draws their street and says: "There are three trees in my street. There's a shop next to my house." Position words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is it Ben's street or Kim's street?" … "How many trees are there in your street?" … "There's a shop next to my house."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a short text about a room and choose the right word for each gap.
  • Describe a street: houses, flats, shops, a park, cars and lorries.
  • Read sentences and decide which picture they are about, and write simple questions.
