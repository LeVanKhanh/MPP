Week 21 · Lesson 1: Who's Playing?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 21: Play with us!

Fun for Starters · Week 21: Play with us! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who's playing? Lesson 2 · What can you ride? Lesson 3 · My flying bike

You Need

  • Toys: a plane, a helicopter, a boat, a kite, a balloon, a train, a bike, a car. Six teddies or dolls with name cards: Kim, Bill, Dan, May, Pat, Sam.
  • This is like the Starters task where children listen and write names next to people.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): My Favorite Toys.
  3. Toy words (5 min): your child names each toy and writes three of them: "This is a helicopter. This is a train." Toy words
  4. Listen and write names (8 min): give each teddy a toy. Then say: "Bill is playing with the plane. May has got the kite. Dan is on the bike." Your child writes each name next to the right teddy on a sheet of paper.
  5. Play and say (6 min): play with the toys together. Ask: "What are you playing with?" "I'm playing with my train!"
  6. Toy freeze (2 min): everyone moves like a toy: a plane, a train, a robot. "Freeze!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's playing with the plane?" … "Bill is!" … "What are you playing with?"
