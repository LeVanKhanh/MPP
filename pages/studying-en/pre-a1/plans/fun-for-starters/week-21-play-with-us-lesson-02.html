Week 21 · Lesson 2: What Can You Ride?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 21: Play with us!

Fun for Starters · Week 21: Play with us! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who's playing? Lesson 2 · What can you ride? Lesson 3 · My flying bike

You Need

  • Six rows of three words: boat · train · bed / bus · box · motorbike / plane · bike · phone / lorry · hat · helicopter / car · truck · cake / skateboard · ship · bear.
  • Paper with three boxes: "It goes on the water", "It can fly", "It goes on the street".
  • "Road" and "sky" are Movers words, so the boxes avoid them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Vehicles Vocabulary Chant.
  3. Cross it out (6 min): in each row there's one thing you can't ride on or in. Your child reads the row and crosses it out: "You can't ride on a bed!"
  4. Where does it go? (6 min): your child writes the transport words in the three boxes: boat and ship go on the water; plane and helicopter can fly… Street words
  5. Watch (3 min): Driving In My Car.
  6. Transport charades (6 min): act a ride without words: "Are you on a motorbike?" "No!" "Are you on a skateboard?" "Yes!" Then swap. Toy words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Can you ride on a bed?" … "No, you can't!" … "A boat goes on the water."
