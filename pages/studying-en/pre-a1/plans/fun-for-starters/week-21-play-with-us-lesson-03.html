Week 21 · Lesson 3: My Flying Bike

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 21: Play with us!

Fun for Starters · Week 21: Play with us! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who's playing? Lesson 2 · What can you ride? Lesson 3 · My flying bike

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five rows of three small pictures (A, B, C): what Sam is riding (a horse, a bike, a boat); what Ann is drawing (a helicopter, a plane, a crocodile); which boy is Tom (with a train, a robot, a car); what Sue is painting (a doll, a bus, a monster); where Nick is (bedroom, kitchen, park).
  • Paper and crayons. A mini crossword with five transport words and picture clues (bus, ship, plane, lorry, train).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (8 min): for each row, say: "What is Sam riding? He's riding a bike!" "What is Ann drawing? A helicopter." Your child ticks A, B or C.
  3. Watch (2 min): Up and Down.
  4. My flying bike (8 min): imagine a bike with wings. Ask: "Would you like to ride a flying bike? Sit on it now and fly! Is it fun? Is it scary? What can you see? Where are you going?" Your child answers, then draws the ride. Street words
  5. Transport crossword (7 min): your child writes the five transport words in your crossword.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What is Sam riding?" … "Would you like to ride a flying bike?" … "What can you see?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name toys and transport: plane, helicopter, boat, ship, train, lorry, motorbike, skateboard…
  • Say what you can ride on or in, and where things go.
  • Listen and choose the right picture, and answer questions about an imaginary ride.
