Week 22 · Lesson 1: School Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 22: In our bags and in our school.

Fun for Starters · Week 22: In our bags and in our school | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · School words Lesson 2 · Sam's bag Lesson 3 · Close your eyes and draw

You Need

  • Your child's school bag with its things inside.
  • A word snake on paper: bookcasedeskcrayonrulerpencilpaperbagpagemousetableteraser.
  • A small crossword with five words and picture clues: pencil, desk, bag, board, playground.
  • "Eraser" and "rubber" are both Starters words for the same thing.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): School Supplies – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Word snake (7 min): your child finds the words in the snake, draws a line between them and writes each one.
  4. What's in your bag? (5 min): your child takes out each thing and names it: "This is my ruler. These are my crayons." School words
  5. Watch (4 min): School Supplies – What's in My Bag?
  6. Mini crossword (4 min): your child writes the five words in the crossword.
  7. Ask and answer (3 min): "What colour is your eraser? How many crayons have you got? What's on your desk?"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in your bag?" … "These are my crayons." … "How many crayons have you got?"
