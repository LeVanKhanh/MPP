Week 22 · Lesson 2: Sam's Bag

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 22: In our bags and in our school.

Fun for Starters · Week 22: In our bags and in our school | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · School words Lesson 2 · Sam's bag Lesson 3 · Close your eyes and draw

You Need

  • Copy the text below, with a box of word pictures: book, ruler, apple, juice, glasses, and two extra words, shirt and shells.
  • A school bag and the things for the packing game: pencils, a ruler, an apple, a book.
  • A small table for the family survey: names down the side, four questions across the top.

Sam's bag: Sam is putting his new (1) ______ in his school bag. He's putting his (2) ______ and his pens in his bag too. He's taking an (3) ______ for his lunch, and some orange (4) ______. He loves it! Sam wears (5) ______ at school. Yes, they're in his bag too.

Answers: 1 book, 2 ruler, 3 apple, 4 juice, 5 glasses.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read Sam's bag together. Your child chooses a word from the pictures for each gap and writes it. Shirt and shells are not in the text!
  3. Family survey (6 min): your child asks people at home and writes their answers: "What colour is your eraser? Has your mum or dad got a tablet? What's on your desk? How many crayons have you got?"
  4. Watch (3 min): Do You Have A Crayon?
  5. Pack the bag (6 min): say: "Put two pencils, a ruler and an apple in the bag!" Your child packs it, then checks: "Two pencils – yes!" School words
  6. What's not here? (2 min): your child closes their eyes; you take one thing out. "What's not here?" "The ruler!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's Sam putting in his bag?" … "Has your dad got a tablet?" … "What's not here?"
