Week 22 · Lesson 3: Close Your Eyes and Draw

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 22: In our bags and in our school.

Fun for Starters · Week 22: In our bags and in our school | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · School words Lesson 2 · Sam's bag Lesson 3 · Close your eyes and draw

You Need

  • Before the lesson, put a school bag on a desk with these things in or next to it: an apple and a banana, three pencils, a red ruler, a book behind the apple, a juice box.
  • Paper and pencils.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look and answer (8 min): ask: "What fruit can you see? How many pencils can you see? What colour is the ruler? What can you see behind the apple? Where is the bag? What drink can you see?" Your child writes one or two words for each answer. Position words
  3. Watch (2 min): What's this? – It's a pencil.
  4. Close your eyes and draw (6 min): your child closes their eyes and draws a school thing. You guess: "Is it a ruler?" Then swap. It's very funny!
  5. Watch (4 min): What's this? – School supplies guessing game.
  6. My school bag (5 min): your child draws their bag and writes: "In my bag there's a pencil, a ruler and a book." School words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many pencils can you see?" … "What can you see behind the apple?" … "Is it a ruler?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read, spell and find the Starters school words: eraser, rubber, ruler, page, tablet, playground…
  • Read a short text about a school bag and choose the right word for each gap.
  • Answer questions about a picture with one or two words.
