Week 23 · Lesson 1: Board and Case

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 23: At our school.

Fun for Starters · Week 23: At our school | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Board and case Lesson 2 · Listen and write Lesson 3 · My school

You Need

  • Word cards with missing letters: ____board (cupboard), ___board (keyboard), board, ____case (bookcase).
  • A few things for his / her / their: a girl's bag, a boy's ruler, a family photo with your pet.
  • "Their" is for two or more people: "It's their dog."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): My Classroom.
  3. Board and case (7 min): your child fills in the missing letters: cupboard, keyboard, bookcase. Then finds a cupboard, a keyboard and a bookcase at home. Home words
  4. School things hunt (7 min): "Find a pencil! … a ruler! … a book! … a board!" Your child brings each thing and names it. School words
  5. His, her or their? (6 min): show each thing: "Whose bag is it? It's her bag." "It's his ruler." "It's their dog." Your child writes his, her or their.
  6. Watch (2 min): What's this? – It's a pencil.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Cup and board make… cupboard!" … "Whose ruler is it? It's his ruler." … "It's their dog."
