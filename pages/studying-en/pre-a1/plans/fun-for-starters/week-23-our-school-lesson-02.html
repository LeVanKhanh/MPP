Week 23 · Lesson 2: Listen and Write

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: School and Family. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 23: At our school.

You Need

  • Paper and pencils. A short description to read about May (below), and Kim's message to read.
  • This is the Starters listening task where children write a name or a number. Spell the names out loud.

May: May is eight. Her brother's name is Nick. Her school is in Park Street. There are twenty-two children in her class. Her friend's name is Grace, G – R – A – C – E. Her English teacher is Mrs Brown. May is in class three.

Kim's message: Hello! I go to Star School. It's in Long Street. I'm in class four. There are twenty children in my class. My teacher is Mr Fish. He's very nice. My friend Lucy sits next to me.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (9 min): read May's description twice. Ask: "Where is May's school? How many children are there in her class? What's her friend's name? Who is her English teacher? What is the number of her class?" Your child writes a name or a number for each. Family words
  3. Read and ask (7 min): read Kim's message together. Your child finishes the questions to ask Kim: "What's the name of your ____? Where's your ____? Which ____ are you in? How many ____ are there in your class? Who sits ____ to you?"
  4. Watch (3 min): Stand Up, Sit Down.
  5. Teacher says (6 min): your child is the teacher: "Open your book! Sit down! Draw a line! Put your pencil on the desk!" You do it. Then swap. School words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many children are there in your class?" … "Who is your English teacher?" … "Open your book!"
