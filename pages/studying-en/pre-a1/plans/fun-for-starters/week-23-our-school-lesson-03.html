Week 23 · Lesson 3: My School

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 23: At our school.

Fun for Starters · Week 23: At our school | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Board and case Lesson 2 · Listen and write Lesson 3 · My school

You Need

  • A frame for writing: "I go to ____ School. I am in class ____. I have ____ classmates. Our English teacher is ____. I sit next to ____." (If you share it, leave out the school's name.)
  • Four riddles, each with six words under it for yes or no (see "Yes or no?" below).
  • Paper and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. About my school (8 min): your child fills in the frame and reads it to you, then asks you about your school when you were small. School words
  3. Yes or no? (7 min): read four clues. Under each, your child writes yes or no next to six words: "It's in a house or a classroom" (a beach? a bookcase? a chicken?…) "It's on a table" (a flower? a door?…) "People write, draw and play with it" "It has lots of letters and numbers on its keyboard." Which word is "yes" every time? A computer!
  4. Watch (4 min): What's this? – School supplies guessing game.
  5. My classroom (6 min): your child draws their classroom and says: "There's a big board. My desk is next to the window."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I go to… School." … "I sit next to…" … "Is a bookcase in a classroom? Yes!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell home and school words that end in -board and -case: cupboard, keyboard, bookcase.
  • Use his, her and their: "It's her bag. It's their dog."
  • Listen for names and numbers, and answer questions about their own school.
