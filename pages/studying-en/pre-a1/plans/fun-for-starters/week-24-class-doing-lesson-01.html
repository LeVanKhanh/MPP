Week 24 · Lesson 1: Picture 1 or Picture 2?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 24: What's the class doing?

Fun for Starters · Week 24: What's the class doing? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Picture 1 or picture 2? Lesson 2 · -ing words Lesson 3 · What are the children doing?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two simple classrooms. Picture 1: a teacher reading a story, children sitting and listening, a boy eating a banana, a boy sleeping on his desk. Picture 2: one girl jumping, one girl painting, two boys (Tom and Nick) drawing a plane on the board.
  • Eight sentences: "The teacher is reading a story." "A boy is eating a banana." "One girl is jumping." "The children are sitting down." "One girl is painting." "The children are listening to the teacher." "A boy is sleeping on his desk." "Two boys are drawing on the board."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Are You Doing? (Dream English).
  3. Picture 1 or 2? (10 min): your child reads each sentence, finds it in a picture and writes 1 or 2.
  4. Listen and answer (4 min): about picture 2: "What are Tom and Nick doing?" "Drawing." "What are they drawing?" "A plane." "What is the girl painting?"
  5. Freeze the class (6 min): act as a class: "What am I doing?" "You're reading a story!" "You're sleeping on the desk!" School words
  6. Watch (3 min): Stand Up, Sit Down.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it picture 1 or picture 2?" … "What are Tom and Nick doing?" … "You're sleeping!"
