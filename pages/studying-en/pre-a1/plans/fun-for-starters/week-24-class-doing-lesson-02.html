Week 24 · Lesson 2: -ing Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 24: What's the class doing?

Fun for Starters · Week 24: What's the class doing? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Picture 1 or picture 2? Lesson 2 · -ing words Lesson 3 · What are the children doing?

You Need

  • Three boxes or envelopes labelled: "+ ing" (read → reading), "double" (run → running), "goodbye e" (smile → smiling).
  • Word cards: read, run, smile, stop, draw, swim, wave, ride, sleep, clap, count.
  • A small crossword of five -ing words with picture clues: running, smiling, reading, swimming, waving.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Spelling boxes (9 min): your child puts each word card in the right box and writes the -ing word: read → reading, sleep → sleeping, count → counting; run → running, stop → stopping, swim → swimming, clap → clapping; smile → smiling, wave → waving, ride → riding.
  3. Watch (4 min): Fast Slow Action Song.
  4. Action crossword (7 min): your child looks at each picture clue and writes the -ing word in the crossword.
  5. What are you doing? (5 min): do an action: "What am I doing?" "You're clapping!" Your child spells it. Then swap. School words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Run… running! Two n's!" … "Smile… smiling! Goodbye e!" … "What are you doing?"
