Week 24 · Lesson 3: What Are the Children Doing?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 24: What's the class doing?

Fun for Starters · Week 24: What's the class doing? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Picture 1 or picture 2? Lesson 2 · -ing words Lesson 3 · What are the children doing?

You Need

  • A busy picture of children (a playground page in a picture book, or a photo of children playing).
  • Action cards for mimes: running, swimming, riding a bike, waving, sleeping, eating, counting, painting.
  • Paper and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look and say (8 min): look at the picture: "What are the children doing now?" "He's waving. She's running. They're playing football." Your child says five sentences and writes two.
  3. Watch (4 min): Yes, I Can!
  4. Action mimes (7 min): your child picks a card and acts it without words. You guess: "You're riding a bike!" Then swap. School words
  5. At home now (6 min): your child draws someone at home and writes what they're doing: "Mum is reading. Grandpa is sleeping!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What are the children doing now?" … "He's waving." … "You're riding a bike!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say what people are doing now: "The teacher is reading a story."
  • Spell -ing words: reading, running, smiling.
  • Look at a picture and say which sentences are true.
