Week 25 · Lesson 1: Find Ten Animals

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 25: Animal challenge.

Fun for Starters · Week 25: Animal challenge | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Find ten animals Lesson 2 · Kim's pets Lesson 3 · Animal riddles

You Need

  • A big, busy animal picture: a picture book page with lots of animals, or an animal poster.
  • Before the lesson, draw four snakes: one in a tree, one in the water, one under a flower, one next to a tiger. Crayons.
  • "Jungle" and "grass" are Movers words, so the lesson doesn't use them.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Wild Animals at the Zoo.
  3. Find ten animals (8 min): your child looks carefully at the picture and writes ten animals. "Can you see a crocodile? Where is it?" Animal words
  4. Listen and colour (7 min): say: "Colour the snake in the tree green. The snake in the water is blue. Colour the snake under the flower yellow and red." Check together: "What colour is the snake next to the tiger?" Colour words
  5. What can you see? (5 min): your child tells you about the big picture: "There's a tiger. It's orange and black. There are two birds in the tree."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Can you find ten animals?" … "Colour the snake in the tree green." … "What can you see?"
