Week 25 · Lesson 2: Kim's Pets

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 25: Animal challenge.

Fun for Starters · Week 25: Animal challenge | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Find ten animals Lesson 2 · Kim's pets Lesson 3 · Animal riddles

You Need

  • Copy the five gapped sentences below. Next to them, your child writes guesses first, then the real answers.
  • Animal word cards and five big labels: sea, trees, water, house, zoo.

Kim's pets: Kim has got four (1) ______. They live in a box in her (2) ______. Kim likes (3) ______ to them. Their names are Sue, Pat, Bill and (4) ______. They eat apples and (5) ______!

Read this to your child: "Kim has got four mice. They live in a box in her bedroom. Kim likes singing to them. Their names are Sue, Pat, Bill and Matt. They eat apples and carrots!"

Answers: 1 mice, 2 bedroom, 3 singing, 4 Matt, 5 carrots.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Guess, then listen (8 min): your child reads the gapped sentences and writes guesses: "Four… cats?" Then read the real text twice. Your child writes the answers. How many guesses were right?
  3. Watch (3 min): Wild Animals What Is It?
  4. My funny pet (5 min): you have a pretend pet. Your child asks: "Is it big? Can it swim? What does it eat?" and guesses the animal. Then swap.
  5. Where do animals live? (7 min): your child puts each animal card under a label: "Jellyfish live in the sea. Monkeys live in trees. Zebras live at the zoo." Then writes two sentences. Animal words
  6. Animal sounds (2 min): make an animal sound; your child names the animal, then makes one for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many mice has Kim got?" … "Where do jellyfish live?" … "Can it swim?"
