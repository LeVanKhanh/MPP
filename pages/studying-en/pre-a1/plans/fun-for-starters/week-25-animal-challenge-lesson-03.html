Week 25 · Lesson 3: Animal Riddles

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 25: Animal challenge.

Fun for Starters · Week 25: Animal challenge | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Find ten animals Lesson 2 · Kim's pets Lesson 3 · Animal riddles

You Need

  • Copy the eight riddles below, with the first letter of each answer: "a e______", "a m____"…
  • Paper and crayons.

1 I'm very big and grey. I've got big ears and a long nose. (a e______)

2 I'm small and grey. Cats like catching me. (a m____)

3 I'm yellow and brown and very, very long! (a g______)

4 I've got eight legs. I catch flies. (a s_____)

5 I live in the sea. I'm not a fish and I haven't got legs. (a j_______)

6 I'm black and white. I eat all day, and you can get milk from me. (a c__)

7 I'm green and I can jump. I live next to water. (a f___)

8 I'm orange and black. I've got a long tail and I eat meat. (a t____)

Answers: 1 elephant, 2 mouse, 3 giraffe, 4 spider, 5 jellyfish, 6 cow, 7 frog, 8 tiger.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (10 min): your child reads each riddle and writes the animal. The first letter helps!
  3. Watch (5 min): The Lion and The Mouse.
  4. Make a riddle (6 min): your child writes a riddle for you: "I'm small and I can fly. I like flowers." "A bee!" Animal words
  5. Animal homes (4 min): your child draws one animal at home: a fish in the sea, a monkey in a tree… and writes "Monkeys live in trees."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "I've got eight legs. What am I?" … "A spider!" … "Where do monkeys live?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find and name lots of animals in a picture.
  • Listen and colour, and listen and write words about pets.
  • Read an animal riddle and write the answer, and write a riddle of their own.
