Week 26 · Lesson 1: Names and Numbers

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Animals and Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 26: How many pets?

Fun for Starters · Week 26: How many pets? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Names and numbers Lesson 2 · One or two? Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • A description to read (below). Paper and pencils.
  • Four question cards and four number cards to match: "How many pets have you got?" (1), "How many brothers do you have?" (3), "How many legs has a spider got?" (8), "How many children are there in your class?" (18).

Read this to your child: "This is Grace. She's nine. She's got five pets: two cats, a dog and two mice. Her dog's name is Sam, S – A – M. Her favourite pet is her old cat, Mr Fish. Mr Fish is fourteen!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read the description twice. Ask: "How many pets has Grace got? What is her dog's name? How many mice does she have? What's the name of her favourite pet? How old is the cat?" Your child writes a name or a number.
  3. Watch (4 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant.
  4. Match the questions (6 min): your child reads each question card and finds its number card. Then asks you the questions about your family. Number words
  5. Talk about pets (5 min): "How many pets have you got? Which pets would you like to have?" Your child answers, then asks you.
  6. Count the legs (2 min): "How many legs has a cat got? A spider? A bird? A snake?"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many pets has Grace got?" … "How many brothers do you have?" … "Which pets would you like to have?"
