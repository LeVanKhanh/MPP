Week 26 · Lesson 2: One or Two?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 26: How many pets?

Fun for Starters · Week 26: How many pets? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Names and numbers Lesson 2 · One or two? Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • Two paper bags, labelled "one" and "two". Word cards: dress, beach, toy, story, cross, mat, baby, body, bus, family, day, tail, sausage, bee, classmate, meatball, teddy.
  • The spelling rules: most words add -s (toys, mats, days); words ending in s, ss, ch add -es (buses, dresses, crosses, beaches); a consonant + y changes to -ies (babies, stories, families, bodies, teddies).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. One and two bags (10 min): your child puts each card in the "one" bag, then writes its plural on a new card for the "two" bag: dress → dresses, baby → babies, toy → toys. Talk about the three rules as you go.
  3. Watch (3 min): How Many Fingers?
  4. Plural race (6 min): say a word: "Story!" Your child says and writes the plural as fast as they can: "Stories!" Then swap.
  5. Count around (6 min): walk round your home: "How many doors are there? How many windows? How many teddies?" Your child writes: "two doors, five windows". Home words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "One baby, two… babies!" … "One bus, two… buses!" … "How many doors are there?"
