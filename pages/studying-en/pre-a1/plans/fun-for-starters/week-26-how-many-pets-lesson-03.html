Week 26 · Lesson 3: Look and Read

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 26: How many pets?

Fun for Starters · Week 26: How many pets? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Names and numbers Lesson 2 · One or two? Lesson 3 · Look and read

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a silly garden: a monkey on a car, two ducks on a bus, a cow under a tree, a snake in the bath, a bird on a man's hat, a door that is open.
  • Six sentences, some true and some not: "A monkey is sitting on the car." "There are three ducks on the bus." "A cow is under the tree." "You can see a snake in the bath." "A bird is on the man's hat." "The door of the house is closed."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look and read (8 min): your child reads each sentence, looks at the picture and writes "yes" or "no". "There are three ducks? No, there are two!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Five Spotted Dogs.
  4. My silly picture (8 min): your child draws their own silly picture and writes three sentences about it, one not true. You find the one that's not true! Animal words
  5. Pet survey (6 min): ask people at home: "How many pets have you got? Which pets would you like to have?" Your child writes the answers.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "There are three ducks on the bus. Yes or no?" … "No, there are two!" … "Which pets would you like to have?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Ask and answer "How many… have you got? / do you have?"
  • Spell plurals: dresses, beaches, babies, stories, teddies, buses.
  • Look at a picture and decide if sentences are true.
