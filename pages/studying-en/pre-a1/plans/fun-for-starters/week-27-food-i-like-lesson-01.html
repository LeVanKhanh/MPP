Week 27 · Lesson 1: A Big Breakfast

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 27: Food I really like!

Fun for Starters · Week 27: Food I really like! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A big breakfast Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Food games

You Need

  • Real breakfast things or pictures: egg, bread, juice, fruit, beans, milk.
  • Letter cards for mixed-up food: c e i r, h s i f, n a b a n a, l e m o n a d e, s a s u a g e s, c h o c o l a t e.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Learn Food Vocabulary – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Breakfast table (8 min): your child names each breakfast thing and writes: "For breakfast I have bread, an egg and some juice." Food words
  4. Food with c (6 min): your child writes six foods with the letter "c" in them: carrots, chicken, chocolate, cake, coconut, rice… Fruit and vegetable words
  5. Watch (2 min): Are You Hungry?
  6. Mixed-up dinner (7 min): your child moves the letter cards to make each food word and writes it: rice, fish, banana, lemonade, sausages, chocolate.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you have for breakfast?" … "Bread and an egg." … "Chocolate has got a c!"
