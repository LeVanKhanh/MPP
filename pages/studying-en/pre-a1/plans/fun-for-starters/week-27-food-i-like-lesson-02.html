Week 27 · Lesson 2: Listen and Tick

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 27: Food I really like!

Fun for Starters · Week 27: Food I really like! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A big breakfast Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Food games

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five rows of three plates (A, B, C): Kim's lunch (fish and chips / meatballs and rice / sausages and beans); May's favourite meat (meatballs / burger / sausages); Alex's breakfast (egg and bread / egg and juice / bread and milk); Hugo's dinner (fish / chicken / burger); Sue's favourite (cake / ice cream / pie).
  • A big sheet with three columns: 🙂 I like… · ☹️ I don't like… · 😐 I don't know…

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (10 min): for each row, say: "What can Kim have for lunch? She can have meatballs and rice." "What is May's favourite meat? Sausages!" Your child ticks A, B or C.
  3. Watch (3 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream?
  4. I like, I don't like (7 min): your child writes foods in the three columns: "I like rice. I don't like onions. I don't know mangoes!" Food words
  5. Food interview (5 min): your child asks people at home: "Do you like fish?" "Yes, I do!" and writes a tick or a cross.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What can Kim have for lunch?" … "Do you like fish?" … "I don't like onions!"
