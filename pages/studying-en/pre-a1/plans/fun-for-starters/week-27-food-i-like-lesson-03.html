Week 27 · Lesson 3: Food Games

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 27: Food I really like!

Fun for Starters · Week 27: Food I really like! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A big breakfast Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Food games

You Need

  • A tray with eight foods (real or pictures) and a cloth to cover it.
  • Toy food or real fruit for a shop, paper and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Do you like…? Yes or No.
  3. Food A to Z (6 min): say food words in alphabet order: "A – apple, B – banana, C – carrot…" How far can you go? Your child writes them. Fruit and vegetable words
  4. Food shop (5 min): "Can I have some bread, please?" "Here you are!" Then swap.
  5. What's not here? (5 min): look at the tray for one minute, then cover it. Take one food away: "What's not here?"
  6. My dinner (6 min): your child draws a favourite dinner and writes: "For dinner I like chicken, rice and peas." Food words
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Can I have some bread, please?" … "What's not here?" … "For dinner I like…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name food for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • Say "I like… / I don't like… / I don't know…" about food.
  • Listen to short talks about food and tick the right picture.
