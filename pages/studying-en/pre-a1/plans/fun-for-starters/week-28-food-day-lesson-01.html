Week 28 · Lesson 1: Me and Food

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 28: My favourite food day.

Fun for Starters · Week 28: My favourite food day | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Me and food Lesson 2 · My favourite food day Lesson 3 · Food, names and animals

You Need

  • A frame with numbered lines: "(1) My favourite food is ____. (2) I don't like ____ or ____. (3) For breakfast I eat ____ and ____. (4) For lunch I drink ____. (5) For dinner I eat ____. (6) My favourite ice cream is ____ ice cream."
  • Six question cards: "What's your favourite ice cream? What do you eat for breakfast? What do you drink for lunch? What's your favourite food? What food don't you like? What do you eat for dinner?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Are You Hungry?
  3. Me and food (9 min): your child fills in the frame about themselves. Food words
  4. Match the questions (5 min): your child reads each question card and writes the number of the frame line it goes with: "What do you eat for breakfast?" – 3.
  5. Ask a friend (6 min): your child asks someone at home the six questions and writes their answers. Are they the same as yours?
  6. Watch (3 min): Healthy Food Vs Junk Food Song.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you eat for breakfast?" … "I don't like onions or beans." … "My favourite ice cream is chocolate ice cream."
