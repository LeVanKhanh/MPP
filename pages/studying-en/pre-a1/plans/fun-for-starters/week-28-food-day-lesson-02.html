Week 28 · Lesson 2: My Favourite Food Day

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 28: My favourite food day.

Fun for Starters · Week 28: My favourite food day | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Me and food Lesson 2 · My favourite food day Lesson 3 · Food, names and animals

You Need

  • A big sheet with three columns: breakfast, lunch, dinner. Crayons.
  • Letter cards: o n i o n s, s a u s a g e s.
  • "Menu" is not a Starters word, so the sheet is called "My favourite food day".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. My favourite food day (9 min): your child writes and draws a perfect food day: what they eat and drink for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Food words
  3. Spell and say (5 min): your child makes "onions" and "sausages" with the letter cards, then writes them. Spot the two "on"s in onions and the two "sa"s in sausages!
  4. Say it three times (3 min): say it fast: "Six silly sausages sit on seven onions!" Then your child makes one.
  5. Watch (4 min): Do you like…? Yes or No.
  6. Lay the table (4 min): set a pretend breakfast table together, naming each thing: "Here's the bread. Here's the juice."
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you eat for dinner?" … "Onions: o – n – i – o – n – s." … "Six silly sausages!"
