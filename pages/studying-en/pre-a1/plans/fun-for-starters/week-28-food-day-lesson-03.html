Week 28 · Lesson 3: Food, Names and Animals

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 28: My favourite food day.

You Need

  • Copy the short text below, with word pictures: fruit, colour, gardens, breakfast, juice, and two extra words, birds and face.
  • Paper with three columns for the game: food, names, animals. Real fruit for fruit faces (with grown-up help for cutting).

An orange: An orange is a (1) ______, and it's a (2) ______ too! You can find orange trees in lots of (3) ______. People love eating oranges for (4) ______ and drinking orange (5) ______. Does your family like oranges?

Answers: 1 fruit, 2 colour, 3 gardens, 4 breakfast, 5 juice.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (9 min): read the orange text together. Your child chooses a word from the pictures for each gap. Birds and face are not in the text!
  3. Watch (2 min): Fruit – Talking Flashcards.
  4. Food, names and animals (9 min): choose a letter, for example B. Everyone writes a food, a name and an animal: bread, Ben, bird! Then try C, M, S… Fruit and vegetable words
  5. Fruit faces (5 min): make a face on a plate with fruit: "The banana is the mouth. The grapes are the eyes!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "An orange is a fruit and a colour!" … "B: bread, Ben, bird!" … "The grapes are the eyes."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Write about the food they like and don't like, for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • Ask a friend food questions and write the answers.
  • Read a short text about a fruit and choose the right word for each gap.
