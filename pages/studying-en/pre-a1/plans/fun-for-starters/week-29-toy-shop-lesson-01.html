Week 29 · Lesson 1: In the Toy Shop

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 29: We're in the toy shop today.

Fun for Starters · Week 29: We're in the toy shop today | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In the toy shop Lesson 2 · A toy shop story Lesson 3 · Toy questions

You Need

  • Lots of toys and a big box or shelf for a toy shop. A cloth to clean with.
  • Questions to ask: "How many toys are in the big box? What am I cleaning? Which toy am I holding?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (4 min): Toys Vocabulary Chant.
  3. Make a toy shop (7 min): your child puts the toys in the shop and names each one: "a red kite, a robot, a doll, two balls." Toy words
  4. Look and answer (7 min): you are the shop helper. Clean the floor, hold a toy… and ask: "What am I cleaning?" "The floor!" "Which toy am I holding?" "A doll!" "How many toys are in the big box?" Your child answers with one word.
  5. Shopping (7 min): "Can I have the red kite, please?" "Here you are!" "Thank you!" Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What am I cleaning?" … "The floor!" … "Can I have the red kite, please?"
