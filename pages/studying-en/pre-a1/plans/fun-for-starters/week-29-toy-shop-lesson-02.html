Week 29 · Lesson 2: A Toy Shop Story

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 29: We're in the toy shop today.

Fun for Starters · Week 29: We're in the toy shop today | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In the toy shop Lesson 2 · A toy shop story Lesson 3 · Toy questions

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a story in three pictures. 1: a man cleaning the floor of a toy shop, lots of toys in a big cupboard. 2: a family comes in; two children sit on a box. 3: outside, the toys are on the family's car, and everyone is waving.
  • Five sentences about picture 3: "The family is waving." "The toys are in the car." "The man is sitting on a chair." "There's a dog in the street." "The car is red."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Look, read and answer (10 min): ask: "How many toys are in the big cupboard? What is the man cleaning? What are the children sitting on? Where are the toys now?" Your child writes one word. Then writes yes or no for the five sentences.
  3. Watch (3 min): Playing With My Car.
  4. Tell the story (7 min): your child tells the story from the pictures: "The man is cleaning the floor. The children are sitting on a box…" Toy words
  5. Stand up and do it (5 min): "Stand up and wave, please! … Stand up and hold your teddy, please!" Your child does it. Then gives you three.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Where are the toys now?" … "On the car!" … "Stand up and wave, please!"
