Week 29 · Lesson 3: Toy Questions

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 29: We're in the toy shop today.

Fun for Starters · Week 29: We're in the toy shop today | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · In the toy shop Lesson 2 · A toy shop story Lesson 3 · Toy questions

You Need

  • A small crossword with six toy words and picture clues: ball, robot, kite, doll, boat, helicopter.
  • Toys set out before the lesson: a boat in a hat, a robot standing up, a doll in a pink and yellow dress, a red, white and blue kite and bike, three toys on the floor.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Toy crossword (8 min): your child writes the six toy words in the crossword.
  3. Look at the toys (7 min): ask: "What's in the hat? Which toys are red, white and blue? What colours are on the doll's dress? How many toys are there on the floor? What is standing?" Colour words
  4. Watch (4 min): What Is It? Song – Toys.
  5. Your turn to ask (6 min): your child moves the toys and asks you questions: "What's under the chair?" "Which toy is pink?" Toy words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's in the hat?" … "A boat!" … "Which toys are red, white and blue?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name lots of toys and describe them by colour and number.
  • Answer questions about a picture story with one word.
  • Follow and give polite instructions: "Stand up and wave, please!"
