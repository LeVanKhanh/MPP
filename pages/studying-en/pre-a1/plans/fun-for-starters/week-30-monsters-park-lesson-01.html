Week 30 · Lesson 1: Monster Sentences

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 30: Monsters in the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 30: Monsters in the park | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Monster sentences Lesson 2 · Monster names Lesson 3 · In the monster hall

You Need

  • Copy this word grid:
    A: A · big · monster · is · the
    B: on · blue · car · under · tree
    C: in · red · water · next to · flowers
    D: yellow · sitting · house · behind · chair
  • Four codes to read: A1 D1 A3 A4 D4 A5 B5 · A1 A2 B2 A3 A4 C1 A5 C3 · A1 C2 A3 A4 D2 B1 A5 B3 · A1 A3 A4 C4 A5 C5
  • A toy monster (or any toy) to hide.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Go Away! (Super Simple Songs).
  3. Code sentences (9 min): your child finds each word in the grid and writes the sentences: "A yellow monster is behind the tree." "A big blue monster is in the water." Then makes a code for you.
  4. Draw the park (7 min): your child draws a park and puts the monsters in it, just like the sentences. Position words
  5. Where's the monster? (5 min): hide the toy monster: "Is it behind the sofa?" "No!" "Is it next to the door?" "Yes!" Then swap.
  6. Watch (2 min): Go Away Monster (Dream English).
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A yellow monster is behind the tree." … "Is it next to the door?" … "Draw a red monster in the water!"
