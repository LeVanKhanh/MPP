Week 30 · Lesson 2: Monster Names

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 30: Monsters in the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 30: Monsters in the park | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Monster sentences Lesson 2 · Monster names Lesson 3 · In the monster hall

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five monsters (one of them red) with an empty line under each: "Mr ____", "Miss ____", "Mr ____", "Mrs ____".
  • Five toys for the position game.
  • Mr is for a man, Mrs for a married woman, Miss for a woman or girl who isn't married.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): say slowly and spell: "This is Mr Green. G – R – E – E – N." "This is Miss Bean. B – E – A – N." "This is Mr Kite." "This is Mrs Lemon." Your child writes each name. The red monster? Your child chooses its name!
  3. Watch (2 min): In Front Of, Behind, Between.
  4. Between (6 min): line up three toys: "The cat is between the dog and the bear." Your child moves them and says a new sentence.
  5. Put it there (5 min): "Put the bear in front of the box! Put the cat behind the chair! Put the dog between the bear and the cat!" Then your child gives you orders. Position words
  6. Watch (4 min): Prepositions Vocabulary Chant.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "This is Miss Bean. B – E – A – N." … "The cat is between the dog and the bear." … "Put the bear in front of the box!"
