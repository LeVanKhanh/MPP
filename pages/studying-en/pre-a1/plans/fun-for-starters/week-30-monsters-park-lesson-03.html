Week 30 · Lesson 3: In the Monster Hall

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: In, On, Under and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 30: Monsters in the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 30: Monsters in the park | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Monster sentences Lesson 2 · Monster names Lesson 3 · In the monster hall

You Need

  • Set up a scene in your hall or living room before the lesson: a phone in your hand, a small table under a mirror, a toy monster behind you, a chair between the table and the door, a lamp next to the door, a mat in front of the monster, books next to a small lamp.
  • Seven sentences with two choices: "The phone is on / in my hand." "The table is under / on the mirror." "The monster is between / behind me." "The chair is in / between the table and the door." "The big lamp is under / next to the door." "The mat is in front of / on the monster." "The books are behind / next to the small lamp."
  • A list of question-game questions from earlier weeks ("How many legs has a spider got? What colour is a banana? Spell cat. Where do fish live?").

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Choose the right word (8 min): your child looks at the scene, reads each sentence and circles the right word. Home words
  3. Watch (2 min): On In Under Song For Kids.
  4. Question game (8 min): ask questions from earlier weeks. One point for each right answer. Then your child asks you!
  5. A monster room (7 min): your child draws a room with a monster in it and writes two sentences: "The monster is behind the door. The cat is between the bed and the chair." Position words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Is the phone on or in my hand?" … "The monster is behind me!" … "The cat is between the bed and the chair."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of and between.
  • Build sentences from a word code and draw them.
  • Listen to spelled names and write them, with Mr, Mrs and Miss.
