Week 31 · Lesson 1: Transport Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 31: Coming and going.

Fun for Starters · Week 31: Coming and going | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Transport words Lesson 2 · How do you go? Lesson 3 · A helicopter

You Need

  • Letter cards for mixed-up words: e k i b, n a l e p, r o y r l, b e k t o r o m i, p e l o t c h r e i, r a c.
  • Toy cars, a lorry, a plane, a helicopter (or pictures) and a big box as a car park.
  • Drive a car, a bus or a lorry; ride a bike, a motorbike or a horse; fly a plane or a helicopter.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Vehicles – Talking Flashcards.
  3. Mixed-up transport (8 min): your child moves the letters and writes each word: bike, plane, lorry, motorbike, helicopter, car. Street words
  4. Park them (7 min): your child parks each toy in the box and says: "The lorry is next to the car. The helicopter is on the box!" Toy words
  5. Mr Happy (5 min): say: "Mr Happy drives his yellow lorry. He flies his plane. He loves riding his motorbike!" Your child writes what Mr Happy drives, flies and rides.
  6. Watch (3 min): Driving In My Car.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What does Mr Happy drive?" … "He drives a lorry." … "He flies a plane!"
