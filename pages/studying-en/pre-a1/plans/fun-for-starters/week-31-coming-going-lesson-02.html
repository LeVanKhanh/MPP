Week 31 · Lesson 2: How Do You Go?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 31: Coming and going.

Fun for Starters · Week 31: Coming and going | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Transport words Lesson 2 · How do you go? Lesson 3 · A helicopter

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five vehicles (a bike, a plane, a lorry, a motorbike, a helicopter), each with a big empty smile on the front. Crayons.
  • "By" (by car, by bus) is used a lot in this unit of the book; on the Cambridge list it is a Movers word, so it's fine to practise it here.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Wheels On The Bus.
  3. How do you go? (7 min): "How do you go to school?" "I go to school by car." "How do you go to the park?" "I walk to the park!" Your child writes three sentences about the family.
  4. I'd like to… (5 min): your child chooses: "I'd like to fly a helicopter!" or "I'd like to drive a bus!" and draws it.
  5. Listen and colour (7 min): say: "Colour the smile on the plane pink. The smile on the lorry is green. The bike's smile is orange." Your child colours each smile. Colour words
  6. Transport sounds (3 min): make a sound (a plane, a motorbike, a bus); your child guesses and makes one for you.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How do you go to school?" … "I go by car." … "I'd like to fly a helicopter!"
