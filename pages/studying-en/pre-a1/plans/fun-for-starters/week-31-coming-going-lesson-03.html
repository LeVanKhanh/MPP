Week 31 · Lesson 3: A Helicopter

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 31: Coming and going.

Fun for Starters · Week 31: Coming and going | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Transport words Lesson 2 · How do you go? Lesson 3 · A helicopter

You Need

  • Copy the text below, with word pictures: man, houses, street, bags, door, and two extra words, cow and hand.
  • A picture with transport and people in it: a picture book page, or a photo from a trip (a boat, a bridge, a bike, a car…).
  • Two lists for matching: "a great book · a funny game · a new bike · a beautiful plane" and "to read · to play · to ride · to fly".

A helicopter: A helicopter can fly. A (1) ______ or a woman flies it. From a helicopter, you can see lots of (2) ______ and the (3) ______ with cars and lorries. People put their (4) ______ in the helicopter. Then its (5) ______ closes, and it flies!

Answers: 1 man, 2 houses, 3 street, 4 bags, 5 door.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and choose (8 min): read the helicopter text together. Your child chooses a word from the pictures for each gap. Cow and hand are not in the text!
  3. Talk about the picture (7 min): "What's the man doing? What colour is the boat? Where's the bike? How many people can you see?" Your child answers, then asks you two questions. Street words
  4. I have… to… (6 min): your child matches the two lists and says: "I have a great book to read. I have a new bike to ride!" Then makes one about a real toy. Toy words
  5. Watch (4 min): Vehicles Vocabulary Chant.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who flies a helicopter?" … "I have a new bike to ride." … "What colour is the boat?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell the transport words: bike, plane, lorry, motorbike, helicopter, car.
  • Say how they go places: "I go to school by car. I walk to the park."
  • Read a short text and choose words for the gaps, and say "I have a book to read."
