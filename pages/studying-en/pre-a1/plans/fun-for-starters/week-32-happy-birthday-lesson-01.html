Week 32 · Lesson 1: A Birthday Picture

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Starters Wordlist and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 32: Happy birthday!

Fun for Starters · Week 32: Happy birthday! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A birthday picture Lesson 2 · Read and write Lesson 3 · Questions about me

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a birthday in a garden with pencil: a table with a cake, a tree, five balloons, a girl with a hat on her head, a boy with a balloon in his hand, a woman with a drink.
  • Crayons, a hat, a balloon, and a wrapped toy or book to give.
  • "Party" and "present" are Movers words. At Starters, say "It's my birthday!" and "This is for you!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Happy Birthday – This is for you. Thanks.
  3. Listen and colour (8 min): say slowly: "Colour the balloon in the tree blue. The girl's hat is pink and purple. Colour the cake brown. The boy's T-shirt is red." Check together: "What colour is the hat?" Colour words
  4. Birthday hunt (7 min): "Find a hat! … a balloon! … something to eat! … something to drink!" Your child names each thing: "This is a balloon!"
  5. Happy birthday! (5 min): act it out: "Happy birthday! This is for you!" "Thank you!" "What is it?" "It's a book!" Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Colour the balloon in the tree blue." … "Happy birthday! This is for you!" … "Thank you!"
