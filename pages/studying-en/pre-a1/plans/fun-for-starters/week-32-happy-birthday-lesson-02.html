Week 32 · Lesson 2: Read and Write

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Clothes and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 32: Happy birthday!

Fun for Starters · Week 32: Happy birthday! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A birthday picture Lesson 2 · Read and write Lesson 3 · Questions about me

You Need

  • The birthday picture from Lesson 1.
  • Eight clues with the first letter of each answer: "You can see food and drink on this. (t____)" "This is behind the people and it's big. (t___)" "A boy is wearing a T-shirt and blue (j____)." "One girl has a balloon in her (h___)." "The birthday girl is wearing this on her head. (h__)" "These are on the people's feet. (s____)" "You can eat this on your birthday. (c___)" "There are five of these. (b_______)"
  • Six small pictures to label: a skirt, a camera, an ice cream, shorts, a pile of clothes, a basketball.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Read and write (9 min): your child reads each clue, looks at the birthday picture and writes the word.
  3. Picture words (6 min): your child writes the word under each picture: skirt, camera, ice cream, shorts, clothes, basketball. Clothes words
  4. Watch (3 min): What Are You Wearing?
  5. Wear, eat or play? (7 min): make three columns: "What can you wear? What can you eat? What sport can you play?" Your child writes the picture words and three more in each column. Food words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "You can see food and drink on this. What is it?" … "A table!" … "What sport can you play?"
