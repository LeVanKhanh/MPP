Week 32 · Lesson 3: Questions About Me

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Food and Drinks and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 32: Happy birthday!

Fun for Starters · Week 32: Happy birthday! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A birthday picture Lesson 2 · Read and write Lesson 3 · Questions about me

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a cake, a jacket and a horse in a row, and three names under them: Grace, Tom, Ann.
  • In the Starters speaking test, the examiner asks a few questions about the child. Short answers are fine: "Yes, I do." "A horse!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw lines (7 min): say: "It's Tom's birthday. He's got a cake." "Grace has got a new pink jacket." "Ann can ride a horse!" Your child draws a line from each name to the right picture.
  3. Answer the questions (8 min): ask three questions for each picture: "What's this? Do you like cake? What do you eat for lunch?" "Have you got a jacket? What clothes are you wearing?" "Can you ride a horse? What's your favourite animal?" Animal words
  4. Watch (3 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream?
  5. My birthday plan (7 min): your child plans a birthday: what to eat and drink, what to wear, which games to play. Writes a short list and draws the cake. Food words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Do you like cake?" … "Can you ride a horse?" … "What's your favourite animal?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Listen and colour a birthday picture, and talk about it.
  • Read clues about a picture and write the words.
  • Answer questions about themselves: "Do you like cake? Can you ride a horse? What's your favourite animal?"
