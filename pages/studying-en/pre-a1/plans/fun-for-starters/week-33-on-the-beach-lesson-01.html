Week 33 · Lesson 1: Beach Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 33: On the beach.

Fun for Starters · Week 33: On the beach | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Beach words Lesson 2 · What are the missing words? Lesson 3 · Who's on the beach?

You Need

  • A beach picture: a picture book page, a holiday photo, or your own drawing with sand, sea, a ball, two boats, a ship, the sun, fish, birds, a mat, shells, a phone and a monkey in a tree.
  • A list of the fourteen words next to the picture: sand, sea, beach, ball, boat, sun, fish, bird, mat, water, shell, phone, monkey, ship.
  • Letter cards for mixed-up words: t c h a w, o o f t, a n a n a s b.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Let's Go To The Beach (Super Simple Songs).
  3. Draw lines (9 min): your child reads each word and draws a line to the thing in the picture. Beach words
  4. Answer questions (7 min): "How many birds can you see? What colour is the boat? Where's the monkey? What's the man doing?" Your child answers, then asks you two questions.
  5. Mixed-up words (5 min): your child moves the letters to make watch, foot and bananas, finds them in the picture (or at home), then mixes up two words for you.
  6. Watch (2 min): Fun At The Beach!
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "How many birds can you see?" … "Where's the monkey?" … "It's in the tree!"
