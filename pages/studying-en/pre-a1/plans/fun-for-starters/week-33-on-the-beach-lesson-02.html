Week 33 · Lesson 2: What Are the Missing Words?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Starters Wordlist and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 33: On the beach.

Fun for Starters · Week 33: On the beach | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Beach words Lesson 2 · What are the missing words? Lesson 3 · Who's on the beach?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw two small pictures. 1: two boats on the sand (one green, one blue) and a white bird walking. 2: four shells and a brown dog sleeping.
  • Questions with gaps and a word box (walking, shell, bird, boat, dog, green, boats, shells, sleeping, white): "What's this? a ____. What colour is it? ____. How many ____ are there? 2. What's the ____ doing? ____." The same again for picture 2.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Missing words (9 min): your child looks at each picture and writes the missing words from the box. Read the questions and answers aloud together at the end.
  3. Watch (6 min): The Beach – Vocabulary for Kids.
  4. Your beach picture (8 min): your child draws a small beach picture with three things in it, then asks you the four questions: "What's this? What colour is it? How many…? What's the… doing?" Beach words
  5. Walk like a bird (2 min): "Walk like a bird! … Swim like a fish! … Sleep like a dog!" Animal words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's the bird doing?" … "It's walking." … "How many shells are there?"
