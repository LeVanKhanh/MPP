Week 33 · Lesson 3: Who's on the Beach?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 33: On the beach.

Fun for Starters · Week 33: On the beach | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Beach words Lesson 2 · What are the missing words? Lesson 3 · Who's on the beach?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a beach with six people: one in a tree, one on a boat, one swimming, one sitting on a chair, one running with a ball, one playing in the sand. Write seven names around it: Matt, Dan, May, Alice, Mark, Jill, Hugo (one name has no person).
  • A bag with beach things, real or pictures: a ball, a hat, a phone, a book, a shell, an apple.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and draw lines (9 min): say slowly: "Dan is in the tree. Alice is sitting on a chair. Hugo is swimming in the sea. Jill is running with a ball." Your child draws a line from each name to the right person.
  3. Watch (2 min): Fun At The Beach!
  4. What's in your beach bag? (8 min): take turns: "In my beach bag there's a ball." "In my beach bag there's a ball and a hat." Then your child feels in the real bag and guesses: "It's a shell!" Beach words
  5. My beach day (6 min): your child draws a day at the beach and writes two sentences: "I'm swimming in the sea. My dad is sleeping on a mat!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who's in the tree?" … "Dan is!" … "In my beach bag there's a ball and…"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name things at the beach: sand, sea, shell, boat, ship, mat…
  • Ask and answer "What's this? What colour is it? How many…? What's the… doing?"
  • Listen and draw lines from names to people in a beach picture.
