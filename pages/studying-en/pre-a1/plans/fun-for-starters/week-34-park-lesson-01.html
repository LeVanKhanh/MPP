Week 34 · Lesson 1: Who Is Doing What?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 34: Let's go to the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 34: Let's go to the park | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who is doing what? Lesson 2 · In the park Lesson 3 · Animal sentences

You Need

  • A park picture: a picture book page, or draw one with a big boy flying a kite, a man running, a woman waving, a girl picking up a pear and a small boy kicking a ball.
  • Five questions with answers to choose (a–e): "What is the big boy doing? Who is running? Who is waving? What is the girl doing? What is the small boy doing?" · a kicking a ball · b the man · c picking up a pear · d the woman · e flying a kite.
  • Five questions for one-word answers: "Who is kicking a ball? What is the man doing? Who is picking up a pear? What is the woman doing? What is the big boy flying?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Fun on the Playground.
  3. Look and choose (8 min): your child looks at the picture, reads each question and writes the letter of the right answer.
  4. One-word answers (6 min): now the other way round: "Who is kicking a ball?" "The small boy!" "What is the big boy flying?" "A kite!" Your child writes one word for each.
  5. Who is…? (6 min): everyone at home does an action. Your child asks and answers: "Who is waving? Mummy is!" "What is Daddy doing? He's running!"
  6. Watch (3 min): Playground – Let's Play!
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who is running?" … "The man!" … "What is the girl doing? She's picking up a pear."
