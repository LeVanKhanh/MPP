Week 34 · Lesson 2: In the Park

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 34: Let's go to the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 34: Let's go to the park | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who is doing what? Lesson 2 · In the park Lesson 3 · Animal sentences

You Need

  • If you can, do this lesson in a real park! If not, use a park picture or a window.
  • A simple park line drawing to colour (trees, a ball, a kite, children), and crayons.
  • A short chant on paper with two gaps: "In the park, in the park, I can run and ______! In the park, in the park, with my ______ today! Hooray!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. You're in the park! (8 min): ask: "How many people are in the park? How many trees can you see? Is it morning, afternoon or evening? Who are you with? What are you doing? Which animals can you see? What are you wearing?" Your child answers; at home, they write three of the answers. Animal words
  3. Listen and colour (7 min): say: "Colour the kite red and yellow. The boy's T-shirt is green. Colour the ball orange." Colour words
  4. Watch and move (4 min): Yes, I Can!
  5. Our park chant (6 min): your child fills the gaps ("jump", "kite") and you say the chant together, clapping. Then change the words: "I can run and swim!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Is it morning, afternoon or evening?" … "Who are you with?" … "Let's go to the park!"
