Week 34 · Lesson 3: Animal Sentences

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 34: Let's go to the park.

Fun for Starters · Week 34: Let's go to the park | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who is doing what? Lesson 2 · In the park Lesson 3 · Animal sentences

You Need

  • Eight funny animal cards (draw them quickly or print pictures): a frog riding a motorbike, a duck driving a car, a crocodile playing tennis, a cat singing, a horse playing with a ball, a fish flying a kite, a spider riding a bike, a monkey playing the guitar.
  • Two players or two groups. One point for each right sentence.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Animal sentences (10 min): take turns: pick a card and say the sentence: "The crocodile is playing tennis!" One point for a good sentence, two points if you write it. Animal words
  3. My funny animal (7 min): your child draws their own funny animal and writes: "The elephant is riding a bike!"
  4. Watch and move (3 min): Fast Slow Action Song.
  5. Act the sentence (5 min): your child acts one of the cards without words; you guess the sentence. Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "The crocodile is playing tennis!" … "What is the monkey doing?" … "It's playing the guitar!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Answer "Who is…ing?" and "What is… doing?" about a picture.
  • Talk about a real park: people, trees, morning or afternoon, what they're doing.
  • Make funny animal sentences: "The crocodile is playing tennis!"
