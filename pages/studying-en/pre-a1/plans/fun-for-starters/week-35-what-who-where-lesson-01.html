Fun for Starters · Week 35: What, who and where? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who, what or where? Lesson 2 · In Sue's bedroom Lesson 3 · Questions about you

You Need

  • Three big cards: Who, What, Where.
  • A hat, a camera (or a toy camera), a flower, a bag and a shell, and a doll or a family member to dress up.
  • Spelling tricks: wh + at = what · w + here = where · t + here = there · h + at = hat.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What's this? – It's a pencil.
  3. Which word? (6 min): point at a person, a thing or a place. Your child holds up the right card: "Who's this? It's Grandma!" "What's this? It's a clock!" "Where's the cat? On the sofa!"
  4. Dress-up questions (9 min): put a hat on the doll, a camera in its hand and a shell in the bag. Your child asks and answers: "Who's this? It's Tony." "What's this? A hat." "Where's the camera? In Tony's hand." Then writes three answers.
  5. Spelling tricks (6 min): write the tricks and say them together. Then play: "Where's your teddy? Here or there?" Your child points and says "Here!" or "There!" Position words
  6. Watch (2 min): Where is it?
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's this?" … "What's she got in her hand?" … "Where's your teddy? Here or there?"
