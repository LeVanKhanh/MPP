Week 35 · Lesson 2: In Sue's Bedroom

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and In, On, Under. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 35: What, who and where?

Fun for Starters · Week 35: What, who and where? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who, what or where? Lesson 2 · In Sue's bedroom Lesson 3 · Questions about you

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures of a story. 1: clothes and books on the floor of Sue's bedroom, two girls on the bed, one playing with a doll, and an angry mother at the door. 2: the clothes are under the bed, the books are in the cupboard. 3: the girls are sitting on the bed, and the mother is opening the cupboard.
  • Four questions with a gap: "____ is angry? ____ are the girls? ____ are the clothes? ____ is playing with a doll?" and the answers in a box: the mother · in Sue's bedroom · on the floor · the girls.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Who or where? (8 min): your child looks at picture 1, writes Who or Where in each question, then finds the answer in the box.
  3. The story (8 min): look at pictures 2 and 3. Ask: "Where are the clothes now?" "Under the bed!" "Where are the books now?" "In the cupboard." "Who is sitting on the bed?" "The girls." "Who is opening the cupboard?" "The mother." Your child writes one or two words for each. Home words
  4. Watch (3 min): Hide & Seek (Around The House).
  5. Put it back (4 min): in your child's room: "Where are your books? Put them in the bookcase!" Your child puts things back and says where they are. Position words
  6. Where's teddy? (2 min): hide teddy: "Where's teddy?" "Under the bed!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who is angry?" … "Where are the clothes now?" … "Under the bed!"
