Week 35 · Lesson 3: Questions About You

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 35: What, who and where?

Fun for Starters · Week 35: What, who and where? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Who, what or where? Lesson 2 · In Sue's bedroom Lesson 3 · Questions about you

You Need

  • Nine questions on paper with a line for each answer: "What do you watch on TV? Who watches TV with you? Where is the television in your house? What do you have for dinner? Who has dinner with you? Where do you have dinner? What games do you play? Who do you play with at school? Where do you play in your school?"
  • Our own monster poem (below), paper and crayons.

My monster
Where's my monster? Is it here? Is it there?
It's on my bed! It's under my chair!
Who's that monster? What's its name?
It's my friend Matt, and we play a game!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Questions about you (9 min): ask the nine questions. Your child answers aloud, then writes short answers on the lines.
  3. Ask me! (6 min): now your child asks you the same questions and listens to your answers.
  4. Watch (2 min): Where are you? In the kitchen.
  5. Read, listen and draw (8 min): read the monster poem together. Then read it again slowly while your child draws the monster on the bed and under the chair. Say it together with a scary voice! Home words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Who watches TV with you?" … "Where do you have dinner?" … "Where's my monster?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Choose the right question word: What, Who or Where.
  • Answer questions about a picture story with one or two words.
  • Answer questions about their own life: TV, dinner, games and school.
