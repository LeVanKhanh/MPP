Week 36 · Lesson 1: My Hobbies

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 36: Great games, great hobbies!

Fun for Starters · Week 36: Great games, great hobbies! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My hobbies Lesson 2 · A computer game Lesson 3 · Which is Tom?

You Need

  • Hobby cards (words or quick pictures): playing football, fishing, drawing, reading, riding a bike, playing the guitar, watching TV, playing computer games.
  • Letter cards for mixed-up toy words: d y e t d, a t b o, e i k t, n a e l p, y e o n k m.
  • A small table for a family hobby survey.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Do You Like To Do? (Super Simple Songs)
  3. Hobby cards (6 min): your child sorts the cards into two piles and says: "I like drawing. I don't like fishing." Toy words
  4. Mixed-up toys (6 min): your child moves the letters to make teddy, boat, kite, plane and monkey, and writes each word.
  5. Hobby survey (7 min): your child asks people at home: "Do you like fishing? Do you like reading?" and ticks or crosses the table. "Daddy likes fishing!"
  6. Watch (4 min): My Favorite Toys.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What do you like doing?" … "I like drawing." … "Do you like fishing?"
