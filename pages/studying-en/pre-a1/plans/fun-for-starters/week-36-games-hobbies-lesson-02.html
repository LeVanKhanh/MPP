Week 36 · Lesson 2: A Computer Game

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 36: Great games, great hobbies!

Lesson 1 · My hobbies Lesson 2 · A computer game Lesson 3 · Which is Tom?

You Need

  • Copy the gapped story below. Your child chooses their own words first; then you read your answers. Both can be right!
  • Paper and crayons. Word cards for long words: cup + board, book + case, play + ground, water + melon, skate + board, foot + ball.

Ben's game: Ben likes playing it in the (1) ______. He sits on his (2) ______ and plays it on his (3) ______. There's a big (4) ______ in the game. It's catching lots of (5) ______! Can you see the (6) ______ in the tree? It likes eating (7) ______!

Our answers: 1 morning, 2 bed, 3 tablet, 4 crocodile, 5 balls, 6 bird, 7 apples.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. You choose! (9 min): your child writes a word in each gap. Then read the story with their words, and then with yours. Which story is funnier?
  3. My computer game (8 min): your child draws a computer game screen and tells you about it: "There's a monkey in it. It's riding a bike. It likes eating bananas!"
  4. Watch (3 min): Playing With My Car.
  5. Long words (5 min): put two cards together to make one long word: "play + ground = playground!" Then your child finds one more. Toy words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's in the game?" … "A big crocodile!" … "Play and ground make… playground!"
