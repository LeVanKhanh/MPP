Week 36 · Lesson 3: Which Is Tom?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Toys and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 36: Great games, great hobbies!

Fun for Starters · Week 36: Great games, great hobbies! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My hobbies Lesson 2 · A computer game Lesson 3 · Which is Tom?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a playground in pencil: a boy with red hair holding a ball, a boy with black hair fishing, a girl in a blue dress drawing, a girl in a yellow T-shirt watching TV in a window, a girl with a kite, a girl with a toy crocodile.
  • Three short dialogues on paper, with names Tom, Kim and Lucy (below).

1 "There's my friend, Tom! He enjoys fishing." "The boy with the red hair?" "No! Tom's hair is black."

2 "Kim is here too. She loves watching TV." "The girl in the blue dress?" "No, Kim is wearing a yellow T-shirt."

3 "And that's Lucy. She likes kites." "The girl with the crocodile?" "No, Lucy's got a fantastic kite!"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and colour (8 min): say: "Colour the boy's ball orange. The girl's dress is blue. Colour the kite red and green." Colour words
  3. Read and draw lines (8 min): read each dialogue together. Your child finds Tom, Kim and Lucy in the picture and draws a line from each name.
  4. Watch (3 min): Playground – Let's Play!
  5. My friend (6 min): your child draws a friend and writes: "My friend Ann likes drawing. She's got long black hair." Toy words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Which is Tom?" … "The boy with the black hair!" … "My friend likes drawing."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Talk about hobbies: "I like drawing. I don't like fishing."
  • Choose words for gaps in a story, then listen and check.
  • Read short dialogues and find the right person in a picture.
