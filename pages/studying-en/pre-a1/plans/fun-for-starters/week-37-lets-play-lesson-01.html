Week 37 · Lesson 1: Ball Games

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Starters Wordlist and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 37: Let's play.

Fun for Starters · Week 37: Let's play | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Ball games Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Would you like to play?

You Need

  • A soft ball and some space (outside is best). A board game, a bat, a football, toy bikes or pictures.
  • Six cards for tick or cross: a board game ("This is a board game."), a horse ("These are bats."), a boat ("This is a goat."), clothes ("These are jackets."), bikes ("These are bikes."), a basketball ("This is a football.").
  • Ball verbs with a missing letter: b_unc_ · c_tch · h_t · k_ck · thr_w.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Sports! (Super Simple Songs).
  3. Tick or cross (6 min): your child reads each card and ticks or crosses: "These are bats. No! They're horses. Cross!" Toy words
  4. Ball verbs (5 min): your child writes the missing a, e, i, o or u: bounce, catch, hit, kick, throw.
  5. Ball time (9 min): with the ball: "Bounce it! … Catch it! … Kick it! … Throw it to me!" Then your child gives the orders. Count how many catches you can do.
  6. Ball words (2 min): "What can you do with a ball?" "I can kick it, throw it and catch it!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Throw it to me!" … "Catch it!" … "What can you do with a ball?"
