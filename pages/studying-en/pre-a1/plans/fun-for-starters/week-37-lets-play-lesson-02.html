Week 37 · Lesson 2: Listen and Tick

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 37: Let's play.

Fun for Starters · Week 37: Let's play | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Ball games Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Would you like to play?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw five rows of three small pictures (A, B, C): Kim's sport today (riding a horse, hockey, swimming); what Lucy is doing (throwing a ball, bouncing a ball, playing baseball); what Tom is watching on TV (tennis, football, table tennis); where Dan is (a street, a park, a beach); which girl is Sam (with a hockey stick, with a tennis racket, with nothing).
  • Five places for sports: a beach, a park, a playground, a garden, a television. Five sentences with a gap (below, in the lesson).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (9 min): for each row, say: "Which sport is Kim doing today? She's playing hockey!" "Where is Dan? He's at the beach." Your child ticks A, B or C.
  3. Watch (3 min): Sports Song – Do You Like Sports?
  4. Sports and places (8 min): read and fill the gap with a sport: "Alex is at the beach. He can ____ in the sea." "Ann goes to the park. She plays ____ there." "Sue watches ____ on TV." "Jill plays ____ in the playground." "Mark plays ____ in the garden." Any sport that fits is right!
  5. Sports mime (5 min): act a sport: "Are you playing tennis?" "No!" "Are you playing badminton?" "Yes!" Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Which sport is Kim doing today?" … "She's playing hockey." … "Where is Dan?"
