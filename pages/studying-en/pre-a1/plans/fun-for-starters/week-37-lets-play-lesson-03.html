Week 37 · Lesson 3: Would You Like to Play?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Starters Wordlist and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 37: Let's play.

Fun for Starters · Week 37: Let's play | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Ball games Lesson 2 · Listen and tick Lesson 3 · Would you like to play?

You Need

  • Answer cards: Cool! · OK! · Great! · Yes, please! · No, thanks!
  • Three invitation bubbles: "Let's play ____!" · "Do you want to play ____?" · "Would you like to play ____?"
  • A list of quick actions for the moving game: jump five times, run to the door, jump like a frog, clap ten times.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Invitations (7 min): your child writes a game in each bubble. Then invite each other: "Would you like to play football?" "Yes, please!" "Let's play tennis!" "Cool!" "Do you want to play a board game?" "No, thanks!" Toy words
  3. Watch (3 min): Sports! (Super Simple Songs).
  4. Let's move! (8 min): take turns: "Let's jump five times!" "OK!" Everyone does it. "Let's run to the door!" "Great!" Your child says each one too.
  5. My favourite sport (7 min): your child draws their favourite sport and writes: "My favourite sport is football. I play it in the park with my dad."
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Would you like to play football?" … "Yes, please!" … "Let's jump!" "OK!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Name Starters sports and the ball verbs: bounce, catch, hit, kick, throw.
  • Listen to short talks about sport and tick the right picture.
  • Invite and answer: "Would you like to play tennis?" "Yes, please!" / "No, thanks!"
