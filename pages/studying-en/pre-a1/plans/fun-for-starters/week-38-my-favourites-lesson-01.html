Week 38 · Lesson 1: Word Groups

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 38: My favourites.

Fun for Starters · Week 38: My favourites | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Word groups Lesson 2 · Ask and answer Lesson 3 · My alien friend

You Need

  • Eight cards with three words each: boots, socks, trousers · drawing, fishing, painting · blue, green, grey · donkey, chicken, tiger · badminton, baseball, football · meatballs, rice, grapes · robot, doll, teddy · milk, juice, water.
  • Group labels: clothes, hobbies, colours, animals, sports, food, toys, drinks.
  • A big paper circle cut into eight parts, and crayons.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What's Your Favorite Color?
  3. Word groups (8 min): your child reads each card and puts the right label on it: "Boots, socks, trousers – clothes!" Then adds one more word to each card.
  4. Group race (5 min): "Three animals – go!" "Cat, horse, frog!" "Three drinks – go!" Then swap.
  5. My favourites wheel (7 min): your child draws a favourite thing in each part of the circle: a favourite animal, colour, drink, food, toy, sport, hobby and clothes.
  6. Watch (3 min): Do you like…? Yes or No.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Boots, socks, trousers – what are these?" … "Clothes!" … "Three animals – go!"
