Week 38 · Lesson 2: Ask and Answer

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Colours and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 38: My favourites.

Fun for Starters · Week 38: My favourites | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Word groups Lesson 2 · Ask and answer Lesson 3 · My alien friend

You Need

  • A table: favourite animal, colour, drink, food, toy, sport, hobby down the side; "My favourite" and two family names across the top.
  • Before the lesson, draw a girl called Lucy with six small pictures round her: green paint, a kite, pears, a glass of milk, a horse, a tennis ball.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Favourites survey (9 min): your child fills in their own column, then asks two people: "What's your favourite animal?" "What's your favourite drink?" and writes the answers.
  3. Fantastic, great, cool (6 min): your child writes a sentence from the table: "Cats are fantastic, football is great and drawing is cool!"
  4. About Lucy (8 min): say: "Lucy is nine." Your child looks at the pictures and completes: "Her favourite colour is ____ and her favourite toy is her ____. She likes eating ____ and drinking ____. Her favourite animal is a ____. She enjoys playing ____." Colour words
  5. Watch (2 min): What Do You Like To Do? (Super Simple Songs)
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What's your favourite animal?" … "Her favourite colour is green." … "She enjoys playing tennis."
