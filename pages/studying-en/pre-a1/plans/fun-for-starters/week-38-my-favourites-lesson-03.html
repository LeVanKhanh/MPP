Week 38 · Lesson 3: My Alien Friend

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 38: My favourites.

Fun for Starters · Week 38: My favourites | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Word groups Lesson 2 · Ask and answer Lesson 3 · My alien friend

You Need

  • Paper and crayons.
  • Six pairs of word cards, British and American: football / soccer, colour / color, shop / store, flat / apartment, lorry / truck, chips / fries. All twelve are on the Starters list.
  • A short letter to read (below).

Ben's letter (London): Hello! My name's Ben. I live in a flat next to a shop in London. My mum drives a big red lorry. My favourite sport is football. My favourite colour is red and I love eating chips! Tell me about you!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. My alien friend (8 min): your child invents an alien friend, draws it and writes: "Zog is my alien friend. His favourite colour is purple and his favourite toy is his robot. He likes eating bananas and drinking milk. His favourite animal is a hippo. He enjoys playing soccer!"
  3. British and American (7 min): mix the twelve cards. Your child finds the pairs: "Lorry in London, truck in New York!" Starters wordlist
  4. Pat's letter (7 min): read Ben's letter together. Pat lives in New York. Your child writes Pat's letter with the American words: "I live in an apartment next to a store… My dad drives a big truck. My favorite sport is soccer… I love eating fries!"
  5. Watch (3 min): Sports! (Super Simple Songs).
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's your alien friend's favourite food?" … "Lorry in London, truck in New York!" … "I live in an apartment."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Sort words into groups: clothes, hobbies, colours, animals, sports, food, toys, drinks.
  • Ask and answer "What's your favourite…?" and write about a friend.
  • Know some American words: soccer, store, apartment, truck, fries, color.
