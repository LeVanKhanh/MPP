You Need

  • Six cards for tick or cross: a chair ("This is a child."), balloons ("These are balloons."), two hands ("These are feet."), a fish ("This is a mouse."), a woman ("This is a person."), a house ("This is a house.").
  • A gap table: a m_use / lots of m_ce · one f__t / two f__t · a m_n / nine m_n · one wom_n / ten wom_n · a ch_ld / four ch_ldren · one f_sh / lots of f_sh · a sh__p / seven sh__p · one p_rs_n / six p__ple.
  • Toy animals and people to count. Fish and sheep stay the same: one fish, two fish; one sheep, two sheep.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): I Have Two Eyes.
  3. Tick or cross (6 min): your child reads each card and ticks or crosses: "These are feet? No, they're hands! Cross!"
  4. Complete the words (8 min): your child fills the gaps in both columns and reads each pair aloud: "one foot, two feet!"
  5. Count around (6 min): count toys and people: "One sheep, two sheep, three sheep!" "One man, two men!" Animal words
  6. Watch (3 min): Five Spotted Dogs.
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "One foot, two…" "Feet!" … "One mouse, lots of…" "Mice!" … "One sheep, two sheep."
