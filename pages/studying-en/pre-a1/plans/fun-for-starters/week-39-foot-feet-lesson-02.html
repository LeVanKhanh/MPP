Week 39 · Lesson 2: There Is, There Are

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 39: One foot, two feet.

Fun for Starters · Week 39: One foot, two feet | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Tricky plurals Lesson 2 · There is, there are Lesson 3 · What's different?

You Need

  • Toys set out before the lesson: two mice, one woman, two feet (a doll's), one man, two sheep, one child, two fish.
  • Seven sentences for yes or no: "There is one fish." "There is one mouse." "There is one woman." "There is one foot." "There are two men." "There are two sheep." "There are two children."
  • Before the lesson, draw a garden by some water: three children, two sheep eating bread, four mice running behind a dog, five fish in the water, people in blue T-shirts. A table with five columns: number · animal or person · is / are · eating / swimming / running / wearing · the rest.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Yes or no? (8 min): your child reads each sentence, counts the toys and writes yes or no: "There are two men? No, there's one man!"
  3. Make sentences (8 min): look at the garden picture. Your child takes one word from each column: "Two sheep are eating some bread." "Five fish are swimming in the water." "Four mice are running behind the dog." Animal words
  4. Watch (3 min): How Many Fingers?
  5. One or more? (6 min): say a word: "Child!" Your child says the plural: "Children!" Then: "Feet!" "Foot!" Make it fast and silly.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "There is one woman." … "There are two sheep." … "Five fish are swimming in the water."
