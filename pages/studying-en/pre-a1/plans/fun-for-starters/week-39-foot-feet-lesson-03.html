Week 39 · Lesson 3: What's Different?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and Family. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 39: One foot, two feet.

Fun for Starters · Week 39: One foot, two feet | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Tricky plurals Lesson 2 · There is, there are Lesson 3 · What's different?

You Need

  • The garden picture from Lesson 2, and a second copy where you change six things: the number of mice, fish, children, sheep, a person's T-shirt colour, and what the dog is doing.
  • The alphabet with numbers (a = 1, b = 2 … z = 26) on paper.
  • A word code to read: 6 – 5 – 5 – 20 (feet) and 13 – 9 – 3 – 5 (mice).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Talk about the picture (7 min): ask about the first picture: "How many children are there? What are the sheep doing? Where are the mice? What colour are the T-shirts?" Your child answers, then asks you. Family words
  3. What's different? (8 min): show the second picture. Your child finds the six changes and says each one: "In this picture there are three mice, but in this picture there are five!"
  4. What's my word? (7 min): your child uses the number alphabet to read your codes (feet, mice), then makes a code for you to read.
  5. Watch (3 min): Ten Little Fingers.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many children are there?" … "In this picture there are five mice!" … "6 – 5 – 5 – 20… feet!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use the tricky plurals: mice, feet, men, women, children, people, fish, sheep.
  • Say "There is one…" and "There are two…", and check sentences against a picture.
  • Talk about a picture and find what is different in a second picture.
