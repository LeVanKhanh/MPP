Week 40 · Lesson 1: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 40: Night and day.

Fun for Starters · Week 40: Night and day | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Morning, afternoon, evening, night Lesson 2 · Listen and write Lesson 3 · Mark's morning

You Need

  • Four quick drawings: a child in bed with the sun at the window, a child asleep with the moon, children going to school, a family watching TV in the evening.
  • Word cards: have breakfast, go to school, have lessons, have lunch, have dinner, have a bath, go to sleep.
  • Starters says "in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening" but "at night". "Get up", "get dressed" and "wash" are Movers words, so they wait until later.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): Wake Up! Daily Routines Song.
  3. Which part of the day? (6 min): your child writes morning, afternoon, evening or night under each drawing.
  4. In the or at? (8 min): make four columns: "in the morning", "in the afternoon", "in the evening", "at night". Your child puts each word card in a column: "I have lunch in the afternoon. I go to sleep at night!" Home words
  5. Act it (8 min): act one card without words: "Are you having a bath?" "Yes!" Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "I have breakfast in the morning." … "I go to sleep at night." … "In the or at?"
