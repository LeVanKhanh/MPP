Week 40 · Lesson 2: Listen and Write

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 40: Night and day.

Fun for Starters · Week 40: Night and day | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Morning, afternoon, evening, night Lesson 2 · Listen and write Lesson 3 · Mark's morning

You Need

  • A description to read (below). Paper and pencils.
  • This is the Starters listening task where children write a name or a number. Spell the names.

Read this to your child: "This is Hugo. He's eight. He's got six things in his school bag. He lives in Park Street, P – A – R – K. He goes to school on bus number twelve. His friend's name is Dan, D – A – N. His teacher's name is Mr Brown, B – R – O – W – N."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and write (8 min): read twice. Ask: "How many things are in Hugo's school bag? What is the name of Hugo's street? What is the number of Hugo's bus? What is Hugo's friend's name? What is Hugo's teacher's name?" Your child writes a name or a number. School words
  3. Watch (5 min): My Day – From Morning to Night.
  4. My day (7 min): your child draws four small pictures of their own day and writes one sentence under each: "In the morning I have breakfast."
  5. Clock game (5 min): set a toy clock (or draw one) to 7, 12, 6 and 8: "What do you do at seven? I have breakfast!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What is the number of Hugo's bus?" … "Twelve!" … "What do you do in the morning?"
