Week 40 · Lesson 3: Mark's Morning

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: At Home and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 40: Night and day.

Fun for Starters · Week 40: Night and day | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Morning, afternoon, evening, night Lesson 2 · Listen and write Lesson 3 · Mark's morning

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw eight small pictures from a boy's morning and mix them up: Mark in bed, a clock, Mark's face, his shoes, his cat, a glass of milk and some bread, his school bag, a school bus.
  • A story to read (below).

Read this to your child: "It's the morning. Mark opens his eyes. He looks at his clock – it's seven! He goes to the bathroom. Then he finds his school clothes and his red shoes. He goes to the living room and says hello to his cat. Then he goes to the kitchen. He has milk and bread for breakfast. He finds his school bag in the hall. He says 'Goodbye!' to his mum and dad, runs to the bus and goes to school."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and number (9 min): read the story slowly. Your child writes a number on each picture, 1 to 8, in the order of the story. Read it again to check.
  3. Tell it back (6 min): your child tells Mark's morning with the pictures: "He opens his eyes. Then he looks at his clock…" Food words
  4. Run to the door (5 min): say: "Run to the door if you have breakfast in the morning! Run to the door if you have a bath at night!" Everyone who does it runs. Then your child gives the orders.
  5. Watch (5 min): My Day – From Morning to Night.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What does Mark do in the morning?" … "He opens his eyes." … "Run to the door if you go to sleep at night!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Say the parts of the day: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night.
  • Talk about their day: have breakfast, go to school, have lunch, have a bath, go to sleep.
  • Listen to a story about someone's morning and put the pictures in order.
