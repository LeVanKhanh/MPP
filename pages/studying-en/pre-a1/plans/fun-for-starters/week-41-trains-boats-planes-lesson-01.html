Week 41 · Lesson 1: Transport Words

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 41: Trains, boats and planes.

You Need

  • Write these first letters with spaces for the rest: b__ · b___ · b___ · p____ · t____ · t____ · s___ · c__ · l____ · m________ · h_________. Around them, draw a circle of letters your child can use: a b c e h i k l m n o p r s t u y.
  • Paper with three boxes: "It can fly", "It goes on the water", "It goes on the street".

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (6 min): What Do You See? – Vehicles.
  3. Letter circle (8 min): your child uses the letters to finish ten transport words: bus, bike, boat, plane, train, truck, ship, car, lorry, motorbike or helicopter. Help with the long ones!
  4. Where does it go? (5 min): your child writes each word in a box: "A ship goes on the water. A helicopter can fly." Street words
  5. Transport charades (6 min): act a ride: "Are you on a train?" "No!" "Are you on a boat?" "Yes!" Then swap.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "B – U – S, bus!" … "A ship goes on the water." … "Are you on a train?"
