Week 41 · Lesson 2: A Day Out

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 41: Trains, boats and planes.

Fun for Starters · Week 41: Trains, boats and planes | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Transport words Lesson 2 · A day out Lesson 3 · My ticket

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw three pictures. 1: two children riding bikes on a street, four birds in the sky. 2: the children at the beach, a woman driving a red car. 3: a boy painting a kite, a girl swimming in the sea.
  • Questions: "How many children are there? What are the children riding? How many birds can you see? Where are the children now? What is the woman driving? What is the boy painting? What is the girl doing?"
  • Five rows of three small pictures (A, B, C): which boy is Bill (on a boat, on a motorbike, on a bus); which sport Sam is playing (tennis, football, basketball); what Tom is doing (playing the piano, singing, playing the guitar); where Mum is (kitchen, bedroom, garden); how Alex comes to school (walks, bus, car).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. A day out (9 min): your child looks at the three pictures and writes one or two words for each question: "2", "their bikes", "at the beach", "a car"…
  3. Watch (3 min): The Wheels On The Bus.
  4. Listen and tick (8 min): for each row, say: "Which boy is Bill? He's on a motorbike." "How does Alex come to school? He walks." Your child ticks A, B or C. Home words
  5. How do you go? (5 min): "How do you come to school? How does Daddy go to the shop?" Your child answers and writes one sentence: "I go to school by car." Street words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What are the children riding?" … "Their bikes!" … "How does Alex come to school?"
