Week 41 · Lesson 3: My Ticket

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Animals. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 41: Trains, boats and planes.

Fun for Starters · Week 41: Trains, boats and planes | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Transport words Lesson 2 · A day out Lesson 3 · My ticket

You Need

  • A busy outdoor picture with a train, cows, a kite, a boat and children running (a picture book page, or your own drawing).
  • Card, scissors and crayons to make tickets, and chairs in two lines for a pretend train.
  • "Ticket" is a Movers word, but this unit of the book is built around it, so it's fine to use here.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. What can you see? (8 min): ask: "What can you see? How many cows are there? What colour is the train? Where is the kite? What are the children doing?" Your child answers, then asks you two questions. Animal words
  3. My ticket (7 min): your child makes a ticket and writes on it: "____ ticket (train / bus / plane) from ____ to ____." Then draws a picture of the place.
  4. All aboard! (7 min): line up the chairs. One person checks the tickets: "Where do you want to go?" "To the beach!" "Here you are!" Then everyone rides the train. Swap jobs.
  5. Watch (3 min): Driving In My Car.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many cows are there?" … "Where do you want to go?" … "To the beach!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Spell the transport words: bus, bike, boat, plane, train, truck, ship, car, lorry, motorbike, helicopter.
  • Answer questions about a picture story and tick the right picture after listening.
  • Talk about a picture and say where they want to go.
