Week 42 · Lesson 1: What Is It?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 42: About a phone.

Fun for Starters · Week 42: About a phone | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the phones Lesson 3 · Let's count

You Need

  • Seven small pictures to label (draw them or cut them out): two friends, a street, a school, some numbers, chips, a bag, a table.
  • Copy the short text below, with the picture words underneath. Chips and school are not in the text.
  • A toy phone or an old phone for pretend photos.

A tablet: People read stories and play games on it. It has got letters and (1) ______ on it. You can find it in a store in my (2) ______. Some people put it on a (3) ______, but my mum puts it in her (4) ______.

Answers: 1 numbers, 2 street, 3 table, 4 bag.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): In Front Of, Behind, Between.
  3. Picture words (6 min): your child writes the word under each picture: friends, street, school, numbers, chips, bag, table. School words
  4. Read and choose (8 min): read the tablet text together. Your child chooses a picture word for each gap and writes it.
  5. What am I? (4 min): your child makes a riddle about a thing at home: "I'm small. I've got letters and numbers on me. People talk to their friends with me." "A phone!"
  6. Say it! (5 min): take pretend photos with the toy phone: "I'm taking photos of me and my friend with my phone!" "I'm taking a photo of the cat!"
  7. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It has got letters and numbers on it." … "What am I?" … "I'm taking photos of me and my friend!"
