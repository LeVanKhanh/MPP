Week 42 · Lesson 2: Listen and Colour the Phones

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Street and Colours. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 42: About a phone.

Fun for Starters · Week 42: About a phone | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the phones Lesson 3 · Let's count

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a street in pencil: houses with windows, a fruit shop, a phone shop, a tree, a car, three birds, two giraffes walking down the street (a silly street!). Draw five phones in the picture: in a girl's hand, on a window, under a bird, in a bag, in the tree.
  • Crayons and two toy phones.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Put the bus on the street (6 min): say: "Draw a bus on the street in front of the phone shop!" Your child draws it. Then: "Draw a balloon next to the house." Street words
  3. Listen and colour (8 min): say slowly: "Colour the phone in the tree red. The phone in the girl's hand is blue. Colour the phone under the bird green." Check together: "What colour is the phone in the bag?" Colour words
  4. Watch (3 min): The Wheels On The Bus.
  5. Phone talk (8 min): with two toy phones, talk from different rooms: "Hello! Where are you?" "I'm in the kitchen!" "What are you doing?" "I'm eating an apple!" Then swap rooms.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Colour the phone in the tree red." … "Hello! Where are you?" … "I'm in the kitchen!"
