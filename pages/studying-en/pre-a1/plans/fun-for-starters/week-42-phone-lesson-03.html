Week 42 · Lesson 3: Let's Count

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and My Street. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 42: About a phone.

Fun for Starters · Week 42: About a phone | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · What is it? Lesson 2 · Listen and colour the phones Lesson 3 · Let's count

You Need

  • The street picture from Lesson 2.
  • Seven sentences with a gap to count and write: "There are six ____ in the houses." "I can see three ____ in this picture." "There are two ____ in the street." "I can see five ____ in this picture." (and three more that fit your drawing).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): Count & Move – Count Up To Twenty.
  3. Let's count (8 min): your child counts in the street picture and writes the word for each gap: "There are six windows in the houses. I can see three birds!"
  4. Count at home (8 min): walk round your home and count: "How many windows are there? How many chairs?" Your child writes three sentences: "There are four chairs in our kitchen." Number words
  5. Listen, spell and answer (7 min): spell a word letter by letter: "P – H – O – N – E." Your child writes it, reads it and answers a question about it: "What can you do with it?" "Talk to my grandma!" Then swap. Street words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many windows are there?" … "There are six windows." … "P – H – O – N – E. What is it?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Read a short text about a thing and choose the right words for the gaps.
  • Listen and colour, and put things in the right place in a street picture.
  • Count things in a picture and write sentences: "There are six windows."
