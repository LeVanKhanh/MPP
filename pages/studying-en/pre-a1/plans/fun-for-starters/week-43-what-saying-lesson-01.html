Week 43 · Lesson 1: Which Picture?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Starters Wordlist and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 43: What are they saying?

Fun for Starters · Week 43: What are they saying? | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which picture? Lesson 2 · What are they saying? Lesson 3 · Who is it?

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw four small pictures: 1 a robot waving "Goodbye" next to a plane; 2 a boy giving flowers to his teacher ("These are yours." "Thank you."); 3 children on a boat waving "Bye!"; 4 a girl giving a dog to a boy ("This is yours." "Hooray! Thanks!").
  • A few things to give and to own: flowers, a toy dog, a toy boat.
  • "Present" is a Movers word, so the lesson says "This is for you!" and "This is yours."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (5 min): Happy Birthday – This is for you. Thanks.
  3. Which picture? (7 min): your child reads and writes the picture number: "This is a boat. Picture 3." "This is a robot." "This is a dog." "This is a teacher." Then: "In which pictures are they saying goodbye? And thank you?"
  4. His, hers or theirs? (6 min): give the flowers to Mummy, the dog to Daddy, the boat to everyone: "The flowers are hers. The dog is his. The boat is theirs!" Your child writes the three sentences.
  5. Here you are! (7 min): take turns giving things: "Here you are. This is yours." "Thank you!" "This is for you!" "Hooray! Thanks!" Toy words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "In which picture are they saying goodbye?" … "The dog is his." … "Here you are!" "Thank you!"
