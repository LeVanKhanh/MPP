Week 43 · Lesson 2: What Are They Saying?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 43: What are they saying?

Fun for Starters · Week 43: What are they saying? | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which picture? Lesson 2 · What are they saying? Lesson 3 · Who is it?

You Need

  • A busy picture with lots of people (a picture book page of a street, a market or a party), or your own drawing of six people.
  • Eight speech bubbles on small cards: Hi! Happy birthday! · Thank you. · Bye! · Pardon? · See you! · Oh dear! · Cool! Thanks. · Here you are.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Speech bubbles (10 min): your child puts a bubble next to people in the picture: "This boy is giving a burger to his friend. He's saying 'Here you are.'" There is no one right answer – it has to make sense!
  3. When do we say it? (7 min): act a moment: drop an apple – "Oh dear!"; say something very quietly – "Pardon?"; wave at the door – "Bye! See you!" Your child says the right phrase, then acts one for you.
  4. Watch (2 min): Hello Hello! Can You Clap Your Hands?
  5. Make the conversations (6 min): say a line; your child answers: "Here you are." – "Thank you." "Happy birthday!" – "Cool! Thanks." "See you!" – "Bye!" Then swap. Hello words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Oh dear!" … "Pardon?" … "Here you are." "Thank you!"
