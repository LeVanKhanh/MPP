Week 43 · Lesson 3: Who Is It?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Clothes and Toys. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 43: What are they saying?

Fun for Starters · Week 43: What are they saying? | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Which picture? Lesson 2 · What are they saying? Lesson 3 · Who is it?

You Need

  • The busy picture from Lesson 2 (or a new one).
  • Before the lesson, write six pairs of sentences about people in the picture, in two mixed-up lists. For example: "This woman is giving a burger to a small boy." ↔ "She's wearing pink trousers." "This man is waving to his grandmother." ↔ "He's standing next to the bus."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Two sentences (9 min): your child reads a sentence in list 1, finds the person, then finds the sentence in list 2 about the same person and draws a line.
  3. Describe and guess (7 min): describe someone in the picture or at home: "He's wearing glasses. He's listening to a boy." Your child guesses who. Then swap. Clothes words
  4. Watch (6 min): Yours? Mine! – Whose bike is this?
  5. What's in my picture? (3 min): hide a small drawing. Your child asks: "Is it an animal? Is it big? Is it yours?" and guesses.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "She's wearing pink trousers." … "Who is it?" … "Is it yours?"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Use everyday phrases: Thank you. Here you are. Bye! See you! Pardon? Oh dear! Cool! Thanks.
  • Use his, hers and theirs: "The dog is his. The boat is theirs."
  • Read two sentences about the same person in a picture and match them.
