Week 44 · Lesson 1: Make the Questions

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Hello and School. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 44: About us.

Fun for Starters · Week 44: About us | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Make the questions Lesson 2 · Which is the right answer? Lesson 3 · About Kim, about me

You Need

  • Five sets of word cards, each set mixed up: name? · your · What's / you? · are · How · old / your · teacher's · name? · What's / friends' · names? · What · are · your / do · you · do? · What · sports.
  • A description to read (below).

Read this to your child: "Hi! My name's Grace. I'm nine. My teacher's name is Miss Lemon. My friends' names are Ann and Sue. I play tennis and I can swim."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (2 min): What Is Your Name?
  3. Word blocks (10 min): your child puts each set of cards in order to make a question and writes it: "What's your name? How old are you? What's your teacher's name? What are your friends' names? What sports do you do?"
  4. Listen and write (6 min): read Grace's words twice. Your child writes her answers to the five questions: "Grace, 9, Miss Lemon, Ann and Sue, tennis…"
  5. Interview (7 min): your child asks someone at home the five questions and writes the answers. School words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What are your friends' names?" … "What sports do you do?" … "How old are you?"
