Week 44 · Lesson 2: Which Is the Right Answer?

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and Food and Drinks. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 44: About us.

Fun for Starters · Week 44: About us | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Make the questions Lesson 2 · Which is the right answer? Lesson 3 · About Kim, about me

You Need

  • Two lists to match with lines, mixed up. List 1: "Throw me the ball, please!" · "Show me your picture." · "Can I watch TV in bed?" · "Give me your dirty shoes!" · "Read me a story!" · "Make me a cake!" · "Tell me about your sister." · "Look! We've got a new bike!"
  • List 2: "OK! Catch it!" · "It's a big green frog. Look!" · "No, sorry. Go to sleep now." · "Dad's cleaning them." · "Which book?" · "A lemon cake or a chocolate cake?" · "She loves drawing cats." · "Hooray! What colour is it?"

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Draw lines (9 min): your child reads each sentence in list 1 and draws a line to the right answer in list 2. Then you read the pairs aloud together like little conversations.
  3. Give me, show me (7 min): "Show me your foot! Give me your hand! Throw me the teddy!" Your child does it. Body words
  4. Watch (2 min): Hello Hello! Can You Clap Your Hands?
  5. Your turn (7 min): your child gives you requests: "Sing me a song, please! Make me a cake!" You answer with a sentence before you do it: "OK! Here you are!" Food words
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Throw me the ball, please!" … "OK! Catch it!" … "Read me a story!" "Which book?"
