Week 44 · Lesson 3: About Kim, About Me

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Hello and At Home. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 44: About us.

Fun for Starters · Week 44: About us | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Make the questions Lesson 2 · Which is the right answer? Lesson 3 · About Kim, about me

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw eight rows of two or three small pictures (A, B, C): a flat / a house; Sun School / Clock School / Beach School; the numbers 11, 7, 12; green / blue / brown eyes; black / brown / yellow hair; a guitar / a piano; a desk / a cupboard / a bookcase; stories about planes / sport / animals.
  • A description to read (below), a writing frame, and two bingo cards.
  • Writing about themselves is for home. If you share it, leave out the school's name and where you live.

Read this to your child: "This is Kim. She lives in a flat. She goes to Sun School, and she's in class seven. Kim's eyes are blue and her hair is brown. She plays the piano. Her books are in the bookcase, and her favourite stories are about animals."

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Listen and tick (9 min): read about Kim slowly, twice. Your child ticks one picture in each row.
  3. Write about you (8 min): your child fills in a frame: "My name's ____. I'm ____. My eyes are ____ and my hair is ____. I play the ____. My favourite stories are about ____." Home words
  4. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name?
  5. About us bingo (6 min): each bingo card has nine facts ("has got blue eyes", "plays the piano", "likes animal stories", "is seven"…). Ask people at home; cover a fact when someone says yes. Three in a line: "Bingo!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "Kim lives in a flat." … "Her favourite stories are about animals." … "My eyes are brown."

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Put words in order to make questions: "What are your friends' names?"
  • Choose the right answer to a request: "Throw me the ball, please!" "OK! Catch it!"
  • Listen to a description and tick the right pictures, and write about themselves.
