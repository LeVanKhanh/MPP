Week 45 · Lesson 1: A to Z

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: Numbers and ABC and Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 45: Happy ending!

Fun for Starters · Week 45: Happy ending! | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A to Z Lesson 2 · Rhyme time Lesson 3 · The circles game

You Need

  • The 26 letters on small cards, and a big sheet with A to Z down the side for an alphabet book.
  • A small crossword with six words from the book and picture clues (apple, bread, jeans, mango, onion, watch).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Watch (3 min): The Alphabet Song.
  3. My A to Z (10 min): for each letter, your child says and writes a Starters word: "A – apple, B – bread, C – coconut, D – dress, E – eleven, F – face…" Some letters are hard (Q – question, X – … ?). Use the Starters wordlist to help.
  4. Mini crossword (7 min): your child writes the six picture words in the crossword.
  5. Alphabet race (5 min): "Give me a word with M!" "Mango!" "Give me a food with P!" "Pear!" Then your child asks you.
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "A – apple, B – bread!" … "Give me a word with M!" … "Mango!"
