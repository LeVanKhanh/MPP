Week 45 · Lesson 2: Rhyme Time

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson page: Numbers and ABC. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 45: Happy ending!

Fun for Starters · Week 45: Happy ending! | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A to Z Lesson 2 · Rhyme time Lesson 3 · The circles game

You Need

  • Six big circles with a word in each: bean, day, right, three, zoo, ball.
  • Twelve word cards: clean, green, they, say, kite, night, sea, bee, two, you, wall, hall.
  • These are words that sound the same at the end (they "rhyme"). Say them aloud – the spelling can be different!

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Word groups (9 min): your child reads each card aloud and puts it in the circle that sounds the same at the end: "bean – clean, green", "zoo – two, you", "ball – wall, hall".
  3. Rhyme pictures (6 min): your child draws two funny pictures and writes under them: "A green bean is clean!" "A kite at night!"
  4. Watch (2 min): The Alphabet Is So Much Fun.
  5. Sound hunt (8 min): say a word: "Cat!" Your child finds something at home that sounds the same: "Hat! Mat!" Then: "Bed!" "Red!" "Ball!" "Wall!"
  6. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Bean, clean, green!" … "Ball, wall, hall!" … "Cat… hat!"
