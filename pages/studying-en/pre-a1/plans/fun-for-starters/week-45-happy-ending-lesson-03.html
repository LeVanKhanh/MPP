Week 45 · Lesson 3: The Circles Game

30 minutes for young learners (5–8). Lesson pages: My Body and Starters Wordlist. Goes with Fun for Starters – Unit 45: Happy ending!

Fun for Starters · Week 45: Happy ending! | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · A to Z Lesson 2 · Rhyme time Lesson 3 · The circles game

You Need

  • Before the lesson, draw a path of about 20 circles from "start" to "end". Write a clue in each circle: "part of a day", "you play this", "an animal", "children play with these", "you eat this", "lives in water", "your mother's father", "part of your body", "you wear this", "not old", "not beautiful", "you see animals here". Put "go back to the start!" in two circles.
  • A dice and two small toys as players. A sheet of paper for a "My Fun for Starters" page and stickers.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. The circles game (14 min): take turns throwing the dice and moving. On each circle, say a word for the clue: "You eat this – banana!" "Lives in water – fish!" No word? Go back one circle. Who gets to the end first?
  3. Watch (2 min): If You're Happy And You Know It.
  4. My Fun for Starters (9 min): your child writes and draws: "My favourite word is ____. My favourite game is ____. My favourite unit is ____." Then puts the last sticker on the page. Well done! Starters wordlist
  5. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "You eat this!" "Banana!" … "Your mother's father?" "Grandfather!" … "Well done!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Find a Starters word for (almost) every letter from A to Z.
  • Hear words that sound the same at the end: bean – green, day – say, ball – wall.
  • Answer quick clues about everything in the book – and finish Fun for Starters!
