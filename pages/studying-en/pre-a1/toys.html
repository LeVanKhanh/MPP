Basic sentences Sentences

What's this? – It's a ___.

  • What's this?
  • It's a teddy.
  • What's this?
  • It's a robot!

Is this a ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  • Is this a car?
  • Yes, it is!
  • Is this a doll?
  • No, it isn't. It's a teddy.

What colour is the ___? – It's ___.

  • What colour is the car?
  • It's blue.
  • I've got a green ball.
  • My train is red and yellow.

Let's play! ___ the ball!

  • Let's play!
  • Throw the ball!
  • Catch the ball!
  • Kick the ball! Hooray!

What's Teddy on? – He's on the ___.

  • What's Teddy on?
  • He's on the slide!
  • What's the doll on?
  • She's on the swing.

Up or down? / I like the ___! – Me too!

  • Is Teddy up or down?
  • He's up!
  • I like the slide!
  • Me too!

Parent's Corner

Game: Is this a…? (yes or no)

  • Hide a toy behind your back and show a little bit of it. "Is this a car?" Your child nods or shakes their head, or says "Yes!" or "No!". Then it's your child's turn to hide a toy.
  • Common mistake: "Yes, it's." → "Yes, it is!" Short answers end with is, not it's. Say it back happily; no correction needed.
  • Common mistake: "It's a car blue." → "It's a blue car!" The colour comes before the toy.
  • Tip: use your child's own toys, and let them choose which one to talk about. Favourite toys are the easiest words to remember.
  • Park words: up and down are Cambridge Movers words, swing is a Flyers word, and slide, seesaw and springer (a rocking horse on a spring) come from preschool courses. The small grey tag on each word says which.

Review Next Time

Ask these with real toys. Nodding, pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a teddy) What's this?
  2. (Show a car) Is this a ball?
  3. (Show a blue toy) What colour is it?
  4. Throw the ball! … Catch the ball!
  5. What's your favourite toy?
