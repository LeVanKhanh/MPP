X: no words at this level. Learners should also know the numbers 21–100, the ordinals 1st–20th and these names: Charlie, Clare, Daisy, Fred, Jack, Jane, Jim, Julia, Lily, Mary, Paul, Peter, Sally, Vicky, Zoe.

Study tips

  • Learn the Starters words first: Movers adds about 400 new words on top of them.
  • Pick one topic a week. Tap each word, then say a sentence with it: "My aunt has got curly hair."
  • Many Movers words are pairs – quick / quickly, loud / loudly, good / better / best. Learn them together.
