1My body🧍

Wordlist Words

Body

Face

Videos for the words Watch

English SingsingBody [Kids Vocab] My Body Parts – Head to ToesPicture-word flashcards from head to feet. Pause and say each word.
Super Simple SongsThis Is My Face – Parts of the Face SongEyes, ears, nose and mouth. Point to each one as you sing.

Basic sentences Sentences

This is my ___. / These are my ___.

  • This is my head.
  • This is my nose.
  • These are my eyes.
  • These are my feet.

I've got ___.

  • I've got two arms and two legs.
  • I've got long hair.
  • I've got a big smile.
  • The monkey has got a long tail.

What's this? – It's my ___.

  • What's this?
  • It's my ear.
  • How many hands have you got?
  • I've got two hands.

Action! ___ your ___.

  • Point to your mouth.
  • Wave your hand.
  • Clap your hands.
  • Close your eyes.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your name?
  • Have you got long hair or short hair?
  • Are you happy or sad today?

2At the zoo🦁

Wordlist Words

Animals in the picture

More animals from the A–Z list

Videos for the words Watch

English SingsingZoo [Kids Vocab] Wild Animals at the ZooLion, elephant, giraffe and more with clear pictures.
Fun Kids EnglishZoo Animals Vocabulary Chant for ChildrenA chant: hear the word, then say it in the gap.

Basic sentences Sentences

It's a ___. / They're ___.

  • It's a giraffe.
  • It's a big elephant.
  • They're monkeys.
  • They're small mice.

The ___ is ___. / The ___ has got ___.

  • The snake is long and yellow.
  • The hippo is in the water.
  • The tiger has got a long tail.
  • The goat has got yellow eyes.

Is it a ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  • Is it a horse?
  • No, it isn't. It's a cow.
  • What colour is the frog?
  • It's green.

I like ___. / I don't like ___.

  • I like monkeys. They're funny!
  • I don't like spiders.
  • My favourite animal is the crocodile.
  • A bird can fly. A duck can swim.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your favourite animal?
  • Which animals can swim?
  • Which animal is very big? Which animal is very small?

  • Monkey is between the boxes.

  • Well done! That's the end of the game.
