Parent's Corner

Game: Hello ball

  • Sit on the floor and roll a ball to each other. Whoever catches it says "Hello! My name is…". Add teddies and dolls to the circle; they say hello too.
  • Common mistake: "My name Anna." → answer happily, "Hello, Anna! My name is Mum." Your child hears the full sentence without being corrected.
  • Common mistake: "I three." → "You're three! I'm three too!" with three fingers up. Then let your child show the fingers.
  • Every session: start with the same hello song and end with the same goodbye song. Very young children love knowing what comes next.
