Week 2 · Lesson 3: What's This?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 1: School.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 2: School | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My school bag Lesson 2 · Red and yellow Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • The school bag with a book, a pencil, and red and yellow crayons inside. Teddy.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Feel and guess (4 min): your child puts a hand in the bag without looking. "What's this? Is it a pencil?" Then take it out: "Yes! It's a pencil!"
  3. Watch (2 min): Do You Have A Crayon? Hold up a crayon every time you hear "crayon".
  4. Please and thank you (4 min): "Can I have a red crayon, please?" Your child gives it: "Here you are." "Thank you!" Then swap.
  5. Watch and sing (3 min): What's Your Favourite Colour? Sing "red" or "yellow" in the answer.
  6. Colour hunt (5 min): "Find a yellow thing!" … "Find a red thing!" Put everything in the bag and name it: "A red ball! A yellow car!"
  7. Teddy goes to school (6 min): Teddy is the new pupil. Your child shows Teddy each thing: "It's a book. It's red!" Then ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (3 min): pack the bag together, then Bye Bye Goodbye and a sticker for the week.

Say this: "What's this? Is it a pencil?" … "Can I have a red crayon, please? Thank you!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to the bag, book, crayon and pencil when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Say "red" and "yellow", and find a red or a yellow thing.
  • Answer "What's this?" with a word or "It's a…".
