Week 7 · Lesson 2: Orange and Three

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 6: Food.

Amanda and Friends 1 · Week 7: Food | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit Lesson 2 · Orange and three Lesson 3 · Fruit shop

You Need

  • The fruit from Lesson 1 (three apples or three small fruits if you can), orange, red, yellow and green crayons, a sheet of paper with an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear drawn on it.
  • "Orange" is a fruit and a colour. "It's an orange. It's orange!" is a fun one to say.
  • One came in week 3 and two in week 5. Today is three: always touch one thing for each number.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Fruit bowl again (3 min): "What's this?" for each fruit. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Orange! (4 min): hold up the orange: "It's an orange. It's orange!" Then the orange crayon. Then the other colours: the apple is red, the banana is yellow, the pear is green. Colour words
  4. Watch (3 min): How many apples? Count on your fingers.
  5. Count to three (6 min): "How many apples? One, two, three. Three apples!" Touch each one. Then "Give me three crayons! … Give me two books! … Give me one ball!"
  6. Watch (2 min): I Want It! Bananas & Apples. Count along.
  7. Colour the fruit (6 min): "Colour the orange orange! Colour the apple red. Colour the banana yellow. Colour the pear green."
  8. Goodbye (3 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, waving three times.

Say this: "It's an orange. It's orange!" … "How many apples? One, two, three. Three apples!"
