Week 1 · Lesson 2: Colours and 1, 2, 3

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Colours and Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Welcome: Hello.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 1: Hello | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello again Lesson 2 · Colours and 1, 2, 3 Lesson 3 · Who's this? What's this?

You Need

  • Five crayons (blue, green, orange, red, yellow), three apples or three small toys, and three cards with a big 1, 2 and 3 written on them.
  • Five sheets of paper, one in each colour (or a big crayon dot on white paper).

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Five colours (5 min): hold up each crayon: "What colour is it?" "It's green!" Then mix them up and ask again. Colour words
  3. Watch (2 min): Red Yellow Green Blue. Hold up the right crayon each time.
  4. What number is it? (5 min): show a number card: "What number is it?" "Two!" Then put the right number of toys next to each card.
  5. How many apples? (5 min): "How many apples? One, two, three. Three apples!" Touch each one as you count. Then take one away and count again.
  6. Watch (3 min): How many apples? Count on your fingers.
  7. Colour jump (5 min): put the coloured sheets on the floor. "Jump to red! … Jump to yellow!" Then put the number cards down too: "Jump to number three!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "What colour is it? It's green!" … "What number is it? It's two!" … "How many apples?"
