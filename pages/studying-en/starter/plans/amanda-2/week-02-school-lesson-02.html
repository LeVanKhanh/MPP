Week 2 · Lesson 2: Brown, Pink, Purple

30 minutes for young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 2 – Unit 1: School.

Amanda and Friends 2 · Week 2: School | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Chair, table, door, window Lesson 2 · Brown, pink, purple Lesson 3 · What colour is the dog?

You Need

  • Brown, pink, purple and orange crayons, paper, and a few toys in those colours.
  • The five colours from week 1 (blue, green, orange, red, yellow) are known; today adds three more.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Room again (3 min): point to the door, window, table and chair: "What's this?" Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Three new colours (5 min): hold up each crayon: "It's brown! … It's pink! … It's purple!" Then look around: "The table is brown!" Colour words
  4. Watch (3 min): Learn Colours – Preschool Chant. Hold up the crayon for each colour you have.
  5. Draw flowers (5 min): "Draw a flower! Colour it pink!" Then a purple flower and a brown one. "It's a pink flower!"
  6. Is this a…? (5 min): point and make silly guesses: "Is this a table?" (pointing at the door) "No, it isn't! It's a door!" Then your child asks you.
  7. Colour hunt (4 min): "Find a pink thing! … Find a purple thing! … Find a brown thing!" Then one orange thing from last year.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's pink! The table is brown!" … "Is this a table? No, it isn't! It's a door!"
