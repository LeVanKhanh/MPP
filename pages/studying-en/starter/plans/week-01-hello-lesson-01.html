Week 1 · Lesson 1: Hello and Goodbye

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Welcome: Hello.

You Need

  • A teddy or a doll (it says hello too), three or four other toys, a soft ball, paper and crayons.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. A happy 15 minutes beats an unhappy 30.
  • About 5–6 minutes of video, watched and sung together.
  • You can explain a game in Vietnamese, then play it in English.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): sit face to face. Wave and say "Hello!" with a big smile. Teddy waves too: "Hello!" Wait for a wave back; a wave is a perfect answer.
  2. Watch (2 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs). Wave every time you hear "hello".
  3. Hello, toys! (5 min): line up the toys. Wave to each one: "Hello, teddy! Hello, car! Hello, ball!" Then your child does it.
  4. Sing and move (4 min): Hello Song for Kids (The Singing Walrus). Wave, clap and jump along.
  5. Hello ball (5 min): roll the ball to each other. Catch it, then say "Hello!" Teddy plays too.
  6. Draw (5 min): draw a big happy face together. Write "Hello!" next to it and let your child colour it.
  7. Bye-bye, toys (4 min): Bye Bye Goodbye. Then wave to every toy: "Bye-bye, teddy!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): "Goodbye! See you!" A high five or a sticker.

Say this: "Hello! Hello, teddy!" … "Bye-bye, teddy! Goodbye! See you!"
