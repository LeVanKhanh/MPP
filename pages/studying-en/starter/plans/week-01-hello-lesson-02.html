Week 1 · Lesson 2: What's Your Name?

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Hello. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Welcome: Hello.

Week 1: Hello | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Hello and goodbye Lesson 2 · What's your name? Lesson 3 · How old are you?

You Need

  • Teddy and two dolls or toys, a soft ball, paper and crayons.
  • Give each toy a name from the Starters list: Tom, Anna, Sam.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): the same hello song as last time: Hello! (Super Simple Songs). Wave together.
  2. Teddy's name (5 min): Teddy says "Hello! My name is Tom. What's your name?" Help your child answer with just the name. Then you say the full sentence: "My name is Mum!" Sentences
  3. Watch (2 min): What's Your Name? (Super Simple Puppets). Say your child's name in the gaps.
  4. Name ball (5 min): roll the ball. Whoever has it says "My name is…". The toys play too (Tom, Anna, Sam).
  5. Stand up, sit down (4 min): "Stand up! … Sit down!" Do it together, then faster and slower. Laugh a lot.
  6. Watch (2 min): What Is Your Name? (Wormhole Learning). Answer before the singer.
  7. Draw me (5 min): your child draws themself; you write their name under the picture and read it: "This is… (name)!"
  8. Goodbye (4 min): Bye Bye Goodbye, then "Bye-bye, Tom! Bye-bye, Anna!"

Say this: "Hello! My name is Tom. What's your name?" … "Stand up! Sit down!"
