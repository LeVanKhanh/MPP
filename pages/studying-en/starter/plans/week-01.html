Week 1: Animals

Three 30-minute sessions for a very young beginner: eight animals, "It's a…!", and counting to five. Lesson page: Animals.

Week of November 2, 2026 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Before You Start

  • Toys or pictures of the eight animals: cat, dog, fish, bird, duck, cow, monkey, elephant.
  • A bag to hide them in, paper and crayons, a ball, and five small toy cars for counting.
  • Each session is many short activities. Move on when attention drops; stop early if your child is tired. A happy 15 minutes beats an unhappy 30.
  • Videos: about 6–8 minutes a session, watched and sung together.
  • You can set up a game in Vietnamese, then play it in English.

Day 1 - Pets 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): sit together, wave and sing "Hello!". Show the bag: "What's in the bag?"
  2. Meet the pets (5 min): take out cat, dog, fish, bird one by one. Say each word three times, make the sound, and let your child point to it. Listen → point → say. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Pets Vocabulary Chant. Point at the screen when you hear "cat" or "dog".
  4. Move (4 min): "Walk like a cat! Swim like a fish! Fly like a bird!" Do every action together.
  5. Where's the dog? (5 min): hide the four toys around the room. "Where's the dog?" When your child finds it: "Yes! It's a dog!"
  6. Song (2 min): It's a Dog, with the actions.
  7. Draw (5 min): draw a big cat together; your child colours. "A cat! A brown cat!"
  8. Bye-bye (3 min): the toys go back in the bag: "Bye-bye, cat! Bye-bye, dog!" Give a sticker or a high five.

Say this: "Look! It's a cat! Can you say cat?" … "Where's the dog?"

☐ Done

Day 2 - Farm and zoo 30 min

  1. Bag again (3 min): the four pets from Day 1. "What's this?" Pointing or one word is perfect.
  2. New animals (5 min): duck, cow, monkey, elephant. Make the sounds: "Quack! Moo!" Then "It's a duck!" Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): The Animals On The Farm. Make every sound together.
  4. Watch and move (4 min): Let's Go To The Zoo. Stand up and move like the animals.
  5. Big and small (4 min): put the toys in two groups. "The elephant is big! The cat is small!" Stretch up tall for big, crouch down for small.
  6. Count the ducks (5 min): use bath ducks or paper ducks. "How many ducks? One, two, three!" Touch each one as you count. Stop at three today.
  7. Feed the animals (6 min): pretend food. "Give the monkey a banana! Give the cow some water!" Your child feeds the right toy, then it's your turn to listen.

Say this: "It's a duck! Quack, quack!" … "How many ducks? One, two, three. Three ducks!"

☐ Done

Day 3 - Count and "can" 30 min

  1. Bag of eight (3 min): all eight animals. "What's this?" Cheer for every one.
  2. Watch (3 min): Five Spotted Dogs. Hold up five fingers and put one down each time.
  3. Count real things (5 min): line up toy animals, then toy cars. "How many cars? One, two, three, four, five!" Touch each one; say the last number again: "Five cars!"
  4. Watch and move (4 min): Yes, I Can! Do every action.
  5. Silly questions (5 min): "A fish can swim!" (act it) … "Can a cow fly?" "Nooo!" Silly is fun at this age. Sentences
  6. Animal football (5 min): stand the animals up as targets. "Kick the ball to the cow!" Knock one down? "Bye-bye, cow!"
  7. My favourite (3 min): "My favourite animal is the monkey! And you?" Your child picks one toy and says its name.
  8. Bye-bye (2 min): sing goodbye; a sticker for the week.

Say this: "How many cars? Let's count! One, two, three, four, five. Five cars!" … "Can a fish swim? Yes, it can!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to the right animal when you say any of the eight words.
  • Say at least four of the animals; "It's a dog!" is a bonus, one word is a success.
  • Count three to five real things, touching one thing for each number.
