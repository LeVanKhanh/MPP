Week 2 · Lesson 2: Red and Yellow

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: School and Colours. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 1: School.

Week 2: School | Lesson 2 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · My school bag Lesson 2 · Red and yellow Lesson 3 · What's this?

You Need

  • The school bag, book, crayon and pencil from Lesson 1.
  • A red crayon and a yellow crayon, and a few red and yellow toys (a ball, a car, a block).
  • A sheet of paper with a big apple and a big sun drawn on it.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Bag again (3 min): "What's this?" for the bag, book, crayon and pencil. Pointing or one word is perfect.
  3. Red and yellow (5 min): hold up the red crayon: "It's red!" Then the yellow one: "It's yellow!" Sort the toys into a red pile and a yellow pile together. Colour words
  4. Watch (2 min): Red Yellow Green Blue. Hold up the red crayon on "red" and the yellow one on "yellow". Today we only need those two.
  5. Find a red thing! (5 min): "Find a red thing!" Your child brings something red. Then "Find a yellow thing!"
  6. Colour it (5 min): "Colour the apple red. Colour the sun yellow." Name the colours as your child colours.
  7. What colour is it? (5 min): hold up a toy: "What colour is it?" "Red!" Then your child asks you, and you answer "It's yellow!"
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "It's red! It's yellow!" … "What colour is it?" … "Find a red thing!"
