Week 3 · Lesson 1: Mummy and Daddy

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 2: Family.

Week 3: Family | Lesson 1 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Mummy and Daddy Lesson 2 · Brother, sister, one Lesson 3 · Who's this?

You Need

  • Family photos (an album or your phone), a teddy and a doll, a toy phone, paper and crayons.
  • Say Mummy and Daddy if your child's class does, and add mum and dad: "Mummy! My mum!"
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 8 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Family photos (5 min): look at photos together. "Who's this? This is Mummy! My mum!" "This is Daddy! My dad!" Your child points; one word is perfect. Word list
  3. Watch (3 min): Family – Talking Flashcards. Point to a photo for each word.
  4. Big steps, little steps (4 min): "Walk like Daddy!" (big steps) "Walk like the baby!" (little steps). Then your child says, and you walk.
  5. Who's this? (5 min): Teddy is Daddy, the doll is Mummy. Hold one up: "Who's this?" "Daddy!" Swap them around and ask again.
  6. Watch and fingers (2 min): Finger Family. Hold up the Daddy finger and the Mummy finger.
  7. Draw (5 min): draw Mummy and Daddy together. Write "Mummy" and "Daddy" under them and read the words to your child.
  8. Phone and goodbye (3 min): pretend to phone: "Hello, Daddy! … Bye-bye, Daddy!" Then Bye Bye Goodbye.

Say this: "Who's this? This is Mummy! My mum!" … "Hello, Daddy! Bye-bye, Daddy!"
