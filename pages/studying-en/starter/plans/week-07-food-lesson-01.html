Week 7 · Lesson 1: Fruit

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson page: Fruit and Vegetables. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 6: Food.

You Need

  • Real fruit: an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear. A bag. A knife and a plate for you, to cut a few small pieces.
  • Many short activities: move on when attention drops, and stop early if your child is tired. About 9 minutes of video, watched together.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Fruit bowl (5 min): take the fruit out one by one. "Look! It's an apple!" Smell it, hold it, say each word three times; your child points. Listen → point → say. Word list
  3. Watch (2 min): Fruit – Talking Flashcards. Hold up the real fruit when it comes on screen.
  4. Run to the fruit (4 min): put the fruit in different corners. "Run to the banana! … Run to the pear!" Then your child says, and you run.
  5. Feel and guess (5 min): a fruit in the bag. Your child feels it: "Is this an apple?" "Yes, it is!" / "No, it isn't!" Then it's your turn.
  6. Watch (4 min): Fruit Song for Kids (The Singing Walrus). Point to each fruit and sing.
  7. Taste (5 min): cut small pieces. "Do you like bananas?" "Yes!" "I like apples!" Name every piece before it is eaten.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye. "Bye-bye, apple!"

Say this: "Look! It's an apple!" … "Is this a banana?" … "Do you like pears?"
