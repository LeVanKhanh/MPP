Week 7 · Lesson 3: Fruit Shop

30 minutes for very young beginners (3–5). Lesson pages: Fruit and Vegetables and Family. Goes with Amanda and Friends 1 – Unit 6: Food.

Week 7: Food | Lesson 3 of 3 | Level: first steps to Pre A1 Starters

Lesson 1 · Fruit Lesson 2 · Orange and three Lesson 3 · Fruit shop

You Need

  • The fruit (real or paper), a bag, the family dolls (Mummy, sister), Teddy, a soft ball.
  • This is the last week of the course units. Make it a little party: cheer for everything your child can say.

Today's Lesson 30 min

  1. Hello (3 min): Hello! (Super Simple Songs).
  2. Set up the shop (3 min): put the fruit on a table together. "Apples here! Bananas here!" Count them: "One, two, three apples!"
  3. Watch (3 min): Apples & Bananas. Laugh at the silly sounds and sing.
  4. Fruit shop (7 min): your child is the shopkeeper. Mummy doll comes first: "Can I have an apple, please?" "Here you are." "Thank you!" Then the sister doll: "Can I have three bananas, please?" Count them into the bag. Then swap roles.
  5. Watch and shout (3 min): Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream? Shout "Yes, I do!" or "No, I don't!"
  6. Fruit ball (4 min): roll the ball. Whoever catches it says a fruit: "Banana!" "Pear!" Then a colour, then a number.
  7. Show Teddy (5 min): ask the five Review questions. Clap after every answer.
  8. Goodbye (2 min): Bye Bye Goodbye and a big sticker: well done!

Say this: "Can I have an apple, please? Thank you!" … "Can I have three bananas, please? One, two, three!"

☐ Done

By the end of this week your child can…

  • Point to an apple, a banana, an orange and a pear when you say the word, and say at least two of them.
  • Count three things, touching one thing for each number.
  • Say orange, red, yellow and green, and say "please" and "thank you" in the fruit shop.
