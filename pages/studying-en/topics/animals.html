Basic sentences Sentences

It's a ___. / They're ___.

  • It's a cat.
  • It's a big elephant.
  • It's a small mouse.
  • They're ducks.

Is it a ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  • Is it a dog?
  • Yes, it is!
  • Is it a horse?
  • No, it isn't. It's a donkey.

How many ___? – One, two, three!

  • How many cows?
  • One, two, three. Three cows!
  • How many birds?
  • There are five birds.

A ___ can ___.

  • A fish can swim.
  • A bird can fly.
  • A frog can jump.
  • A horse can run.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your favourite animal?
  • Have you got a pet?
  • Which animal can fly? Which animal can swim?

Parent's Corner

Game: Animal walk

  • Call out an animal and do the action together: "Walk like an elephant! Jump like a frog! Swim like a fish! Fly like a bird!" Then let your child call out the animal and you do the action.
  • Common mistake: "It's dog." → say back happily, "Yes! It's a dog!" and move on. No "wrong".
  • Common mistake: "two mouse", "three sheeps", "two fishes" → "Two mice! Three sheep! Two fish!" Sheep and fish stay the same; mouse becomes mice.
  • Counting tip: touch each animal as you count, slowly, so every number goes with one thing. Say the last number again: "One, two, three. Three cows!"

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session with toys or pictures. One word answers are fine.

  1. (Show a giraffe) What's this?
  2. (Show a dog) Is it a cat?
  3. (Put out three ducks) How many ducks?
  4. Can a fish fly?
  5. What's your favourite animal?
