Basic sentences Sentences

I can see ___.

  • I can see a big mountain.
  • I can see lots of stars in the sky.
  • There's a waterfall next to the rocks.
  • There are some boats on the lake.

What's it like there? – It's ___ and ___.

  • What's the jungle like?
  • It's hot and wet.
  • What's it like at the top of the mountain?
  • It's cold and windy.

Be careful! There are ___.

  • Be careful! There are crocodiles in the river.
  • Don't swim near the waterfall.
  • Be careful on the rocks!
  • It's dangerous to climb a mountain in the snow.

Would you like to go to ___? – Yes, I would!

  • Would you like to go to an island?
  • Yes, I would! I'd like to swim in the sea.
  • Would you like to go to the jungle?
  • No, I wouldn't. It's too hot!

💬 Let's talk

  • Are there any mountains or rivers near your home?
  • What can you see in the sky at night?
  • Would you like to go to a jungle or an island? Why?

Parent's Corner

Game: Land, water or sky?

  • Say a word; your child points down for land (mountain, field, forest), makes waves for water (river, lake, sea) or points up for sky (cloud, star, moon). Then your child says the words.
  • Common mistake: "I see the moon in sky." → "Yes, you can see the moon in the sky!" (say "the sky", "the sea", "the moon").
  • Common mistake: "leafs" → "leaves" (one leaf, two leaves). "Wild", "land" and "high" are Flyers words.
