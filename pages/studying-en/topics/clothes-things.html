Basic sentences Sentences

What's this? – It's a ___. / They're ___.

  • What's this?
  • It's a hat.
  • What are these?
  • They're shoes.

What colour is it? / What colour are they?

  • What colour is your dress?
  • It's pink.
  • What colour are your trousers?
  • They're brown.

I'm wearing ___. / She's wearing ___.

  • I'm wearing a red T-shirt.
  • I'm wearing blue jeans.
  • She's wearing a black hat.
  • He's wearing white socks.

Is it hot or cold? – It's ___.

  • Is it hot or cold?
  • It's hot! Look at the sun!
  • The ice cream is cold.
  • It's cold! Put on your coat.

Can you see the ___? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

  • Can you see the skirt?
  • Yes, I can!
  • Can you see the boots?
  • No, I can't.

Dressing up: I'm a ___!

  • I'm a pirate!
  • I'm a doctor.
  • She's a princess.
  • He's a superhero!

Parent's Corner

Game: Dress Teddy

  • Put some doll or baby clothes in a pile. "It's cold! Put on Teddy's hat!" … "It's hot! Put on Teddy's T-shirt!" Your child dresses Teddy, then gives you the orders.
  • About the words: coat, hot, cold and snow are Cambridge Movers words; jumper (a British word, "sweater" in American English) and fire come from preschool courses. All are fine to learn now.
  • Common mistake: "a trousers", "a shoes" → "trousers!", "shoes!" Trousers, jeans and shorts are always plural.
  • Common mistake: "It's red shoes." → "They're red!" Use they're for shoes, socks and trousers.

Review Next Time

Ask these with real clothes. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a hat) What's this?
  2. (Show shoes) What colour are they?
  3. What are you wearing?
  4. (Show a picture of the sun) Is it hot or cold?
  5. It's cold! What do you put on?
