Basic sentences Sentences

It's ___. / It's a ___ ___.

  • It's red!
  • It's a yellow pencil.
  • It's a blue car.
  • It's a green frog.

What colour is it? – It's ___.

  • What colour is it?
  • It's orange.
  • What colour is the sun?
  • It's yellow.

My favourite colour is ___.

  • What's your favourite colour?
  • My favourite colour is pink.
  • I like purple.
  • I don't like brown.

Colour the ___ ___.

  • Colour the apple red.
  • Colour the sun yellow.
  • Colour the sea blue.
  • Find a white thing!

Parent's Corner

Game: Colour hunt

  • Hold up a crayon: "Find a red thing!" Your child runs and brings back something red. Put everything on one big pile and name it together: "A red ball! A red book!"
  • Common mistake: mixing up colour names (blue for green, for example) is normal at 3–4. Hold up both: "This is blue. This is green." and play again.
  • Common mistake: "It's a car red." → "It's a red car!" In English the colour comes before the thing.
  • Tip: teach two colours at a time, the way the course does (red and yellow first), not all eleven at once.

Review Next Time

Ask these with crayons or toys. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a red crayon) What colour is it?
  2. (Show a yellow thing) Is it red or yellow?
  3. Find a yellow thing!
  4. What colour is your bag?
  5. What's your favourite colour?
