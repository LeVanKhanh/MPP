Basic sentences Sentences

There's a ___. / There are ___.

  • There's a tractor in the field.
  • There are three ducks on the lake.
  • There's a kitten on the roof!
  • There are lots of sheep on the farm.

Where do ___ live? – They live ___.

  • Where do cows live?
  • They live on a farm.
  • Where do monkeys live?
  • They live in the jungle.

Which one is different? – The ___, because…

  • Cow, sheep, shark, goat. Which one is different?
  • The shark, because it lives in the sea.
  • Kitten, puppy, tractor, rabbit. Which one is different?
  • The tractor, because it isn't an animal.

I'd like to be a ___.

  • I'd like to be a horse.
  • I'd like to be a bird, because I'd like to fly.
  • Would you like to be a farmer?
  • Yes, I would!

Parent's Corner

Game: Which one is different?

  • Put four cards down, three that go together and one that doesn't: cow, sheep, shark, goat. Your child says which one and why: "The shark, because it lives in the sea." Then your child makes a set for you.
  • Common mistake: "It live on a farm." → "Yes, it lives on a farm!" (say it back with -s, no "wrong").
  • Common mistake: mixing up there, their and they're, which sound alike: "There's a cow." "The children help their grandpa." "They're on the farm." Only for children who already read and write well.
