Basic sentences Sentences

Every day I ___. Then I ___.

  • Every day I wake up and get up.
  • Then I have a shower and get dressed.
  • I have breakfast in the kitchen.
  • I put on my coat and say goodbye.

When do you ___? – At ___ o'clock.

  • When do you get up?
  • At seven o'clock.
  • When do you catch the bus?
  • At eight o'clock.

Do you ___? – Yes, every day! / No, never!

  • Do you make your bed?
  • Yes, every day!
  • When do you play outside?
  • In the afternoon, after school.

Did you ___? – Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

  • Did you have a shower this morning?
  • Yes, I did.
  • Did you clean your teeth?
  • No, I didn't! I'm going now!

She didn't ___.

  • Kim woke up at eight o'clock.
  • She didn't have breakfast.
  • She didn't catch the bus.
  • What a morning!

💬 Let's talk

  • When do you get up? What do you have for breakfast?
  • How do you go to school? Who do you go with?
  • What did you do this morning? What didn't you do?

Parent's Corner

Game: My morning in pictures

  • Draw six small pictures of your child's morning together. Mix them up. Your child puts them in order and says: "First I wake up. Then…" Next day, ask: "What did you do this morning?"
  • Common mistake: "I didn't went." → "You didn't go." After didn't, use the "now" word.
  • Word tip: at Movers, say "I clean my teeth". "Brush", "late", "early", "hurry", "forget" and "umbrella" are Flyers words.

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. When do you get up?
  2. What do you dry your body with?
  3. Did you have breakfast this morning?
  4. Say three things you do every morning.
  5. Have – had. Catch – …? Wake – …?
