Basic sentences Sentences

On ___, I ___.

  • On Monday, I go swimming.
  • On Saturday morning, we go to the market.
  • What do you do on Sunday?
  • I play with my cousins.

I always / often / sometimes / never ___.

  • I always have breakfast at seven o'clock.
  • We often go to the park at the weekend.
  • Dad sometimes cooks dinner.
  • My cat never swims!

What time is it? – It's ___ o'clock.

  • What time is it?
  • It's three o'clock.
  • I go to bed at eight o'clock.
  • School starts at eight o'clock in the morning.

In the holidays, we ___.

  • In the school holidays, we go to the beach.
  • Every day we swim in the sea.
  • What did you do yesterday?
  • I went fishing with my uncle.

💬 Let's talk

  • What day is it today? What's your favourite day? Why?
  • What do you always do in the morning? What do you never do?
  • What do you do in the school holidays?

Parent's Corner

Game: My busy week

  • Draw seven boxes with the days. Your child draws one thing for each day and says: "On Tuesday I always play football." Then you guess: "What do you do on Friday?"
  • Common mistake: "In Monday." → "Oh, on Monday!" We say on a day, at seven o'clock and in the morning.
  • Common mistake: "I go always swimming." → "You always go swimming!" The how-often word goes before the action word, but after is / are: "It's always sunny."
  • Tip: days of the week start with a big letter: Monday, not monday. "Usually", "tomorrow", "month" and "minute" are Flyers words.
