Basic sentences Sentences

She's got ___ hair. / He's got a ___.

  • She's got long straight hair.
  • He's got short curly hair.
  • My dad's got a beard.
  • Grandpa's got a grey moustache.

Has she got ___? – Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.

  • Has she got curly hair?
  • No, she hasn't.
  • Has he got glasses?
  • Yes, he has.

Which girl is ___? – The girl with ___.

  • Which girl is Kim?
  • The girl with long fair hair.
  • Which man is your uncle?
  • The tall man with the beard.

You look ___! / He looks ___.

  • Your hair looks great!
  • You look surprised!
  • He looks really scary.
  • She looks happy today.

💬 Let's talk

  • Is your hair long or short? Is it curly or straight?
  • Who in your family has got glasses?
  • Has your grandpa got a beard or a moustache?

Parent's Corner

Game: Who is it?

  • Lay out family photos or six drawn faces. Choose one secret person; your child asks yes/no questions to find it: "Has he got a beard?" "No, he hasn't." "Has she got curly hair?" Then swap.
  • Common mistake: "She has got hair long." → "Yes, she's got long hair!" (the describing words go before "hair": long curly brown hair).
  • Common mistake: "He have got a beard." → "Yes, he's got a beard!" Also: the Movers list has both "blond(e)" and "fair" for light hair. "What does he look like?" is a Flyers question; at Movers say "Has he got…?"

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What colour is my hair? Is it long or short?
  2. Is your hair curly or straight?
  3. Who in your family has got glasses?
  4. Draw a man with a beard and a moustache.
  5. Make a surprised face! How do you look?
