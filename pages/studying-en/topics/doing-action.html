Basic sentences Sentences

I can ___. / I can't ___.

  • I can jump.
  • I can swim and ride a bike.
  • I can't fly.
  • Birds can fly.

Can you ___? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

  • Can you hop on one foot?
  • Yes, I can.
  • Can you skate?
  • No, I can't.

What's she doing? – She's ___ing.

  • What's she doing?
  • She's skipping.
  • What's he doing?
  • He's climbing a tree.

I'm good at ___ing.

  • I'm good at running.
  • She's good at dancing.
  • Are you good at swimming?
  • Yes, I am!

He sings very ___ly.

  • That man sings very loudly.
  • My grandpa drives very slowly.
  • The cat walks quietly into the kitchen.
  • Please carry the plates carefully!

Do you ___ quickly or slowly?

  • Do you eat quickly or slowly?
  • I eat slowly.
  • Can you clap really loudly?
  • Yes, I can! And I can sing well!

💬 Let's talk

  • What can you do with a ball?
  • What are you good at?
  • Can your dad hop on one foot?
  • Who in your family talks loudly? Who drives slowly?

Parent's Corner

Game: Simon says

  • "Simon says hop! … Simon says clap! … Skip!" Your child moves only when you say "Simon says". Then your child is Simon.
  • Common mistake: "She skipping." → "Yes, she's skipping!" (say it back with "she's", no "wrong").
  • Common mistake: "I'm good at run." → "You're good at running!" Hop is on one foot; jump is on two feet. Skip here means jumping over a rope (US: jump rope).
  • Common mistake: "He sings very loud." → "Yes, he sings very loudly!" Add -ly to say how someone does something. But good → well: "She swims well."

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. Can you hop on one foot? Show me!
  2. What am I doing? (Do an action: climb, dance, skip.)
  3. Can a fish walk?
  4. What are you good at?
  5. What can you do with a ball?
