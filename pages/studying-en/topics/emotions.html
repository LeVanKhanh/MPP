Basic sentences Sentences

Are you ___? – Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

  • Are you happy today?
  • Yes, I am!
  • Are you hungry?
  • No, I'm not. I'm thirsty.

What's the matter? – I'm ___.

  • What's the matter?
  • I'm tired.
  • What's the matter with your dog?
  • He's wet and cold!

Why is ___ crying? – Because ___.

  • Why is Sally crying?
  • Because her teddy is dirty.
  • Why is the girl afraid?
  • Because she can see a big spider!

He's laughing. / She's crying.

  • The baby is crying. He's hungry.
  • Everyone is laughing because the clown is funny.
  • Dad looks surprised!
  • Now Sally is happy because her teddy is clean.

If I'm ___, I ___. / When I feel ___, I ___.

  • If I'm thirsty, I drink some water.
  • When I feel bored, I usually read a comic.
  • When I'm really excited, I can't stop laughing!
  • I feel afraid when there's a storm.

💬 Let's talk

  • Are you happy or tired today? Why?
  • What makes you laugh? When do you cry?
  • What are you afraid of?

Parent's Corner

Game: Face cards

  • Draw faces on cards: happy, sad, angry, tired, frightened, surprised. Pick a card without showing it and make the face. Your child guesses: "You're tired!" Then asks: "Why?" You answer: "Because I went to bed late." Then swap.
  • Common mistake: "I am cry." → "Oh, you're crying!" Use -ing for crying and laughing now.
  • Common mistake: "Why sad?" – "Because tired." → "Why are you sad?" – "Because I'm tired." "Feel", "bored", "worried", "excited" and "Oh dear" are Flyers words; at Movers say "I'm…" and "What's the matter?"

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. Make a happy face! Now a surprised face!
  2. What do you say when you want a drink?
  3. Why do babies cry?
  4. What makes you laugh?
  5. What's the matter when you want to go to bed?
