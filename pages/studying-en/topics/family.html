Basic sentences Sentences

Who's this? – This is my ___.

  • Who's this?
  • This is my mum.
  • Who's this?
  • It's my grandpa.

He's my ___. / She's my ___.

  • He's my brother.
  • She's my sister.
  • He's my dad.
  • She's my grandma.

Have you got a ___? – Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

  • Have you got a brother?
  • Yes, I have. I've got one brother.
  • Have you got a sister?
  • No, I haven't.

I love my ___.

  • This is my family!
  • I love my mum.
  • I love my dad.
  • Look! A photo of my grandma.

Who's the oldest? – ___ is the oldest.

  • Who's the oldest in your family?
  • Grandpa is the oldest.
  • Who's the youngest?
  • My baby cousin is the youngest.

What did you do? – We went to ___.

  • What did you do at the weekend?
  • We went to my grandma's house.
  • My aunt made a chocolate cake.
  • I played with my cousins.

💬 Let's talk

  • Who's in your family?

Parent's Corner

Game: Photo peekaboo

  • Cover a family photo with a sheet of paper and pull it away slowly. "Who's this? Is it Grandpa?" When the face shows: "Yes! It's Grandpa!"
  • Daddy or dad? Little children, and many preschool courses, say Daddy and Mummy. The Cambridge Starters list uses dad and mum. Both are right: use the words your child's class uses, and say the other one too ("Mummy! My mum!").
  • Common mistake: "He's my sister." Mixing up he and she is normal for a long time. Point and say it back: "She's my sister!" No "wrong".
  • Common mistake: "This my mum." → "This is my mum!" with a smile.

Review Next Time

Ask these with family photos. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a photo of Mum) Who's this?
  2. (Show a photo of Dad) Is this your mum?
  3. Have you got a brother?
  4. (Show a photo of a sister, cousin or friend) Who's this?
  5. I love my… (your child finishes the sentence)
