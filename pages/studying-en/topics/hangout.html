Basic sentences Sentences

Happy birthday! – Thank you!

  • Happy birthday! This is for you.
  • Thank you! What a great present!
  • How old are you today?
  • I'm nine!

Did you enjoy your party? – Yes, it was great!

  • Did you enjoy your party?
  • Yes, it was great!
  • What presents did you get?
  • I got a kite and a comic.

I must ___.

  • I must make a cake.
  • I must buy some lemonade.
  • I must wash the glasses.
  • We must invite Grandma!

Would you like to come to my party?

  • Would you like to come to my party on Saturday?
  • Yes, please! Thank you.
  • Can I invite my friend Daisy?
  • Yes, you can.

Why don't we ___? / How about ___ing?

  • Why don't we go to the funfair?
  • How about playing a board game?
  • We could make a cake for Grandma.
  • Good idea! / No, it's too cold.

Shall we ___? – Good idea!

  • Shall we go to the cinema?
  • Good idea!
  • Shall we have a picnic in the park?
  • Yes, let's go!

💬 Let's talk

  • What do you like doing at parties?
  • What food do you like at a party?
  • Where do you like going with your friends?

Parent's Corner

Game: Stop the music!

  • Play a party song and dance. When you stop the music, everyone stands still. Then ask a quick question: "What must we buy for the party?" "Lemonade!" Start the music again.
  • Common mistake: "I must to buy lemonade." → "Yes, you must buy lemonade!" (no "to" after "must").
  • Common mistake: "Happy birthday!" "Happy birthday!" → the answer is "Thank you!" "Surprise" is a Flyers word; at Movers just shout "Happy birthday!"

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What do we say to someone on their birthday?
  2. What must you do before a party?
  3. What can you put in a bottle? In a bowl?
  4. Would you like to come to my party?
  5. Shall we go to the cinema or have a picnic?
