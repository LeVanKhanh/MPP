Basic sentences Sentences

What's the matter? – I've got a ___.

  • What's the matter?
  • I've got a headache.
  • What's the matter with Tom?
  • He's got a stomach-ache.

My ___ hurts.

  • My arm hurts.
  • My tooth hurts. I must go to the dentist.
  • Does your leg hurt?
  • Yes, it does.

You must ___. / You mustn't ___.

  • You've got a temperature. You must go to bed.
  • You must drink lots of water.
  • You mustn't go to school today.
  • You mustn't eat lots of sweets.

Are you better now? – Yes, I'm well.

  • Are you better now?
  • Yes, I'm well, thank you.
  • Be careful! The floor is wet.
  • The doctor says I'm very brave!

Why weren't you at school? – I was ill.

  • Why weren't you at school yesterday?
  • I was ill. I had a bad cough.
  • What was the matter?
  • I hurt my foot.

He ate ___. Now he's got a ___.

  • He ate four ice creams. Now he's got a stomach-ache.
  • She played outside in the wind. Now she's got an earache.
  • I fell off my bike and hurt my arm.
  • Don't worry! You're OK now.

💬 Let's talk

  • What do you do when you've got a cold?
  • Who helps you when you're ill?
  • What must you do to be well?

Parent's Corner

Game: Teddy hospital

  • Your child is the doctor and the toys are ill. "What's the matter, Teddy?" "I've got a toothache." "You must go to the dentist!" Then you are the doctor.
  • Common mistake: "I have headache." → "Oh no! You've got a headache!" (say "a": a headache, a cold, a temperature).
  • Common mistake: "My head is hurt." → "Your head hurts?" "Ambulance" and "medicine" are Flyers words; at Movers say "go to the hospital" or "see the doctor".

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  2. What's the matter? (Hold your head.)
