Basic sentences Sentences

I like ___ing. / I don't like ___ing.

  • I like drawing.
  • I love reading comic books.
  • I don't like watching DVDs.
  • My brother likes playing table tennis.

What do you like doing? – I like ___.

  • What do you like doing at the weekend?
  • I like ice skating!
  • What does your sister like doing?
  • She likes playing the piano.

Can you play the ___? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

  • Can you play the guitar?
  • Yes, I can.
  • Can you play the piano?
  • No, I can't, but I want to learn!

Would you like to ___? – Yes, I'd like to. / No, I wouldn't.

  • One day, would you like to sail round the world?
  • Yes, I'd like to!
  • Would you like to sleep in the jungle?
  • No, I wouldn't. It's too scary!

When I'm older, I want to ___.

  • When I'm older, I want to draw comic books.
  • When I'm older, I want to sail a big boat.
  • I practise the guitar every day.
  • I want to play in a band!

💬 Let's talk

  • What do you like doing after school?
  • What does your mum or dad like doing?
  • What do you want to do when you're older?

Parent's Corner

Game: Three-word story

  • Write three words (for example "guitar, bus, school"). Your child makes one sentence with all three: "Lily took her guitar on the bus to school." Then your child gives you three words.
  • Common mistake: "I like read." → "Yes, you like reading!" (after "like", "love" and "enjoy" we use -ing).
  • Common mistake: "I play piano." → "You play the piano!" (instruments take "the"; sports don't: "play football").

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What do you like doing at the weekend?
  2. Can you play the guitar or the piano?
  3. Do you like reading comic books?
  4. What does your friend like doing?
  5. What do you want to do when you're older?
