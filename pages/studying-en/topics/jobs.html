Basic sentences Sentences

What's your work? – I'm a ___.

  • What's your work?
  • I'm a nurse. I work in a hospital.
  • My uncle is a farmer. He works on a farm.
  • A dentist looks at your teeth.

I like ___ing. / I have to ___.

  • I like watching TV.
  • I have to clean my teeth.
  • I don't have to go to school on Sunday.
  • Do you have to work at the weekend?

Before, she had to ___. Now she ___.

  • Before, Kate had to catch the bus.
  • Now she walks to work.
  • When I was three, I had to sleep after lunch.
  • Now I don't have to!

Who do you help? – I help ___.

  • Who do you help at home?
  • I help Dad. I wash the plates.
  • Last weekend I washed the car with Mum.
  • Grandma is planting flowers in the garden.

What's the matter? – I can't find my ___.

  • What's the matter?
  • I can't find my kitten!
  • Where were you when you lost her?
  • Near the library. Can you help me?

I'll be ___. / I might be ___. / I won't be ___.

  • In ten years I'll be at university.
  • I might be an engineer.
  • I won't be a pilot – it's boring!
  • Will you travel to the moon? – I might!

What do you want to be? – I want to be ___.

  • What do you want to be?
  • I want to be a doctor.
  • Why?
  • Because I like helping people.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's Mum's work? What's Dad's work? Where do they work?
  • What do you have to do every day? What do you like doing?
  • What do you want to be? Why?

Parent's Corner

Game: Who am I?

  • Give two clues: "I work in a kitchen. I make food for lots of people." Your child guesses: "You're a cook!" Then your child gives the clues.
  • Common mistake: "I have to went to school." → "I had to go to school." Use the "now" word after have to and had to.
  • Word tip: "Job", "uniform", "pilot", "police", "fire" and "waiter" are Flyers words. At Movers, say "What's your work?"; at Flyers, all the jobs on this page are fine.

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.
