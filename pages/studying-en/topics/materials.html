Basic sentences Sentences

What's it made of? – It's made of ___.

  • What's your table made of?
  • It's made of wood.
  • My sweater is made of wool.
  • The windows are made of glass.

Is it made of ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  • Is it made of metal?
  • No, it isn't. It's made of plastic.
  • Is it soft or hard?
  • It's hard and quite heavy.

We use ___ to make ___.

  • We use sand to make glass.
  • We use wood to make paper.
  • Some rings are made of gold. They're very expensive!
  • Bridges are made of metal because it's strong.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your bed made of? Your bag? Your shoes?

Parent's Corner

Game: Find it in this room!

  • Say a material: "Glass!" Your child has ten seconds to touch something made of glass and say: "This window is made of glass." Then swap.
  • Common mistake: "It's made from wood." and "It's made of wood." are both fine, but at Flyers say "made of". "Cotton", "leather" and "cardboard" are not on the Flyers list – use "card" for thin, hard paper.

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What are windows made of?
  2. Name three things made of wood.
  3. Is gold cheap or expensive?
  4. What do we use to make glass?
  5. What's your favourite sweater made of?
