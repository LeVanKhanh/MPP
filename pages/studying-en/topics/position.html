Basic sentences Sentences

Where's the ___? – It's in / on / under the ___.

  • Where's the cat?
  • It's in the box.
  • Where's the ball?
  • It's under the table.

The ___ is on / under the ___.

  • The book is on the chair.
  • Teddy is on the bed.
  • The dog is under the bed.
  • My socks are in the bag.

Next to, behind, in front of, between

  • The lamp is next to the bed.
  • The car is behind the box.
  • The ball is in front of the door.
  • Teddy is between the dolls.

Is it in the ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  • Is the mouse in the box?
  • No, it isn't. It's on the box!
  • Is Teddy under the sofa?
  • Yes, he is!

💬 Let's talk

  • Where's your bed? What's on your bed?
  • Where are your shoes?
  • What's under the table?

Parent's Corner

Game: Where's the mouse?

  • Your child closes their eyes while you hide a small toy in, on or under something. "Where's the mouse? Is it under the chair?" Your child looks and says where it is. Then swap.
  • Start with three: in, on and under first. Add next to, behind, in front of and between only when those are easy.
  • Common mistake: "It's under table." → "It's under the table!" Just say it back.
  • Tip: use your hands. One hand is the box, the other is the toy: put it in, on, under. Children copy the hands and the words together.

Review Next Time

Ask these with a box and a toy. Putting the toy in the right place is a good answer.

  2. Put the ball on the table!
  3. Is the cat under the chair?
  4. Where are your shoes?
  5. Put Teddy next to the bed!
