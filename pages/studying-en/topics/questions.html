Basic sentences Sentences

What's your ___? / How old are you?

  • What's your favourite colour?
  • It's blue.
  • How many brothers and sisters have you got?
  • I've got one sister.

Who / Where / Which ___?

  • Who do you sit next to in class?
  • I sit next to Lucy.
  • Where do you do your homework?
  • Which is your favourite school day?

When ___? / Why ___? – Because ___.

  • When did you see the film?
  • On Saturday.
  • Why do you like it?
  • Because it's really funny!

Is it ___? / Can it ___? – Yes, it is. / No, it can't.

  • Is it an animal?
  • Yes, it is.
  • Can it fly?
  • No, it can't. It can swim!

How do you spell ___? / Can you say that again, please?

  • What's this?
  • Sorry, I don't know.
  • It's a kangaroo.
  • Can you say that again, please? How do you spell it?

How often ___? / How do you ___?

  • How often do you see your friends?
  • Every day at school.
  • How do you come to school?
  • By bus. Sometimes I walk.

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your favourite film? Why do you like it?
  • Who do you sit next to in class?
  • Can you play the piano or the guitar? Are you good at singing?

Parent's Corner

Game: Interview

  • Your child is a reporter with a toy microphone. They ask you five questions (who, what, where, when, why) and write short answers. Then swap: you interview your child.
  • Common mistake: "Where you live?" → "Where do you live?" Most questions need do / does / did: "What did you see?"
  • Common mistake: "Why you like it? – Because funny." → "Why do you like it? – Because it's funny!" A full answer after "because" sounds better in the speaking test.
  • Tip: in the Movers speaking test, one or two words is OK, but a little more is better: "By bus. My mum comes with me." "Left" and "right" (for directions) are Flyers words.

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. How old are you?
  2. Who is your best friend?
  3. When is your birthday?
  4. Why do you like your favourite toy?
  5. How often do you eat ice cream?
