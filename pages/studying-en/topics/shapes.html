Parent's Corner

Game: Shape hunt

  • Cut a circle, a square and a star out of paper. Hold one up: "Find a circle!" Your child finds a clock, a plate, a ball… and you name it together: "The clock is a circle!"
  • About the words: circle, square, star and shape are in the Cambridge A1 Movers list, one level above Starters. Preschool courses teach them early, which is fine: learn them with the hands and eyes first.
  • Common mistake: "It's circle." → "It's a circle!" Just say it back with a smile.
  • Tip: make shapes with your body: a big circle with your arms, a square with your fingers.

Review Next Time

Ask these with paper shapes. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a circle) What shape is it?
  2. (Show a square) Is it a circle or a square?
  3. Find a circle in the room!
  4. Draw a big square!
  5. (Put out three stars) How many stars?
