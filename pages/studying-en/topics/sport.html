Basic sentences Sentences

I play ___. / I go ___ing.

  • I play football with my friends.
  • I play badminton on Saturdays.
  • I go swimming every week.
  • I go fishing with my grandpa.

What did you do on Saturday? – I played ___.

  • What did you do on Saturday?
  • I played tennis in the morning.
  • I watched a football game on TV.
  • It rained, but we laughed a lot!

Can you ___? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

  • Can you play table tennis?
  • Yes, I can.
  • Can you sail a boat?
  • No, I can't.

What's your favourite sport? – It's ___.

  • What's your favourite sport?
  • It's basketball!
  • Who's your favourite player?
  • We scored three goals!

Parent's Corner

Game: Sports charades

  • Act a sport without words; your child guesses: "You're playing tennis!" "You're going swimming!" Then swap. Add the past: "What did I do?" "You played basketball!"
  • Common mistake: "I play swimming." → "You go swimming!" (We play ball games; we go swimming, fishing, sailing, ice skating.)
  • Common mistake: "Yesterday I play football." → "Yesterday you played football!" The -ed sounds like "t" in walked, watched, laughed, and like "d" in played and rained. "Team", "win" and "match" are Flyers words.

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What sports do you play with a ball?
  2. Do we say "play swimming" or "go swimming"?
  3. What did you do last Saturday?
  4. Can you play badminton?
  5. What's your favourite sport? Why?
