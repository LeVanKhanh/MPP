Basic sentences Sentences

Last weekend I went to ___.

  • Last weekend I went to the funfair.
  • I took my scarf because it was cold.
  • We saw a film at the cinema.
  • Then we had chicken and rice for dinner.

What did you do? – I ___.

  • What did you do yesterday?
  • I played football in the park.
  • What did you buy?
  • I bought some crayons.

Where were you? – I was at ___.

  • Where were you on Saturday?
  • I was at Grandma's house.
  • Who were you with?
  • I was with my cousins.

When I was young, I ___ed.

  • When I was young, I played football every day.
  • Grandma danced at every party.
  • My brother dropped his ice cream on the floor.
  • We all laughed at the funny clown.

I couldn't ___ because ___.

  • Could you swim when you were three?
  • No, I couldn't. But I could ride a bike!
  • I couldn't play outside because it was raining.
  • I couldn't find my bag. My cat hid it!

Did you enjoy it? – Yes, it was ___!

  • Did you enjoy your trip?
  • Yes, it was great!
  • Was the film scary?
  • No, it wasn't. It was boring!

💬 Let's talk

  • Where were you on Saturday? Who were you with?
  • What did you do that was fun last weekend?
  • What was the weather like on Sunday? How was your weekend?

Parent's Corner

Game: Weekend news

  • Every Monday, your child tells you three things about the weekend: where they went, what they did and how it was. Draw one picture together and write one sentence under it.
  • Common mistake: "Yesterday I go to the park." → "Oh, you went to the park!" Say the past word back with a smile.
  • Common mistake: "Did you went?" → "Did you go?" After did, use the "now" word. "Ago" is a Flyers word; at Movers say "yesterday" or "last week". The Movers word for a jumper is "sweater".
  • Common mistake: "I droped it." → "You dropped it!" Short words like drop and stop double the last letter. Words with e at the end only add -d: move → moved, dance → danced.
  • Common mistake: "I didn't can swim." → "I couldn't swim." The past of can is could; the past of can't is couldn't.

Review Next Time

Ask these at the start of the next session. Short answers are fine.

  1. What did you have for dinner yesterday?
  2. Where did you go last weekend?
  3. Go – went. See – …? Take – …?
  4. Was your weekend boring or exciting?
  5. What did you buy last time we went shopping?
