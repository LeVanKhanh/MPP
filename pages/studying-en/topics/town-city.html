Basic sentences Sentences

What's this? – It's a ___. / Is it a ___?

  • What's this?
  • It's a bus!
  • Is it a boat?
  • No, it isn't. It's a ship.

What number is the ___? – It's number ___.

  • What number is the bus?
  • It's number six.
  • What colour is the car?
  • It's red.

I ride / drive / fly ___.

  • I ride my bike in the park.
  • My dad drives a blue car.
  • The plane flies.
  • The boat is on the water.

Where's the ___? – It's in front of / behind ___.

  • Where's the car?
  • It's in front of the bus.
  • The bike is next to the tree.
  • Stop! … Go!

Who lives at number ___? – ___ lives there.

  • Who lives at number 5?
  • Mr and Mrs Brown live there.
  • Who lives in the flat with the big balcony?
  • My friend Daisy lives there.

What's he like? – He's really ___.

  • What's Mrs Brown like?
  • She's really nice.
  • What's Mr Brown like?
  • He's funny, and he's very kind.

Where can you ___? – At the ___.

  • Where can you see a doctor?
  • At the hospital.
  • Where can you catch a bus?
  • At the bus station.

We went to the ___. Then we ___.

  • We took the bus to the town centre.
  • First we went to the market and bought some fruit.
  • Then we had lunch in a café.
  • After that we watched a film at the cinema.

Parent's Corner

Game: Stop! Go!

  • Hold up a red paper: "Stop!" A green paper: "Go!" Your child drives a toy car, or runs, and stops and goes. Then your child holds the papers and you drive.
  • Common mistake: "It's train." → "It's a train!" Just say it back with a smile.
  • Common mistake: "I ride bike." → "I ride my bike!"
  • Tip: put a toy in a vehicle and ask "What's Teddy in?" "A car!" It is a favourite question in preschool books.

Review Next Time

Ask these with toy vehicles. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show a toy bus) What's this?
  2. (Put a number card on the bus) What number is the bus?
  3. Stop! … Go!
  4. (Put Teddy in a car) What's Teddy in?
  5. Which do you like: a car or a train?
