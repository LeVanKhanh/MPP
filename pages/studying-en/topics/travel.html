Basic sentences Sentences

How did you travel? – By ___.

  • How did you travel to Grandma's house?
  • By train.
  • We went by car, and then by boat.
  • I'd like to go on a trip by helicopter!

I sent you a ___.

  • I sent you a text this morning.
  • Did you get my email?
  • No, I didn't.
  • Can you send me the photos?

Hello! Can I talk to ___, please?

  • Hello! This is Daisy. Can I talk to Jim, please?
  • Yes. Jim! It's Daisy on the phone!
  • I'm phoning because I'm on holiday.
  • See you on Saturday. Bye!

I'd love to go to ___.

  • I'd love to go to an island.
  • I'd love to travel there by plane.
  • I'd love to go there with my cousins.
  • I'd love to swim with dolphins!

I'm going on a trip. I'm taking ___.

  • I'm going on a trip to the mountains.
  • I'm taking a map and my tablet.
  • Have you got your ticket?
  • Yes, it's in my bag!

💬 Let's talk

  • How do you travel to school? How did you travel on your last holiday?
  • Who do you phone or send messages to?
  • What do you take on a trip?

Parent's Corner

Game: Pack your bag

  • Take turns: "I'm going on a trip and I'm taking a map." "I'm going on a trip and I'm taking a map and a ticket." Each person adds one thing. Who can remember them all?
  • Common mistake: "I went by the car." → "Oh, you went by car!" (no "the" after "by").
  • Common mistake: "Yesterday I send a message." → "Yesterday you sent a message!" "Taxi", "journey", "airport" and "speak" are Flyers words.
