Basic sentences Sentences

What's this? – It's a ___. / It's an ___.

  • What's this?
  • It's an apple.
  • Is this a pear?
  • No, it isn't. It's a banana!

How many ___? – Three!

  • How many apples?
  • One, two, three. Three apples!
  • How many bananas?
  • Two bananas.

I like ___. / Do you like ___?

  • I like bananas!
  • I don't like onions.
  • Do you like pears?
  • Yes, I do!

Can I have ___, please? – Here you are.

  • Can I have an orange, please?
  • Here you are. – Thank you!
  • Would you like a banana?
  • Yes, please!

💬 Let's talk

  • What's your favourite fruit?

Parent's Corner

Game: Fruit shop

  • Put real or paper fruit on a table. Your child is the shopkeeper. "Can I have an apple, please?" "Here you are." "Thank you!" Then swap: your child buys and you sell.
  • Common mistake: "a apple", "a orange" → "an apple! an orange!" Before a, e, i, o, u sounds we say an. Just say it back.
  • Common mistake: "three apple" → "Three apples!" Hold up the three fingers as you say it.
  • Tip: snack time is English time. Cut real fruit, smell it, taste it, and count the pieces together.

Review Next Time

Ask these with real fruit. Pointing or one word is a good answer.

  1. (Show an apple) What's this?
  2. (Show a pear) Is this a banana?
  3. (Put out three apples) How many apples?
  4. What colour is an orange?
  5. Do you like pears?
