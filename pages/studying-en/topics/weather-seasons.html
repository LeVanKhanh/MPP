Basic sentences Sentences

What's the weather like? – It's ___.

  • What's the weather like today?
  • It's sunny and hot.
  • What's the weather like in the mountains?
  • It's cold and windy.

It's raining. / It's snowing.

  • It's raining. Put on your coat!
  • It's snowing! Let's play outside.
  • Look! There's a rainbow in the sky.
  • My clothes are wet!

When it's ___, I ___.

  • When it's sunny, I go to the beach.
  • When it's windy, I fly my kite.
  • When it's raining, I read a book.
  • When it's cold, I wear a scarf.

Do you like ___ weather? – Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

  • Do you like windy weather?
  • Yes, I do! I can fly my kite.
  • Do you like cold weather?
  • No, I don't.

It was ___ yesterday. / It rained yesterday.

  • It was cold yesterday.
  • It was windy and cloudy on Monday.
  • It rained yesterday. My shoes were wet!
  • It snowed in the mountains yesterday.

the coldest, the hottest, the wettest…

  • Antarctica is the coldest place in the world.
  • Today is the sunniest day this week!
  • Which is the windiest day this week?
  • Friday was the windiest day.

Parent's Corner

Game: Weather actions

  • Say a weather and your child acts it: "It's windy!" (lean and wobble), "It's raining!" (hands over head), "It's cold!" (shiver), "It's hot!" (fan your face). Then your child says one for you.
  • Common mistake: "It's rain." → "Yes, it's raining!" ("rain" is the thing; "it's raining" is what it does).
  • Common mistake: "How is the weather like?" → "What's the weather like?" or "How's the weather?" Seasons (spring, summer, autumn, winter) are Flyers words; keep them for later or as bonus words.
